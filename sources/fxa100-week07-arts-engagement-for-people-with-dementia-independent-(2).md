## Arts Engagement for People with Dementia: Independent Evaluation of the Art Access Program

The report evaluates the Art Access Program at the Art Gallery of New South Wales (AGNSW) for people living with dementia. The program aims to provide a meaningful engagement with art through guided visits facilitated by trained staff and volunteers. Since its inception in 2010, the program has evolved significantly, receiving support from sponsors such as State Street and training assistance from Alzheimer’s Australia NSW. The evaluation highlights that these programs offer social inclusion and cognitive stimulation for individuals with dementia, reducing isolation and stigma associated with the condition.

The study employed a mixed-method approach combining ethnographic observations, interviews, and survey questionnaires to assess the program's impact. Key findings reveal positive benefits such as enhanced mood, improved communication skills among participants, and a sense of community and shared experience. The report underscores the importance of tailored approaches in facilitating art engagement for people with varying stages of dementia, emphasizing flexibility and adaptability in program design.
