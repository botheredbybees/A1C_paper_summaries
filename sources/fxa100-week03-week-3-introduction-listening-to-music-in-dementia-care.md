## Week 3 Introduction: Listening to Music in Dementia Care

This document introduces Week 3 of a course focusing on the role of music in dementia care. The content highlights various ways listening to music can benefit individuals with dementia, enhancing their quality of life through non-pharmacological interventions. Two notable programs are discussed: The Music and Memory project and the Power of Music and Dementia Study, both of which explore how music can be used effectively based on neuroscientific research. Additionally, cutting-edge research from the University of Melbourne is mentioned, involving wearable devices to monitor biomarkers for agitation and adjusting musical playlists accordingly. Another key aspect covered includes the cognitive benefits of rhythmic entrainment through percussion instruments and emotional responses triggered by music. The document also touches upon retaining musical memories and increasing dopamine release. For this week's activity, participants are instructed to prepare a culturally and generationally appropriate playlist for individuals living with dementia based on Levitin’s Chapter 8 from 'This Is Your Brain on Music'.
