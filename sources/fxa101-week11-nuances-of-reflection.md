## Nuances of Reflection

The document discusses the development of a structured reflection model aimed at enhancing practitioners' reflective abilities in clinical and educational settings. The author, Chris Johns, presents a ninth edition of the model which evolved through collaborative work with reflective practitioners. Key changes include simplifying descriptions and emphasizing the patient's perspective in reflection. The model is designed to enable practitioners to access and make sense of their experiences more effectively, thereby improving their practice. Reflection is seen as a reflexive and dynamic process, with the model adapting to the needs and experiences of the practitioners using it. The paper highlights the importance of context and contributing factors in understanding experiences and concludes that the model should be responsive and reflective to remain useful.
