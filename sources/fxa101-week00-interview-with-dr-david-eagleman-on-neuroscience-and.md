## Interview with Dr. David Eagleman on Neuroscience and Perception

The article features an interview with Dr. David Eagleman, a neuroscientist at Stanford University and founder of the Center for Science and Law. The conversation covers his background in neuroscience, sparked by a childhood fall from which he developed an interest in time perception. His research spans various topics including synesthesia, illusions, and sensory substitution, leading to innovations such as a vest that enables deaf individuals to perceive sound through vibrations. Dr. Eagleman discusses the challenges and mysteries of understanding brain function, particularly how thoughts and concepts are stored and interact within neural networks.
