## Threads of Hope: The Living Healing Quilt Project

Kirsty Robertson's paper discusses the Living Healing Quilt Project (LHQP) initiated by Alice Williams to document the traumatic experiences of residential school survivors and intergenerational survivors through quilts. The project aims to reveal the 'knotted underside' of Canada’s national identity, much like Pierre Elliott Trudeau's metaphorical tapestry. Robertson examines LHQP as an intervention into mainstream narratives of nation-building, highlighting how residential schools produced a fractured sense of home by stripping Indigenous children of their cultural identity and subjecting them to abuse. Quilts serve as a form of reclamation and healing for the trauma experienced by these individuals, reflecting both loss and hope.
