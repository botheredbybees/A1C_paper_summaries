## Addressing Issues of Mental Health in Schools through the Arts

This document provides excerpts from reviews of a book titled 'Addressing Issues of Mental Health in Schools through the Arts' by Nick Clough and Jane Tarr. The book discusses innovative approaches to integrating therapeutic and musical pedagogies within educational settings, emphasizing the role of music and arts therapy in addressing young learners’ mental health challenges. Experts across different disciplines, including education, psychology, and music therapy, praise the book for its rigorous scholarship, practical insights, and accessible format that enhances understanding of the intersection between creative arts and mental well-being. The reviews highlight the significance of creating safe spaces for therapeutic teaching practices within classrooms to foster resilience, inclusivity, and sustainable learning environments.
