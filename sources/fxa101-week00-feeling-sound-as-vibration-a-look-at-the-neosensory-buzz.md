## Feeling Sound as Vibration: A Look at the Neosensory Buzz

The article explores Neosensory's Buzz wrist-worn device which translates sound into vibratory patterns. It is designed for deaf and hard-of-hearing individuals, musicians, app developers interested in unique sensory experiences. Based on the scientific foundation laid by neuroscientist Dr. David Eagleman’s TED Talk, Buzz uses four vibration motors to generate over 4 billion unique patterns based on sound intensity and pitch. The device aids users in understanding auditory information through skin sensation, adapting over time as the brain learns to interpret these vibrations. Features include adjustable sensitivity levels via an app interface and training exercises for differentiation of sounds. Designed with an open API for developers, Buzz offers potential benefits beyond traditional hearing aid devices, especially for those with severe or profound hearing loss.
