## Art helps veterans and first responders heal from post-traumatic stress and depression

This article from ABC News details the therapeutic benefits of art for veterans and first responders struggling with post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and depression. Jillian Oliver, a former police officer who sustained severe injuries in an accident, initiated an art therapy group to help individuals cope with work-related traumas. The class provides participants with a sense of community and purpose, aiding them in overcoming psychological challenges and reintegrating into civilian life. Through shared experiences and creative expression, members find relief from their symptoms, build connections, and achieve personal growth.
