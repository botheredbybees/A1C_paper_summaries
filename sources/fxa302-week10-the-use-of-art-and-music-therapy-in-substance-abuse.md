## The Use of Art and Music Therapy in Substance Abuse Treatment Programs

The study examines the use of art and music therapy in substance abuse treatment programs using data from a nationally representative sample of U.S. programs. It finds that 36.8% of programs offer art therapy and 14.7% offer music therapy. Programs with a higher proportion of women and adolescents are more likely to use these therapies. The study also notes that treatment ideologies such as Motivational Enhancement Therapy and contingency management are positively associated with the use of art and music therapies, respectively. Additionally, the requirement of 12-step meetings is positively correlated with the use of both therapies. The research highlights the growing importance of complementary and alternative medical practices in addressing unique patient needs, particularly for women and adolescents.
