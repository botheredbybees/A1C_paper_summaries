## What is Music Therapy Flyer

The document outlines the benefits and application of music therapy, a research-based allied health profession that uses music to support individuals in improving their physical and mental health. It emphasizes that participants do not need musical skills and highlights the role of Registered Music Therapists (RMTs) who are certified by the Australian Music Therapy Association (AMTA). RMTs work with people across all ages and abilities, addressing a range of conditions such as dementia, cancer, and mental health issues. The flyer also details how music therapy can be employed in various settings like hospitals, schools, and residential care facilities to improve aspects including communication, cognitive function, and emotional well-being.
