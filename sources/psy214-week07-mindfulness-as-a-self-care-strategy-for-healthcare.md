## Mindfulness as a Self-Care Strategy for Healthcare Professionals to Reduce Stress and Implicit Bias

The article 'Mindfulness as a Self-Care Strategy for Healthcare Professionals to Reduce Stress and Implicit Bias' discusses the importance of mindfulness in managing stress and implicit bias among healthcare professionals, especially during challenging times like the pandemic. It highlights that prolonged stress can lead to physical health issues such as increased blood pressure and heart disease, while also contributing to higher levels of implicit biases affecting patient care. The authors argue for integrating short mindfulness practices like uncoloring thoughts, loving-kindness meditation, and noticing thoughts exercises into daily routines to alleviate stress and reduce unconscious biases. Research indicates that even brief interventions can significantly decrease both stress and implicit bias among healthcare professionals, improving overall well-being and patient outcomes.
