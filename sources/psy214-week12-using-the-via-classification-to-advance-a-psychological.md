## Using the VIA Classification to Advance a Psychological Science of Virtue

The paper explores the potential of the VIA Classification of Character Strengths and Virtues to advance a psychological science of virtue. It examines the dimensional versus categorical characterization of virtue, the identification of cardinal virtues, and discusses the three-dimensional model of virtues encompassing moral, self-regulatory, and intellectual domains. The authors emphasize the VIA Classification's role in facilitating empirical research on virtues and their impact on individual and communal flourishing. They also consider the evolutionary perspective on virtues and the concept of practical wisdom, arguing that these frameworks can contribute to a scientific understanding of virtues beyond traditional philosophical inquiries.
