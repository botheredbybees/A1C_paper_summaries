## Learning Critical Reflection: Experiences of the Transformative Learning Process

The book 'Learning Critical Reflection: Experiences of the Transformative Learning Process,' edited by Laura Béres and Jan Fook, delves into the profound impact of critical reflection on the professional development of social work students and practitioners. It emphasizes the importance of a deep, analytical approach to refection that transcends simple introspection, fostering transformative personal and professional growth. Contributors share their personal journeys, highlighting both the challenges and benefits of critically reflecting on their practice. The editors also incorporate their own insights and research findings, providing a comprehensive guide for educators and practitioners on how to effectively teach and implement critical reflection in social work education and professional settings. The book is essential reading for anyone seeking to enhance their critical thinking and reflective practice in the field of social work, offering invaluable insights into the complexities and nuances of this pivotal aspect of professional development.
