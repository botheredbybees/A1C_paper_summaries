## An Updated Evaluation of the Dichotomous Link Between Creativity and Mental Health

This review explores the relationship between creativity and mental health, focusing on the longstanding debate regarding their correlation. The authors first summarize recent studies and meta-analyses, then present an updated evaluation using a modified dual-pathway model of creativity. This model aims to explain the paradoxical relationship between creativity and mental health by suggesting that creativity can both positively and negatively correlate with mental health. The review highlights the importance of examining this link, especially during times of uncertainty like the ongoing COVID-19 pandemic. The article also critiques existing research methods and calls for more integrated, accurate, and coherent understanding of the topic. The theoretical framework proposed in the paper offers valuable insights for future research and practical applications in the field of creativity and mental health.
