## Web Lecture 2, Week 2

This document is a web lecture for Week 2 of the FXA303 course at UTAS, focusing on the developmental role of creative arts and play in children's health and wellbeing. It highlights that creative play supports healthy brain development and emotional regulation, providing opportunities to process experiences through symbolic representation. The importance of grey and white matter in early neurodevelopment is discussed, emphasizing the critical period from prenatal stages until age two when rapid cell growth and neural connections form. This phase can be disrupted by various stressors, underscoring the need for preventative measures. The lecture also covers Erikson's psychosocial development theory, Piaget’s cognitive development stages, and Vygotsky's sociocultural approach to learning through social interactions. Different types of play are described along with children’s drawing styles as indicators of cognitive and emotional development, referencing studies by Chappell and Steitz (1993) and Cannoni et al. (2023). The content emphasizes the integral role of creative arts and play in shaping a child's future mental wellbeing.
