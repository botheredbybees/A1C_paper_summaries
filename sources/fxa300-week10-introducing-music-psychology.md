## Introducing Music Psychology

This chapter introduces the field of music psychology, exploring its interdisciplinary nature and the questions it seeks to answer. It begins by illustrating the power of music to affect human emotions and behavior, such as the morale-boosting effect of bagpipes on soldiers. The chapter outlines key research questions, including the evolutionary basis of human musicality, the universality of music, and the neurological basis of musical perception and response. The chapter also presents a model of music psychology that integrates various disciplines such as biology, physics, linguistics, and more, to provide a comprehensive understanding of music's role in human experience. This interdisciplinary approach highlights the diverse ways in which music influences human behavior, emotions, and cognitive processes, underscoring the field's relevance to creative arts and health.
