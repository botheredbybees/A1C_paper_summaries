## Limitations of the Evidence Base for Mindfulness

This lesson discusses the limitations of the evidence base for mindfulness interventions, emphasizing that while mindfulness practices can be beneficial, they may not be universally effective or safe for everyone. Key concerns include the lack of clear definitions of mindfulness, the quality of studies, and the potential adverse effects, such as negative thoughts and flashbacks for individuals with trauma histories. The lesson highlights the importance of caution and the need for further research to address these limitations, recommending that mindfulness interventions should be paired with other treatments and used cautiously in cases where research evidence is insufficient.
