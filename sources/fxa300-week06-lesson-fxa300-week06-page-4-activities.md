## FXA300 Music, Mind and Body - Week 6 Activities

This week's activities in FXA300 focus on preparing for Assessment Task 1 (Quiz) and initiating work on Assessment Task 2. The quiz is open from August 10 to August 19, 2026, with details provided in the unit outline. For Assessment Task 2, students are tasked with selecting a case study on music therapy interventions or therapeutic programs for neurological or respiratory conditions, evaluating it against existing literature and presenting findings via PowerPoint slides and video. The discussion forum asks students to find research studies related to music and wellbeing, detailing their design methodologies. Additionally, there is an e-tivity task involving the DASS (Depression, Anxiety, Stress) scales.
