## Metaphorical Humor in Satirical News Shows: A Content Analysis

This paper explores the use of metaphorical humor in satirical news shows, employing the Humoristic Metaphors in Satirical News (HMSN) typology and integrating it with the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH). The study aims to understand how metaphors contribute to the hybridity of satirical news by analyzing the six Knowledge Resources (KRs) identified by GTVH across various American satirical news shows. Findings indicate that some KRs facilitate the fulfillment of metaphorical jokes' communicative functions, while others constrain such expression.
