## Week Three Lecture Transcript: Photography, Health, and Social Media

This week's lecture transcript explores the relationship between photography and health, with a focus on social media impacts and mental well-being. It discusses how ubiquitous mobile phones and their cameras facilitate both beneficial and potentially harmful photographic practices, such as maintaining connections through visual documentation versus fostering anxiety or unrealistic self-representation online (nomophobia). The discussion introduces concepts of therapeutic photography, distinct from clinical phototherapy, highlighting personal and artistic projects that enhance mental health. Practical mindfulness activities are suggested to integrate photography with mindful living, encouraging students to engage in a Mindfulness Photo Walk as part of their assignment. Additionally, the transcript provides insights into how such practices can positively influence well-being and creativity through reflection and sharing on discussion boards.
