## I’ve Got No Skills to Maintain – to Keep That Going”: A Qualitative Study of People with Chronic Aphasia and Their Partners About Factors Contributing to the Maintenance of Aphasia Therapy Gains

This qualitative study explores the perspectives of individuals with chronic aphasia and their partners regarding factors that contribute to maintaining therapeutic gains from aphasia therapy. The study involved eight in-depth semi-structured interviews with four people with chronic aphasia and four partners. Through inductive thematic analysis, five themes were identified: beliefs about change, personal abilities, external support, engagement in real-life communication, and knowledge and service gaps. The findings highlight the complexity of maintaining therapy gains and suggest that consistent real-life communication routines and support from external sources are crucial for long-term benefits. The study underscores the importance of understanding and addressing the multifaceted challenges faced by individuals with chronic aphasia and their partners to enhance the effectiveness of aphasia interventions and service development.
