## Week 6 Web Lecture: Constraints and Creativity

The document discusses the concept of creativity being constrained rather than unstructured. It highlights that while some constraints can inhibit innovation, accepting a few strategic ones can enhance focus and broaden creative solutions. The text introduces the idea of 'scarcity salience,' where limited options can promote unconventional thinking, and presents a model by Stokes (2014) that uses paired constraints to facilitate creativity in expert artists. Additionally, it explores various mental blocks such as stress, trauma, anxiety, and lack of motivation that hinder creative processes. The lecture also includes insights from influential figures like Miles Davis, Hilma af Klint, Rick Rubin, and Haruki Murakami, emphasizing their approaches to overcoming challenges and maintaining creativity throughout their careers. Finally, it provides practical advice on balancing the creative process with regular breaks and continuous learning.
