## Social Studies: The Humanities, Narrative, and the Social Context of the Patient-Professional Relationship

Rebecca Garden's chapter, 'Social Studies: The Humanities, Narrative, and the Social Context of the Patient-Professional Relationship,' argues for the critical role of health humanities in developing a fuller understanding of patients within their social contexts. Garden asserts that literature and creative cultural production can illuminate social factors and power relations that are often obscured in the biomedical approach to healthcare, thus contributing to disparities and discrimination. The chapter highlights the importance of narratives, particularly autobiographical ones, in presenting patients' perspectives and experiences, which can challenge normative views and recalibrate power dynamics in clinical settings. By incorporating a biopsychosocial approach, healthcare providers can better address the social determinants of health and illness, leading to more ethical and effective care.
