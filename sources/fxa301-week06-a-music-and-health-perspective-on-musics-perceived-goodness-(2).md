## A Music and Health Perspective on Music's Perceived ‘Goodness’

In this paper, Jane Edwards explores the perception that music participation and listening are inherently beneficial to health and well-being. From a 'music as health' perspective, Edwards critically examines how music is often constructed as contributing to social gains in various applications. She argues against the commodification of music's goodness, suggesting that its value should not solely be measured by its ability to create predictable positive outcomes or social cohesion. Instead, Edwards advocates for recognizing music as a situated mode of communication within cultural production and emphasizes its role in human experience beyond mere therapeutic benefits.
