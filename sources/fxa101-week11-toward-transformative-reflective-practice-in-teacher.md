## Toward Transformative Reflective Practice in Teacher Education

This chapter, part of the edited volume 'Reflective Theory and Practice in Teacher Education,' explores the concept of reflective practice in education. The authors argue that despite its widespread use, reflective practice is often applied inconsistently and without a clear understanding of its true purpose. They discuss how different forms of reflective practice, such as evaluative, adaptive, collaborative, and transformative, are driven by various agendas and stakeholders. The chapter highlights the confusion caused by a lack of consensus on what constitutes effective reflective practice and what evidence is required to demonstrate its success. It advocates for a more authentic and transformative approach to reflective practice that goes beyond mere compliance with policies, emphasizing the importance of critical reflection and ethical considerations.
