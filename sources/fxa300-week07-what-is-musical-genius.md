## What is musical genius?

Paul Robertson's article explores the multifaceted concept of musical genius, emphasizing the cultural and historical contexts that shape its definition and perception. Robertson argues that while musical genius must have a neurobiological basis, it is deeply influenced by prevailing cultural frameworks. The author discusses the challenge of defining genius, noting that it is not only about high functioning but also about aesthetic qualities that are culturally specific. Historical views of musical excellence, particularly the association between genius and 'madness' or melancholy, are examined, illustrating how these ideas have evolved over time. Robertson also considers the roles of child prodigies, autism, and personality in the development of musical genius, suggesting that certain personality traits may be linked to exceptional musical ability. The article highlights the complex interplay between nature and nurture, and the cultural expectations that influence perceptions of musical genius.
