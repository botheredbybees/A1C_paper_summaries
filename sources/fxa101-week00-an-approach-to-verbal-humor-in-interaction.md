## An Approach to Verbal Humor in Interaction

This paper proposes a method to study ironic humorous utterances in Spanish conversations, applying the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH) by Attardo and considering verbal irony as an inversion of conversational principles from S.C. Levinson's pragmatics framework. The author discusses how humor and irony can coexist without being aggressive or attacking interlocutors, serving instead to strengthen social ties and enhance group cohesion. The study utilizes real conversation corpora (COVJA and CREA) to analyze humorous ironic statements in non-humorous contexts, emphasizing the communicative purpose of such utterances.
