## A Proposed Glossary for Use in Affective Response Literature in Music

Harry E. Price's article, 'A Proposed Glossary for Use in Affective Response Literature in Music,' published in the Journal of Research in Music Education in Autumn 1986, addresses inconsistencies and misunderstandings within terminology used to describe affective responses to music. The paper outlines a methodological approach to compiling a glossary of commonly used terms in affective response literature with agreed-upon definitions. Price explains that this initiative aims to standardize language in the field, thereby improving communication among researchers. By surveying selected books and journals, he identifies frequently used but ambiguously defined terms. The resultant glossary includes entries such as 'aesthetic experience,' 'behavioral preference,' and 'hedonic value,' each providing a clear definition based on contextual usage within the literature. This standardization is crucial for advancing research in music education that focuses on emotional responses and preferences, contributing significantly to the field of creative arts and health by fostering clarity and consistency.
