## Traditional Female World: Preservation and Revival of Crafts in the Dagestanovskiy District of Bashkortostan

The article explores the preservation and revival of traditional female crafts in the Dagestanovskiy district of Bashkortostan, based on ethnographic fieldwork conducted in July 2024. The study involved interviewing thirteen informants from five settlements about their craft practices, focusing on sewing, embroidery, knitting, and weaving. Women’s handcrafts such as rug weaving, felt production, clay modeling, beading, and cloth making were examined for their cultural significance. Notable craftspeople like Nazia Ahmadvalieva and Klara Hamidullina are highlighted for their contributions to preserving these traditions.
