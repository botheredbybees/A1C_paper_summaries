## Constraints and Frugal Innovation: Enabling Sustainable Production Through the New Product Development Process

This paper explores how constraint-based thinking can foster sustainable innovation under resource limitations, particularly in the context of frugal innovation. Through five case studies of small and medium-sized enterprises developing medical devices across diverse regions, it examines how market, infrastructure, and user constraints influence design choices and innovation outcomes during the new product development (NPD) process. The study argues that by treating constraints as structured inputs rather than barriers, organizations can leverage them to create inclusive, affordable, and scalable solutions in underserved markets. This reframing of scarcity as a creative driver is crucial for understanding how constraints catalyze frugal innovation and contribute to sustainable development. By extending the constraint-based thinking framework into an iterative, open innovation process, the research offers practical guidance for managers, entrepreneurs, and policymakers seeking to innovate under resource scarcity.
