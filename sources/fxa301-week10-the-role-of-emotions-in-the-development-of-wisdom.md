## The Role of Emotions in the Development of Wisdom

This chapter explores the relationship between emotions and the development of wisdom, examining how emotional regulation and positive/negative emotions contribute to wise decision-making processes. The authors argue that wisdom is not merely a cognitive process but also involves values and emotions. They discuss the evolution of the concept of wisdom, emphasizing its holistic nature and focus on broad interests and long-range effects. Wisdom is seen as distinct from more modern cognitive domains due to its lack of a systematic symbolic system. This characteristic allows it to be adaptable and inclusive, though it also makes it difficult to define clearly. The chapter also investigates the role of positive emotions in engaging with tasks requiring wisdom, suggesting that emotional commitment is crucial for wisdom's development and transmission.
