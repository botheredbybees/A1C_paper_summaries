## Youth Survey Report 2025

The 2025 Mission Australia Youth Survey provides a comprehensive overview of the challenges and aspirations facing young Australians aged 14 to 19 years old. Key issues identified by participants include cost of living, mental health, climate change, and housing/homelessness. The survey highlights that cost of living has become the most pressing national issue for young people, with over 60% citing it as a top concern. Additionally, housing and homelessness have emerged as critical issues, with concerns doubling since 2022. Mental health remains a significant challenge, with nearly one in five participants experiencing high levels of psychological distress.

Despite these societal pressures, many young people express optimism about their future, sharing aspirations for meaningful employment, stable relationships, financial security, and educational opportunities. The report underscores the importance of social connections and community support for young individuals, while also highlighting ongoing issues of discrimination and inequality affecting Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander youth and gender-diverse youth.

The findings from this survey provide critical insights into the lived experiences of Australian youth and emphasize the need for comprehensive policy interventions to address these challenges. Such measures are essential not only for supporting individual well-being but also for fostering a resilient and inclusive society.
