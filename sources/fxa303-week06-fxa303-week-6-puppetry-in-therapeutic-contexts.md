## FXA303 Week 6: Puppetry in Therapeutic Contexts

This week's lesson in the FXA303 Creative Arts, Play and Mental Wellbeing module focuses on the therapeutic applications of puppetry. The text emphasizes how puppets can facilitate non-verbal communication and serve as a mediated form of expression for individuals struggling to articulate their feelings or experiences directly. It highlights examples such as interactions between aged care residents and Scaredy Cat from Terrapin Puppet Theatre, illustrating how puppets can act as effective icebreakers and encourage engagement through curiosity and play. The lesson also encourages sustainable practices in puppet making by using recycled materials, fostering creativity while promoting environmental awareness. The module suggests refining puppet design to enhance movement and expressive capabilities, which is relevant for the upcoming Assessment Task 2. The week includes a split schedule due to mid-semester break, allowing students time to focus on their puppet-making projects.
