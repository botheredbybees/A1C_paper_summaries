## FXA302 Week 7 Poster Assessment Instructions

This lesson outlines the requirements for Assessment Task 2, a Poster assignment in FXA302, focusing on the arts and health and wellbeing. Students are instructed to read the assessment criteria and rubric, use appropriate templates, and adhere to APA 7 referencing style. The lesson emphasizes the importance of research skills, critical analysis, and reflection. Peer discussions and checking referencing are encouraged, and students are reminded to submit their work as a PDF file by the due date. The lesson also invites students to share their posters in the gallery for peer review.
