## The Role of Haptics in Training and Games for Hearing-Impaired Individuals: A Systematic Review

This systematic review explores the use of haptic and vibrotactile technology in training and game-based activities for individuals with hearing impairments, focusing on rehabilitation methods and their effectiveness. The study includes a comprehensive literature search covering Scopus® and PubMed® databases from 1920 to 2023, identifying 35 relevant studies. Key findings indicate an increase in research activity over the last five years, with most studies evaluating the efficacy of vibrotactile devices for auditory rehabilitation. Challenges include difficulties in participant recruitment, particularly among hearing-impaired individuals, leading to small sample sizes (mean of 8.31 participants). Positive outcomes were reported when vibrotactile devices converted sound into tactile sensations, suggesting potential for haptic technology as a viable alternative or enhancement in hearing rehabilitation.
