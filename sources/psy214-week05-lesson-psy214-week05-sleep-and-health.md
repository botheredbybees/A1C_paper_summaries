## Sleep and Health

This lesson covers the importance of sleep for health across the lifespan. It discusses how much sleep is needed at different ages and highlights that individual needs can vary. The lesson also addresses common sleep problems such as difficulty falling asleep or staying asleep, which affect a significant portion of adults in Australia. Health risks associated with inadequate sleep include impaired physical and cognitive performance, increased risk of accidents, weakened immune system, weight gain, hypertension, and chronic conditions like obesity, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and mental health disorders. The relationship between sleep and stress is also explored, noting that poor sleep can exacerbate the body's stress response. Additionally, factors such as caffeine intake, alcohol consumption, physical activity levels, and prolonged sitting are linked to sleep quality.
