## The Creativity Premium: Exploring the Link between Childhood Creativity and Life Outcomes

This paper emphasizes the importance of creativity as a key skill in modern society, particularly its role in open-ended problem solving and resistance to automation. The authors use longitudinal data from the National Child Development Study (NCDS) to explore how childhood creativity impacts life outcomes such as labor market success and educational attainment. They find that more creative individuals tend to earn higher salaries, hold positions of greater occupational status, and achieve higher levels of education. Additionally, the study highlights that creative people possess a combination of practical skills which helps them succeed in environments where continuous learning is crucial. The research underscores the benefits of interventions aimed at fostering creativity from childhood and designing educational and work settings that encourage creative thinking.
