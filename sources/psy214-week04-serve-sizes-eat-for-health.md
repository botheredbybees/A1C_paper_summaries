## Serve sizes - Eat For Health

The document 'Serve Sizes' from the Australian Government's Eat for Health initiative outlines standard serving sizes across various food groups to promote balanced and healthy eating. For vegetables, a serve ranges from half a cup of cooked green or orange varieties to one cup of salad greens. Fruit servings include one medium-sized piece like an apple or banana, or 125ml of fruit juice occasionally. Grain foods such as bread slices, rice, pasta, or cereals are also detailed with their respective measurements. For protein sources, a serve might be 65g of cooked lean red meat, 80g of chicken, or 3 tablespoons of nuts. Dairy servings cover milk, yoghurt, cheese, and alternatives like soy drinks fortified with calcium. The guide emphasizes the importance of these serving sizes for maintaining a balanced diet and managing energy intake.
