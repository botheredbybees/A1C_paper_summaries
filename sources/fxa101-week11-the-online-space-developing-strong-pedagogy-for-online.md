## The Online Space: Developing Strong Pedagogy for Online Reflective Practice

This chapter by Jones and Ryan delves into the intricacies of designing an optimal online learning environment for pre-service teachers to reflect on their practical teaching experiences critically. The authors describe the pedagogical journey of two teacher educators who initiated various teaching initiatives aimed at fostering critical reflective practice among pre-service teachers during their practicum. They established online forums to mitigate the isolation often felt by pre-service teachers during this period, promoting social and cognitive development through critical reflection. The study highlights the necessity for teacher educators to balance addressing the social and cognitive needs of pre-service teachers effectively. The importance of teaching reflective practice skills to novice teachers is emphasized, as it is during the practicum that they are best positioned to develop critical thinking about their teaching practices.
