## A Glimpse at the Humor of Woody Allen’s Writing

This paper analyzes the humor in Woody Allen's short story 'The Whore of Mensa' from literary and linguistic perspectives. The literary analysis highlights its parodies of intellectualism and Victorian society, with numerous references to prominent figures and literature. Linguistically, it uses a repetitive structure based on false analogies between intellectual conversation and prostitution, challenging the notion that humor must be diverse to maintain interest.
