## A comparison of Irish set dancing and exercises for people with Parkinson’s disease: A phase II feasibility study

This study evaluates the feasibility of conducting a randomized controlled trial (RCT) comparing Irish set dancing and routine physiotherapy exercises for individuals with Parkinson’s disease (PD). The research aimed to assess whether engaging in Irish set dancing could be as effective and more enjoyable than standard exercises, potentially leading to better long-term adherence. The study involved 24 participants who were randomly assigned to either the Irish set dancing group or the physiotherapy exercise group for a period of six months. Both interventions were found to be feasible and safe. Participants in the dancing group showed superior results in terms of freezing of gait, balance, and motor disability compared to those in the physiotherapy group. The study highlights the potential benefits of incorporating enjoyable and engaging activities like dancing into PD rehabilitation programs to improve adherence and therapeutic outcomes. This pilot study sets the stage for a larger multi-center trial to further establish the effectiveness of Irish set dancing in enhancing mobility, balance, and quality of life for individuals with idiopathic Parkinson’s disease.
