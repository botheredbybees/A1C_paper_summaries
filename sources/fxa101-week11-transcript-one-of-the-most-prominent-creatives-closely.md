## Yayoi Kusama's Infinity Mirror Rooms

The transcript explores the life and work of Japanese artist Yayoi Kusama, renowned for her infinity mirror installations. Born in the late 1920s in Japan, Kusama defied societal expectations to pursue her artistic career, moving to New York in the 1950s. Despite challenges, she became a pioneering figure in the art world with her repetitive and polka-dotted motifs. Kusama's work, which includes performance, painting, sculpture, and installation, has been influenced by her experiences and mental health struggles. Her infinity mirror installations, such as Narcissus Garden and Phalli's Field, have captivated audiences globally, with her most recent works continuing to attract widespread attention.
