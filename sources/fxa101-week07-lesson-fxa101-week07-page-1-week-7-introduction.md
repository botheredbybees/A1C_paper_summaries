## Introduction to Week 7

This lesson introduces Week 7 of FXA101 Creativity and Ageing, focusing on sensory inputs and their processing. It highlights the importance of learned associations in optimizing reactions and discusses the role of memory in response. The week covers the selection of color and creative use of texture in a mini-project context. The lesson explores the history of color perception, color theories, and the psychological and emotional impacts of colors and textures. It includes research on color and dominance, as well as the therapeutic use of color and texture in art and design.
