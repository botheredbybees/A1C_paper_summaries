## Mindfulness research: Limitations

The document outlines several limitations inherent in mindfulness research, emphasizing the need for more precise definitions and standardized measurement tools. It discusses the importance of control groups in experimental designs and highlights the lack of long-term follow-ups to assess the sustained impact of mindfulness interventions. Additionally, the document addresses issues related to the dosage and delivery of interventions, self-report data reliability, sample size adequacy, and the consideration of potential adverse effects. These limitations are critical to the understanding and advancement of mindfulness practices within the creative arts and health fields, as they affect the reliability and validity of research findings.
