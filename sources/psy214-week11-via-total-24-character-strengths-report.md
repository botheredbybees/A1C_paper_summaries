## VIA Total 24 Character Strengths Report

The document is a personalized report titled 'VIA Total 24 Character Strengths Report' generated for Peter Shanks on October 15, 2025, by the VIA Institute on Character. The report offers an overview of his character strengths based on the VIA Survey. The introduction highlights the purpose and benefits of the survey, which measures 24 positive qualities or character strengths. The report is organized into two main sections: the first section focuses on the Spotlight, providing a detailed profile of Peter’s character strengths categorized into signature, middle, and lesser strengths. The second section explores Perspectives, offering insights into optimal strengths use, virtues, character strengths expression map, and happiness strengths. The report emphasizes the importance of understanding raw scores and trusting one’s self-perception over survey results. It also encourages reflecting on signature strengths to identify those that are essential, effortless, and energizing for personal growth.
