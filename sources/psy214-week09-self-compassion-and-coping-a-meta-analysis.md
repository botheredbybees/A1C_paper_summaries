## Self-Compassion and Coping: a Meta-Analysis

This meta-analysis explores the relationship between self-compassion and coping mechanisms in stressful situations. Self-compassion is defined as a caring attitude towards oneself, particularly in challenging or painful circumstances. The study includes 136 samples with a total of 38,913 participants and finds a positive correlation between self-compassion and adaptive coping strategies (r = .306) and a negative correlation with maladaptive coping strategies (r = −.500). The research further identifies emotional approach coping and problem-focused coping as positively associated with self-compassion. Notably, age is identified as a significant moderator of these associations. The findings underscore the importance of self-compassion in enhancing psychological and physical well-being by fostering adaptive coping strategies and mitigating maladaptive ones. This study is particularly relevant to creative arts and health as it highlights the role of compassionate self-awareness in managing stress and promoting resilience.
