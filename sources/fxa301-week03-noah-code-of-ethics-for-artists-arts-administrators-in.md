## NOAH Code of Ethics for Artists & Arts Administrators in Healthcare and Community Settings

The National Organization for Arts in Health (NOAH) has published an updated Code of Ethics to guide the practice of arts in health, emphasizing ethical principles such as respect, integrity, and quality. This document is designed to support artists and arts administrators working in healthcare and community settings by providing a framework that ensures their work adheres to clear ethical standards. The code outlines five core ethical principles: Respect for Persons, Do No Harm, Integrity, Quality, and Justice & Equity. Each principle is detailed with guidelines on how it should be applied in practice, such as ensuring informed consent, protecting confidentiality, and promoting cultural sensitivity. Additionally, the document underscores the importance of professionalization through ongoing self-evaluation, collaboration, and the use of evidence-informed practices. This code serves to enhance the credibility and effectiveness of arts in health initiatives by fostering a culture of ethical responsibility.
