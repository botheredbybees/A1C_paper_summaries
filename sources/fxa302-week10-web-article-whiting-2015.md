## The World Today Archive - ABC Radio's Midday Current Affairs Program

The World Today Archive is a section of the ABC Radio website that houses daily episodes of the comprehensive midday current-affairs program 'The World Today'. The archive allows users to browse episodes by date or by topic. It serves as a repository for current events and issues discussed in the program, providing a retrospective view of the news and analysis presented each day on the show. Users can explore the breadth of content by selecting specific months or topics covered over the years, making it a valuable resource for those interested in Australian and international news.
