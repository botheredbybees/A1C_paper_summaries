## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Compassion and Self-Compassion

This lesson explores the benefits of compassion and self-compassion, comparing them to mindfulness practices. General outcomes include increased well-being and reduced psychological distress. The lesson discusses shared pathways with mindfulness, such as reducing negative thinking, fostering emotion regulation, and enhancing well-being. Specific pathways for compassion and self-compassion include reducing shame and perfectionism, building empathy, fostering prosocial behaviors, and increasing social connectedness. Research supports the idea that compassion training can lead to prosocial behaviors, such as offering a seat to someone in need.
