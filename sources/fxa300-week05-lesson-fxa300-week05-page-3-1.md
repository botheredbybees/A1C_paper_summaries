## Musical Taste and Preference

This lesson discusses how musical taste and preference are influenced by social factors such as status, group identity, age, gender, ethnicity, and education. Theories of French sociologist Pierre Bourdieu play a crucial role in understanding the diffusion of taste through society and its relation to cultural capital. Recent research challenges some of Bourdieu’s theories, highlighting the impact of increased social mobility and digital access on musical preferences. The lesson also explores 'omnivorous' tastes that are becoming more common, where individuals appreciate a wide range of music genres. It includes studies by Greasley et al., (2013) exploring musical preferences through qualitative interviews, Coulangeon's work on the link between omnivorous taste and social mobility, and Ginocchio's study on the impact of musical training on classical music preference. Additionally, it examines how age influences musical tastes and preferences over a lifetime.
