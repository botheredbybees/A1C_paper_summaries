## Care Staff and the Creative Arts: Exploring the Context of Involving Care Personnel in Arts Interventions

This review article explores the involvement of care staff in creative arts interventions within residential dementia care facilities, emphasizing their role in enhancing resident well-being and improving care practices. The authors highlight that while the importance of arts-based interventions for people with dementia is recognized, such activities are rarely integrated into daily care routines. Through a systematic literature search covering various databases from 2000 onwards, they identified 14 relevant papers that examine how staff engagement in creative arts programs influences resident outcomes and interaction dynamics. Key findings indicate that training sessions for care personnel can effectively educate them on techniques beneficial to dementia care. The review concludes by suggesting that involving care staff in these activities fosters better interpersonal relationships between residents and staff, leading to improved quality of life for individuals with dementia.
