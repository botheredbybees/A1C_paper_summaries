## “Making for Others”: A Creative Inquiry Into Understanding Older Men’s Motivations for Making

This paper explores the motivations of older men involved in 'Men’s Sheds' for engaging in DIY and making activities over an extended period. Using a creative inquiry method, researchers provided participants with a probe kit consisting of recycled timber and materials to create an artifact for someone else within two weeks. Interviews were conducted post-project completion to understand their design processes and motivations. The study revealed three key themes: (1) creative expressions; (2) memories and social connections; and (3) practical needs. These findings highlight the altruistic ethos of 'making for others' among older men, contributing to their sense of purpose and empowerment.
