## Person-Centred Approach in Early Years Practice

This chapter discusses the application of a Person-Centred Approach (PCA) in early years practice, emphasizing its relevance beyond traditional therapeutic settings. It outlines stages in client development through therapy and highlights key principles such as unconditional positive regard, congruence, and empathy. The text also explores Carl Rogers' influence on PCA, his life story, and the role of educational practitioners in fostering emotional well-being without practicing actual therapy. Specific applications include facilitating play environments that promote self-discovery and resilience in children.
