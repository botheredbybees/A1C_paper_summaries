## Can Photography Help Depression?

The article 'Can Photography Help Depression?' explores the potential of daily photography practices in enhancing mental well-being, focusing particularly on individuals with depression. The piece references a study indicating that taking photos regularly can improve general wellbeing by fostering reminiscence, community interaction, and stress management. Interviews with five artists reveal diverse ways in which photography serves as both an escape and a means to confront and process difficult emotions. For some, like Daniel Smith, it offers motivation and a sense of structure; Ashley Whitt uses it as a therapeutic outlet for trauma; Tsoku Maela sees it as a way to directly engage with his mental health challenges; Marianna Cardenio finds it helps articulate feelings that are hard to express verbally; and Suzie Larke highlights the joy and cathartic process inherent in photography. The article underscores how these practices not only benefit individual artists but also foster connections among individuals experiencing similar struggles.
