## FXA101 Creativity and Ageing: Week 11 Activities

Week 11 of the FXA101 Creativity and Ageing course focuses on the symbolic use of mirrors and reflection in art. The lesson includes a reading by Christopher Jones discussing mirrors and reflections in art, a creative exercise involving the exploration of reflection in visual art and photography, and a discussion forum for students to share their findings and experiences. The week also covers the submission requirements for Creative Project 3, including documentation of creative work and the use of APA 7 citation style. The web lecture for the week discusses reflective practices and models, and the course concludes with references and further reading on the topic of reflection in art.
