## Collaborating: A Role for Music Therapy within Interprofessional Teams and Beyond

The chapter explores the role of music therapy within interprofessional teams, emphasizing collaboration as essential for the advancement of the music therapy profession and the improvement of client care outcomes. It discusses the necessity of interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary approaches to achieve shared goals and objectives, highlighting the importance of clear communication, role understanding, and supportive organizational structures. The text outlines the benefits of effective teamwork in interprofessional settings, including enhanced care coordination and delivery, reduced diagnostic burdens on individual disciplines, and improved patient-centered care. The chapter also addresses the challenges of teamworking, such as conflicting professional jargon and hierarchical relationships, and underscores the need for a pragmatic approach to categorizing teamwork that considers the specific needs and resources of each team. Overall, it argues that collaboration is vital for the future development and recognition of music therapy within healthcare and social care sectors.
