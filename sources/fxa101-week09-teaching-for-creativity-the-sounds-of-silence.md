## Teaching for Creativity: The Sounds of Silence

Robert J. Sternberg's article 'Teaching for Creativity: The Sounds of Silence' explores the paradox of abundant literature on teaching creative thinking despite its limited presence in classrooms. Sternberg identifies three core issues hindering the implementation of creative teaching: standardized testing, teacher education, and entrenchment. He argues that standardized tests, which are a major legislative focus in the U.S., often discourage creative thinking. Additionally, traditional teacher education programs rarely emphasize creative teaching methods. The article highlights the challenge of scaling up innovative teaching practices across diverse educational settings, suggesting that without significant systemic changes, the current educational framework is unlikely to support widespread creative teaching.
