## Research Designs 2: Experimental and Quasi-Experimental Studies

This lesson covers experimental and quasi-experimental research designs commonly used in music psychology. It explains the differences between these two types of studies, emphasizing that experiments require random assignment to groups with control and experimental conditions, while quasi-experiments use pre-existing groups without randomization due to ethical or practical constraints. The lesson also discusses limitations in concluding causation from quasi-experimental designs and highlights measures like mean, median, and mode for analyzing group differences. Examples of designing both types of studies are provided, along with considerations on statistical significance.
