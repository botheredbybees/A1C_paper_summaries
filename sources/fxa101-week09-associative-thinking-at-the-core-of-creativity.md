## Associative Thinking at the Core of Creativity

This review synthesizes cognitive, computational, and neuroscience research to elucidate the role of associative thinking in creativity. It challenges the traditional view of associative processes in memory and highlights recent advances in computational models that quantify semantic distance and memory search strategies. The review distinguishes between free- and goal-directed association and their relevance to creative thought. It illustrates how highly creative individuals navigate semantic space more flexibly, switching between more semantic subcategories and making larger leaps between associations. The research also links associative thinking to brain systems involved in semantic and episodic memory, providing empirical support for the associative theory of creativity. This work underscores the multifaceted nature of creativity and its domain-specific expertise while emphasizing the general mechanism of associative thinking as a driving force in idea generation.
