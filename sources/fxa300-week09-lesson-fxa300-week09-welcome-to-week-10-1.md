## Music and Mental Health, and Developing a Research Proposal

This lesson, led by Dr Brendan Lamb, introduces the use of music as therapy in mental health contexts. It covers the benefits of music for mental health and wellbeing, with a focus on stress and anxiety reduction. The session also outlines the structure of a research proposal, including ethical considerations when conducting research with human participants. Students are expected to understand how music therapy is applied, discuss recent research findings, and develop their own research question and design. The module aims to equip students with the skills to engage with literature and justify their research question, while also addressing ethical risks.
