## Creativity and Innovation under Constraints: A Cross-Disciplinary Integrative Review

This review article examines the impact of constraints on creativity and innovation across various disciplines, including strategic management, entrepreneurship, industrial organization, technology and operations management, organizational behavior, and marketing. The authors propose a taxonomy of constraints—input, process, and output—which are linked to distinct mediating mechanisms affecting creativity and innovation. They argue that while conventional wisdom advocates for the removal of all constraints to foster creativity, the reality is that organizations often face numerous rules, regulations, limited resources, and tight deadlines. These constraints can either hinder or enhance creative processes depending on their nature and the context in which they are applied. The review synthesizes conflicting findings by integrating empirical evidence from different fields into an integrative framework. This framework suggests an inverted U-shaped relationship between constraints and creativity/innovation, indicating that moderate levels of constraints can be beneficial for fostering innovative thinking and problem-solving. The study highlights the importance of understanding how to manage these constraints effectively within organizations to enhance creative output and innovation at individual, team, and organizational levels.
