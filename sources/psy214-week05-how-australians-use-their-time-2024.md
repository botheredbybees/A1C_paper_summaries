## How Australians Use Their Time, 2024

The Australian Bureau of Statistics released the 'How Australians Use Their Time' report for 2024, analyzing trends in how people spend their daily activities. Key findings indicate that more than a third of females and 30% of males often feel rushed or pressed for time. The survey revealed significant disparities between genders in unpaid work, with females spending an average of 4 hours 53 minutes per day compared to males at 3 hours 52 minutes. Parents with children under 15 reported the highest levels of unpaid work, particularly lone parents and those living as couples, who averaged 7 hours 4 minutes and 6 hours 16 minutes respectively on such activities. Additionally, females were more likely than males to supervise a child while engaged in another activity for an average of 2 hours 49 minutes daily. The report also highlighted that people working from home spent more time on unpaid work but less time on paid work compared to those not working from home.
