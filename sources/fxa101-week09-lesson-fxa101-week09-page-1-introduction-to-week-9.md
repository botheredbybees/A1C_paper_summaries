## Introduction to Week 9: Developing Creative Focus and Skills

The lesson introduces Module 3 of FXA101, focusing on enhancing creativity and critical thinking skills through Creative Project 3. Students are encouraged to explore contrasting creative projects to learn new skills and techniques. The week's activities involve research, quiet observation, and sensory attention to generate novel ideas. Key learning objectives include conducting research on creative models, reflecting on sensory experiences, and brainstorming concepts and processes.
