## Hildegard of Bingen: Visions and Validation

Barbara Newman's article examines the visionary experiences and public validation of Hildegard of Bingen, a prominent abbess and prophet in medieval times. The text details how Hildegard received visions from an early age, described as an “umbra viventis lucis” (reflection of living light), which she believed to be direct communication with God. Despite initial reluctance due to societal norms around women's religious authority, Hildegard eventually gained recognition for her prophetic teachings and writings. Newman underscores the importance of Hildegard’s visionary experiences in validating her message and establishing her credibility as a prophet rather than just a mystic. The article highlights Hildegard’s unique position as a female leader who navigated the expectations of medieval society while claiming divine inspiration, providing valuable insights into the role of women in religious leadership during this period.
