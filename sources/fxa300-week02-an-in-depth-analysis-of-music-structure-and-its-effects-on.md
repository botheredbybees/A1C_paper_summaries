## An in-depth analysis of music structure and its effects on human body for music therapy

This study provides an in-depth analysis of music structure and its effects on the human body for use in music therapy, focusing particularly on Indian classical ragas and their influence. The paper explores how different musical compositions can generate varying emotional responses and affect physiological parameters such as cortisol levels, blood pressure, and heart rate. It also discusses the significance of various types of music—such as natural sounds, classical music, and Western music—in alleviating anxiety and enhancing mental well-being. The authors emphasize that Indian ragas are uniquely structured around timing and specific scales, which can induce different emotions depending on their application. This study highlights the potential use of artificial intelligence in recommending personalized music therapy sessions to improve patient outcomes for conditions like diabetes and hypertension.
