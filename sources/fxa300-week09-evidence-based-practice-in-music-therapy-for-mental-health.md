## Evidence-based practice in music therapy for mental health

The chapter delves into the concept of evidence-based practice (EBP) in the context of music therapy for mental health. It emphasizes the need for a systematic approach to identify, disseminate, and apply evidence-based practices (EBPs) to improve mental health care. EBP is defined as an ongoing and collaborative process integrating the best available research evidence, clinician expertise, and service user preferences and values to inform healthcare decisions. The chapter highlights the importance of considering the clinical context, user-centric preferences, and the evolving literature base in EBP. It also discusses the historical development of EBP from evidence-based medicine (EBM) and its relevance in contemporary clinical practice. The chapter concludes with the assertion that music therapists must understand EBP to design effective interventions and provide the highest quality of care.
