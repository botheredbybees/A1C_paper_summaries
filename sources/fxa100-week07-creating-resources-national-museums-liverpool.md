## Creating resources | National Museums Liverpool

The document from National Museums Liverpool outlines the use of memory trees and memory boxes as tools for dementia care, emphasizing their role in starting conversations and capturing personal histories. Memory trees involve placing pictures or words on a printed tree to illustrate a person's identity, passions, and culture. This process, which can be done over time, encourages dialogue and storytelling. Memory boxes, on the other hand, serve as a repository for items that hold personal significance, such as photos, toys, and memorabilia. These items are curated to engage multiple senses, enhancing memory triggers. Both resources are designed to facilitate meaningful interactions and preserve cherished memories, highlighting the importance of involving the person living with dementia in the creation process.
