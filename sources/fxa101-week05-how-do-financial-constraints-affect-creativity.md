## How Do Financial Constraints Affect Creativity?

The paper explores the impact of financial constraints on creativity, examining whether such constraints hinder or enhance creative outcomes in various tasks like product ideation and repair. The research suggests that financial constraints may actually be beneficial for creativity. Products generated under these conditions are often deemed more innovative despite using fewer resources compared to unconstrained scenarios. Financial constraints prompt a more efficient use of available resources and encourage a top-down processing strategy, as opposed to the bottom-up approach seen in resource-abundant environments. This effect is particularly pronounced among individuals with high novelty-seeking tendencies, who might otherwise struggle under unconstrained conditions due to overwhelming options. The findings challenge conventional wisdom that abundant financial resources are essential for fostering creativity, suggesting instead that limited budgets can stimulate innovative thinking and more cost-effective solutions.
