## PSYCHOLOGICAL TREATMENT OF MUSICAL PERFORMANCE ANXIETY: CURRENT STATUS AND FUTURE DIRECTIONS

This article provides a comprehensive review of the research evaluating psychological treatments for musical performance anxiety (MPA), highlighting the prevalence and impact of MPA among musicians. The review discusses the effectiveness of exposure and cognitive therapy methods, noting that while these approaches show promise, there is no clear evidence of one method's superiority over the other or the benefits of combining them. The study critiques past research for relying too heavily on self-report measures and suggests improvements for future studies, such as incorporating treatment manuals, utilizing multiple therapists, and including follow-up data. Clinically, the review recommends that therapists consider the level of dysfunction and technical ability of musicians when treating MPA, and suggests incorporating exposure and cognitive restructuring into treatment plans.
