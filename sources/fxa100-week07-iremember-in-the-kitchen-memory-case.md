## iRemember In the Kitchen Memory Case

The document describes the 'iRemember In the Kitchen' memory case developed by the Tasmanian Museum and Art Gallery (TMAG) in collaboration with the Wicking Dementia Research and Education Centre. This free loan resource consists of 16 kitchen-related objects designed to facilitate communication and storytelling among people living with dementia, their friends, families, partners, and carers. Each object is accompanied by cards that provide additional information and discussion points to enhance user engagement and stimulate conversations about shared experiences. The aim is to encourage enjoyable interactions through the use of tangible items that evoke memories and foster a sense of connection and understanding.
