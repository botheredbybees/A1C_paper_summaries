## Flow as a Component of Well-being and Its Impact on Motivation, Stress, and Self-care

The lesson discusses the concept of flow and its relationship to well-being, motivation, and stress management. Flow is defined as a state of absorption in an activity characterized by a balance between challenge and skill. It is argued to be a component of well-being, particularly through the engagement aspect of the PERMA model, contributing to positive emotions and life satisfaction. The lesson also explores how flow relates to intrinsic motivation and need satisfaction within the framework of self-determination theory. Additionally, it considers the impact of flow on stress reduction and the role of the central executive network and default mode network in flow states. Finally, the lesson touches on the 'work-flow paradox,' where individuals experience more flow during work than during leisure, despite a preference for leisure activities.
