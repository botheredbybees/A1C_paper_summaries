## House of Memories

The House of Memories is a museum-led dementia awareness programme initiated by National Museums Liverpool, aiming to provide training and resources for professionals in health and social care. This initiative also offers tailored workshops for family members, friends, and community volunteers to support individuals living with dementia. The programme includes various components such as memory strolls, where older people can explore the city's past through guided museum visits; memory suitcases containing historical items to facilitate reminiscence sessions; and an innovative mobile museum called 'On The Road' that brings immersive experiences directly into communities. Additionally, House of Memories offers bespoke training for different organizations and educational institutions, including higher education courses on dementia awareness. The program also provides a My House of Memories app designed for people living with dementia and their carers to share memories and connect through digital means.
