## Sleep, Circadian Rhythms and Health

Russell G. Foster's review discusses the critical role of circadian rhythms and sleep in human health, emphasizing that these biological processes are not merely reactions to external cues but are generated internally and synchronized with daily environmental cycles. The paper outlines how disruptions to these natural rhythms due to societal pressures or disease can lead to significant health issues. It also examines the relationship between disrupted sleep patterns and mental illness. Foster underscores the need for individuals and employers to consider the temporal biology of humans when scheduling activities, highlighting that while some negative effects of sleep disruption can be mitigated, others are unavoidable without addressing underlying social practices like shift work.
