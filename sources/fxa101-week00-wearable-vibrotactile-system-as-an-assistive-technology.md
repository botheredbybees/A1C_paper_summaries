## Wearable Vibrotactile System as an Assistive Technology Solution

This paper introduces a wearable vibrotactile system designed to deliver customizable spatiotemporal tactile patterns, offering an assistive technology solution for sensory substitution and augmentation. The system includes nine vibration motors arranged in a 3x3 array on a belt worn on the back of users. It can generate complex feedback with adjustable intensity levels and is intended to aid individuals with visual or auditory impairments by converting various inputs into tactile sensations. Experiments show that participants could achieve high recognition rates for alphanumeric characters when provided with personalized vibration patterns, reducing cognitive load and enhancing memorability.
