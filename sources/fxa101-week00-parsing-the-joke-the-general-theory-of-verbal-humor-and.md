## Parsing the joke: The General Theory of Verbal Humor and appropriate incongruity

This article scrutinizes the Semantic Script Theory of Humor (SSTH) and its successor, the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH), from the perspective of appropriate incongruity. It explores how these theories have been used to analyze jokes based on script opposites and triggers but raises questions about their adequacy in joke analysis. The paper also introduces six Knowledge Resources informing a joke: Language (LA), Narrative Strategy (NS), Target (TA), Situation (SI), Logical Mechanism (LM), and Script Opposition (SO). These resources help categorize variations of the same joke, illustrating how changes in one resource affect another. However, the author expresses concerns about GTVH’s hierarchical structure and its ability to capture all essential components of jokes.
