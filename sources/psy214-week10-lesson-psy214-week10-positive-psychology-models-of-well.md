## How Positive Psychology Activities Work: Models and Mechanisms

This lesson explores the models and mechanisms of positive psychology activities, specifically focusing on positive emotions, the Positive Activity Model, and the Hedonic Adaptation Prevention Model. It discusses how these activities contribute to well-being through positive emotions, thoughts, behaviors, and need satisfaction. The lesson also examines the social connections built through gratitude and acts of kindness, and the role of gratitude in offsetting negativity bias and hedonic adaptation. Additionally, it covers the beneficial effects of acts of kindness on well-being through positive emotions, need satisfaction, and psychological needs.
