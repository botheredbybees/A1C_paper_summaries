## A musician-centered approach to management of performance-related upper musculoskeletal injuries

This paper presents an evidence-based framework for the assessment, prevention, and treatment of performance-related musculoskeletal disorders (PRMD) in musicians. The study highlights the unique demands and risks faced by different instrumental groups, such as pianists, high and low strings players, percussionists, woodwind, and brass musicians. It emphasizes the importance of a musician-centered approach that includes tailored assessment and treatment strategies based on the specific requirements of each instrument. The authors advocate for a comprehensive evaluation of the musician’s playing habits, posture, and performance history to identify and address the source of pain and injury. The proposed framework aims to reduce the incidence and severity of PRMD by providing clinicians with a practical and instrument-specific guide. This research underscores the need for healthcare providers to be knowledgeable about the occupational demands of musicians to effectively manage and prevent PRMD, thereby supporting the health and career longevity of performing artists in the creative arts.
