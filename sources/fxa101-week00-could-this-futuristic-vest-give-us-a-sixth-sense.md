## Could This Futuristic Vest Give Us a Sixth Sense?

This article discusses the Versatile Extra-Sensory Transducer (VEST) developed by neuroscientist David Eagleman. The VEST is a wearable technology featuring 32 vibrating motors distributed around the torso that can translate various types of data into vibrational patterns, potentially enabling users to develop a new 'sense'. It aims to assist individuals with hearing impairments and has been tested successfully on deaf volunteers who could interpret audio from vibrations within weeks. The article also mentions potential commercialization in the near future and its appearance on HBO's show Westworld.
