## Now I Live on a Painful Planet: Frida Kahlo Revisited

Marta Zarzycka’s article ‘Now I Live on a Painful Planet’ revisits Frida Kahlo’s art in the context of contemporary society’s fascination with her legacy. Zarzycka critiques the commodification of Kahlo’s image and examines how her portrayal of pain has been transformed into a charming and glamorous icon. The article highlights the paradox of how Kahlo’s pain, central to her work, has been repackaged as a source of allure. Zarzycka argues that existing scholarship often neglects the visual language of pain in Kahlo’s paintings, focusing instead on her biography and medical history. She emphasizes the importance of revisiting Kahlo’s imagery of pain to reassess her art and its relevance to feminist art criticism. The article also discusses the gendered perception of pain and its impact on medical treatment, suggesting that Kahlo’s exploration of pain challenges traditional patriarchal stereotypes and represents an important contribution to feminist discourse.
