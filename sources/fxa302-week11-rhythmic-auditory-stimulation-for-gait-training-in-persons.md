## Rhythmic auditory stimulation for gait training in persons with unilateral transtibial amputation: A randomized-controlled trial

This study explores the efficacy of rhythmic auditory stimulation (RAS) in gait training for patients with unilateral transtibial amputations. The research compares RAS intervention with conventional therapy, focusing on the duration of prosthetic training and gait parameters. Participants included 29 amputees divided into RAS and control groups. RAS involved using rhythmic beats during prosthetic training sessions. Results showed that while RAS did not significantly reduce the time needed for training, it did improve some gait parameters, such as the timed up and go (TUG) test. The study concludes that RAS may offer benefits in decreasing training time and enhancing walking endurance for unilateral transtibial amputees. However, further research is required to confirm these preliminary findings.
