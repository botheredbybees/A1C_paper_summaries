## Week 3 Lecture - FXA101 Creativity for Life

The lecture delves into the brain's structure and function, emphasizing the roles of different lobes and the default mode network. It discusses brain development, particularly in adolescence, highlighting the importance of creativity training. Stress and its impact on brain development are also covered, with a focus on the distinction between moderate stress and toxic stress. The cognitive reserve theory is introduced, explaining how it helps the brain adapt to aging or damage. The lecture explores the 'Mozart Effect,' examining its claims and empirical evidence regarding the impact of music on cognitive development. It concludes with insights into arts-based education and its benefits for cognitive and emotional development. The lecture underscores the relevance of creative arts in fostering healthy brain development and enhancing cognitive resilience.
