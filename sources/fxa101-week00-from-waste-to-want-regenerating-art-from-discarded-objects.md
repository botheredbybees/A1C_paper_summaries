## From 'Waste to Want': Regenerating Art from Discarded Objects

This paper discusses the creative potential of transforming discarded objects into new forms of art and utility in a context of waste management and environmental sustainability. It highlights that discarded items can be repurposed through processes such as recycling, reusing, or converting them into valuable products, thereby reducing pollution. The author emphasizes the importance of creativity and resourcefulness in giving old materials second life, citing examples from Leonardo da Vinci's engineering inventions to contemporary global campaigns like 'waste to energy' and 'zero waste'. Additionally, it argues for integrating Western technological advancements with traditional African art practices to enhance societal appreciation and economic value.
