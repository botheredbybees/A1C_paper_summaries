## The Owner's Manual to the Voice: A Guide for Singers and Other Professional Voice Users

The introduction of 'The Owner's Manual to the Voice' by Rachael Gates and colleagues discusses the complexities singers face when their voice malfunctions. It highlights the challenges singers encounter when seeking help for voice issues, as general practitioners or pharmacists may not have the specialized knowledge needed to diagnose voice-related problems accurately. The manual emphasizes the importance of singers understanding their voice's physiological functions and recognizing subtle changes that may affect performance. It underscores the necessity for singers to communicate effectively with healthcare providers about their vocal health and provides an accessible guide to various medical conditions that can impact professional voice users. This resource aims to empower singers to take an informed approach to maintaining their vocal health, thereby ensuring they can continue to perform at their best.
