## Population based epidemiology of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis using capture-recapture methodology

The study investigates the epidemiology of amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) in the Netherlands from 2006 to 2009 using capture-recapture methodology to account for unobserved cases. The research found an incidence rate of 2.77 per 100,000 person-years and a prevalence of 10.32 per 100,000 individuals. The study highlights a higher median age at onset for incident cases compared to prevalent cases and a peak incidence in the 70-74 age group, followed by a rapid decline in older age groups. The male-to-female ratio in premenopausal age groups was not significantly higher than in postmenopausal age groups, suggesting a limited protective role of female sex hormones in ALS. The study underscores the importance of including incident cases in studies examining susceptibility or disease-modifying factors in ALS.
