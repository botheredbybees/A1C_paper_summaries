## Perspectives in Performing Arts Medicine Practice: A Multidisciplinary Approach

The book 'Perspectives in Performing Arts Medicine Practice: A Multidisciplinary Approach' edited by Sang-Hie Lee, Merry Lynn Morris, and Santo V. Nicosia provides a comprehensive overview of the intersection between performing arts and medicine. It highlights the historical development of the field, emphasizing the collaborative efforts of artists, educators, and healthcare professionals. The first part of the book includes chapters that offer unique perspectives on the field, such as the lived history of performing arts medicine by Dr. Richard Lederman and an exploration of empathy and presence in medical and theatrical practices. The second part delves into research chapters that contribute original insights, ranging from innovative exercise programs for musicians to the application of motor control theory to piano playing. The third part focuses on diverse contemporary practices and cultural dance, reflecting the growing importance of interdisciplinary approaches in addressing the health needs of performing artists.
