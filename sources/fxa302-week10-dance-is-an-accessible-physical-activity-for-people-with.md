## Dance Is an Accessible Physical Activity for People with Parkinson’s Disease

This review article evaluates the effectiveness of dance therapy for people living with Parkinson's Disease (PD) through various modes of delivery, including face-to-face, digital, and virtual formats. The study synthesizes findings from 38 eligible research studies, concluding that dance therapy positively impacts balance, gait, quality of life, and disability among PD patients. The review highlights the feasibility and accessibility of digital modes of dance therapy, suggesting they can be effective alternatives to face-to-face sessions. The research emphasizes the importance of offering a range of therapeutic exercises to maintain long-term exercise adherence and compliance, thereby enhancing mobility and overall quality of life for individuals with PD. This study underscores the potential of dance as a creative and engaging form of exercise that can mitigate movement disorders and improve the well-being of individuals with chronic neurological conditions.
