## Music Therapy with Adults Diagnosed with Cancer and Their Families

This chapter explores the application of music therapy in supporting adults diagnosed with cancer and their families. It addresses the multifaceted challenges associated with cancer, including its physical, emotional, and psychological impacts. The authors discuss the various types of cancer and their treatments, highlighting the adverse effects of chemotherapy, radiotherapy, and surgery on patients and their families. They emphasize the importance of psychosocial support and outline how music therapy can mitigate these challenges by providing emotional and psychological relief. The chapter presents evidence-based practices that demonstrate the efficacy of music therapy in enhancing the quality of life for cancer patients and their loved ones, making a compelling case for its integration into cancer care.
