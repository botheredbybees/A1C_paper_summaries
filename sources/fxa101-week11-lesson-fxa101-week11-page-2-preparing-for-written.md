## Preparing for your Written Reflection

The lesson discusses the importance of critical reflection in enhancing creative outcomes, drawing on historical practices and modern theoretical models. Critical reflection is described as a cyclical process of experimenting, making, and reflecting, which has been formalized into models like Gibbs' Six-step Model, Johns' Model, and Borton's 'What? So What? Now What?' model. These models guide practitioners in evaluating their work objectively, fostering lifelong learning and personal growth. The lesson also highlights the application of structured reflection in various fields and its adaptability to creative practice.
