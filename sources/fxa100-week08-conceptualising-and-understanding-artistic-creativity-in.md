## Conceptualising and Understanding Artistic Creativity in the Dementias: Interdisciplinary Approaches to Research and Practise

This paper explores the concept of artistic creativity in individuals with dementias, highlighting the need for a reevaluation of traditional notions of creativity that typically focus on individual genius and tangible creative outputs. The authors argue that such definitions do not adequately capture the range of creative activities observed among people living with dementia, emphasizing collective rather than solely individual creativity. They critique existing research frameworks for their limited applicability to individuals with cognitive impairments and advocate for interdisciplinary approaches to better understand and foster artistic creativity in this population. The article discusses various forms of artistic expression such as dance, visual art, music, and poetry within the context of dementia care, suggesting that creativity can play a vital role in enhancing well-being and quality of life for those affected by dementia.
