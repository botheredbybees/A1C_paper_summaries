## Intergenerational Continuity of Taste: Parental and Adolescent Music Preferences

This article explores the intergenerational continuity of music preferences from parents to their adolescent children through a multi-actor design, linking parental and adolescent educational levels as covariates. The study identifies musical genres such as Pop, Rock, Highbrow, and Dance for both generations, revealing that parents’ tastes significantly influence their children's preferences. Fathers' and mothers' preference for Pop music correlates with adolescents preferring Pop or Dance, while a father’s taste for Rock influences his daughter but not his son. Parents’ passion for Highbrow music predicts similar interests in their offspring. The research highlights the stability of musical preferences as a characteristic trait and underscores how cultural tastes can reflect social background differences.
