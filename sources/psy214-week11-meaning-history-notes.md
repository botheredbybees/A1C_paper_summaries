## Meaning: History Notes

The document 'Meaning: History Notes' explores the concept of meaning in life, drawing heavily on Viktor Frankl's theories. Frankl distinguishes between the 'will to pleasure' and 'will to power' as proposed by Freud and Adler, respectively, and introduces the 'will to meaning.' He argues that individuals should recognize the responsibility of defining their own life's meaning rather than seeking an external definition. The notes also touch on existentialism, particularly the idea that existence precedes essence and the concept of an 'existential vacuum' characterized by a sense of emptiness and meaninglessness. To counter this vacuum, the document outlines three sources of meaning: doing or creating something, experiencing something or someone, and adopting an attitudinal stance towards life. Logotherapy, a form of psychotherapy developed by Frankl, is introduced as a method to address existential issues, employing techniques such as dereflection, paradoxical intention, and Socratic dialogue.
