## Personality and Uses of Music as Predictors of Preferences for Music Consensually Classified as Happy, Sad, Complex, and Social

This study replicates and extends earlier research on the relationship between personality traits, specifically the Big Five traits and trait emotional intelligence (EI), and everyday uses of music such as background use, cognitive/intellectual use, and emotional regulation. It investigates how these factors predict preferences for music categorized as happy, sad, complex, or social. The findings indicate that openness to experience predicts preference for complex music, while extraversion is associated with liking happy music. Background use of music correlates positively with preference for both social and happy music, whereas emotional use of music relates to a higher preference for sad music. Additionally, the study notes gender differences in preferences, with males showing a greater inclination towards sad music and cognitive uses compared to females.
