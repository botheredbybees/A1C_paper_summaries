## Psychology 2e

Psychology 2e is a comprehensive textbook produced by OpenStax, a Rice University initiative dedicated to providing free, peer-reviewed, and openly licensed educational resources. The book is structured into 14 chapters, each covering a different aspect of psychology, including biopsychology, sensation and perception, learning, memory, lifespan development, emotion and motivation, personality, social psychology, and stress, lifestyle, and health. The authors, Rose M. Spielman, William J. Jenkins, and Marilyn D. Lovett, emphasize the importance of understanding psychological principles through research, theoretical perspectives, and practical applications. Each chapter includes key terms, summaries, review questions, critical thinking questions, and personal application questions to enhance learning and comprehension. The text is licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution Non-Commercial ShareAlike 4.0 International License, allowing users to freely access and adapt the material for noncommercial purposes. The book is a valuable resource for students and educators seeking to deepen their understanding of psychological concepts and methodologies.
