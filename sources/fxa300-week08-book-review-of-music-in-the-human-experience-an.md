## Book Review of Music in the Human Experience: An Introduction to Music Psychology

This book review evaluates 'Music in the Human Experience: An Introduction to Music Psychology' by Donald A. Hodges and David C. Sebald, published by Routledge in 2011. The review highlights the text's focus on an interdisciplinary approach to music psychology, specifically tailored for university music students. The book covers a wide range of topics, including the history of music psychology, philosophical issues, the evolution of musical capabilities, and acoustical foundations. The authors provide extensive multimedia tutorials on a CD-ROM and include discussion questions and examples to engage students in practical applications. The reviewer commends the book for its accessibility, well-organized structure, and thorough coverage of the field.
