## The Diving Bell and the Butterfly (Le Scaphandre et le Papillon)

Greta Rait reviews the film 'The Diving Bell and the Butterfly,' which tells the story of Jean-Dominique Bauby, a French editor who suffered a stroke and fell into a 'locked-in' syndrome. The film explores Bauby's experiences in a neurological rehabilitation facility, focusing on the challenges of communication and the emotional toll of his condition. It highlights the contrast between clinical and compassionate care, the relationship between Bauby and his family, and the loss of physical intimacy. Despite its somber subject matter, the film is noted for its humor and portrayal of Bauby's resilience and humanity.
