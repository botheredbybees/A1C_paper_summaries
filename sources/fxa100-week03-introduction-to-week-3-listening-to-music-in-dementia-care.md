## Introduction to Week 3: Listening to Music in Dementia Care

This document introduces the topic of listening to music in dementia care for Week 3. It discusses how music can positively affect individuals with dementia by improving their quality of life and leveraging aural memory for reminiscence. The text mentions two renowned programs, The Music and Memory project and the Power of Music and Dementia Study, which explore the efficacy of music-based interventions grounded in neuroscientific research. Additionally, it highlights cutting-edge research from the University of Melbourne on using wearable devices to personalize musical playlists based on biomarkers for agitation. The document also emphasizes the cognitive benefits of rhythmic entrainment and synchronization provided by percussion instruments, such as enhancing emotional states and dopamine release. Finally, it outlines an activity based on Levitin's Chapter 8 from 'This Is Your Brain on Music,' where students prepare a culturally and generationally appropriate playlist for people living with dementia.
