## Phototherapy and Therapeutic Photography in a Digital Age

This book explores the evolution of phototherapy and therapeutic photography in the digital era. Edited by Del Loewenthal, it is divided into three main parts: an introduction to theories and approaches; practical applications in various therapeutic contexts such as re-enactment therapy, community work, self-portraiture, and family therapy; and a conclusion on future research directions and theoretical developments. The book synthesizes contributions from multiple experts across different disciplines, including psychology, psychotherapy, and art therapy. It underscores the potential of photographs to facilitate healing, personal growth, and therapeutic dialogue in various clinical settings.
