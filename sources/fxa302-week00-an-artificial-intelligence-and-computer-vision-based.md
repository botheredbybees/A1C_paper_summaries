## An Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision Based EyeWriter

This paper presents the development of an EyeWriter system utilizing artificial intelligence, computer vision, and Python frameworks like dlib and OpenCV, aiming to assist individuals with communication disabilities due to paralysis or ALS. The system uses a traditional webcam to track eye movements and blinks, allowing users to control a virtual keyboard. The proposed model is cost-effective and can be used without specialized hardware, making it accessible to a wider population. It includes two phases: detection of eye movement and blink, and virtual keyboard interaction. The system's implementation results showed an accuracy of error pixels at 21 points or 0.76 in degrees of visual angle, demonstrating its potential for widespread application.
