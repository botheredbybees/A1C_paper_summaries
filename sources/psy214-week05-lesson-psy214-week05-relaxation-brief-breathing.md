## Relaxation: Brief Breathing Approaches

This lesson explores brief breathing techniques for stress management, including box breathing and cyclic sighing. Box breathing involves equal inhalation, exhalation, and breath holding phases tailored to individual CO2 tolerance. Cyclic sighing consists of a double inhalation followed by a prolonged exhalation. Both methods are shown to reduce respiration rates and improve emotional well-being over one month when practiced daily for five minutes.
