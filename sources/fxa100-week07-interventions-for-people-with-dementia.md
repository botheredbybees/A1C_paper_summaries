## Interventions for People with Dementia

This document provides an overview of both pharmacological and non-pharmacological interventions used in dementia care. Pharmacological interventions include drugs such as Acetylcholine Inhibitors (e.g., donepezil, rivastigmine) and Memantine, which aim to alleviate symptoms but have limited disease-modifying effects. Non-pharmacological interventions highlighted include Cognitive Stimulation Therapy (CST), Reminiscence Therapy, Validation Therapy, Reality Orientation, Physical Exercise, Multisensory Stimulation in Snoezelen rooms, and Aromatherapy. Each intervention is described with its benefits and potential impact on quality of life for individuals with dementia. The document underscores the importance of personalized care plans tailored to individual needs, emphasizing respect, involvement, and choice, which are crucial for effective non-pharmacological interventions.
