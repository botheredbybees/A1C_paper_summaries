## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Practical Advice

This lesson provides a comprehensive guide for students on how to access, complete, and submit their PebblePad workbooks for the PSY114 and PSY214 courses. It outlines key deadlines, submission processes, and formatting requirements for workbook entries, emphasizing that each assessed part consists of one 400-word entry focusing on self-care activities. The guide includes video walkthroughs detailing how to navigate the PebblePad platform, submit assignments manually, confirm submissions, view feedback and grades, and troubleshoot common issues.
