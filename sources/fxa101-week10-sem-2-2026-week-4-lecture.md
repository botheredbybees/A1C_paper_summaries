## Sem 2 2026 – Week 4 Lecture

The lecture discusses the concept of cognitive reserve, which refers to the brain's ability to adapt and compensate for age-related changes and damage. Dr. Brendan Lamb highlights that engaging in cognitive activities can enhance neuroplasticity and neurogenesis, as evidenced by research such as Sajeev et al. (2016) and Bugos (2019). The lecture also explores the role of education and cognitive resilience in mitigating cognitive decline. Brain training apps and creative arts activities are discussed as potential tools for enhancing cognitive functions. Additionally, the lecture touches on the developmental patterns of creativity across the lifespan, noting that creativity can peak at various ages depending on the individual's style and approach. The benefits of engaging in creative arts for older adults, including improvements in mental well-being and cognitive health, are also examined. Finally, the lecture concludes with an overview of Australian organizations dedicated to creative arts therapies and outreach.
