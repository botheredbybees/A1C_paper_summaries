## The Flourishing Writer

Megan Hayes explores the concept of a 'flourishing writer' through positive psychology and creative writing research, challenging traditional views that see writing primarily as therapeutic or cathartic. Hayes draws on empirical studies to argue that writing can contribute to psychological well-being beyond healing trauma. She discusses theories such as Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi's concept of 'flow', the notion of self-affirmation, and resource activation in expressive writing, suggesting these processes enrich a writer’s life and enhance their sense of self.
