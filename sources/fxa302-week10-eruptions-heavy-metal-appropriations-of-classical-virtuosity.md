## Eruptions: Heavy Metal Appropriations of Classical Virtuosity

Robert Walser's article explores how heavy metal guitarists appropriate elements from classical music, focusing on virtuosity and theoretical knowledge. Walser discusses the influence of classical music on heavy metal, highlighting the innovative creativity of guitarists who fuse classical techniques with blues sensibilities. He argues that this fusion challenges the cultural value judgments that traditionally elevate classical music over popular forms. Walser examines how classical music's prestige and semiotic significance are utilized by heavy metal musicians to enhance their virtuosity and theoretical depth. The article also critiques the institutionalization of classical music, suggesting that its perceived prestige is a social construct reflecting historical power dynamics. Walser’s work is relevant to the intersection of creative arts and health, as it explores how artistic expression challenges and redefines cultural hierarchies, impacting social perceptions of value and identity.
