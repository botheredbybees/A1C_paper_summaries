## Becky's Journey: From Eye Gaze Technology to Digital Art and Academia

This transcript features Becky, an artist, gamer, and student at the University of Dundee, discussing her journey from using eye gaze technology for digital art to pursuing a computing degree. Becky highlights her independence in creating artwork and the pivotal role of technology in enabling her creativity. She emphasizes the importance of representation and mentorship in academia, mentioning her admiration for Professor Annie Louie who also has cerebral palsy. The transcript also touches on the inclusive nature of the University of Dundee, its architecture, and its recognition by UNESCO as a city of design. Becky encourages younger self and others facing skepticism to persist and make significant contributions.
