## Activities for Week 9: Perspectives of the Arts on Health and Wellbeing

Week 9 of the course explores the therapeutic uses of arts therapies and music for chronic pain management. Students are required to engage with readings on arts therapy and chronic pain, as well as an article on how music can help relieve chronic pain. A discussion is to be initiated involving an image search for _Memento Mori_ or _Vanitas_ paintings, analyzing their symbols and their relevance today. Additionally, students are expected to participate in web lectures, drop-in sessions, and contribute to a virtual poster gallery.
