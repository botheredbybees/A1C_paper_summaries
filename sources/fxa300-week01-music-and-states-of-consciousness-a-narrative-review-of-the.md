## Music and states of consciousness: A narrative review of the broader significance of music to understanding absorption, mind wandering and creative thought

This narrative review explores the significance of music in understanding various states of consciousness, including absorption, mind wandering, and creative thought. The authors argue that due to its unique capacity to alter mental and physical states temporarily, music provides a rich field for investigating consciousness. They highlight key contributions from empirical research in music psychology and neuroscience to our understanding of both ordinary and non-ordinary states of consciousness, emphasizing the broader theoretical implications of these studies. The review synthesizes contemporary perspectives on altered states of consciousness and highlights music-related studies that have provided important insights into phenomena such as absorption, flow, and creative thought. It also discusses how musicking—any musical act from performing to listening—provides unique opportunities for exploring conscious experience. Overall, the authors advocate for integrating music into broader consciousness research and suggest avenues for future exploration in this interdisciplinary field.
