## Week 4 Activities - FXA300 Music, Mind and Body

The week's activities for the course 'Music, Mind and Body' are outlined with a focus on engaging with music-related brain processes. Recommended reading includes Tillmann et al.'s (2014) paper on musical expectations from WIREs Cogn Sci. The discussion prompt asks students to reflect on how they engage with music and which parts of the brain might be involved, considering potential benefits for brain function. A reminder is given about Quiz 1's upcoming availability and structure, emphasizing timely completion after reviewing Weeks 1-5 materials. Additional references include various studies on auditory imagery, tonal structures, and working memory. The next week will explore musical taste in relation to personal and environmental factors.
