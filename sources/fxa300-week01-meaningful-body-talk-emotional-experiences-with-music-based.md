## Meaningful body talk: Emotional experiences with music-based group interactions

The article 'Meaningful body talk: Emotional experiences with music-based group interactions' investigates the role of the body in emotional experiences during music-based group interactions (MGI) at festivals. Utilizing Damasio's neurocognitive-evolutionary theory, the study employs a go-along method to explore how individuals experience emotions through their interaction with others and music. Three patterns of MGI are identified: dissociative, reflective, and immersive, based on the degree of alignment between social and bodily rhythms. The research highlights that MGI allows participants to achieve a sense of unity between body and mind, fostering emotional well-being and group cohesion. This study underscores the significance of embodiment in understanding emotional experiences within music tourism and offers implications for event management practices aimed at enhancing visitors' well-being.
