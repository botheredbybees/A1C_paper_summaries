## How to Create a Good Poster

This lesson provides guidance on creating effective academic posters based on advice from New York University (NYU) Library. Key points include readability at a distance of about 10 feet with clear titles and concise text. The suggested word count ranges between 300 to 800 words, using bullet points, numbering, and headings for clarity. Visual elements such as graphics, colors, and fonts should be used effectively while maintaining a consistent layout. Essential components like references, author details, and institutional affiliation must also be included. Templates are available from various sources including Office 360, PosterPresentations.com, and Genigraphics.
