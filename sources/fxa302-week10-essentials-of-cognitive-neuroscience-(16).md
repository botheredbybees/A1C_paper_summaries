## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The document discusses the cognitive neuroscience of neurodegenerative diseases, specifically focusing on primary progressive aphasia (PPA) and semantic dementia. The work examines the progression and symptoms of these conditions, which are characterized by language impairments and progressive loss of semantic memory. Key findings include the distinction between fluent PPA and semantic dementia, with evidence suggesting that both conditions involve deficits in semantic memory. The authors present case studies and experimental tasks, such as the Levels of Specificity and Typicality (LOST) word-matching task and object-color decision task, to illustrate the nuanced impact of these diseases on cognitive function. The relevance to creative arts and health is seen in understanding the cognitive underpinnings of language and memory, which can inform therapeutic approaches in art therapy and other creative interventions.
