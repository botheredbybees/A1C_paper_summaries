## Why Is There No Cure for Tinnitus?

The article examines the lack of effective treatment options for tinnitus, a common auditory symptom without a definitive cure. Despite its prevalence and impact on patients' quality of life, few interventions aim to address the underlying cause of the condition rather than mitigating its effects. Psychological treatments like cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) have shown effectiveness in reducing associated distress but not in altering the perception of tinnitus itself. The authors highlight significant dissatisfaction among patients with existing treatment modalities and a marked gap between patient expectations and healthcare providers' approaches. The financial burden on both individuals and health systems is substantial, indicating a clear need for research investment to identify biomarkers and objective measures for tinnitus, improve clinical trial design, and develop pharmacological treatments. The British Tinnitus Association has established the Map of Tinnitus, an evidence-based repository aiming to clarify the research landscape and guide future studies in this under-researched field.
