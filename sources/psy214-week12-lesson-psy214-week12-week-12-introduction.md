## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Positive Psychology 3

The lesson focuses on the concepts of 'flow' and 'creativity' within the context of positive psychology. It introduces the notion of 'flow,' a state where individuals are fully immersed in an activity, and its connection to creativity as initially described in the context of artistic endeavors. The lesson encourages students to reflect on their own experiences with 'flow' and their engagement in creative activities. It also covers the psychological underpinnings of flow and creativity, including the concept of 'dark flow' where flow occurs in negative situations. The content underscores the importance of self-care through activities that induce flow and creativity, and provides guidance on the required readings for the course.
