## Week 3 Introduction - Policy around Arts, Health, and Wellbeing

This lesson introduces week three of FXA301, focusing on policy development related to arts, health, and wellbeing from an international perspective. It discusses frameworks established in the United States, the United Kingdom, and Australia, detailing how these policies were informed by evidence and are translated into community initiatives. The course materials explore the implications of such policies for various community groups and the intersection with other government programs. The lesson also encourages students to delve deeper into these topics through their own research and practice.
