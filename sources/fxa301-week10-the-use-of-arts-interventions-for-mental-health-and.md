## The use of arts interventions for mental health and wellbeing in health settings

This literature review highlights the growing evidence supporting arts interventions as a beneficial tool for mental health and wellbeing in healthcare settings, particularly in Nordic countries where there is an increasing need for non-medical interventions due to rising mental health issues. The authors present findings from 20 empirical studies that demonstrate positive psychological effects of various arts activities on patients with different diagnoses, including reduced anxiety, improved quality of life, and increased social interaction. These benefits extend beyond clinical settings to participatory arts programs in communities and healthcare facilities. The article emphasizes the importance of establishing more widespread access to such interventions as non-medical tools for promoting public health and mental wellbeing.
