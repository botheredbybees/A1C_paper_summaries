## Artistic Skills Recovery and Compensation in Visual Artists after Stroke

This article explores the recovery and compensation of artistic skills in visual artists after stroke. The authors review the neurobiological basis of artistic creation and the role of various brain regions in this process. They identify ten right cerebral stroke cases and five left cerebral stroke cases of famous artists who continued to create art after their strokes. The study highlights the rapid onset of recovery and compensation within days of a stroke, with some artists even changing their artistic style as a result of the stroke. The article emphasizes the clinical importance of understanding these processes in medical rehabilitation and neuroesthetics. Despite the observed phenomena, the molecular mechanisms underlying artistic creation remain poorly understood, suggesting a need for further standardized clinical and experimental studies.
