## FXA302 Perspectives of the Arts on Health and Wellbeing - Week 6 Activities

This week's module in FXA302 focuses on how visual arts enhance health and well-being. The primary reading material is Chapter 6 from 'Health Humanities' by Crawford et al., discussing the visual arts’ transformative effects. Additional resources include a Gresham College lecture about blindness iconography by Professor William Ayliffe, which provides historical perspectives on artistic depictions of blindness. Students are also required to complete Quiz 1 covering weeks 1-4 by August 12th, engage in a creative discussion activity inspired by the Getty Museum Challenge, and start working on Assessment Task 2, a poster project involving their chosen creative work. Various scholarly articles and resources related to artists with visual impairments or blindness are provided for further research.
