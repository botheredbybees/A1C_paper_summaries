## Music Therapy for People Who Have Alzheimer’s Disease

This chapter by Alicia Ann Clair explores the role of music therapy in caring for individuals with Alzheimer’s disease. Clair begins by sharing her personal experience with dementia care, which originated from her early childhood interactions with her grandfather who had dementia. This personal narrative sets the stage for a review of clinical research studies Clair conducted on music therapy and dementia care. The chapter also updates readers on recent dementia information relevant to current music therapy practice and provides a theoretical framework for music therapy in this context. Clair discusses the importance of managing behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia, emphasizing the need for interventions that improve the quality of life for both care receivers and caregivers. She highlights the evolution of specialized care for dementia patients and the development of music therapy protocols designed to enhance emotional and social connections between individuals with dementia and their families. The chapter concludes with a discussion on the current state of music therapy research and the future directions for this therapeutic approach in dementia care.
