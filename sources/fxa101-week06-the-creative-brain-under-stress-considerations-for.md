## The Creative Brain Under Stress: Considerations for Performance in Extreme Environments

The article explores the impact of stress on creativity, focusing on the interaction between three large-scale brain networks: the default network (DN), executive control network (ECN), and salience network (SN). The authors review existing literature to elucidate how these neural systems contribute to creative cognition under optimal conditions. They argue that while much is known about the functioning of these networks in ordinary settings, less is understood regarding their role during extreme environmental stress. Stress impacts DN connectivity, potentially reducing mind-wandering and divergent thinking necessary for creativity. ECN functions are also affected by acute and chronic stress, which may impair the top-down control needed to refine creative ideas. The salience network modulates switching between internal and external attention, crucial for balancing task-relevant thought with novel idea generation. Understanding these neural dynamics can inform strategies to enhance creativity under challenging conditions.
