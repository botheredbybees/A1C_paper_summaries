## A TEXT-BOOK FOR AGRICULTURAL ECONOMISTS

The review discusses 'Farm Management' by Prof. John D. Black and his collaborators. The text is critiqued for its broad approach to farm management, which emphasizes the integration of agricultural practices with societal needs rather than focusing solely on profit maximization for individual farmers. The book's extensive coverage includes economic principles, systems of farming, and management methods, but the reviewer notes that British students may find the American-centric material somewhat distracting. Despite this, the review highlights the book's value in providing methods for analyzing and solving farm management problems. Additionally, the review briefly mentions 'The Diary and Sundry Observations of Thomas Alva Edison' edited by Dagobert D. Runes, emphasizing Edison's enduring curiosity and his contributions to various fields including motion pictures and atomic energy.
