## The arts and the social determinants of health: findings from an inquiry conducted by the United Kingdom All-Party Parliamentary Group on Arts, Health and Wellbeing

This article discusses findings from an inquiry by the United Kingdom All-Party Parliamentary Group on Arts, Health and Wellbeing into how arts engagement impacts health and wellbeing throughout life stages. The study utilized a realist method encompassing round-table discussions, expert meetings, and desk-based research to explore the relationship between arts engagement and social determinants of health. Key findings indicate that engaging with the arts can mitigate negative health outcomes related to socioeconomic disparities by influencing mental health during pregnancy, cognitive development in children, employment opportunities, work-related stress, individual resilience, and community strength. The report highlights the need for further research to substantiate these observations and underscores the potential role of cultural activities in enhancing societal well-being.
