## Cognitive and memory training in adults at risk of dementia: A Systematic Review

This study presents a systematic review evaluating cognitive training interventions for individuals with mild cognitive impairment (MCI), a condition characterized by an increased risk of developing dementia. The researchers aimed to determine the efficacy of cognitive exercises and memory strategy training in this population. After analyzing ten studies involving a total of 305 participants, they found moderate effects on memory outcomes from seven trials. Cognitive exercises were associated with larger effect sizes compared to memory strategies. Despite these findings, the review highlights that most studies are not randomized controlled trials (RCTs), indicating a need for further high-quality research. The implications for the creative arts and health fields could include integrating cognitive exercises into therapeutic programs aimed at mitigating cognitive decline in older adults.
