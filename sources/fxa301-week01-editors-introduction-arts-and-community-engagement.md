## Editor’s Introduction: Arts and Community Engagement: Intersect Music, Dance and Drama in Arts Education, Arts Therapy, and Arts Practice with Community Engagement Projects

This editor's introduction to a special issue of Wacana Seni Journal of Arts Discourse explores the intersection of music, dance, and drama within arts education, therapy, and practice in the context of community engagement projects. Hanafi Hussin discusses various approaches and methodologies used by different stakeholders, including academics, art practitioners, and marginalized communities, to engage with diverse groups such as cancer survivors, seniors with dementia, disabled dancers, children, and rural youth. Key themes include therapeutic interventions through dance and music for health benefits, the challenges faced by music therapists in Malaysia, and innovative educational methods like interactive theatre and creative dance in kindergarten settings. The introduction highlights how these arts-based practices contribute to social inclusion, holistic development, and the empowerment of participants.
