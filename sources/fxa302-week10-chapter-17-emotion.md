## Chapter 17: Emotion

The chapter on emotion in Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience by Bradley R. Postle explores the role of the amygdala in processing emotional stimuli. Key themes include the amygdala's function in implicit, quick-and-dirty triage of visual stimuli, influencing judgments of trustworthiness and emotional content in declarative memories. The amygdala is also central to the acquisition and expression of conditioned fear, and its influence on norepinephrine levels via its outputs to the locus coeruleus. The chapter discusses the relevance of amygdala studies to mental health conditions such as depression and anxiety, which are linked to exaggerated implicit emotional reactions and compromised prefrontal function. The role of the amygdala as a control center for emotions is also highlighted, with implications for the regulation of fear and other emotions. This chapter underscores the amygdala's critical role in emotional processing and its significance in understanding psychopathology.
