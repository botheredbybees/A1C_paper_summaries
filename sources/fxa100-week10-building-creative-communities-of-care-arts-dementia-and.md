## Building creative communities of care: Arts, dementia, and hope in the United States

Anne Basting explores the potential of arts and cultural community development in transforming dementia care experiences in the United States. Using her work with the Penelope Project as a case study, she outlines core elements for creating 'Creative Communities of Care' that focus on open systems, accessibility, integration of art into care environments, long-term project development, and leveraging existing assets and rituals to foster meaningful engagement and community support. The article contrasts biomedical approaches seeking to cure dementia with social models focused on enriching the lives of those affected through robust relationships and supportive communities. Basting highlights the arts' role in countering stigma, promoting social inclusion, and enhancing quality of life for people living with dementia.
