## Constraint-Based Thinking: A Structured Approach for Developing Frugal Innovations

This article introduces the concept of constraint-based thinking to guide the development of frugal innovations, particularly in resource-scarce environments. The authors argue that constraints are not merely limitations but can be harnessed as opportunities for innovative solutions. By focusing on two case studies from the medical device industry—UE LifeScience’s iBreastExam and GE Lullaby Warmer—the article proposes a structured approach to identify and map constraints to product requirements, fostering creative innovation under stringent conditions. The methodology involves iterative steps of identifying constraints, conducting root-cause analysis, mapping causes to product features, and developing minimal viable products or prototypes. This approach emphasizes the proactive utilization of constraints in the development process, addressing a gap in existing literature on ex-ante perspectives of frugal innovation.
