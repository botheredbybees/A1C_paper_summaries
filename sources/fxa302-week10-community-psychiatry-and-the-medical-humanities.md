## Community Psychiatry and the Medical Humanities

The chapter discusses the historical evolution of community psychiatry in the United States, focusing on the period from 1850 to the late 20th century. It highlights the transition from institutional care to community-based care, the impact of deinstitutionalization, and the challenges faced in implementing effective community mental health systems. Key themes include the role of psychiatric medications, policy changes, and the social and economic factors influencing mental health care. The author emphasizes the contested nature of the field and the tensions between its various stakeholders, suggesting that contributions from the medical humanities can aid in understanding recovery from mental illness. The chapter underscores the importance of interdisciplinary approaches to address the complex issues within community psychiatry.
