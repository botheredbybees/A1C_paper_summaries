## Music Therapy and Parkinson’s Disease: A Systematic Review from 2015–2020

This systematic review compiles scientific evidence published between 2015 and 2020 on the effects of music therapy in patients with Parkinson’s disease (PD). The review, conducted using the Web of Science and Scopus databases, aimed to identify the positive impacts of music therapy on various aspects of PD, including motor, communication, swallowing, breathing, and emotional functions. After applying inclusion and exclusion criteria, 58 papers were analyzed, demonstrating that music therapy programs yield improvements in multiple areas of patients' lives. The review highlights the efficacy of music-based interventions in enhancing motor function, balance, gait, and mental health. It also emphasizes the potential of singing programs to enhance memory, language skills, executive function, and respiratory strength in PD patients. The study underscores the physical, emotional, and social benefits of music therapy, reinforcing its role as a complementary treatment to pharmacological interventions in managing PD.
