## Week 4: Creativity and Health in Mediaeval and Early Modern Europe

This lesson explores the intersection of health, wellbeing, and creative arts from the fall of the Roman Empire to the early modern era. It covers significant historical events, societal structures, and cultural advancements in Western Europe and the Middle East during this period. The influence of the Church, architectural wonders like Gothic cathedrals, and the role of monasteries and convents are highlighted. Contributions from Islamic scholars such as Ibn Sina and Al-Zahrawi are noted for their medical knowledge. The shift towards the Renaissance, marked by the spread of ancient Greek and Roman literature, is also discussed alongside religious reforms. Artistic changes reflecting humanist ideals and the rise of secular patronage are detailed.
