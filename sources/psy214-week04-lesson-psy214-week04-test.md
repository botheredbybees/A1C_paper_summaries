## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Lifestyle Theme Overview

The lesson introduces the concept of lifestyle medicine as a critical aspect of biopsychosocial well-being. It discusses biological factors like immune system and stress response, psychological aspects such as life satisfaction and reward systems, and social/environmental influences on health behaviors. The unit emphasizes the importance of making healthy choices despite challenging environments, highlighting areas like diet, sleep hygiene, physical activity, and nature engagement.
