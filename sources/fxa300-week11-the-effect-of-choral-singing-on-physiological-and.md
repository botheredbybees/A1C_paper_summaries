## The effect of choral singing on physiological and psychosocial measures. An Italian pilot study

The study, part of the Dedalo Vola project, investigates the impact of a choral singing program on the physiological and psychosocial well-being of middle-aged and older adults. It highlights significant improvements in weight, oxygen saturation, heart rate, and psychological needs satisfaction. While the changes in blood pressure, cholesterol, triglycerides, stress, and mental health did not reach statistical significance, a positive trend was observed. The study also emphasizes the importance of cross-disciplinary collaboration between healthcare and arts to promote health and well-being. The research underscores the potential benefits of choral singing in enhancing social capital, reducing social isolation, and improving mental health among older adults.
