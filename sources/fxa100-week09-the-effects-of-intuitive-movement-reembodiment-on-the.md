## The Effects of Intuitive Movement Reembodiment on the Quality of Life of Older Adults With Dementia: A Pilot Study

This study explores the impact of intuitive movement reembodiment (IMR) on the quality of life (QoL) for older adults with varying stages of dementia. The IMR program uses familiar music and natural movements to engage participants in dance-like exercises, aiming to stimulate memory, improve mood, and encourage social interaction. Over ten weekly sessions, 22 participants were categorized into mild, moderate, and advanced dementia groups. Self-reported QoL assessments using the WHO-5 questionnaire and qualitative observations indicated significant improvements after session six. The study highlights how repetitive musical patterns and movement improvisation can positively influence well-being in individuals with cognitive impairment. Findings suggest that such creative activities could enhance the daily lives of older adults with dementia, though larger randomized controlled trials are needed to confirm these preliminary results.
