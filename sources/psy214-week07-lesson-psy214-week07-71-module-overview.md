## Mindfulness: Overview and Everyday Practices

This lesson introduces the concept of mindfulness, focusing on its general definition and the distinction between 'everyday' or 'informal' mindfulness and 'formal' mindfulness. The content outlines various everyday mindfulness activities such as mindful eating and walking. It also briefly touches on Langerian mindfulness as an alternative conceptualization. The lesson includes reflective questions to help students identify instances where they operate on 'automatic pilot' and engage less mindfully in their daily routines. The required readings for the week are provided in the unit's reading list, which also guides students on how to navigate and use the reading list effectively.
