## The Effects of Bimanual Coordination in Music Interventions on Executive Functions in Aging Adults

This study investigates the impact of bimanual coordination in music training on executive functions in older adults (ages 60-80 years). Participants underwent a 16-week program with three groups: piano (fine motor), percussion (gross motor), and music listening instruction. Results indicated significant enhancements in motor synchronization, visual scanning, and working memory for those who actively performed music compared to passive listeners. The study suggests that fine and gross motor skills contribute differently to cognitive benefits.
