## Music-based therapeutic interventions for people with dementia

This systematic review evaluates the effects of music-based therapeutic interventions on emotional well-being, mood disturbance, behavioral problems, social behavior, and cognition in individuals with dementia. The study includes 30 randomized controlled trials involving 1720 participants across 15 countries, assessing both individual and group interventions. Results indicate that music therapy likely improves depressive symptoms and may improve overall behavioral issues at the end of treatment compared to usual care. In contrast, there is no strong evidence for significant long-term effects beyond four weeks post-treatment on any outcomes except social behavior and anxiety. Adverse effects were rare but inconsistently reported. The review underscores the potential benefits of music therapy in enhancing specific aspects of well-being in dementia patients, highlighting its relevance to creative arts and health interventions.
