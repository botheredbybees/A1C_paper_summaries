## Week 3 Introduction: Arts, Health, and Wellbeing Policy

This document serves as an introduction to Week 3 of FXA301, focusing on the creation of policy around arts, health, and wellbeing from an international perspective. It outlines frameworks established in the United States, United Kingdom, and Australia, detailing the evidence supporting these policies and their translation into community initiatives. The materials explore how arts-focused policies interleave with other government programs and consider implications for various community groups. Overall, this week's content provides a foundational understanding of policy development and its practical applications.
