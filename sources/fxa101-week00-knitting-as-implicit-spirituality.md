## Knitting as Implicit Spirituality

The paper explores the resurgence of knitting as a craft practice and its significance for some practitioners in central Scotland. It examines how knitting can be considered implicitly religious or spiritual despite not being explicitly tied to traditional religious beliefs. Through participant observation, focus groups, and interviews with knitters, the study finds that knitting provides social connections, personal development, and a sense of community akin to what is typically associated with religious congregations. The research discusses the concept of implicit religion as well as the role of knitting in subjective wellbeing and spiritual practices.
