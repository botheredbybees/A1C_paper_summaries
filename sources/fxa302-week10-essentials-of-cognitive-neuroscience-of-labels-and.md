## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience: OF LABELS AND MECHANISMS

In this excerpt from 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience,' Postle explores the neural mechanisms underlying cognitive functions such as attention and oculomotor control. The text discusses how multivariate pattern analysis (MVPA) techniques have enabled researchers to decode brain activity patterns associated with different cognitive tasks. Specifically, the work of Jerde et al. (2012) is highlighted, demonstrating that a classifier trained on neural data from attention tasks could accurately predict neural activity during intention tasks, and vice versa. This finding suggests that the neural mechanisms involved in attention and oculomotor control might be more closely related than previously thought. Postle raises the question of whether the distinction between 'oculomotor control' and 'attentional control' is merely semantic, given the evidence that the same neural systems may underlie both processes. This has implications for how we categorize and understand cognitive functions at the neural level, challenging traditional boundaries between different psychological domains.
