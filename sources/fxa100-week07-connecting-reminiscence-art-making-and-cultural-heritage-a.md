## Connecting reminiscence, art making and cultural heritage: A pilot art-for-dementia care programme

This article describes a pilot participatory visual art program called Let’s Have Tea at the Museum designed for clients with Alzheimer's disease at an Alzheimer's Disease Association (ADA) day care center in Singapore. The program integrates reminiscence and art-making to explore cultural heritage, aiming to reawaken memories and promote personal expression among participants who are aged 70 or older. Key findings indicate that the program fosters self-discovery and socialization, stimulates multi-sensorial engagement through visual arts, and enhances morale and playfulness. The study's qualitative data analysis underscores the potential of art programs in enriching the lives of individuals with dementia by fostering a sense of purpose and connection to cultural heritage.
