## Weaving Textile-form Interfaces: A Material-Driven Design Journey

This paper explores the process of designing textile-form interfaces through weaving, focusing on the challenges and opportunities within Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). The authors describe a material-driven design journey undertaken by a multidisciplinary team to create woven textile-form interfaces using techniques like multi-layer weaving with conductive yarns. They highlight the importance of shared knowledge across disciplines to overcome the lack of accessible tools for novice designers interested in this field. The paper also introduces a pictorial guide, designed as a paper prototyping tool, aimed at helping designers understand and experiment with woven textile-form interfaces.
