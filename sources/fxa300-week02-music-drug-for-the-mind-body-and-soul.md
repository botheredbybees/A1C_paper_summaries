## Music: Drug for the mind, body, and soul

The editorial 'Music: Drug for the mind, body, and soul' by Chhabra et al. explores how music can serve as a therapeutic intervention in various medical contexts, particularly in anesthesia. The authors discuss the benefits of using music to reduce anxiety and pain, improve recovery, and enhance patient satisfaction during surgery and critical care. Studies show that music therapy can lower cortisol levels, increase endorphin expression, and promote parasympathetic activity. Personalized playlists are highlighted as more effective than preselected ones in alleviating stress and anxiety. The article emphasizes the potential of music to be integrated into standard medical protocols for improved patient outcomes.
