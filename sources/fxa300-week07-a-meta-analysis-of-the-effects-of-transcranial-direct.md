## A Meta-Analysis of the Effects of Transcranial Direct Current Stimulation on Creative Thinking

This meta-analysis evaluates the effects of transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS) on creative thinking, synthesizing 32 studies and 125 effect sizes with 1,538 participants. The analysis reveals small positive effects of anodal tDCS on creative performance, particularly on convergent thinking tasks. Anodal tDCS over the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) and right inferior frontal gyrus (IFG) enhanced creative performance, whereas stimulation over the left IFG decreased it. The study highlights the importance of precise targeting and task specificity for enhancing creative performance using tDCS. These findings contribute to the understanding of neural mechanisms underlying creative cognition and provide insights for potential applications in enhancing creativity in arts and sciences.
