## Intrinsic Motivation and Effective Teaching

The chapter 'Intrinsic Motivation and Effective Teaching' by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi discusses the role of intrinsic motivation in university education and teaching effectiveness. The author challenges traditional views on teaching, suggesting that it should not merely involve information transfer but rather instill a lifelong passion for learning. He argues that professors who are intrinsically motivated to learn and teach will be most effective in fostering similar intrinsic motivation in their students. Csikszentmihalyi highlights the importance of an environment that supports intrinsic motivation, rather than extrinsic rewards or external control mechanisms, which can undermine this intrinsic drive. Drawing from historical contexts and contemporary research, he illustrates how current educational systems often fail to create such supportive environments, leading to a disconnection between work (or study) and personal well-being. He advocates for an approach that integrates work with other meaningful aspects of life, aiming to enhance the quality of learning experiences through fostering intrinsic motivation.
