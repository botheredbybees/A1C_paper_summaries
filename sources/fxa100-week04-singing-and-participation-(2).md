## Singing and Participation

The document discusses the social benefits of singing for individuals with dementia, emphasizing its role in mitigating isolation and depression. Studies indicate that group singing can enhance mood and reduce agitation even among those in late-stage dementia. Research suggests a decrease in stress hormones and an increase in melatonin levels associated with active participation in singing activities. Case studies like Mind Song UK highlight the success of community-based musical interventions, such as Silver Song Clubs and Singing for the Brain programs, which aim to provide social cohesion and emotional support for those affected by dementia.
