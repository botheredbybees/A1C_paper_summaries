## Photography and Digital Storytelling in English for Tourism Classroom

This article presents a digital storytelling project aimed at enhancing English language learning and developing 21st-century skills among tourism students in Portugal. The study uses photography to engage students with their local city as a tourist destination, fostering creativity and communication in the process of producing narratives about their experiences. Key findings include increased student engagement in the learning process through self-generated content, improved use of English language skills such as speaking and writing, and development of critical thinking and collaborative work skills. The project underscores the importance of integrating digital media tools into language education to prepare students for professional environments in tourism.
