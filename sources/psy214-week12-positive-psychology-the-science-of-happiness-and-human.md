## Positive Psychology: The Science of Happiness and Human Strengths (Second Edition)

Alan Carr’s 'Positive Psychology: The Science of Happiness and Human Strengths' is a comprehensive and accessible introduction to the field of positive psychology. The second edition updates the original work with recent research and advancements, particularly including a new chapter on positive psychotherapy and expanded coverage on character strengths and virtues, as well as groundbreaking research on emotional intelligence. The book serves as a valuable resource for students and lecturers in psychology and related fields such as clinical psychology, social work, and counseling. Carr, a professor and director at University College Dublin, discusses major theories, research, and practical applications of positive psychology. Each chapter covers topics like happiness, positive traits, hope and optimism, flow, emotional intelligence, giftedness, creativity, wisdom, positive self, positive relationships, and psychological therapy, providing learning objectives, summaries, and further reading suggestions to enhance understanding and application of the material. The book highlights the importance of positive psychology in promoting well-being and resilience, making it a significant contribution to the field.
