## Facilitating creativity in dementia care: the co-construction of arts-based engagement

This paper explores the engagement of people with dementia in creative and arts-based activities through a relational model of citizenship and concepts from adult learning theory. The authors conducted a theoretically-driven secondary analysis of observational and interview data to understand how staff, volunteers, and participants co-create engaging experiences during an arts-based intervention at a day center and care home. Key findings highlight the importance of building confidence for learning, fostering social and physical connections, and affirming creative self-expression as central processes in such interventions. The study emphasizes the facilitator's crucial role in reinforcing agency among participants and integrating their prior experiences to enhance creativity and positive outcomes for individuals and care staff.
