## Reflection on designing: metacognitive interventions to enhance metacognitive awareness, motivation, and performance in design learning

This study explores the impact of structured metacognitive interventions on architecture students' metacognitive awareness (MA), goal orientations (GO), and academic performance in design courses. Through a quasi-experimental approach, 84 third-year architecture students were divided into an experimental group receiving three structured interventions over six weeks and a control group. The study found significant improvements in MA levels, mastery-performance orientation, and course grades among the intervention group compared to controls. Students with initially high awareness also showed marked progress. The research underscores the critical role of metacognitive strategies in enhancing learning outcomes and motivation within complex, open-ended problem-solving contexts typical of design education.
