## Creativity and the Exceptional Aging Artist

This article explores the relationship between aging and creativity, focusing on visual artists who experience a resurgence of creative activity late in life. The authors review factors that contribute to this phenomenon, including personal qualities such as resilience and adaptability, changes in artistic approach, and external circumstances like market demand. They emphasize the importance of ongoing engagement with one's practice and highlight instances where older artists achieve remarkable levels of creativity despite common assumptions about cognitive decline in later years. The article also discusses how societal perceptions of aging can impact an artist’s career trajectory, advocating for programs that support elderly artists to maintain or enhance their creative output.
