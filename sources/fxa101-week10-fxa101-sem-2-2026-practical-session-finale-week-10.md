## FXA101 Sem 2 2026 – Practical Session Finale Week 10

This document outlines the concluding practical session for the FXA101 course in Semester 2 of 2026. Dr Brendan Lamb leads the session, reflecting on the course's journey through various creative exercises, including improvisation, design, drawing, and word association games. The session highlights the development of neural connections within the brain, such as the visual cortex, temporal cortex, and motor cortex, as students engage in these activities. Students are encouraged to reflect on their creative practices and how they have evolved over the course. The document also provides information about the final web lecture and the deadlines for the Creative Project 1C and the Module 3 Discussion Forum.
