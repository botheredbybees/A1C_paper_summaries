## Art and Dementia | Art Gallery of NSW

The Art and Dementia initiative at the Art Gallery of New South Wales is a comprehensive arts engagement model aimed at enhancing the social inclusion and wellbeing of individuals living with dementia, their care partners, and the broader community. The program offers a range of activities, including monthly discussion sessions and sensory art-making experiences, which are designed to foster imaginative thinking, personal connections, and emotional responses through the engagement with artworks. Research, such as the evaluation by Dr. Gail Kenning in 2016, highlights the positive impact of these programs on self-identity, confidence, and overall quality of life. The initiative has also been extended to various community settings through the Arts Engagement for Liveable Communities project, demonstrating its potential for replication and integration into residential care facilities and community centers. The program underscores the importance of arts engagement in combating social isolation and fostering meaningful connections, contributing significantly to the health and wellbeing of those living with dementia.
