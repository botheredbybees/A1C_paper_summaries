## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The document excerpts from 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Bradley R. Postle, discussing the neural mechanisms involved in decision-making processes. The text covers perceptual decision-making, where the brain integrates sensory information to make choices about the objective state of the world, and value-based decision-making, which involves subjective preferences and choices influenced by expected value. The study by Donner et al. (2009) and the monkey study by Platt and Glimcher (1999) are highlighted, illustrating how motor cortex activity and lateral intraparietal (LIP) neurons respectively contribute to perceptual and value-based decision-making processes. The text emphasizes the role of LIP neurons in representing the subjective value of eye movements, suggesting a re-conceptualization of the frontoparietal oculomotor complex as a 'saccade value' map rather than a salience map. This insight is crucial for understanding how the brain integrates sensory and reward information to guide behavior, which is highly relevant to the creative arts and health by informing therapeutic approaches to cognitive and motor function impairments.
