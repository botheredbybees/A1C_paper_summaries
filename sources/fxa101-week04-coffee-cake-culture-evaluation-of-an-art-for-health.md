## Coffee, Cake & Culture: Evaluation of an art for health programme for older people in the community

The article evaluates the Coffee, Cake & Culture programme organised and delivered by Whitworth Art Gallery and Manchester Museum in 2012 for older people living in care homes or supported living facilities. The study highlights the benefits of arts for health initiatives on older individuals' wellbeing, social engagement, learning, inclusion, and creativity. It underscores that these programmes can significantly enhance quality of life and social participation among elderly participants, particularly those with memory loss conditions like dementia. Moreover, it notes that the majority of older people and care staff involved in the programme had not previously engaged with such cultural institutions. The study concludes with lessons for future planning and emphasizes the need for further research involving interdisciplinary collaborations between researchers, curators, artists, care professionals, and elderly participants to sustainably promote arts-based interventions.
