## Instruments and injuries: Hearing loss and stress affecting musicians

This lesson focuses on the impact of hearing loss and stress on musicians. It highlights the case of Dame Evelyn Glennie, a renowned percussionist who lost nearly all her hearing by age 12 but continued to perform professionally. The lesson discusses the gradual nature of hearing loss due to noise exposure, the role of earplugs in hearing protection, and the prevalence of tinnitus among musicians, especially those who play amplified instruments. It also touches on the legal implications of hearing damage, as exemplified by a violist's successful case against the Royal Opera House. The lesson includes an examination of mental health issues such as stress, anxiety, and depression in professional musicians, citing a study that found 73% of independent musicians dealt with some form of mental illness. The importance of workplace safety and mental health awareness is emphasized.
