## FXA303 Week 5 Welcome

This document introduces the content of Week 5 in FXA303, marking a transition to Module 2 which focuses on play and creative arts for mental wellbeing. The module emphasizes hands-on activities involving puppet creation as a method of expressing empathetic responses to trauma through creative means. It includes an overview of puppetry history, design techniques, and mechanisms for creating expressive puppets. Additionally, the text provides guidance on self-care strategies for students engaging with traumatic material, highlighting potential vicarious trauma from empathizing with others' experiences.
