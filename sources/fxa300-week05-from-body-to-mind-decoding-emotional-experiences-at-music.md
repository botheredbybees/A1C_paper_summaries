## From body to mind: Decoding emotional experiences at music festivals

This study explores the interplay between bodily expression and emotional experiences at music festivals, emphasizing the body-mind connection in tourism research. Utilizing an 'emotion–feeling–consciousness' (E–F–C) framework based on Damasio’s theory of consciousness, it investigates how individuals process emotions through their physical sensations, feelings, and cognitive awareness during multi-site qualitative data collection at four Midi Music Festivals over two years. The findings reveal three overarching themes: emotional turmoil, feeling what happened, and consciousness with self-renewal. These results highlight the importance of embodied emotion in creating memorable tourism experiences and underscore the significance of bodily engagement for self-renewal. This study contributes to the understanding of how tourism activities facilitate emotional well-being through direct physical interactions with the environment.
