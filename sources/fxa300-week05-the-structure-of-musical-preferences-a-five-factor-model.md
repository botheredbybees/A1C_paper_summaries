## The Structure of Musical Preferences: A Five-Factor Model

The article by Rentfrow, Goldberg, and Levitin introduces a five-factor model for understanding the structure of musical preferences based on affective reactions to music from various genres. The authors conducted three independent studies which identified latent factors underlying music preferences: Mellow (relaxing styles), Unpretentious (sincere and rootsy music), Sophisticated (classical, operatic, world, jazz), Intense (loud and energetic), and Contemporary (rhythmic and percussive). These findings suggest that musical preferences are primarily influenced by emotional/affective responses rather than social or auditory characteristics of the music. The research also highlights the wide variety of functions music serves in society and its potential to reflect individual psychological traits, thereby contributing significantly to theories on creative arts and health.
