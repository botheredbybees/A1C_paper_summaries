## Familiar Music Reduces Mind Wandering and Boosts Behavioral Performance During Lexical Semantic Processing

This study examines the impact of familiar versus unfamiliar music on mind wandering and performance during a lexical-semantic judgment task. Participants listened to non-vocal classical music pieces (either familiar or unfamiliar), as well as an environmental sound clip while performing the task. The primary outcome measures were accuracy, reaction times, and frequency of mind wandering episodes probed throughout the task. Results indicated that listening to familiar music was associated with faster lexical-semantic decisions and a lower incidence of mind wandering compared to unfamiliar music or control sounds. This suggests that familiar music enhances cognitive performance by reducing distraction and enhancing attentional resources.
