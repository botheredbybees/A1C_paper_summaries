## Web Lecture Week 9: Colour, Default Mode Network, Focus and Attention

This lecture, titled 'Web Lecture 6: Colour, Default Mode Network, Focus and Attention,' explores various themes related to creativity, including the influence of color on perception and mood, the role of the Default Mode Network (DMN) in creative thinking, and the impact of music and silence on cognitive processes. The lecture discusses how colors like blue and red can evoke specific emotional responses due to cultural and learned associations. It highlights research showing that the color red is often perceived as dominant and can trigger caution in both humans and primates. The lecture also delves into the concept of qualia and its relationship with attention, emphasizing the importance of short-term memory and the open-ended nature of qualia outputs. It further examines the DMN's role in creative thinking, noting that it activates during tasks that require novel ideas and coordinates with other brain regions involved in complex problem-solving and decision-making. The lecture also explores the benefits of music during creative exercises, discussing the state of Experiential Fusion and the effects of binaural beats on attention and focus. Finally, it addresses the potential for silence and music to become interference, citing studies that suggest the absence of stimuli can lead to mental challenges, while curated music can enhance specific tasks if used appropriately.
