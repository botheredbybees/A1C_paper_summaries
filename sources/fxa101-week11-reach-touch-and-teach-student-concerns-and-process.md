## Reach, Touch, and Teach: Student Concerns and Process Education by Terry Borton

Ernie Karsten's review of Terry Borton's book, 'Reach, Touch, and Teach: Student Concerns and Process Education,' focuses on Borton's advocacy for 'process education' in classrooms. Borton encourages teachers to move beyond traditional curricula to address students' real-life problems and help them develop personal growth processes. Karsten highlights Borton's three-step approach—What, So What, Now What—to guide both curriculum design and classroom activities. The review also emphasizes Borton's belief in the importance of understanding and modifying personal growth processes to enhance educational outcomes. This approach is particularly relevant to creative arts and health by promoting a holistic and student-centered educational experience, encouraging critical thinking and self-awareness.
