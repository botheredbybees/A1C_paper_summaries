## Week 8 Lecture on Applying Theatre and Literature for Health Benefits

The document outlines the health benefits of engaging with theatre and literature in communities. It highlights how laughter from theatrical performances can reduce stress, improve circulation, lower blood pressure, release endorphins, and protect against depression. Programs utilizing humour are applied in hospitals and aged care settings to enhance well-being. Case studies such as 'Sharing Stories' (WA, 2008) illustrate the use of theatre for sexual health education among culturally diverse youth. Additionally, shared reading sessions have been shown to alleviate chronic pain symptoms and psychological distress. Creative writing workshops also contribute to mental health by fostering self-expression and community bonding. The lecture emphasizes the importance of planning an eight-week arts project that promotes better health and well-being, supported by relevant literature on health outcomes. This underscores the potential for creative arts interventions in enhancing physical and mental health within diverse communities.
