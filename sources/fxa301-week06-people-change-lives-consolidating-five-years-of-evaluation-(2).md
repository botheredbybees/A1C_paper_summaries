## People change lives: consolidating five years of evaluation learning from Sistema Scotland’s Big Noise centres in Stirling, Glasgow and Aberdeen

The report 'People Change Lives' by Chris Harkins and Katie Moore summarizes five years of evaluations undertaken at Sistema Scotland’s Big Noise centers located in Stirling, Glasgow, and Aberdeen. The central argument underscores the significant positive impacts these musical programs have on young participants. Key findings include improved confidence, discipline, and resilience among children and young people; enhanced team-working skills and leadership capabilities; academic skill improvements such as listening and concentration; and strong development of musical abilities. Additionally, there is an uptick in physical activity and healthy eating habits while avoiding detrimental behaviors. The report emphasizes that these positive outcomes are largely a result of long-term, supportive relationships between the musicians and participants who act as educators, mentors, and role models. The relevance to creative arts and health lies in demonstrating how consistent engagement with music education can foster social development, improve mental well-being, and potentially reduce health inequalities across disadvantaged communities.
