## The Arts/In the Community

Elaine Raichle's article discusses the importance of art education in fostering community engagement and support for school programs. In Irvington, New Jersey, a pioneering Arts Festival was organized to bridge the cultural gap between parents and children educated with modern arts perspectives. The initiative involved collaboration among various departments within schools (Fine Arts, Homemaking, Industrial Arts) and included extensive community participation through committees formed by both teachers and lay members of the community. The three-day event featured displays of student artwork along with performances in music, dance, and drama, aiming to showcase a broad range of creative activities and learning processes. By involving multiple aspects of arts education, the festival not only raised awareness about the importance of art in schools but also generated substantial community interest, leading to increased financial support for educational programs.
