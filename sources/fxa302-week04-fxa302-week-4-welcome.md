## FXA302 Week 4 Welcome

This welcome message for FXA302 Week 4 introduces the focus on medieval and Renaissance perspectives of health and wellbeing through artistic representations. It highlights the influence of classical anatomy, medicine, and healing in these periods, with a particular emphasis on Hildegard of Bingen’s contributions to music, theology, and medical writings. The week also explores the impact of the Black Death on European society and culture, and how creative works from this era reflect the experiences of those who lived through it. Additionally, students will engage with Renaissance art that captures both the idealized human form and its imperfections, including representations of disease, thus enhancing observational skills through visual thinking strategies. The importance of participating in discussions and reviewing previous weeks’ materials is stressed to prepare for Quiz 1.
