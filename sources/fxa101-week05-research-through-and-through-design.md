## Research through and through design

Johan Redström's article explores the concept of 'research through design' by examining three interpretations: through practice, making, and judgement. He argues that despite ambiguity around what constitutes research or design, this approach offers a unique perspective on integrating these disciplines. Through practice emphasizes the extensive nature of practice beyond methodological frameworks, highlighting its complexity in transdisciplinary contexts. The paper questions how to define 'practice' when there is no established one, particularly in sustainability-focused research. Redström suggests that different interpretations allow for a more expansive and inclusive understanding of design research, promoting radical approaches over traditional boundaries.
