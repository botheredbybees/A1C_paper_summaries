## Research Designs: Case Studies, Correlational and Longitudinal Studies

This lesson focuses on three research designs commonly used in music psychology: case studies, correlational studies, and longitudinal studies. Case studies provide detailed information about individual responses to therapy over time but cannot establish causation due to the lack of generalizability to a larger population. They allow for the examination of client factors such as attitudes towards therapy and engagement levels, contributing significantly to therapeutic change outcomes (40%). Correlational studies explore relationships between two variables without establishing causality. Examples include studying the correlation between musicianship metrics like years of training with personality traits measured by scales like Big Five or HEXACO. Longitudinal studies, not covered in detail here but mentioned for future reference, are useful for understanding long-term effects and trends.
