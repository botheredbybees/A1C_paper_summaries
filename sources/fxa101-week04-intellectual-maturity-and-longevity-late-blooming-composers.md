## Intellectual maturity and longevity: Late-blooming composers and writers live longer than child prodigies

This study investigates the relationship between intellectual maturity and longevity in composers and creative writers from historical biographical records spanning from 1400 to 1915 AD. The researchers utilized multilevel regression analysis to model the correlation between age at first work (a proxy for onset of intellectual maturity) and age at death, adjusting for sex, date of birth, and nationality. Findings indicate that later completion of a first creative work correlates with higher longevity among composers and writers. This suggests that a delayed onset of intellectual maturity may be associated with increased lifespan in these groups.
