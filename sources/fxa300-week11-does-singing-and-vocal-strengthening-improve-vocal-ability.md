## Does singing and vocal strengthening improve vocal ability in people with Parkinson’s disease?

The study by Tanner, Rammage, and Liu (2016) examines whether singing and vocal strengthening can enhance vocal abilities in individuals with Parkinson’s disease (PD). The research introduces a cross-disciplinary group vocalization protocol that integrates singing and vocal effort exercises. This protocol aims to improve vocal abilities in people with PD, especially in those early in the disease progression where vocal problems are not yet severe enough to necessitate individualized treatment. The study found significant improvements in vocal intensity and average frequency after 12 treatment sessions. The results suggest that group vocal strengthening activities, including singing, may help maintain vocal skills and slow down vocal deterioration associated with PD. This approach provides a promising avenue for addressing the long-term vocal challenges faced by PD patients, particularly in terms of prevention and maintenance of vocal skills.
