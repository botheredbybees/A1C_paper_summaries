## Conscious Creativity: Look, Connect, Create — Chapter 2: Structure, Practice and Obstacles

Chapter 2 of 'Conscious Creativity' by Philippa Stanton discusses the importance of structure and practice in fostering creativity, while also acknowledging common obstacles to beginning creative work. The chapter emphasizes finding a personal approach that balances aspiration with achievability, such as through visual aids like lists and sticky notes. It suggests creating structures that are flexible and adaptable over time. Stanton stresses the value of both engaging with familiar stimuli and exploring new ones for inspiration. Through exercises and anecdotes, she highlights how even simple activities can enhance creative practice by encouraging immersion in the moment and observing natural processes.
