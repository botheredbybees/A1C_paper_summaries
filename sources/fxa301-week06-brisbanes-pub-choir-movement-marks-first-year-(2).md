## Brisbane’s Pub Choir movement marks first year

Andrew McMillen’s article in The Australian discusses the growth and impact of Brisbane's Pub Choir movement over its first year. Starting with a modest group of people who gathered monthly to learn songs without judgment, the event has grown tenfold due to its inclusive nature and welcoming atmosphere for music enthusiasts of all skill levels. This phenomenon is celebrated as a form of communal engagement where individuals feel a sense of belonging despite being strangers. Pub Choir offers participants an escape from societal pressures that often demand excellence or recognition in creative activities such as singing, instead emphasizing the joy derived from collaborative experiences and the freedom to enjoy the moment without focusing on outcomes. The article highlights how events like this contribute positively to communities by fostering social connections and reducing anxiety associated with public performances.
