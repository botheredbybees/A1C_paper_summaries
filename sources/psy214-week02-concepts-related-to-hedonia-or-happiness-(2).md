## Concepts related to hedonia or ‘happiness’

The document explores concepts related to hedonia or happiness, focusing on affective forecasting and the hedonic treadmill theory. Affective forecasting involves predicting future emotions, but people tend to be inaccurate in this prediction, overestimating both positive and negative effects of life events. This is exemplified by research comparing lottery winners and individuals who became paraplegic (Brickman et al., 1978). The hedonic treadmill concept suggests that people return to a baseline level of happiness after significant life changes due to adaptation, highlighting the importance of understanding long-term emotional impacts. Research on well-being emphasizes materialism’s limited impact on happiness, suggesting activities and giving as more effective pathways to sustained satisfaction.
