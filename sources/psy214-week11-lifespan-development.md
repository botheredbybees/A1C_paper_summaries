## Lifespan Development

The document 'Lifespan Development' is an educational resource published by OpenStax, a free, peer-reviewed, and openly licensed textbook initiative. The book covers the psychological and developmental theories, genetic and environmental factors influencing development, physical and cognitive growth from infancy through adolescence, and the social and emotional development in various stages of life. Each chapter includes a detailed exploration of key theories, themes, and developmental milestones, supported by research and case studies. The text emphasizes the importance of understanding development across the lifespan to better support individuals at different stages, providing valuable insights for fields such as psychology, education, and health care. The relevance to creative arts and health is evident in its focus on holistic development, including emotional and social aspects, which are crucial for fostering creativity and resilience in young individuals.
