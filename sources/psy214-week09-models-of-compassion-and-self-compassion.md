## Models of compassion and self-compassion

The document discusses the models of compassion and self-compassion proposed by Kristen Neff and colleagues. The models emphasize three key components: mindfulness, kindness, and common humanity. Mindfulness involves bringing awareness to suffering, whether it is one's own or others', and responding with acceptance and non-reactivity. Kindness is the recognition that everyone experiences pain and hardship, fostering a sense of connection rather than isolation. Common humanity acknowledges that being human entails being imperfect and experiencing vulnerability. The opposite poles of these components include disengagement and indifference towards others, and overidentification and self-judgment towards oneself. The document highlights the importance of these concepts in alleviating suffering and promoting care and concern, both for oneself and others, making it relevant to creative arts and health practices that focus on emotional well-being and community support.
