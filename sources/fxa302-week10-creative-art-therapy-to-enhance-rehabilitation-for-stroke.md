## Creative art therapy to enhance rehabilitation for stroke patients: a randomized controlled trial

This randomized controlled trial investigates the efficacy of creative art therapy combined with conventional physical therapy for enhancing rehabilitation outcomes in stroke patients. The study compared the impact of creative art therapy alongside physical therapy with physical therapy alone on cognitive ability, physical functions, psychological status, and quality of life. Participants were randomized into intervention and control groups, receiving either additional creative art therapy sessions or only physical therapy. Key findings indicated that the intervention group experienced significantly greater improvements in depression, physical functions, and quality of life compared to the control group. Patients reported high satisfaction with the art therapy sessions and noted improvements in concentration, emotions, self-confidence, and motivation. The study concludes that creative art therapy can complement physical therapy to improve mental health and quality of life in stroke patients.
