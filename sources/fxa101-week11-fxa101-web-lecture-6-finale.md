## FXA101 Web Lecture 6 - Finale

The document is a web lecture summary for FXA101, focusing on the reflection of light and shade, and the broader implications of creative processes in the arts. Key topics include the use of chiaroscuro and shadow in visual arts, reflections on practice through models such as Borton’s and Gibb’s, and the neurological aspects of creativity and cognitive reserve. The lecture also touches on the influence of synaesthesia on artistic expression and the importance of mental health in creative endeavors. It highlights the work of artist Yayoi Kusama and discusses the role of brain training apps in enhancing cognitive functions. The text concludes with assessment task guidelines, emphasizing the critical reflection on creative processes and challenges, and the cognitive effects of engaging in creative projects.
