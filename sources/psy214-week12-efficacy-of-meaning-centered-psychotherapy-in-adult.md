## Efficacy of Meaning-Centered Psychotherapy in adult patients with advanced cancer: A systematic review and meta-analysis

This study conducts a systematic review and meta-analysis of clinical trials to assess the efficacy of Meaning-Centered Psychotherapy (MCP) for adult patients with advanced cancer. The review included seven trials for the systematic analysis and four for the meta-analysis. The findings indicate that MCP is effective in enhancing spiritual well-being, quality of life, sense of meaning, and reducing psychological distress, including anxiety and depression. Despite some inconsistencies between studies, MCP showed superior therapeutic effects in pre-post comparisons for the aforementioned outcomes. Notably, MCP did not lead to a higher risk of dropout. The study underscores the potential of MCP as a promising intervention to improve spiritual well-being and quality of life while reducing psychological distress in patients with advanced cancer. However, the authors suggest that further research is needed to establish a more robust evidence base with higher methodological quality studies.
