## Arts in the community as a place-making event

The document discusses arts activities in communities through an ethnographic approach, using a specific example from Nottingham to highlight how these initiatives can serve as place-making events. The chapter explores the work of Hanby and Barrett, a community theatre company, which has been working collaboratively with academics for six years on various projects, including one marking the 125th anniversary of Raleigh bicycles in Nottingham. It emphasizes that such activities involve an ethnographic approach to understand the context better, recognition of local knowledge and narratives, and engagement with language and storytelling. The authors argue that this method counters negative discourses surrounding deprived areas and promotes a more positive view of community assets and strengths.
