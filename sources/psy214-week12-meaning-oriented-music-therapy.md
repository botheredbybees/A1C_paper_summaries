## Meaning-Oriented Music Therapy

The document discusses the concept of meaning in the context of health and well-being, emphasizing its significance in work, mental health, and personal life. Viktor Frankl's logotherapy, a psychotherapeutic approach emphasizing the search for meaning, is highlighted as a fundamental aspect of addressing meaning-related issues. The text explores the potential of music therapy in fostering meaning, resilience, and psychological well-being. It also introduces the idea of 'meaning-oriented music therapy' as a discipline that integrates logotherapy and existential analysis into therapeutic practices. This approach aims to provide support to individuals experiencing meaninglessness or existential frustration through creative and arts-based methods. The document underscores the importance of the human will to meaning, a central concept in logotherapy, and discusses various ways individuals can find meaning in life through creative, experiential, and attitudinal values. It concludes by advocating for the integration of music therapy and logotherapy to address the growing importance of meaning in contemporary life.
