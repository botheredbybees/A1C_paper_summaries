## How do I interpret scholarly sources?

This lesson provides an overview of interpreting scholarly sources related to managing stress and enhancing well-being. It covers key concepts such as hypotheses, statistical significance (p-values), effect sizes, systematic reviews, and meta-analyses. The lesson explains the evidence base for different approaches, including those with a strong foundation like regular exercise and social connections versus newer practices like mindfulness that have an evolving research landscape. Students are guided through understanding various types of evidence such as primary research articles, review articles (including narrative and systematic), and meta-analyses. Hypothesis testing is described alongside examples of statistical tests and their implications for interpreting data. The lesson also introduces effect sizes to gauge the practical significance beyond mere statistical relevance.
