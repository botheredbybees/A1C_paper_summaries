## Cognitive Exercise and Its Role in Cognitive Function in Older Adults

This article explores the role of cognitive exercise in maintaining and improving cognitive function among older adults, with a focus on cognitive training (CT) as defined within this research context. It reviews clinical studies examining CT's effectiveness across different stages of cognitive decline, including normal aging, mild cognitive impairment, and dementia. Despite methodological limitations noted in existing research, the review suggests that multidomain CT can enhance cognitive performance in healthy older adults and potentially slow cognitive decline in those with impairments. The paper also evaluates commercial products used for cognitive training and provides recommendations for further research.
