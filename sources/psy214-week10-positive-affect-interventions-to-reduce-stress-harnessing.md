## Positive Affect Interventions to Reduce Stress: Harnessing the Benefit While Avoiding the Pollyanna Trap

The chapter discusses the benefits of positive affect interventions in reducing stress, emphasizing the adaptive consequences of positive emotions such as improved coping and better physical health. Researchers review various multi-component interventions that teach skills for enhancing positive experiences, improving relationships, and pursuing personal development, targeting populations dealing with significant stressors such as chronic illness, affective disorders, and major life stressors. The interventions aim to harness the benefits of positive affect while avoiding the potential drawbacks of dismissing negative emotions. The authors highlight the need for effective interventions to manage stress and present their own recently piloted intervention aimed at reducing stress through increased positive affect. The chapter also addresses measurement issues and the importance of defining positive affect clearly to enhance the effectiveness of these interventions.
