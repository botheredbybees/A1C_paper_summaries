## Week 5 Activities: FXA300 Music, Mind and Body

This week's activities in the course 'Music, Mind, and Body' include suggested readings on individual differences in musical taste, social status influences, omnivorous taste, environmental factors, age-related trends, and gender differences. It also involves a discussion activity where students reflect on insights gained from taking questionnaires related to musical preferences. The week concludes with reminders about the upcoming quiz covering content up to Week 5.
