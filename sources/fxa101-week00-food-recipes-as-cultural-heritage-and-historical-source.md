## Food Recipes as Cultural Heritage and Historical Source

Yrsa Lindqvist explores the historical significance of handwritten recipe books in Finland, highlighting their value as cultural and historical sources. The article discusses a digital project called 'Historiska recept,' which aims to make early Finnish manuscript recipe books accessible for research and practical use. These manuscripts provide insights into food consumption patterns, trade routes, social hierarchies, and daily life practices of the upper classes in 18th- and early 19th-century Finland. The recipes often include ingredients indicative of a luxury lifestyle unattainable by most citizens at the time, such as spices, lemons, almond flour, lobster, and oysters. Lindqvist emphasizes the importance of preserving these documents for their educational value and practical application in modern culinary contexts.
