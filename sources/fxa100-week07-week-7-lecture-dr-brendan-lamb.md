## Week 7 Lecture - Dr Brendan Lamb

The lecture covers key considerations and programs for dementia care within arts settings, emphasizing the importance of gallery and museum programs. It highlights the ARTZ program, which provides artistic experiences for individuals with Alzheimer's in care homes and communities, and the 'Meet Me at MOMA' initiative, which builds confidence and respect among participants. The National Art Gallery of Australia's programs for dementia patients and their caregivers are also discussed, as are various other arts access programs across Australia. The lecture discusses the benefits of reminiscence-based programs, such as fostering emotional connections and personal engagement, and the potential for creativity through gallery interactions. Key planning recommendations for successful gallery visits are provided, including direct and smooth routes, clear introductions, and positive reinforcement. The lecture concludes with a discussion on Baines' (2007) concepts of 'Potential Beyond Problems' and invites reflections on the approach to dementia care, focusing on cognition or emotion.
