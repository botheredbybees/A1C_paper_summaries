## Exploring Musical Preferences: An In-Depth Qualitative Study of Adults' Liking for Music in Their Personal Collections

This article reports a qualitative study investigating the breadth, content, and rationale of musical preferences among adults aged between 18 and 73 years old. The study utilized in-depth interviews conducted at participants' homes with their music collections present as an aide memoire for preferred styles. Key findings include that individuals generally prefer a wide range of musical genres, supporting the trend towards omnivorous tastes; there are substantial differences in how various characteristics (such as lyrics and instrumentation) and responses to music shape preferences; and musical preferences play a significant role in shaping self-identity. The research underscores the importance of qualitative methods for exploring subjective perceptions, interpretations, and evaluations of musical likes and dislikes. It also emphasizes the need to move away from approaches that rely on predetermined style categories due to their varied interpretation among listeners.
