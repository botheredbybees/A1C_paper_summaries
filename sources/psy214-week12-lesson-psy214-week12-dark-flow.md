## Dark Flow: The Potential Negative Consequences of Flow Experiences

This lesson explores the concept of 'dark flow,' which refers to the potential negative consequences of experiencing flow states, a term originally associated with positive psychological benefits. The lesson highlights that while flow can enhance well-being and personal growth, it can also lead to harmful outcomes, such as neglecting other life domains, addiction, and diminished autonomy. The content discusses how certain activities, including digital activities and high-risk behaviors, can trigger flow states that might have negative societal and personal impacts. The lesson also examines the link between flow and dissociation, noting that extreme cases of flow can mimic psychopathological phenomena. Researchers emphasize the importance of evaluating the broader context and potential costs of flow-inducing activities, suggesting that disengagement from such activities might be necessary for some individuals.
