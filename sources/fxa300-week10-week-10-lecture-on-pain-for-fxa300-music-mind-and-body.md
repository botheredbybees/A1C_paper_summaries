## Week 10 Lecture on Pain for FXA300 Music, Mind and Body

The lecture discusses various physical and mental health issues affecting musicians, focusing on pain and its implications in the field of music performance. It covers musculoskeletal pain, orofacial disorders, repetitive strain injuries, and carpal tunnel syndrome, highlighting the unique challenges faced by musicians due to prolonged and repetitive motions. The lecture also explores the impact of hearing loss and tinnitus, noting that musicians, especially those who play amplified instruments, are at a higher risk of developing these conditions. Furthermore, the lecture addresses the psychological impacts of performance anxiety, including its physiological and psychological symptoms, and the relationship between performance anxiety and the Yerkes-Dodson law, which suggests that anxiety can be reduced with proper preparation and rehearsal. The lecture concludes with an overview of research findings on music performance anxiety among students in Spanish conservatories, emphasizing the importance of understanding and addressing these issues to improve the overall health and well-being of musicians.
