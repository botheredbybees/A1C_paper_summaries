## FXA303 Web Lecture Week 11

The document provides details for Assessment Task 3 and Assessment Task 4 in the FXA303 course at the University of Tasmania, focusing on peer discussions and an essay on enhancing mental wellbeing through creative arts and play. For Assessment Task 3, students must contribute regularly to online discussion forums, demonstrating knowledge of trauma-informed care principles. The task is graded based on regularity of posts and quality of contributions. Assessment Task 4 requires synthesizing interdisciplinary knowledge to analyze practices aimed at improving mental health for younger people. Students are expected to critically evaluate arts-based programs and argue for adaptations to enhance outcomes in a specific cohort, applying scholarly research to support their arguments. The document also outlines guidelines for using generative AI tools responsibly during the course of completing these assignments.
