## Neurologic Music Therapy Training for Mobility and Stability Rehabilitation with Parkinson’s Disease – A Pilot Study

This pilot study evaluates the efficacy of Neurologic Music Therapy (NMT) techniques in improving gait and balance in patients with Parkinson’s Disease (PD). The study hypothesizes that the combination of Rhythmic Auditory Stimulation (RAS), Patterned Sensory Enhancement (PSE), and Therapeutic Instrumental Music Performance (TIMP) can enhance spatiotemporal gait parameters and postural stability. Fifty-five PD patients, divided into experimental and control groups, underwent optoelectrical 3D movement analysis and computerized dynamic posturography before and after a 4-week therapy cycle. The experimental group participated in NMT sessions four times a week, while the control group maintained their usual activities. The results indicated significant improvements in gait parameters and balance for the experimental group compared to the control group, suggesting that NMT can be a valuable therapeutic approach for PD rehabilitation.
