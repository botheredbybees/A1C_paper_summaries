## The Employment of Art Therapy to Develop Empathy and Foster Well-being for Junior Doctors in a Palliative Medicine Rotation: A Qualitative Exploratory Study on Acceptability

This paper discusses a qualitative exploratory study assessing the acceptability of an innovative art therapy-focused educational initiative among junior doctors during palliative care rotations in Singapore's National Cancer Centre. The study aimed to cultivate empathy and promote well-being through sessions involving collage making, group reflection, and sharing artistic creations. Key findings include mixed perceptions regarding program acceptability due to factors such as systemic barriers and a lack of pre-existing knowledge about the humanities among participants.
