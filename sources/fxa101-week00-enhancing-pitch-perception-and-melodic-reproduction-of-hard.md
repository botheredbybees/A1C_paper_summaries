## Enhancing Pitch Perception and Melodic Reproduction of Hard-of-Hearing Individuals Using a Vibrotactile Glove

This study examines the use of a vibrotactile glove, TTSþ, designed to assist hard-of-hearing individuals in enhancing their pitch perception and melodic reproduction skills through haptic feedback. The device converts musical tones into corresponding vibrotactile patterns and is supported by an associated training application. Post-training assessments reveal notable improvements in participants' pitch perception and singing abilities compared with pre-test baselines. User experiences during training sessions and interviews offer valuable insights, highlighting the potential of such wearable systems for auditory rehabilitation.
