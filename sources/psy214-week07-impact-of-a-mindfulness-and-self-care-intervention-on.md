## Impact of a mindfulness and self-care intervention on stress, self-compassion, and attentiveness in university healthcare students and faculty

This study evaluated a 12-week online intervention incorporating mindfulness and self-care practices among university healthcare students and faculty to assess its impact on stress, self-compassion, attentiveness, and physical activity levels. The findings revealed significant increases in mindful attention awareness, days of mindfulness practice, and self-compassion among participants, who also reported lower perceived stress levels and improved mental health outcomes. Notably, higher engagement in mindfulness practices was negatively correlated with stress and positively linked to self-care activities. This intervention demonstrated both effectiveness and feasibility, indicating its potential for mitigating psychological distress in healthcare education settings.
