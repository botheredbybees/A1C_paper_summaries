## A Brief Report on Reviews of Existing Creative Art–Based Interventions in Dementia Care From 2010–2020

This brief report provides an overview of reviews conducted on creative art-based interventions for dementia care from 2010 to 2020, focusing on the benefits and limitations found in these studies. The report identifies that creative arts interventions have been increasingly used as non-pharmacological methods to manage symptoms, improve well-being, and enhance quality of life for individuals with dementia. Key findings suggest that while visual arts, music, dance, and theater/drama offer tangible benefits, there is a lack of comprehensive reviews focusing on theater and museum-based interventions. The report highlights the importance of creativity in fostering autonomy and engagement among people with dementia despite their cognitive decline. It underscores the need for further research to address gaps identified by previous studies, such as the efficacy of certain art forms and potential long-term benefits.
