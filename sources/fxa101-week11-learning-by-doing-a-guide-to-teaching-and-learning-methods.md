## Learning by Doing: A Guide to Teaching and Learning Methods

The document 'Learning by Doing: A Guide to Teaching and Learning Methods' by Graham Gibbs is a seminal work in the field of experiential learning, particularly influential in education, healthcare, and staff development. Gibbs outlines a reflective learning cycle which involves describing, feeling, evaluating, analyzing, concluding, and action planning to link theory and practice through practical experiences. The guide emphasizes the importance of experiential methods in teaching and learning, providing concrete examples and case studies of their application. Gibbs' work encourages educators to move beyond traditional teaching approaches and embrace experiential learning to enhance critical reflection and the practical application of knowledge in various professional settings.
