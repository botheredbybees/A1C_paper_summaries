## Beethoven's Late Period: A Musical Transformation

The late period of Beethoven’s musical compositions is characterized by a shift from clear dramatic narratives to a more poetic and improvisatory style. This transformation was influenced by his increasing deafness, which made him rely on an inner monologue for composition. Additionally, Romantic-era critiques like E.T. A. Hoffmann provided new perspectives on Beethoven's own work. Despite these influences, the late period is not entirely a break from earlier styles; rather, it intensified contrasts and complexities while also achieving simplicity in its most profound forms.
