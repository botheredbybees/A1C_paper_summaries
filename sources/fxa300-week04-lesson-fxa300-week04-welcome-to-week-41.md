## How the Brain Helps Us Process Music

In this week's lesson on sensation and perception within cognition, Dr Brendan Lamb introduces core concepts related to how we process music. The content covers bottom-up and top-down processing mechanisms that influence our perception of pitch, intervals, tonality, and rhythm in musical sounds. Additionally, the course explores specific regions of the brain involved in these processes, referencing research from MIT neuroscientists who have identified neural populations selectively responding to categorically musical sounds.
