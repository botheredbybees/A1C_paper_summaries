## Positive Psychology 1: Concepts, Models, and Interventions

This lesson introduces positive psychology, focusing on its concepts, models, and interventions. It contrasts positive psychology with positive thinking and discusses the influence of life circumstances, genes, and personal actions on well-being. The lesson also covers gratitude and acts of kindness as positive interventions and explores the effectiveness of these interventions. Students are encouraged to reflect on their understanding of positive psychology and its impact on their daily lives.
