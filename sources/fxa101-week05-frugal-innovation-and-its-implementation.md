## Frugal Innovation and Its Implementation

This book, 'Frugal Innovation and Its Implementation,' edited by Nivedita Agarwal and Alexander Brem, explores the concept of frugal innovation as a means to address constraints in resource availability and foster innovations tailored for emerging markets. It highlights how frugal innovation can create faster and more affordable solutions compared to traditional approaches. The book delves into various aspects such as the evolution of frugal innovation, its characteristics, challenges, engineering and development processes, and global perspectives including industry 4.0 impacts. Key areas include customer awareness, sustainability, quality assurance in frugal products, social impact, local adaptations, digitalization effects, and policy implications for scaling up frugal innovations globally. This work is significant for understanding how creativity and resourcefulness can drive positive change in health and creative arts contexts by addressing global challenges with innovative yet economical solutions.
