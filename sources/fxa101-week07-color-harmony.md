## Color Harmony

Kenneth E. Burchett's article 'Color Harmony' explores the concept of color harmony, a phenomenon where colors produce a pleasing affective response when seen together. The study identifies key attributes and related terms used to describe color harmony through a content analysis of color literature. These attributes include order, tone, configuration, interaction, similarity, association, attitude, and area. The article clarifies that color harmony is a complex and multifaceted concept, with various interpretations and applications across different fields such as art and psychology. It also highlights the importance of understanding color harmony for artists and the creative arts, emphasizing the need for a clear model to explain this concept. The research contributes to the understanding of color theory and its relevance to creative practices in the visual arts and health-related fields.
