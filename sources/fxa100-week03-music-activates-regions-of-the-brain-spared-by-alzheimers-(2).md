## Music Activates Regions of the Brain Spared by Alzheimer’s Disease

This press release from University of Utah Health describes a study that investigates the impact of music on brain activity in dementia patients. The research highlights the salience network, which remains relatively intact even as Alzheimer’s disease progresses and disrupts other cognitive functions. By playing familiar music to dementia patients, researchers observed increased functional connectivity across various brain networks including the visual, executive, cerebellar, and corticocerebellar regions. These findings suggest that personalized music programs can enhance engagement and potentially alleviate anxiety in dementia patients. Despite promising results, the study notes limitations such as a small sample size and a single imaging session per patient. The researchers emphasize that while music may not cure Alzheimer’s disease, it could improve quality of life and reduce care costs for affected individuals.
