## Defining frugal innovation: a critical review

This article critically reviews the concept of frugal innovation (FI), which aims to develop affordable solutions in resource-constrained environments for low-income consumers. The authors discuss the proliferation of diverse definitions and conceptualizations of FI, noting a lack of theoretical underpinning in many cases. Despite this ambiguity, there is consensus on core characteristics such as affordability, adaptability, sustainability, and accessibility. They suggest that defining FI should focus more on its practical implementation rather than on developing a unified definition, emphasizing the importance of comparing FI solutions to incumbent alternatives for clarity. The review also highlights the potential benefits of FI in fostering innovation and sustainable practices across various sectors including healthcare, manufacturing, food, automotive, energy, and academia.
