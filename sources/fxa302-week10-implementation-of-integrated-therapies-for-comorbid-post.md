## Implementation of integrated therapies for comorbid post-traumatic stress disorder and substance use disorders in community substance abuse treatment programs

The article reviews challenges and barriers to implementing evidence-based integrated therapies for comorbid post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and substance use disorders (SUDs) in community substance abuse treatment programs. It highlights the high prevalence of PTSD among SUD patients, emphasizing the need for integrated treatments to address both conditions simultaneously. Common barriers include organizational policies, funding limitations, and provider and patient attitudes towards new therapies. The article discusses trauma-informed care approaches and outlines several integrated therapy models, such as COPE, that combine exposure therapy with cognitive behavioral therapy for SUD. These treatments show promising results in reducing PTSD and SUD symptoms, leading to better long-term recovery outcomes. Understanding and addressing these barriers is crucial for the adoption and implementation of effective, integrated treatments in community settings.
