## “Sound Health: Music and the Mind”: The New Initiative of the National Institutes of Health and National Endowment for the Arts

This editorial discusses the 'Sound Health: Music and the Mind' initiative, a collaborative effort between the National Institutes of Health (NIH) and the National Endowment for the Arts aimed at exploring music's impact on brain health. The article highlights how music therapy has evolved since World War II and its diverse clinical applications across various conditions including anxiety, psychotic symptoms, poor sleep quality, and depression. It emphasizes that while music therapy shows promising effects, more research is needed to understand its mechanisms and efficacy for different conditions. The initiative seeks to bring together experts in neuroscience, healthcare, and music therapy to discover new knowledge about the brain's response to music and to explore optimal applications of musical interventions in health care settings.
