## R3. THE TRIUMVIRATE OF THEORY, RESEARCH, AND PRACTICE

The document discusses the interplay between theory, research, and practice in the field of music psychology, education, and therapy. It highlights the philosophical underpinnings guiding researchers like Leonard Meyer and Alf Gabrielsson, who represent opposing views on the meaning of music. The text emphasizes the need for clearer philosophical alignment in research and practice. It also addresses the gap between these three components, particularly noting the disconnect in music therapy and music education. The authors suggest that while research may not directly impact practice, its influence can be seen through indirect channels such as graduate students, in-service teachers, and faculty integration of findings. This interconnection is crucial for fostering growth in theoretical development and practical application within the field of music.
