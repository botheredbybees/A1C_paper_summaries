## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience, authored by Bradley R. Postle, is a comprehensive resource on cognitive neuroscience, specifically focusing on the South Shore of Long Island's densely populated environment and the cognitive processes involved in decision-making. The text delves into the detailed mapping of cognitive functions across various brain regions, particularly highlighting the significance of the medial frontal cortex and subcortical structures in decision-making processes. It covers key concepts such as perceptual decision-making, the influence of social interactions, and the role of different brain areas like the lateral intraparietal (LIP) region in cognitive tasks. The book also explores the nuances of value estimation, the common currency concept in decision-making, and the implications of neuroeconomic research on understanding economic behaviors. It further discusses the differences in foraging decisions compared to perceptual and value-based decisions, emphasizing the role of specific brain areas in these processes. This text is relevant to the creative arts and health disciplines by providing insights into the neural mechanisms underlying human decision-making and cognitive functions, which can inform the creation and interpretation of artworks that reflect human cognitive processes.
