## CBC News: The National - Lung-Lung and Maxim Londo's Concert

This transcript from CBC News features an interview with renowned classical pianist Lung-Lung and his 15-year-old protege, Maxim Londo, discussing Lung-Lung's recovery from tendinitis and the upcoming special performance where Londo will assist on the keyboard. The transcript also highlights the challenges artists face due to chronic pain and disabling injuries, and introduces Dr. Chase McMurran's unique clinic in Canada that offers specialized care for artists. The clinic's importance is underscored by the story of drummer Steve Thomas, who was diagnosed with dystonia after years of repetitive drumming and successfully rehabilitated, emphasizing the need for tailored healthcare for performers.
