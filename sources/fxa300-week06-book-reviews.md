## Book Reviews

The article presents a review of the book 'Reflections on human potential: Bridging the person-centred approach and positive psychology' edited by B.E. Levitt. The review highlights the book's comprehensive coverage of the actualizing tendency, which is central to Carl Rogers’ theories, but emphasizes that it does not effectively bridge person-centered approaches with positive psychology as its title suggests. Instead, the book predominantly reclaims ground for person-centred theory through a variety of perspectives and critiques. It includes historical discussions on the actualizing tendency’s connection to positive psychology, theoretical explorations by known practitioners, and contemporary applications focusing on personal development and holistic health. The review praises the clarity with which Levitt synthesizes current thinking and challenges traditional views on mental illness. Each section builds upon previous chapters, encouraging readers to question their preconceptions about human potential and well-being.
