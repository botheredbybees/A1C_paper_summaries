## Dementia and the Power of Music Therapy

The article explores the ethical and therapeutic benefits of music therapy for dementia patients, emphasizing its restorative impact on social agency. Dementia is characterized by a gradual loss of narrative agency, which affects one's ability to engage meaningfully in relationships and activities. Music therapy, due to its low cost and practicality, offers a potent way to temporarily restore these lost capacities. By triggering long-term memories and fostering interaction between patients and caregivers, music therapy can enhance well-being and the quality of life for those suffering from dementia. The author highlights the importance of understanding how music therapy works not just on a neuropsychological level but also in its social dimensions, suggesting that such interventions are crucial in addressing the ethical implications of care for individuals with severe cognitive impairments.
