## Sensory Stimulation and Music Therapy Programs for Treating Disorders of Consciousness

This article discusses sensory stimulation and music therapy as potential treatments for disorders of consciousness resulting from severe brain injuries. The authors argue that sensory stimulation programs, based on the concept of environmental enrichment, aim to enhance brain plasticity and recovery in patients with brain injuries. Despite theoretical benefits, the efficacy of sensory stimulation programs has been debated due to methodological limitations in previous studies. Recent research indicates that sensory stimulation may have a positive impact on the recovery of consciousness, as demonstrated by behavioral and neuroimaging evidence. Additionally, the article explores the use of music therapy, which leverages live music to engage patients' cognitive functions and increase arousal. Studies suggest that music therapy can be a promising alternative or complement to traditional sensory stimulation programs in facilitating the recovery of consciousness. The authors emphasize the need for further research to better understand the mechanisms and potential benefits of these therapeutic approaches.
