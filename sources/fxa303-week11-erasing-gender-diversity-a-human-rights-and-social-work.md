## Erasing Gender Diversity: A Human Rights and Social Work Perspective on Trump’s Executive Order

Karun Karki and Hannah Kia critically analyze the implications of Donald Trump’s executive order that restricts recognition of gender identities beyond male and female. The authors argue that such a policy undermines fundamental human rights and personal autonomy for transgender, non-binary, and other gender-diverse individuals. They cite the U.S. Supreme Court's ruling in Bostock v. Clayton County (2020) and the proposed Equality Act as recent strides towards protecting LGBTQ+ individuals from discrimination. However, Trump’s statement poses significant challenges to these advancements by institutionalizing a binary conception of sex and gender that disregards diverse identities. The article highlights how such policies exacerbate mental health issues within marginalized communities and foster social exclusion. It also examines the ethical dilemmas faced by social workers committed to inclusivity and equity in their practice. Overall, the piece underscores the importance of recognizing the complexity of human identity and protecting the dignity and rights of all individuals regardless of gender identity.
