## FXA303 Week 5 Welcome

This document is the welcome text for FXA303 Week 5, introducing Module 2 which focuses on creative play and arts-based activities to promote healing and mental wellbeing. The module emphasizes hands-on activities that explore various puppet-making techniques, with a focus on how puppets can convey non-verbal communication and address sensitive topics through storytelling. It highlights the role of puppetry in entertainment and education, touching on its historical development from the Bronze Age to modern times. Additionally, the text provides tips for self-care when dealing with vicarious trauma while engaging empathetically with traumatic narratives.
