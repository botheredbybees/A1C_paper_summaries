## Mindful Attitudes Full Page Slides

This document provides an overview of mindful attitudes, emphasizing the expansion of mindfulness beyond mere attention to the present moment. The text highlights several key attitudes that foster mindfulness, including attention/awareness, intention, and attitude. It further elaborates on beginner’s mind, which involves suspending preconceived notions and embracing a fresh perspective. This attitude contrasts with the expert’s mind, which tends to narrow possibilities due to entrenched knowledge. The document also discusses non-judgment, which is distinct from simply being non-judgmental. It involves observing and suspending automatic judgments while maintaining the capacity for sensible judgments. Acceptance is another crucial attitude, encouraging openness to both pleasant and unpleasant experiences without passively resigning oneself to them. The concept of letting go is explored, focusing on releasing attachments to judgments, situations, and especially to the desire for control, permanence, and predictability. Non-striving is also introduced, emphasizing the importance of not aiming to achieve anything specific but rather focusing on being present without any agenda or expectation of a particular outcome. These attitudes collectively form a comprehensive framework for mindfulness, which is particularly relevant to stress reduction and self-care in the creative arts and health contexts.
