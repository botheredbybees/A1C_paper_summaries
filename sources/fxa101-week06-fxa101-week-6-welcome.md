## FXA101 Week 6 Welcome

This document is a welcome message for students in the FXA101 course, focusing on the sixth week of the semester. The instructor encourages engagement with new ideas and skills related to the creative project mini-projects. Students are advised to conduct research to inform their design process, including exploring techniques and materials, while documenting these efforts in their journals. The message highlights strategies for overcoming challenges such as 'creative blocks' by sharing insights from other creatives who have experienced similar issues. Additionally, it emphasizes the importance of developing creative thinking patterns through posed questions and discussions. Students are reminded that help is available via tutor sessions or online forums.
