## Electrotactile Adaptation on the Abdomen: Preliminary Results

This paper explores electrotactile adaptation by examining changes in sensation thresholds during and after moderately intense conditioning stimuli. The study uses a computer-controlled system to deliver electrotactile pulses through abdominal skin, with variations in current, burst repetition rate (BRR), and number of pulses per burst (NPB). Seven observers participated, each completing three sessions focusing on different waveform parameters. Results indicate that adaptation is rapid and complete within approximately 15 minutes, with threshold elevation depending on the intensity of conditioning stimuli. The paper concludes by suggesting further research into electrotactile communication methods.
