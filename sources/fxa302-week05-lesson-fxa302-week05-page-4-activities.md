## Week 5 Activities: Perspectives of Arts on Health and Wellbeing

This week's activities focus on the relationship between music and disability, particularly hearing loss. The course material includes a reading by Joseph Straus from 'The Health Humanities Reader' (Jones et al., 2019). Students are encouraged to discuss methods of enhancing musical engagement for individuals with significant hearing impairments, including the use of modern technologies. Additional activities include an online discussion, a web lecture, and a quiz covering weeks 1-4 materials. Assessment Task 2 involves creating a poster on a creative work that references health or wellbeing.
