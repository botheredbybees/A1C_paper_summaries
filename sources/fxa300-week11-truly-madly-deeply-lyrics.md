## Truly Madly Deeply lyrics

The provided text contains the lyrics of the song 'Truly Madly Deeply,' which express a deep and romantic commitment. The lyrics describe the singer's desire to be the dream, wish, and fantasy of their partner, pledging to be strong and faithful.
