## Incongruity Without Novelty: Perfect Joke and Reinterpretation

The paper explores the concept of perfect jokes within stand-up comedy and their relation to incongruity theory in humor studies. It examines how these jokes, characterized by near-identity between setup and punchline, challenge traditional views on humor generation. The author argues that perfect jokes rely on reinterpretation rather than introducing new semantic content, supporting a resolution-centered understanding of incongruity theory.
