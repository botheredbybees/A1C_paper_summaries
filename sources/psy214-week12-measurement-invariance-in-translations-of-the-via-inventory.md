## Measurement Invariance in Translations of the VIA Inventory of Strengths

The study evaluates the cross-cultural relevance of the VIA Classification of Character Strengths and Virtues by assessing measurement invariance in translations of the VIA Inventory of Strengths (VIA-IS). Using a sample of 15,540 individuals from 16 nations, the research applies multigroup confirmatory factor analysis and alignment analysis to investigate the consistency of the model across different cultural contexts. The findings support configural and metric invariance, with scalar invariance demonstrated under certain conditions. This study lends credibility to the VIA Classification's cross-cultural applicability and the validity of its translated versions, reinforcing the notion that character strengths recognized in Western cultures are also valued in diverse global contexts.
