## The Effectiveness of Art Therapy for Anxiety in Adults: A Systematic Review

This systematic review evaluates the effectiveness of art therapy (AT) in reducing anxiety symptoms among adults through a comprehensive analysis of randomized and non-randomized controlled trials published between 1997 and October 2017. The study identifies only three relevant publications out of 776 hits, all with high risk of bias. Findings suggest that AT may be effective for pre-exam anxiety in students and possibly helpful for reducing pre-release anxiety among prisoners. However, due to the limited evidence base, no strong conclusions can yet be drawn regarding AT's effectiveness for treating general anxiety disorders or specific anxiety conditions.
