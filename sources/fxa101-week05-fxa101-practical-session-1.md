## FXA101 – Practical Session 1

The document outlines the importance of creative endeavors for cognitive and emotional health benefits as discussed by Marshall & Rose (2024) and Ivancovsky et al. (2023). It emphasizes that engaging in novel or unfamiliar creative activities can enhance problem-solving skills, activate semantic networks, and generate optimal stress. Reflexivity is introduced as a continuous examination process for insights into personal development and decision-making. The text also touches upon the concept of directed attention fatigue (DAF), which occurs after prolonged periods of concentration, leading to decreased cognitive performance. To mitigate DAF, the document suggests incorporating creative practices that can build up cognitive reserve and executive functions. Additionally, it highlights the role of light color temperature in reducing fatigue. Various creativity games are suggested to stimulate different aspects of thinking processes and encourage group collaboration.
