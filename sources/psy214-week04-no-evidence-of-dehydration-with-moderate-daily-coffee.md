## No Evidence of Dehydration with Moderate Daily Coffee Intake: A Counterbalanced Cross-Over Study in a Free-Living Population

This study investigates the impact of moderate coffee consumption on hydration status in male adults, addressing the misconception that coffee causes dehydration. The research involved a counterbalanced cross-over design with 50 habitual coffee drinkers who consumed either coffee (containing 4 mg/kg caffeine) or water for three consecutive days each. Multiple markers of hydration were measured, including total body water, urine volume, and hematological indicators. Results indicated no significant differences in hydration status between the coffee and water groups across various measures. The study concludes that moderate daily coffee intake does not lead to dehydration and provides similar hydrating qualities to water for caffeine-tolerant individuals.
