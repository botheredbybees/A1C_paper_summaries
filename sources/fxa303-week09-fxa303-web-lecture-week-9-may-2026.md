## FXA303 Web Lecture Week 9, May 2026

This document provides a comprehensive guide to the ninth week of the FXA303 Creative Arts and Mental Wellbeing module at the University of Tasmania for the year 2026. It includes information on assessment tasks, particularly Task 4, which involves researching specific cohorts of young people facing mental health challenges and exploring creative arts interventions designed to improve their wellbeing. The lecture also highlights the importance of trauma-informed practices in creative arts programs, focusing on how these activities can facilitate emotional expression and processing, especially after traumatic events like disasters. A case study from Christchurch following a series of earthquakes is used to illustrate how community-based art projects can serve as therapeutic tools for healing and resilience building among children and young people affected by trauma. The lecture emphasizes the need for safety, trust, choice, collaboration, empowerment, and respect for diversity in designing such programs.
