## The Arts as a Medium for Care and Self-Care in Dementia: Arguments and Evidence

The article argues that arts-based interventions can significantly benefit individuals with dementia by addressing their psychosocial needs and enhancing quality of life, particularly in the absence of effective pharmacological treatments. The study aligns with Interdem's manifesto, emphasizing dignity, autonomy, reciprocity, lack of stigma, and social integration through the use of visual art, music, and dance. It outlines three dimensions for defining arts interventions: intervention level (individual, relationship-based, or organizational), severity of dementia, and facilitator expertise. The author discusses specific benefits such as cognitive, emotional, and social outcomes, supported by a review of existing research on these activities in dementia care contexts. This work highlights the need for further development and evaluation to enhance the uptake of arts-based interventions in dementia care.
