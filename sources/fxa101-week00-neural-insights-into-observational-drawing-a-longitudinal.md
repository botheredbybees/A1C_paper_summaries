## Neural insights into observational drawing: A longitudinal resting state functional connectivity study using 7 t MRI

This longitudinal study explored the neural mechanisms underlying observational drawing through resting-state functional connectivity (FC) using high-resolution MRI scans on undergraduate art and non-art students over a 16-week period. The research found that sustained engagement in observational drawing led to significant brain plasticity, specifically enhanced FC within and between cerebellar, default mode, and salience networks. These findings provide insights into the neural adaptations supporting artistic abilities and suggest potential therapeutic applications for neurological disorders.
