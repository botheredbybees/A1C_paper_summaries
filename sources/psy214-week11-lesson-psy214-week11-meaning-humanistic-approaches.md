## Meaning in Life: History and Recent Approaches

This lesson explores the concept of meaning in life within psychological models such as Martin Seligman's PERMA and Ryff's six-factor model. It discusses the historical roots of studying meaning in life, particularly highlighting Viktor Frankl's work. The lesson then delves into a more recent model proposed by Martela and Steger, which divides meaning into coherence, purpose, and significance. Coherence refers to the comprehensibility of life, purpose involves having direction and goals, and significance relates to the value and worth of life. The lesson also emphasizes the importance of multiple sources of meaning and the shared nature of these sources among individuals, noting that finding meaning in common experiences and relationships is both normal and valid.
