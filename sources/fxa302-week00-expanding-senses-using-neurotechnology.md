## Expanding Senses using Neurotechnology

This book provides a comprehensive overview of brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) and their applications in neurotechnology. It covers various technologies and methodologies for direct brain-to-device communication, including chapters on stroke rehabilitation, spinal cord injury rehabilitation, and communication BCIs for individuals with motor impairments. The text also delves into the ethical, legal, and social implications of neurotechnology, and explores future directions in the field, emphasizing the potential for neural enhancement and the societal impacts of these advancements.
