## Meta-Analysis of Action Video Game Impact on Perceptual, Attentional, and Cognitive Skills

The study presents a meta-analysis of the impact of action video games on perceptual, attentional, and cognitive skills. The researchers focused on cross-sectional studies documenting the cognitive profiles of habitual players and intervention studies exploring causal impacts through playing these games from 2000 to 2015. They found that regular play positively affects cognition with an average effect size of about half a standard deviation (g = 0.55) in cross-sectional studies, while intervention studies showed a slightly smaller effect size (g = 0.34). The analysis highlighted specific domains such as top-down attention and spatial cognition where action games have robust positive impacts. However, the authors caution against publication bias and suggest the need for larger cohort studies and more intervention studies to confirm findings. This research is significant given the widespread use of video games globally and their potential in cognitive training applications.
