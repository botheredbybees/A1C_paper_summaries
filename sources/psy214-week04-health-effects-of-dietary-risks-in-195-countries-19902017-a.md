## Health Effects of Dietary Risks in 195 Countries, 1990–2017: A Systematic Analysis for the Global Burden of Disease Study 2017

This study evaluates the consumption of major foods and nutrients across 195 countries to quantify the impact of suboptimal dietary intake on non-communicable disease (NCD) mortality and morbidity. The research, part of the Global Burden of Diseases, Injuries, and Risk Factors Study (GBD) 2017, utilized geographically representative data from various sources to estimate dietary intake among adults aged 25 years or older. Key findings indicate that 11 million deaths and 255 million disability-adjusted life-years were attributable to suboptimal diet in 2017, with high sodium intake, low whole grain consumption, and insufficient fruit intake being the leading risk factors globally. The study highlights the need for improving dietary habits across nations and underscores the importance of evidence-based interventions to mitigate NCD-related health burdens.
