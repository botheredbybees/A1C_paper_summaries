## Community arts, identity and recovery: A realist review of how community-based arts activities enables the identity change recovery process from serious mental illness

This article explores how community arts activities contribute to the recovery process of individuals with serious mental illness (SMI) through identity change. The review, conducted between January and September 2021, synthesizes findings from 22 articles and reports to develop a theory on mechanisms involved in this process. It identifies that a safe and empowering context is crucial for activating key pathways: feeling in control of SMI through coping, achieving acceptance through connectedness, and overcoming personal challenges. These pathways foster positive self-awareness, enabling individuals to redefine their identity beyond the label of SMI. The review integrates theories such as Social Identity Approach to Health and Transformative Learning Theory to explain these mechanisms. It highlights that community arts can facilitate a holistic approach to recovery, encompassing both social and personal identities.
