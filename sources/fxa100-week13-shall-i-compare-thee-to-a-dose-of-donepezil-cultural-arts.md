## Shall I Compare Thee to a Dose of Donepezil?: Cultural Arts Interventions in Dementia Care Research

This article explores the potential of cultural arts interventions in dementia care, contrasting them with pharmacologic and psychosocial approaches. It reviews findings from systematic and integrative studies and highlights that while individual cultural arts intervention studies often report positive outcomes, they are frequently excluded from systematic reviews due to methodological weaknesses such as small sample sizes and lack of randomization. The authors suggest that the unique challenges posed by the subjective nature of cultural arts interventions necessitate a rethinking of research methodologies aimed at capturing meaningful personal experiences rather than just measurable changes. They advocate for moving beyond traditional clinical trial models to better understand the benefits of personalized cultural arts activities in enhancing quality of life and managing symptoms for individuals with dementia.
