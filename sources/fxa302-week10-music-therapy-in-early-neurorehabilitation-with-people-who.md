## Music Therapy in Early Neurorehabilitation with People Who Have Experienced Traumatic Brain Injury

Simon Gilbertson's research study explores the clinical changes observed in music improvisation therapy as part of early neurorehabilitation for individuals with severe traumatic brain injury (TBI). The study employs qualitative methods, including therapeutic narrative analysis and musicological analysis, to identify significant changes in musical expression, communication, agency, emotionality, motility, and participation linked to musical interventions. Gilbertson's work highlights the importance of relationship-building in early neurorehabilitation and contributes to the understanding of the rehabilitative process through music therapy. This study underscores the potential of music therapy in fostering adaptation and integration into society for individuals recovering from TBI, emphasizing the role of music in facilitating social and emotional recovery.
