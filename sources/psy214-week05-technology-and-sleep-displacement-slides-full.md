## Technology and Sleep Displacement Slides Full

The document discusses the impact of technology on sleep displacement within the context of stress, self-care, and mindfulness. It highlights how digital environments are designed to promote prolonged engagement through various mechanisms such as positive reinforcement, intermittent rewards, social validation, negative reinforcement for unpleasant feelings like boredom or loneliness, and numerous cues to engage users continuously without natural stopping points. The presentation includes an example from Netflix's CEO who acknowledges that the company competes with sleep by drawing viewers into extended screen time late at night. Strategies suggested to manage this include maintaining good sleep hygiene practices such as keeping devices out of the bedroom, buffering screen activity before bedtime, and making screens less engaging (e.g., disabling autoplay and notifications). This displacement effect is particularly relevant in today's digital age where technology often encroaches upon personal time dedicated to rest and relaxation.
