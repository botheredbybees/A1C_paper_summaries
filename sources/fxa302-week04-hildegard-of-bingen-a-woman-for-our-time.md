## Hildegard of Bingen: A Woman for our Time

June Boyce-Tillman's article explores the significance of twelfth-century abbess Hildegard of Bingen in contemporary times. Boyce-Tillman argues that Hildegard offers a way of knowing that has been marginalized by post-Enlightenment values, emphasizing the need for balance between reason and emotion, individual freedom and community, among others. The article highlights Hildegard's holistic view of life, where relationships are central and interconnected with all aspects of existence. Through her music, theology, and artistic expressions, Hildegard exemplifies a model of connected knowing that fosters collaboration, intuition, expressivity, and healing. This perspective challenges dominant cultural narratives and offers insights relevant to creative arts and health by promoting inclusivity, holistic well-being, and the integration of diverse perspectives.
