## Social benefits of Arts engagement and evidence on arts-health relationship

This web lecture by Dr Brendan Lamb for the course FXA301 Arts in the Community explores the social and health benefits of arts engagement. It highlights research indicating that participation in the arts can positively impact mental and physical well-being, cognitive skills, self-esteem, mood, and social cohesion. The lecture cites studies such as Daykin (2017), Zarobe & Bungay (2017), Saarikallio et al. (2020), and Davies et al. (2016) to demonstrate the role of arts in enhancing mental health, developing resilience, and fostering community pride. Additionally, it discusses the economic benefits of arts engagement and its potential as a form of social prescribing. The lecture emphasizes the need for robust methodologies in future research designs to validate these claims further.
