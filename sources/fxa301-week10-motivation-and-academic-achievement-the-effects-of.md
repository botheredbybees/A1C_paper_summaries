## Motivation and Academic Achievement: The Effects of Personality Traits and the Quality of Experience

This chapter explores motivational and personality factors influencing academic performance, particularly among gifted students. It contrasts cognitive approaches to learning with the importance of intrinsic motivation, task commitment, and emotional engagement in educational success. The authors discuss how long-term goals (extrinsic or intrinsically motivated) and immediate enjoyment (intrinsic rewards) impact achievement. They find that while both are essential for outstanding academic performance, a balance is necessary as excessive focus on either can be detrimental. Furthermore, the chapter examines the role of intrinsic motivation and task commitment in predicting scholastic success.
