## Inattentional deafness in music

This study investigates the phenomenon of inattentional deafness in music, where participants fail to perceive unexpected auditory events when their attention is focused on a specific aspect of a musical piece. The research is conducted through two studies involving participants listening to Richard Strauss' Thus Spake Zarathustra and counting timpani beats. An unexpected guitar solo is introduced to test the level of inattentional deafness. Study 1 compares non-musicians and musicians to assess the impact of expertise, while Study 2 uses a more salient unexpected stimulus to explore the extent of the inattentional deafness effect. The findings demonstrate that inattentional deafness occurs even among musicians, highlighting the influence of attentional focus on auditory perception. This research offers insights into the parallels between inattentional blindness and inattentional deafness, emphasizing the role of attention in perceptual processes across different sensory domains.
