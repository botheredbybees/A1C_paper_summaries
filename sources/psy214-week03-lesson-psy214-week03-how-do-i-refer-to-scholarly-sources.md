## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: Referring to Scholarly Sources

This lesson from the PSY114 and PSY214 course focuses on the proper use of APA 7th edition referencing in academic work. It emphasizes the importance of acknowledging sources by providing in-text citations and full references when using someone else's ideas. The content includes an example of a journal article citation, guidance on accessing resources like the APA Academic Writer tutorial for understanding in-text citations and reference lists, and links to additional guides from UTAS library and Purdue University Online Writing Lab. It also highlights the importance of avoiding direct quotes in favor of paraphrasing or summarizing ideas, emphasizing that this approach better demonstrates personal knowledge and understanding.
