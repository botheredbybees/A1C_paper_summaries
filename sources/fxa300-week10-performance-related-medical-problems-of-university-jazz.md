## Performance-Related Medical Problems of University Jazz Studies Majors

The article explores the performance-related medical issues faced by university-level jazz studies majors. The research highlights that over a quarter of the participants reported experiencing such problems, with pain severity averaging 3.58 on a scale of 1 to 10. The study suggests that these issues are more prevalent among younger musicians and tend to diminish with age. Additionally, the problems were found to be specific to different instrument types. The article underscores the importance of addressing these health concerns, given the rigorous practice and performance schedules of jazz students, and the need for further research on this specific group within the broader field of musician health.
