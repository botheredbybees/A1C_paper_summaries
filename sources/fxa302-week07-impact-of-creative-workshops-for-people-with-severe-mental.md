## Impact of Creative Workshops for People with Severe Mental Health Problems

This paper evaluates the impact of creative workshops on individuals with severe mental illness (SMI). The study involves participants and observers who engage in art activities within a museum setting, focusing on psychological well-being, social connectivity, and subjective experiences. Key findings indicate that such workshops promote recovery by enhancing motivation, concentration, and connections among participants. The research highlights the transformative potential of creative practices to reshape perceptions of mental illness beyond clinical outcomes towards holistic health improvement. This contributes significantly to understanding how artistic interventions can foster resilience and well-being in individuals with SMI and their support networks.
