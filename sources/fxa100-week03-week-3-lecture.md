## Week 3 Lecture

The document provides an overview of the third week's lecture in the FXA100: The Arts and Dementia Care course, focusing on how music impacts cognitive function, emotional response, and memory among individuals with dementia. Key findings from two renowned programs—Music and Memory (started by Dan Cohen) and Power of Music and Dementia (research by Kevin Gavaghan)—are discussed, highlighting the benefits of personalized playlists and rhythmic entrainment in enhancing engagement and stimulating neural pathways. The lecture also delves into the emotional responses triggered by music through the mesolimbic pathway and dopamine release, emphasizing the role of memory associations with music. Additionally, it explores the importance of rhythm-based instruments for tactile stimulation and visual cues during dementia care sessions.
