## Age trends in musical preferences in adulthood: Sources of social influences as determinants of preferences

This article investigates how social influences affect age trends in musical preferences among adults using the Music Preferences in Adulthood Model (MPAM). The study explores two primary sources of influence: social networks (family and peers) and interpersonal dispositions such as conformity. Through two studies involving over 950 participants, it was found that these social factors significantly impact adult musical preferences, particularly concerning different musical dimensions like genres versus clips. Specifically, the authors discovered a moderating role for conformity in shaping age-related changes in music tastes. The findings underscore the importance of social influences, especially conformity tendencies, in forming and evolving musical preferences over time.
