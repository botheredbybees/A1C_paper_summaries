## Lifting Stress from the Day: A Women’s Well-Being Online Community Art Project

The article discusses a community engagement project initiated by the author, Suzy Tutchell, during the COVID-19 lockdown in 2020 to support vulnerable women through online art workshops. The primary goal was to create an inclusive and non-judgmental space where these women could engage in artmaking as a form of stress relief and empowerment. Drawing from her previous experience with similar projects on campus, Tutchell adapted the initiative to an online platform when face-to-face interactions became impossible due to lockdown measures.

The study highlights how hybrid 'spaces'—combining virtual and physical elements—can serve as pedagogical tools for fostering collective care and supportive environments. By facilitating weekly drop-in workshops, Tutchell observed significant shifts in participants’ subjectivities through situated art experiences. The emphasis was on providing a safe space where women could freely express themselves without fear of judgment or stigma.

Key findings suggest that such interventions not only offer immediate relief from daily stresses but also contribute to long-term empowerment and well-being among vulnerable populations. The article underscores the potential of online arts-based approaches in promoting inclusivity, mutual support, and holistic development within communities facing societal hardships.
