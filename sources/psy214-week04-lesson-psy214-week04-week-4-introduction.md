## Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: Lifestyle Approaches to Health and Well-being

This lesson introduces the theme of lifestyle approaches to self-care, focusing on healthy eating as a key aspect. It prompts students to reflect on how their personal lifestyle factors influence health and well-being. The week's topics include an overview of healthy eating guidelines, mental health implications, energy balance, and mindful consumption habits. Students are directed to consult the unit Reading List for required readings pertinent to their assessments.
