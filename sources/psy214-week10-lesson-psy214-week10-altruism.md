## Acts of Kindness in Positive Psychology

This lesson explores the concept of acts of kindness within the context of positive psychology interventions. It differentiates between random and targeted acts of kindness based on the recipient, and spontaneous and planned acts based on the timing. Random acts of kindness typically involve anonymous beneficiaries, while targeted acts are directed towards known individuals. The lesson also highlights the well-being benefits of performing acts of kindness, supported by research studies such as those by Elizabeth Dunn and colleagues, which demonstrate increased happiness when spending money on others. The content emphasizes the importance of acts of kindness as simple, one-off behaviors rather than ongoing commitments.
