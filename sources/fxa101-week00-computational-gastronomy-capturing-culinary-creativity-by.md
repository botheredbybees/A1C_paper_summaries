## Computational Gastronomy: Capturing Culinary Creativity by Making Food Computable

This paper introduces Computational Gastronomy, a novel approach to studying food and cooking using data-driven methods. It explores how computational techniques can be applied to recipes, flavor profiles, nutrition, health implications, and sustainability. The authors discuss the creation of databases like RecipeDB and FlavorDB which compile structured recipe and flavor data from various cuisines globally. They also delve into challenges such as the complexity in compiling rich culinary data across diverse cultural contexts and highlight the potential for personalized nutrition solutions derived from large datasets.
