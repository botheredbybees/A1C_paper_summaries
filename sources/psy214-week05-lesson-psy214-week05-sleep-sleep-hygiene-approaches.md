## Sleep Hygiene, Screen Use Impact on Sleep

This lesson covers the concept of sleep hygiene and its importance for maintaining good quality sleep. It discusses various habits that can improve sleep, such as creating a conducive sleeping environment and avoiding screens before bed due to their impact on melatonin production and mental stimulation. The content highlights evidence indicating that screen use can disrupt sleep through direct displacement of time, mental stimulation, and physiological disruptions caused by light exposure. Furthermore, it mentions problematic patterns of phone usage both immediately upon waking and before going to bed, emphasizing the need for healthier habits to improve overall sleep quality.
