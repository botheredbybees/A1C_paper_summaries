## Handmade Therapy: The Hedonic Impacts of Engaging in Pottery Making

This paper explores the hedonic psychological well-being impacts of engaging with clay, drawing on digital sensory ethnographic research and literature on clay therapy during the COVID-19 pandemic in Britain. The study adopts Material Engagement Theory (MET) to argue that the materiality of clay, its tactile properties, and malleability are central to shaping affective states through interactions between individuals, materials, and their environment. It highlights how engagements with clay can foster mindfulness, immersion, and relaxation, contributing positively to participants' hedonic well-being. The paper also examines the complexities involved in control over material processes and skill development.
