## Music Therapy: An Evidence-based Allied Health Service Applicable Through the Life Span

This chapter by Jane Edwards discusses the complexities and multifaceted nature of music therapy as an evidence-based allied health service applicable throughout the lifespan. It highlights the roles of the music therapist, who serves as a responder, communicator, and evaluator within therapeutic sessions. The context of music therapy practice and the populations served are key points of reference for effective communication and understanding. Edwards emphasizes the need for music therapists to navigate clients' initial impressions and support evolving verbal and musical responses skillfully. The chapter also addresses the importance of understanding the specific needs and characteristics of various client groups and the contexts in which music therapy services are provided. It underscores the significance of integrating contextual information with evidence-based practice to enhance therapeutic outcomes and prevent potential harm from misapplying techniques. The text illustrates how music therapy can be effectively implemented across diverse settings such as medical, developmental and educational, mental health, and community contexts, reflecting the broad applicability of the discipline in addressing varied human needs and enhancing well-being.
