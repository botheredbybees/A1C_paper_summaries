## Hildegard of Bingen and the Greening of Medieval Medicine

Victoria Sweet’s paper explores Hildegard of Bingen's contributions to medieval medicine, situating her work within the broader context of twelfth-century European medical practices and cultural developments. Sweet begins by providing an overview of Hildegard’s life and significant works, including her visionary writings and medical text Causae et curae. She highlights Hildegard’s innovative approach to medicine as a practice that integrates spiritual insights with practical knowledge, reflecting the interconnectedness between humans and nature in premodern European society. Sweet argues that Hildegard's medicine offers valuable insight into early medieval medical pluralism, providing a bridge between traditional Greco-Roman sources and local folk practices. By examining Hildegard’s unique perspective on health and healing, Sweet underscores her relevance to contemporary understandings of holistic approaches to healthcare within the creative arts and health disciplines.
