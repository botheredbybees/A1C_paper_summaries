## Enhancing communication between dementia care staff and their residents: an arts-inspired intervention

This research explores an arts-inspired intervention called 'Creative Conversations' aimed at enhancing communication between dementia care staff and residents in the United Kingdom. The study, conducted across fourteen care homes with twenty-eight participating staff members, utilized various artistic activities such as poetry, film, music, and art-making to facilitate skills development. Pre- and post-intervention qualitative data gathered through interviews revealed that staff initially found task-focused communication a barrier to building meaningful relationships with residents. Post-intervention themes highlighted the enhancement of communication skills through simplicity and subtlety, innovation in communication, strengthening non-verbal communication, creative approaches to care, professional introspection, and empathy development. The intervention validated staff's abilities, boosting confidence for spontaneous interactions that could be innovative and improvisational.
