## Interventions – Dementia Services Information and Development Centre

The document from the Dementia Services Information and Development Centre outlines various pharmacological and non-pharmacological interventions used to support individuals with dementia. Pharmacological interventions, including Acetylcholine Inhibitors and Memantine, aim to address symptoms and slow cognitive decline, though evidence for improved quality of life is limited. Non-pharmacological interventions such as Cognitive Stimulation Therapy (CST), Reminiscence Therapy, and physical exercise are highlighted for their potential benefits in enhancing quality of life and addressing behavioral issues. CST uses themed activities to engage and stimulate cognitive function, while Reminiscence Therapy taps into long-term memory to improve mood and self-esteem. Physical exercise is recommended to maintain physical health and reduce depressive symptoms. The document underscores the importance of personalized approaches and the need for further research to validate the effectiveness of various interventions.
