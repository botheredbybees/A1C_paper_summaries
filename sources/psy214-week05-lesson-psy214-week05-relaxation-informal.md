## Relaxation: Informal Approaches

This lesson explores the concept of free time and its impact on well-being in Australia. It categorizes time use into necessary, contracted, committed, and free time, with a focus on social interaction and recreation-leisure activities. The Australian Bureau of Statistics reports that Australians spend an average of 5 hours and 13 minutes daily on free-time activities, primarily watching TV/videos and using the internet. Research indicates these activities can enhance subjective well-being through detachment-recovery, autonomy, mastery, meaning, and affiliation (DRAMMA model). However, screen-based leisure may offer fewer benefits than other forms due to lower levels of satisfaction and reduced engagement with eudaimonic needs. The lesson introduces 'breathers' for brief detachment and 'restorers' for more active restoration of physical and psychological resources.
