## Advancing SDG 12 Through Material-Driven Jewelry Design with Exploration of Rosin and Sawdust Waste

This study explores the use of rosin and sawdust waste in jewelry design to promote sustainable practices aligning with SDG 12. By employing a Material Driven Design (MDD) approach, researchers investigated the characteristics and potential applications of these natural materials. Experiments combined rosin with beeswax and sawdust to create composite materials suitable for jewelry production. Results indicate that a specific ratio of rosin-to-beeswax produces optimal strength while maintaining aesthetic appeal. The study demonstrates rosin's potential as an eco-friendly alternative material in various industries.
