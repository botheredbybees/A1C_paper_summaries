## Healthy Eating Guidelines and Models

This lesson discusses various dietary guidelines and traditional eating patterns from around the world, focusing on Australia's and the Mediterranean diet's principles. It highlights broad models for healthy eating such as the Blue Zones approach, emphasizing plant-based diets and moderate consumption of animal products. The Planetary Health approach is introduced, which aims to balance human health and environmental sustainability. Key points include the importance of vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts, seeds, and healthy fats while reducing processed foods and red meat.
