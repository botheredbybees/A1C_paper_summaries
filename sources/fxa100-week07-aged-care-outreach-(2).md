## Aged Care Outreach

The document provides an overview of reminiscing kits designed for use in aged care settings to engage elderly individuals through social history items that evoke memories. These kits include various themes such as rock and roll, childhood games, dance hall days, Italian migration experiences, and transportation from the mid-20th century. The purpose is to stimulate discussions and emotional connections among older adults, aiding in dementia care by providing tactile and sensory stimulation. Museums Victoria offers these resources for activity coordinators to enhance quality of life through interactive reminiscing sessions.
