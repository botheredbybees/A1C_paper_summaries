## Music, business, and health

The chapter examines the impact of music on the economy and the music industry. It discusses how the music industry influences musical behavior and examines the economic implications of musical innovation, superstardom, and piracy. The chapter also explores the role of music in advertising and its effects on customer behavior in retail and service businesses. Additionally, it highlights the indirect economic benefits of music, such as its influence on workplace productivity and physical health. The text emphasizes the commercial nature of music and its role in driving economic outcomes.
