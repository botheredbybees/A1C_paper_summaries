## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness and Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness

This lesson focuses on formal mindfulness practices such as the body scan and sitting meditation, which are integral to the Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) curriculum. The body scan involves moving attention systematically through the body, while sitting meditation emphasizes present-centered awareness. Both practices aim to develop dispositional mindfulness, enhancing mindful behavior across various situations. The lesson includes optional guided audio exercises and emphasizes the importance of being mindful of potential discomfort and distress during practice. It also provides information on the taxonomy of meditation types, distinguishing between attentional, constructive, and deconstructive approaches. Participants are advised to consult healthcare providers if they have concerns about adverse effects or clinical conditions.
