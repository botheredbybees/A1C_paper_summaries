## Wearable Haptic Music Player with Multi-Feature Extraction Using Spectral Flux and Yin Algorithms

This paper introduces a wearable haptic music player (HMP) designed to enhance the musical experience by integrating vibrotactile feedback with audio stimuli using piezoelectric actuators and advanced signal processing techniques. The system utilizes the YIN algorithm for accurate pitch tracking and spectral flux for beat estimation, alongside software optimizations to ensure synchronization of visual, audio, and haptic feedback. Key findings include high accuracy in displaying music features such as pitch, rhythm, melody, and timbre with mean average error (MAE) = 0.1020 and dynamic time warping (DTW) distance = 0.1518 for the YIN algorithm, and rhythmic pattern accuracy of 97.56%. The authors demonstrate that their method improves upon existing HMP limitations by efficiently extracting multiple music features without reliance on MIDI data or transduction methods.
