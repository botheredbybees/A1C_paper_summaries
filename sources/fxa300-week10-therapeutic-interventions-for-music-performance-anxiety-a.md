## Therapeutic Interventions for Music Performance Anxiety: A Systematic Review and Narrative Synthesis

This systematic review evaluates published literature on interventions for treating music performance anxiety (MPA) among musicians. The review, adhering to PRISMA guidelines, includes 40 quantitative pre-post interventional studies involving 1365 participants. Principal intervention types include cognitive behavioral therapy, acceptance and commitment therapy, music therapy, yoga, mindfulness, virtual reality, hypnotherapy, biofeedback, and multimodal therapy. While many studies show positive improvements in MPA following these interventions, the evidence base is still in its early stages, with methodological weaknesses such as small sample sizes and lack of standardized MPA assessments. The review highlights the need for more rigorous research to establish definitive treatment recommendations.
