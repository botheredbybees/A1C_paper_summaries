## FXA300 Music, Mind and Body Week 7 Activities

The lesson for Week 7 of FXA300 Music, Mind and Body focuses on the impact of music on creative thinking. Students are directed to read an academic article by Ritter and Ferguson, which investigates how listening to happy music can enhance divergent thinking. The discussion section encourages students to reflect on their personal use of music in creative tasks and during study. Students are also instructed to start planning their Case Study assessment task, reviewing the instructions and compiling necessary information for their PowerPoint presentation slides. The week concludes with references to various scholarly works on music and creativity, including articles and books by experts in the field. The upcoming week will explore therapeutic uses of music in healthcare settings.
