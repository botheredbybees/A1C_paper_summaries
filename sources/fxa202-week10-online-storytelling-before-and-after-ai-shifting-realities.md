## Online Storytelling Before and After AI: Shifting Realities in Text, Image, Video, and Audience Experiences

This study examines the evolution of online storytelling before and after the integration of artificial intelligence (AI), focusing on changes in text, image, video, and audience experiences. The research highlights how digital media platforms adapt their content strategies to address AI-driven challenges and innovations. It reveals that while text remains a primary form of storytelling, images and videos are increasingly important due to shifts in consumer preferences and technological advancements. The study also discusses the implications of AI on journalistic practices, including personalization algorithms and data journalism, and emphasizes the need for privacy-preserving frameworks in online newsrooms. Overall, it underscores how digital platforms navigate evolving media landscapes through adaptive content strategies to enhance audience engagement.
