## Psychoneuroendocrine Research on Music and Health: An Overview

This chapter explores the impact of musical activities such as listening, singing, and dancing on the endocrine system. The authors discuss the psychological processes associated with musical experiences and their influence on hormonal systems in the brain and body. They provide an overview of psychoneuroendocrinology (PNE) and relevant hormonal systems, followed by a review of empirical studies on hormonal responses to musical stimulation. The chapter concludes with suggestions for future research, emphasizing the potential of music to induce physiological effects similar to those produced by pharmacological substances. The central argument is that music can serve as a psychoactive stimulus influencing health and wellbeing through its effects on neurohumoral processes. This research is particularly relevant to the creative arts and health, as it highlights the therapeutic and social benefits of musical engagement in improving mental and physical health.
