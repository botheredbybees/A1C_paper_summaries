## Crafting Representations of Rare Disease: Collage as Qualitative Inquiry

This paper explores the use of collage as a qualitative research method to represent and understand the experiences of families affected by rare genetic conditions. The study involves ten weeks of collaging workshops where participants created artistic representations of their experiences, fostering open discussions about daily challenges. Collage is seen as an effective medium for expressing complex themes such as motherhood, care, being heard, and balancing family life with medicalization. By allowing a tentative yet conceptual approach to sensitive topics, collage enables the broaching of elusive themes in a safe and expressive manner.
