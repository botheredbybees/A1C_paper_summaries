## Effectiveness of Creative Story Therapy for Dementia: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

This study presents a systematic review and meta-analysis evaluating the effectiveness of creative story therapy in dementia treatment compared to routine nursing care. The research analyzed nine randomized controlled trials involving 597 participants, finding significant improvements in cognitive function, depression scores, quality of life, and social communication for those receiving creative story therapy combined with routine care. No significant difference was noted in basic needs communication between the two groups. Creative story therapy is highlighted as a person-centered approach that empowers dementia patients to express themselves through storytelling, thereby enhancing their cognitive abilities and overall well-being. The study underscores the need for further high-quality research to validate these findings.
