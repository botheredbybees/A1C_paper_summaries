## Good Arts, Good Mental Health®: the effectiveness of an Australian health promotion media campaign in promoting community mental wellbeing via the arts

This study evaluates the effectiveness of an Australian health promotion media campaign called Good Arts, Good Mental Health® (GAGMH) aimed at promoting community mental wellbeing through arts engagement. The campaign targeted individuals aged 18-65 years in Western Australia across various platforms and channels from August to September 2024. Key findings indicate that the campaign was successful in increasing brand awareness, comprehension, and agreement with the tagline 'Good Arts, Good Mental Health®'. Engagement with the campaign's website also saw a significant increase post-campaign, demonstrating high reach and frequency. The study highlights the potential of arts engagement as a mental health prevention strategy, emphasizing the need for population-level promotion to address growing mental health issues. Future waves of the GAGMH campaign aim to further reinforce message awareness, deepen understanding of the benefits, and measure behavioral outcomes more extensively.
