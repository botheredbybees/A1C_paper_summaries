## Evaluation of Food Presentations Using Picture Fuzzy Analytical Hierarchy Process

This paper explores the use of Picture Fuzzy Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) to evaluate food presentations in culinary education and competitions, aiming to provide a fair and quantitative assessment methodology. The study evaluates five Korean Cuisine course presentations using criteria such as presentation, creativity of names, taste, and cultural fusion balance. By applying AHP with picture fuzzy sets, the method quantifies subjective judgments of experts, addressing the uncertainty inherent in gastronomy evaluations. This approach offers an innovative framework for culinary product assessments.
