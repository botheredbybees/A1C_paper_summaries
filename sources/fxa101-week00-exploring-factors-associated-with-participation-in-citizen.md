## Exploring Factors Associated with Participation in Citizen Science Among UK Museum Visitors Aged 40-60

This qualitative study examines factors influencing citizen science participation among UK museum visitors aged 40-60, utilizing the Theoretical Domains Framework (TDF) and the Capability Opportunity Motivation-Behaviour (COM-B) model. By conducting structured interviews followed by semi-structured discussions with 47 participants, researchers identified eight themes impacting participation: awareness of citizen science, personal characteristics and skills, beliefs about capability, project purpose importance, interests and incentives, family influence, project details facilitating easy involvement, and environmental factors like time availability. The study reveals a need for addressing multiple influencing factors to enhance citizen science engagement among this demographic.
