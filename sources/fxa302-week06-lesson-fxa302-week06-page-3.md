## Visual Loss and Painting: Monet, Bramblitt, Armağan

This lesson explores how visual impairment affects artists' perception and creativity. It focuses on Claude Monet's experience with cataracts, altering his color palette significantly before and after surgery. Additionally, it highlights John Bramblitt's technique of 'haptic visualization,' enabling him to paint vivid landscapes post-blindness due to epilepsy. Eşref Armağan, born blind in Turkey, teaches himself painting through tactile methods and verbal descriptions from others, challenging the belief that being born blind limits one’s understanding of visual imagery.
