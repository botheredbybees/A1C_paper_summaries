## A ‘Music, Mind and Movement’ Program for People With Dementia: Initial Evidence of Improved Cognition

This paper presents a study evaluating the 'Music, Mind and Movement' (MMM) program designed for individuals with dementia, focusing on its potential to improve cognition, mood, identity, and motor skills. The MMM program is based on seven therapeutic capacities of music: persuasive, engaging, emotional, personal, physical, social, and synchronization. Participants included 20 people with mild to moderate dementia who underwent a series of group sessions followed by individual booster sessions. Assessments were conducted at baseline, post-treatment, and one month later. The study found that participants showed improvements in global cognition, particularly in attention and verbal fluency. These preliminary results suggest the potential of music-based interventions as a therapeutic tool for enhancing cognitive functions in dementia patients.
