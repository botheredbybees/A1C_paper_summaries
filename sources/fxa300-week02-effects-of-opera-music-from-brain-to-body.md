## Effects of Opera Music from Brain to Body

The book 'Effects of Opera Music from Brain to Body' edited by Lorenzo Lorusso, Michele Augusto Riva, and Vittorio Alessandro Sironi explores the profound influence of opera music on neurological health and wellbeing. The central argument is that opera not only provides aesthetic pleasure but also has therapeutic benefits for various neurodegenerative conditions and mental disorders. Key findings include the role of audiovisual-motor mirror neurons in learning and performing opera, which helps explain how opera can enhance motor skills and emotional regulation. Additionally, the book discusses the cognitive implications of music as observed by Oliver Sacks and emphasizes the neural basis of musical imagination. The relevance to creative arts and health lies in its interdisciplinary approach that bridges neuroscience and cultural studies, offering new insights into how art can be used for therapeutic purposes.
