## It’s never too late to be a late bloomer

The transcript discusses the concept of 'late bloomer' through the story of Carol Gardner, who started her successful business at age 52. The episode includes interviews with experts such as Rich Karlgaard, Professor Scott Barry Kaufman, and Professor Dean Keith Simonton, who emphasize the potential for individuals to achieve significant success at any age. Gardner's journey illustrates how a chance encounter with a dog contest sparked a multi-million dollar business, inspiring others to pursue their passions regardless of age. The experts highlight the brain's plasticity and the importance of self-compassion, challenging societal norms that discourage late career changes. This narrative is relevant to creative arts and health by encouraging individuals to embrace their potential and pursue creative endeavors irrespective of age.
