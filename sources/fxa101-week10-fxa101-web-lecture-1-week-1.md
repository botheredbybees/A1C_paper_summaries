## FXA101 Web Lecture 1 Week 1

Dr Brendan Lamb's web lecture for FXA101 in Week 1 of 2026 provides an overview of the unit structure, assessment tasks, and the importance of creative projects. The lecture emphasizes the use of unconventional methods in the creative process and introduces the theme words 'Air', 'Cell', and 'Sea'. It also highlights the role of visual journaling in neurodevelopment and the benefits of creative activities for the brain. The lecture includes tips on how to prepare for the upcoming quiz and emphasizes the need to avoid using AI for academic work due to reliability and academic integrity concerns. Dr Lamb encourages students to participate actively in discussions and engage in creative thinking exercises.
