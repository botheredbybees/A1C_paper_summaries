## The impact of viewing art on well-being—a systematic review of the evidence base and suggested mechanisms

This systematic review examines the impact of viewing art on well-being, synthesizing evidence from multiple databases and analyzing suggested mechanisms through which such impacts might occur. The authors find that while engaging with visual arts can offer benefits to mental health—particularly in promoting eudemonic well-being—the current research base is fragmented and lacks rigorous methodology. Key findings indicate diverse contexts and outcomes for art viewing experiences, ranging from individual encounters with paintings to group activities within museums. Thematic analysis revealed several mechanisms such as affective engagement, cognitive stimulation, social interaction, self-transformation, and resilience building, all of which can contribute to well-being but are often context-dependent. The review concludes that future research should focus on identifying specific components of art viewing experiences that promote well-being and developing clearer methodological guidelines.
