## Music Therapy comforts, soothes those with varying degrees of dementia - transcript

The document discusses the role of music therapy in comforting and soothing individuals with Alzheimer's disease and other forms of dementia. Moren Bosch, a program coordinator and board-certified music therapist at Clare Bridge ofal Beach, emphasizes that music can activate multiple areas of the brain, making it possible to reach patients who have lost their ability to speak through musical engagement. The transcript highlights cases like Larry, an accomplished artist and musician who developed severe dementia after a seizure. Despite significant cognitive decline, Larry's interaction with music has been revitalized, showcasing his capacity for emotional response and active participation in musical activities. This underscores the importance of integrating music therapy into care plans to enhance quality of life and preserve aspects of personality and self-confidence among individuals suffering from dementia.
