## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

This document is a segment of the ebook 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Bradley R. Postle, published by John Wiley & Sons, Incorporated in 2015. The excerpt discusses the principles of how information is stored and retrieved in the brain, focusing on the distinction between familiarity and recollection in memory retrieval. It explains that while familiarity contributes to performance based on repeated exposure to stimuli, recollection depends on hippocampal function. The text also mentions that hippocampal lesions may impair recollection without significantly affecting familiarity. The chapter concludes with questions about the limitations of fMRI and ERP data in studying memory processes, emphasizing the importance of understanding how information is organized in the brain for retrieval. The document also references the work of Murray, Bussey, and Saksida (2007) and discusses the potential role of familiarity in memory tasks, suggesting that this topic will be covered in the next chapter.
