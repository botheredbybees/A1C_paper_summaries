## Relationship of therapeutic recreation inpatient rehabilitation interventions and patient characteristics to outcomes following spinal cord injury: The SCIRehab project

This paper investigates the impact of therapeutic recreation (TR) interventions during inpatient rehabilitation on functional, participation, and quality of life outcomes for patients with spinal cord injury (SCI). The study, part of the SCIRehab project, employs a multi-center observational approach to analyze the relationship between TR activities and patient outcomes. Key findings indicate that increased participation in TR-led leisure activities positively correlates with better functional independence, higher quality of life, and greater likelihood of returning to work or school at the one-year injury anniversary. The research highlights the importance of TR in aiding patients' reintegration into community life and reducing rehospitalization rates and pressure ulcer development. The study underscores the need for further research to explore the long-term impact of TR on SCI patients' quality of life and independence.
