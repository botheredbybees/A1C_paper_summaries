## Week 2 – Making Pictures

The document 'Week 2 – Making Pictures' emphasizes the importance of having a camera at hand to capture spontaneous moments, focusing on three main types of cameras: mobile phone cameras, point and shoot cameras, and DSLRs. It highlights that while a mobile phone camera is convenient and often used for candid photography, it may not offer as high resolution or flexibility as other options. The text provides tips for improving photo quality with each type of camera, such as keeping the lens clean and utilizing manual settings on DSLRs to maximize creativity and control over images. The lesson encourages experimentation with different camera functions to enhance photographic skills.
