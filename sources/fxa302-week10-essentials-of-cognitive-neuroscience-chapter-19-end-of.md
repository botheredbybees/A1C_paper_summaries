## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience (Chapter 19: END-OF-CHAPTER QUESTIONS)

The document provides a set of end-of-chapter questions from 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Bradley R. Postle, focusing on the chapter dedicated to the biological basis of consciousness. The questions explore various aspects of consciousness, such as differentiating coma from persistent vegetative state/unresponsive wakefulness syndrome and locked-in syndrome, the role of the reticular activating system in physiological arousal, and the effects of general anesthesia. The questions also delve into the neural bases of consciousness, including the theories of Global Workspace Theory, Recurrent Processing Theory, and Integrated Information Theory. The chapter emphasizes the importance of connectivity and cortical activity in determining the level of consciousness and highlights the continuous discoveries in cognitive neuroscience.
