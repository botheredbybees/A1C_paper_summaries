## Reducing Musical Performance Anxiety: A Review of Literature and a Self-Help Manual

Jon Rodney Waite's document aims to synthesize psychological literature on musical performance anxiety and propose practical methods for its reduction. The study underscores the importance of understanding the causes of performance anxiety, which can hinder musical expression and enjoyment. Waite delineates various psychological techniques such as psychoanalytic positions, social learning theory, systematic desensitization, relaxation training, hypnosis, and humanistic psychology, as well as the use of medications and meditation, to help musicians manage anxiety. The central argument revolves around the idea that performers can improve their ease and enjoyment in public performances by enhancing their playing ability and reducing inhibitory factors like anxiety and distraction. Waite emphasizes the role of thorough preparation, psychological reconditioning, and maintaining a relaxed physical state. This work is particularly relevant to creative arts and health by providing actionable strategies for musicians to overcome performance anxiety, thereby enhancing their artistic expression and mental well-being.
