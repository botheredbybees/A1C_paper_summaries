## Music and Mental Health

This lesson explores the role of music in mental health, focusing on its historical use and recent research. It defines mental health as a state of well-being enabling people to cope with life's stresses and includes a discussion on the prevalence of mental disorders in Australia and the efficacy of music therapy. Music therapy is highlighted as an adjunct treatment for mental health conditions, with various theories explaining its effectiveness, such as enhancing self-expression and insight. The lesson also covers recent studies on music therapy's impact on reducing stress and anxiety in various contexts, including neonatal intensive care units and dental patients. It concludes by noting the potential for future research in the field.
