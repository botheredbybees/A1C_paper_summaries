## Happy creativity: Listening to happy music facilitates divergent thinking

This study explores the impact of music, particularly 'happy' music, on divergent and convergent creativity. The authors, Ritter and Ferguson, investigate whether listening to classical music with high arousal and positive mood enhances divergent thinking, which involves generating multiple solutions to a problem. The research found that participants who listened to 'happy music' performed better in divergent thinking tasks compared to those who worked in silence. However, no significant effect was observed for convergent thinking tasks. The study suggests that music listening can be a practical method to facilitate creativity in daily life and various professional settings, given its simplicity and low cost. The findings highlight the potential for music to influence cognitive processes related to creativity, offering new insights into enhancing creative thinking through auditory stimulation.
