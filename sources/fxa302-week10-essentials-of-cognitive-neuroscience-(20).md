## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience by Bradley R. Postle explores the biological basis of consciousness and cognitive neuroscience. The text delves into how connectivity and cortical activity influence the level of awareness, differentiating various states of consciousness such as coma, vegetative state/unresponsive wakefulness syndrome, and locked-in syndrome. The book also examines the role of the reticular activating system in physiological arousal and how it varies across different sleep stages. Additionally, the text discusses the physiological and behavioral effects of general anesthesia and its relation to sleep and coma states. Postle underscores the importance of understanding these neural mechanisms for advancing our knowledge of consciousness and cognitive processes. This work is highly relevant to the creative arts and health fields, as it provides insights into how the brain functions during different states of awareness, which can inform artistic expression and therapeutic interventions.
