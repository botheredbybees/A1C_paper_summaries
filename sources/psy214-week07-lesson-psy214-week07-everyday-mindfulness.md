## Informal/everyday mindfulness

This lesson discusses the practice of informal or everyday mindfulness, which involves engaging with routine tasks with full awareness and attention. It contrasts with formal meditative practices and includes examples such as personal hygiene tasks, domestic chores, waiting, commuting, eating, and walking. The lesson outlines how to approach everyday tasks mindfully, focusing on sensory experiences and gently guiding attention back to the task when the mind wanders. It emphasizes the importance of being open to both pleasant and unpleasant experiences during mindfulness practice. The lesson also addresses the impact of informal mindfulness on well-being, noting that while informal practices are generally well-tolerated, more research is needed to understand their specific outcomes and mechanisms compared to formal practices.
