## Transcript: Composed Documentary, trailer

The transcript captures the raw emotions and challenges faced by a musician during a performance. The speaker describes experiencing intense performance anxiety and the feeling of 'tunnel vision' when deeply immersed in music. They reflect on the unique way music affects individuals and the frustration of not being able to perform as well on stage as in practice. The discussion includes the perspective of musicians as 'small muscle athletes' and the importance of understanding how to manage performance anxiety through breathing techniques, movement, and positive thinking.
