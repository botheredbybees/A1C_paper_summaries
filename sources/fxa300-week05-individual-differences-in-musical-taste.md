## Individual Differences in Musical Taste

The article explores the relationship between personality traits and musical taste, focusing on a broad range of styles rather than narrow subsets previously examined in the literature. The study involved over 36,000 participants who rated their liking for 104 different musical styles and completed a Big Five personality inventory. The research found that personality factors do correlate with musical preferences, although these correlations are generally modest, explaining less than 10% of variance. Other individual differences such as age, sex, income, and self-esteem also significantly influence music preference, often more closely than personality traits. The study suggests that while personality plays a role in shaping musical tastes, other factors may have stronger influences. This research has implications for understanding the complexity of musical taste formation and its relevance to broader psychological constructs.
