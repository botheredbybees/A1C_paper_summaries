## Running on 'autopilot': Mindfulness, Schemas, and System 1 Thinking

This lesson explores the concept of 'autopilot' in relation to mindfulness and how it can be beneficial or detrimental depending on the context. Mindfulness is described as paying attention and accepting the present moment, contrasting with 'autopilot' behavior, which involves acting without full conscious awareness. The lesson introduces the concept of schemas, mental frameworks built from life experiences, which help in efficiently processing information. However, over-reliance on schemas can lead to cognitive biases and errors. The lesson also discusses Daniel Kahneman's 'System 1' and 'System 2' thinking, where System 1 is fast and uses cognitive shortcuts while System 2 is slower, more deliberate, and less prone to biases but more mentally demanding. The lesson emphasizes the importance of understanding when each type of thinking is appropriate and how mindfulness can enhance awareness of mental processes to mitigate negative outcomes.
