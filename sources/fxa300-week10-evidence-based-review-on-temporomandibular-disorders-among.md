## Evidence-based review on temporomandibular disorders among musicians

This study reviews the evidence on temporomandibular disorders (TMDs) among musicians playing specific instruments. The review evaluates observational studies focusing on wind instruments, violins/violas, and vocalists. The authors find limited evidence suggesting that playing wind instruments might be a risk factor for TMDs, while no evidence indicates that vocalists are at higher risk. Studies on violin and viola players produced mixed results, with some studies finding no association and others reporting an association when clinical examinations were conducted. The review highlights the importance of methodological quality in observational studies and concludes that previous literature reviews on this topic did not conform to current standards of evidence-based medicine.
