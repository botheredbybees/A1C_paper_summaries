## Facilitating Arts Participation for Creative Ageing: An Action Research in South Korea

This study examines the facilitation of arts participation among older adults who have little prior experience with artistic activities, focusing on creative ageing in South Korea. The research employs an action research methodology consisting of two phases: hands-on creation activities and appreciation of professional artists' works. Key findings highlight the importance of facilitation in initiating and sustaining engagement with the arts for older individuals, emphasizing the need to center potential participants in discussions about creative initiatives. The study also underscores that arts participation not only enriches individual lives but contributes positively to community wellbeing and social value creation. This research suggests developing 'facilitation for arts participation' as a specialized professional field.
