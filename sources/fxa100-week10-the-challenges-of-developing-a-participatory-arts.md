## The Challenges of Developing a Participatory Arts Intervention for Caregivers of Persons with Dementia

This paper discusses the development and implementation challenges of a web-based participatory arts intervention designed for caregivers of persons with dementia. The study aimed to create an 'artistic toolkit' consisting of seven online modules involving various creative activities such as music, singing, dancing, poetry, and painting. Despite initial difficulties with caregiver recruitment and adherence to lesson plans, the intervention was well-received qualitatively by participating caregivers. Key challenges identified include recruitment issues and compliance concerns. The study highlights the need for further research in identifying effective sensory art interventions for dementia care and emphasizes the importance of addressing caregiver burden through creative arts.
