## A Short Performance Anxiety Scale for Musicians

This study introduces the Mazzarolo Music Performance Anxiety Scale (M-MPAS), a concise five-item scale aimed at assessing the extent of music performance anxiety (MPA) among musicians. The scale evaluates the frequency, intensity, and aversion tendency related to MPA. A total of 102 participants, including various instrumentalists, completed the M-MPAS and an established MPA inventory. The study reports high internal reliability (Cronbach’s alpha = 0.894) and construct validity, with the M-MPAS correlating strongly with an established MPA measure. The scale ranges from 0 to 30, with a score of 11 or above indicating potential high MPA. The research highlights the benefits of using a brief scale for initial assessment, offering practical implications for both research and clinical settings in addressing MPA.
