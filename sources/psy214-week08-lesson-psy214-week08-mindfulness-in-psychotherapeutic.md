## Mindfulness in Psychotherapy

This lesson introduces additional mindfulness-based psychotherapeutic approaches beyond the Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) program. It covers the development of Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT) and provides detailed information on Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) and Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT). ACT, based on relational frame theory, aims to increase acceptance of psychological experiences and promote behavior change toward valued goals. DBT, designed to reduce ineffective action tendencies linked with deregulated emotions, includes four modes of therapy and has a solid evidence base for treating various disorders, including borderline personality disorder and depression. The lesson also references the Australian Psychological Society's classification of evidence-based treatments.
