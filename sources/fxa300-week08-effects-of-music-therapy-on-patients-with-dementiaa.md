## Effects of Music Therapy on Patients with Dementia—A Systematic Review

This systematic review evaluates the effects of music therapy on patients with dementia. It includes 82 studies, of which 43 are interventional clinical trials and 39 are systematic reviews or meta-analyses. The review examines various modalities of music therapy and their impact on cognitive function, behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD), and quality of life. Key findings indicate significant improvements in verbal fluency and reductions in anxiety, depression, and apathy. However, there were no significant improvements in overall cognition or daily functioning. The review highlights the need for more large-scale clinical trials to clarify the benefits of music therapy in dementia care.
