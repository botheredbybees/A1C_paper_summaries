## HOMESIDE—A home-based family caregiver-delivered music and reading intervention for people living with dementia: A randomised controlled trial

The HOMESIDE study aims to address behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD) through a home-based intervention delivered by informal family caregivers. The randomized controlled trial includes three groups: standard care, a music program plus standard care, and a reading program plus standard care. This research highlights the importance of non-pharmacological approaches in managing BPSD and improving quality of life for individuals with dementia and their caregivers. Training cohabiting family members to deliver these interventions could significantly enhance informal caregiving practices. The study's outcomes are expected to provide valuable insights into the effectiveness of caregiver-delivered music therapy in reducing BPSD symptoms and enhancing relationship quality, resilience, and overall well-being.
