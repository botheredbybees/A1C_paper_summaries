## Unconscious creativity: When can unconscious thought outperform conscious thought?

This research explores the relationship between unconscious thought and creativity, finding that unconscious deliberation can outperform conscious thought on creative tasks under specific conditions: when the duration of unconscious thought is moderate rather than very short or long. The study reveals an inverted-U shaped relationship between the duration of unconscious thought and creativity performance. Unconscious thought enhances novelty but does not necessarily improve appropriateness in creativity dimensions, indicating that while it aids in generating novel ideas, it may not always ensure these ideas are appropriate. These findings highlight the importance of calibrating the use of unconscious thought to boost creativity effectively.
