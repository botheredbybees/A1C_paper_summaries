## A synthesis process model of creative thinking in music composition

This article presents a comprehensive study of the compositional process through a three-year single case study of a composer. The research aims to track cognitive processes in real-time using a combination of data collection methods, including digital MIDI files, audio recordings, interviews, and immediate retrospective verbal accounts. The findings suggest that the compositional process involves both linear and recursive phases, characterized by problem proliferation and solution implementation. Insights related to Gestalt theory indicate that moments of creative insight are often associated with restructuring problems and finding unexpected solutions. The study synthesizes various creative process theories to provide a nuanced model of creative thinking in music composition.
