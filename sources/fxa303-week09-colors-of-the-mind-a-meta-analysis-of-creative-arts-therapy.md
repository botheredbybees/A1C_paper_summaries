## Colors of the mind: a meta-analysis of creative arts therapy as an approach for post-traumatic stress disorder intervention

The study by Wang et al., published in BMC Psychology, conducts a meta-analysis to assess the efficacy of creative arts therapy in treating post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD). The research involved systematic searches through electronic databases from inception until November 2023. Seven controlled comparative studies were analyzed using the Cochrane risk of bias tool, and standardized mean differences were calculated for outcomes. Results indicated a significant reduction in PTSD symptoms following creative arts therapy, with drama therapy showing notably effective results compared to other modalities like music or dance/movement therapies. The study highlights the potential of creative arts therapy as an adjunct treatment for PTSD but acknowledges limitations such as limited number of studies and participant sizes, methodological quality, and heterogeneity across studies. This research underscores the need for further exploration into tailored therapeutic interventions for individuals with PTSD to address residual symptoms and enhance overall mental health outcomes.
