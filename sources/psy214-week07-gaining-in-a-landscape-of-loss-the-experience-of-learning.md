## Gaining in a Landscape of Loss: The Experience of Learning and Using Mindfulness Whilst Caring at the End of Life

This thesis, 'Gaining in a Landscape of Loss: The Experience of Learning and Using Mindfulness Whilst Caring at the End of Life,' by Linda M. Jaffray, explores the impact of mindfulness practices on caregivers who are providing end-of-life care to loved ones. Through qualitative research involving interviews with informal palliative caregivers, Jaffray investigates how mindfulness can serve as a tool for personal growth and resilience amidst challenging circumstances. The study highlights the emotional and psychological benefits that participants experienced through mindfulness training, such as increased self-awareness, reduced stress, and improved coping mechanisms. This work is significant in the creative arts and health field as it bridges the gap between traditional caregiving approaches and innovative mental wellness strategies, offering practical insights for caregivers to enhance their well-being while caring for others.
