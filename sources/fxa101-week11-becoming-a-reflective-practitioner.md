## Becoming a Reflective Practitioner

The document 'Becoming a Reflective Practitioner' discusses the use of narrative and performance to foster reflective practice in healthcare settings. The author describes a project, 'Jane's Rap,' where drama and psychiatric students read empathic poems representing the voices of self-harm patients and a nurse working in an Accident and Emergency department. This exercise provided profound insights into self-harm and facilitated a deeper understanding among the participants, especially those with personal experiences of self-harm. The author also mentions the use of visual art to complement the spoken word, enhancing the emotional and educational impact. The text emphasizes the potential of performance narratives to transform the educational curriculum, encouraging active and dynamic learning among students and practitioners. This method of storytelling and performance is suggested as a valuable tool for teachers and researchers who want to explore self-reflection and empathy through narrative and artistic expression.
