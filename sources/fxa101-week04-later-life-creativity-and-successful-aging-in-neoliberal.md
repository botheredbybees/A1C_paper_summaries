## Later-Life Creativity and Successful Aging in Neoliberal Agendas

The article examines the relationship between later-life creativity and the discourse of successful aging within neoliberal agendas, highlighting that while creativity is increasingly recognized as a key driver for enhancing mental and physical health in older age, its intersection with the model of successful aging requires further exploration. The author argues that although successful aging often fails to capture alternative meanings of aging and creative engagement, many older individuals challenge these notions by creating their own models of well-being that do not align with neoliberal ideals of productivity and optimization. Critically engaging with interdisciplinary perspectives on later-life creativity helps broaden understandings beyond reductionist views and rethinks the current meanings of old age.
