## Investigating key drivers influencing AI-based detection and identification of plants

This paper examines the impact of location precision on AI-based plant identification accuracy, specifically in detecting invasive alien plants (IAP) using iNaturalist and PlantNet platforms. The study found that restricting geographic area significantly hinders iNaturalist’s ability to identify IAPs accurately. The research also highlighted lower identification accuracy for species in the Poaceae family and images featuring only leaves. This suggests a critical need for recognizing location influence when monitoring IAPs.
