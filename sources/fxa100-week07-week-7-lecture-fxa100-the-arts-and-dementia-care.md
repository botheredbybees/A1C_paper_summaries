## Week 7 Lecture - FXA100: The Arts and Dementia Care

The lecture for Week 7 of FXA100: The Arts and Dementia Care focuses on the integration of arts programs for individuals with dementia, emphasizing national and international initiatives. Key programs discussed include ARTZ, which provides artistic experiences for people with Alzheimer’s disease, and the Meet Me at MoMA program designed to build confidence and foster respect through art. The lecture also covers the National Art Gallery of Australia’s initiatives and other arts access programs such as the Art Gallery of NSW and the MCA Artful. These programs highlight the benefits of reminiscence-based activities and the potential for creativity through gallery interactions. The lecture concludes with a discussion on planning for gallery visits, emphasizing accessibility and engagement strategies. Additionally, the lecture includes an analysis of Baines' (2007) article, exploring the potential beyond problems in dementia care and encouraging students to reflect on their approach to cognition versus emotion in dementia care.
