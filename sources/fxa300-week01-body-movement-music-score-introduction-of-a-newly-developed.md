## Body Movement Music Score: Introduction of a Newly Developed Model for the Analysis and Description of Body Qualities, Movement and Music in Music Therapy

The paper introduces the Body Movement Music Score (BMMS), a new model developed for analyzing body qualities, movement, and music in music therapy treatments, particularly for patients with schizophrenia. The BMMS aims to provide a comprehensive framework that evaluates these aspects equally, distinguishing it from existing models which focus on either one or two of these elements. The study presents the application of this model through an example involving a video-taped session with a schizophrenic patient and demonstrates how the BMMS can be used in practical and clinical settings for documentation and research purposes. Key findings indicate that while various scales and approaches exist within music therapy, there was a gap in analyzing all three components (body qualities, movement, and musical expression) simultaneously. The BMMS fills this gap by providing detailed analyses including Body Qualities I & II Analysis, Movement Analysis, Playing Style of Musical Instruments Analysis, and Music Analysis Profiles. The model’s development is based on the Emotorics-Emotive Body Movement Mind Paradigm (EBMMP), which was initially used to analyze motor behavior in music therapy for schizophrenic patients. Future work will involve establishing reliability measures to further validate its use in clinical practice.
