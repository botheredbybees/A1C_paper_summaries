## Statistical analysis plan for HOME-based caregiver-delivered music intervention for people living with dementia (HOMESIDE) trial

The paper outlines the statistical analysis plan for a randomized controlled trial (RCT) investigating the effectiveness of home-based music interventions delivered by caregivers to people living with dementia. The primary objective is to determine whether an additional music intervention (MI), in combination with standard care, reduces behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD) compared to standard care alone or a reading intervention plus standard care. Participants are dyads comprising the person with dementia (PwD) and their caregiver, recruited from Australia, Germany, UK, Poland, and Norway. The study employs a three-arm parallel group design, randomizing participants to receive MI + SC, RI + SC, or SC alone. Outcome measures include the Neuropsychiatric Inventory-Questionnaire (NPI-Q), quality of life, depression, cognition, distress, resilience, competence, and caregiver-patient relationship. This statistical analysis plan aims to enhance study validity and reduce bias through rigorous longitudinal analysis methods.
