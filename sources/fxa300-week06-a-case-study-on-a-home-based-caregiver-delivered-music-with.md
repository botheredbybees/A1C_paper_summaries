## A Case Study on a Home-Based Caregiver-Delivered Music-with-Movement Intervention for People with Early Dementia

This case study explores the effects of a music-with-movement (MWM) intervention on a Chinese female with early dementia. The patient, aged 78, experienced memory loss and declining verbal communication after suffering from a stroke and being diagnosed with early dementia in 2011. The MWM intervention was delivered by her husband over an eight-week period at home, aiming to reduce anxiety and improve sleep quality. Results indicated improvements in sleep quality and depressive symptoms, although there were no significant changes in cognitive assessments like the Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE). However, the patient's RAID score increased during the intervention, contrasting with typical outcomes of music interventions which usually show reduced anxiety levels. The study highlights the importance of caregiver understanding and flexibility in adapting MWM protocols to suit individual needs for effective treatment.
