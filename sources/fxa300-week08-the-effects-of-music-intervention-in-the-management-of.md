## The Effects of Music Intervention in the Management of Chronic Pain

The study evaluates the efficacy of a music intervention method in managing chronic pain and anxiety/depression. The intervention involved patients listening to music twice daily during hospitalization and continued at home for two months. The study found a significant reduction in pain levels, anxiety, and depression among patients who received the music intervention compared to those who did not. The music intervention also led to a reduction in the consumption of medication for pain and anxiety. The results affirm the value of music as a non-pharmacological treatment for chronic pain and related psychological symptoms, suggesting it can be a useful addition to conventional medical therapies.
