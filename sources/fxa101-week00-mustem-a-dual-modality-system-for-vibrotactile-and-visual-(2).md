## MUSTEM: A Dual-Modality System for Vibrotactile and Visual Translation of Music

MUSTEM is a novel system designed to translate music into sensory experiences for the deaf and hard-of-hearing (DHH) community, addressing accessibility challenges in emotional and structural music experience. The system combines vibrotactile feedback and visual displays to convey musical elements such as rhythm, harmony, and frequency spectrum. It includes a hardware prototype with real-time audio analysis mapping distinct frequency bands to a four-channel vibrotactile system and a high-fidelity software simulation for visual translation. MUSTEM offers a comprehensive framework grounded in psychoacoustics principles, providing an accessible pathway for the DHH community to experience music as structured and emotionally resonant sensory input.
