## Physical Activity and Health

The lesson explores the benefits of physical activity on both mental and physical health. It highlights how regular exercise reduces risks of various diseases and improves mental health conditions such as depression and anxiety. Vigorous exercises like aerobic activities are shown to enhance mood, while gentle forms like yoga can aid in relaxation. The discussion includes the biological mechanisms behind these effects, involving reduced inflammation, stress management improvements, and the release of endorphins. Additionally, environmental factors are discussed which often discourage physical activity and promote sedentary behavior.
