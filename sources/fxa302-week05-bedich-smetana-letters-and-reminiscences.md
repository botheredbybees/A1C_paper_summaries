## Bedřich Smetana. Letters and Reminiscences

The document is a review by Francis Cameron of 'Bedrich Smetana. Letters and Reminiscences' by Frantisek Bartos, published in the Musical Times in February 1956. The reviewer expresses that while there are interesting details about Franz Liszt’s generosity and Smetana's relations with the Czech Theatre Association, the book lacks intensity compared to Dvorak's letters. He criticizes the absence of critical perspectives on Smetana in favor of adulatory content. Despite these shortcomings, the reviewer praises the presentation quality, format, and binding of the book. The review highlights the historical context of national music movements and their decline, suggesting a shift towards modernist compositions that transcend folk influences.
