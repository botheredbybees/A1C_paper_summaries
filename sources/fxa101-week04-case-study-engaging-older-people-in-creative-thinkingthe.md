## Case study: engaging older people in creative thinking—the Active Energy project

Loraine Leeson's case study discusses the Active Energy project, an initiative that engages older men in creative thinking through technology. The project was initiated as part of a broader commission from SPACE Studios London and Queen Mary University of London to address social isolation among retired men over 50 years old in the London Borough of Tower Hamlets. The Geezers Club, founded following research on older people's participation in clubs and classes, aimed to reduce isolation by encouraging men back into community activities. Leeson facilitated discussions with the group about technology that could support their lives or communities, leading them to explore tidal power as a sustainable energy source. Through this project, members gained technical knowledge and became effective advocates for renewable energy, presenting their ideas at public events. The study highlights how creative arts can empower older individuals, foster intergenerational connections, and contribute to societal well-being.
