## An Online Descriptive Study of Music Educator and Performer Views on Music Performance Anxiety (MPA)

The study explores the views of music educators and performers regarding Music Performance Anxiety (MPA). Previous research has focused on the underlying causes and coping mechanisms of MPA, but this study aims to provide a descriptive analysis of the condition based on participants' experiences and perspectives. The study used descriptive statistics and nonparametric analyses to examine the responses of 409 participants to 54 Likert-type items, assessing their views on the relationship between MPA and specific personality traits. Significant differences were found based on participants' sex assigned at birth, location, teaching and performing experience, and primary teaching areas. A key finding was that those with more teaching and performing experience rated items lower than those with less experience. This study provides new insights into the multidimensional impact of MPA, highlighting the need for further research and effective interventions to manage the condition. Understanding MPA is crucial for educators and performers, as it can significantly affect performance quality and life outcomes.
