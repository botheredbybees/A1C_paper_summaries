## Living With Toxic Wounds: The Voices and Visual Self-Representations of Gulf War Veterans

This qualitative study explores the experiences of Gulf War veterans with Gulf War Illness (GWI), focusing on symptoms, cognitive functioning, quality of life, and care received. Utilizing grounded theory, the researchers conducted interviews and visual art elicitation tasks with 40 veterans. The findings highlight challenging symptoms, persisting uncertainties about GWI's etiology, and variability in treatment effectiveness. Veterans expressed a need for recognition, support, improved healthcare provider knowledge, and better quality of care.
