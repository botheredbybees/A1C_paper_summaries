## Effectiveness of Collage Activity Based on a Life Review in Elderly Cancer Patients: A Preliminary Study

This preliminary study examines the efficacy of collage activities combined with life reviews in elderly cancer patients undergoing palliative care. The research aims to evaluate spiritual well-being, anxiety levels, and self-efficacy before and after intervention. Eleven patients participated in two sessions where they created collages reflecting their life experiences. Evaluations using the FACIT–Sp Scale, HADS, and SESTC showed significant improvements in spiritual well-being, reduced anxiety and depression, and increased self-efficacy among participants. The study highlights the potential benefits of such activities in enhancing quality of life for elderly cancer patients.
