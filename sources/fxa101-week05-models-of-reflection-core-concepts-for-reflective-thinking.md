## Models of reflection – core concepts for reflective thinking

The document explores various models of reflection used in educational and professional contexts, emphasizing their importance for deeper learning. Boud’s triangular representation is introduced as a simple cyclic model illustrating that reflection leads to further learning but lacks guidance on the nature of reflection or its integration back into experience. Gibbs’ reflective cycle offers a more detailed breakdown by incorporating emotional responses and analysis, yet it may still result in superficial reflection due to its lack of critical thinking components. Atkins and Murphy’s cyclical model addresses these limitations by encouraging a deeper level of reflection through identifying challenges, exploring alternatives, and evaluating the impact of learning on practice. This document is relevant for creative arts and health professionals as it enhances their ability to critically analyze experiences and improve their reflective practices.
