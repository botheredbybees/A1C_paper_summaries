## Obesity and healthy eating in Australia

This evidence summary by VicHealth outlines the trends, impacts, and inequities associated with obesity and unhealthy diets in Australia. The document highlights that over a third of Australian adults are overweight or obese, contributing significantly to chronic diseases such as diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and cancer. Socioeconomic disparities exacerbate these issues, with higher rates of obesity among individuals from lower socioeconomic backgrounds, rural residents, and Indigenous populations. Key dietary risk factors include low intake of fruits, vegetables, fiber, milk, nuts, seeds, and whole grains, alongside high consumption of salt, saturated fats, sugar-sweetened beverages, and processed meats. The report underscores the need for tailored interventions to address health inequities related to diet and obesity, emphasizing the importance of healthy eating in reducing disease burden and promoting overall wellbeing.
