## Reading Anna Freud: Advocacy and Relevance in Child Mental Health

This document discusses Anna Freud's relevance in contemporary child mental health through the lens of a book review by Schuyler W. Henderson. The review highlights how Midgely's book 'Reading Anna Freud' presents Freud's contributions while emphasizing her advocacy role, which remains crucial today. The text critiques the separation of intellectual, clinical, research, and advocacy agendas in the field and draws parallels with historical figures like Aichhorn and Montessori. It also addresses the contemporary issue of standardized testing and its impact on children's well-being, advocating for a return to active involvement in education and public health. The review concludes by emphasizing the importance of fulfilling the promises made by pioneering figures in child mental health, despite the challenges posed by modern societal norms.
