## The General Theory of Verbal Humor, twenty years after

This preface introduces a journal issue that collects articles related to the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH), which was proposed twenty years prior. The editor, Salvatore Attardo, explains how these papers were submitted independently and are not meant to provide an exhaustive view of the GTVH's status. He also discusses his own involvement with a co-authored article by Christian Hempelmann that faced delays in publication due to backlog issues at the journal. Acknowledgments are made to Victor Raskin for arranging peer reviews, which followed the precedent set by the original GTVH paper where reviewers remained anonymous but published their comments. The preface concludes by noting improvements in publication timelines following an increase in page count.
