## Health, Wellbeing & Creativity in Mediaeval Europe

This lesson explores health and wellbeing concepts from medieval European society through the lens of creativity. It discusses how medical practices were influenced by religious beliefs, folk medicine, and astrology, with formal medical knowledge rooted in ancient Greek theories but often inaccessible to common people due to expense. Hospitals emerged during this period, inspired by interactions with Islamic medical traditions. Hildegard of Bingen's visionary experiences and medical writings are highlighted as examples of how spiritual insights were intertwined with healthcare practices. The lesson also touches upon the devastating impact of the Black Death on societal structures and beliefs about disease causation.
