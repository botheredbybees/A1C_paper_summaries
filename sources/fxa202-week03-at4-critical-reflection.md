## AT4. CRITICAL REFLECTION

Ishara Love's reflective piece explores the profound impact nature has on human well-being and the therapeutic value of photography. Drawing from Erich Fromm's concept of biophilia, Love recounts her childhood experiences in nature and how these moments offered solace during difficult times. She notes the healing effects she experienced while creating a photo essay, which documented the interplay between humans and their natural surroundings. Love also reflects on the emotional resonance she felt when revisiting her photographs, an experience akin to Roland Barthes' concept of 'punctum.' The process of critically analyzing her work deepened her understanding of how visual storytelling can capture complex emotions and convey a sense of timelessness. This reflection underscores the importance of nature in mental health and the therapeutic potential of photography.
