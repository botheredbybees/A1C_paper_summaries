## FXA101 Week 10 Welcome

This document welcomes students to the tenth week of the FXA101 unit, emphasizing the exploration of light, lighting, and shadow in creative works. It encourages students to study historical and contemporary examples that focus on these elements and to apply their knowledge in a two-dimensional work experiment. The week also guides students to develop a plan for their mini-project for Creative Project 3, emphasizing the importance of regular journal entries and adherence to the assessment rubric. Students are advised to review APA 7 referencing and formatting guidelines. The document concludes by noting that the following week will be the last of the learning materials, providing guidance for the final creative project and reflective review.
