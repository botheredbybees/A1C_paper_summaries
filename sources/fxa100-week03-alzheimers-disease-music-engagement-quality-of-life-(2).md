## Alzheimer’s Disease & Music Engagement: Quality of Life Economic Impact Analysis

The report 'Alzheimer's Disease & Music Engagement: Quality of Life Economic Impact Analysis' was prepared for the NeuroArts Blueprint Initiative by Deloitte LLP in May 2025, with support from AARP and the A. Barry Rand Fund of the AARP Foundation. The study aims to assess the potential economic impact of providing music engagement interventions to individuals diagnosed with Alzheimer's Disease and their caregivers. It identifies specific quality-of-life metrics linked to music engagement and explores downstream economic impacts through monetization pathways. Key findings include positive correlations between active music engagement, reduced caregiver burden, improved quality of life for patients, and cost savings associated with healthcare utilization reduction. The report concludes by emphasizing the importance of considering the broader benefits of arts-based interventions in dementia care policies and funding decisions.
