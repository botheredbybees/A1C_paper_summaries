## FXA302 Week 11 Welcome

FXA302 Week 11 focuses on the final week of learning materials, emphasizing reflection on the student's journey through the course. The week explores how creative and performing artists adapt to injuries that significantly alter their practices, such as Frida Kahlo and JT Hall. The materials also delve into the impact of physical disabilities on music, exemplified by the work of heavy metal musicians and jazz guitarist Django Reinhardt. Additionally, the week includes discussions on the transformative role of the arts in reconstructing wellbeing, highlighted through Doug Auld’s State of Grace project. This project features portraits of young burn victims, challenging traditional notions of beauty. The learning materials also introduce technological advancements that facilitate engagement with the arts for individuals with disabilities. Despite being the last week of provided materials, the module remains open for further discussions and contributions.
