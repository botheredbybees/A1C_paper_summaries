## Experimental Designs in Music Psychology

This lesson introduces research methods and experimental designs used in music psychology. It covers the scientific method's cyclical nature involving observation, formulating hypotheses, testing, and refining theories based on evidence. Key aspects include understanding research questions, developing falsifiable and measurable hypotheses, operationalizing variables, and addressing potential confounds such as participant bias, experimenter influence, fatigue effects, and environmental factors that may impact the study outcomes.
