## A taste of Kandinsky: Assessing the Influence of Artistic Visual Presentation on Dining Experience

This paper explores the impact of visually artistic food presentation on dining experience, using a salad prepared in three different ways: simply mixed, neatly arranged without artistic pretensions, and inspired by Kandinsky's art. Participants rated the artistically presented dish as more complex, artistic, liked, and worthy of higher payment before consumption, and found it tastier after eating compared to other presentations. The study underscores the significant role visual aesthetics play in influencing diners' expectations and actual sensory experience.
