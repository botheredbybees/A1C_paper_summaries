## Humanities and the Medical Home

The chapter 'Humanities and the Medical Home' by Mark Clark, Howard Brody, and Rebecca Hester explores the role of humanities in shaping patient-centered primary care. The authors argue that the medical home model, which emphasizes quality healthcare and patient relationships, requires an interdisciplinary humanities approach to understand and foster effective patient-professional relationships. The chapter highlights the importance of care over cure in healthcare, drawing on historical perspectives from figures like Francis W. Peabody and Edmund Pellegrino. It also discusses the challenges of balancing professional competencies with the dynamic nature of patient care in an ever-evolving healthcare environment. The relevance to creative arts and health lies in the emphasis on holistic care and the importance of cultural and contextual factors in healthcare relationships.
