## Equity: the Arts and Disability Associated Plan

The document 'Equity: the Arts and Disability Associated Plan' is an initiative under Australia’s Cultural Policy, Revive, aiming to improve equity, access, and inclusion in the arts and cultural sector for people with disabilities. It acknowledges the diverse experiences of individuals with disabilities and emphasizes the importance of centring their voices in policy development. The plan highlights the evolving nature of language used to describe disability, advocating for identity-first language to respect individual preferences. It also addresses the intersectionality of disability, recognizing that barriers to access and inclusion can compound based on multiple identities and lived experiences. The plan outlines priorities such as recognizing the central role of d/Deaf and disabled artists, fostering inclusive governance, and ensuring equitable access to arts and culture for all audiences. It also includes actions to build the foundations for change, challenges and opportunities, and emphasizes the need for monitoring and evaluation to assess the impact of the initiatives. This plan is significant for the creative arts and health by promoting inclusive practices and recognizing the value of diverse voices in shaping cultural policies.
