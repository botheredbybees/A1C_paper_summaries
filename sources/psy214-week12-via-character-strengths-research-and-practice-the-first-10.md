## VIA Character Strengths - Research and Practice: The First 10 Years

The document discusses the development and application of the VIA Classification of character strengths and the VIA Inventory of Strengths over the first decade of its existence. The VIA Classification, founded by Martin E. P. Seligman and Christopher Peterson, identifies six core virtues—wisdom, courage, humanity, justice, temperance, and transcendence—and 24 character strengths associated with these virtues. The strengths are considered universal across cultures and are described as the building blocks of human goodness and flourishing. The article highlights the research and practical applications of these strengths in various fields, including positive psychology, psychotherapy, coaching, education, business, and institutional practices. It also emphasizes the VIA Inventory of Strengths, a widely used online assessment tool with good reliability and validity, which has been translated into multiple languages. The document notes the prevalence of certain strengths across different demographics and the consistency of the VIA Classification through various factor analyses.
