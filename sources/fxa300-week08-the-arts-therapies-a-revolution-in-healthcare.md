## The Arts Therapies: A Revolution in Healthcare

The document explores the historical development and cultural contexts of arts therapies, including music therapy and dramatherapy. It challenges the notion of a singular origin story for arts therapies, emphasizing the complexity and multiplicity of influences. The text highlights the resurgence of interest in the therapeutic use of the arts in the 20th century, following a period where the connection between the arts and healthcare was often neglected in Western medical traditions. It discusses the impact of historical events such as World Wars on the development of music therapy, and the evolution of dramatherapy from early theatrical practices to its recognition as a distinct therapeutic discipline. The work underscores the importance of interdisciplinary approaches and the need to consider diverse cultural perspectives in understanding and advancing arts therapies. This exploration is relevant to creative arts and health by providing a historical framework for understanding the development of therapeutic practices and their potential for healing across different cultural contexts.
