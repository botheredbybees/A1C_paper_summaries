## The occurrence of performance anxiety among musicians

This study investigates the occurrence and impact of performance anxiety among musicians at the University of Iowa School of Music. A questionnaire was distributed to 454 music students and faculty members, with a 66.5% response rate. The findings indicate that 16.5% of respondents experienced performance impairment due to anxiety, 21.3% reported marked distress, and 16.1% reported that anxiety adversely affected their careers. Women reported higher levels of distress and impairment compared to men. Common symptoms included poor concentration, rapid heart rate, tremor, sweating, and dry mouth. The study suggests that performance anxiety is a significant issue that may require medical treatment in severe cases.
