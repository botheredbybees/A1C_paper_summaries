## Week 2 – Making Pictures

The document provides an overview of different camera types and their advantages and disadvantages, focusing on mobile phone cameras, point-and-shoot cameras, and digital single-lens reflex (DSLR) cameras. The text emphasizes the importance of having a camera available at all times to capture spontaneous moments and offers tips for improving picture quality with each type of device. For mobile phones, it stresses keeping the lens clean and utilizing apps like Snapseed or Open Camera. Point-and-shoot cameras are praised for their ease-of-use and versatility but noted as limiting when focusing on specific parts of a subject. DSLRs are highlighted for high-quality photos and flexibility in settings but criticized for being bulkier and more expensive than other options. The tutorial encourages experimentation and fun, suggesting that basic photography skills can lead to exciting creative images.
