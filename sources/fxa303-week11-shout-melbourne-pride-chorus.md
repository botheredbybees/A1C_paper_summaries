## shOUT - Melbourne Pride Chorus

Since its inception in 2005, the shOUT Youth Chorus has established itself as a pioneering LGBTQIA+ youth choir in Australasia. Catering to individuals aged between 18 and 29, shOUT offers a unique platform for young people to express their voices through music. The chorus is celebrated for its diverse membership and creative repertoire, which includes dynamic and joyful musical pieces that challenge traditional norms. Over the years, shOUT has garnered significant attention and invitations to perform at prestigious events such as the Melbourne Writers' Festival, Gaytimes Festival, and Paul Mac's performance 'The Rise and Fall of Saint George.' This growing recognition not only highlights the quality of their music but also underscores their importance as emerging leaders in the community. The chorus rehearses weekly on Wednesdays from 6:00 pm to 7:00 pm in South Yarra, with some members also participating in the main Melbourne Pride Chorus (MPC) ensemble. Membership in shOUT provides automatic access to MPC without additional fees. Alex Gorbatov serves as the Musical Director of shOUT and is renowned for his work as a lyric tenor and director of both historical and contemporary music performances.
