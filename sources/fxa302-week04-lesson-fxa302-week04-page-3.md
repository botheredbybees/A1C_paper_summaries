## Health, Wellbeing and Creativity in the Renaissance

This lesson examines the Renaissance period's influence on health and wellbeing through advancements in medicine, art, and societal perceptions of disease. It highlights significant medical thinkers like Fracastorius and Paracelsus who challenged traditional beliefs about illness origins, proposing theories on infectious diseases that diverged from humoral imbalance concepts. Artistic developments are discussed with emphasis on anatomical studies by Leonardo da Vinci and Michelangelo, showcasing enhanced realism in art depicting human anatomy and disease manifestations. The lesson also covers the depiction of cancer signs in Renaissance paintings and the cultural phenomenon of dancing mania as a response to societal stress during outbreaks.
