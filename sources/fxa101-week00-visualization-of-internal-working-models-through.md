## Visualization of Internal Working Models Through Transactional Analysis (TA) Developmental Collage Therapy: A Case Report

This case report explores the use of Transactional Analysis (TA) Developmental Collage Therapy in visualizing and modifying Internal Working Models (IWMs). A woman in her 20s with a history of childhood stuttering created six collages depicting her IWM developmentally from birth to adulthood. The study employed pre- and post-intervention assessments using the Tokyo University Egogram Third Edition (TEG3) and Experiences in Close Relationships inventory-Generalized Other version (ECR-GO). The findings suggest that TA Developmental Collage Therapy can visualize IWM and facilitate its transformation, potentially providing a therapeutic framework for addressing attachment-related difficulties.
