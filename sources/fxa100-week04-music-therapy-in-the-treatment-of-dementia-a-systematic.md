## Music Therapy in the Treatment of Dementia: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

This systematic review and meta-analysis examines the efficacy of music therapy in improving cognitive function, quality of life, and depressive symptoms for individuals with dementia. The study includes eight high-quality randomized controlled trials conducted from 1990 to present, using databases such as Medline, PubMed Central, Embase, PsycINFO, and Cochrane Library. Results indicate that music therapy significantly enhances cognitive function and improves quality of life in the short-term, but does not show evidence of sustained improvement for depressive symptoms. The review concludes that while music therapy offers promising benefits, further research is needed to standardize protocols based on different stages or types of dementia.
