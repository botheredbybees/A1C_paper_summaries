## Introduction to Behavioral Neuroscience

The document 'Introduction to Behavioral Neuroscience' is an educational resource developed by OpenStax, a non-profit initiative at Rice University. This textbook serves as a comprehensive guide to understanding the biological basis of behavior, spanning across various chapters that cover fundamental topics such as the structure and function of the nervous system, neurophysiology, neurochemistry, comparative neuroscience, neurodevelopment, sensory systems, motor control, sexual behavior, stress, and emotion. The book is structured to provide a detailed overview of each topic, including foundational concepts, recent advances, and ongoing research questions. Each chapter is enriched with illustrations, summaries, key terms, and interactive exercises designed to enhance learning and comprehension. This resource is crucial for students and educators in neuroscience, psychology, and related fields, providing a robust foundation in the neural mechanisms underlying behavior and cognition.
