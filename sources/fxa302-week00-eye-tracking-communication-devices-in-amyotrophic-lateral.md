## Eye tracking communication devices in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis: Impact on disability and quality of life

This study evaluates the impact of Eye Tracking Communication Devices (ETCDs) on communication ability and quality of life (QoL) in late-stage Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS) patients with tetraplegia and anarthria. Thirty-five participants used ETCDs, and their communication ability was assessed using the Individually Prioritized Problem Assessment (IPPA) scale, revealing a significant improvement in communication ability compared to the Eye Transfer (ETRAN) board and no device conditions. The Psychosocial Impact of Assistive Devices Scale (PIADS) indicated a large increase in QoL, and the Quebec User Evaluation of Satisfaction with Assistive Technology (QUEST 2.0) showed high user satisfaction. These findings suggest that ETCDs can reduce communication disability and enhance QoL in late-stage ALS patients.
