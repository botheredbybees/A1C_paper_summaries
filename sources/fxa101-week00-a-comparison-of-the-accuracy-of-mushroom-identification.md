## A comparison of the accuracy of mushroom identification applications using digital photographs

This study examines the accuracy of three popular mushroom identification applications (Picture Mushroom, Mushroom Identificator, and iNaturalist) in identifying mushrooms involved in exposures reported to the Victorian Poisons Information Centre and Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria from 2020-2021. Researchers used digital photographs of 78 specimens to test these apps independently, with results confirmed by an expert mycologist. Picture Mushroom was found to be the most accurate app, correctly identifying mushrooms in 49% of cases compared to 35% for both Mushroom Identificator and iNaturalist. The study highlights that while mushroom identification applications may assist clinical toxicologists and the public, they are not sufficiently reliable to exclude exposure to potentially poisonous species.
