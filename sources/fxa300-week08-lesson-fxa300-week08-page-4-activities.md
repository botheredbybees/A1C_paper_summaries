## Week 8 Activities: Music and Health

This week's lesson in the FXA300 Music, Mind, and Body course focuses on the therapeutic benefits of music, particularly through the example of a collaborative program, Strokestra, which involves the Royal Philharmonic Orchestra and the community stroke service in Hull, UK. The video accompanying this lesson explores how group music-making activities can improve physical function and emotional wellbeing for stroke survivors. Recommended reading includes Chapter 15 from Hodges and McPherson's 2025 book, Music in the Human Experience, which discusses music therapy in treating various medical conditions. The week also includes a reminder about the upcoming due date for Assessment Task 2: Case Studies, and provides references to key studies and theories in the field of music therapy and its applications in medical rehabilitation.
