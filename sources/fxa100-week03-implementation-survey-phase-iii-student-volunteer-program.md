## Implementation Survey Phase III - Student Volunteer Program

The document presents the results of an implementation survey conducted in August 2016 for Phase III of the Wisconsin Music & Memory Student Volunteer Program. The survey focuses on assessing the progress and impact of the program across various dimensions such as the number of songs added to iTunes libraries, improvement shown by residents participating in the program, reduction or cessation of anti-psychotic/anti-anxiety medications, and use of bed alarms. Additionally, it evaluates methods used to expand the program, including facility iPod drives, community donations, and media promotions. The survey also gauges overall effectiveness for residents, family/friends, staff, and the impact of monthly webinars on volunteers’ experience. Key findings indicate positive impacts on resident engagement, behavior, medication reduction, and increased volunteer hours contributed by student volunteers.
