## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Positive Psychology 2

The lesson explores positive psychology, focusing on character strengths and meaning in life. It prompts students to reflect on their personal strengths and whether they use these regularly. The lesson also invites consideration of the meaningful aspects of life and how these are engaged with. Topics covered include an overview of character strengths, a signature strengths activity, the history and recent approaches to understanding meaning in life, and the practices and outcomes related to finding meaning. The required readings for the week are outlined, emphasizing their importance for the assessment tasks.
