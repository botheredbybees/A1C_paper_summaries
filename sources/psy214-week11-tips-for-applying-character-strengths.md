## Tips for Applying Character Strengths

This webpage, authored by Dr. Ryan Niemiec, provides a series of tips for individuals to apply their character strengths in novel ways to enhance their well-being. The tips are organized around the 24 universal character strengths identified by the VIA Classification of Character Strengths. Each strength is paired with practical suggestions for daily application, such as using creativity to solve problems through non-verbal means, practicing mindfulness and perspective by engaging with diverse viewpoints, or expressing zest through energetic and vibrant activities. The intervention described is based on research showing that using one's signature strengths in new ways can boost happiness and reduce depression. The article also encourages readers to reflect on their personal experiences and to create their own unique applications of these strengths. The tips are intended to promote personal growth and well-being by encouraging individuals to explore and leverage their strengths in innovative contexts.
