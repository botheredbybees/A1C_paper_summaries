## Signature Strengths Activity: Applying Character Strengths for Well-being

This lesson explores the concept of signature strengths and their application in enhancing well-being. It emphasizes that merely identifying one's strengths is insufficient; the key lies in applying them in new ways. The VIA Institute on Character offers a survey to identify top strengths, and the lesson suggests finding novel applications for these strengths. Research indicates that deploying strengths can increase positive emotions and decrease distress, leading to improved engagement, enjoyment, and performance in various settings. Studies by Seligman et al. (2005) and others demonstrate short-term benefits of strength application, including increased happiness and reduced depressive symptoms. Additionally, using strengths is linked to goal achievement and the satisfaction of psychological needs, contributing to overall well-being.
