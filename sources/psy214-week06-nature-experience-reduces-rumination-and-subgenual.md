## Nature Experience Reduces Rumination and Subgenual Prefrontal Cortex Activation

This study investigates the impact of nature exposure on mental well-being, focusing specifically on how a brief walk in natural settings can reduce rumination and neural activity in the subgenual prefrontal cortex (sgPFC). Researchers found that participants who took a 90-minute walk through a natural environment experienced decreased levels of self-reported rumination and reduced sgPFC activation compared to those walking in an urban setting. The study highlights potential mechanisms by which nature engagement may buffer against mental illness, suggesting that easily accessible green spaces within urban areas could play a critical role in maintaining psychological health amid increasing urbanization.
