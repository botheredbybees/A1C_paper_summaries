## A Mindfulness Hospital Stay

Olivia Bowman's 'mindfulness hospital stay' is a series of photographs taken during an unexpected visit to the emergency department due to a severe migraine. The photos capture both the physical environment and Bowman's emotional state as she navigates the unfamiliar setting with her camera. Each image highlights different aspects of her experience, such as the stark contrast between focused medical equipment (like heart rate monitors) and blurred surroundings, illustrating feelings of vulnerability amidst clinical precision. By photographing her IV setup and meals, Bowman transforms mundane hospital activities into visually intriguing compositions, using techniques like shallow depth of field to emphasize specific elements. The series also includes abstract images of blankets seen as landscapes, showcasing her exploration of movement and light under constraints. Through these photographs, Bowman finds a way to engage with her environment curiously rather than passively, turning a potentially distressing situation into an opportunity for creative expression.
