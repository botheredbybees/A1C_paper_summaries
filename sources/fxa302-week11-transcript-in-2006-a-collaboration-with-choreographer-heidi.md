## Five Open Mouths: An Autobiographical Contemporary Dance Work

In 2006, Lisa Bufano collaborated with choreographer Heidi Latsky to create an autobiographical contemporary dance piece titled 'Five Open Mouths.' The performance is centered around a pivotal moment in Bufano's life when she first unwrapped the bandages from her hands after the amputation of her fingers. The choreography uses an 'unwinding' gesture to symbolize this significant event. The dance performance includes scenes where Bufano uses carbon-fibre prostheses to run and dance, and it unfolds through five distinct tableaux. The work explores themes of resilience and the interplay between disability and art.
