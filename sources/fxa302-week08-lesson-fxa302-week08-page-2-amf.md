## Mental Illness and Visual Art

This lesson explores the connection between mental illness and visual art through the works of Vincent van Gogh and Edvard Munch. Van Gogh's life and art are examined through case studies, highlighting how his mental health struggles influenced his artistic output, such as his self-portraits and the painting 'Starry Night.' The use of visual elements like muted tones and sideways glances are discussed as representations of his mental state. Similarly, Munch's iconic 'The Scream' is analyzed as an expression of anxiety and existential angst, reflecting his personal experiences with mental health issues. The lesson concludes by emphasizing the therapeutic potential of creative arts for individuals with mental illnesses.
