## Injuries in Musicians - Imaging and Management

Injuries in Musicians - Imaging and Management is a comprehensive resource that addresses the unique injuries musicians face due to repetitive movements, sustained postures, and performance-related stress. Edited by Kanaka Durga Prasad Bhamidipaty, Rajesh Botchu, and Veenadhari Bhamidipaty, the book integrates clinical expertise with imaging interpretation to provide a systematic approach to understanding and managing music-related injuries. The text explores the cultural and historical context of global music, classifies musical instruments, and discusses specific injuries associated with vocal and instrumental performance. It also covers postural abnormalities and ergonomic challenges unique to musicians, along with rehabilitation strategies. The final chapter emphasizes the importance of restoring artistic expression alongside physical function, reflecting on the interplay between physical fitness and musical aesthetics. This book is a valuable resource for physicians, radiologists, and rehabilitation specialists involved in the care of performing artists, aiming to support the health and longevity of musicians worldwide.
