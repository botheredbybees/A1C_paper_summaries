## Music, Dance and Neurological Illness

The lesson explores the impact of neurological conditions on music and dance, focusing on the therapeutic benefits and personal stories of resilience. It discusses the use of rhythmic music and dance in neurorehabilitation for conditions like Parkinson's disease and traumatic brain injury. It also examines the role of music therapy in dementia and stroke rehabilitation, and how arts-based therapies enhance substance abuse recovery. Case studies include Jason Becker, a guitarist with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, who continues to compose music using eye-tracking technology, and Michelle Ryan, a dancer who transitioned to a new career after being diagnosed with multiple sclerosis.
