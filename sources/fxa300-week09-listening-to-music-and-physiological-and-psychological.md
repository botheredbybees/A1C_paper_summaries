## Listening to music and physiological and psychological functioning: The mediating role of emotion regulation and stress reactivity

This study investigates the relationship between habitual music listening behavior and physiological and psychological functioning, focusing on the mediating roles of emotion regulation and stress reactivity. The research involved an internet-based survey of university students, measuring various aspects of music listening behavior, emotion regulation, stress reactivity, and physiological and psychological functioning. The findings indicate that qualitative reasons for listening to music, such as reducing loneliness and aggression or arousing specific emotions, are significantly related to psychological and physiological functioning. These effects are mediated by distress augmenting emotion regulation and individual stress reactivity. The study concludes that habitual music listening is a multifaceted behavior influenced by personal dispositions and does not have a clear direct link to physiological and psychological functioning. This research contributes to understanding the underlying mechanisms of music's impact on health and well-being.
