## Wisconsin Music & Memory Implementation Survey Results

The document presents the results of a survey conducted to assess the implementation and effectiveness of the Wisconsin Music & Memory program in long-term care facilities during two phases: Phase I (June - December 2014) and Phase II (January - June 2015). The primary focus was on evaluating improvements in residents' well-being, reductions in medication usage for behavioral issues, and overall satisfaction with the program among staff, families, and residents. Key findings indicate significant progress across multiple dimensions: improved resident mood and engagement, reduced reliance on anti-psychotic or anti-anxiety medications, decreased use of bed alarms, and high levels of enthusiasm from all stakeholders involved. The survey also highlighted positive feedback regarding program expansion efforts and the utility of support webinars offered by DHS staff for implementing Music & Memory effectively across facilities.
