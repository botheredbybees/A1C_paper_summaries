## AutoStory: Generating Diverse Storytelling Images with Minimal Human Efforts

The article introduces AutoStory, a system designed to generate diverse and high-quality storytelling images with minimal human intervention. The authors highlight the challenges of creating visually consistent story scenes that are both aesthetically pleasing and aligned with text descriptions while maintaining character identity consistency across multiple images. Previous approaches have overly simplified these requirements by focusing on specific styles or requiring detailed user inputs such as sketches. AutoStory overcomes this limitation through a method that leverages large language models for layout planning and uses dense control conditions like keypoints to generate high-quality images. The system includes a module for transforming sparse control signals into more detailed ones, enhancing the quality of generated images while allowing intuitive user interactions. Additionally, it offers a technique to ensure consistent character representations across different views without needing extensive manual input or image data collection. This innovation supports broader creative applications in areas like art creation and education by providing flexible and efficient ways to visualize narratives.
