## Music Therapy Training Requirements

The chapter 'Music Therapy Training Requirements' by Suzanne B. Hanser discusses the skills and competencies expected of trained music therapists, focusing on the standards and guidelines established in the USA since the first course in 1919 and the first professional association in 1950. It includes perspectives from eighteen music therapy educators who emphasize the integration of various elements of training, such as supervised clinical experience, self-growth through music, and critical thinking skills. Educators highlight the importance of a student's personal development and the teacher/student relationship in fostering effective learning. The chapter also addresses the diversity of ideologies that influence training practices and the challenges in providing comprehensive education for music therapists.
