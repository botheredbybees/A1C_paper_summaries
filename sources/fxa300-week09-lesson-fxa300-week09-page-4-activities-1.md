## FXA300 Music, Mind and Body Week 9 Activities

This week's lesson in the FXA300 Music, Mind, and Body course focuses on reading and analyzing the introduction sections of several studies to understand their structure and content. Students are directed to read the introductory parts of studies by Peretz et al., Koreimann et al., and Schubert & Stevens, paying attention to how the authors present rationale, aims, and hypotheses. It also includes a discussion on the use of binaural beats in stress and anxiety therapy, addressing issues of evidence quality and potential ethical concerns. The lesson concludes with advice on planning for the research proposal (Assessment Task 4) and emphasizes the importance of not using a case study for this assignment.
