## The role of music therapy in physical rehabilitation: a systematic literature review

Claire M. Weller and Felicity A. Baker conducted a systematic literature review to evaluate the role of music therapy (MT) in physical rehabilitation. The review explored the effectiveness of MT interventions, the functions of music in rehabilitation, and the role of music therapists. They analyzed 15 studies published between January 2000 and June 2008, focusing on participants with physical impairments such as Parkinson's disease, stroke, cerebral palsy, and osteoarthritis. The review found consistent positive and significant results in MT methods for rehabilitation of gait parameters, fine, and gross motor functioning. Music was found to increase motivation, provide an external timekeeper, and facilitate structured rehabilitation interventions. The music therapists engaged patients through music interventions, assessed behaviors, adapted interventions, and provided physical support. Despite these promising findings, the authors emphasize the need for further research to explore the role of MT in current standard rehabilitation services. This review provides valuable insights into the potential of music therapy to enhance physical, psychological, cognitive, and emotional functioning in physical rehabilitation.
