## Motivation and Non-Participation in a Contributory Citizen Science Project

This research explores the motivations and factors influencing participation in Earthworm Watch, a UK-based citizen science project focused on soil biodiversity data collection. The study found that while direct contact with project staff significantly increased survey return rates, physical materials had no significant impact. Female participants were more numerous across all age groups, and younger individuals cited career-related motives more frequently. Understanding motivations varied by demographic factors but influenced the quantity of surveys submitted rather than participation likelihood. These insights suggest a tailored engagement approach considering participant demographics could enhance future citizen science projects' success.
