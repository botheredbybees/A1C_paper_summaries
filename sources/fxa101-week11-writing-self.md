## Writing Self

The chapter 'Writing Self' by Christopher Johns discusses the importance of bringing the mind home to engage in reflective practice. It emphasizes the need to slow down and clear the mind to reconnect with one's experience and find meaning in life. The author highlights that mindfulness can be achieved through various practices, such as breathing exercises and yoga, rather than just formal meditation. Susan Brooks, a healthcare professional, shares her experience of learning to bring her mind home, which has enhanced her leadership skills and personal well-being. The chapter underscores the value of writing as a means to capture and reflect on one's experiences, fostering a deeper understanding of one's vision and purpose in professional practice. Reflective writing is portrayed as a tool to uncover personal potential and enhance professional artistry, encouraging practitioners to document their encounters and reflections to better understand their role in healthcare.
