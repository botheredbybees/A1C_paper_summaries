## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: Orientation to Unit Assessments

This module provides an orientation to the major assessments for PSY114 and PSY214, including a 35% information sheet and a 45% workbook with two assessed parts. It emphasizes systematic engagement with the material based on students' familiarity levels, highlighting key resources such as rubrics, scholarly evidence usage, academic integrity guidelines, and practical walkthroughs for assignments. The content is designed to help students navigate their assessments successfully by providing comprehensive guidance and feed-forward information.
