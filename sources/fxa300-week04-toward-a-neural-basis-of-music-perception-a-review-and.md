## Toward a neural basis of music perception – a review and updated model

Stefan Koelsch's review article updates a previous model of music perception, focusing on the neural correlates and processes involved in perceiving music. The study delineates various stages such as acoustic analysis, auditory memory, and auditory scene integration, emphasizing the role of different brain modules. It highlights findings from EEG and fMRI studies illustrating how these processes unfold over time and where they occur within the brain. Koelsch also discusses musical training effects on auditory brainstem responses and its implications for language learning and emotional communication. The review underscores music’s importance in understanding human cognition, emotion, and social functions.
