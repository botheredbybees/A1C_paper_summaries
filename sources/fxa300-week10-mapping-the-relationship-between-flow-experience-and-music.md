## Mapping the relationship between flow experience and music performance anxiety: a scoping review

This scoping review examines the relationship between Flow Experience (FE) and Music Performance Anxiety (MPA) in the context of musical performance and education. The review synthesizes literature suggesting a predominantly negative correlation between FE and MPA, with moderate anxiety levels potentially facilitating flow when perceived as a challenge rather than a threat. Key findings indicate that emotional self-regulation, self-efficacy, and self-esteem are mediating factors in this relationship, and interventions involving yoga, mindfulness, and self-regulation training can reduce MPA and enhance FE. The review highlights the need for future research to adopt multimodal and longitudinal approaches to better understand the underlying mechanisms and develop evidence-informed strategies for performance preparation and emotional regulation among musicians.
