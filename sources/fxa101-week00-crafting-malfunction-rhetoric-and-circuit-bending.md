## Crafting Malfunction: Rhetoric and Circuit-Bending

The article explores the intersection between circuit-bending, an art practice from the 1960s involving creative short-circuiting of battery-powered toys and instruments, and rhetoric and writing. The authors document their own circuit-bending process with a Casio SK-1 synthesizer, emphasizing access, chance, indeterminacy, invention, discovery, and collaboration between humans and nonhumans. They discuss the value of embracing malfunction in teaching creative processes and highlight the potential for circuit-bending as an educational tool to encourage students' creativity and experimentation. The article concludes by noting the current challenges faced by circuit-bending due to changes in technology manufacturing but argues its continued relevance in understanding rule-based systems.
