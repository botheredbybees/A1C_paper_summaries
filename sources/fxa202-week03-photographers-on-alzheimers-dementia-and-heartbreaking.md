## Photographers on Alzheimer's, Dementia, and Heartbreaking Memory Loss

This article by Jon Feinstein discusses various photographers who explore Alzheimer's disease and other forms of memory loss through their work. The piece highlights the personal and artistic responses of Stephen DiRado, Cheryle St. Onge, Birthe Piontek, and others who document their loved ones' experiences with dementia. DiRado’s series 'With Dad' captures his father's progression through Alzheimer's over two decades, emphasizing the emotional toll on both photographer and subject. Similarly, St. Onge photographs her mother during her battle with vascular dementia in a project called 'Calling the Birds Home,' aiming to preserve dignity and maintain connection despite cognitive decline. Piontek’s series 'Abendlied' uses surreal imagery to convey the psychological impact of dementia through family objects and portraits. These projects illustrate how photography can serve as both a coping mechanism and a means to raise awareness about Alzheimer's, offering insights into the emotional and physical transformations brought on by the disease.
