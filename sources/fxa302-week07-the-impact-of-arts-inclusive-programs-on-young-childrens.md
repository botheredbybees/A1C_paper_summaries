## The impact of arts-inclusive programs on young children’s mental health and wellbeing: a rapid review

This rapid review evaluates the impact of arts-inclusive programs (AIPs) on young children's mental health and wellbeing, synthesizing literature from three databases: SCOPUS, psycINFO, and ERIC. The study focuses on children aged 0-6 years and includes nine studies encompassing 3,671 participants. Key themes identified include music-related activities (n=4), artmaking (n=3), and storytelling/drama (n=2). All included studies reported positive outcomes on children’s wellbeing following engagement in AIPs. However, the review highlights that most studies were of low quality and utilized varying outcome measures. The findings suggest emerging evidence for positive impacts of arts engagement on young children's wellbeing but stress a lack of high-quality research in this area. This study underscores the need for further robust research to validate the benefits of arts programs for early childhood mental health and development.
