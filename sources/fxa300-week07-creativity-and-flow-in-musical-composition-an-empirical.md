## Creativity and flow in musical composition: an empirical investigation

The article explores the relationship between creativity and the concept of flow, as developed by Csikszentmihalyi, in the context of musical composition. The study involves first-year university students completing a group composition task over three meetings, with detailed questionnaires assessing the experience of the compositional process. The compositions are then rated for quality and creativity by both the participants and a group of music education specialists. Results indicate significant correlations between the quality of compositions and students' experiences of creativity and flow. The research aims to enhance understanding of the processes and outcomes of group music composition tasks and suggests practical implications for music education, emphasizing the importance of social and psychological factors in fostering creativity. This study contributes to the field by providing a methodological framework for investigating the link between flow and creative music-making, thus informing educational strategies for developing creative skills in music.
