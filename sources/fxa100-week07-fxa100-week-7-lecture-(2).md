## FXA100 Week 7 Lecture

The lecture for week seven of the FXA100 course focuses on the arts and dementia care, specifically discussing renowned gallery programs designed to engage individuals with dementia through reminiscence and creativity. Key initiatives such as ARTZ – Artists for Alzheimer’s are highlighted for their direct impact in fostering emotional connections and independence among people living with dementia. The lecture outlines ARTZ’s planning recommendations for conducting tours and mentions the success of similar programs at institutions like the Museum of Modern Art (MoMA). Additionally, it touches upon various Australian initiatives such as the National Gallery of Australia's Making It program aimed at engaging both individuals with dementia and their caregivers through interactive art experiences online. The lecture also emphasizes the importance of accessibility and tailored support when planning gallery visits for people with dementia, underlining how these programs contribute to enhancing emotional well-being and cognitive engagement.
