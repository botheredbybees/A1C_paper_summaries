## Design for Your Research Proposal - FXA300 Music, Mind and Body

This lesson focuses on designing a research proposal for assessing the effects of music on the brain and body or strategies for mitigating musicians' injuries. The proposal should include an introduction, method, expected results and discussion sections. The introduction requires an overview of the topic, literature review, rationale, aim, and hypothesis. The method section should detail participants, materials, and procedure. The lesson emphasizes ethical research, clear language, and active voice in writing.
