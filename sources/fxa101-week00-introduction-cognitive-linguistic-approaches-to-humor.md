## Introduction: Cognitive Linguistic Approaches to Humor

This paper introduces the concept of cognitive linguistic approaches to humor, drawing on Victor Raskin's seminal work and discussing the influence of cognitive linguistics (CL) on humor research. It contrasts CL with formalist traditions, emphasizing that language is part of cognition rather than a separate module. The paper highlights the role of conceptualization processes in construing meaning and explores how these processes are applied to understand humor. Key topics include semantic mechanisms, script theory of humor, salience, and frame-shifting. The authors argue for integrating cognitive linguistic principles with humor research to explore marked language use and unexpectedness in humorous texts.
