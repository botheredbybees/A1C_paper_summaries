## FXA303 Web Lecture 6 wk 6 2026

This document provides guidance for students in the FXA303 Creative Arts, Play and Mental Wellbeing course at University of Tasmania. The main focus is on researching an event that impacted mental wellbeing through survivor accounts, medical literature, news articles, and other publicly accessible sources such as websites and blogs. Students are encouraged to create a puppet based on their research and express personal responses to the events experienced by survivors. Key elements include acknowledging sources in APA 7 style, evaluating emotional and physical reactions to survivor stories, and discussing how these insights influenced the creation of the puppet. The document outlines specific questions for reflection and writing about the event's impact on mental health and the process of making a creative response to it.
