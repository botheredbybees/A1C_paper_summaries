## Feminist HCI and Narratives of Design Semantics in DIY Music Hardware

This article explores the feminist Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) perspective on DIY musical instruments created by women, focusing on design semantics and fabulations of alternative futures. It examines ten self-made music hardware devices through the lens of feminist HCI principles to uncover narratives that challenge gender biases in commercial music technology. The analysis reveals how these DIY instruments contribute to a more inclusive technological landscape by questioning established norms and fostering diverse perspectives.
