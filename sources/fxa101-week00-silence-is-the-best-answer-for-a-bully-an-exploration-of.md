## Silence is the best answer for a bully: an exploration of humour techniques in selected Nigerian newspaper political cartoons

This paper explores the use of linguistic and semiotic humour techniques in Nigerian newspaper political cartoons, focusing on three major newspapers: Punch, The Guardian, and Daily Trust. Utilizing Salvatore Attardo and Victor Raskin's General Theory of Verbal Humour (GTVH), the study identifies six humor techniques - exaggeration, humorous metaphor, distortion of formulaic expressions, neologism, innovative collocation, and sarcasm - employed to satirize governance issues such as misgovernance, corruption, and insecurity. The research highlights how political cartoons serve not only as a form of social critique but also as a means to influence public opinion through humor.
