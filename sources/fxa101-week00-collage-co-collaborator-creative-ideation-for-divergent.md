## Collage Co-Collaborator: Creative Ideation for Divergent Thinking in Art Making

This paper presents the Collage Collaborator, a human-AI co-creation system that supports early-stage creative exploration in physical collage-making. The system combines an interactive web interface with a generative AI backend to provide rapid image generation and customization based on user input. It aims to address challenges faced by novice collagers by offering visual suggestions that users can adapt, reinterpret or reject without replacing their authorship. Through preliminary evaluations, the study finds that this form of scaffolding promotes divergent exploration and creative confidence but also highlights tensions around feasibility and originality. The research underscores the potential for AI as an ideation partner in everyday creative practices.
