## Looking into Later Life: A Psychoanalytic Approach to Depression and Dementia in Old Age

The document 'Looking into Later Life: A Psychoanalytic Approach to Depression and Dementia in Old Age' edited by Rachael Davenhill is a comprehensive exploration of the psychological and emotional well-being of the elderly through a psychoanalytic lens. The book, part of the Tavistock Clinic Series, applies psychoanalytic theories to the complex issues of depression and dementia in later life. It covers a wide range of topics, including individual psychotherapy, couples therapy, group therapy, and institutional consultation, highlighting the refractory nature of these conditions and the need for specialized therapeutic approaches. The text also includes personal narratives and creative works that convey the experience of living with these conditions, offering a nuanced understanding of the psychological mechanisms involved and the importance of reflective observational methods in clinical practice. This volume not only advances the theoretical understanding of mental health issues in the elderly but also critiques the inadequate provision of psychological care for this demographic.
