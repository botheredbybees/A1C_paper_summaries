## FXA101 Week 9: Atmosphere and Creative Sensing

This week's lesson in the FXA101 Creativity and Ageing module focuses on the experience of sensing atmosphere through a period of quiet contemplation. Students are instructed to spend 10 minutes in a quiet setting, either outdoors or looking through a window, to attune themselves to the atmosphere of the place. Following this, they are to write a brief description (30-40 words) of their experience. The discussion task involves reflecting on the transition from contemplation to writing, with an emphasis on referencing an academic journal article. Students are also guided to choose a creative mini-project for their Creative Project 3, ensuring it differs from previous projects in both approach and genre. The objectives include developing research, creative, analytical, and communication skills.
