## Making Noise: Using Sound-Art to Explore Technological Fluency

The paper discusses a course designed to enhance technological fluency among undergraduate students from diverse backgrounds by exploring sound-art and experimental electronic music through hands-on projects involving electronics and programming. The aim is to introduce computational principles in an engaging context, fostering creativity and problem-solving skills. Course content includes readings on pioneering figures in electronic music and sound-art, weekly listening assignments, and practical sessions using Arduino for programming and hardware hacking. Projects range from circuit-bending toy hacks to custom oscillator circuits used as live-performance instruments, encouraging interdisciplinary collaboration and creative expression.
