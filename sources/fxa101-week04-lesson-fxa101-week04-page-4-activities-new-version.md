## FXA101 Creativity for Life Week 4 Activities

This week's lesson in FXA101 Creativity for Life focuses on enhancing creativity and well-being among older adults. Key readings include 'Facilitating arts participation for creative ageing: an action research in South Korea' by Cho & Chang (2024), which explores self-reported benefits of creativity for improved wellbeing, sense of self, and healthy aging. Suggested further reading includes McDonough & Dulas's 2024 article on cognitive functioning activities that are beneficial to brain health as we age. The required exercise is a creative activity using only triangles to depict vehicles, buildings, or Australian native animals/birds. Discussions and assessment tasks, including a quiz covering weeks 1-4 content, are scheduled with deadlines.
