## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Week 10 Wrap-up

This lesson provides an overview of current and upcoming assessments for PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness. It includes information about the regular weekly quiz for Week 10, the deadline for AT2 Information Sheet, and the assessable workbook activities for Part 3, which focuses on Positive Psychology. The lesson directs students to various resources within the course for further details and encourages them to review feedback from Part 1 of the workbook.
