## Defining Mindfulness, Revisited

This lesson revisits the definition of mindfulness provided by Jon Kabat-Zinn, emphasizing the importance of paying attention in the present moment, purposefully and non-judgmentally. It mentions the challenges in mindfulness research due to multiple definitions and introduces the concept of mindful attitudes integral to mindfulness training programs such as MBSR. The lesson includes a video by Kabat-Zinn, slides for further reading, and a check-in section for self-assessment.
