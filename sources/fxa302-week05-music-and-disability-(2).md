## Music and Disability

This essay recontextualizes the relationship between music and disability beyond medicalized approaches by advocating for a social and cultural perspective that views disability as an aspect of human diversity rather than pathology. The author explores three types of musical activities influenced by disabilities: autistic music making, deaf music making, and mobility-inflected music making. Autistic music making emphasizes local coherence, private associations, and imitation, suggesting unique approaches to pitch perception, composition, performance, and comprehension based on detailed attention and idiosyncratic associative networks. This perspective enriches understanding of musical creativity within the broader context of disability studies.
