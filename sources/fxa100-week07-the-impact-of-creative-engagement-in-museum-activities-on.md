## The impact of creative engagement in museum activities on the well-being of people living with dementia, their carers, and family members: The arts programming “Susitikime muziejuje” case in Lithuania

The study examines the impact of a museum arts program called “Susitikime muziejuje” in Lithuania on the well-being of people living with dementia, their caregivers, and family members. The research highlights that engaging in art activities through this cross-sector partnership provides participants with a safe and friendly environment to learn, express themselves, and experience positive emotions without negative outcomes. The program includes museum tours and workshops designed specifically for individuals with dementia and their support networks. Evaluations conducted through focus groups and observations revealed significant benefits such as increased self-esteem, mood enhancement, community building, and improved communication among participants. This initiative underscores the potential of cultural institutions to contribute positively to dementia care by fostering person-centered approaches and cross-sectorial collaborations.
