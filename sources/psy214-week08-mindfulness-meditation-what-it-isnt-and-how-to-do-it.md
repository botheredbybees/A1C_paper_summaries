## Mindfulness Meditation: What It Isn’t and How to Do It

The document outlines common misconceptions about mindfulness meditation and provides a brief guide on how to practice it. It clarifies that mindfulness meditation is not about clearing or emptying the mind, controlling thoughts, or achieving a specific state of relaxation, bliss, or happiness. Instead, it is a practice that involves paying attention to the present moment, particularly to one's breath. The process involves getting prepared, focusing on the breath, recognizing when the mind has wandered, acknowledging the distraction, and returning to the breath. This cycle is repeated frequently, and the document emphasizes that mindfulness meditation is not usually an easy practice, particularly not in the beginning stages.
