## Art Therapy Improves Mood, and Reduces Pain and Anxiety When Offered at Bedside During Acute Hospital Treatment

This study explores the impact of art therapy on mood, anxiety, and pain levels in patients undergoing acute hospital treatment at a large urban teaching hospital. Through a chart review of 195 participants who voluntarily engaged in bedside art therapy sessions led by registered art therapists, significant improvements were observed across all demographic groups in terms of mood, pain, and anxiety levels. Art therapy was found to be effective regardless of gender, age, or specific medical diagnosis, suggesting its potential as a safe and cost-effective adjunct intervention alongside traditional medical management. The study highlights the therapeutic benefits of creative expression for managing psychosocial distress among hospitalized patients.
