## Psychomusicology: Music, Mind, & Brain Salutes New Scholars—Special Issue on the SysMus16 Conference

This editorial by Annabel J. Cohen introduces a special issue in Psychomusicology: Music, Mind & Brain dedicated to the SysMus16 conference proceedings from the University of Jyväskylä. The special issue showcases research contributions from young scholars, highlighting their innovative work on topics such as bodily motion responses to music, educational settings, and social tagging on the Internet. The article emphasizes the importance of these studies for advancing systematic musicology and psychomusicology fields, noting that they explore cutting-edge ideas relevant to creative arts and health.
