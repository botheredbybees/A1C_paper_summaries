## Effects of repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation combined with music therapy in non-fluent aphasia after stroke

This study investigates the clinical efficacy of low-frequency repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation (rTMS) combined with music therapy for treating non-fluent aphasia after stroke. A randomized controlled trial was conducted with 60 patients who were randomly assigned to a traditional therapy group, a music therapy group, and a combined therapy group. The study evaluated language function and depression before and three weeks after treatment using standardized scales. Results indicated that the combined therapy group showed significant improvements in all outcomes compared to traditional therapy, and better results in depression than the music therapy group. The music therapy group also showed significant improvements in repetition and depression compared to the traditional therapy group. The study concludes that low-frequency rTMS combined with music therapy is a feasible and safe method for improving language function and depression in patients with non-fluent aphasia. The findings contribute to the understanding of effective treatment strategies for aphasia and highlight the potential benefits of combining rTMS and music therapy in rehabilitation settings.
