## A Programming Platform Design for Children to Create and Explore Hybrid Musical Instruments

This article details the design and development of a Python-based platform for children to create hybrid musical instruments using LEGO sensors and Raspberry Pi technology in educational workshops. The study aimed to engage young learners in coding activities, focusing on inter-disciplinary STEAM education combining music, construction, and programming. Researchers created a simplified API to enable students with little or no previous experience to successfully code within one hour. The platform was tested with 61 children aged 11-13 years across two workshops, fostering skills like design thinking, teamwork, and problem-solving through hands-on learning. The article discusses technical challenges, solutions, and positive feedback from teaching staff.
