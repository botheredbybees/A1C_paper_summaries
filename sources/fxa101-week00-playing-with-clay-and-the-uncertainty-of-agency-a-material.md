## Playing with Clay and the Uncertainty of Agency: A Material Engagement Theory Perspective

This paper explores the process of sculpting clay through the lens of Material Engagement Theory (MET) to understand the mutable sense of agency and self during artistic creation. It discusses how sculpting can lead to a loss of personal identity, create anxiety about systemic phenomena rather than personal attributes, and develop meaning in art associated with an anterospective viewpoint. The paper also delves into the concept of emergent meaning within the logic of extended cognition, highlighting that artistic creativity is not opaque but proceeds similarly to other human sense-making activities. Finally, it proposes using clay sculpting as a tool for material conceptualization.
