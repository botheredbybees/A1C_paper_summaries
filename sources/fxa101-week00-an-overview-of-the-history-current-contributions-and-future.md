## An Overview of the History, Current Contributions and Future Outlook of iNaturalist in Australia

The paper provides an overview of iNaturalist's history and current contributions in Australia, focusing on the exponential growth of citizen science data since its inception in 2008. The authors discuss how iNaturalist, one of the most successful biodiversity citizen science platforms globally, has become increasingly integrated into professional scientific practices due to advances in statistical techniques that mitigate quality concerns. They highlight significant events like the closure of BowerBird and its migration to iNaturalist, as well as collaborations with the Atlas of Living Australia (ALA) and participation in the City Nature Challenge, which have driven user engagement and data contributions. The paper also outlines important future directions for maximizing the utility of these data for ecological research, conservation, and policy.
