## Healthy Eating: The Big Picture

This lesson discusses the importance of healthy eating and physical activity in lifestyle medicine, emphasizing that there is strong consensus among experts about key features of a healthy diet. The concept 'Eat food, not too much, mostly plants' by Michael Pollan reflects scientific evidence. The Australian Dietary Guidelines recommend diverse nutritious foods from five groups daily while limiting discretionary and ultra-processed foods. Australians generally consume insufficient plant-based foods and excessive processed foods, contributing to health risks. Eating more whole grains, fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts, and seeds is crucial for optimal health and sustainability.
