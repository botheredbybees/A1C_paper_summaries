## The impact of a commercial wearable tactile bass system on the emotions expressed by music

This paper explores the effectiveness of a commercial wearable tactile bass system (WTBS) in conveying musical emotions to deaf and hearing individuals. The study employed a set of film music excerpts presented under two conditions: auditory and tactile, and tactile with an auditory masking stimulus. Participants rated their emotional responses on arousal and valence scales. Results indicated that while the WTBS retained arousal levels, it diminished participants' ability to recognize valence accurately. This suggests limitations in using WTBS for transmitting the full emotional spectrum of music.
