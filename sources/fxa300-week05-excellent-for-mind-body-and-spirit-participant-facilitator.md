## 'Excellent for mind, body and spirit': Participant, facilitator, and community stakeholder experiences of Music and Movement for Health

The study explores the opinions and experiences of participants, facilitators, and community stakeholders involved in the Music and Movement for Health (MMH) intervention, an interdisciplinary arts-based program designed to enhance health and wellbeing among older adults. The qualitative research employed a phenomenological approach grounded in the constructivist paradigm, utilizing focus groups and semi-structured interviews with 71 participants, eight facilitators, and four community stakeholders. Analysis of the data led to the overarching theme 'Positive ageing in place,' which encompasses two themes: 'Intersection of exercise and arts' and 'Fostering a sense of belonging.' Participants viewed MMH as a unique intervention combining music and dance, offering numerous perceived physical and psychological benefits. The program was particularly valued for providing routine and social opportunities during the COVID-19 pandemic. Overall, the study highlights the potential of arts-based interventions in promoting health and social engagement among older adults.
