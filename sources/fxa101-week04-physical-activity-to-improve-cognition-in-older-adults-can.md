## Physical activity to improve cognition in older adults: can physical activity programs enriched with cognitive challenges enhance the effects? A systematic review and meta-analysis

This systematic review and meta-analysis examines the cognitive benefits of physical activity (PA) programs enriched with cognitive challenges for older adults, particularly in comparison to PA-only or cognitive activity (CA)-only interventions. The study finds that combined PA + CA intervention yields significantly larger gains in cognition compared to control groups, and shows small but significant improvements over PA-only interventions. No significant difference was observed between combined PA + CA and CA-only interventions. The research also indicates that simultaneous delivery of PA and CA appears more effective than sequential delivery methods. These findings suggest the potential for integrating cognitive challenges into PA programs to enhance cognitive health in older adults, which could be a valuable approach for both prevention and treatment of cognitive decline.
