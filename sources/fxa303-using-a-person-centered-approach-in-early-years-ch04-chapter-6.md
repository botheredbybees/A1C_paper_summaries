## Person-Centred Planning: Teamwork and Leadership in Early Years Practice

This chapter discusses the application of Rogers' Person-Centred Approach (PCA) in early childhood education, emphasizing teamwork and leadership. It outlines how PCA can foster inclusive practices that center children and their families, addressing individual needs and aspirations through continuous listening and learning. The text introduces a model called Person-Centred Early Education Practice (PEEP), which integrates three core conditions essential to person-centred relationships: congruence, authenticity, and acceptance. Various elements of the PEEP model are explored, including leading and learning from children and experienced practitioners, maintaining respectful relationships with families, promoting holistic play, and establishing caring communities within early years settings.
