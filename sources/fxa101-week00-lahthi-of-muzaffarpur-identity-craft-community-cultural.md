## Lahthi of Muzaffarpur: Identity, Craft, Community & Cultural Heritage

This paper explores the Lahthi (lac bangle) craft of Muzaffarpur in Bihar, India, examining its role as a carrier of identity, memory, and community knowledge. Through an analysis based on secondary data, the authors highlight how Lahthi production is embedded in intergenerational knowledge systems, gendered practices, and local economies. The article discusses the aesthetic significance, cultural value, and socio-economic implications of this craft, noting its role in festivals, marriages, and regional identity. Challenges such as raw material constraints and competition from synthetic substitutes are addressed alongside efforts to improve income opportunities through institutional interventions. The paper argues for a balance between tradition and adaptive innovation to ensure the sustainability of Lahthi in contemporary India.
