## Arts, health & wellbeing: reflections on a national seminar series and building a UK research network

This article reflects on a UK national seminar series dedicated to arts, health, and wellbeing that took place between 2012 and 2013. Funded by the Economic and Social Research Council (ESRC), the series aimed to foster awareness and networking among researchers in this field across the UK. It comprised four seminars held in Nottingham, Bristol, Glasgow, and London, addressing current issues and challenges facing arts, health, and wellbeing research. The authors discuss the outcomes of these seminars, which included advancing theoretical, conceptual, and methodological developments in the field and developing strategies for advocacy and knowledge exchange with policymakers. The seminar series also led to the establishment of a Special Interest Group for Arts, Health, and Wellbeing funded by Lankelly Chase Foundation and supported by the Royal Society for Public Health. This group seeks to promote evidence-based policy and practice in arts, health, and wellbeing.
