## Action Research and Reflective Practice

Paul McIntosh's book 'Action Research and Reflective Practice' explores the intersection between evidence-based practice and reflective practice, arguing that reflection is a form of research. The central argument posits that the use of reflection and action research can become mechanistic without fresh, creative approaches. McIntosh emphasizes the importance of incorporating creativity and visual methods to enhance the depth and effectiveness of reflective practice. The book advocates for a more integrated and theoretically robust approach to action research and reflection, which can improve evidence-based research in education, health care, and social sciences. By doing so, it aims to foster a deeper understanding of events, identity, and self, benefiting students and researchers across various disciplines.
