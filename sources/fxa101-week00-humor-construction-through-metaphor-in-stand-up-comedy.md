## Humor Construction through Metaphor in Stand-Up Comedy

This paper examines the role of metaphor in constructing humor within stand-up comedy, using Conceptual Blending Theory (CBT) to analyze 'The King of Comedy.' The study investigates how different types of metaphorical blends generate humor and identifies four categories: simplex networks, mirror networks, single-scope networks, and double-scope networks. These blends create humor through role reassignment, structural parallelism, frame dominance, and deep conceptual integration. The research highlights that the depth of conceptual blending and emergence of novel structures within blended mental spaces influence the construction of humorous content. This contributes to a nuanced understanding of metaphorical humor and extends CBT's application in discourse analysis.
