## Musicians & RSI – How to prevent it

Dr. Jonathan Kuttner discusses the importance of hand positioning for musicians in preventing Repetitive Strain Injury (RSI). He emphasizes that the hand's position significantly affects its functionality and strength. By demonstrating various hand positions, Dr. Kuttner illustrates how certain positions can make the hand less effective, which is a common issue among musicians. He advises that musicians should identify their 'sweet spot' where their hand functions optimally. Dr. Kuttner also highlights the role of posture and technique in preventing RSI, noting that proper use of the body can be assessed without requiring medical knowledge of musical instruments. This insight is crucial for musicians and educators to understand and address potential health issues related to playing techniques.
