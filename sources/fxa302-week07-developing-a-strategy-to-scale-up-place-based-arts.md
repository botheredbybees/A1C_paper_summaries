## Developing a strategy to scale up place-based arts initiatives that support mental health and wellbeing: A realist evaluation of ‘Arts for the Blues’

This study investigates the development of a strategy to scale up place-based arts initiatives aimed at supporting mental health and wellbeing, focusing on the example of ‘Arts for the Blues’. The research utilizes realist evaluation to explore theoretical assumptions about scaling such initiatives based on qualitative data from focus groups and training sessions with diverse stakeholders. Key findings indicate that successful scaling requires adaptability, clarity, collaboration, evidence-based practice, personalization, and transformative approaches at the initiative level; organizational readiness, understanding of arts, resource availability, staff commitment, skillsets, and external support are crucial for implementation; and policy attention to shifts in attitudes towards arts, adherence to service standards, addressing gaps in provision, seeking early interventions, considering delivery changes, and fostering local champions is essential. The study provides a novel framework for scaling up place-based arts initiatives that can be applied across various contexts locally, nationally, and internationally.
