## Case Study: Impact of Brain Tumor on Musical Function

This transcript details the case of Dan, a professional musician and teacher who underwent brain surgery due to an unusual tumor affecting his auditory perceptions. The tumor was located in the right posterior superior temporal gyrus (pSTG), crucial for music processing. Preoperative functional MRI (fMRI) and intraoperative electrical stimulation were used during awake craniotomy to map brain regions critical for musical function while preserving language abilities. During surgery, direct electrical stimulation of the pSTG selectively disrupted Dan's ability to perceive and repeat melodies without affecting his language skills. Post-surgery, Dan recovered full musical capabilities and resumed teaching and playing saxophone.
