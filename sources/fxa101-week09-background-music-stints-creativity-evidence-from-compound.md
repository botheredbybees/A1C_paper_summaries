## Background Music Stints Creativity: Evidence from Compound Remote Associate Tasks

The study investigates the impact of background music on creativity, specifically focusing on performance in Compound Remote Associate Tasks (CRATs). The authors argue that while background music is often believed to enhance creativity, empirical evidence supporting this notion is limited. Through three experiments, the researchers found that background music, whether it has foreign lyrics, is instrumental, or contains familiar lyrics, significantly impaired CRAT performance compared to quiet conditions. These findings challenge the common belief that background music enhances creativity. The study discusses these results in the context of auditory distraction and processing disfluency theories, suggesting that background music may act as a distractor, thereby impeding creative cognitive processes.
