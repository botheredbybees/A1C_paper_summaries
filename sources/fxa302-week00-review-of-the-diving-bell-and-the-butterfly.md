## Review of The Diving Bell and the Butterfly

This review critically examines the film 'The Diving Bell and the Butterfly,' directed by Julian Schnabel and based on Jean-Dominique Bauby's memoir. The review discusses Bauby's experience with locked-in syndrome, his emotional journey, and the film's portrayal of disability, challenging the notion of disability as purely social and emphasizing the role of embodiment. It critiques the limitations of traditional disability film criticism and advocates for a more nuanced analysis focusing on the unique effects of cinema on bodily perception and sensation.
