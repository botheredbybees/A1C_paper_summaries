## FXA302 Perspectives of the Arts on Health and Wellbeing - Week 4 Activities

This module focuses on the connection between art and health in the Renaissance era. Students are required to read an article by Mortlock (2020) about wealth and health during this period and perform a close reading of Gerard van Honthorst's painting 'The Procuress' (1625). The discussion involves selecting a work from the Mediaeval or Renaissance periods that represents disease or love-sickness using symbolic representation. Students must provide details such as the creator, title, online link, summary, and interpretation of symbols in their chosen piece. Quiz 1 covering weeks 1-4 is scheduled for completion by August 12, 2026.
