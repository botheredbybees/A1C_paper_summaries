## Reflective Theory and Practice in Teacher Education

The chapter discusses the importance of reflective practice for pre-service teachers and how it can be effectively facilitated through online forums. It highlights the challenges pre-service teachers face during practicum, particularly their isolation from peers and university lecturers, which hinders supportive professional exchange. The authors emphasize the need for a balance between social and cognitive needs in fostering reflective practice and suggest that online discussions can counteract the isolation. The chapter also reviews the conceptualization of reflective practice, emphasizing its cognitive process and the role of communication with others. Reflective practice is seen as crucial for teachers to develop a repertoire of instructional methods and strategies while remaining critical and reflective. The authors advocate for integrating online learning spaces into teacher education to support reflective practice and enhance the theory-practice nexus.
