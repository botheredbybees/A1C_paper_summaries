## Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness and Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness

The lesson focuses on formal mindfulness practices, contrasting them with informal mindfulness. It outlines the components of mindfulness such as structured programs like MBSR and MBCT. Students are encouraged to reflect on the benefits and drawbacks of meditation and consider how mindfulness works. The module also covers limitations of the evidence base and challenges in mindfulness research and application, preparing for further discussions on compassion and self-compassion in the upcoming weeks.
