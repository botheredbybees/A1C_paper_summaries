## Visual Art and Transformation

The chapter 'Visual Art and Transformation' explores the therapeutic benefits of visual art within healthcare contexts. It highlights that vision is humans’ dominant sense, with most information derived from visual stimuli, emphasizing the potential of art to impact mood and cognitive function. The text discusses historical evidence supporting art’s role in human existence since prehistoric times and its cultural significance as a tool for communication and transformation. Various forms of visual art are considered, including their use in enhancing clinical environments and fostering community connections through participatory practices. Key themes include the neurological responses to viewing art, such as those studied in neuroaesthetics, and the communicative power of abstract or figurative artworks in conveying complex emotions and experiences. The chapter also examines how art can serve therapeutic functions for individuals with dementia and other conditions by facilitating communication, identity construction, and cognitive engagement.
