## Principles – Happy Museum Project

The Happy Museum Project outlines principles aimed at transforming museums into entities that foster community well-being, resilience, and environmental stewardship. The project emphasizes the importance of creating conditions for individual and communal well-being through activities like playfulness, creativity, and interaction. It also advocates for museums to value and protect natural and cultural environments and promote sustainable practices. Additionally, the principles encourage active citizenship by engaging communities in civic society, promoting mutual relationships with diverse stakeholders, learning for resilience, and measuring what truly matters to individuals rather than just counting visitors. The project underscores the potential of museums as hubs for community engagement and learning, fostering a more interconnected and resilient society.
