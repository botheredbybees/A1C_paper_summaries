## Book Essay: Before There Were Words, There Was Music

Jenifer Nields reviews three books on psychoanalysis and music published in the aftermath of the pandemic, highlighting their significance in understanding the therapeutic potential of music during challenging times. The reviewed works by Julie Jaffee Nagel explore how musicians can reimagine their roles within society post-pandemic and delve into the psychological dimensions of musical compositions. Adam Blum, Peter Goldberg, and Michael Levin’s book examines the philosophical and psychoanalytic underpinnings of music's impact on mental health. Nields emphasizes the interdisciplinary synergy between psychoanalysis and music, illustrating how both fields intertwine to provide profound insights into human development and emotional wellbeing.
