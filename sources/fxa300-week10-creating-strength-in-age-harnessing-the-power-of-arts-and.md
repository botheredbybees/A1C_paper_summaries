## Creating Strength in Age: Harnessing the Power of Arts and Humanities Networks

This document highlights the role of arts and humanities networks in enhancing the well-being of older populations. It includes presentations from the GSA 2019 Annual Scientific Meeting, featuring initiatives like Sound Health, which explores the therapeutic benefits of music, and the Center for Performing Arts Medicine at Houston Methodist, which integrates arts and health programs to improve patient care. The symposium also discusses the importance of arts, ethics, and humanities in gerontology education, emphasizing the need for interdisciplinary approaches in academic programs. These collaborative efforts demonstrate the potential of arts and humanities in combating cognitive and physical decline in older adults, fostering social networks, and enhancing overall quality of life.
