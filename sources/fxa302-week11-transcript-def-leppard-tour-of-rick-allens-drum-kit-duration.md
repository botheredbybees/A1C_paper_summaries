## Tour of Rick Allen's Drum Kit

In this brief video, Jeff, Rick Allen's Drum Tech for DEF LEPPARD, provides an overview of Rick Allen's unique electronic drum setup. The rig consists primarily of electronic components including electronic kicks, snares, and floor toms, with multiple pads and triggers for additional sound effects and loops. The setup includes a mix of live drum components for backup in case of electronic failure. Jeff explains the consistency and complexity of Rick's drum kit, highlighting the use of cowbells and multiple high hats, and the importance of maintaining a consistent tempo across performances.
