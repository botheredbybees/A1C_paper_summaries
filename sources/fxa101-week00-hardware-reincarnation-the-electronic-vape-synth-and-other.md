## Hardware Reincarnation: the Electronic Vape Synth and Other Upstream Salvaged Circuit

This paper discusses a full-day workshop on 'Upstream Salvage', which focuses on transforming discarded electronics into creative and experimental devices, particularly through the creation of synthesizers from salvaged vape hardware. The workshop aims to address ecological concerns while fostering hands-on learning about design, technology, and policy. It situates itself within global traditions of resourceful making, such as gambiarra in Brazil and jugaad in India, emphasizing the value of provisional making with recycled materials. Participants engage in disassembly, re-creation, and prototyping to explore new human-device interactions. The paper also outlines theoretical frameworks for understanding salvage as a design methodology and educational approach.
