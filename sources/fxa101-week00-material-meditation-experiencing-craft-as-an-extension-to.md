## Material Meditation: Experiencing Craft as an Extension to Soma Design

This paper presents the method 'material meditation,' a practice inspired by soma design, which involves auto-ethnographic reflections on interactions with materials to explore material qualities and build relationships between maker and material. The authors conducted various craft exercises, including wood cutting, glass blowing, metalworking, and plastic shaping, followed by reflective writing about each experience. The method aims to enhance awareness of bodily perceptions during the crafting process, emphasizing the significance of engaging deeply with materials for environmental sustainability.
