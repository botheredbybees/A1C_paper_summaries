## How Participation in Group Singing in the Community Impacts on Emotional Wellbeing: A Qualitative Study on the Perspectives of Group Singing Participants

This study explores the emotional wellbeing impacts of group singing in community settings, providing a qualitative analysis of participants' experiences. Sixty-eight community choir members were surveyed to understand the benefits and perceptions of group singing. The study identified five main themes: an enhanced sense of self in group presence, experiencing group singing as uplifting, meaningful camaraderie, engagement with expressive capacities, and experiencing a sanctuary for self. The research supports previous findings on group singing's ability to improve affect, facilitate social connection, and provide a personal sanctuary. Unique findings highlight singing as accepting, non-judgmental, reliably mood-enhancing, and somatically exhilarating. The study suggests group singing can serve as a valuable adjunct or transitional therapy in music and psychotherapy practices. The authors recommend further research to explore the therapeutic mechanisms of group singing in community contexts.
