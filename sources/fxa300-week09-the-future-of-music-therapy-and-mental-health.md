## The future of music therapy and mental health

This chapter discusses the future of music therapy within mental health care and the challenges it may face. It starts by referencing the President's New Freedom Commission on Mental Health's optimistic vision for mental health care in the United States. The author acknowledges the progress made in mental health care but emphasizes the ongoing need for improvement. Predictions for the future of music therapy in mental health settings are complex, and the chapter highlights the enduring necessity for inpatient and outpatient care, as well as the role of specialized practitioners. The chapter also underscores the importance of involving service users more actively in their own recovery and in the development of mental health services. Additionally, it explores the use of treatment manuals in psychotherapy, detailing their benefits and limitations. The author argues that while manuals can standardize and facilitate the study of treatments, they may also limit the flexibility and creativity of therapists. The chapter concludes by suggesting alternative terms and approaches to manualized treatments, emphasizing the importance of a balanced focus on both procedures and principles in cognitive-behavioral therapy.
