## 'Making it Easy to Do Hard Things': How experts help novices perceive craft as accessible

This paper explores how novices in the maker movement perceive craft as accessible through non-traditional pathways facilitated by experts. Using interview and observational data from Maker Faires, it reveals that experts employ a creative craft approach to convey knowledge and skills to novices, which involves scaffolding, relaxing hierarchy, and cultivating fun and whimsy. This engenders an experience of enchanted engagement where novices feel they can engage in craft despite lacking requisite skills or knowledge, reinforcing their perception of accessibility.
