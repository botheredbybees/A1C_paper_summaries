## Community Music Therapy

The chapter explores the concept of Community Music Therapy (CoMT) as a movement within the field of music therapy, characterized by its international scope and diverse origins. The authors argue that CoMT is not a unified or easily definable concept but rather a social movement that has emerged in response to the demands and opportunities of globalization, cultural plurality, and the restructuring of health and social care services. This movement is seen as a critique of traditional music therapy theories and practices, advocating for a more flexible and socially-oriented approach. Key pioneers and historical contexts are discussed, highlighting the roots of CoMT in various national traditions and its evolution into a cohesive international movement. The chapter also addresses the controversies surrounding CoMT, including debates about its definition and legitimacy within the field of music therapy. Overall, CoMT is presented as a response to the need for a more inclusive and interdisciplinary approach to music therapy, reflecting the changing needs and contexts of contemporary society.
