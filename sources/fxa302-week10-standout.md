## Standout

Lisa Iezzoni's chapter 'Standout' reflects on her journey in medical academia despite being diagnosed with multiple sclerosis (MS) and using a wheelchair for over two decades. Iezzoni describes encountering significant barriers early in her career due to her disability, including negative attitudes from senior physicians and discriminatory job offers. The passage highlights the transformative impact of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and how its protections have allowed her to achieve success in her career. Despite these advancements, Iezzoni notes that subtle stigma and discrimination persist, affecting her ability to publish disability-related research in certain medical journals. The chapter concludes with a call for a generational shift in attitudes towards disability and a recognition that disability rights are essential for everyone, given the universal nature of disability.
