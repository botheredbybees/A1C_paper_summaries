## Plating Manifesto (II): The Art and Science of Plating

This review paper discusses the influence of plateware and food presentation on eating behavior, flavor perception, and overall dining experience. It highlights how factors like color, shape, and arrangement of food can impact consumption habits and physiological responses. The authors argue for interdisciplinary research between chefs, psychologists, sensory scientists, and designers to explore these effects further. Key areas of investigation include the visual cues provided by plateware and their interaction with other sensory inputs.
