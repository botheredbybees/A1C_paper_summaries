## Sensory Substitution in Music: Enhancing Deaf Perception Through Vibrotactile Feedback

This paper presents a study on the use of vibrotactile feedback to enhance music perception for individuals with hearing impairments. The research involves converting audio signals into tactile sensations through wearable devices like wristbands, using sinusoidal modeling and Head-Related Transfer Function (HRTF) techniques. The method transforms sound files into tactile stereo vibrations that can be perceived on the skin. The study includes an experiment with ten participants who evaluated different vibrotactile feedback effects. Results show significant potential for sensory substitution in music perception through touch.
