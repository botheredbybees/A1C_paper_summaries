## Movement-Based Music in the Classroom: Investigating the Effects of Music Programs Incorporating Body Movement in Primary School Children

This study investigated the impact of incorporating body movement into classroom-based music programs on primary school children's development in Hungary. Two studies were conducted: Study 1 involved science-focused classes receiving Kodály music lessons with either teacher-directed movements or no movement activities, while Study 2 included intensive music classes combining Kodály lessons with either teacher-directed or improvised movements. Participants were measured three times over 1.5 years for musical abilities, sensorimotor entrainment, phonemic awareness, rapid naming, reading, executive functions, and IQ. The findings suggest that diverse movement-based music programs support similar growth in musical skills, sensorimotor entrainment, early literacy, and cognitive development. These results contribute to understanding the role of body movement in enhancing developmental outcomes within comprehensive classroom music education.
