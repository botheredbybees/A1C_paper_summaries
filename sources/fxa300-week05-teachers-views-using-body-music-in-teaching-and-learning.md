## Teachers’ Views: Using Body Music in Teaching and Learning Primary School Subjects

This paper explores the use of body music in primary education through a phenomenological study conducted with ten female teachers from village schools in Turkey. The research investigates their views on using KeKeÇa, an approach to body percussion that emphasizes embodiment, play orientation, and arts integration. Teachers underwent training to create and utilize body music games for various subjects. Data analysis revealed significant improvements in lesson control, student motivation, learning retention, and reduced time consumption. Participants noted a positive impact on students' overall learning quality and emotional well-being, suggesting the KeKeÇa approach enhances both educational outcomes and teaching practices.
