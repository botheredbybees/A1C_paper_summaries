## Trauma Aware and Anti-Oppressive Arts-Health and Community Arts Practice: Guiding Principles for Facilitating Healing, Health and Wellbeing

This paper reviews existing literature on trauma-informed practice in arts-health and community arts, emphasizing the need for practices that prevent retraumatization and promote healing, particularly in contexts of oppression-related collective trauma such as racial trauma. The study includes articles written in English, published in peer-reviewed academic journals, incorporating creative arts components with intentional trauma-informed or aware approaches. Although no specific guidelines were identified, interim principles are proposed to facilitate healing and wellbeing through arts activities. The research underscores the importance of a holistic paradigm that integrates body, mind, spirit, and environment for individuals and communities experiencing profound historical and ongoing marginalization.
