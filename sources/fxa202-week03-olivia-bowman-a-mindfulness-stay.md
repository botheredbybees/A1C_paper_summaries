## Olivia Bowman: A Mindfulness Stay

The document 'Olivia Bowman: A Mindfulness Stay' details the author's experience of a hospital stay due to a migraine, focusing on how photography served as a tool for mindfulness and engagement with her surroundings. The narrative captures the initial discomfort and vulnerability felt in an unfamiliar environment amidst strict COVID-19 restrictions. Photography helped Bowman shift focus from her ailment to the unique aspects of the hospital setting, such as lighting patterns, architectural features, and everyday objects like IV drip bags and meals served on hospital trays. Through these images, she documented both literal and metaphorical landscapes, creating a record that provided personal comfort and an external perspective on her condition. The series explores themes of curiosity, observation, and resilience in the face of illness and isolation, offering insights into how creative expression can serve therapeutic functions within healthcare environments.
