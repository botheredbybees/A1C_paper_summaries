## Support Programs at UT MD Anderson Cancer Center

The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center provides a range of support programs designed to assist adolescent and young adult cancer patients through the challenges they face. The Adolescent and Young Adult Program (AYA Program) caters specifically to individuals aged 15-39, offering counseling and care that helps manage life’s milestones during treatment or survivorship. Additionally, the Arts in Medicine program integrates visual arts, music, theater, and dance into patient care, aiming to reduce anxiety, pain, and stress while enhancing a sense of control and community among patients and families. Programs such as career and vocational counseling help young people prepare for educational and professional transitions despite their illness. These initiatives underscore MD Anderson’s commitment to holistic cancer care that addresses not only medical needs but also the emotional, social, and developmental challenges faced by young patients.
