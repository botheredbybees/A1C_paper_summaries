## Effects of Acoustically Screened Five-Element Music Combined with Traditional Chinese Mind–Body Exercises on Emotion Regulation, Working Memory, and Functional Brain Connectivity in Older Adults: A Randomized Repeated-Measures Study

This study investigated the effects of acoustically screened Five-Element Music combined with traditional Chinese mind–body exercises (Taijiquan and Baduanjin) on emotion regulation, working memory, and functional brain connectivity in older adults. Using a randomized repeated-measures design, the research recruited 42 healthy participants aged 60 to 70 years. Participants were assigned to five groups: Five-Element Music alone (FE), Taijiquan (TJ), Baduanjin (BDJ), FE combined with TJ (FE+TJ), and FE combined with BDJ (FE+BDJ). The study assessed emotion regulation, working memory using the N-back task, and functional brain connectivity through fNIRS. Results indicated significant improvements in emotion regulation acceptance rates for high-intensity unpleasant pictures in FE and FE+TJ groups compared to baseline. Working memory performance was notably enhanced in the FE+TJ group, with a lower reaction time observed in the FE condition. Additionally, higher functional connectivity between specific brain regions (CH45-CH9, CH29-CH6) was noted in the FE+TJ condition. The study concluded that integrating Five-Element Music and Taijiquan offers synergistic benefits for cognitive and emotional health in older adults.
