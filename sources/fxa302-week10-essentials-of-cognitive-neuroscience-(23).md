## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The document provides an excerpt from 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Bradley R. Postle, focusing on the cognitive neuroscience of language and its application to understanding dyslexia. It discusses the impact of brain lesions on verbal fluency and auditory comprehension, utilizing voxel-based lesion-symptom mapping to identify critical brain regions. The text also covers the work of neuroscientists such as Robert Knight and Nicholas Barbaro, who have used electrocorticography to study the dynamics of language processing. Additionally, it explores the genetic and neurophysiological bases of dyslexia, highlighting the importance of phonological awareness and the role of white matter integrity in the posterior temporoparietal cortex. Research indicates that dyslexia may involve deficits in filtering sensory noise and synchronizing oscillations in posterior temporal cortex with speech rhythms. The text concludes by discussing neonatal auditory evoked potential studies that predict later reading difficulties with high accuracy, emphasizing the potential for early intervention.
