## Effects of Group Drumming Interventions on Anxiety, Depression, Social Resilience and Inflammatory Immune Response among Mental Health Service Users

This study explores the efficacy of group drumming interventions on mental health outcomes among service users. The research, conducted over 10 weeks, compared a drumming group with a non-music control group, finding significant improvements in depression, anxiety, social resilience, and mental well-being in the drumming group. Additionally, the study revealed a shift from a pro-inflammatory to an anti-inflammatory immune profile in the drumming group. These findings support the therapeutic potential of group drumming for mental health conditions, suggesting both psychological and biological benefits.
