## Arts Therapy and Its Implications in Chronic Pain Management: A Narrative Review

This narrative review explores the efficacy of arts therapy as a mind-body intervention in managing chronic nonmalignant pain, emphasizing its role beyond traditional biopsychosocial approaches. The authors analyze various forms of arts therapy, including visual art, music, dance/movement, and written expression, to assess their potential benefits for patients dealing with chronic pain. Despite evidence suggesting positive effects on emotional well-being and quality of life, the review highlights significant methodological limitations in existing studies, indicating that further high-quality research is necessary to substantiate these findings. The article underscores the need for a more integrated approach involving arts therapy within multidisciplinary care settings, considering both its theoretical underpinnings and practical applications.
