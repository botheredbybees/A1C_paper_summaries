## Mindful Strategies for Helping College Students Manage Stress

The book 'Mindful Strategies for Helping College Students Manage Stress' by Dye, Burke, and Mason is a practical guide aimed at higher education professionals to support students in managing anxiety and emotional distress through mindfulness practices. The authors emphasize the unique role of educators and counselors in providing mental health support without being licensed therapists. Drawing from fields such as Mental Health, Counseling, and Student Affairs, they present evidence-based techniques grounded in neuroscience. Chapters cover topics like brain basics, relaxation methods, meditation, mindful movement, focus enhancement, and self-care strategies. The authors highlight the importance of community connections and holistic well-being for students to thrive academically and personally.
