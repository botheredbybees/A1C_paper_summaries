## Jazz Musicians Reveal Role of Expectancy in Human Creativity

This study explores the role of expectancy in human creativity, using jazz musicians as a model due to their ability to improvise and create novel sound sequences. The research compares jazz improvisers, non-improvising musicians, and non-musicians in tasks involving divergent thinking and preference ratings for chord progressions that vary in expectation. Behavioral results indicate that jazz musicians prefer unexpected chord progressions, while EEG findings reveal larger early and mid-latency ERP responses (ERAN and P3b) for unexpected stimuli in jazz musicians, followed by smaller long-latency responses. These findings highlight the importance of expectancy in creativity, suggesting that the processing of unexpected events is key to creative processes.
