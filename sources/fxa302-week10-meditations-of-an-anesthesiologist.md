## Meditations of an Anesthesiologist

Audrey Shafer's poem, 'Meditations of an Anesthesiologist,' explores the intersection of healthcare and spirituality. The poem juxtaposes the clinical reality of patient care with the emotional and existential questions it raises. Through vivid imagery, Shafer reflects on the complexities of being a medical professional, balancing the technical aspects of her work with the human experience of patients and the emotional toll on caregivers. The commentary highlights the poem's exploration of wishful thinking, blurred boundaries, and the search for meaning within the healthcare setting. Shafer emphasizes the importance of the humanities in fostering deeper connections and understanding in healthcare. The piece underscores the significance of spirituality as a continuous and transformative process, rather than a static belief system. It argues that moments of awe and gratitude, even in the mundane or challenging aspects of medical practice, are crucial for renewing the sense of wonder and connection in healthcare.
