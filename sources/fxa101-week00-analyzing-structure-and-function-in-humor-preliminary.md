## Analyzing structure and function in humor: Preliminary sketch of a message-centered model

This paper presents a model for analyzing conversational humor based on Attardo’s General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH) and Meyer's rhetorical functions of humor, integrating insights from conversation analysis and membership categorization. The model proposes that in humorous exchanges, an initial incongruous script is introduced followed by a second script opposition that resolves the first through a logical mechanism. This resolution can either unite or divide interlocutors based on whether it aligns with preferred responses established in the setup. The paper applies this framework to analyze two jokes and conversational sequences, emphasizing the structural components of humor and their social implications.
