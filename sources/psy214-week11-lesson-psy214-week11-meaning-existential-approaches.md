## Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: Meaning Practices and Outcomes

This lesson focuses on the concept of meaning in life and its implications for stress reduction and mental well-being. It explores various methods for individuals to reflect on and cultivate meaningful experiences, such as meaningful photography and Socratic questioning. Research suggests that engaging in these practices can lead to increased life satisfaction and a greater sense of meaning. The lesson also discusses the limitations of current research and the need for further exploration into the effects of different meaningful and non-meaningful activities. Additionally, the lesson delves into how meaning contributes to well-being, distinguishing between eudaimonic and subjective well-being, and how meaningful experiences may not necessarily equate to happiness.
