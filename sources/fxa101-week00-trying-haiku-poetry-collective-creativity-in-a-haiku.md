## Trying Haiku Poetry: Collective Creativity in a Haiku Gathering

This ethnographic study by Susan Paige Taylor examines the social practice of haiku composition in Tokyo's Jimbocho district, focusing on a bi-monthly gathering at Zugaikotsu’s bar. The author challenges the notion that poetry is a solitary activity by highlighting how collaborative creativity occurs within these gatherings. Participants engage in composing, ranking, and critiquing haiku poems, with a democratic scoring system encouraging peer feedback and collective improvement. Taylor argues that situating creative practice in communal settings like bars fosters relational creativity through shared critique and playful competition, contrasting this approach with the traditional image of solitary literary genius.
