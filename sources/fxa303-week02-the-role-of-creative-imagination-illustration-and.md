## The Role of Creative Imagination, Illustration and Storytelling in Supporting Social, Emotional and Mental Health in Educational Settings

Ellie Baker’s paper explores the use of creative imagination, illustration, and storytelling to support social, emotional, and mental health in educational settings. The study outlines two collaborative projects led by Baker as a child mental health expert and artist. These initiatives aimed to empower children through creativity and help them confront issues such as Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs), trauma, and eco-anxiety. Through the projects 'Colour Me Wild' and 'Rabbit was a Worrier: Storytelling Aspects of Self', Baker demonstrates how visual self-expression can positively regulate thoughts and emotions. The research integrates theories from illustration pedagogy, arts therapy, neuroscience, and attachment theory to build bonds between children and nature, fostering resilience and emotional literacy. This work highlights the importance of creative activities in enhancing well-being and promoting secure attachments.
