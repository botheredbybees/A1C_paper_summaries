## Music, Health, and Wellbeing (Section 1: Introductory Chapters - Setting the Scene)

This chapter, titled 'What is music and why is it important?', delves into the multifaceted relationship between music and health. It begins by emphasizing the intrinsic link between music and social, cultural, and psychological variables, highlighting its therapeutic potential as supported by historical documentation from early civilizations to contemporary research. The authors discuss the complexities of defining key terms such as 'music', 'health', and 'wellbeing' and address the challenge of establishing causal links between musical activities and individual health benefits. They advocate for a multidisciplinary approach involving music therapy, psychology, engineering, education, and community settings to understand music's influence on wellbeing.

The chapter also explores various reasons why music has beneficial effects on individuals: its ubiquity due to technological advancements, emotional impact on mood regulation, engaging nature leading to neurological benefits, and distracting qualities in stressful situations. The text concludes by emphasizing the broad support for the idea that music can significantly enhance health and wellbeing across diverse contexts.
