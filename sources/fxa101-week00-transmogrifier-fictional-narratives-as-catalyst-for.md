## TRaNsMOGRiFiER: Fictional Narratives as Catalyst for Experimental Instrument-Building and Musical/Artistic Collaboration

The paper discusses the TRaNsMOGRiFiER project, a week-long installation and durational performance that uses fictional narratives as catalysts for experimental instrument-building and collaborative artistic practices. The project, inspired by Calvin's transmogrifier from the comic strip 'Calvin and Hobbes,' encourages participants to transform donated objects into new forms using cardboard boxes, electronics, and hot glue, emphasizing reanimation of discarded objects. Key elements include capacitor trees and a network that create rhythmic pulses, rotating sculptures called “the eye,” and a gallery space for exhibiting transmogrified objects. The authors highlight the project's focus on hands-on learning, open-ended processes, and collective creativity.
