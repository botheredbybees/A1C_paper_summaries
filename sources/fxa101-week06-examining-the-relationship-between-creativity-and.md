## Examining the relationship between creativity and innovation: A meta-analysis of organizational, cultural, and environmental factors

This paper presents a meta-analysis of 52 empirical studies comprising 10,538 observations to examine the relationship between creativity and innovation across various organizational, environmental, and cultural contexts. The study finds a strong positive correlation (r = 0.46) between creativity and innovation at an individual level. However, this relationship varies significantly based on contextual factors such as firm size, industry type, and degree of uncertainty avoidance in the national setting. Large firms are more effective than small ones in converting creative ideas into innovations. Process-related innovations exhibit a higher rate of conversion compared to product-based innovations. The study also indicates that moderate levels of uncertainty avoidance enhance the creativity-innovation link. These findings underscore the importance of considering contextual factors when studying and managing innovation processes, providing valuable insights for both researchers and practitioners.
