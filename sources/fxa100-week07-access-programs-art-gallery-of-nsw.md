## Access Programs | Art Gallery of NSW

The Art Gallery of New South Wales offers a variety of access programs designed to engage diverse audiences, including those with disabilities and special needs. Programs such as Auslan-interpreted tours, tactile and sensory tours for the visually impaired, and audio-described tours cater to different accessibility requirements. A notable program is the Art and Dementia initiative, which provides meaningful and interactive engagement with artworks for people living with dementia and their carers. This program aims to promote lifelong learning, intellectual stimulation, and social interaction. These initiatives are crucial for inclusive art appreciation and underscore the gallery's commitment to fostering creative arts and health by ensuring that everyone, regardless of ability, can enjoy and benefit from the gallery's collections and exhibitions.
