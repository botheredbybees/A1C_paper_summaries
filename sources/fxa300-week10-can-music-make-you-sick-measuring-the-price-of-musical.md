## Can Music Make You Sick? Measuring the Price of Musical Ambition

The book 'Can Music Make You Sick? Measuring the Price of Musical Ambition' by Sally Anne Gross and George Musgrave explores the psychological and social impacts of pursuing a career in music. The authors examine the high price musicians pay in terms of mental health, financial insecurity, and personal relationships. Through interviews and surveys, they highlight the common struggles faced by musicians, including anxiety, depression, and the pressure to succeed in a highly competitive industry. The book argues that the music industry's emphasis on success and achievement often leads to personal sacrifices and stress, affecting the well-being of musicians. The central argument is that the pursuit of musical ambition can indeed have negative consequences on health and relationships, and the authors call for more support and awareness within the music community. The relevance to creative arts and health lies in the need for better understanding and addressing the mental health issues faced by artists in the creative industries.
