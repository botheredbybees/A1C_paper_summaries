## Art-making as a Gateway to the Inner Self: A Phenomenological Exploration of Intuitive Collage in Coaching

This academic paper explores the use of intuitive collage as a tool for health and wellness coaching through a retrospective, phenomenological study. It examines how coaches perceive and utilize art-making in their practice to enhance client self-awareness, creativity, and well-being. The research highlights that intuitive collage can serve as an effective means for unlocking deeper insights and fostering personal growth within the coaching relationship. The findings suggest that this approach may expand the coaching repertoire by tapping into clients' visual processing and spatial intelligence, thereby offering a non-verbal pathway to emotional exploration.
