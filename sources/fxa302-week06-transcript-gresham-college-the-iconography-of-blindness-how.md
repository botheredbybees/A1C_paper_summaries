## The Iconography of Blindness: how artists have portrayed the blind

This lecture explores the artistic representation of blindness throughout history. Professor William Ayliffe discusses various artworks and artists who have depicted blind individuals in their works, examining how these portrayals reflect cultural attitudes towards disability and the role of sight and vision.
