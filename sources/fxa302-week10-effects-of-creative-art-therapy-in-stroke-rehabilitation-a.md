## Effects of Creative Art Therapy in Stroke Rehabilitation: A Systematic Review

The systematic review by Hamad and Meshref (2026) explores the impact of creative art therapy in stroke rehabilitation. The study aims to systematically review evidence on the effectiveness of creative art therapy interventions among stroke patients. A comprehensive search of electronic databases up to May 2025 identified 15 studies involving a total of 670 stroke patients, including six randomized controlled trials, one cohort study, and eight pre–post single-arm studies. The review highlights that art therapy interventions are associated with significant improvements in anxiety, depression, and quality of life. Despite the moderate methodological quality of the studies, the findings support the role of creative art therapy as a complementary approach in stroke rehabilitation. The review underscores the need for larger randomized controlled trials to further validate these promising results. This study is relevant to the field of creative arts and health, emphasizing the psychological and quality-of-life benefits of creative art therapy in enhancing stroke rehabilitation outcomes.
