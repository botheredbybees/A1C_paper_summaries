## Music Therapy in Mental Health for Illness Management and Recovery

This chapter discusses the role of music therapy in mental health settings for illness management and recovery. The author argues that music therapy functions as a complementary and integrative therapy rather than a stand-alone treatment, emphasizing its additive value to conventional treatments. The text highlights the relevance of brief music therapy sessions, especially in acute care mental health units, where patients may not be immediately engaged in therapeutic programming due to their condition. The chapter introduces the concept of Music Therapy for Illness Management and Recovery (MT-IMR), which centers on autonomy and self-determination, aligning with self-determination theory and recovery-oriented care. MT-IMR is described as a philosophy and theoretical framework that supports service users in developing skills for community integration and living meaningful lives. The chapter also differentiates MT-IMR from traditional symptom reduction approaches, emphasizing the importance of agency and the therapeutic alliance in promoting recovery. This framework is relevant to the creative arts and health field as it provides a structured approach to integrating music therapy into comprehensive mental health treatment plans.
