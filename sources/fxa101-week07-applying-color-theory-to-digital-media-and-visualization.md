## Applying Color Theory to Digital Media and Visualization

Applying Color Theory to Digital Media and Visualization by Theresa-Marie Rhyne provides an in-depth exploration of how fundamental color theory concepts can be applied to digital media and visualization. The book delves into the historical progression of color models, color vision principles, and the application of color harmony and data color schemes. It includes a thorough review of color gamut, color spaces, and color systems, as well as techniques for simulating and designing for color deficiencies. The author also introduces modern tools and applications, such as generative AI solutions for color suggestion, and provides practical examples and case studies to illustrate the concepts discussed. This work is particularly relevant for digital media and visualization content creators and developers, offering a comprehensive guide to using color effectively in their work.
