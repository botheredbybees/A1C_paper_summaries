## FXA101 Creativity and Ageing - Week 6 Activities

Week 6 of the FXA101 Creativity and Ageing course focuses on enhancing creative thinking through cognitive exercises. The lesson recommends reading Ritter & Mostert's (2017) paper which explores methods to improve creativity using various training techniques such as Silence, Lines of Evolution, Random Connections, and SCAMPER. Students are introduced to the SCAMPER technique for manipulating ideas and encouraged to engage in creative thinking challenges based on this method. Additional activities include participating in a discussion forum where students can share their reflections from these exercises and interact with peers. The lesson also guides students through developing their Creative Project 2, emphasizing the importance of gathering materials, testing techniques, and documenting research.
