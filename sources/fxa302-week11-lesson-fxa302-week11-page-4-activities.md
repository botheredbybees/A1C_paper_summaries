## FXA302 Perspectives of the Arts on Health and Wellbeing - Week 11 Activities

Week 11 of the FXA302 course focuses on the role of assistive and prosthetic technologies in art creation and expression by artists with disabilities. Students are required to read an article by Watlington (2022) on disability arts and explore the Australian government's four-year plan for increasing equity for people with disabilities in the arts. A discussion activity asks students to find and analyze artwork that features assistive or prosthetic technologies, exploring how these technologies extend capabilities and affect artistic expression. Upcoming assessments include an essay and a quiz, with specific due dates provided.
