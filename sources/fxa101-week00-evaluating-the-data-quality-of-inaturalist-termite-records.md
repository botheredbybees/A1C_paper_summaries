## Evaluating the Data Quality of iNaturalist Termite Records

This research assesses the value of citizen science (CS) contributions to termite distribution data on iNaturalist, comparing them with the University of Florida Termite Collection and GBIF. The study examines record abundance, species diversity, spatial coverage, and taxonomic accuracy at genus and family levels through expert review based on photo identification. Findings indicate that while iNaturalist records complement traditional sources in some regions, their overall abundance is lower. Combining data from different sources significantly improves the number of termite genera meeting requirements for ecological niche modeling but adding iNaturalist observations has a moderate effect. The research highlights that non-research grade observations can still be valuable for mapping species presence without compromising quality.
