## Introduction to Week 5 - FXA101 Creativity and Ageing

Week 5 of the FXA101 Creativity and Ageing course introduces Module 2 with a practical focus on creativity development through challenges. The week's activities include working on mini-projects for Creative Project 2 and engaging in weekly readings, exercises, and discussions to support learning outcomes such as conducting research into creative models, solving constrained problems, generating novel ideas, and documenting the creative process. The module explores how constraints can enhance creativity by focusing attention and encouraging innovative solutions, drawing on theories like those of Patricia Stokes and the concept of 'scarcity salience'. Students are encouraged to consider their problem-solving approaches in light of these insights.
