## Music and Creativity

The lesson explores the concept of creativity, particularly in the context of music. It differentiates between eminent creativity (Big-C) and ordinary creativity (small-c), introducing the 4-C Model of Creativity by Kaufman and Beghetto. The lesson also discusses the psychological aspects of creativity, including fluency, flexibility, originality, and elaboration. It outlines methods to measure creative ability, such as the Alternate Uses Test and Torrance Tests of Creative Thinking. Additionally, the lesson covers the neural basis of creativity, focusing on the prefrontal cortex and working memory, and discusses the concept of genius, emphasizing the influence of nurture alongside nature. The content also touches on musical prodigies and savants, and the concept of flow in musical creativity.
