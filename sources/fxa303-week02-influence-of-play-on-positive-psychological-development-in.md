## Influence of play on positive psychological development in emerging adulthood: A serial mediation model

The study examines the impact of play on psychological development in emerging adulthood, focusing on emotional intelligence (EI), emotional traits, and resilience. Participants were full-time undergraduate students aged 19 to 25. Results indicated that engaging in play cultivates EI and enhances resilience, with a serial mediation model suggesting that play indirectly influences resilience through its effect on positive emotions and EI. The research underscores the importance of play for stress relief and psychological well-being during this transitional period. Play can foster emotional competence, leading to better management of life challenges and contributing to overall health and well-being in emerging adults.
