## Art and Place: Crossing Borders in the Work of Perejaume

The paper explores the Catalan artist Perejaume's representation of art movements and landscapes, challenging traditional views on art history and place. Through various media like drawing, painting, and installation, Perejaume visualizes intangible journeys across time and space, offering alternative models to conventional linear or cyclical theories of artistic development. By integrating nature with cultural symbols, his work critiques the distinction between natural scenery and its representation in art. He uses framing devices, digital manipulation, and literary techniques to highlight how culture shapes our perception of landscapes, questioning notions of local specificity versus universality.
