## Material Driven Design (MDD): A Method to Design for Material Experiences

The article introduces the concept of Material Driven Design (MDD) as a methodology aimed at guiding designers in creating products that evoke meaningful user experiences with materials beyond their functional attributes. The authors argue that while new materials often possess superior functionalities, achieving widespread adoption necessitates not only addressing practical needs but also fostering positive emotional and experiential connections. They draw upon historical examples like Tupperware to illustrate how innovative applications can transform perceptions of a material's value and utility. Through MDD, designers are encouraged to collaborate closely with material scientists to develop products that fully leverage the unique qualities of new materials, thereby accelerating their acceptance in consumer markets.
