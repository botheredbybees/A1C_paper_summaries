## Group Singing for Physical and/or Mental Wellbeing

This lesson explores the benefits of group singing for physical and mental health, focusing on its therapeutic effects for people with respiratory diseases such as COPD. It discusses research showing that singing can improve respiratory function and quality of life, highlighting a randomized control study by Bonilha et al. (2009) and a systematic review by Fang et al. (2022). The lesson also covers emotional and physical wellbeing, including anxiety and depression reduction in elderly nursing home residents and improvements in mood and social isolation in stroke and Parkinson’s Disease patients. Additionally, it mentions the release of neurochemicals like endorphins and the role of group singing in social bonding and community support networks. The lesson concludes with the potential of singing-based interventions in medical settings and the social benefits of singing groups.
