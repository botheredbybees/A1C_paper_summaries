## Creating When You Have Less: The Impact of Resource Scarcity on Product Use Creativity

This research examines the relationship between resource scarcity and product use creativity among consumers, proposing that a general sense of resource scarcity enhances product use solutions' novelty in independent consumption contexts. The authors suggest that scarcity salience activates a constraint mindset, reducing functional fixedness and promoting innovative thinking beyond traditional product functionalities. This work contributes to existing literature by establishing an empirical link between the perception of resource availability (scarcity vs. abundance) and creativity, providing process evidence for this relationship through reduced functional fixedness. The study challenges prevailing notions that abundance stifles creativity and offers insights into how contextual cues about resource availability impact consumer behavior in creative consumption scenarios.
