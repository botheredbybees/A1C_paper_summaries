## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The chapter from 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Postle (2015) explores the role of the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (PFC) in cognitive control. It discusses how damage to this region can lead to behavioral disinhibition, indicating its critical role in maintaining control over cognitive processes. The chapter further delves into the involvement of midbrain dopamine neurons, the default mode network (dmFC), and the pulvinar nucleus of the thalamus in coordinating cognitive control through synchronization and regulation of neural activity. The text also poses questions for reflection on personal experiences of cognitive control failures and brain regions associated with this control, emphasizing the multi-faceted nature of cognitive control and its neural underpinnings. This understanding is crucial for creative arts and health professionals who engage with cognitive neuroscience, as it highlights the interplay between cognitive processes and the brain's structural and functional organization.
