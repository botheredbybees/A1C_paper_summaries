## Brain training using cognitive apps can improve cognitive performance and processing speed in older adults

The study explores the efficacy of Cognitive Mobile Games (CMG) in enhancing cognitive performance and processing speed among older adults, particularly those aged over 60 years. It addresses a significant public health challenge as global aging leads to increased cognitive decline. The research analyzed game scores and processing speeds of over 12,000 participants who completed 100 sessions using CMGs. Results indicate that regardless of age, users showed improvement in their performance and speed throughout the training period. This finding suggests potential for older adults, including those aged above 80 years, to benefit from these brain-training applications. The study also notes a linear trend where younger participants tend to start with higher scores compared to older ones but all groups improved over time. Importantly, the duration of training had varying impacts on performance progress across different games, highlighting the complexity in determining optimal training periods for diverse user populations.
