## Mindfulness-Based Social Work and Self-Care with Social Work Professionals: Replication and Expansion of a Randomised Controlled Trial

This study aimed to replicate and expand the findings of an earlier randomized controlled trial (RCT) on the Mindfulness-based Social Work and Self-Care programme (MBSWSC). The research involved evaluating MBSWSC against an active control group among a broader cohort of social work professionals, including managers. Results confirmed that MBSWSC was effective in improving stress, emotional exhaustion, service user depersonalization, anxiety, depression, well-being, and mindfulness mechanisms. These findings suggest the programme's acceptability, effectiveness, and durability for enhancing cognitive and emotion regulation among social workers. The research underscores the potential of mindfulness-based interventions to mitigate workplace stress and burnout in social work practice.
