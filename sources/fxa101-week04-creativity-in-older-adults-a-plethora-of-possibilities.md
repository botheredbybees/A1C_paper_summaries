## Creativity in Older Adults: A Plethora of Possibilities

This paper explores the role of creativity in enhancing mental and physiological health among older adults, emphasizing its positive impact on adaptation to aging challenges such as chronic illnesses and functional decline. The authors review research findings indicating that creative activities can improve overall well-being by fostering a sense of purpose, personal growth, self-acceptance, and social interaction. They argue that creativity extends beyond traditional artistic pursuits like painting or drawing; it includes reflective and problem-solving activities that contribute to cognitive and emotional resilience. By engaging in such activities, older adults develop adaptive strategies for coping with age-related changes, thus enriching their quality of life.
