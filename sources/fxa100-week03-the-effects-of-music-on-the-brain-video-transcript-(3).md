## The Effects of Music on the Brain – video transcript

This document is a transcript of a Nova video segment featuring Oliver Sachs, exploring how the brain reacts to music. Dr. Sachs participates in an experiment where his emotional responses and brain activity are recorded while listening to pieces by Bach and Beethoven. The findings suggest that even when Dr. Sachs could not consciously distinguish between the two composers' works, his brain still recognized them and showed a clear preference for Bach, specifically through heightened activity in the amygdala during Bach's music. This research highlights how music can evoke distinct neurological responses and emotional processing, providing insights into the complex interplay between musical perception and brain function.
