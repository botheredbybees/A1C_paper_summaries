## The Decoding of the Human Spirit: A Synergy of Spirituality and Character Strengths Toward Wholeness

The paper by Niemiec, Russo-Netzer, and Pargament explores the synergistic relationship between spirituality and character strengths, which are both fundamental aspects of human experience. The authors argue that while the study of character strengths has seen a surge in recent years, there has been little attention given to the intersection with spirituality. Similarly, the scientific study of spirituality has occasionally referenced select character strengths but has largely ignored the broader VIA Classification of character strengths and virtues. The article posits that there is a strong interconnection between these two areas, with spirituality promoting character strengths and vice versa. They propose a framework for integrating spirituality within the VIA Classification, suggesting that spirituality can be understood as a meta-strength that contributes to a sense of wholeness. The authors identify two pathways of integration: the grounding path, where character strengths provide a tangible foundation for spiritual practices, and the sanctification path, where spirituality elevates character strengths. Additionally, they discuss the potential for character strengths to enhance spiritual practices and vice versa, thereby enriching our understanding of human well-being.
