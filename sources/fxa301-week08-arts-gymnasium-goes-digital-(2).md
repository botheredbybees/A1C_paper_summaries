## Arts Gymnasium Goes Digital

Margaret Emerton, a participant in the Arts Gymnasium group for individuals aged over 50 at the Belgrade Theatre, recounts her experience transitioning from in-person to online poetry workshops during the COVID-19 pandemic. The article highlights the challenges faced by older adults with varying levels of technological proficiency as they adjusted to digital learning platforms like Zoom. Despite initial hurdles, members successfully engaged in creative activities such as writing haikus and incorporating sound recordings and visual elements into their work. This adaptation underscores the importance of intergenerational collaboration and the role of arts organizations in fostering technology literacy among older populations. The article also mentions ongoing research by occupational therapy students aimed at identifying supportive resources for seniors in using digital tools, thereby enhancing accessibility to creative arts programs.
