## A Qualitative Study of Family Centrality in Modified Dignity Therapy Experiences Among Korean Patients With Advanced Cancer

This qualitative study explored the modified dignity therapy (mDT) intervention among Korean patients with advanced cancer, incorporating a photo-collage method to address cultural and familial factors influencing psychological well-being. The research involved thirteen participants who experienced significant existential distress and utilized mDT that integrates family relationships as central themes in their coping processes. Findings indicate that family dynamics play a crucial role in understanding illness experiences and decision-making processes for these patients. The study highlights the benefits of integrating visual and verbal narratives to capture cultural nuances not evident solely through interviews, emphasizing the need for culturally sensitive psychosocial interventions.
