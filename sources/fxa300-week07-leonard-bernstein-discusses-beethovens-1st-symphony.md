## Leonard Bernstein discusses Beethoven’s 1st Symphony

In this transcript, Leonard Bernstein explores Beethoven's First Symphony, emphasizing its revolutionary nature despite its Haydn-like appearance. Bernstein highlights the Scherzo movement, marked as a Menuetto but played at a much faster pace than traditional minuets, reflecting Beethoven's innovative approach. Bernstein speculates that Beethoven initially conceived the music in a slower minuet tempo but chose to transform it into a Scherzo, dramatically speeding up the rhythm and setting the stage for the Romantic era. This discussion underscores Beethoven's pivotal role in changing the landscape of classical music, bridging the 18th and 19th centuries. For the creative arts and health, this insight into Beethoven's creative process offers valuable lessons on the transformative power of innovation and the importance of breaking conventional boundaries in artistic expression.
