## Self-care in Music Therapy: The Art of Balancing

The chapter 'Self-care in Music Therapy: The Art of Balancing' by Gro Trondalen addresses the importance of self-care for qualified music therapists. It emphasizes the need for nurturing oneself through love, care, and support, both from oneself and others, including through the medium of music. The chapter aims to raise awareness and reflection on how to maintain self-care and personal development in daily work and during periods of leave or sabbaticals. It outlines a theoretical framework that integrates biological, psychological, and contextual factors affecting self-care, emphasizing the interconnectedness of physical and mental health. The text also discusses the challenges and roles of music therapists, including their professional and personal lives, and highlights the importance of balance to prevent burnout, compassion fatigue, and compassion stress. Trondalen argues that a comprehensive approach to self-care is necessary, considering existential dimensions of health, and that health should be viewed as a continuum rather than a static state. The chapter concludes with a reflection on the need for a holistic understanding of self-care in the complex roles of music therapists.
