## Artful: Art and Dementia | For visitors with dementia | MCA Australia

The document discusses the 'Artful' program offered by MCA Australia, designed specifically for individuals living with dementia and their support partners. The initiative uses contemporary art as a catalyst to enhance life experiences and promote well-being through various activities such as gallery visits and hands-on art-making sessions. Key findings highlight that these programs provide significant benefits including reduced stress levels, improved cognitive engagement, and opportunities for social interaction. Participants have reported feeling more calm and able to think clearly during the art sessions, indicating a positive impact on their mental health and quality of life. The program also includes tailored community days where participants can exhibit their artwork and celebrate together with friends, families, and carers. Moreover, there are online Creative Care Sessions that provide gentle monthly activities for carers and the individuals they support through simple at-home art-making. This initiative underscores the importance of creative arts in dementia care and supports the idea that artistic engagement can be a powerful tool to enrich the lives of those affected by dementia.
