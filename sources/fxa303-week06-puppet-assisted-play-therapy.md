## Puppet-Assisted Play Therapy

Cheryl Hulburd's book 'Puppet-Assisted Play Therapy' is an innovative guide that enhances the field of play therapy through the use of puppets. It covers a comprehensive approach including history, creative interventions, case studies, and practical applications in puppetry techniques for mental health professionals working with children. The book argues that using puppets in therapeutic settings can foster trust, connection, and creativity among children, helping them to address various emotional and psychological challenges more effectively. Hulburd's work is particularly relevant to the creative arts in health, as it integrates play therapy with puppetry art forms to provide a unique tool for therapists, counselors, and educators.
