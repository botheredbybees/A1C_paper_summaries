## Imagination and Creativity: A Necessary Connection?

Caterina Moruzzi's critical notice of Amy Kind's book 'Imagination and Creative Thinking' explores the intricate relationship between imagination and creativity, focusing on Kind's argument that imagination is essential for creativity. Moruzzi discusses how imagination, despite its non-truth-bound nature, can provide useful information about the world through the imposition of constraints. The text delves into the different forms of imagination (propositional and sensory) and their varied uses, such as transcendent and instructive purposes. Moruzzi highlights Kind's assertion that imagination is a necessary tool for generating new ideas and insights, even in scientific and artistic domains, and emphasizes the importance of understanding how constraints shape the imaginative process. This review is significant for understanding the intersection of imagination and creativity in various fields, including the creative arts and health.
