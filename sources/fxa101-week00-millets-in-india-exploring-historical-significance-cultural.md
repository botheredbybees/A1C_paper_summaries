## Millets in India: Exploring Historical Significance, Cultural Heritage and Ethnic Foods

This review paper explores the historical significance of millets in India, their cultural heritage, and their role in ethnic foods. The study is based on an extensive literature search of official government reports, journals, and books. It highlights archaeological evidence and historical records indicating the prominence of millet cultivation since ancient times, particularly during the Vedic era, Indus Valley Civilization, and Mughal rule. Millets are integral to religious ceremonies, festivals, and folklore across India, contributing to diverse culinary applications in various states. The nutritional value of different types of millets is discussed, along with recent technological advancements for processing them into food products. Challenges associated with promoting millet consumption are also addressed.
