## Positive Expressive Writing Interventions, Subjective Health and Wellbeing in Non-Clinical Populations: A Systematic Review

This systematic review examines the effects of positive expressive writing interventions on subjective health and wellbeing in non-clinical populations. The authors conducted a search across four databases identifying peer-reviewed articles from 1930 to August 2023, resulting in the inclusion of 51 studies focusing on various positive writing techniques such as gratitude, best possible self, and benefit finding. While consistent benefits were observed for wellbeing outcomes like optimism and happiness, less reliable effects were found for negative affect, psychological health (stress, anxiety), and physical health. The review identifies best possible self and gratitude interventions as the most consistently beneficial. It concludes that more rigorous research methods are necessary to better understand individual differences influencing efficacy.
