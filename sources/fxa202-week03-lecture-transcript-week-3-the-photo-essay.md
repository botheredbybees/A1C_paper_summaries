## Lecture Transcript: Week 3 - The Photo Essay

This week's lecture in the Photo Essay course focuses on the relationship between photography and health, particularly emphasizing social media's impact on mental well-being. The discussion highlights both positive and negative aspects of digital photo sharing, such as maintaining connections but also fostering nomophobia or fear of being without one’s phone. The transcript introduces the concept of Therapeutic Photography, which is self-initiated versus professional Phototherapy led by a clinical practitioner. Various strategies and case studies are presented to explore how photography can positively affect mental health and memory. Practical activities, including mindfulness photo walks, are suggested for students to engage in and share their experiences on discussion boards. The lecture encourages further exploration of these themes during group discussions and practical sessions.
