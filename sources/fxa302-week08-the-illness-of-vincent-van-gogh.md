## The Illness of Vincent van Gogh

Dietrich Blumer’s article in the American Journal of Psychiatry explores the mental health of Vincent van Gogh, focusing on the last two years of the artist’s life. Despite the lack of substantial medical evidence, over 150 medical professionals have proposed various diagnoses for van Gogh's condition, ranging from temporal lobe epilepsy to bipolar disorder. Blumer reviews the historical and medical evidence, highlighting van Gogh's intense emotional life and multiple depressive episodes. He argues that van Gogh’s mental state included both reactive depression and bipolar aspects. Blumer also discusses the artist’s use of absinthe and its potential role in triggering his psychotic episodes. The article’s contribution to the understanding of van Gogh’s complex mental health is significant, especially in relation to the creative arts and health studies, as it delves into the interplay between mental illness and artistic productivity.
