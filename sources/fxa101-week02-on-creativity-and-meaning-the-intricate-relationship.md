## On Creativity and Meaning: The Intricate Relationship Between Creativity and Meaning in Life and Creativity as the Means to Repay Existential Debt

The article explores the intricate relationship between creativity and meaning in life, emphasizing that creativity is a critical trait for human survival and thriving. It argues that modern societal conditions can lead to existential crises characterized by meaninglessness, which negatively impacts creativity and vice versa. The paper discusses how creativity provides essential meaning through its capacity to solve complex problems and promote psychological well-being. It also highlights the evolutionary debt humans owe to their ancestors, emphasizing the necessity of contributing novel and valuable ideas to ensure continued human progress. By fostering a society that prioritizes creativity, individuals can enhance their mental health and find profound purpose in life.
