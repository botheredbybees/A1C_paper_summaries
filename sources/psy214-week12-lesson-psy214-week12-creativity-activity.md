## Creative Activities for Stress Reduction and Mindfulness

This lesson introduces three creative activities aimed at reducing stress and enhancing mindfulness. The first activity involves taking photographs of natural phenomena, linking to the biophilia hypothesis and attention restoration theory. The second activity involves crafting a gratitude card, combining the benefits of expressing gratitude and engaging in creative activities. The third activity is a breathing and painting exercise that uses watercolour paints to express one's emotional state through color. Each activity emphasizes the importance of observing the process rather than focusing on the outcome, fostering a mindful approach to creativity.
