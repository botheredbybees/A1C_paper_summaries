## Effects of Music on Agitation in Dementia: A Meta-Analysis

This meta-analysis investigates the impact of music interventions on agitation in dementia patients. Agitation, common in dementia and encompassing behaviors like repetitive acts, restlessness, wandering, and aggression, can significantly reduce positive social interactions and increase caregiver burden. Despite the use of pharmacological treatments, the study highlights the potential of non-pharmacological methods, such as music interventions, as promising alternatives. The research reviewed twelve studies, concluding that music interventions have a medium overall effect on reducing agitation in dementia, indicating clinically and statistically robust outcomes. The study underscores the need for further research to differentiate between various types of music interventions for optimal clinical application.
