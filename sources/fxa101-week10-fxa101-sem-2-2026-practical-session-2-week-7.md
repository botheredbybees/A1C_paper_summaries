## FXA101 Sem 2 2026 – Practical Session 2 Week 7

This lesson text from FXA101 Sem 2 2026 explores the relationship between mood and creativity. It discusses how positive moods can enhance creative thinking and motivation, citing studies by Yefet and Glicksohn (2020) and Smith et al. (2022). Fredrickson’s Broaden and Build Theory (2004) is highlighted, emphasizing that positive emotions allow for greater flexibility and originality in ideas. However, the lesson also notes that negative emotions can serve as a motivator for creative efforts, particularly when experienced strongly (Zenasni & Lubart, 2002; Smith et al., 2022). The text cautions against overindulgence in positive behaviors that could lead to decreased satisfaction and increased negativity due to excessive screen time (Tsai, 2023). To illustrate these concepts, the lesson includes several creative exercises designed to engage students in different forms of creative thinking, such as back-ronym, shiritori, hirameki, and impossible animals. These activities encourage reflection on internal thoughts and feelings, potentially leading to a greater sense of confidence and creativity. The relevance to creative arts and health is evident in the exploration of how emotional states influence creative processes and outcomes, impacting both individual well-being and creative productivity.
