## Practising Compassion and Self-Compassion

This lesson covers the principles and practices of compassion and self-compassion. It introduces the kindness exercise, which involves directing unconditional kindness towards oneself and others through mental repetition of well-wishes. The lesson also details the self-compassion break, a structured practice developed by Kristen Neff and her colleagues, aimed at cultivating self-compassion by focusing on mindfulness, common humanity, and self-kindness. The exercises are part of the Mindful Self-Compassion (MSC) program and are supported by research indicating increased self-compassion and well-being among participants.
