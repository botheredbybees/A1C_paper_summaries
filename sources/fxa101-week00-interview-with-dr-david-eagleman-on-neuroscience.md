## Interview with Dr. David Eagleman on Neuroscience, Consciousness, and Synesthesia

Dr. David Eagleman discusses his work in neuroscience focusing on the uncharted territories that make it an exciting field for researchers. He highlights his collaboration with Francis Crick, where he conducted studies on consciousness and time perception, published in Science, revealing that human perception operates with a delay. Additionally, Dr. Eagleman explores synesthesia, a neurological condition where senses intertwine unexpectedly. Utilizing the advent of the internet, he developed an uncheatable online test called the Synesthesia Battery to systematically study this phenomenon across large populations, collecting data from over 40,000 individuals with verified synesthesia.
