## How Deafness Shaped My Love of Music

Jessica White's personal essay explores how her deafness has influenced her relationship with music. Despite losing hearing in one ear entirely and significant loss in the other, White maintains a deep appreciation for music, particularly through synthesizers and vibrations. She reflects on her childhood experiences with music, including piano lessons and listening to music through vibrations. The essay highlights the complexity of deafness, challenging misconceptions that deaf individuals cannot enjoy or create music. White also discusses the importance of representation in music and deaf culture, advocating for inclusivity and the celebration of deaf artists and their contributions to the music world. This piece underscores the intersection of creativity, disability, and personal identity, offering insights into the diverse ways in which deaf individuals engage with music.
