## FXA303 Week 3: Introduction to Creative Arts, Play and Mental Wellbeing

Welcome to the third week of FXA303, a course focused on creative arts, play, and mental wellbeing. The document emphasizes the significance of adolescence as a critical period for brain development and neural pathway formation. It highlights how research in neuroscience and developmental psychology has deepened our understanding of cognitive processing and hormone levels during this crucial decade of life. Adolescence is marked by significant physical and emotional changes, with the brain undergoing major reconfiguration to support future cognitive functioning and mental wellbeing. The text underscores the role of creative arts engagement as a means for adolescents to express emotions, enhance neural pathways, and foster supportive social networks that aid in developing coping mechanisms essential for adult life. Additionally, the document encourages students to review web lecture recordings if they missed them, stressing that these sessions are integral not only for reinforcing weekly content but also for preparing for formative quizzes.
