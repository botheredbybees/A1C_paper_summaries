## Week 6 - Sight, Insight and Visual Artists

This week's lesson focuses on the perspectives of blindness and visual impairment in creative arts and literature. It covers historical and contemporary depictions of vision loss in the works of visual artists, poets, and musicians. The lesson aims to challenge stereotypes about people with visual impairments engaging in and contributing to the visual arts through specific case studies and contextual discussions. Students are expected to learn about the lived experiences of visually impaired individuals, their adaptive strategies, and the societal impacts on wellbeing.
