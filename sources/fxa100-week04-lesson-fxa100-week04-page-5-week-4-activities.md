## FXA100 The Arts and Dementia Care - Week 4 Activities

This week's activities in the FXA100 course emphasize the importance of checking enrollment status before the census date and completing required readings on community arts projects. The main reading assignment is 'Making Art with Communities' by Arts Victoria and Victorian State Government, focusing on pages 22-34 of the PDF document. An optional supplementary article discusses music therapy's role in dementia care. Students are instructed to complete a quiz worth 5% of their final grade and participate in a discussion about language used when discussing dementia. References include studies on singing benefits for elderly patients with dementia, evaluation frameworks for arts-based health interventions, and guidelines from Dementia Australia.
