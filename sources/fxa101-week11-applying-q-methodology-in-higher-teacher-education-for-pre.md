## Applying Q methodology in higher teacher education for pre-service teachers’ (self-)reflection, learning and professional development

This paper investigates the application of Q methodology in fostering (self-)reflection among pre-service teachers in higher education. The authors argue that traditional reflection methods often lack the structure and effectiveness needed to promote meaningful and deep reflection. The study develops a reflection design that combines Q-sorting with various reflective stimuli, such as peer feedback and researcher input, to help pre-service teachers critically examine their beliefs and attitudes about teaching. This approach is tested in a graduate course in southern Germany, where participants engage in multiple reflection sessions to enhance their professional development. The findings indicate that Q methodology is a valuable tool in university settings for pre-service teachers, as it facilitates both self-reflection and continuous professionalization. The paper highlights the potential of Q methodology to support the development of teaching practice based on reflective insights.
