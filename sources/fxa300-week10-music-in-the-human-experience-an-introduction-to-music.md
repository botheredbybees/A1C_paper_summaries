## Music in the Human Experience: An Introduction to Music Psychology (Conclusion)

The document concludes by highlighting the collaborative efforts between music therapists, music medicine practitioners, and performing arts medicine professionals in promoting research and public awareness about the impact of music on health and wellness. Music therapists use specialized skills to help clients attain and maintain health and wellbeing, addressing various physical, social, emotional, and psychological goals. Music medicine involves health care professionals utilizing music to alleviate pain and anxiety, particularly in surgical and chronic pain contexts. Performing arts medicine focuses on treating profession-related injuries and difficulties faced by musicians. Despite commonalities, each field maintains a unique identity, with music therapy having the longest heritage. The text emphasizes the pervasive influence of music on health and wellbeing due to its emotional, engaging, and social nature, as well as its ability to affect behavior and identity. The discussion questions prompt reflection on the roles and benefits of these disciplines across a person's lifespan, the qualifications needed for a music therapist, and the challenges faced by musicians in terms of physical and mental health. The importance of a comprehensive musicians' wellness plan is also underscored.
