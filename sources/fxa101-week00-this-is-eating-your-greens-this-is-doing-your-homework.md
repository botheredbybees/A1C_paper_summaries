## [T]his is eating your greens, this is doing your homework': writing and rehearsing a full-length stand-up show

This article explores the creative processes used in stand-up comedy through an analysis of materials from the British Stand-Up Comedy Archive and a personal project documenting Oliver Double's own full-length show 'Break a Leg.' The focus is on how comedians document their work and reflect on it, moving from tacit knowledge to explicit reflection. It discusses issues related to the ephemerality of stand-up performances and the motivations behind documentation practices.
