## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Bringing it together

This lesson discusses the intersection of creativity with various unit themes, focusing on the DRAMMA model, mindful attention, flow, and meaning-making. It explores how creative activities can foster autonomy, mastery, meaning, and affiliation, providing positive distractions and promoting detachment-recovery. The lesson mentions the impact of viewing artwork in healthcare settings and the role of art in cognitive focus and mindfulness. It also highlights the benefits of flow states in creative activities and how they contribute to personal achievement and optimism. Additionally, the text emphasizes the role of meaning-making in transforming daily observations into meaningful objects or experiences, enhancing mental health and resilience.
