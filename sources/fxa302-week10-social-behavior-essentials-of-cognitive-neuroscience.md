## Social Behavior - Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The document 'Social Behavior' from 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Postle explores the influence of social context on various cognitive and behavioral processes. It highlights how social trust affects individuals' decisions to delay gratification, a trait linked to success in many life aspects. The case of Phineas Gage, whose medial prefrontal cortex (PFC) was damaged, demonstrates the role of the PFC in social behavior regulation. The text also discusses neural abnormalities in the ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC) in individuals with psychopathy. Additionally, it delves into the 'theory of mind' (ToM) network, examining the role of the right temporoparietal junction (TPJ) in moral decision-making and attentional reorienting. Observational learning is another key theme, illustrating how observing others' actions can contribute to learning new information. This interdisciplinary approach underscores the importance of considering social factors in understanding cognitive neuroscience, particularly in relation to health and well-being in creative arts and health contexts.
