## The Effects of Different Amounts and Types of Music Training on Music Style Preference

This study examines the influence of varying amounts and types of music training on college non-music majors' preferences for different musical styles. A total of 176 participants were asked to rate their preference for 19 diverse musical examples. The results indicate that those with five or more years of music training exhibited significantly higher overall music preference ratings compared to others with less training. Additionally, significant differences in preference ratings emerged among subjects who had participated in choir, band, and piano lessons, particularly in non-popular styles of music where participation in band appeared to have the strongest impact.
