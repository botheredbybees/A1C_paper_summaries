## Brain morphometry shows effects of long-term musical practice in middle-aged keyboard players

This study investigates the effects of long-term musical practice on brain structure in middle-aged keyboard players. The research compares 20 male professional musicians and 19 matched controls using T1-weighted MR images analyzed with deformation-based morphometry. Key findings include significant volumetric differences in the brains of intensive practicing keyboard players compared to controls in right sensorimotor areas, corticospinal tract, entorhinal cortex, and left superior parietal lobule. The study concludes that intensive and specific musical training impacts brain structure throughout life, not just during sensitive periods of childhood. These results contribute to understanding brain plasticity and the influence of long-term practice on neural development.
