## Rare Conditions, Diagnostic Challenges, and Controversies in Clinical Neuropsychology

This book addresses rare and challenging conditions in clinical neuropsychology, focusing on cases that are difficult to diagnose and manage. It includes 23 cases that fall outside routine clinical practice, divided into sections covering rare and unusual conditions, diagnostic challenges, and controversial issues. The book aims to fill a gap in the literature by documenting the experiences of clinicians who have worked with such cases, providing insights into how to approach complex cases and manage conditions that may be less common but still encountered in clinical settings. It emphasizes the importance of understanding rare conditions for enhancing clinical practice and improving patient care.
