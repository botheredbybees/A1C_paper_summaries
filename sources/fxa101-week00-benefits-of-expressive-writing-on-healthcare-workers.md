## Benefits of Expressive Writing on Healthcare Workers’ Psychological Adjustment During the COVID-19 Pandemic

This paper examines the psychological impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on healthcare workers (HCWs) and evaluates the efficacy of an expressive writing (EW) intervention based on Pennebaker’s paradigmatic protocol. The study hypothesized that Italian HCWs would experience high levels of psychological distress during the pandemic, with EW being effective in reducing this distress. Fifty-five participants were randomly assigned to either an EW or neutral writing condition and assessed for PTSD, depression, global psychopathology symptoms, perceived social support, and resilience before and after three writing sessions. The results showed significant improvements in EW group participants’ psychological adjustment, with variations linked to individual differences such as age, gender, marital status, and baseline symptom levels.
