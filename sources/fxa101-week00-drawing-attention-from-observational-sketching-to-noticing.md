## Drawing Attention: From Observational Sketching to Noticing

This paper explores the concept of noticing as a design practice that includes more-than-human perspectives, using sketching to facilitate concentrated observation and detailed documentation in multi-species ethnography. The author developed elements to scaffold reflection on sketches for noticing during a four-month residency and later introduced this practice to nine participants. This study reveals how guided noticing practices can foster attentive engagement with the environment, exposing blind spots in human-centric perspectives and promoting continuous noticing in design processes.
