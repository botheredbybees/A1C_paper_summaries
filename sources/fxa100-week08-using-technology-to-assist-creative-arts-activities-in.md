## Using technology to assist creative arts activities in dementia care

This poster presentation explores the application of technology to support creative arts activities for individuals with dementia, aiming to enhance their leisure and well-being experiences. The study involved a scoping review focusing on how current technologies are utilized in various art forms such as music, visual arts, storytelling, and filmmaking. By examining 51 relevant articles from multiple databases including PubMed and Web of Science, the research identified that most technological interventions were custom-made devices rather than applications using commonly available electronic gadgets like smartphones or computers. These devices were predominantly designed for individual use under caregiver supervision, with limited adaptability for group settings. The outcomes emphasized the validation of feasibility in both technology and arts-based activities but highlighted a gap in comprehensive evaluation and comparative studies across different art forms. The authors recommend future research to focus on developing more versatile technological tools that can cater to diverse needs and interests among people living with dementia.
