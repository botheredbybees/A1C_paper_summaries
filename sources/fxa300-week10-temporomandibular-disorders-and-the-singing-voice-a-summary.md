## Temporomandibular Disorders and the Singing Voice: A Summary of the Research

Jane Clukey's article provides a comprehensive summary of the relationship between temporomandibular disorders (TMDs) and the singing voice. TMDs are prevalent conditions affecting the temporomandibular joints and surrounding structures, often causing pain and dysfunction. The article explores the intricate connection between TMDs and vocal function, emphasizing the importance of TMJ function in normal voice production. It highlights that changes in jaw function have a significant impact on voice parameters, such as laryngeal tension and resonance. Clukey discusses the biopsychosocial model to explain the etiology of TMDs, noting that factors like psychological stress, biological trauma, and social influences can contribute to the development and perpetuation of TMDs. The article also examines the specific challenges faced by singers with TMDs, including the impact on voice quality and the need for adapted vocal techniques. Clukey concludes by identifying gaps in current research and suggesting areas for future investigation, particularly concerning the interplay between TMDs and vocal performance in singers.
