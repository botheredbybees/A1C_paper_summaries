## Art is Therapy at the Rijksmuseum

In this transcript of Alain de Botton's presentation at the Rijksmuseum, he discusses the role of museums and art in a secular society. He argues that the rise of museums coincided with a decline in religious belief and suggests that art can fulfill some of the functions previously served by religion, such as providing moral guidance and consolation for life's hardships. De Botton contrasts the traditional didactic approach to art, where works were meant to convey specific messages or beliefs, with the modern view that art is enigmatic and private. He advocates for a return to using art in a more therapeutic manner, emphasizing its role in fostering emotional maturity by engaging with tragedy and loss. The discussion also touches on the practical demonstration of this concept through an exhibition called 'Art as Therapy' at the museum.
