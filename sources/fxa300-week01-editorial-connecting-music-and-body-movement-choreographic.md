## Editorial: Connecting Music and Body Movement: Choreographic Approach of Performance

This editorial discusses the exploration of choreography in musical performance, focusing on how performers produce different sound qualities through specific body movements. It highlights research that aims to understand and optimize these relationships from ergonomic and physiological perspectives. The article synthesizes contributions from various fields such as neuroscience, ergonomics, and technology, emphasizing the importance of interdisciplinary approaches for a comprehensive analysis of music performance dynamics. Relevant studies include investigations into mirror neuron activity during opera performances, the application of deep learning techniques to analyze musical gestures in violinists' practices, and methods to enhance pianist’s timbre development through teacher-student interactions. The work underscores the potential of technological advancements to refine our understanding of sound production processes and their impact on performance quality.
