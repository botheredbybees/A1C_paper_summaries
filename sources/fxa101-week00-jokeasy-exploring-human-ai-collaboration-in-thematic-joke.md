## Jokeasy: Exploring Human-AI Collaboration in Thematic Joke Generation

This paper presents Jokeasy, a prototype system designed to explore human-AI collaboration in the creation of thematic jokes. The research addresses the inherent challenges of joke writing, which relies on fresh and culturally relevant material that traditional Large Language Models (LLMs) often struggle to provide dynamically. Jokeasy integrates LLMs as both material scouts and writers, supporting a multi-stage workflow for gathering timely web content, organizing inspiration blocks, and drafting jokes. The study includes qualitative analysis from 18 participants, revealing insights into effective human-AI collaboration in joke creation, including the importance of real-time web integration and balanced agency between human creators and AI.
