## Overcoming Music Performance Anxiety Through Mental Training

This transcript discusses music performance anxiety and explores the concept of mental training, a practice well-established in sports psychology but less so in music. The speaker, a pianist and piano pedagogue, outlines the goals of her research on mental training for musicians, categorizing existing techniques into four areas: activation regulation, motivation regulation, regulation of emotions, and mental practicing. She conducts an experiment with nine participants, revealing a significant decrease in somatic and cognitive anxieties and an increase in self-confidence after mental training. The speaker also emphasizes the importance of understanding the purpose of performing and uses visualization as a technique to prepare for performances.
