## Can music therapy engage patients in group cognitive behaviour therapy for substance abuse treatment?

This clinical study explores the effectiveness of integrating music therapy as an adjunct to group cognitive behavior therapy (CBT) for substance abuse treatment. The researchers aimed to assess whether music therapy could enhance patient engagement in a 7-week trial within a private hospital's group program. Data from 24 anonymous surveys were analyzed, showing a high attendance rate of 75%. Patients reported high levels of enjoyment and motivation during music therapy sessions. The majority of participants indicated they would attend future music therapy sessions, and many felt it helped them feel more connected to the group. The study found that music therapy could engage patients regardless of age or primary substance of abuse. These results suggest that music therapy is a promising approach to improve engagement in substance abuse treatment programs, particularly among younger patients who may be less likely to engage in traditional CBT.
