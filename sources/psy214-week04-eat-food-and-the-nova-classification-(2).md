## “Eat food” and the NOVA classification

The document discusses the concept of 'eat food' as a principle that emphasizes consuming whole foods rather than processed alternatives. It introduces the NOVA classification system, which categorizes food based on their degree of processing into four categories: unprocessed or minimally processed (NOVA 1), processed culinary ingredients (NOVA 2), processed foods (NOVA 3), and ultra-processed foods (NOVA 4). The document provides examples for each category and highlights the importance of distinguishing between different types of food based on their processing level. It also offers practical advice for consumers, such as examining packaging and ingredient lists to make informed choices about what they eat. This classification system is particularly relevant in discussions of diet quality and its impact on health within creative arts and health contexts.
