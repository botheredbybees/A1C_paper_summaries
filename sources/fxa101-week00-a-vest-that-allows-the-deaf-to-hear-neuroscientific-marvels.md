## A Vest That Allows the Deaf to 'Hear': Neuroscientific Marvels and Legal System Intersections

Dr. David Eagleman discusses his work on a wearable device for the deaf, which converts sound into tactile sensations, allowing users to 'feel' auditory information. He also addresses the intersection of neuroscience and law, advocating for a legal system that recognizes individual brain differences through specialized courts for mental health and drug addiction issues. Dr. Eagleman's new book, 'The Runaway Species,' explores human creativity and its role in societal advancement.
