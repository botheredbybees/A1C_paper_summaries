## Character Strengths Notes Slides

The document 'Character Strengths Notes Slides' outlines the framework of character strengths within positive psychology, emphasizing their importance beyond traditional DSM classifications. It details the development of the VIA (Values in Action) character strengths framework by Christopher Peterson and Martin Seligman, involving a 3-year project that incorporated insights from world religions, philosophers, and psychologists. The framework identifies 24 character strengths across six virtues: wisdom, courage, humanity, justice, temperance, and transcendence. However, recent research challenges the theoretical grouping of these virtues, suggesting a more empirical structure of intellectual, social/interpersonal, and self-regulatory/intrapersonal virtues. The document highlights the unique constellation of strengths each individual possesses and encourages self-assessment through the VIA Character Strengths Survey. Understanding and leveraging character strengths is proposed as a beneficial approach to mental well-being and personal development, reflecting the positive psychology's shift towards strengths-based approaches rather than solely focusing on pathology.
