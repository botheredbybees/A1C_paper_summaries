## Dancing for Parkinson’s Disease Online: Clinical Trial Process Evaluation

This paper presents a process evaluation of the 'ParkinDANCE Online' clinical trial, a co-produced dance therapy program for people with Parkinson's disease (PD) delivered via online platforms. The evaluation was structured to assess contextual factors, implementation procedures, and stakeholder views on the success of the program. The study included 12 participants with PD, four dance instructors, and two physiotherapists. The program was well received, with no attrition or adverse events reported. Participants valued the skills mastery and engagement provided by the program. The dance instructors found digital delivery to be practical and engaging. The evaluation concluded that it is feasible to deliver online dancing to people with early PD, facilitating greater accessibility to therapeutic interventions for those with chronic neurological conditions.
