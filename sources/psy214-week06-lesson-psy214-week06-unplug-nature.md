## Effects of Time Spent in Nature on Mental Health

This lesson explores the mental health benefits associated with spending time in nature. It emphasizes that 'nature' includes both urban green spaces and wilderness areas, highlighting the importance of increased exposure to natural environments for reducing stress and enhancing well-being. Theoretical perspectives such as Attention Restoration Theory, Stress Reduction Theory, and Connectedness to Nature are discussed, along with a 2011 meta-analysis indicating enhanced positive effects of outdoor exercise compared to indoor activity. A Stanford University study also showed that walking in nature versus urban settings has distinct impacts on brain activity and mental health.
