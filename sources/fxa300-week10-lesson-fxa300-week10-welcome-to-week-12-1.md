## Instruments, Injuries and Performance Anxiety

This lesson, part of the FXA300 Music, Mind and Body course, focuses on the interconnection between music-making and physical and mental health. Dr Brendan Lamb discusses common physical injuries and mental challenges faced by musicians, emphasizing the importance of awareness and knowledge for injury prevention and effective recovery. The lesson highlights the collaboration between conservatories and medical experts to disseminate knowledge and promote healthy practices among musicians. Key strategies for well-being and injury prevention are outlined, and learners are encouraged to reflect on how the mind can support the body in avoiding or recovering from injuries. The lesson includes two short videos and their transcripts, along with learning objectives and a time commitment of 5-6 hours.
