## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The document provides an introduction to the field of cognitive neuroscience, highlighting its focus on understanding the neurobiological bases of mental phenomena and cognition. It distinguishes cognitive neuroscience from other disciplines by emphasizing its grounding in neuroscience methods and its priority on studying the neural bases of thinking. The chapter traces the origins of cognitive neuroscience to debates in the 19th century regarding the localization of brain functions versus the mass action principle. Early research established the validity of localization for motor control, vision, and speech production. The text also discusses the contribution of non-human animal research to the field and the three perspectives that shaped early cognitive neuroscience: systems neuroscience, behavioral neurology/neuropsychology, and experimental psychology. The document underscores the importance of rigorous thought and validity in studying cognitive functions, noting that not all aspects of human behavior are equally suited to cognitive neuroscience research.
