## How mindfulness works: The big picture

The document 'How mindfulness works: The big picture' explores the concept of mindfulness as a means to foster greater attention and awareness of present moment experiences. It distinguishes between informal mindfulness practices, which focus on short-term outcomes such as positive and negative affect, and formal/psychotherapeutic mindfulness practices, which aim for long-term outcomes and complex mechanisms of action. The document posits that mental ill health arises from dysregulation of cognitive and affective systems, and mindfulness can improve mental health by better regulating these systems. It outlines a perspective where life is often challenging, and our mental habits can exacerbate this challenge. Through mindfulness, individuals can alter their automatic mental habits, which often involve negative judgements, desires, aversions, and unrealistic thoughts. Mindfulness practices aim to increase self-awareness, observe thoughts without judgment, regulate emotions, and reduce automatic reactions, thereby making life less stressful and enhancing overall well-being.
