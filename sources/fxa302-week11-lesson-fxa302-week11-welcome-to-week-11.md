## FXA302 Week 11 Refigured Bodies

This lesson explores the impact of severe injuries on the lives of creative and performing artists, focusing on how such injuries refigure both their bodies and their practices. It discusses the psychological challenges faced by amputees, including phantom limb syndrome, and the role of the creative arts in therapy and personal transformation. The lesson highlights the use of arts-based therapy programs for emotional expression and self-esteem rebuilding, emphasizing the contributions of technology in facilitating creativity for those with physical impairments. It aims to challenge perceptions and assumptions about physical disability and its impact on wellbeing.
