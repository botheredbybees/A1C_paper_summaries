## Sleep Hygiene

The document 'Sleep Hygiene' by Kate Talsma of the University of Tasmania provides guidance on establishing healthy sleep habits. Key recommendations include reserving the bed for sleeping and not engaging in other activities, creating a quiet, dark, and comfortable environment conducive to sleep, and avoiding naps if they are unnecessary or taken late at night. The importance of winding down before bedtime by engaging in relaxing activities rather than stimulating ones is emphasized. Additionally, the document advises caution with the use of substances such as stimulants and sedatives that can disrupt sleep patterns. Consistency in sleep routines and managing worries through designated worry times are also suggested to improve sleep quality.
