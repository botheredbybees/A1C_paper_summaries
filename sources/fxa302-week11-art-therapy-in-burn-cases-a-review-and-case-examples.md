## Art Therapy in Burn Cases: A Review and Case Examples

This article reviews the literature on the use of art therapy for patients with burn injuries, emphasizing its potential to address complex physical and psychosocial needs. The authors discuss the contributions of art therapy through an examination of seven peer-reviewed articles, highlighting its role in enhancing overall psychosocial adjustment and providing case examples of burn survivors. The review identifies gaps in current research and underscores the need for further exploration into the contextual, social, and relational aspects of burn treatment. Art therapy is proposed as an integrative therapeutic intervention to support holistic recovery, addressing trauma, anxiety, and social isolation. The article concludes with recommendations for future research to better understand the benefits of art therapy in burn care settings.
