## Dementia Language Guidelines

The document 'Dementia Language Guidelines' emphasizes the importance of using appropriate and respectful language when discussing dementia to avoid perpetuating stigma and misinformation. It advocates for accurate, inclusive, empowering, and non-stigmatizing terminology that focuses on the abilities rather than deficits of individuals living with dementia. The guidelines stress the uniqueness of each person's experience, urging respect for individual preferences regarding how they wish to be described in relation to their condition. Additionally, it underscores the need for cultural sensitivity when using language about dementia within diverse communities, suggesting that terms and phrases used should reflect an understanding of different cultural backgrounds and perspectives.
