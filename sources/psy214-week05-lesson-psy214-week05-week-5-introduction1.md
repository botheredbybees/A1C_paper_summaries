## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Week on Rest and Relaxation

The lesson focuses on the importance of rest and relaxation in managing stress and enhancing well-being. It emphasizes the significance of sleep quality and quantity for physical and mental health, providing evidence from experts to highlight the current state of poor sleep hygiene among Australians. Strategies for improving sleep are discussed along with an introduction to formal and informal relaxation techniques that can mitigate everyday distress. The lesson prompts reflection on personal experiences with stress, well-being, barriers to healthy sleeping habits, and the effectiveness of various relaxation activities.
