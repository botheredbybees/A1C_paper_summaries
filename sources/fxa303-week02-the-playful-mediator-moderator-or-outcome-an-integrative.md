## The playful mediator, moderator, or outcome? An integrative review of the roles of play and playfulness in adult-centered psychological interventions for mental health

This article evaluates the role of play and playfulness in adult psychological interventions aimed at enhancing mental health. The authors conducted an integrative review of empirical evidence from 20 studies, focusing on how these elements contribute as mediators or outcomes in therapeutic contexts. They found preliminary evidence suggesting that play can act as a causal agent for positive changes in adults' mental health through two mechanisms: as a mediator and as a medium. However, the evidence regarding the moderating effect of participant playfulness on intervention effectiveness was limited and unclear. The review highlights the need for more rigorous quantitative assessments to better understand the role of play and playfulness in psychological interventions. It underscores the importance of distinguishing between trait and state playfulness when examining outcomes. Overall, the study emphasizes the potential therapeutic power of play in improving adult health and wellbeing.
