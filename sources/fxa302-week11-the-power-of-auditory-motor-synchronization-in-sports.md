## The Power of Auditory-Motor Synchronization in Sports: Enhancing Running Performance by Coupling Cadence with the Right Beats

This study explores the effects of auditory-motor synchronization and motivational music on running performance. The researchers hypothesized that both factors could enhance performance, with auditory-motor synchronization helping to maintain a consistent pace and motivational music boosting physiological effort. The experiment involved 19 participants running to exhaustion on a treadmill under three conditions: control (no acoustic stimuli), metronome (beeps synchronized with the runner’s cadence), and motivational music (synchronous with the runner’s cadence). The results showed that both conditions with acoustic stimuli led to longer times to exhaustion compared to the control condition. However, there was no significant difference between the metronome and motivational music conditions, despite motivational music slightly reducing perceived exertion and heart rates at high intensities. The study concludes that optimal performance can be achieved by combining auditory-motor synchronization with motivational music that has a prominent and consistent beat, enhancing both work efficiency and effort. This research is relevant to creative arts and health, demonstrating how auditory stimuli can be used to improve physical performance and potentially inform artistic and therapeutic practices.
