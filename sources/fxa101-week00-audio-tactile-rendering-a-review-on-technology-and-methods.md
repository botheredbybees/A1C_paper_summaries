## Audio-Tactile Rendering: A Review on Technology and Methods to Convey Musical Information through the Sense of Touch

This review article discusses the implementation of tactile rendering in digital musical instruments (DMIs) and haptic music players (HMPs), focusing on methods to convey musical information through vibrotactile stimuli for enhancing user experience and aiding hearing-impaired individuals. The authors explore various technologies, such as voice coil actuators and piezoelectric motors, used in HMPs. They also examine the psychological processes involved in tactile perception, including mechanoreceptors and spatial resolution. Additionally, the review highlights recent advancements in musical haptics and sensory substitution systems, emphasizing the need for standardized evaluation methods to ensure future scalability of these technologies.
