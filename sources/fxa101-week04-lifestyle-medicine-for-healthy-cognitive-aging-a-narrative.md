## Lifestyle medicine for healthy cognitive aging: A narrative review

The article discusses the role of lifestyle medicine in preventing dementia and cognitive decline among aging populations. The review highlights that neurodegenerative diseases, particularly Alzheimer's disease, pose significant economic and social challenges due to rising healthcare costs and caregiver burden. To mitigate these issues, the authors emphasize the importance of preventive measures such as dietary interventions, exercise, and mindfulness practices. These lifestyle changes aim to alleviate the cognitive decline associated with aging and reduce the societal impact of neurodegenerative diseases. The review underscores the potential for affordable and scalable strategies to improve cognitive health in later life, thus contributing positively to public health and quality of life.
