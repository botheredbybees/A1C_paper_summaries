## Positive Psychology Overview

The document provides an overview of positive psychology, a subdiscipline of psychology that focuses on optimal functioning and well-being. It contrasts positive psychology with abnormal or clinical psychology and discusses its history, rooted in the late 1990s with Martin Seligman's presidency of the American Psychological Association. The text elaborates on the concept of positive thinking and its various forms, including dispositional optimism, cognitive restructuring, and benefit-finding or meaning-making coping strategies. It also highlights the importance of distinguishing between positive thinking as a beneficial practice and the risky overemphasis on positive thinking that can lead to avoidance of negative realities and self-deception. The document emphasizes the value of positive psychology interventions, such as gratitude and kindness practices, in enhancing well-being and offers practical examples to illustrate contrasting approaches.
