## FXA303 Week 2: Introduction to Child Development and Play

Week 2 of the FXA303 Creative Arts, Play and Mental Wellbeing course introduces foundational concepts related to child development. The document emphasizes the importance of a child's experiences during the first 1000 days of life for optimal brain development. It highlights how play supports the creation of neural pathways necessary for learning, social interaction, and emotional well-being. Additionally, the role of creative arts in enhancing cognitive and emotional development is discussed. The week also includes drop-in sessions to facilitate peer and tutor discussions on course content.
