## The Predictable Semiotic Essence of Humor

Christian Hempelmann addresses a renewed criticism of the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH) by Elliott Oring, focusing on the misunderstanding and misdirection in Oring's critique. He argues that neither opposition nor logical mechanism alone is sufficient for humor but rather they work together to create humorous texts. Hempelmann clarifies misconceptions about labeling concepts and provides examples such as the 'man of letters' joke and the alcoholic tutor joke to illustrate how opposition and overlap contribute to humor. The paper emphasizes the need for an updated canon with unified definitions by practitioners within the paradigm, highlighting the importance of logical mechanism (LM) in creating script overlap essential for humor.
