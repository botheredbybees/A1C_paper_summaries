## Mindsong – transcript

This transcript features interviews with individuals involved in Mind Song, a project that integrates music and arts into dementia care. Anthia Holland, the director, initiated this initiative as an outreach program for the Three Choirs Festival in 2007 after working extensively with elderly patients in care homes. Fiona Taylor, a music therapist, highlights how music therapy can reconnect individuals who have lost communication abilities due to dementia, engaging them through improvised and familiar songs. Sylvia Arron, a volunteer nurse, emphasizes her desire to continue in a caring role through Mind Song, merging her passion for old people and music. Jane Krampton discusses the therapeutic benefits of music in enhancing physical health, social interactions, and emotional well-being among dementia patients. The transcript underscores the potential of arts-based interventions to improve quality of life and foster meaningful connections within care settings.
