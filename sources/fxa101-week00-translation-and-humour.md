## Translation and Humour

This paper discusses the application of the General Theory of Verbal Humour (GTVH) to humor translation, focusing on a metric of joke similarity based on six Knowledge Resources: Language, Narrative Strategy, Target(s), Situation, Logical Mechanisms, and Script Oppositions. It argues that while literal translations may preserve meaning, they often fail to maintain pragmatic force or cultural nuances critical for humor comprehension across languages. The paper proposes translating humor by considering the similarity between meanings and pragmatic forces of source and target texts, emphasizing a more nuanced approach than traditional translation theories offer.
