## Haptic-assistive Technologies for Audition and Vision Sensory Disabilities

This review paper analyzes haptic-assistive technologies designed to substitute for auditory and visual senses in individuals with sensory disabilities, including deafness, blindness, or both (deaf-blindness). It evaluates the effectiveness of these systems that convert audiovisual information into tactile feedback via various actuation methods. The study identifies gaps in user acceptance and highlights future research directions towards developing more accessible, miniaturized, and cost-effective haptic interfaces for broader use among sensory-disabled populations.
