## In-home online music-based intervention for stress, coping, and depression among family caregivers of persons with dementia: A pilot study

This pilot study investigated the effects of an eight-week online music-based intervention on stress, coping, and depressive symptoms among family caregivers providing in-home care for individuals with dementia. The study involved two groups: a group receiving the intervention and a comparison group without it. Results indicated that the intervention significantly improved coping skills compared to the control group at post-test. Additionally, the intervention group maintained lower levels of overall stress and better coping throughout the study period, whereas the control group experienced increased stress and diminished coping capabilities. The research highlights the potential benefits of music-based interventions in supporting family caregivers who face significant emotional and physical challenges while caring for people with dementia.
