## Neurologic Music Therapy in Stroke Rehabilitation

This paper discusses the application of neurologic music therapy (NMT) in stroke rehabilitation, emphasizing its potential to improve motor, speech, and cognitive functions through standardized clinical interventions. The research highlights the neural basis of music perception and production, particularly rhythm, which influences motor learning and rehabilitation. The authors review studies showing that rhythmic auditory stimulation (RAS) can significantly enhance gait parameters and reduce electromyographic variability in stroke patients. These findings suggest that rhythmic cues can modulate motor neuron activity, leading to more synchronized and regular motor patterns. The paper concludes that NMT offers a promising approach to stroke rehabilitation by leveraging the brain's capacity to retrain neural functions through music-based interventions.
