## Music and Memory: Wisconsin Projects Results

The 'Music and Memory' project in Wisconsin involved two studies examining the impact of personalized music on nursing home employees and community caregivers caring for individuals with dementia. The nursing home employee study surveyed 686 staff members across 83 long-term care facilities, finding that while baseline job satisfaction was higher than follow-up, personalized music significantly improved perceived pleasure and caregiving efficacy among staff. However, overall job satisfaction decreased over time, contrary to expectations. Community caregivers participated in a project where 25 caregivers (with 22 completing the program) used iPods to provide personalized music for their loved ones with dementia. The Caregiver Distress Scale showed that personalized music significantly reduced caregiver distress, emotional burden, and relationship strain, highlighting its positive impact on quality of life and caregiving experience.
