## Ethical Considerations in Psychology Research

This lesson introduces the ethical considerations in psychological research, emphasizing the importance of adhering to the National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research 2025. It discusses the four key values of respect for human beings, research merit and integrity, justice, and beneficence, as well as the themes of risk and benefit, and consent. The lesson highlights the historical context of unethical research and outlines the ethical principles necessary for conducting modern psychological studies. It also provides guidance on working with specific groups that may experience increased risk, such as Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander communities, and the role of the Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) in approving research.
