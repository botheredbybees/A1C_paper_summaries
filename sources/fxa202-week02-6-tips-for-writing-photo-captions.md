## 6 tips for writing photo captions

Vicki Krueger's article '6 Tips for Writing Photo Captions' from Poynter provides guidance on how to write effective photo captions. The central argument emphasizes the importance of adding context and new information beyond what is visually apparent in the image. Key tips include checking facts, avoiding repetition of headline or summary content, identifying main individuals in photographs, using present tense for immediacy, employing conversational language, and aligning tone with the picture's mood. This article highlights the significance of photo captions in enhancing storytelling within journalism and media production. It underscores their role in engaging readers by providing additional insights that can deepen understanding and enhance the overall narrative quality.
