## Human Aspects of IT for the Aged Population: 10th International Conference, ITAP 2024

The document provides an overview of the 10th International Conference on Human Aspects of IT for the Aged Population (ITAP 2024), which is part of the 26th Human-Computer Interaction International Conference (HCII 2024). This conference, held in Washington, DC, USA, focuses on advancements in human-computer interaction, particularly in the context of supporting older adults. The proceedings are part of the Lecture Notes in Computer Science series and feature contributions from academics, researchers, and industry experts from around the world. The conference highlights the importance of innovative IT solutions for elderly populations, addressing issues such as usability, accessibility, and technological adaptation. It underscores the role of interdisciplinary collaboration in advancing knowledge and practical applications in this field, thereby contributing to the wellbeing of elderly individuals through technological means.
