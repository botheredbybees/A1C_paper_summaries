## Physical Activity and Nature Engagement

This lesson focuses on physical activity and nature engagement as crucial elements of lifestyle medicine. It highlights the importance of sufficient physical activity in combating sedentary behavior prevalent in developed countries. The benefits of nature engagement are also discussed, including its positive impact on both physical and mental well-being, providing a sense of connection to the world beyond personal health outcomes. Challenges such as urbanization and work-related sitting hours are addressed, emphasizing the need for adaptive choices to promote health.
