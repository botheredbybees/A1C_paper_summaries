## Group Nature-Based Mindfulness Interventions: Nature-Based Mindfulness Training for College Students with Anxiety

This paper discusses the mental health crisis among college students, particularly focusing on anxiety as a prevalent issue exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic. The authors propose an innovative intervention called Nature-Based Mindfulness Training (NBMT) that integrates mindfulness and Attention Restoration Theory (ART). The framework aims to reduce anxiety by combining mindfulness practices with exposure to natural environments. The study highlights the benefits of nature-based interventions and mindfulness for college students' mental health, emphasizing the need for a structured yet accessible approach.
