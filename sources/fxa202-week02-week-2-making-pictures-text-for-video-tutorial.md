## Week 2 – Making Pictures (Text for Video Tutorial)

This tutorial discusses the advantages and disadvantages of different types of cameras, focusing on camera phones, point-and-shoot cameras, and DSLRs. The author emphasizes that while a camera phone is convenient for capturing spontaneous moments, it may lack in terms of image resolution and stability due to users often neglecting maintenance. Tips include keeping the lens clean and utilizing photo apps to enhance picture quality. Point-and-shoot cameras offer more functions than phone cameras but have limitations like fixed lenses and automatic settings that might not suit all shooting conditions. DSLRs provide high-quality photos with flexibility in manual settings, though they are expensive and bulkier. The tutorial encourages learners to explore their chosen camera's capabilities through experimentation and practice of basic photography skills.
