## Material Experimentation in the Architectural Design Studio: An Experimental Pedagogical Model for Incorporating Craft Mediums into Studio Education

This paper explores the integration of material experimentation in architectural design studios through a pedagogical model that incorporates various craft mediums. It utilizes atelierz, a vertical architectural design studio, as a case study to analyze the impact of installations and prototypes on understanding and applying materials. The research combines theoretical insights from Gilles Deleuze's philosophy with practical methodologies, emphasizing the role of material experimentation in fostering creativity and interdisciplinary collaboration.
