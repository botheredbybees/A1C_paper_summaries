## Mechanisms of Mindfulness

This document explores the mechanisms of mindfulness, detailing how mindfulness practices influence mental and emotional processes. Key mechanisms include awareness (meta-awareness) and decentering, which involve perceiving thoughts and feelings as temporary mental events rather than reality, thereby reducing experiential fusion. Attentional networks, such as the Central Executive Network (CEN) and the Default Mode Network (DMN), are discussed in relation to mindfulness, highlighting how mindfulness practice can enhance attention regulation and reduce mind wandering, spontaneous negative thinking, and rumination. Additionally, the document outlines emotion regulation strategies practiced in mindfulness, which involve creating space between emotional experiences and responses, thereby decreasing reactivity to unpleasant emotional states, pain, and cravings. Specific mechanistic pathways, such as reduced attentional bias and decentering, are noted to impact various mental health conditions, including depression, by reducing repetitive negative thoughts and enhancing non-reactivity to emotional and physical stimuli. This document is relevant to the study of creative arts and health as it elucidates the psychological processes through which mindfulness can enhance mental well-being and stress resilience.
