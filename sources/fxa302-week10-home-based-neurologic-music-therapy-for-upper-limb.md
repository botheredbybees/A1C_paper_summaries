## Home-based neurologic music therapy for upper limb rehabilitation with stroke patients at community rehabilitation stage—a feasibility study protocol

The study explores the feasibility and efficacy of home-based neurologic music therapy (NMT) for upper limb rehabilitation in stroke patients at the community rehabilitation stage. The primary objective is to investigate the application of a protocol involving 12 different instrumental exercises to promote measurable changes in upper limb function. The research includes a cross-over design with five repeated measures, where participants are randomized into either a treatment or wait-list control group. The intervention involves twice-weekly sessions over six weeks, using rhythmic auditory stimulation (RAS) techniques to enhance motor function. Key findings from the study will contribute to understanding the neural mechanisms underlying audio-motor coupling and brain connectivity, which are crucial for rehabilitation. This research is highly relevant to creative arts and health, as it demonstrates the potential of music therapy in enhancing patient engagement, motivation, and functional recovery in stroke rehabilitation.
