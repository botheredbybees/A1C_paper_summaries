## Problem Solved? Absurdist Humour and Incongruity-Resolution

This article explores the role of absurdist humor in literary narratives, focusing on Douglas Adams's work as a case study. It challenges traditional views that absurdist humor lacks resolution by arguing that resolution occurs at the level of interpretation rather than within the text itself. The paper reviews historical and philosophical perspectives on incongruity and humor, integrating cognitive theories with schema theory to analyze how literary context influences understanding and appreciation of humorous elements.
