## Activities for Week 7 - FXA101 Creativity and Ageing

This week's lesson for the course FXA101 Creativity and Ageing involves reading specific chapters from Philippa Stanton's 'Conscious Creativity' and participating in a creative exercise focusing on color and texture. Students are tasked with selecting objects of the same primary or secondary color, noting their textures, and arranging them based on shared textural characteristics. The exercise aims to enhance understanding of color and texture through sensory exploration. A discussion forum is provided where students share photographs of their arrangements and reflect on their experiences. Additionally, students are reminded to work on their mini-projects for Creative Project 2 and to adhere to submission guidelines by the deadline of August 31, 2026. References to various scholarly articles on color perception and texture are included.
