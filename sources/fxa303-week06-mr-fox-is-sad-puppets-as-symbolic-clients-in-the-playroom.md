## Mr. Fox Is Sad: Puppets as Symbolic Clients in the Playroom

This chapter discusses the use of puppets in play therapy as a tool for children to project their inner emotions and engage in therapeutic activities. Puppets are seen as symbolic clients that enable children, especially those with anxiety or trauma, to express themselves safely without verbal communication. The author explains practical considerations such as choosing culturally diverse puppets, presenting them visually to encourage engagement, and using various forms like hand puppets and Muppets. Techniques include the 'Hidden Puppet Technique' where a puppet symbolizes a shy child's reluctance to participate in therapy, thereby providing a safe outlet for fear expression. The chapter also describes combining bibliotherapy with puppet play, wherein stories help normalize experiences and empower children through reenactment by symbolic clients. A case example is provided, detailing how Emily, a 5-year-old with emotional dysregulation, used the fox puppet to express her feelings of loneliness and learn to manage them better, marking a turning point in her therapy.
