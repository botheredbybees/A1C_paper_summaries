## Reflective Practice in Teacher Professional Standards: Reflection as Mandatory Practice

This chapter examines the inclusion of reflective practice as a mandatory requirement in national professional standards for teachers across various countries. It analyzes how policymakers characterize reflective practice and highlights the tension between promoting critical awareness among teachers and the regulatory purposes of standards documents. The authors discuss the evolution of reflective practice theories and their application in teaching contexts, emphasizing the importance of considering multiple perspectives and values in reflective processes. They argue that while standards encourage critical reflection, they often do not suggest that teachers reflect on broader sociopolitical aspects of education systems. This chapter contributes to understanding the complexities and nuances of reflective practice in teacher education and its implications for educational reform.
