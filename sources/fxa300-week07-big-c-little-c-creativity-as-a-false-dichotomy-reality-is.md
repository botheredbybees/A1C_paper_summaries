## “Big C, Little c” Creativity as a False Dichotomy: Reality is not Categorical

In this article, Mark A. Runco challenges the commonly accepted distinction between Big C and little c creativity, arguing that it is a false dichotomy. Runco asserts that both forms of creativity stem from the same creative processes but differ in external factors such as social recognition, which are not inherent to creativity itself. He emphasizes that Big C creativity, characterized by fame and eminence, often involves additional elements like persistence and luck that are not required for creativity. The article highlights the importance of focusing on the continuity and commonality between Big C and little c creativity to better foster creative potential and development.
