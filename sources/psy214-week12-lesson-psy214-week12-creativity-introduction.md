## Creativity: Overview and Stress Management

This lesson explores the concept of creativity, distinguishing between 'little-c' creativity in everyday life and 'Big-C' transformative creativity. It emphasizes the role of creativity, particularly as an artistic form of expression, in enhancing coping mechanisms, emotion regulation, and well-being. The lesson reviews research indicating that creative activities can reduce stress by engaging imagination, interrupting negative thought patterns, and impacting physiological mechanisms associated with relaxation. It also discusses the benefits of creative activities such as art-making, music, rhythmic activities, and expressive writing in fostering self-development, emotion regulation, and overall well-being. The research highlights that even brief creative activities can enhance well-being and that the process of creativity is more important than skill level.
