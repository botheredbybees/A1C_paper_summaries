## 10 tips to organize your design portfolio

Slava Shestopalov's article '10 tips to organize your design portfolio' provides guidance for designers on how to effectively showcase their work to potential employers. The central argument revolves around optimizing portfolios for clarity and engagement, emphasizing the importance of clear navigation, storytelling, informative case studies, and a balanced presentation of text and visuals. Key findings include the necessity of separating case studies clearly with high-contrast dividers, telling a coherent story rather than simply showcasing outputs, adding bottom navigation to lengthy case studies, making cards more informative by including product descriptions and company profiles, highlighting key achievements before diving into detailed projects, captioning complex artifacts for clarity, and maintaining a balance between text and visuals. The relevance of these tips to creative arts and health lies in the transferable principles of effective communication and presentation, which are crucial for professionals in these fields to convey their skills and expertise to stakeholders.
