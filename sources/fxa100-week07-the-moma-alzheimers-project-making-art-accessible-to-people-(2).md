## The MoMA Alzheimer’s Project: Making Art Accessible to People with Dementia

The Museum of Modern Art's (MoMA) guide for museums aims to make art accessible and engaging for individuals living with dementia, particularly Alzheimer’s disease, and their caregivers. The MoMA Alzheimer’s Project is a nationwide initiative supported by the MetLife Foundation grant, expanding upon the success of MoMA’s Meet Me at MoMA program which started in 2006. This guide provides museum professionals with strategies to design programs that cater to cognitive impairments, covering program development, staffing requirements, community outreach, and logistical considerations. The project emphasizes the importance of tailored educational tours focusing on iconic artworks, interactive gallery discussions, and practical training for educators. Additionally, it includes a structured approach for planning sessions in galleries, including selecting themes, choosing art pieces, and facilitating participatory activities to enhance cognitive engagement and emotional well-being.
