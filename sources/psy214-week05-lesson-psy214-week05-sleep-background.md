## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: Sleep Background

This lesson discusses the biological mechanisms of sleep regulation through circadian rhythms. Circadian rhythms are synchronized to the light-dark cycle via pathways connecting the retina and the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) in the hypothalamus, which regulates melatonin production for sleep promotion. Cortisol follows a similar rhythm with peak levels early morning, aiding alertness. Disruptions like jet lag or shift work can affect circadian rhythms and correlate with mental health issues such as depression and anxiety. The lesson also covers different stages of sleep—REM and non-REM (N1, N2, N3)—and their patterns throughout the night, emphasizing deep sleep's restorative function and REM sleep’s complex biological role.
