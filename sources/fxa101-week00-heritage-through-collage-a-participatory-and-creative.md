## Heritage through Collage: A Participatory and Creative Approach to Heritage Making

This paper explores a participatory and creative approach to heritage making through collage work created by residents in Great Yarmouth, England. The study highlights how the non-linear nature of collaging allows participants to reconnect with their town's history, preserving or restoring a sense of place and belonging. The research demonstrates that while collage may seem chaotic, it can bring order to people’s thinking about their past and help communicate those thoughts through tangible artworks. The paper also discusses the role of heritage institutions in engaging with community-led practices and emphasizes the importance of inclusivity and social justice.
