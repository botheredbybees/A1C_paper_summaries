## Reading and Trauma: How the Openness of Contemporary Poetry and Haiku Facilitates Engagement

This article explores the use of contemporary poetry and haiku in engaging participants of ARRTS (Arts for Recovery, Resilience, Teamwork, and Skills), an intensive four-week program for wounded or ill military personnel. The authors discuss how poetry can help individuals process trauma through its unique ability to express experience and perception. They emphasize the importance of enjambment and spatial techniques in contemporary poetry, which facilitate emotional engagement and open interpretation. Through case studies involving William Carlos Williams’ poem 'This is just to say' and other works, they illustrate how reading and discussing poems can help participants re-author their experiences, aiding recovery and resilience building.
