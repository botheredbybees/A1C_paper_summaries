## How does the Brain Perceive Music?

This lesson explores how the brain perceives music through sensation and perception. Sensation involves receiving information via sensory systems, while perception combines this to form a percept understood by the brain. Key processes include feature extraction (combining individual frequencies into pitch) and categorical perception (grouping sounds). The lesson also covers bottom-up and top-down processing in understanding musical structures like pitch, rhythm, timbre, tempo, meter, contour, loudness, and spatial location. It emphasizes the role of cultural background in tonality perception.
