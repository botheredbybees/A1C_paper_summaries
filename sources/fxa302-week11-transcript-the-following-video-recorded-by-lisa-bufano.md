## Lisa Bufano's Performance Art with Prosthetics and Adapted Stilts

The transcript captures an interview with Lisa Bufano, a performance artist and dancer who is also an amputee. She discusses her work involving various prosthetic devices and adapted stilts for her performances. Bufano explores how her use of prosthetics impacts her sense of body and identity, leading to innovative performances that include wooden stilts, underwater dance, and suspended aerial movements. She aims to create prosthetics that allow her to interact with the environment in unique ways, emphasizing the transformative potential of disability arts.
