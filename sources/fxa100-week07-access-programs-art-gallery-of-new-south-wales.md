## Access Programs - Art Gallery of New South Wales

The Art Gallery of New South Wales offers a range of access programs designed to engage diverse audiences, including those with disabilities and special needs. Programs such as tactile and sensory tours cater specifically to individuals who are blind or have low vision, providing guided experiences that involve touching selected sculptures and using tactile materials and props. For visitors with intellectual disabilities, the gallery provides tailored tours free of charge if booked in advance. Additionally, an Art and Dementia program offers meaningful engagement for people living with dementia and their carers through interactive sessions with artworks from the gallery’s collection. These initiatives not only promote accessibility but also enhance inclusivity by fostering creative ageing, lifelong learning, and reducing stigma around conditions like dementia.
