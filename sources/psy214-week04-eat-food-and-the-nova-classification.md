## “Eat food” and the NOVA classification

The document titled '‘Eat food’ and the NOVA classification' presents classifications for different types of food based on their level of processing. It distinguishes between unprocessed/minimally processed foods (Nova 1) that include fresh produce, whole grains, and dairy products in their natural or nearly natural state; processed culinary ingredients (Nova 2), which are extracted from Nova 1 items but not typically consumed alone; moderately processed foods (Nova 3), which combine Nova 1 with added ingredients like sugar or salt; and ultra-processed foods (Nova 4) characterized by high levels of industrial processing, additives for palatability, and extended shelf life. Examples of ultra-processed foods include carbonated drinks, packaged snacks, convenience meals, and protein bars. The document underscores the importance of understanding food classifications to promote healthier dietary choices that align with self-care practices in managing stress and enhancing overall well-being.
