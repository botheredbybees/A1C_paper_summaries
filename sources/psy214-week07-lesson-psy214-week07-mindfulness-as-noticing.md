## Langerian Mindfulness: Noticing and Engagement

This lesson introduces Langerian mindfulness, a concept developed by Ellen Langer, which differs from traditional mindfulness. Langerian mindfulness is defined as a cognitive mode characterized by noticing and engaging curiously with the present, challenging assumptions, and promoting creativity and innovation. It contrasts with traditional mindfulness, which emphasizes observing experiences with non-judgment and acceptance. The lesson discusses Langer's Langer Mindfulness Scale (LMS) and highlights the methodological concerns with some of Langer's research, particularly the nursing home plant care study and the 'counter-clockwise' ageing study. These studies have been criticized for lack of randomization, small sample sizes, and potential bias.
