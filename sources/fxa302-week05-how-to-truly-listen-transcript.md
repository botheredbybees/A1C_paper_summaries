## How to truly listen — Transcript

The transcript discusses the importance of listening in music and life, emphasizing that true listening goes beyond just hearing. The speaker illustrates how musicians interpret rather than merely translate musical scores, engaging fully with their instruments to create a deeper connection and richer interpretation. He recounts his early experiences as a musician facing skepticism about his capabilities due to his deafness, which led to changes in music institution policies regarding access for differently-abled individuals. The lecture highlights the therapeutic value of sound, particularly for those who may struggle with movement or sensory impairments, demonstrating how music can provide unique and meaningful experiences tailored to individual listeners.
