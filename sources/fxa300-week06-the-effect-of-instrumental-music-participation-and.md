## The Effect of Instrumental Music Participation and Socioeconomic Status on Ohio Fourth-, Sixth-, and Ninth-Grade Proficiency Test Performance

This study examines the relationship between instrumental music participation and academic achievement, specifically focusing on standardized test scores of students from Columbus Public Schools in Ohio. It aims to clarify whether involvement in instrumental music programs positively impacts student performance across various subjects including citizenship, math, science, and reading. The research also considers how socioeconomic status (SES) interacts with these outcomes. By comparing fourth-, sixth-, and ninth-grade proficiency test results between instrumental and non-instrumental students at different SES levels, the study finds that instrumental music participants outperformed their peers in every category tested. Importantly, it highlights a pattern where lower SES instrumental music students eventually surpass higher SES non-music students by the ninth grade, suggesting long-term benefits from early participation in music education programs.
