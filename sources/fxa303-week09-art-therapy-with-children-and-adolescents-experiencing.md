## Art therapy with children and adolescents experiencing acute or severe mental health conditions: A systematic review

This systematic review evaluates the effectiveness and acceptability of art therapy for children and adolescents with acute or severe mental health conditions. The authors conducted a comprehensive search in five electronic databases, identifying 90 studies that met their criteria, including both original research papers and case studies. They found high levels of acceptability among participants and positive outcomes across various mental health issues, particularly post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD). Art therapy was shown to reduce symptoms of PTSD, depression, suicidal ideation, anxiety, improve emotion regulation, self-awareness, distress tolerance, communication skills, and overall quality of life. The review highlights the adaptability of art therapy to different settings and conditions, advocating for increased access to this form of therapy for young people experiencing acute mental health crises.
