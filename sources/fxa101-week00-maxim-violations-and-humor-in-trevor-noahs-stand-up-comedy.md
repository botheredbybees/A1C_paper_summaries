## Maxim Violations and Humor in Trevor Noah's Stand-Up Comedy: A Pragmatic Analysis

This study examines conversational maxim violations in Trevor Noah's stand-up comedy performance 'How to Not Order at an Indian Restaurant.' Using Grice’s Cooperative Principle as a framework, the analysis identifies and categorizes utterances that deviate from norms of quantity, quality, relevance, and manner. The results show that all four types are violated, with relevance being the most frequent. These violations contribute humorously by exposing cultural stereotypes and enhancing audience engagement through deliberate linguistic deviation.
