## Training, Education, and Professional Issues in Music Therapy

This chapter by Jane Edwards explores the multifaceted process of training and professional practice in music therapy. It highlights the importance of experiential learning and the development of practical skills alongside theoretical understanding. The author discusses the challenges and benefits of postgraduate training and the creation of new music therapy positions within institutions. Key themes include the impact of group dynamics on student learning, the necessity of personal development through individual and group therapy, and the unique role of music therapists in pioneering new therapeutic opportunities. The text underscores the significance of empathy and active listening in therapeutic practice and emphasizes the importance of professional regulation and accreditation. This chapter is crucial for understanding the professional development of music therapists and the evolving nature of music therapy as a recognized and respected field.
