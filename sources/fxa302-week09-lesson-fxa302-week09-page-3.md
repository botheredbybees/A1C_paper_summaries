## Musical Perspectives on Pain and Mortality

This lesson explores the therapeutic use of music in pain management and its role in addressing mortality. It highlights the endorphin release triggered by music, which aids in pain reduction and enhances focus. Case studies of John Tavener and David Bowie illustrate how music can serve as a therapeutic tool, reflecting on mortality and life's impermanence. Tavener's compositions, influenced by Orthodox Christianity and Eastern spirituality, delve into themes of death and suffering. Bowie's final album, 'Blackstar,' released shortly before his death from liver cancer, is examined for its symbolic references to mortality. The lesson emphasizes the clinical application of music therapy and the creative process as a form of self-therapy for those living with chronic pain or life-limiting illness.
