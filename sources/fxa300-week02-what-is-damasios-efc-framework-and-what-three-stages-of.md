## What is Damasio's E–F–C framework, and what three stages of emotional experience does it describe?

Antonio Damasio's E–F–C framework—Emotion–Feeling–Consciousness—is a hierarchical model that explains how biological survival mechanisms evolve into complex mental states and self-awareness. The three stages of emotional experience are described as: Emotion (physical/bodily reactions), Feeling (private mental perception of these bodily changes), and Consciousness (awareness that the feeling belongs to oneself). This framework emphasizes the progression from unconscious, physical responses in the body to conscious recognition of feelings, highlighting the role of biological signals in informing mental experiences. Key experiments like the Iowa Gambling Task demonstrate how biological reactions precede mental awareness, supporting Damasio's bottom-up approach to understanding emotions and consciousness. The E–F–C framework diverges from traditional theories by emphasizing the distinct nature of emotion and feeling, the hierarchical structure of basic versus social emotions, and the priority of bodily signals over cognitive appraisal in generating emotional responses.
