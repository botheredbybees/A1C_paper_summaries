## Do Character Strengths-Based Interventions Change Character Strengths? Two Randomized Controlled Intervention Studies

This paper explores the effects of character strengths-based interventions on both well-being and character strengths themselves. The study consists of two parts: Study 1 examines seven different variants of character strengths-based interventions, including learning about the VIA classification of character strengths and using signature strengths in a new way or with a challenge. It found that using signature strengths in a new way and forming a strengths-based habit increased well-being and character strengths states and traits. Study 2, a four-week multi-component program, showed increases in well-being and character strengths states, and reductions in stress and neuroticism. The research concludes that character strengths interventions not only improve well-being but also modify character strengths states and traits, potentially influencing broader personality traits like neuroticism.
