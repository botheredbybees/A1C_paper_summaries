## Week 11 Discussion: Group Singing and Emotional Wellbeing

The lesson focuses on the benefits of group singing for emotional wellbeing, using the study by Densley and Andrews (2021) as a basis. Students are asked to read and analyze the article and discuss the benefits of group singing, personal experiences, differences between group and solo singing, and potential negative aspects and mitigation strategies. The lesson includes various references to support the discussion on the impact of singing on physical and mental health.
