## Bricolage as capability for frugal innovation in emerging markets in times of crisis

This research explores whether bricolage can serve as a capability for companies in emerging markets to develop frugal innovations during times of crisis, specifically focusing on resource scarcity and innovation under constraints. The authors conducted a study using structural equation modeling with data from 215 Brazilian companies to test their hypotheses. They found that bricolage has a positive impact on the development of frugal innovation in these contexts. This capability is particularly crucial for organizations operating in emerging markets affected by crises, as it allows them to innovate effectively despite resource limitations. The study contributes to literature on frugal innovation and bricolage, operationalizing the concept of frugal innovation through a measurement scale. Practical implications suggest that managers should adopt bricolage practices such as recombining resources for new purposes, seeking underutilized external resources, and exploiting low-cost opportunities to foster innovative solutions in resource-constrained environments.
