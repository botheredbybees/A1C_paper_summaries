## Tony Iommi Discusses His Musical Journey and the Origins of Heavy Metal

Tony Iommi, known as the 'Dark Lord of the Riff' and key figure in heavy metal, discusses his upbringing in Birmingham and early musical influences. Iommi's career was significantly shaped by his factory job where he lost parts of his fingertips, leading him to innovate and develop his unique playing style. This accident inspired him to create custom guitar tips and to use lighter strings, setting a precedent for heavy metal guitarists. His early bands played covers and Iommi experimented with tuning down his guitar strings to create a heavier sound, influencing future generations of musicians. The discussion also touches on his work ethic and dedication to refining his craft.
