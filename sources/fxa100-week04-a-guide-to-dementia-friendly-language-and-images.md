## A Guide to Dementia-Friendly Language and Images

This document provides guidelines for using inclusive and non-stigmatizing language when discussing or writing about individuals with dementia, emphasizing the importance of respectful communication that supports dignity and self-esteem. The guide includes examples of preferred terms to use and avoid in various contexts, such as describing the condition, symptoms, impact on society, and more. Key recommendations include prioritizing the person over the disease, being sensitive to cultural differences, using clear and meaningful language, and avoiding derogatory expressions or ageist stereotypes. The document also highlights the significance of culturally appropriate communication and engagement with indigenous leaders and healers for better understanding and care in diverse communities.
