## Transcript of MJHS Dementia Care - Music Therapy Program

The transcript discusses the implementation and impact of a music therapy program at Shorefront, funded by the New York State Department of Health. The program aimed to reduce agitation, depression, and anxiety in mid-stage dementia residents through various music-assisted caregiving protocols. Music is highlighted for its ability to engage individuals across all stages of dementia, even when verbal communication becomes challenging. Key findings include significant reductions in resident depression and agitation levels, increased socialization among residents, improved productivity and attitudes among caregivers, and financial benefits due to lower implementation costs. The program also enhances family interactions by making visits more positive experiences for relatives. Overall, music therapy not only improves the quality of life for dementia patients but also provides substantial support to caregiving staff.
