## Executive Summary and Recommendations: Why Creative Health?

The document 'Executive Summary and Recommendations: Why Creative Health?' emphasizes the fundamental role of creativity in health and wellbeing across various stages of life. It highlights a growing body of evidence indicating that engaging with creative activities such as music, crafts, dance, and gardening can improve mental health, support physical recovery, foster social connections, reduce loneliness, and contribute to holistic care for those suffering from chronic conditions like dementia and COPD. The report advocates for the integration of creativity into healthcare systems as a vital resource to enhance patient outcomes and quality of life. It also underscores the need for collaborative efforts between policymakers, funders, local authorities, schools, cultural sectors, and voluntary, community, and social enterprise (VCSE) organizations to ensure equitable access to creative health opportunities. The document presents successful case studies from Wales and England where partnerships have led to demonstrable improvements in public health outcomes at relatively low costs. It calls for a whole-system approach that mobilizes existing assets and addresses local priorities through the development of person-centered, community-led initiatives informed by lived experiences. This vision aims to reduce healthcare pressures, prevent illness, support long-term conditions management, and improve economic prosperity.
