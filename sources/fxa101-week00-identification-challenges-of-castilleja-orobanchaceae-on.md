## Identification challenges of Castilleja (Orobanchaceae) on iNaturalist

This study investigates the identification challenges of Castilleja species on iNaturalist, a citizen science platform used for biodiversity monitoring. The researchers analyzed 105,218 observations and found that initial identifications made with computer vision algorithms were more accurate (53.3%) than those without (48.5%), but still required substantial time to achieve Research-Grade status. Castilleja densiflora and exserta are the most frequently confused species, while C. rubicundula and campestris commonly misidentified as Triphysaria. The findings underscore the need for improved algorithms and expert verification in taxonomically challenging groups.
