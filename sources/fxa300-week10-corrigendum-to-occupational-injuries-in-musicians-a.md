## Corrigendum to: Occupational Injuries in Musicians: A Literature Review

This document is a corrigendum to a previously published literature review on occupational injuries in musicians. The authors, LT Trevor Elam, MC, USN, Capt Steven Mowen, MC, USAF, and Lt Col Christopher Jonas, MC, USAF, correct an error in the author list of the original publication. The correction is crucial for the proper attribution of the review and its findings regarding the health risks and injuries that musicians face in their professional capacity. This corrigendum highlights the importance of accurate authorship in academic and medical literature, particularly in the context of military research and medical practice. The revised author list ensures that the contributions of each author are correctly acknowledged, which is essential for the credibility and integrity of the research in the field of music and health.
