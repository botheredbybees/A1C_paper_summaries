## Musical development and education

This chapter explores the developmental social psychology of music and music education, focusing on how social and cultural contexts influence musical development from infancy to adulthood. The authors discuss the shift from individualistic to social perspectives in developmental psychology, emphasizing the role of interpersonal interactions and cultural factors. They review key theoretical models of musical development and their limitations from a social psychological viewpoint, and examine formal and informal learning processes in music education. The chapter also highlights international variations in music educational practices and the role of teachers and learners in shaping the music learning agenda. By integrating insights from social psychology, the chapter underscores the importance of understanding musical development within broader social and cultural frameworks.
