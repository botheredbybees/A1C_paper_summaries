## Evaluation of the ‘Unforgettable’ Art Programme by People with Dementia and Their Care-Givers

The study evaluates the 'Unforgettable' art program for people with dementia and their caregivers in Dutch museums. Participants rated the program very positively, highlighting social interaction as a key factor in its appreciation. The mood of both participants with dementia (N=95) and caregivers (N=104) improved after participating in the program. This evaluation underscores the potential value of art programs for enhancing the quality of life and emotional well-being of individuals with dementia and their care-givers, contributing to a broader understanding of how such programs can be optimized.
