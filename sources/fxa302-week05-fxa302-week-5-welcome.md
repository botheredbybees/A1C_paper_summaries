## FXA302 Week 5 Welcome

This document introduces the content of Week 5 in the FXA302 course, focusing on how hearing loss affects musical creativity and performance. The central argument is that despite significant hearing impairment, individuals can still engage deeply with music through alternative methods such as inner hearing and creative strategies. Key case studies include Ludwig van Beethoven and Bedřich Smetana, who both experienced severe hearing issues but continued to produce impactful work influenced by their auditory experiences. Additionally, the document highlights perspectives from performers who were born deaf or lost their hearing early in life but successfully pursued careers in music through innovative techniques. The text emphasizes the importance of inclusive approaches in music education that accommodate diverse abilities and challenges common assumptions about the necessity of normal hearing for musical appreciation.
