## Revive: Australia’s Cultural Policy for the Next Five Years

The document 'Revive: Australia's Cultural Policy for the Next Five Years' outlines a comprehensive strategy to support and develop Australia's arts sector over the coming five-year period. The policy, authored by the Commonwealth of Australia, is structured around five interconnected pillars aimed at fostering cultural growth and inclusivity. These pillars include prioritizing First Nations stories and cultures, ensuring diverse narratives are shared, affirming the role of artists in society, strengthening cultural infrastructure, and enhancing engagement with audiences both domestically and internationally.

The Prime Minister emphasizes the importance of arts in uniting Australians and projecting national identity globally. The Minister for the Arts elaborates on the policy's foundational elements, such as the recognition of First Nations artistic traditions and the integration of diverse cultural contributions from various parts of the world. Additionally, the document addresses the need for robust infrastructure to support artists and their works.

Highlighting the challenges faced by the arts sector during the recent pandemic period, Revive seeks to provide a renewed framework that acknowledges the essential role of artists as both creators and workers while expanding beyond traditional government-owned cultural institutions to include commercial sectors. This holistic approach aims to enhance economic opportunities for creative professionals and deepen Australia's cultural identity.
