## Mindfulness: Overview

This lesson introduces the concept of mindfulness, emphasizing its secular nature and scientific grounding. Mindfulness is described as a multifaceted construct studied through various research methods and applied in different ways by practitioners and individuals. The lesson includes a video overview of mindfulness definitions, measurement, and practices, focusing on the Five Facet Mindfulness Questionnaire (FFMQ). It also provides opportunities to engage with mindfulness practices such as the 'raisin exercise' and the 'mindful 5-4-3-2-1' activity, which aim to enhance present-moment awareness and sensory experience.
