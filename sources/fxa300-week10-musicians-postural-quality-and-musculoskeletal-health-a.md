## Musicians, postural quality and musculoskeletal health: A literature’s review

This literature review analyzes research papers published between 1989 and 2015, focusing on the relationship between postural quality during musical performance and various health and performance factors, particularly musculoskeletal health. The study explores the impact of poor posture on musicians' health and performance, highlighting the significant prevalence of musculoskeletal disorders among musicians, which can severely affect their quality of life and performance. The review identifies poor posture as a key risk factor for these disorders. Despite the high interest in the field, the review reveals that empirical research is limited due to disparities in measuring instruments and operationalization of variables, making rigorous comparative analysis difficult. The authors emphasize the importance of collaboration between medical professionals and music educators to develop postural training programs that enhance performance quality and reduce health risks.
