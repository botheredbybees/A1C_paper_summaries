## Arts in Nature with Children and Young People

The book 'Arts in Nature with Children and Young People', edited by Zoe Moula and Nicola Walshe, explores the intersection between arts and nature and their combined impact on children's and young people's wellbeing. It highlights how creative experiences in natural settings contribute to health equality, personal growth, social justice, cultural awareness, and environmental sustainability. The collection includes both research-based chapters that provide empirical evidence for these benefits and practice-oriented chapters that offer insights into effective methodologies and strategies from professionals working with youth. By presenting a diverse range of 'arts-in-nature' practices—such as visual arts, music, movement, drama, and poetry—the book emphasizes the transformative power of creative engagement in natural environments across various settings like forests, beaches, schools, and community areas. The relevance to the field of creative arts and health is underscored by its potential to inspire both professionals and families to foster healthier relationships with nature through art, thereby enhancing overall well-being.
