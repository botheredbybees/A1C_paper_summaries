## FXA101 Week 11 Welcome

This document provides an introduction to the final week of the FXA101 course, focusing on the importance of reflection in creative arts and health. The instructor emphasizes the significance of critical reflection in various fields, including education, nursing, and business, and introduces models and principles for writing a critical reflection. The document also briefly explores the use of reflection as a process and symbol in creative arts, providing historical and contemporary works as prompts for future creative expression. Students are encouraged to complete their creative project and start their written reflection, with support and assistance available from the teaching team.
