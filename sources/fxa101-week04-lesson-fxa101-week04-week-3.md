## Brain Changes in Ageing

This lesson explores brain changes associated with ageing through a presentation by Professor Alison Canty from the Wicking Dementia Research and Education Centre. It covers brain anatomy including neurons, axons, dendrites, synapses, grey matter, white matter, ventricles, and cerebrospinal fluid. MRI studies reveal that aged brains show decreased volume in both grey and white matter with corresponding increases in ventricle size. Cognitive functions decline due to reduced cortical volume, particularly affecting the frontal lobe and parietal lobe. Education appears to provide cognitive reserve allowing individuals to maintain higher functional abilities despite brain degeneration. Late-life cognitive activities reduce Alzheimer's risk; however, 'brain training' apps vary in effectiveness. Programs combining social interaction, physical exercise, and cognitive stimulation yield greater benefits than single-factor programs.
