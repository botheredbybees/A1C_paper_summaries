## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: How do I find scholarly sources?

The lesson covers strategies for finding scholarly sources relevant to the unit's content. It emphasizes the importance of starting with the Reading List provided in the course and engaging with the Library Essentials Online module. The lesson also provides guidance on using Google Scholar effectively by linking it to the UTAS library system to access full-text articles directly. Additionally, it introduces two research strategies: the ancestry approach, which involves tracing back references in an article's bibliography, and the descendancy approach, which uses citation tracking from Google Scholar or database searches to find subsequent studies.
