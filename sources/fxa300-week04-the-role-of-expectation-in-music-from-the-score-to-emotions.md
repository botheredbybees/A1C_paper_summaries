## The role of expectation in music: from the score to emotions and the brain

This article examines the central role of expectations in music, exploring how these expectations influence both perception and emotional experience. The authors discuss how musical structures can induce tension and relaxation through specific patterns that listeners anticipate based on context and style. Expectations are not limited to sensory experiences but also involve cognitive processing related to syntax and stylistic elements. The hierarchical organization of tension and relaxation is explained using the concept of 'prolongation,' which is visualized as a tree structure in musical pieces. This framework helps composers manipulate listener expectations, contributing to the expressive power of music. Additionally, the article highlights parallels between language processing and music cognition, emphasizing the importance of studying musical expectations for understanding broader brain functions.
