## Performance Anxiety in Musicians

This lesson explores performance anxiety in the context of music, defined as apprehension about negative evaluation and task failure. It discusses physical and psychological symptoms experienced by musicians and highlights the impact on various age groups and skill levels. The lesson features insights from Professor Dianna Theadora Kenny and examines strategies to manage performance anxiety, such as deep breathing, mock performances, and the use of medications like beta blockers. Famous musicians who have struggled with performance anxiety, including Frédéric Chopin and Barbra Streisand, are cited. The lesson also reviews recent literature on music performance anxiety (MPA) and its subtypes, emphasizing the importance of understanding individual histories and psychosocial conditions.
