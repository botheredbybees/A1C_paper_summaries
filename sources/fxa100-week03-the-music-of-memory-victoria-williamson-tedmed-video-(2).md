## The Music of Memory: Victoria Williamson TEDMED – Video transcript

In her TEDMED presentation titled 'The Music of Memory,' Victoria Williamson explores the power and intricacies of musical memory. She presents three stories to illustrate different facets of musical memory: the star (Arturo Toscanini), the survivor (Clive Wearing), and the miscreant (involuntary earworms). Toscanini's story highlights how expert musicians use structural knowledge to remember vast amounts of music, while Wearing’s case demonstrates that even after severe brain damage, musical memories can persist. Williamson argues that music activates deep emotional centers in the brain, making it resilient against memory loss. This is particularly relevant for individuals with dementia who often retain their ability to engage with music long after other forms of memory have faded. The presentation also touches on how people use existing structures and knowledge to enhance memory retention through music, such as memorizing PIN numbers using melodies. Overall, Williamson's talk underscores the profound impact of musical memory on cognitive health and personal well-being.
