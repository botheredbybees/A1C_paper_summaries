## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness

This lesson explores the impact of nature engagement on mental health and well-being. It discusses various studies that suggest different durations of time spent in nature for mental benefits (e.g., 120 minutes a week, 30-minute weekly visits). The importance of urban green spaces is highlighted alongside potential cultural differences in nature interventions. The negative effects of integrating technology into nature breaks are examined through a study showing reduced restorative effects when participants engage with laptops during their breaks. The concept of 'forest bathing' or Shinrin-yoku from Japan is introduced, focusing on its positive health outcomes like improved immune function and decreased stress hormones. Additionally, the lesson includes the Connectedness to Nature Scale for self-assessment and discusses how climate change impacts mental health while advocating for individual actions towards sustainability.
