## Health Effects of Dietary Risks In 195 Countries, 1990-2017: a Systematic Analysis for the Global Burden of Disease Study 2017

A recent study published in The Lancet and summarized by NewsRx explores the significant impact of suboptimal diet on non-communicable diseases (NCDs) across 195 countries from 1990 to 2017. Researchers at the University of Washington analyzed dietary data, quantifying how various foods and nutrients contribute to NCD mortality and morbidity. The study found that high sodium intake, low consumption of whole grains, and insufficient fruit consumption were leading risk factors for diet-related deaths and disability-adjusted life-years (DALYs). These findings underscore the need for improved dietary interventions globally to reduce the burden of chronic diseases.
