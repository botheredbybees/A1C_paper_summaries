## FXA101 Creativity and Ageing - Week 5 Activities

This week's lesson in FXA101 focuses on the psychological phenomenon of pareidolia and its creative manifestations. The reading discusses how humans see faces or patterns where there are none due to evolutionary advantages. Klecksography, a form of ink blot art popularized by Julius Kerner, is highlighted alongside Hermann Rorschach's adaptation for psychological assessment. Creative exercises involve drawing with closed eyes and using non-dominant hands to explore creativity under constraints. Additional readings on creativity and constraints from various sources are suggested.
