## Music and Health

The chapter 'Music and Health' discusses the roles of music therapy, performing arts medicine, and music medicine in promoting health and wellness. Music therapy uses music within a therapeutic relationship to address physical, emotional, cognitive, and social needs, with evidence supporting its effectiveness in rehabilitation and motivation for treatment engagement. Performing arts medicine focuses on healthcare for musicians, dancers, actors, dealing primarily with surgical and pain patients, while music medicine involves physicians using music for patient care. The chapter highlights the evolution of these fields from ancient practices to modern therapeutic applications, emphasizing their potential to enhance well-being through diverse client populations and interdisciplinary approaches.
