## Ballet Moves for Adult Creative Health

The report titled 'Ballet Moves for Adult Creative Health' presents research findings on the motivations and wellbeing outcomes of ballet participation among active older adults. Initiated by Queensland Ballet (QB) in partnership with Queensland University of Technology, the study examines QB's Seniors in Studio program, particularly the Ballet for Seniors class. The research methods include a questionnaire completed before and after teaching modifications, focus groups pre- and post-modifications, and classroom observations over three months. Key findings indicate that social interaction, love for ballet, and accessibility are significant motivators for participation. Participants reported improved physical wellbeing, including increased energy levels, body control, flexibility, and overall wellness from engaging in ballet classes.
