## Poetry and well-being: A Pilot Programme to Evaluate the Impact of Creative Writing for Patients in Short-term and Long-term Rehabilitation

This research paper presents a pilot programme that assesses the impact of creative writing, specifically poetry, on patients' well-being in short-term and long-term rehabilitation at the University of Canberra Hospital (UCH). The project aimed to create a safe space for open discussion, offer participants basic skills for expressive and creative writing, and evaluate if writing poetry can reframe personal narratives beneficially. Delivered through eight one-hour face-to-face workshops over two blocks from February to May 2023, the programme engaged 14 participants with varying conditions such as stroke recovery, serious accidents, amputations, and Parkinson's disease. Evaluative methods included a survey yielding overwhelmingly positive responses, indicating that creative writing facilitates meaningful self-reflection and well-being.
