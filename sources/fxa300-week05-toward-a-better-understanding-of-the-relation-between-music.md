## Toward a better understanding of the relation between music preference, listening behavior, and personality

The paper explores the relationship between personality traits and music preferences, focusing on the limitations of genre labels in measuring these preferences accurately. The study included a sample size of 395 participants who reported their personality types using the Big Five trait model and provided information about their music preferences through self-reporting and listening behavior recorded over three months via a music database. Results indicated correlations between personality traits like Neuroticism and Classical music preference, as well as Openness to Experience with Jazz music preference. The study highlights issues with genre labels in measuring music preferences and suggests that direct measurement of actual listening behavior provides a more accurate reflection of individual musical tastes.
