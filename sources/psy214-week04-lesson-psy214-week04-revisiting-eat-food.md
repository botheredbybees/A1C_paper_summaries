## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: Think critically, eat joyfully

The lesson discusses the challenges of translating nutritional knowledge into healthier eating habits in Western societies. It highlights that rule-based diets focusing on calorie counting or specific macronutrient ratios are often unsustainable and can lead to stress and guilt around food consumption. The text emphasizes the importance of a varied diet rich in minimally processed plant-based foods, encouraging an approach centered on abundance rather than restriction. Critical thinking is promoted through examination of studies that demonstrate no meaningful difference between various macronutrient profiles for most people, suggesting adherence to dietary plans as more crucial than specific nutrient ratios. Additionally, the lesson addresses misconceptions about water intake and hydration, debunking myths such as the necessity of drinking eight glasses of water daily or the dehydrating effects of caffeine. The overall message encourages joyful eating by focusing on the pleasure derived from food rather than rigid rules.
