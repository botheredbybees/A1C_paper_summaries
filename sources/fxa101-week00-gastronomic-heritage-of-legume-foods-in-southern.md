## Gastronomic heritage of legume foods in Southern Mediterranean cuisine

This review article explores the significance of traditional legume-based foods in Southern Mediterranean cuisine, focusing on their cultural importance, nutritional value, and culinary diversity. The study conducted a comprehensive review by accessing scholarly databases such as PubMed, Scopus, and Web of Science to identify relevant publications from January 2000 to May 2024. It highlights the prominence of legumes like chickpeas, lentils, and fava beans in Middle Eastern and North African cuisines due to their rich nutritional profile and affordability. The review catalogs 34 traditional dishes from Southern Mediterranean countries, demonstrating the region's culinary heritage. Additionally, it discusses the commercialization trends driven by globalization and the need for future research on the sustainability and consumer perceptions of these traditional legume-based foods.
