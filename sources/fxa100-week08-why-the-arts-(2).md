## Why the Arts?

The chapter explores the concept of creativity, particularly its relevance to individuals with dementia. The authors define creativity as an umbrella term that can encompass various human activities when approached with an open-minded and experimental attitude. They emphasize several key characteristics of creative activities: pursuing the activity for its own sake, involving a making process, giving pleasure to participants and observers, being expressive, fostering concentration and focus, and providing a sense of flow. Creativity is also distinguished from 'big C' creativity (reserved for exceptional talent) to 'small c' creativity, which focuses on enhancing wellbeing through personal development and achievement. The text highlights the importance of addressing barriers to participation in creative activities due to misconceptions or negative early experiences with art and emphasizes that everyone possesses an innate sense of creativity, especially crucial for those with dementia who may face chronic underachievement.
