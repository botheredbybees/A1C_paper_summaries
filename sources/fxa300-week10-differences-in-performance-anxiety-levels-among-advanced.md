## Differences in performance anxiety levels among advanced conservatory students in Spain, according to type of instrument and academic year of enrolment

This study examines differences in musical performance anxiety (MPA) among advanced conservatory students in Spain, focusing on instrument type (solo or orchestra) and academic year of enrollment. The research sample consisted of 476 students who completed the Kenny Music Performance Anxiety Inventory (K-MPAI). The findings indicate that soloists experience higher levels of MPA compared to orchestra musicians, particularly as they progress through their academic years. The study suggests that variations in training conditions and career prospects contribute to these differences. The results have implications for understanding MPA's impact on music students' professional development and highlight the need for tailored interventions to support students' mental health and performance skills.
