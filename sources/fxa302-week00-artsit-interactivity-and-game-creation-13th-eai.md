## ArtsIT, Interactivity and Game Creation 13th EAI International Conference, ArtsIT 2024 Proceedings, Part II

This proceedings document records the 13th edition of the European Alliance for Innovation (EAI) International Conference on ArtsIT, Interactivity & Game Creation (ArtsIT 2024), held in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. The conference featured a wide array of sessions and discussions, focusing on the intersection of art, technology, and innovation. It included 42 papers, 6 keynote speeches, 2 workshops, 2 performances, and 1 panel discussion. Key topics ranged from AI, robotics, and music to XR and gaming. Notable contributions were made by speakers and participants from various prestigious institutions and organizations, such as Google, the Australian Network for Art and Technology, and the Dubai Future Foundation. The conference emphasized the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration in fostering new systems of thought and being, with a special focus on accessibility and experimental practice.
