## Assessment of Real-Life Creativity: The Inventory of Creative Activities and Achievements (ICAA)

This article introduces the Inventory of Creative Activities and Achievements (ICAA), a comprehensive tool for assessing real-life creativity at both Little-C and Pro-C levels across eight creative domains. The study presents evidence from seven Little-C samples and two Pro-C samples, totaling 1,566 participants, demonstrating the reliability and validity of ICAA test scores. Key findings include the distinctiveness between frequency of engagement in everyday creative activities (Little-C) versus public acknowledgment of achievements (Pro-C). The paper also explores how individual differences such as personality traits, cognitive abilities, and environmental factors contribute to creativity at different levels. By distinguishing Little-C from Pro-C creativity, this tool offers a versatile approach for researchers studying creativity across various domains.
