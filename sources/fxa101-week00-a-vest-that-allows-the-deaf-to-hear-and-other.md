## A Vest That Allows the Deaf to 'Hear' and Other Neuroscientific Marvels

This article discusses advancements in neuroscientific research and technology, particularly focusing on synesthesia and sensory substitution for the deaf. Dr. David Eagleman explains that his work is contributing to big data in the field of synesthesia and exploring genetics associated with it. The piece delves into various forms of synesthesia such as spatial sequence synesthesia and sound-color associations. Additionally, Dr. Eagleman describes a vest developed by him and Scott Novak, which translates sounds into vibrations for the deaf, offering an alternative to cochlear implants. This technology is being commercialized through NeoSensory, with plans for broader availability.
