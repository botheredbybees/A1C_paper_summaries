## The Later Works of William Utermohlen

The catalog for the exhibition 'The Later Works of William Utermohlen' held at the New York Academy of Medicine in 2006 documents the artist's progression through his diagnosis and decline due to Alzheimer's disease. William Utermohlen, an accomplished painter, created a series of self-portraits that reveal the emotional and psychological journey of living with dementia. The exhibition, organized by the Alzheimer’s Association, New York City Chapter, provides a unique visual narrative of Utermohlen's subjective experience, offering insights into the challenges faced by individuals with Alzheimer's disease. The work highlights the artist's struggle to maintain his identity and sense of self through his paintings, despite the progressive loss of cognitive abilities. This exhibition contributes to public awareness and understanding of Alzheimer's disease, emphasizing the importance of continued research and support for those affected by the disease.
