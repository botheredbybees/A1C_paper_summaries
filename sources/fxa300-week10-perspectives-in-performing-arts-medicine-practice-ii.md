## Perspectives in Performing Arts Medicine Practice II

This book compiles contemporary perspectives and research in the field of performing arts medicine, focusing on occupational health, public health, and the therapeutic applications of the arts. The editors and contributors explore the health and well-being of performing artists, emphasizing both physical and mental health issues. It highlights the interdisciplinary nature of the field, integrating medical, scientific, and artistic approaches to address the unique challenges faced by musicians, dancers, and other performers. The volume underscores the importance of preventive measures and mental health support, reflecting a shift from traditional sports medicine models to more holistic and culturally inclusive practices. The book is a valuable resource for healthcare providers, researchers, and educators working with performing artists, offering insights into recent advancements and practical applications in the field.
