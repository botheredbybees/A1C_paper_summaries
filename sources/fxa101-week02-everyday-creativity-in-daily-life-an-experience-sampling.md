## Everyday Creativity in Daily Life: An Experience-Sampling Study of “Little c” Creativity

The study, 'Everyday Creativity in Daily Life: An Experience-Sampling Study of “Little c” Creativity,' explores the frequency and context of everyday creative activities among young adults. The research builds on Richards' theory that such creativity is both a cause and effect of positive psychological processes. Through a week-long experience-sampling study, participants were randomly surveyed about their daily activities and emotional states to assess moments of creativity. Key findings revealed that individuals who reported feeling happy and active were more likely to engage in creative pursuits. Personality traits like openness to experience and conscientiousness also significantly influenced the likelihood of engaging in creative activities, while negative emotions did not predict such engagement. This study highlights the importance of everyday creativity in psychological well-being and underscores the utility of experience-sampling methods for understanding creative behavior in natural settings.
