## Disability Arts Now

The article 'Disability Arts Now' by Catherine Watlington discusses the growing influence of disabled artists in the art world and their contributions to the disability justice movement. The author highlights how disabled artists, through their diverse works across various mediums, challenge ableist norms and advocate for accessibility. Key examples include Christine Sun Kim, who creates works that bridge Deaf and hearing audiences, and Park McArthur, who exhibits makeshift ramps to bring attention to the lack of accessibility in galleries and museums. The article underscores the importance of first-person narratives and diverse perspectives in shaping a more inclusive cultural landscape. By engaging with these narratives, the creative arts can foster greater understanding and empathy towards disability, thereby contributing to broader social justice goals.
