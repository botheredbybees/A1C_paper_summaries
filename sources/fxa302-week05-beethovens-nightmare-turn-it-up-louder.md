## Beethoven’s Nightmare – Turn it Up Louder

The document is a transcript of a musical performance by a band composed entirely of deaf musicians. The lyrics emphasize the need to turn up the volume louder for clarity and enjoyment, highlighting the unique experience of performing music without hearing it traditionally. This piece explores themes of overcoming adversity and creativity in the face of physical limitations, which are relevant to discussions on the intersection of disability and artistic expression within health and wellbeing contexts.
