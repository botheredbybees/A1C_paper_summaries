## Eight Signs You May Have Performance Anxiety

This transcript discusses eight signs of performance anxiety, also known as stage fright, which can affect individuals in various professions and personal situations. Symptoms range from physical sensations like cold, clammy hands and dry mouth to psychological effects such as mind blanks and speech impediments. The transcript highlights the physiological mechanisms behind these symptoms, including the body's stress response and its impact on the nervous system. It also mentions treatments and coping strategies for individuals dealing with performance anxiety, such as the use of note cards and the Memory Palace technique.
