## The EyeWriter Project: A Collaborative Effort in Interactive Art

The article by Andy Cameron discusses the EyeWriter project, a remarkable interactive art piece that combines technology and art to empower a paralyzed graffiti artist, Tempt 1, to continue his work. The EyeWriter is a device that allows users to draw with their eyes, using off-the-shelf, low-cost, and open-source hardware and software. The project highlights the collaborative nature of the digital art community, as a group of young media artists and programmers developed the EyeWriter to help Tempt 1 maintain his artistic career despite his physical limitations. The article praises the EyeWriter for its ability to enhance human ability and accessibility and for its inspirational impact on the broader community of disabled individuals.
