## Burn Survivor Artist Changes Artistic Approach by Sharing How Other Survivors of Traumatic Events Can Use Art to Heal Themselves by Helping Others Heal

Joel Thomas Hall, a burn survivor and artist, discusses how his traumatic experience led to a significant shift in his artistic approach. Initially, he focused on creating simple paintings from photos, but after his severe burns, his work became more meaningful and emotionally driven. Hall emphasizes the healing potential of art when used to help others, particularly burn survivors. His new approach involves creating art that not only brings him peace but also serves as a source of comfort and inspiration for others dealing with similar challenges. By sharing his art and experiences, Hall aims to create a sense of community and support among burn survivors. This article highlights the importance of art in fostering emotional healing and resilience among individuals who have endured traumatic events.
