## Teaching Narratives of Mental Illness

The chapter discusses the educational value of autobiographical narratives in understanding mental illnesses and their subjective experiences. Anne Hudson Jones reflects on teaching narratives of mental illness to various groups, including medical students and healthcare professionals. These narratives provide insights into the lived reality of individuals with mental health conditions and can offer hope and encouragement to those struggling with mental illness. The chapter emphasizes the importance of reading multiple narratives to understand the variability in disease manifestations and treatment responses. Jones highlights 'The Quiet Room' by Lori Schiller and Amanda Bennett as a particularly effective narrative due to its comprehensive coverage of mental health issues and its engaging yet powerful storytelling.
