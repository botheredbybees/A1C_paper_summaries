## Week 10 - Developing Project 3 - Light & Shade

This lesson in the FXA101 Creativity and Ageing course focuses on the theme of light and shadow in creative projects. It encourages students to reflect on their creative process and the role of visual perception in problem-solving. The lesson includes the application of research to enhance creativity, critical reflection on lighting effects on perception, and the documentation of personal creative choices. Students are expected to continue developing their mini-projects for Creative Project 3 and to allocate six to seven hours to complete the week's activities.
