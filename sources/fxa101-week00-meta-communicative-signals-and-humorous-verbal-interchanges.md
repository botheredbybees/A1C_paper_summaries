## Meta-communicative signals and humorous verbal interchanges

This paper introduces a two-pronged analytical model for the study of verbal humor in interactions, integrating General Theory of Verbal Humor with meta-communicative signals to identify on-stage and off-stage humor. The author analyzes an Italian film by Totò, applying this model to distinguish between types of humor based on script opposition and logical mechanism. Key findings include defining a Meta-Knowledge Resource as the seventh knowledge resource in verbal humor analysis.
