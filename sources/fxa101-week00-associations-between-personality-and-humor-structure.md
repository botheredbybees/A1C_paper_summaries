## Associations between personality and humor structure appreciation

This paper investigates the relationship between personality traits and humor appreciation, focusing on two structural dimensions of humor: incongruity-resolution (INC-RES) and nonsense (NON). The study employs the Humor Structure Appreciation Scale and Big Five Inventory-2 to assess these associations. Results indicate that open-mindedness is linked with a preference for NON humor, while conscientiousness correlates with INC-RES humor. Specific facets of openness such as aesthetic sensitivity predict liking of NON over INC-RES humor. Extraverted individuals enjoy all types of humor more than introverted neurotics.
