## Week 5 Introduction for Spring 2025

This document serves as an introduction to Week 5 of a course on creativity, marking the start of Module 2. The module aims to enhance creative thinking and practical skills through engaging in mini-projects that challenge students to step outside their comfort zones. Each project provides detailed briefs, background information, examples, and preparatory steps to guide student projects. The week focuses on understanding how constraints can aid creativity and includes a critical exercise involving drawing with both hands to explore the concept of control versus flow. Students are reminded to document all creative processes in their journals for future assessments. Additionally, students are prompted to review Module 1 materials ahead of an upcoming quiz.
