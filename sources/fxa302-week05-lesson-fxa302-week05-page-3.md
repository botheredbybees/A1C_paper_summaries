## Hearing Loss and Musical Performance

This lesson explores how profound hearing loss impacts musical performance and engagement with music. It discusses two case studies: Dame Evelyn Glennie, a Grammy award-winning Scottish percussionist who became profoundly deaf in childhood but continues to perform internationally; and Beethoven's Nightmare, an American rock band comprising musicians with varying degrees of hearing impairment. The case studies highlight how these performers adapt their techniques to overcome auditory limitations, often challenging societal assumptions about the necessity of hearing for musical participation and enjoyment.
