## Measuring Compassion and Self-Compassion

This lesson focuses on the measurement of self-compassion and compassion using scales developed by Neff and colleagues. It explains the Self-Compassion Scale, which assesses self-compassion through three main components: self-kindness, common humanity, and mindfulness, alongside their opposites: self-judgment, isolation, and over-identification. The lesson also details the Compassion Scale, which measures compassionate behavior towards others, considering mindfulness, kindness, common humanity, and indifference. The Compassion Scale's development involved statistical analysis, identifying a single 'opposite' factor called 'indifference.' Both scales provide insights into the psychological constructs of compassion and self-compassion.
