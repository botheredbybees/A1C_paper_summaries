## Approaches and Models of Music Therapy

The chapter 'Approaches and Models of Music Therapy' by Jane Edwards discusses the diverse models and approaches in the field of music therapy, emphasizing their significance in the practice and training of music therapists. The inclusion criteria for the models and approaches detailed in the Oxford Handbook of Music Therapy are outlined, with a focus on those that are integral to music therapy training programs and have a presence in published literature. Key among the eleven models and approaches presented are Nordoff-Robbins Music Therapy, Feminist Perspectives, The Field of Play, Psychodynamic Music Therapy, Community Music Therapy, Developmental Music Therapy, Resource-Oriented Music Therapy, Anthroposophical Music Therapy, Culture-Centered Music Therapy, Aesthetic Music Therapy, and Vocal Psychotherapy. Each model or approach is characterized by its unique perspective on music therapy, such as the importance of musical, sociocultural, relational, and behavioral aspects. The chapter highlights the necessity of understanding the role of music, the role of the therapist, and the therapeutic processes involved, rather than just the techniques and methods. It underscores the importance of self-awareness and cultural context for practitioners and the emphasis on inclusive and collaborative interactions in community music therapy.
