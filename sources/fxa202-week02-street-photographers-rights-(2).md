## Street photographer's rights

The document 'Street Photographer's Rights' by the Arts Law Centre of Australia provides guidance on legal aspects and ethical considerations for street photography in public spaces. It clarifies that, generally, one can take photographs without permission in public areas but highlights specific limitations regarding private property and sensitive situations involving children or harassment concerns. The sheet emphasizes the importance of understanding privacy laws, including potential future developments towards a tort of invasion of privacy, and discusses how sports organizations might regulate photography within their venues, particularly on private properties. Additionally, it advises photographers to obtain model releases for commercial purposes and warns against unauthorized entry onto private land which can result in legal consequences such as trespassing charges or injunctions. Overall, the document underscores the need for photographers to be aware of their rights while respecting the boundaries of others.
