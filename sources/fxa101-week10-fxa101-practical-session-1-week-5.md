## FXA101 Practical Session 1 Week 5

The document discusses the numerous health benefits of engaging in creative activities, including cognitive improvements, emotional regulation, and some physical benefits. The text highlights that novel creative mediums and experiences can generate problem-solving skills, activate semantic networks, and provide optimal stress, which helps in building cognitive reserve and improving executive functions. Reflexivity is emphasized as a process of continuous examination and explanation of influences, crucial for personal growth and insight. Visualisation techniques are mentioned as popular approaches in various fields, contributing to improved focus and attention. The document also addresses the concept of Directed Attention Fatigue (DAF) and suggests balancing creative tasks with rest. Finally, it includes several creativity games and tasks designed to enhance problem-solving skills and cognitive flexibility, encouraging participants to reflect on their thought processes and experiences.
