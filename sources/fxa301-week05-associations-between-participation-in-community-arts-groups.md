## Associations between participation in community arts groups and aspects of wellbeing in older adults in the United States: a propensity score matching analysis

The study explores the relationship between participation in community arts groups and various aspects of wellbeing among older adults in the United States, utilizing a propensity score matching analysis to control for demographic, socioeconomic, and health-related factors that could confound results. The research finds significant associations between engaging in participatory arts activities and higher positive affect, life satisfaction, purpose in life, and personal mastery compared to those not participating. Notably, the findings suggest no impact on negative affect or constraints on personal control. Sensitivity analyses confirm these benefits persist four years later. This study underscores the potential for community-based arts groups to enhance various dimensions of wellbeing among older adults, indicating that facilitating such engagement could contribute positively to healthy aging and overall quality of life.
