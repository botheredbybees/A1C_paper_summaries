## The Murk Effect: A Demonstration of Visual Influence on Auditory Perception

This transcript describes an experiment demonstrating the murk effect, where visual cues can alter auditory perception. The actress in a video clip says one word while audio plays another, illustrating how the brain integrates conflicting sensory information, leading to misperception.
