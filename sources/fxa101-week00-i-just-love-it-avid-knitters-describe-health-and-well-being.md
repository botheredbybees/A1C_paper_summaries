## I just love it: Avid Knitters Describe Health and Well-being Through Occupation

This paper explores the role of knitting in enhancing health and well-being among avid knitters. The authors conducted interviews with 21 members of a knitting guild, including those with chronic conditions, and observed seven guild meetings. Phenomenological principles guided the research to understand how knitting contributes to physical, mental, emotional, and cognitive health; happiness and well-being; and occupational identity formation and maintenance. Five main themes emerged: 'makes me happy', 'the mental challenge I need', social connections and skill development, sustains identity ('can’t imagine life without knitting'), and reflects personality through creative expression.
