## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The text excerpt from 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Bradley R. Postle emphasizes the importance of motor control in human behavior. It discusses the concept of mirror neurons proposed by V.S. Ramachandran, which suggests that these neurons enable humans to emulate and imitate other people's actions, potentially explaining key human behaviors and their associated neurological disorders. However, the text also references cautionary perspectives from other researchers who question the extent of mirror neurons' role in action understanding. The core argument of Postle’s text is the central role of motor control principles in understanding higher levels of cognition, including the use of population coding, efference copy, and neuromodulatory tone. This work is relevant to the intersection of creative arts and health by highlighting the neural mechanisms that underpin the execution of movements and the potential for these mechanisms to influence artistic expression and performance in therapeutic contexts.
