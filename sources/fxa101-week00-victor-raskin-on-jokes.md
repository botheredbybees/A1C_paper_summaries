## Victor Raskin on Jokes

This paper discusses the contributions of Victor Raskin's General Theory of Verbal Humor to the study of jokes and humor. It highlights how Raskin distinguished between serious communication and humorous modes, emphasizing the role of conventional scripts in joke construction. The author also explores transformations of elephant jokes into ethnic stupidity jokes through script oppositions and targets, illustrating how modern jokes require fewer knowledge resources than their predecessors.
