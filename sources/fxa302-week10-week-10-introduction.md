## Week 10 Introduction

This document serves as an introductory text for Week 10 of a course on the perspectives of the arts on health and wellbeing. The instructor welcomes students and reflects on the progress of the semester, highlighting the importance of the course content and student engagement. The week's focus is on the expressions of neurological disorders through the creative arts, specifically through the work of artists affected by conditions such as Alzheimer's disease, epilepsy, and Parkinson's disease. Case studies of artists like William Utermohlen, Anton Raederscheidt, Francisco Goya, Jason Becker, and Michelle Ryan are discussed, exploring how these artists' creative outputs reflect the effects of neurological disorders and the adaptive strategies they employ. Additionally, the document provides an overview of current research into the therapeutic benefits of visual arts, music, and dance for neurological conditions, emphasizing the role of creative arts in rehabilitation and physical therapy. The text underscores the significance of these creative expressions in enhancing health, wellbeing, and resilience among individuals with neurological disorders.
