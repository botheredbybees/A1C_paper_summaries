## Creativity and Innovation

The book 'Creativity and Innovation,' edited by Jonathan A. Plucker, explores the multifaceted nature of creativity and its significance in contemporary society. It brings together contributions from leading researchers to provide a comprehensive understanding of creativity, innovation, and entrepreneurship. Key topics include theoretical frameworks, personality traits associated with creative individuals, and practical applications for fostering creativity in educational settings. The second edition includes updated chapters on group creativity, ethics, developmental aspects, makerspaces, and lessons learned from various disciplines. Emphasizing the interplay between creativity and intelligence, leadership, and other psychological constructs, the book argues that creativity is not just a personal trait but can be nurtured through deliberate practice and supportive environments. This collection offers valuable insights for educators, professionals, and individuals aiming to enhance their creative capacities.
