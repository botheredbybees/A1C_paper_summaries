## Traditional Craft Conservation and Technological Innovation of Xuan Paper

This paper explores the balance between preserving traditional methods of Xuan paper production and integrating modern technological innovations to meet contemporary needs in Chinese mixed-media art. It highlights how advancements in fiber processing, pulp preparation, molding techniques, drying processes, and hybrid material integration have transformed the paper's durability and versatility while maintaining its cultural heritage. The article discusses notable examples such as Xu Bing’s 'Book from the Sky' installation that integrates digital printing with traditional Xuan paper to create unique mixed-media artworks. It emphasizes the importance of balancing tradition with technological progress to ensure the continued relevance and significance of Xuan paper in modern Chinese art.
