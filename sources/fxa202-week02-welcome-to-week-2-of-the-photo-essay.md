## Welcome to Week 2 of the Photo Essay

The document provides an introduction to Week 2 of a photo essay course, emphasizing the importance of taking photographs using various devices such as camera phones, digital SLRs, or tablets. It highlights the need for students to practice and get comfortable with their chosen equipment, whether they are novices or experienced photographers. The week's activities focus on exploring color and composition in photography, encouraging creativity by applying and breaking rules of color theory and experimenting with compositional techniques. Students are advised to continue the theme 'window into your world' and engage actively in weekly discussion boards for interaction and feedback.
