## FXA300 Music, Mind and Body Week 10 Activities

FXA300 Music, Mind and Body Week 10 focuses on occupational injuries and performance anxiety affecting musicians. The lesson includes recommended readings on topics such as occupational injuries, performance anxiety, and the psychological well-being of professional musicians. Students are asked to choose a scholarly source on a type of injury or anxiety not covered in the course materials and discuss the study type, main findings, limitations, and usefulness of the research. Additional references provide a comprehensive list of studies and articles covering various aspects of musicians' health and performance anxiety.
