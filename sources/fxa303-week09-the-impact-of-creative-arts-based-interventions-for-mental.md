## The impact of creative arts-based interventions for mental health in conflict-affected contexts: A systematic narrative review

This systematic narrative review explores the effectiveness of creative arts-based interventions (ABIs) for mental health in conflict-affected contexts. The authors conducted a realist analysis using academic studies retrieved from four databases and included 24 papers after screening and quality appraisal. They found that approximately 83% of the reviewed studies had a positive impact on participant mental health, suggesting ABIs can be effective across various contexts. However, due to variability in context, population, methodology, and evaluation methods, the review highlights limitations in determining why certain interventions are more effective than others. The study recommends further research focusing on specific intervention typologies or theoretical approaches and using realist, process, or formative evaluation designs to better understand mechanisms of change. Overall, this review underscores the need for participatory action research (PAR) and structured school-based interventions that utilize resilience frameworks or specific therapeutic methods like dance-movement and music therapy.
