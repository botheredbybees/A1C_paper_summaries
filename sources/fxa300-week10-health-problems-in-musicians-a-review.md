## Health problems in musicians: A review

This review article, authored by E. Zuskin and colleagues, highlights the unique health challenges faced by musicians due to their occupational and lifestyle factors. The primary health issues include musculoskeletal disorders, skin disorders, respiratory disorders, and noise-induced hearing impairment. The article emphasizes that the specific type of instrument played and individual performer characteristics determine the particular occupational disorder a musician might experience. The authors stress the importance of preventive measures to ensure musicians can maintain long and healthy careers, given that these health problems can potentially become career-ending disabilities. The review also references existing literature and resources aimed at hearing conservation for young people and school-based education programs, advocating for a comprehensive approach to mitigate occupational health risks among musicians.
