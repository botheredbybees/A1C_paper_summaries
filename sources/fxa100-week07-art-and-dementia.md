## Art and Dementia

The Art and Dementia project, initiated by the Art Gallery of New South Wales, focuses on creating engaging arts experiences for individuals living with dementia, their care partners, and communities. The model aims to enhance social inclusion, stimulate imagination, and improve wellbeing through focused observation and discussion about art pieces. Since its inception in 2009, this program has included sensory art-making sessions, monthly discussions, and collaborative projects like the Arts Engagement for Liveable Communities (AECL) project, which tested innovative models of arts engagement outside gallery settings. Research by Dr Gail Kenning highlights positive impacts such as enhanced self-identity, social inclusion, and quality of life among participants. The program's success in fostering meaningful connections through art suggests its relevance to creative arts and health initiatives aimed at supporting individuals with dementia.
