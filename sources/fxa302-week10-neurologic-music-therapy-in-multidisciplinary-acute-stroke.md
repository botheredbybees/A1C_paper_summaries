## Neurologic music therapy in multidisciplinary acute stroke rehabilitation: Could it be feasible and helpful?

The study investigates the feasibility and acceptability of incorporating Neurologic Music Therapy (NMT) into multidisciplinary acute stroke rehabilitation. Over a two-year period, a two-day per week NMT service was provided to patients in a 26-bed stroke and rehabilitation unit, with no control group. Data were collected on patient referrals, session attendance, and feedback from patients, relatives, and staff regarding the effectiveness of NMT. Results showed that 177 out of 201 referred patients received treatment, with an average satisfaction score of 3.34 on a 4-point scale. While the study did not find statistically significant improvements in mood, there was a trend towards reduced sadness and increased happiness post-session. The findings suggest that NMT is feasible and potentially helpful, particularly for enhancing patient mood and engagement in rehabilitation. Further research is needed to determine the generalizability of these results in different stroke rehabilitation environments.
