## Improvement of spontaneous language in stroke patients with chronic aphasia treated with music therapy: a randomized controlled trial

This randomized controlled trial evaluates the impact of music therapy (MT) combined with speech-language therapy (SLT) on chronic aphasia in stroke patients. The study includes two groups: one receiving both MT and SLT, and the other receiving only SLT. The experimental group underwent 30 individual MT sessions over 15 weeks in addition to SLT, while the control group received only SLT. The results indicate a significant improvement in spontaneous speech for the experimental group compared to the control group. Additionally, half of the participants in the experimental group showed improved vitality scores on the Short Form Health Survey. The study highlights the potential of combining MT with SLT to enhance rehabilitation outcomes for patients with aphasia.
