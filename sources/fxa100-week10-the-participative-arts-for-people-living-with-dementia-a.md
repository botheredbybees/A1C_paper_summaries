## The Participative Arts for People Living with Dementia: A Critical Review

The article 'The Participative Arts for People Living with Dementia: A Critical Review' by Hannah Zeilig, John Killick, and Chris Fox critically examines the role of participatory arts activities in enhancing quality of life for individuals living with dementia. The review synthesizes literature to understand how various art forms can provide therapeutic benefits beyond pharmacological treatments. It explores interventions such as music, storytelling, visual arts, and others, highlighting their potential to improve emotional well-being and social engagement. Despite the growing interest in these activities, the authors note gaps in empirical evidence regarding their efficacy and long-term impact. The review underscores the importance of participatory arts within a broader healthcare framework that supports non-pharmacological interventions for dementia care.
