## Moving Towards Gray: Art Therapy and Ambivalence in Substance Abuse Treatment

The article explores the integration of art therapy with newer models of substance abuse treatment, specifically motivational interviewing (MI) and the stages-of-change (SOC) model, which focus on ambivalence and self-efficacy rather than confrontation of denial. The author questions the dominance of the 12-step method in the field of art therapy and examines literature that predominantly adheres to this approach. The article highlights how art therapy can address ambivalence and support recovery by engaging clients in the here-and-now process of artmaking, rather than focusing on absolute transformation. The relevance of art therapy in the context of emerging treatment models is discussed, emphasizing its potential to strengthen sobriety and recovery by fostering self-efficacy and reducing ambivalence.
