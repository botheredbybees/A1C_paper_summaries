## Promoting Health and Wellbeing Through Non-Clinical Arts Programming: Multi-Stakeholder Perspectives From the Veterans Health Administration

This study investigates the impact of non-clinical arts programs on Veterans' health and wellbeing within the context of the Veterans Health Administration (VHA). The researchers conducted semi-structured qualitative interviews with Veterans, VA staff, and community partners from seven VAMCs implementing such programs. Key findings indicate that these programs contribute to mental health improvements, a renewed sense of purpose, increased social connectedness, improved self-esteem, and proactive engagement in art activities. Participants across all stakeholder groups perceived significant benefits for Veteran wellbeing through non-clinical arts programming offered both inside and outside healthcare facilities. The study highlights the potential of such initiatives in enhancing holistic approaches to public health.
