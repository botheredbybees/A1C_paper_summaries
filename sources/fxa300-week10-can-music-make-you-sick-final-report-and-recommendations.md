## Can Music Make You Sick? Final Report and Recommendations

The report 'Can Music Make You Sick?' by Help Musicians UK, based on a commissioned study from the University of Westminster, explores the mental health challenges faced by musicians in the UK. The research, involving over 2,200 self-selected respondents and qualitative interviews with 26 musicians, reveals that those working in music are up to three times more likely to experience depression compared to the general population. The study highlights the impact of the unpredictable and precarious nature of the music industry on mental health, including high levels of stress, anxiety, and depression. It also identifies the importance of musicians' relationships with their work, their need for self-belief, and the challenges of accessing professional mental health support. The report calls for improved working conditions and greater understanding of the unique challenges faced by creative workers, emphasizing the need for industry-wide support through initiatives like the 24/7 support line and service #MusicMindsMatter. This research is crucial for addressing mental health issues in the creative arts and underscores the need for comprehensive support systems for musicians.
