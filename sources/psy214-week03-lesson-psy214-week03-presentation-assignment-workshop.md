## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Information Sheet Assessment Criteria

This lesson outlines the assessment criteria for an information sheet assignment in a psychology course. The content focuses on specific requirements and weightings for each criterion, emphasizing differences from previous workbook assignments. Criteria include describing mindfulness practices (10% and 20%), explaining how activities work (20%), integrating scholarly evidence effectively (20%) without direct quotes, applying evidence appropriately (5%), and adhering to neutral, objective presentation for a general audience (15%). The lesson advises against personal reflections in the information sheet, unlike in previous assignments.
