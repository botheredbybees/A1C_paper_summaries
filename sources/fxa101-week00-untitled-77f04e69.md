## Семіотика ТА ТЕХНІКИ ГУЦУЛЬСЬКОЇ ВИШИВКИ У КОНТЕКСТІ НАЦІОНАЛЬНОЇ ІДЕНТИЧНОСТІ

This paper analyzes Hutsul embroidery as a unique cultural phenomenon that integrates aesthetic, symbolic, ritual, and communicative aspects. The study examines the symbolism and techniques of Hutsul embroidery within the context of national identity in the Carpathian region. Historically documented elements of traditional clothing and embroidery from researchers like I. Vahylevych and others are considered visual texts conveying social status, lineage, age, sacred beliefs, and regional identity. Techniques such as nyzynka (underrunning stich), zanyzuvannia (reverse stitch), satin stitch, cross-stitch, and prutyk demonstrate technological diversity and local craftsmanship. Symbolic ornamentation includes the rhombus (fertility, feminine principle), rosette (sun, eternity), infinity symbol (continuity of existence), cross (protection, faith), and comb patterns (movement of energy). The role of color in conveying vital, sacred, and protective meanings is also explored.
