## Renée Fleming’s Music and Mind

Renée Fleming, a renowned opera singer and advocate for the arts in healthcare, is highlighted in this article by Jeanine Young-Mason. The piece discusses Fleming's initiatives that explore the intersection of music, health, and neuroscience. Notably, it covers her Sound Health collaboration with the National Institutes of Health (NIH) and the National Endowment for the Arts, which aims to investigate and promote research on how music can contribute to health and wellness. Additionally, Fleming launched a live-streamed series called 'Music and Mind LIVE With Renée Fleming,' featuring discussions between Fleming and experts in various fields related to music's impact on mental and physical well-being during the COVID-19 pandemic. The article also mentions her recently published anthology titled 'Music and Mind: Harvesting the Arts for Health and Wellness.' This book compiles essays from leading scientists, artists, creative arts therapists, and healthcare professionals who explore how music can enhance health and the human experience.
