## Healthy Eating and Mental Health

This lesson explores the relationship between diet quality and mental health. It highlights reciprocal relationships between physical and mental health, emphasizing that poor dietary habits can exacerbate both conditions. Research indicates a direct impact of diet on mental health through various mechanisms including inflammation and oxidative stress. Notably, diets high in discretionary and processed foods are associated with increased levels of these stressors. Studies such as the SMILES trial and Helfimed trial demonstrate significant reductions in depressive symptoms following dietary interventions based on Mediterranean principles and guidelines for healthy eating. The lesson also discusses anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties found in plant-based foods like berries, leafy greens, nuts, and seafood rich in omega-3 fatty acids. It acknowledges the complexity of stress-related eating behaviors but promotes mindful awareness as a beneficial approach.
