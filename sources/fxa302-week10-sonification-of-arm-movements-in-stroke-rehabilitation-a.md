## Sonification of Arm Movements in Stroke Rehabilitation – A Novel Approach in Neurologic Music Therapy

This study introduces a novel rehabilitation technique using musical sonification to improve upper extremity function in stroke patients. The researchers designed a therapy where arm movements are translated into sound, aiming to motivate patients and provide sensory input regarding limb position. The study involved 25 patients, divided into a music group (MG) and a control group (CG). Participants engaged in a 10-day training session (30 minutes/day) involving the exploration of acoustic effects of arm movements and the playing of simple melodies. Significant improvements were observed in the MG in terms of reduced joint pain and enhanced movement smoothness, suggesting that musical sonification may be a promising therapeutic approach. The study highlights the potential of integrating auditory feedback into rehabilitation to enhance neuroplasticity and motor recovery, with implications for creative arts and health applications.
