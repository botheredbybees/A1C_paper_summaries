## Neurological Disorders and the Visual Arts

The lesson explores the role of visual arts in the rehabilitation of individuals suffering from neurological disorders such as stroke, Parkinson's disease, and multiple sclerosis. It highlights how art-making can serve as a non-verbal means of expression, improving mental well-being and physical functions. The case studies of Anton Räderscheidt and Francisco Goya illustrate how neurological impairments influenced their artistic works and personal lives, providing valuable insights into the therapeutic benefits of art-making. Räderscheidt's post-stroke paintings showcase the effects of left-side neglect, while Goya's work reflects his struggle with Susac syndrome, emphasizing the transformative power of art in the face of neurological challenges.
