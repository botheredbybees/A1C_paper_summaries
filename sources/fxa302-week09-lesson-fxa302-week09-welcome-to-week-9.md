## Week 9 - Pain and Mortality

This lesson explores the intersection of art, pain, and mortality, focusing on cultural and artistic expressions of these themes. It covers the concepts of mortality, memento mori, and vanitas, and examines how chronic pain and life-threatening illnesses influence creativity. The lesson includes case studies of artists like Tanya Paterson and Anna Cowley Ford, who use their work to express the lived experience of pain and illness, and discusses the therapeutic benefits of creativity for those suffering from chronic conditions. The lesson aims to deepen understanding of pain through artistic expression and encourages reflection on the impacts of creativity on perceived wellbeing.
