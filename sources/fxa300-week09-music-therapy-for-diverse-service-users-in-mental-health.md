## Music Therapy for Diverse Service Users in Mental Health Settings

This chapter explores the application of music therapy for diverse service users in mental health settings, emphasizing the importance of culturally sensitive practices. It highlights the significant demographic shifts in the United States, leading to an increased need for mental health services among marginalized groups. The chapter underscores the critical role of cultural humility, sensitivity, and awareness in effective therapy. It also discusses the importance of understanding cultural tendencies and the concept of acculturation to build therapeutic rapport and facilitate effective treatment. The text suggests that while behavioral approaches can be particularly effective with diverse populations due to their action-oriented nature, cognitive behavioral therapy may not always be appropriate, as it can devalue culturally significant spiritual practices. The chapter concludes by advocating for the need to adapt therapeutic approaches to align with the cultural values and identities of diverse service users.
