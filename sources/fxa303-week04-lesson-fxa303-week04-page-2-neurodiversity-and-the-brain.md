## Neurodiversity and Autism Spectrum in Childhood

This lesson explores neurodiversity in childhood, focusing on autism spectrum disorder (ASD) and the medical model of disability. It discusses the diagnostic criteria from the DSM and brain development differences observed in ASD through imaging studies. Theories suggest that ASD is a developmental disconnection syndrome affecting neural networks rather than specific areas. Therapies for autism include behavior modification programs that capitalize on early childhood neuroplasticity, with various categories like ABA therapy and developmental approaches to improve social interactions and quality of life. Recommendations by Zwaigenbaum et al. (2015) emphasize the importance of family involvement, cultural considerations, and early intervention.
