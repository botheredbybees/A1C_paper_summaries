## Exploring the Art/Creative Process through Masks Making and Playing: Continuity, Disruption, Resilience

This article discusses an arts therapy training workshop held at Brunel University in July 2025, focusing on the creative process through mask making and playing as a means of exploring personal and social emotional regulation and change. The author introduces concepts from the Expressive Therapies Continuum (ETC) model and demonstrates how creating paper masks can facilitate resilience and healing continuity from disruption to transformation in clinical contexts.
