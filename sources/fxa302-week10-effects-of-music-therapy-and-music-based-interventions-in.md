## Effects of music therapy and music-based interventions in the treatment of substance use disorders: A systematic review

This systematic review examines the efficacy of music therapy (MT) and music-based interventions (MBIs) in treating substance use disorders (SUD). The authors conducted a comprehensive search of published articles and found 34 quantitative and six qualitative studies. While there was an increase in randomized controlled trials (RCTs) in recent years, the studies were diverse, making a meta-analysis infeasible. The review highlights beneficial effects of MT/MBIs on emotional and motivational outcomes, group interaction, development of skills, and quality of life. However, the results were inconsistent, and no longitudinal studies were found. The review concludes that more research is needed, particularly focusing on social and health variables, to clarify the efficacy of MT/MBIs in SUD treatment.
