## Creativity and Constraints: Exploring the Role of Constraints in the Creative Processes of Research and Development Teams

This article investigates the role of constraints in fostering creativity within research and development (R&D) teams, addressing a paradox in organizational creativity literature that suggests both freedom and constraint are essential to creative processes. Rosso's study identifies two broad categories of constraints: process and product constraints, each affecting team creativity differently depending on the context and dynamics of the R&D environment. Through field research with four R&D teams within a multinational corporation known for innovation, the author demonstrates how enduring enabling or disabling dynamics influence whether constraints enhance or inhibit creative outcomes. Rosso challenges the traditional view that constraints inherently stifle creativity by showing that in certain conditions, embracing constraints can lead to greater freedom and enhanced creativity. This study contributes significantly to understanding team-level creativity, particularly within R&D contexts, highlighting the importance of contextual factors in constraining environments for fostering innovation.
