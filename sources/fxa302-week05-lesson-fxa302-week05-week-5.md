## Week 5 Deafness, Inner Hearing and the Romantic Mindset

This week's lesson in FXA302 focuses on the impacts of hearing loss and deafness on musicians and composers. It covers the process of hearing, types of hearing impairments such as conductive and sensorineural losses, and the role of modern technologies like digital hearing aids and cochlear implants. The content explores how historical and contemporary musicians have coped with auditory disabilities and how these conditions influence their creative processes. Key concepts include the mechanics of hearing and the neurological processing involved in sound interpretation.
