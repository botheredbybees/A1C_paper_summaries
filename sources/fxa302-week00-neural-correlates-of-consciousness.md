## Neural Correlates of Consciousness

This chapter examines the neural correlates of consciousness (NCC) in various altered states such as anesthesia, sleep, and disorders of consciousness (DoC) following severe acquired brain injuries. It defines NCC as the minimum neural mechanisms sufficient for specific conscious perceptions and discusses the challenges in integrating findings from physiological, pharmacological, and pathological states to produce a definitive account of NCC. The chapter reviews neurophysiological findings and explores the background conditions and consequences of consciousness, aiming to understand the relationship between consciousness and the brain.
