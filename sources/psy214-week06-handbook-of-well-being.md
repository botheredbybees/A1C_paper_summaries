## Handbook of Well-Being

The 'Handbook of Well-Being' is an extensive resource edited by Ed Diener, Shigehiro Oishi, and Louis Tay, focusing on the study of subjective well-being (SWB). The introduction provides a historical overview of SWB research, highlighting significant advancements from questioning reliability and validity in the 1980s to understanding core evaluative and affective components today. It discusses demographic, biological, cultural, and psychological factors influencing SWB. Additionally, it explores interventions for enhancing well-being and societal policies that promote well-being. The handbook emphasizes the importance of open access to this knowledge, aiming to democratize education and research.
