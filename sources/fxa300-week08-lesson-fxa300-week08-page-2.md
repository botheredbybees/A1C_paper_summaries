## Music in Healthcare Settings

This lesson explores the use of music in healthcare settings, including its role as background, personal listening, live performance, and therapeutic intervention. It discusses the benefits of music therapy, which includes stress and pain management, communication enhancement, and physical rehabilitation. Music therapy is defined as the use of music by trained professionals to improve health, functioning, and well-being. Various specialized approaches, such as Nordoff-Robbins Music Therapy, Guided Imagery and Music (GIM), and Neurologic Music Therapy (NMT), are also highlighted. The lesson covers the effectiveness of music in pain relief and its potential in treating chronic conditions like Parkinson's Disease and Dementia.
