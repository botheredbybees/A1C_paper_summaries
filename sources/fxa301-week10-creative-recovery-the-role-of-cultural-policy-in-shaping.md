## Creative Recovery? The Role of Cultural Policy in Shaping Post-COVID Urban Futures

This report, authored by a team including Jonathan Gross and Lucy McFadzean among others, examines how cultural policymakers in cities responded to the COVID-19 pandemic. It explores three key areas: city cultural policymakers' responses to support culture during the crisis; the role of the World Cities Culture Forum in facilitating collaboration and innovation; and the implications these responses have for shaping urban futures post-COVID. The report highlights that while the pandemic exacerbated pre-existing economic, social, political, and environmental crises, it also revealed the resilience and adaptability of cultural ecosystems. Through a combination of focus groups, interviews, surveys, and analysis of existing data from World Cities Culture Forum webinars, the research documents over 270 policies and insights from 40 global cities. The authors argue that culture plays a crucial role in driving recovery by reanimating public spaces, encouraging community engagement, and improving working conditions for creative workers. However, they also emphasize ongoing challenges and the need to build resilience within cultural sectors moving forward.
