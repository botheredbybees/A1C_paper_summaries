## Inventing Conflicts of Interest: A History of Tobacco Industry Tactics

This article explores how the tobacco industry responded to emerging scientific evidence linking smoking to various diseases in the mid-twentieth century. In a crisis mode, the industry developed sophisticated strategies involving public relations and academic collaborations to undermine the established medical research and create doubt around the harmful effects of cigarettes. These tactics included creating conflicts of interest within academia by funding studies that supported their narrative while questioning those that did not. The article highlights how these actions have set a precedent for other industries facing similar scientific challenges, influencing contemporary debates on conflict of interests in science.
