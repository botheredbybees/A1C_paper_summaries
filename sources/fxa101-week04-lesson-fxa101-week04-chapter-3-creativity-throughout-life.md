## Creativity throughout life

This lesson explores the perception of declining creativity with age and provides counterexamples to challenge this notion. It discusses how some artists achieve greatness later in life, contrasting with historical figures who died young but left extraordinary legacies. The text also highlights that increased life expectancy has led to more creative achievements past age 75, citing examples like Michelangelo and Titian. Research suggests a possible shift in the peak of creativity to around age 50 due to more time for pursuit. Creative interventions are shown to improve problem-solving skills and attitude towards aging, with case studies illustrating positive impacts on community engagement and health.
