## The Relationship Between Stressors and Creativity: A Meta-Analysis Examining Competing Theoretical Models

This study presents a meta-analysis of 76 experimental studies to clarify the relationship between stressors and creativity. The research explores different theoretical models that posit either positive, negative, or curvilinear relationships between stress and creative performance. The analysis reveals a curvilinear relationship for evaluative stress: low levels enhance creativity, while high levels diminish it. Uncontrollability is found to have a linearly negative impact on creativity. These findings suggest the complexity of stressors' effects on creativity, indicating that understanding boundary conditions can explain inconsistent results in previous studies.
