## The effects of music intervention on burn patients during treatment procedures: a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

This systematic review and meta-analysis examines the efficacy of music interventions for burn patients during treatment procedures. The study, conducted by Li, Zhou, and Wang (2017), synthesizes findings from 17 randomized controlled trials involving 804 patients. The authors highlight that music interventions are associated with significant reductions in pain and anxiety levels, as well as a decrease in heart rate among burn patients. Despite these positive outcomes, the review also identifies inconsistencies in previous research and calls for further high-quality studies to refine the understanding of music's therapeutic effects in burn treatment. This research underscores the potential of music therapy as a non-invasive and accessible approach to managing pain and anxiety in burn patients, contributing to the holistic care of these patients.
