## FXA302 Week 10 Welcome

The document introduces Week 10 of the FXA302 course, focusing on the impact of neurological disorders on creative arts. It explores conditions such as stroke, epilepsy, Alzheimer's, Multiple Sclerosis, and Parkinson’s disease, and examines how these disorders are reflected in the works of artists like William Utermohlen, Anton Raederscheidt, and Francisco Goya, composer Hector Berlioz, and dancer Michelle Ryan. The course materials also review current research on the therapeutic benefits of visual arts, music, and dance for neurological conditions, highlighting their role in rehabilitation and physical therapy. The relevance to creative arts and health is evident in the adaptive strategies employed by artists to cope with neural deficits, offering insights into how creative expression can support health and resilience.
