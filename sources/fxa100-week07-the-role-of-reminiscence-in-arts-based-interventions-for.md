## The Role of Reminiscence in Arts-Based Interventions for Dementia Care: A Scoping Review

This scoping review examines the role of reminiscence in visual art interventions for individuals with dementia. The study highlights that as traditional treatments for dementia have been ineffective in managing negative responsive behaviors, there has been a growing interest in alternative strategies such as art and music therapy. Previous reviews on creative arts therapies for people with dementia have not specifically focused on reminiscence within these interventions. This review aims to assess the state of evidence related to the role of reminiscence, mapping the range, nature, and focus of relevant articles while identifying gaps for future research.

The review analyzed 21 empirical articles retrieved from databases like CINAHL, PubMed, Web of Science, Scopus, and Google Scholar. Key findings indicate that reminiscence in arts-based interventions fosters social connection, bridges the past with the present, and enhances enjoyment and confidence among individuals living with dementia. The positive impact of art on these individuals' communication and overall well-being was noted across diverse studies. While the review highlights significant benefits, it also points to a need for further research to better understand and utilize the role of reminiscence in such interventions.

This study underscores the importance of integrating reminiscence into arts-based therapies as a vital component that facilitates human connection and supports emotional expression in dementia care.
