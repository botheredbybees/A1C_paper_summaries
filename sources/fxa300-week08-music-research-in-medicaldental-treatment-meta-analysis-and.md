## Music Research in Medical/Dental Treatment: Meta-Analysis and Clinical Applications

Jayne M. Standley's article provides a comprehensive analysis and application of music therapy research in medical and dental treatments. The paper reviews historical and contemporary studies on the physiological effects of music, including its impact on heart rate, blood pressure, and pain response. It highlights the role of music as an audioanalgesic in clinical settings, particularly in dental procedures and childbirth, demonstrating its potential to reduce pain and anxiety. The study identifies several factors contributing to the analgesic effect of music, such as neurologic suppression of pain, distraction from the procedure, and enhanced patient control over the environment. These findings are crucial for developing clinical applications of music therapy in healthcare settings, emphasizing its relevance to creative arts and health.
