## Learn to Sing: Breathing

The document titled 'Learn to Sing: Breathing' provides guidance on proper breathing techniques for singing. It emphasizes the importance of deep diaphragmatic breathing and offers practical exercises to enhance breath control and vocal capacity. The text suggests lying flat with hands on the stomach to ensure correct inhalation and exhalation patterns, and includes specific breathing exercises such as hissing and snatched breaths to improve technique. These methods aim to increase lung capacity and enable singers to maintain consistent sound quality throughout longer musical phrases.
