## Adult learners learning from experience: using a reflective practice model to support work-based learning

The article by Elaine Cox discusses the use of reflective practice models to enhance learning from work-based experiences for adult learners. Cox emphasizes the importance of structured reflection for fostering deeper understanding and personal development, particularly in contexts where tacit knowledge is prevalent. The paper outlines a research project involving students training as mentors and highlights the benefits of using a reflective practice model to improve learning outcomes. Cox argues that reflective practice supports perspective transformation, critical consciousness, and theoretical reflectivity, making it a valuable tool for adult learners in various professional settings. Despite some resistance and initial difficulties in developing reflective skills, the study demonstrates the effectiveness of the model in promoting self-awareness and informed responses in work contexts.
