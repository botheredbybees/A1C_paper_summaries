## Case Study of Musician with Brain Tumor Impacting Musical Function

A case study is presented focusing on a musician and music teacher who had a tumor in the brain region critical for musical function. The researchers utilized intraoperative direct electrical stimulation during awake surgery to demonstrate that stimulating the right posterior superior temporal gyrus selectively disrupted the patient's music abilities without affecting language skills. Preoperative functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) and intraoperative mapping techniques were employed to guide surgical intervention, ensuring the tumor could be removed while preserving the individual’s musical capabilities.
