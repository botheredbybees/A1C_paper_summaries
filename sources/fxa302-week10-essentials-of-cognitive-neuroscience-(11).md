## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The text discusses the challenges in designing studies that accurately measure cognitive functions, particularly in the context of semantic memory and primary progressive aphasia (PPA). It highlights the difficulty in creating definitive studies for abstract cognitive functions and the importance of considering embodied cognition in cognitive neuroscience. The chapter focuses on the semantic variant of PPA and references clinical criteria from previous studies. It also poses questions about interpreting selective naming deficits in patients and the significance of findings from case studies and neuroimaging studies, emphasizing the interdisciplinary nature of cognitive neuroscience.
