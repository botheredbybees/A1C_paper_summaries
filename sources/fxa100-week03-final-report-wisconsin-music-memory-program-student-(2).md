## Final Report: Wisconsin Music & Memory Program – Student Volunteer Program

This final report documents the activities and results of the Wisconsin Music & Memory Program’s Student Volunteer Program. The initiative aimed to provide personalized music services to nursing home residents in Wisconsin through funding from Civil Money Penalty (CMP) grants. An additional 67 nursing homes became certified as Music & Memory organizations, with 37 participating in the student volunteer program that involved collaboration with schools and engagement of high school and college students for volunteering opportunities. The report highlights outreach efforts targeting HOSA Future Health Professionals chapters and other relevant school groups to promote dementia care awareness and involvement. Successful engagements included presentations at various educational conferences and state leadership events, as well as projects like the creation of promotional videos and participation in international health professional student competitions.
