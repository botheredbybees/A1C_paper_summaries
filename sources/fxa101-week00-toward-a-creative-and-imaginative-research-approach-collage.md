## Toward a Creative and Imaginative Research Approach: Collage as a Method of Inquiry in Social Work

This article explores the use of collage as a methodological tool in social work research, situated within an arts-based research framework. The study examines participants' experiences with collage-making and its potential to generate empirical data through creative processes that challenge conventional qualitative methods like interviews and observations. Key themes include the dynamic visual language used in collages, their role in reflecting personal and shared experiences, ethical considerations of associative creation, and the importance of time, space, and commitment for effective engagement with this method. The research highlights the promise of collage as a means to capture complex narratives and foster meaningful connections between individuals' lived experiences and broader social phenomena.
