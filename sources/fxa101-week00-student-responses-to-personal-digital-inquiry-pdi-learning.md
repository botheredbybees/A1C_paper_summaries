## Student Responses to Personal Digital Inquiry (PDI) Learning Integrated with Citizen Science Project (CSP) on Biodiversity Materials

This study examines student responses to Personal Digital Inquiry (PDI) learning integrated with Citizen Science Project (CSP) in biodiversity education for senior high school students. The research aims to assess the effectiveness of this innovative approach, which combines scientific inquiry and digital technology with citizen science activities. Questionnaires were used to gather data from 42 participants who completed a biodiversity project involving Instagram-based “digital herbariums.” Results indicate that students felt enthusiastic about participating in learning, gained new experiences interacting with scientists and their community, built stronger relationships with teachers and peers, and developed awareness of the importance of biodiversity conservation through social media. The study underscores the potential of PDI-CSP integration to enhance student engagement and understanding of complex environmental issues.
