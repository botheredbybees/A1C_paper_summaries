## Integrating Art Therapy Methodology in Brief Inpatient Substance Abuse Treatment for Adults

The article discusses the integration of art therapy methodology into short-term hospital-based inpatient substance abuse treatment programs. It highlights the multifaceted nature of drug addiction and the necessity of comprehensive treatment responses. Art therapy is presented as a valuable component that can enhance traditional treatment protocols, providing a mechanism for collecting comprehensive client information, facilitating therapeutic change, and documenting client progress. The author argues that art therapy is particularly beneficial for brief inpatient programs where clients are diverse, in crisis, and at high risk for relapse. The paper provides a conceptual framework and outlines practice guidelines and specific treatment techniques through case examples. Art therapy is shown to help clients express and manage emotions, develop coping skills, and enhance interpersonal connections, thereby strengthening their self-efficacy and resilience in the face of drug-related stressors.
