## Review of 'German Post-Expressionism. The Art of the Great Disorder 1918-1924' by Dennis Crockett

This review by Andreas Strobl discusses Dennis Crockett's book 'German Post-Expressionism. The Art of the Great Disorder 1918-1924.' Strobl commends Crockett for his deep understanding of German art from the 1920s and for utilizing extensive archival research, including previously unexploited sources. The book examines the development of post-expressionist art in Germany following World War I, focusing on the interplay between social and political contexts and artistic expressions. It explores the emergence of the 'New Objectivity' and 'Post-Expressionism' movements, providing historical context for the period's art. The review highlights Crockett's detailed analysis of key artists such as Otto Dix and George Grosz, and the broader cultural dynamics of post-war Germany. Strobl notes the book's thorough engagement with the international influences on German art and its examination of the art market during the hyperinflation era. The review concludes by appreciating Crockett's work for its significant contribution to understanding the complex artistic landscape of the early 20th century.
