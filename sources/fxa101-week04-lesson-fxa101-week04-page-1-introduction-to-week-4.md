## FXA101 Creativity and Ageing Week 4 Introduction

This week's course material focuses on the effects of aging on brain structures and cognitive functioning. It discusses neuroplasticity as a regenerative process that persists into old age, enabling new neural connections and brain resilience. The lesson covers popular 'brain training' applications, their marketing claims, and recent research about them. Other activities that can keep the brain active and improve memory are also explored, alongside theories on maximizing cognitive resilience through combinatorial activities. The course examines creativity in later life, characteristics of creative late bloomers, and how aging affects the nature of creativity. A case study from the UK is introduced to illustrate innovative creative projects for older men.
