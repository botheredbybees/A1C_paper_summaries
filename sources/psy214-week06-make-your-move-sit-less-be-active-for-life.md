## Make your move – Sit less: Be active for life!

The brochure 'Make Your Move – Sit Less: Be Active for Life!' emphasizes the importance of physical activity and minimizing sedentary behavior to maintain health and well-being among adults aged 18-64 years. It outlines Australia's Physical Activity and Sedentary Behaviour Guidelines, which recommend a minimum of 150 minutes of moderate intensity or 75 minutes of vigorous intensity physical activity per week, alongside muscle-strengthening activities at least twice weekly. The document highlights numerous health benefits associated with regular physical activity, including reduced risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes, and some cancers, as well as improved mental health and blood sugar levels. Additionally, it underscores the negative impacts of prolonged sitting, such as increased risk for type 2 diabetes and overall poorer health outcomes. The brochure provides practical tips to incorporate more activity into daily routines, from active travel options like walking or cycling instead of driving, to engaging in workplace activities that promote movement. Overall, the publication aims to encourage individuals to take small steps towards increasing their physical activity levels and reducing sedentary behaviors for better overall health.
