## Music and Health

The text explores the connection between music and health, emphasizing the positive and negative impacts of musical engagement on wellbeing. It discusses various studies and resources that highlight the role of music in mental and physical health, including stress reduction, emotional regulation, and social cohesion. Research indicates that music can improve wellbeing by fostering social interactions, enhancing personal values, and facilitating coordinated actions within groups. The text also mentions the role of music in enhancing mental health, particularly during the pandemic, and suggests that active music-making can lead to physiological and psychosocial improvements. However, it also notes that excessive music engagement can sometimes contribute to maladaptive daydreaming, which may interfere with wellbeing and functioning. The chapter underscores the importance of incorporating practices for healthy living, such as diet, exercise, and meditation, into the regimen of musicians to promote overall health and performance.
