## Resource-Oriented Perspectives in Music Therapy

The chapter 'Resource-Oriented Perspectives in Music Therapy' by Randi Rolvsjord traces the historical roots and development of resource-oriented approaches within the field of music therapy. It highlights the early contributions of authors like William W. Sears and explores the influence of humanistic psychology and the Nordoff-Robbins tradition. The chapter discusses how perspectives focusing on positive health, quality of life, and health promotion have shaped current resource-oriented practices. Key concepts such as music as a health resource, salutogenic perspectives, and contextual approaches are emphasized. The work of scholars like Ruud, Bruscia, and Schwabe is cited to illustrate how resource-oriented music therapy has evolved to integrate cultural and contextual understandings of music and its therapeutic potential. This evolving perspective emphasizes strengths and resources over pathology, contributing to a more holistic and inclusive approach in music therapy.
