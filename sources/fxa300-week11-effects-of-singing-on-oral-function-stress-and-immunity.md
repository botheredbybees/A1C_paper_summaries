## Effects of Singing on Oral Function, Stress, and Immunity

This study investigates the impact of singing on oral function, stress, and immune system health. The research was conducted in two phases, involving 13 and 17 participants respectively, who sang karaoke regularly for eight weeks. Results showed significant improvements in oral functions such as swallowing and saliva production. Additionally, participants experienced reduced levels of psychological stress markers, including cortisol and adrenaline, and exhibited enhanced immune responses, as indicated by increased T and B cell counts. The study concludes that regular singing can positively influence oral health, reduce stress, and improve immune function, contributing to overall health and quality of life.
