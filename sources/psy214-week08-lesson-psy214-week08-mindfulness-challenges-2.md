## How Mindfulness Works

This lesson explores the mechanisms and outcomes of mindfulness practices, particularly focusing on formal mindfulness interventions. It outlines significant findings in pain perception and management, depressive relapse and symptom reduction, anxiety, addiction, and general wellbeing. The evidence suggests that mindfulness can reduce pain symptoms, decrease the risk of depressive relapse by 50% compared to standard treatments, and alleviate anxiety symptoms comparable to Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT). For addiction, mindfulness practices, such as 'urge surfing,' help individuals manage cravings and reduce relapse rates. Additionally, the lesson discusses the mechanisms through which mindfulness achieves these outcomes, emphasizing emotional regulation, self-awareness, and the reduction of stress-reactivity.
