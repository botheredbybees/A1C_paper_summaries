## Integrating Reflection Patterns and Strategies Through a Documentation Tool Into Industrial Design Studio Education for Enhanced Reflective Learning

This doctoral thesis by Hatice Merve Demirci Berberoğlu explores the role of reflective practice in industrial design education and aims to enhance it through a structured documentation tool based on Donald Schön's theoretical framework of reflection-in-action and reflection-on-action. The study involves four phases: understanding students' competencies and reflection habits, identifying nine emerging reflection patterns via visual content analysis, integrating these insights into a Miro-based project template for pilot testing, and evaluating the tool’s effectiveness at the UTAK 2024 National Design Research Conference. Key findings indicate that the documentation tool aids in articulating design decisions, incorporating feedback, and engaging in reflective practices among industrial design students. This research contributes to educational models for translating Schön's theoretical framework into practical applications within diverse design studio environments.
