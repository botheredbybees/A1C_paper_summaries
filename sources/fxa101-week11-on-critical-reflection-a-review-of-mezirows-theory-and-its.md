## On Critical Reflection: A Review of Mezirow’s Theory and Its Operationalization

This article provides an integrative literature review of empirical studies on critical reflection based on Mezirow’s transformative learning theory. The authors analyze 12 research studies and identify different approaches to operationalizing critical reflection. They conclude that there is little consensus on how to operationalize reflection and suggest four improvements: integrating different critical reflection traditions, using multiple data collection pathways, opting for thematic embedding, and attending to feelings. The aim is to stimulate closer alignment of approaches in critical reflection research across adult education and human resource development (HRD). Critical reflection is seen as essential for gaining new perspectives in professional practice, including healthcare, leadership, and higher education. The review highlights the need for more substantive data and exploration of means to capture critical reflection accurately. The article underscores Mezirow’s foundational work and its enduring influence in adult learning and HRD.
