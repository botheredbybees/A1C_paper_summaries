## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The chapter 'Turning Our Attention to the Future' in 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Bradley R. Postle explores the neural underpinnings of spatial cognition and attention. It discusses how disruptions in temporoparietal cortical regions can severely affect behaviors such as awareness of objects in different parts of the visual field. The chapter builds on the concept of the parietal cortex and spatial cognition to introduce new ideas about the neural organization of behavior and cognition, particularly focusing on the dorsal stream's role in guiding interactions between the body and objects in the environment. It also delves into how parietal and frontal cortical systems coordinate with subcortical systems to control actions and how a dynamic 'salience map' guides perception. The chapter concludes with questions that encourage deeper understanding of unilateral neglect symptoms and the integration of sensory information with eye-position data to transform coordinates from retina-centered to head-centered, and then from head-centered to trunk-centered. These insights are relevant to the creative arts and health, particularly in understanding how neurological processes influence perception and interaction with the environment, which can inform the creation of art and therapeutic interventions.
