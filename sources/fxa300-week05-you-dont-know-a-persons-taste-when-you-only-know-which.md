## You don’t know a person(’s taste) when you only know which genre they like: taste differences within five popular music genres based on sub-genres and sub-styles

The study investigates musical taste differences within five popular music genres—European classical, electronic dance (EDM), metal, pop, and rock—by examining sub-genre preferences among a representative German sample of 2,086 participants. Using Latent Profile Analysis, the research found distinct taste classes within each genre based on preferences for specific sub-genres and styles. Across all genres, two types of taste profiles emerged: one indicating varying degrees of appreciation across all sub-genres and another showing biases towards mainstream versus more niche or complex variants. Logistic regression models revealed correlations between these taste profiles and demographic variables as well as personality traits from the Big Five inventory. The findings challenge conventional genre-based categorizations of musical taste, suggesting that detailed understanding requires examining tastes at the level of specific sub-genres rather than broad genres alone.
