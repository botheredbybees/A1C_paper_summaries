## Joke Synonymy Sensitivity Among Working Comedians and the General Theory of Verbal Humor

This study explores whether working comedians exhibit increased sensitivity to joke synonymy compared to non-comedians, based on the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH) by Attardo and Raskin. Participants included 71 professional comedians recruited through social media and word-of-mouth. The research utilized a questionnaire with pre-test questions and three sets of seven jokes differing in six knowledge resources: language, narrative strategy, target, situation, logical mechanism, and script opposition. Results showed that comedians’ similarity ratings closely followed the GTVH prediction, indicating slightly greater sensitivity to joke synonymy compared to non-comedians.
