## FXA101 Sem 2 2026 - Web Lecture week 6

This document provides a comprehensive lecture summary for the sixth week of the FXA101 Creativity for Life course, focusing on the balance between constraints and creativity. The lecture discusses how creativity is often mistakenly perceived as an unstructured process, but it is actually enhanced by an optimal number of constraints. It explores the concept of 'scarcity salience,' where limited options can promote innovative thinking. Additionally, it delves into the experiences of notable artists and innovators like Miles Davis, Hilma af Klint, Rick Rubin, and Haruki Murakami, highlighting their methods for overcoming creative blocks and fostering innovation. The lecture also emphasizes the importance of allowing the creative process to unfold naturally, without rushing, and encourages engaging in diverse creative activities to stimulate the mind. References to scholarly articles and further reading materials are provided to support the discussion.
