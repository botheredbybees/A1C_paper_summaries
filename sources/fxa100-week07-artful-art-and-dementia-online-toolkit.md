## Artful: Art and Dementia online toolkit

The Artful: Art and Dementia online toolkit is a creative resource designed for individuals living with dementia and their support networks. It consists of 10 artmaking activities, each tailored to various stages of dementia and adaptable to different settings such as home or healthcare facilities. Activities are inspired by artworks in the MCA Collection and cover themes like painting to music, printmaking, and continuous line drawing. Each activity includes warm-up questions, written and video instructions, and tips for group work. The toolkit aims to enhance individual agency, foster connections, encourage conversation, and stimulate memory recall. Since 2015, the Artful program has supported over 200 participants, showing positive impacts on self-confidence and wellbeing. Research by the Brain and Mind Centre at the University of Sydney and Dementia Australia indicates that engaging in art can improve neuroplasticity and overall wellbeing for people with dementia. The toolkit's development was supported by the Jibb Foundation, which funds initiatives striving to improve equality of opportunity. This resource is crucial for those seeking to integrate creative arts into dementia care to enhance quality of life and social cohesion.
