## Sleep Hygiene Notes Slides

The document 'Sleep Hygiene Notes Slides' by Kate Talsma provides guidelines for maintaining healthy sleep habits through the concept of sleep hygiene. It emphasizes that beds should be reserved solely for sleeping and not used for activities such as work or screen time, which can disrupt sleep patterns. Creating an ideal environment conducive to restful sleep is also highlighted, including recommendations for a quiet, dark room with comfortable bedding at a suitable temperature, and avoiding screens before bedtime due to their stimulating effects.

A wind-down routine is suggested to prepare the body and mind for sleep through relaxation techniques and limiting mental stimulation from screens or other activities. The document advises caution regarding naps, recommending that they be short and taken early in the day if needed but not as a substitute for nighttime sleep. It also cautions against overreliance on apps for sleep management due to potential drawbacks.

The influence of substances like stimulants and sedatives is mentioned, along with the importance of consistency in sleep habits and managing intrusive thoughts through techniques such as designating specific 'worry time' outside of bedtime routines.
