## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The document excerpts from the book 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Bradley R. Postle, focusing on visual processing and the interplay between different brain regions such as V1, MT, and IT. It discusses how higher-level visual object recognition influences earlier stages of visual processing, emphasizing the hierarchical nature of object recognition. The text also touches upon the ventral visual stream and its anatomical connectivity, highlighting the role of IT cortex in object recognition and the PPC in spatial processing. The chapter explores the concept of 'grandmother cells' and the distributed, combination-coding scheme in IT cortex. It raises questions about the boundaries between perception and memory, and the implications of agnosias and category specificity in ventral temporal cortex. This material is relevant to creative arts and health by providing insights into how visual information is processed and integrated in the brain, which can inform artistic practices and therapies that rely on visual perception.
