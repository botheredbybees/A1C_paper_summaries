## Does music training enhance working memory performance? Findings from a quasi-experimental longitudinal study

The study explores whether instrumental music training enhances working memory performance in primary school-aged children over an 18-month period. The participants were divided into two groups: one receiving weekly instrumental music training and the other receiving enhanced natural science education. Working memory was assessed using Baddeley's model, focusing on phonological loop and central executive components. Results showed significant group by time interactions for the phonological loop and central executive subtests, indicating better development in children engaged in music training compared to those in the control group. These findings support previous research suggesting that music training can enhance specific cognitive functions related to auditory information processing.
