## Week 6: Puppetry in Therapeutic Contexts

This document outlines the content and learning objectives of Week 6 in the FXA303 Creative Arts, Play and Mental Wellbeing course. The focus is on the therapeutic use of puppets to facilitate non-verbal communication and emotional expression. Puppets are highlighted as a tool for promoting joy, communication, and expressive play for both children and adults across various abilities. The week includes videos from Hobart's Terrapin Puppet Theatre showcasing interactions with aged care residents using puppetry techniques. Additionally, the document provides guidance on refining puppet design for movement and expression and emphasizes sustainable practices in puppet making. This week is a split week due to mid-semester break, allowing students time to work on Assessment Task 2 and catch up on Module 2 discussions.
