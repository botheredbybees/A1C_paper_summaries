## Postmodern Death and Dying: A Literary Analysis

This chapter explores the transition from premodern to postmodern conceptions of death and dying through a literary analysis. It highlights the shift from involuntary to voluntary death due to the advent of life-sustaining technologies. The authors discuss the seminal work of Elisabeth Kübler-Ross, 'On Death and Dying', noting its influence despite methodological issues. They argue that Kübler-Ross's work, though not scientifically rigorous, established a therapeutic context for understanding death, marking a modern approach. The chapter questions whether contemporary literature on death and dying offers new insights and considers the difficulty of accurately representing the subjective experience of dying.
