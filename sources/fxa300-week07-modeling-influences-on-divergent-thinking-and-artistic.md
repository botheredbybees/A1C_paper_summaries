## Modeling influences on divergent thinking and artistic creativity

This study explores the impact of modeling on divergent thinking (DT) and artistic creativity among Chinese middle school students. The participants were divided into groups, with half exposed to highly creative models and the other half to no models. The results indicate significant and large modeling effects on both DT and artistic creativity. However, post hoc analyses suggest that the DT effects are primarily restricted to verbal DT tasks. The research highlights the importance of creative environments and role models in fostering creativity and divergent thinking. This study contributes to understanding the social and cognitive factors that influence creative behavior and provides insights for educators and policymakers aiming to enhance creative skills in educational settings.
