## The Meaning in Life Questionnaire

The Meaning in Life Questionnaire (MLQ) is a tool designed by Dr. Michael F. Steger to assess an individual's perception of meaning in life and their search for meaning. The questionnaire consists of a series of statements that the respondent rates on a scale from 'Absolutely Untrue' to 'Absolutely True'. The MLQ evaluates two main dimensions: the presence of meaning in life and the search for meaning in life. The individual's responses provide a quantitative measure of how meaningful their life feels and whether they are actively seeking purpose. In the example provided, the user reports a high score for the presence of meaning in life but a moderate score for the search for meaning, indicating a sense of existing purpose but also ongoing exploration. This tool is relevant in the field of creative arts and health as it can help individuals reflect on their life's purpose and guide them in creating a more fulfilling life through self-care and mindfulness practices.
