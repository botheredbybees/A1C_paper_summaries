## Introduction to Music and the Brain: Earworms and Sad Music

This lesson from FXA300 introduces the concept of earworms - songs that get stuck in your head. It features Elizabeth Hellmuth Margulis's TED-Ed video on earworms and includes a transcript for further study. The lesson also explores why people enjoy listening to sad music, linking it with personality traits such as absorption and empathy. Dr Sandra Garrido's TEDx talk on the topic is referenced, along with research by Zachary Wallmark et al., which examines how empathetic individuals process music differently in the brain compared to others. Key areas of focus include the limbic system, dorsal striatum, and basal ganglia.
