## The communities and materials of repair: collectively reflecting on repair practices of everyday artefacts

This paper explores the practice of repairing everyday objects and the role of materials, tools, and social interactions in this process. The authors used autoethnography to document their lived experiences as design researchers conducting repair workshops from January 2023 to May 2024. Through these activities, they highlight how reflective conversations can foster a shared understanding among research teams and contribute to generating deep insights into the motivations and challenges of repair practices. The study underscores the importance of material engagement in guiding creative processes and fostering sustainable consumption and community bonding.
