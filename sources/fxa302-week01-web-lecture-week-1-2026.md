## Web Lecture Week 1, 2026

This web lecture by A/Prof Anne-Marie Forbes outlines the course requirements and learning strategies for FXA302 - Perspectives of the Arts on Health and Wellbeing. Key aspects include quizzes, a poster project focusing on an artist's response to illness or disability, and discussion board contributions. The lecture emphasizes the interdisciplinary field of health humanities, which integrates perspectives from the humanities, social sciences, and arts to explore health, illness, and medicine beyond patient-centered care. It highlights works such as Timothy P. Schmalz’s 'When I was sick' bronze sculpture and references scholars like Rita Charon, whose narrative medicine program enhances clinicians’ abilities to communicate empathetically with patients by developing skills through literature and creative engagement. The lecture underscores the importance of fostering reflexivity, empathy, and patient-centered care in medical education.
