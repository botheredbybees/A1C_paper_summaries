## Why Does Nature Make You Feel Better?

The transcript from the SciShow Psych video on Nerdfighteria Wiki explores why exposure to nature enhances mental health and well-being. It highlights that while the benefits of natural environments are not new, modern research supports their efficacy for both clinical and non-clinical populations. The article cites several studies indicating that exercising outdoors leads to higher revitalization levels compared to indoor settings, and walking in nature can provide mood boosts and cognitive benefits even for those with depression. Additionally, evidence suggests similar advantages for individuals with schizophrenia and ADHD. The central argument posits that the healing effects of nature are likely linked to a sense of belonging and connection rather than merely physical factors like vitamin D absorption.
