## Art in the Moment: Evaluating a Therapeutic Wellness Program for People with Dementia and their Care Partners

The article discusses the implementation and evaluation of 'Art in the Moment,' a therapeutic wellness program for individuals with Alzheimer’s disease and their caregivers, developed through collaboration between the Art Institute of Chicago and CJE SeniorLife. The initiative aims to provide arts-based therapy by organizing gallery discussions and art-making sessions tailored to participants’ needs. Anecdotal evidence suggested positive outcomes; however, formal evaluation was conducted to assess efficacy. Challenges and opportunities encountered during the program's development are highlighted, emphasizing the benefits of collaboration between museums and healthcare organizations in addressing dementia care through creative arts.
