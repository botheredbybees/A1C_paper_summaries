## FXA302 Perspectives of the Arts on Health and Wellbeing - Week 7 Assignment Week

Week 7 of FXA302 Perspectives of the Arts on Health and Wellbeing is designated as an Assignment Week, focusing on providing students with extra time to complete Assessment Task 2, which is a Poster. There is no new learning content introduced this week, and students are encouraged to review Module 2 material and ensure their notes are up-to-date. Students also have the opportunity to participate in discussion forums before their closure.
