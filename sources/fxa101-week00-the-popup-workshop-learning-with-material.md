## The PopUp Workshop: Learning with Material

The article discusses the PopUp Workshop, a prototyping space that promotes interdisciplinary and experimental pedagogy through hands-on learning. Four key pedagogical approaches are outlined: verification and modification, where students test theoretical concepts in full-scale realizations; learning through play, which involves discovering design principles through playful exercises; thinking with materials, focusing on the interaction between designer and material to drive creative research; and design, build, inhabit, emphasizing practical experience in public spaces. The workshop aims to enhance sensitivity towards materiality and collective work.
