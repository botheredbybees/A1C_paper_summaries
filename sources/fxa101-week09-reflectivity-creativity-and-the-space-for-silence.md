## Reflectivity, Creativity, and the Space for Silence

Jane Dawson's paper explores the relationship between reflection, creativity, and the necessity of silence in fostering critical thinking and self-knowledge. The author draws from personal experience with meditation to argue that while educational discourse emphasizes critical reflection and creativity, practical educational settings often lack the 'space for silence' necessary for these processes to flourish. Dawson highlights the importance of contemplative practices like meditation in creating the mental space that allows for deeper thought and creative expression. The paper critiques the prevalence of noise and haste in contemporary academic life, which inhibits the conditions needed for meaningful reflection and creative activity. Through this analysis, Dawson underscores the critical role of silence in enhancing both intellectual and artistic pursuits, challenging educators to consider how they can create environments that encourage such reflective and creative practices.
