## A Vest That Can Cure Deafness, and Maybe One Day Give Us Entirely New Senses

This article discusses a haptic vest prototype developed by Neuroscientist David Eagleman and his company Neosensory. The VEST (Versatile Extra-Sensory Transducer) translates audio input into vibratory signals that can be interpreted by the brain in place of auditory information, potentially curing deafness or expanding sensory experiences. It explores philosophical questions around consciousness, ethics, and the potential for creating new senses through technological means. Eagleman suggests that this technology could enable humans to perceive imperceptible environmental data, such as stock market fluctuations, leading to profound implications on human experience.
