## How to Waste a Break: Using Portable Electronic Devices Substantially Counteracts Attention Enhancement Effects of Green Spaces

The study examines the interaction between portable electronic device use and exposure to green spaces in relation to attentional functioning. It involved 81 participants who were randomly assigned to rest treatments including green and barren settings with or without a laptop computer, and their attention was measured three times. Results indicated that using an electronic device in green spaces counteracts the beneficial effects of nature on enhancing attention. This finding highlights potential negative impacts on cognitive restoration when people use devices in natural environments instead of disconnecting from technology to benefit cognitively.
