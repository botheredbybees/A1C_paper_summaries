## Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience

The document 'Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience' by Postle (2015) explores the concept of Theory of Mind (ToM) and its neural underpinnings. ToM is a critical aspect of social cognition, enabling individuals to understand others' mental states, intentions, and behaviors. The literature discusses the neural networks involved in ToM, emphasizing the role of the anterior paracingulate cortex, posterior superior temporal sulcus, and temporoparietal junction (TPJ). Neuroimaging studies reveal that the TPJ is particularly active during ToM tasks, suggesting a crucial role in mentalizing. The discussion also highlights the relevance of ToM impairments in conditions like autism spectrum disorder (ASD), indicating the importance of ToM for social interactions and communication in health and well-being contexts.
