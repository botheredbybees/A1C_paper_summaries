## More Body: A Performance for Five (or More) Bodies

Gretchen A. Case's text 'More Body' presents a performance script designed to explore the concept of the body through interactive engagement. The performance involves five participants who interpret and enact a series of scenes that question and challenge conventional understandings of bodily experience and control. Each scene builds in complexity, moving from initial reluctance to more intricate explorations of bodily agency and expression. Through these performances, participants are encouraged to reflect on how their bodies interact with space, other bodies, and the environment, thereby questioning the nature and boundaries of the body. The text draws inspiration from scholars like Barbara Browning, Judith Butler, and Michel Foucault, who have contributed to contemporary debates about the body's role in social and cultural contexts. This work is relevant to creative arts and health by highlighting the body as a site of social and cultural negotiation, inviting performers and audiences to reconsider the dynamic and fluid nature of the body within a performative context.
