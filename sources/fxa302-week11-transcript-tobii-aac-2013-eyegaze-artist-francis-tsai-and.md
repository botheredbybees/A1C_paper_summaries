## Francis Tsai's Journey with Tobii PCEye

The transcript discusses Francis Tsai, an artist who uses the Tobii PCEye eye gaze technology to continue his artistic career despite physical limitations. Initially, Tsai's hands weakened, making it difficult for him to use traditional drawing tools. He adapted to using a mouse, but eventually, even that became too challenging. After receiving the Tobii PCEye, Tsai was able to produce art again, posting on social media and selling products like t-shirts and posters through an online store. The eye gaze technology enabled Tsai to communicate and engage with others in a meaningful way, providing a sense of freedom and connectivity that was previously impossible.
