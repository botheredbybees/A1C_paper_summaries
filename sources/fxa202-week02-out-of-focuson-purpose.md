## Out of Focus—On Purpose

Jim Richardson's article discusses the intentional use of out-of-focus techniques in photography to enhance visual storytelling and highlight subjects by softening backgrounds. He explains that while controlling aperture (f-stop) is a common method, other factors such as proximity to the subject and the relative distance between the subject and background also significantly influence depth of field. Richardson emphasizes getting close to the main subject and positioning oneself lower to achieve greater blurring effects on the background. Additionally, he suggests using larger cameras with longer lenses for better control over out-of-focus elements. He advises against representing manipulated images as non-fiction, highlighting the importance of ethical considerations in creative photography.
