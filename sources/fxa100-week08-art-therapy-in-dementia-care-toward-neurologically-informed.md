## Art Therapy in Dementia Care: Toward Neurologically Informed, Evidence-Based Practice

The article discusses the potential of art therapy to improve psychological well-being and quality of life for individuals with dementia, while addressing neuropsychiatric symptoms (NPS) that are common during the progression of the disease. The author highlights the increasing interest in nonpharmacological approaches such as art therapy due to the limitations and adverse effects associated with pharmacological interventions like neuroleptics. Despite this growing interest, there is a need for more rigorous research studies to establish strong evidence supporting the effectiveness of art therapy among diverse dementia populations. Existing literature includes case studies and small clinical trials suggesting positive outcomes from art therapy sessions, such as reduced stress-related behaviors and improved NPS. The article emphasizes the importance of developing neurologically informed art therapy practices that adhere to evidence-based standards, which is vital for advancing the discipline's credibility and effectiveness in dementia care.
