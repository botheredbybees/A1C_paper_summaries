## Association Between Haiku Poetry Creation and Self-Transcendent Emotions

This study examines the relationship between creating haiku poetry and experiencing self-transcendent emotions like gratitude for serenity and awe. Using a Bayesian implementation of Generalized Linear Mixed Models, researchers compared responses from 192 haiku writers to 177 non-writers on scales measuring gratitude for serenity, general gratitude, and trait awe. Results indicated that haiku writing significantly increased the frequency of gratitude for serenity and awe, independent of interest in art or educational level. The study suggests that engaging in regular haiku creation fosters unique emotional perspectives and experiences.
