## Automatic species identiﬁcation from images for Aotearoa

This paper presents neural network-based image classification models for automatic species identification in New Zealand, using data from iNaturalist. The authors trained EfficientNetV2 models on a dataset of 14,991 species, including animals, plants, and fungi, achieving over 76% accuracy across all classes. Temperature scaling calibrated the probability estimates to gauge model confidence. These models are publicly available as downloadable files or through web/mobile applications for both scientific research and public engagement in conservation efforts.
