## Interdisciplinarity for Social Justice Enterprise: Intersecting Education, Industry and Community Arts Perspectives

This article explores the role of interdisciplinarity in achieving transformative learning outcomes, particularly in the context of Tasmania, which faces significant educational challenges exacerbated by socio-economic disparities and the impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic. The authors examine interdisciplinary collaborations between education, creative industries, and community arts practices within two case studies from Tasmania. They argue that traditional disciplinary approaches are insufficient for addressing complex societal problems like those arising during crises such as the pandemic. Through a focus on social justice imperatives, the article illustrates how interdisciplinarity can enable more effective responses to socio-economic challenges, fostering innovation and resilience in communities.
