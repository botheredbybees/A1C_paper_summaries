## Creative health: the arts for health and wellbeing

Lord Howarth of Newport, Co-Chair of the All-Party Parliamentary Group (APPG) on Arts, Health and Wellbeing, discusses recommendations from an inquiry into arts practices in health and social care. The inquiry highlighted growing momentum within the arts and health movement, emphasizing that the arts can contribute to keeping people healthy, aiding recovery, saving costs, and addressing challenges such as aging populations and mental health issues. Key findings include the need for a strategic center for arts, health, and wellbeing at the national level, culture change in recognizing the value of arts for health improvement, and the importance of cross-sector collaboration and international dialogue to enhance arts-based initiatives. The report calls for leadership that transcends conventional boundaries to promote a more holistic approach to healthcare.
