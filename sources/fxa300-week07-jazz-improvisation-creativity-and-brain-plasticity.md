## Jazz Improvisation, Creativity, and Brain Plasticity

In this commentary, Hugues Duffau discusses the case of a jazz guitarist who underwent a left temporal lobectomy for an arteriovenous malformation (AVM). Despite severe cognitive and musical deficits post-surgery, the patient eventually recovered his musical abilities and virtuoso status. Duffau emphasizes the brain's neuroplasticity and its ability to reorganize neural networks following injury. The commentary highlights the role of jazz improvisation in brain reorganization and its potential therapeutic benefits in music therapy for neurologically impaired individuals. The author also explores the implications of jazz improvisation for understanding the neural mechanisms underlying high-order functions such as semantic processing and creativity. Overall, the case report underscores the brain's remarkable capacity for reorganization and the potential for novel therapeutic approaches in neurological rehabilitation.
