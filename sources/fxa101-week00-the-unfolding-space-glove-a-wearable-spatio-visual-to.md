## The Unfolding Space Glove: A Wearable Spatio-Visual to Haptic Sensory Substitution Device for Blind People

This paper describes the design and evaluation of a wearable haptic sensory substitution device called the Unfolding Space Glove, aimed at assisting blind people in navigating their environment. The glove translates spatial information from its surroundings into vibratory feedback on the user's hand, enabling tactile exploration of space depth. It is portable, works under all lighting conditions, and does not require external hardware. The prototype was tested by both blind and sighted participants who successfully used it for object recognition and wayfinding tasks though still performed better with a traditional white cane.
