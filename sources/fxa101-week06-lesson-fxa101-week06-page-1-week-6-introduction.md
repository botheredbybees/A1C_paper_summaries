## Introduction to Week 6: Developing Creativity Through Experimentation

Week 6 of the FXA101 Creativity and Ageing course focuses on enhancing creativity through experimentation with techniques and materials for a mini-project. The session emphasizes looking for inspiration from various sources, such as nature or artistic works by others. Journalling is encouraged to document idea development and creative process. Students are tasked with conducting research on creative models, solving practical problems under constraints, developing a concept, and critically reflecting on their work. The lesson also addresses the issue of creative block by suggesting strategies like disciplined daily practice and exploring outside one's comfort zone. References include Byron et al., 2010; Resnick, 2017.
