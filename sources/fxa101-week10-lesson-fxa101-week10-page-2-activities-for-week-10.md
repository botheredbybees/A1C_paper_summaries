## FXA101 Creativity and Ageing - Week 10 Activities

This lesson for FXA101 Creativity and Ageing focuses on the interplay of light and shadow in art and photography. Students are assigned to read 'Light and Shadow' from Phillipa Stanton's Conscious Creativity and Simon Unwin's book on shadow architecture. The activities include finding a professional artwork that uses light and shadow effectively, and creating two photographs of the same object under different lighting conditions to observe the impact on mood and perception. Students then discuss their findings and reflect on the use of chiaroscuro in art forms other than painting, supporting their points with scholarly references.
