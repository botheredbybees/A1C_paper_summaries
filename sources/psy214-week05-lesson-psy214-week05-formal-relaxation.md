## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - Formal Relaxation

The lesson explores formal relaxation techniques designed to aid stress reduction over time. It highlights the physiological mechanisms of the 'relaxation response' and contrasts it with the 'stress response.' Key methods include deep diaphragmatic breathing (DDB) and progressive muscle relaxation (PMR). The vagus nerve's role in stress regulation is explained, emphasizing controlled breathing exercises to enhance its activity. DDB involves slow, deep breaths engaging the diaphragm, while PMR focuses on sequential tightening and relaxing of muscles. Both techniques are supported by research for managing stress, anxiety, and enhancing relaxation levels.
