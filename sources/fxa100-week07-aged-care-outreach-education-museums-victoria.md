## Aged care outreach | Education | Museums Victoria

The webpage 'Aged care outreach' by Museums Victoria provides a comprehensive overview of reminiscing kits designed specifically for aged care settings. These kits aim to evoke nostalgic memories through a variety of themed objects and resources, such as music, sports, and childhood games. Each kit is tailored to specific themes like rock 'n' roll, dance halls, and transport and travel, encouraging older adults to engage in reminiscing sessions. The kits are intended to be used by activity coordinators to facilitate social interaction and stimulate mental engagement among elderly participants. This approach not only enhances the quality of life for individuals in aged care but also fosters a sense of community and connection to cultural heritage. The initiative underscores the importance of leveraging cultural resources to support the well-being of older adults, particularly in the context of dementia care and mental health.
