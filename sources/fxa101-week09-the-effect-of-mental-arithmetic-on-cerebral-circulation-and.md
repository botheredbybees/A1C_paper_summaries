## The Effect of Mental Arithmetic on Cerebral Circulation and Metabolism

This study investigates the impact of mental arithmetic on cerebral circulation and metabolism in healthy young men. The researchers used the nitrous oxide technique to measure cerebral blood flow and oxygen consumption. Subjects were asked to solve mental arithmetic problems while cerebral functions were measured. The results showed significant increases in mean arterial blood pressure and pulse rate during mental arithmetic tasks. The study also found that mental activity correlates with an increased cerebral oxygen consumption, suggesting that conscious mental processes require energy derived from oxidative processes. This research contributes to the understanding of how mental activities influence brain physiology, which is relevant to creative arts and health by providing insights into the biological underpinnings of cognitive functions.
