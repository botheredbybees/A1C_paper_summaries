## The Forgotten Legacy of Schön: From Materials to “Mediums” in the Design Activity

This article explores the legacy of Donald Schön’s work on design practice, focusing specifically on his concept of materials in the reflective conversation model. The authors argue that while Schön introduced the idea that materials ‘talk back’ to designers, current discussions suggest a need for rethinking what constitutes a material in contemporary design contexts. They propose the concept of ‘mediums,’ derived from Dewey’s theory, as an alternative framework to better encapsulate both physical and social dimensions of materials used in design. The paper reviews Schön’s dialogic model of design activity, emphasizing iterative processes like 'seeing-moving-seeing' that shape a designer's understanding and interaction with materials. It highlights the limitations of considering materials purely from their physical properties and advocates for a broader perspective that includes cultural, ethical, and aesthetic aspects of mediums in design practice.
