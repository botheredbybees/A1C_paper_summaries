## NSW Health and Wellbeing Strategy: Museums & Galleries of NSW Submission

The report by Museums & Galleries of New South Wales (M&G NSW) outlines the critical role of arts and culture in enhancing mental health and well-being. It advocates for integrating evidence-based initiatives such as Arts on Prescription and Culture Dose for Kids into the upcoming NSW Mental Health and Wellbeing Strategy. These programs have demonstrated measurable benefits, including reduced anxiety levels and improved social connectedness among participants. However, M&G NSW highlights the current financial strain faced by cultural institutions due to funding cuts, which threatens the continuation of these essential community-focused initiatives. The report underscores the need for policy recognition and sustainable co-funding between health and arts sectors to ensure long-term success and scalability of such programs.
