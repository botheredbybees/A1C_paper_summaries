## Initiating and continuing participation in citizen science for natural history

This paper explores the motivations behind citizen participation in natural history through the Open Air Laboratories (OPAL) program, which is a significant biodiversity monitoring and engagement project in England. It outlines a continuum of participant engagement from previously unengaged to casually engaged and already engaged individuals. The study emphasizes the role of amateur naturalists and discusses how OPAL activities provide opportunities for both casual leisure and serious involvement in citizen science projects. Findings indicate that committed amateurs find value in contributing to OPAL, while new participants gain confidence and validation through their engagement.
