## Quiet and Creativity

This lesson explores the relationship between quietness and creativity, emphasizing the importance of the Default Mode Network (DMN) for creative thinking. The DMN is active during periods of rest and daydreaming, facilitating processes like recollection, future planning, and novel idea generation. Research indicates that the DMN, when engaged, can enhance creativity by drawing on semantic networks and past experiences to form new associations. The lesson also critiques the notion of 'groupthink' and highlights the benefits of solitude for creative individuals, suggesting that quiet spaces are crucial for fostering creativity and innovation. It concludes with practical tips for creating a conducive environment for creative thinking.
