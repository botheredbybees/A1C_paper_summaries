## FXA303 Web Lecture 5 Week 5 2026

This document outlines the upcoming schedule and activities for FXA303 in Week 5 of 2026. It includes deadlines for module discussions, quizzes, and assignments such as AT2 Puppet as Research Response due on April 20th. The lecture emphasizes the use of puppets in therapeutic contexts, highlighting their ability to facilitate self-expression, reveal subconscious thoughts, provide cathartic release, teach key concepts, enable abreaction, promote awareness of feelings, develop rapport, aid problem-solving, and prepare for stressful events. It also provides guidance on creating a puppet that can express emotional responses through gestures and incorporates recycled materials, adhering to time constraints. The document includes references to various types of puppets and mechanisms, such as rod puppets, hand-controlled puppets, and string puppets (marionettes), offering resources for their construction.
