## The Social and Applied Psychology of Music

The chapter by Adrian North and David Hargreaves presents a significant shift in the fundamental paradigm of music psychology towards a greater emphasis on the social and applied aspects of music. It discusses how the field now integrates social and applied topics, reflecting the influence of naturalistic methodologies and the digital revolution. The authors highlight the evolution of music consumption, production, and the role of technology in reshaping musical behavior. They also emphasize the importance of considering the social context in which music is created, performed, and perceived. The chapter explores research on musicianship, musical taste, and the role of music in adolescent subcultures and health. It underscores the interconnectedness of various psychological processes and the need for interdisciplinary approaches in studying music.
