## The Use of Music Therapy in the Treatment of Mental Illness and the Enhancement of Societal Wellbeing

This article discusses the application of Music Therapy (MT) in addressing various mental health issues and enhancing social cohesion. MT is defined as a therapeutic approach that uses music to improve mental health, involving active engagement rather than passive listening alone. The authors review evidence on its efficacy in treating depression, anxiety, schizophrenia, sleep disorders, and dementia. They highlight the role of singing and musical participation in improving mental health outcomes. Research indicates that structured music therapy sessions can reduce symptoms such as anxiety and depression, improve sleep quality, and enhance overall well-being. While MT is found to be beneficial, it should be seen as an adjunct to standard care treatments for mental illness.
