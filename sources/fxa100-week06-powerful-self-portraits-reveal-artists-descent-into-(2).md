## Powerful Self-Portraits Reveal Artist's Descent Into Alzheimer's Disease

William Utermohlen, an American artist diagnosed with Alzheimer's at the age of 61 in 1995, embarked on a series of self-portraits to document his journey through dementia. These portraits vividly depict his deteriorating condition as they evolve from detailed and expressive works into increasingly abstract representations, reflecting the loss of memory and spatial sense characteristic of advanced stages of Alzheimer's. Utermohlen’s widow, Patricia, describes how he continued drawing until it was no longer possible for him to do so in 2000, marking a significant turning point before his death in 2007. This series not only serves as an intimate record of personal struggle but also offers profound insights into the psychological and creative impacts of neurodegenerative diseases on artists.
