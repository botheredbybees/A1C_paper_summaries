## Compassion: context and concepts

This lesson explores the concept of compassion within the context of mindfulness practice and its role in fostering prosocial behavior and altruism. It distinguishes between empathy and compassion, noting that compassion includes both an awareness of suffering and a motivation to alleviate it, whereas empathy involves understanding and sharing another's emotional experience. The lesson also discusses various motivations for altruistic behavior, including empathy, reciprocal altruism, and social exchange theory. It highlights the ethical importance of compassion in professional and institutional settings, and introduces models of compassion and self-compassion developed by Kristen Neff for psychological research and practice.
