## Effect of Kindness-Based Meditation on Health and Well-Being: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis

This study conducted a systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) to evaluate the effects of kindness-based meditation (KBM) on health and well-being. The research, involving 22 studies, found that KBM significantly reduced self-reported depression and enhanced mindfulness, compassion, self-compassion, and positive emotions. The meta-analysis indicated that KBM is moderately effective against passive control groups but the results were inconclusive when compared to active controls. The study concluded that KBM holds promise for improving individual and community well-being and suggested that further research, including well-designed large RCTs, is needed to better understand its long-term effects.
