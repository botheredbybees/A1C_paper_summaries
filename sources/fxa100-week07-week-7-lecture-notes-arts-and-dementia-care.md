## Week 7 Lecture Notes - Arts and Dementia Care

This document contains the lecture notes for Week 7 of the course 'FXA100: The Arts and Dementia Care.' Dr. Brendan Lamb outlines housekeeping details, including feedback on assessment tasks and APA referencing guidelines. Key content areas include discussion of nationally renowned gallery and museum programs designed for people with dementia, such as ARTZ and the Meet Me at MoMA program. These initiatives emphasize the importance of reminiscence, creative interaction, and personalized engagement to enhance quality of life and cognitive well-being. The lecture also provides a checklist guide for planning visits to galleries and museums tailored to individuals with dementia, focusing on accessibility, interest-driven content, and supportive environments. Additionally, it prompts reflection on Baines' (2007) concepts concerning potential beyond problems in dementia care, encouraging students to consider their approach to balancing cognitive versus emotional aspects of dementia support.
