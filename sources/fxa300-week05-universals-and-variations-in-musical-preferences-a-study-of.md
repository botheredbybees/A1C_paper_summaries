## Universals and Variations in Musical Preferences: A Study of Preferential Reactions to Western Music in 53 Countries

The study investigates the universality of musical preferences across different cultures by examining reactions to Western music in 53 countries and through audio-based assessments among 36 countries. The research finds consistent patterns in musical preferences, organized into five latent factors: Mellow, Unpretentious, Sophisticated, Intense, and Contemporary, suggesting a universal structure in preference for Western music irrespective of cultural boundaries. Correlations between personality traits (like Extraversion and Openness) and musical styles were also largely consistent across the studied populations. The findings support the idea that there are commonalities in how people around the world perceive and relate to music, which provides foundational insights into understanding the interplay between music, psychology, biology, and culture.
