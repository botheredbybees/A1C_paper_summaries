## Youth Survey Report 2024

The 2024 Youth Survey Report by Mission Australia provides insights into the challenges and opportunities facing young Australians aged 15 to 19. The report, based on responses from 17,480 participants, highlights that the cost of living is the top national concern for youth, more than doubling in importance over the past two years. Climate change remains a significant issue but ranks second due to the immediate financial hardships many young people face. Violence, safety and crime have risen in prominence, reflecting broader societal concerns about domestic violence and gender-based violence. Mental health continues to be critical with high rates of stress, anxiety, loneliness, and depression reported. Young people struggle with barriers such as motivation and confidence when pursuing education and employment goals. The report underscores the need for greater support for marginalized groups like Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander youth and those identifying as gender diverse, who experience higher levels of discrimination and inequality.
