## Artistic Creativity in Neurological Illnesses

This lesson focuses on the impact of neurological disorders on artistic creativity. It explores how neurological diseases like stroke, epilepsy, Alzheimer's Disease, and Multiple Sclerosis affect physical ability, cognitive processes, and perception of self and the external world. Through case studies of professional artists affected by these conditions, the lesson examines the adaptive strategies employed by artists and the role of the creative arts in rehabilitation, physical therapy, and enhancing health, wellbeing, and resilience. The learning objectives include understanding the symptoms and effects of various neurological conditions, contextualizing the lived experiences of creative and performing artists, and reflecting on how creativity influences wellbeing and coping strategies for those with neurological illnesses.
