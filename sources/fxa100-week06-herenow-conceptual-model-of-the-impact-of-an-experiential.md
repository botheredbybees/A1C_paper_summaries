## here:now – Conceptual model of the impact of an experiential arts program on persons with dementia and their care partners

The study explores the impact of a museum-based experiential arts program, here:now, on persons with dementia and their care partners. Using qualitative methods, including in-depth interviews, the research evaluates perceptions and experiences related to gallery tours and art classes. The findings highlight positive outcomes such as high engagement, mindfulness, social connection, and improved quality of life. Key components of the program include the museum space itself, facilitation processes, and interactions with other participants. This study provides a conceptual model for assessing arts programming benefits in dementia care, emphasizing the unique role museums can play in supporting these individuals.
