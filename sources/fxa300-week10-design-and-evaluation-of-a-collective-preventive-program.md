## Design and Evaluation of a Collective Preventive Program for Musical Performance Anxiety (ConﬁDance)

The article discusses the development and evaluation of a collective preventive program for musical performance anxiety (ConﬁDance) designed to assist professional musicians in training. The program was developed based on existing research and qualitative insights from focus groups. A pilot study was conducted with 17 professional musicians in classical music performance training, using a quasi-experimental design with pre-post and one-year follow-up assessments. Results indicated a significant decrease in MPA after the program implementation, with sustained improvement one year later. This research highlights the potential of preventive programs in mitigating MPA and improving the mental health and performance quality of musicians, emphasizing the need for further investigation to refine such programs. The article underscores the importance of addressing MPA as a multifaceted issue requiring both psychological and educational interventions.
