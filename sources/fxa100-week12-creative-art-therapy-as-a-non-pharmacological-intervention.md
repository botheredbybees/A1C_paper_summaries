## Creative Art Therapy as a Non-Pharmacological Intervention for Dementia: A Systematic Review

This systematic review evaluates the efficacy of art therapy as a non-pharmacological intervention for dementia, focusing on its impact on wellbeing, quality of life, and reduction of behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD). The study analyzed seventeen research articles published between 2015 and 2020 involving 853 participants. Key findings indicate that art therapy is beneficial for people with dementia, enhancing communication and fostering person-centered therapeutic activities. Eighty-eight percent of the studies reported significant outcomes in one or more domains, including wellbeing, quality of life, and BPSD reduction. The review underscores the importance of incorporating elements of 'being in the moment' to maximize the effectiveness of art therapy interventions.
