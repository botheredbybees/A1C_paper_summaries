## Do-It-Yourself Barbie in 1960s Australia

The article explores the DIY culture surrounding Barbie dolls in Australia during the mid-1960s, highlighting how women and girls created homemade clothing and accessories for their dolls despite the availability of mass-produced items. It discusses the economic benefits and creative expression associated with DIY practices, focusing on articles from 'The Australian Women’s Weekly' that provided patterns and instructions for making Barbie outfits using household materials or craft kits. The article also examines historical contexts where women engaged in DIY activities as a means to express personal identity and navigate economic constraints.
