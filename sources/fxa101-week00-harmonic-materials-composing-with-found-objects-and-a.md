## Harmonic Materials: Composing with found objects and a camera-based synthesiser

This paper explores the compositional process using a camera-based synthesiser called the pattern organ through a materialist and bricolage approach. The authors investigate how physical materials can influence sound production in digital musical instruments, focusing on the interplay between player, instrument, environment, and materials. They use field notes, video documentation, and graphic scores to provide a detailed account of creating Harmonic Materials #1, emphasizing indeterminacy and emergent structure. Key concepts like material drift, distributed cognition (4Es), and inherent scores are discussed.
