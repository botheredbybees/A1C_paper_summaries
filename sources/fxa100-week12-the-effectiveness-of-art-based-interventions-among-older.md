## The effectiveness of art-based interventions among older adults with dementia: A systematic review and meta-analysis

This systematic review and meta-analysis evaluates the effectiveness of art-based interventions among older adults with dementia, focusing on cognitive function, neuropsychiatric symptoms (NPS), quality of life (QoL), anxiety, depression, agitation, and social interaction. The study synthesizes evidence from 26 randomized controlled trials and finds that art-based interventions are associated with improved global cognition and overall NPS but have no significant impact on QoL or specific symptom domains such as anxiety and agitation. Depression shows a slight improvement, while other symptoms remain unchanged. These findings suggest potential cognitive and neuropsychiatric benefits of art-based interventions for dementia care, highlighting the need for larger-scale studies to confirm these outcomes.
