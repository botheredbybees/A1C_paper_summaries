## Music Through the Ages: Trends in Musical Engagement and Preferences

This study explores developmental trends in musical engagement and preferences across different age groups, from adolescence to middle adulthood. Using data from over a quarter of a million individuals, the research found that while younger people attribute greater importance to music and listen to it more frequently compared to older adults, music remains significant for adults but often in private contexts. Preferences for certain types of music also evolve with age; dimensions like 'Intense' and 'Contemporary' decrease as people get older, whereas preferences for 'Unpretentious' and 'Sophisticated' increase. These trends correlate closely with personality development. The findings highlight the psychological basis of musical engagement and preferences across life stages.
