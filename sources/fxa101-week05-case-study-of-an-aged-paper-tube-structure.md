## Case study of an aged paper tube structure

The study presents a case analysis of an aged paper tube structure used in the Takatori Catholic Church, Japan, which has been operational for over 10 years and is now functioning as a temporary building material. The research evaluates the mechanical properties of these structures through various tests such as compressive strength, axial tensile, flat crush compression, hardness, and bending strength. Findings indicate that despite aging, the paper tubes retain significant structural integrity with minimal differences in hardness values and elastic modulus compared to new ones. This suggests that aged paper tube structures can continue to be utilized safely for extended periods, possibly up to 25 years, contingent on regular inspections and maintenance. The study highlights the potential of paper-based materials as sustainable building solutions while addressing practical concerns about their long-term durability.
