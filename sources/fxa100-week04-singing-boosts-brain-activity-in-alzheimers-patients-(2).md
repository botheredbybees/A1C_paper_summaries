## Singing boosts brain activity in Alzheimer’s patients, scientists say

A study presented at the Society for Neuroscience meeting in San Diego demonstrated that singing in group music sessions significantly improves cognitive abilities and life satisfaction among patients with moderate to severe dementia. Researchers observed two groups of Alzheimer’s patients over four months: one engaged in singing activities while the other listened passively to the same songs. Post-intervention testing revealed that the active singing participants showed marked improvements in cognitive function compared to those who merely listened, indicating that music engagement can positively influence brain activity and quality of life for dementia sufferers.
