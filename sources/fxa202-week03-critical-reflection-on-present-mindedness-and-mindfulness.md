## Critical Reflection on Present Mindedness and Mindfulness Practices

Grace Rosevear's critical reflection discusses the benefits of present mindedness and mindfulness practices, emphasizing their role in reducing anxiety and fostering a sense of belonging. Drawing from research by Ackerman (2021) and Schutte & Malouff (2018), Rosevear highlights how these practices can be applied to address increasing rates of anxiety and social isolation. She explores her photo essay project that aims to inspire viewers to contemplate the connections between human bodies and nature, promoting a deeper sense of presence and reflection. The essay reflects on the effectiveness of using visual and written content to evoke contemplation and belonging, suggesting these practices have broader implications for mental health and environmental awareness.
