## The effects of music therapy for older people with dementia

This literature review explores the impact of music therapy on the behavior of older individuals with dementia. The authors, Wall and Duffy, examine studies from 2003 to 2009, focusing on the effects of music therapy on agitation, mood, and socialization skills in dementia patients. The review highlights that music therapy has been shown to reduce agitation levels and improve emotional well-being and social interactions in elderly patients with dementia. However, the authors note methodological limitations in the reviewed studies and call for further research to validate these findings. The review emphasizes the potential of music therapy as a holistic tool to enhance caregiver-client relationships and overall quality of life for dementia patients.
