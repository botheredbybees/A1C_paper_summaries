## Individuality of movements in music – Finger and body movements during playing of the flute

The study by Albrecht et al. (2014) investigates individual movement patterns in skilled flute players, focusing on finger and body movements during the performance of Mozart’s Flute Concerto No. 2. Six semiprofessional and four professional musicians were observed over three different days as they played the same piece multiple times. The researchers used 3D motion capture technology to record finger and body movements, which were then analyzed using linear and nonlinear classification methods. Results showed that finger movements could identify individuals with up to 100% accuracy, while body movements identified individuals with up to 94% accuracy. These findings challenge traditional teaching methods for wind instruments that emphasize minimalistic and efficient movement patterns. The study highlights the unique individuality of motor skills even among highly skilled performers, suggesting that personal adaptations should be considered in the learning process.
