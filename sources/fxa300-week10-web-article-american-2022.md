## Performance Anxiety

Performance anxiety is a psychological state characterized by excessive fear or worry related to performing in front of others. It can manifest in various contexts such as public speaking, sports, and artistic performances. Symptoms include physical symptoms like sweating and trembling, as well as psychological symptoms such as fear of embarrassment or failure. The entry provides a concise definition and context for understanding this common psychological phenomenon.
