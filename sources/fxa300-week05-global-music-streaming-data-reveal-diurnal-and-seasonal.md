## Global music streaming data reveal diurnal and seasonal patterns of affective preference

This study examines the diurnal and seasonal patterns of affective preference through global music streaming data, analyzing over 765 million plays by one million individuals across 51 countries using Spotify. It identifies consistent daily and seasonal trends in musical choices that reflect emotional regulation strategies. Individuals tend to listen to more relaxing music late at night and more energetic music during daytime hours. Younger people generally prefer more intense music than older groups, while geographic regions like Latin America and Asia show distinct preferences. Seasonal patterns correlate with day length rather than changes in daylight hours. The research underscores how musical choices both influence and mirror emotional states, offering insights into universal human behaviors across diverse cultures.
