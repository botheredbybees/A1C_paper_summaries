## Introduction to Sociology 3e

The 'Introduction to Sociology 3e' is an educational resource published by OpenStax, a non-profit initiative of Rice University. Authored by Conerly, Holmes, and Tamang, this textbook covers various foundational topics in sociology such as the history of sociology, theoretical perspectives, research methods, culture, social interaction, socialization, groups and organizations, deviance, media and technology, social stratification, global inequality, race and ethnicity, gender, aging, family, and religion. The content is designed to introduce students to the discipline of sociology and its application in understanding social phenomena. This book provides a comprehensive overview of sociological theories and concepts, emphasizing the importance of critical thinking and empirical research. It serves as an essential tool for understanding how societies are structured and how social processes influence individual behavior and group dynamics. The book also addresses the ethical considerations in sociological research and promotes the use of technology and open educational resources to enhance learning experiences.
