## Woven Identity: The Evolution and Cultural Significance of Santali Textile Traditions

This paper explores the historical trajectory of Santali weaving traditions in Eastern India, tracing their evolution from a self-sufficient practice integrated into local ecology to near extinction under modernization pressures. It contrasts this decline with contemporary revival efforts led by grassroots organizations that emphasize women's economic empowerment and sustainable production methods. Through ethnographic data points and case studies like designer Shakuntala Marndi's personal history and the Mauna Dhwani Foundation's initiatives, the research highlights how traditional textiles serve as a semiotic system for cultural identity, social structure, and collective memory. The paper emphasizes the resilience of Santali textile traditions in adapting to globalized contexts while preserving ancestral knowledge.
