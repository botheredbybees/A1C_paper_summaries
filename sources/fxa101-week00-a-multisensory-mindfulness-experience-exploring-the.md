## A Multisensory Mindfulness Experience: Exploring the Promotion of Sensory Awareness as a Mindfulness Practice

This paper presents a preliminary and multidisciplinary study that explores the enhancement of mindfulness through multisensory experiences designed to mimic a 'beginner's mind.' The research assesses anxiety levels and heart rate variability before and after two conditions: guided audio mindfulness practice and an experience with successive olfactory, auditory, visual stimulation followed by interactive biofeedback. Results show increased parasympathetic activity in both groups, indicating physiological relaxation beyond reported psychological state changes.
