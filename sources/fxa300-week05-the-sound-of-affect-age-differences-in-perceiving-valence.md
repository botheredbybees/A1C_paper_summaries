## The sound of affect: Age differences in perceiving valence and arousal in music and their relation to music characteristics and momentary mood

The study explores age-related differences in the perception of valence and arousal within music compared to sounds, examining how these perceptions are influenced by musical characteristics and momentary mood. The research indicates significant differences in affective perceptions of music between various age groups but not for non-musical sounds. Musical features and current emotional states were found to play a role in shaping individuals' interpretations of the affective qualities in music, highlighting the importance of these factors in experimental designs that utilize musical stimuli across different ages.
