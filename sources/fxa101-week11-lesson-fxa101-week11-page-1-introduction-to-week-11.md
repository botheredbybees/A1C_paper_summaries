## Introduction to Week 11 - FXA101 Creativity and Ageing

This lesson introduces the final week of formal learning in the course FXA101 Creativity and Ageing, focusing on the concept of reflection in the visual and creative arts. It explores how reflective surfaces are used in artworks to provide interactive dimensions and symbolic reflections, exemplified through Bert Flugelman's stainless steel sculpture 'Spheres' in Adelaide's Rundle Mall. The lesson also delves into the physics of reflections, the symbolism of mirrors, and their use in conveying meaning and illusion in art. It includes discussions on 'The Arnolfini Portrait' by Jan van Eyck and Yayoi Kusama's infinity mirror installations. The content aims to prepare students for their critical reflection and appraisal of their creative processes and outcomes.
