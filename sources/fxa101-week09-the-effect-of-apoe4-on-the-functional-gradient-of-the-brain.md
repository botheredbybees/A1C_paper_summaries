## The effect of APOEε4 on the functional gradient of the brain default network in Alzheimer’s disease and its relationship with cognitive functions

This study investigates the impact of the APOEε4 allele on the functional connectivity gradient of the default mode network (DMN) in Alzheimer's disease (AD) and its correlation with cognitive functions. From September 2022 to September 2024, the researchers recruited 271 subjects, including healthy controls and AD patients, who underwent cognitive function testing, blood tests for AD biomarkers, and resting-state functional magnetic resonance imaging (rs-fMRI). The analysis showed a significant interaction between the presence of the APOEε4 gene and the functional gradient in the right temporal lobe of the DMN. In APOEε4 non-carriers, the DMN functional gradient was higher in AD compared to healthy controls, whereas in APOEε4 carriers, it was lower in AD. The study also found that APOEε4 carriers demonstrated a positive correlation between DMN functional gradients in the right temporal lobe and cognitive domains. Mediation analyses revealed that AD markers affect DMN resting-state functional connectivity by altering the DMN functional gradient, leading to cognitive deterioration in carriers of the APOEε4 gene.
