## Poised in the Creative Now: Principles of Nordoff-Robbins Music Therapy

The chapter 'Poised in the Creative Now: Principles of Nordoff-Robbins Music Therapy' by Nina Guerrero, David Marcus, and Alan Tury discusses the foundational work and principles of Nordoff-Robbins music therapy, a pioneering approach that emphasizes interactive music-making between the therapist and the client. Founded by Paul Nordoff and Clive Robbins, this therapy integrates musicality as a universal human capacity, focusing on the concept of the 'music child' which encapsulates an individual's inherent creative potential. The approach views music as a medium for therapeutic change, with the therapist engaging in spontaneous musical interactions and improvisation to support clients' growth and development. The chapter highlights the therapeutic goals of fostering resilience, flexibility, and adaptability through creative engagement, reflecting the humanistic principle of self-actualization. This method is characterized by its unique use of music to stimulate personal growth and enhance clients' intrinsic motivation for musical expression, aiming to transcend behavioral or dynamic patterns that hinder self-enhancement.
