## Mindful Eating and Mindful Walking

This lesson covers the concepts of mindful eating and mindful walking. Mindful eating involves focusing fully on the experience of eating, engaging all senses, and being aware of body signals. It includes practices such as the raisin exercise and considerations before and during meals to enhance awareness and gratitude. Mindful walking focuses on observing one's breathing, posture, and bodily sensations while walking, integrating environmental awareness as well. Both practices are suggested to improve eating behaviors and subjective well-being, with preliminary evidence indicating benefits in reducing psychological distress, enhancing mindfulness, and improving quality of life. However, further rigorous research is needed to substantiate these effects.
