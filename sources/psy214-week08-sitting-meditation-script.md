## Sitting Meditation Script

This document provides a guided sitting meditation script that emphasizes breath awareness as the primary object of focus. The script encourages participants to sit in a dignified and comfortable posture, observing the natural flow of breath without trying to control it. It acknowledges the tendency for the mind to wander and instructs meditators to gently redirect their attention back to the breath, fostering a non-judgmental attitude towards distractions and bodily sensations. The script also suggests ways to handle physical discomfort and encourages curiosity and openness towards various sensory experiences. The overall aim is to cultivate mindfulness, enhance awareness of the present moment, and foster a friendly, non-reactive stance towards one's experiences during meditation.
