## Puppet Play Therapy: A Practical Guidebook - Section IV Puppet Therapy Techniques

The document discusses the use of puppets in play therapy for facilitating self-expression and emotional exploration among children, particularly those who are anxious or traumatized. Susan M. Carter emphasizes that puppets serve as symbolic clients, allowing children to project their inner world and externalize emotions through nonverbal communication. Practical considerations include choosing culturally diverse puppets and ensuring accessibility of a varied collection in the playroom. Techniques described involve interventions like 'The Hidden Puppet Technique,' where resistant clients are engaged by helping to coax a puppet out of hiding, thereby addressing fears and anxieties. Another technique includes using bibliotherapy combined with puppet play to bring children's stories to life, enabling them to identify with characters facing similar challenges. A case example illustrates how the author used a story about 'Mr. Fox Is Sad' to engage a young client named Emily in reenacting her own emotional struggles through puppets. These interventions are designed to empower children and provide them with safe avenues for self-expression.
