## Arts in Adolescence: Mental Wellbeing and Development

The lesson discusses the role of arts in adolescent mental wellbeing, focusing on findings from Mission Australia's Youth Survey. It highlights issues such as cost of living, climate change, violence, and mental health among youth aged 15-19 years. The survey reveals that around 30% of respondents faced discrimination, with higher rates for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander youth and gender-diverse individuals. Moreover, the lesson explores how arts-based activities can combat anxiety and other mental health issues in adolescents. It cites studies showing cognitive and socio-emotional benefits from participating in music, drama, and dance, which foster emotional resilience and self-regulation of emotions. The concept of 'Arts on Prescription' is introduced as a strategy for improving mental health through engagement with art forms prescribed by medical practitioners. Research suggests that arts engagement can reduce anxiety and depression levels.
