## Design Q&A: Shigeru Ban

This document features an interview with renowned architect Shigeru Ban, discussing his architectural philosophy and projects. Ban emphasizes the importance of integrating emotional expression and practical utility in design. His work encompasses a range of projects, from cultural institutions like the Aspen Art Museum to humanitarian efforts providing temporary housing for disaster-affected communities. Notably, Ban has pioneered sustainable practices by using recycled paper tubes and timber in construction. He is dismissive of labels such as 'altruism' but acknowledges his obligation as an architect to address人居无害的材料，例如纸管和木材。他强调，他的设计理念在于找到每种材料的合理性，并且反对浪费材料、时间和能量。此外，Ban 认为建筑设计不应完全依赖计算机技术，而是需要时间来精心设计和建造。总的来说，Ban 的工作体现了在创意艺术与健康领域中对可持续性和人文关怀的关注。
