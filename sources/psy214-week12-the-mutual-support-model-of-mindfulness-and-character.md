## The Mutual Support Model of Mindfulness and Character Strengths

This paper explores the mutual relationships between mindfulness and character strengths within the framework of the Values in Action (VIA) classification of character strengths. Through two studies, the authors investigate the associations between mindfulness and character strengths. Study 1, conducted with 1335 participants, confirmed positive relationships between mindfulness and character strengths and identified a list of character strengths that overlap with mindfulness, such as creativity, curiosity, open-mindedness, and love of learning. Study 2, using a randomized-control design with 42 participants, showed that an 8-week mindfulness-based training program significantly increased strengths like love, appreciation of beauty, gratitude, and spirituality. The findings support a mutual support model suggesting that mindfulness and character strengths influence each other positively.
