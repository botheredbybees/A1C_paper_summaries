## Creative Health Review

The 'Creative Health Review' report emphasizes the significant role of arts and creativity in enhancing public health outcomes alongside traditional medical interventions. The document argues that while modern medicine is essential for transforming health, there are substantial opportunities to integrate creative activities such as art, culture, sports, and nature into healthcare delivery systems. The authors assert that these practices can not only improve individual health but also alleviate financial and workload pressures on the National Health Service (NHS). Key findings include a growing body of scientific evidence supporting the efficacy of social interventions based on creativity in reducing health inequalities and improving overall well-being. The report advocates for creative health to be incorporated into mainstream healthcare strategies, suggesting that it should complement rather than replace existing medical services. Furthermore, it highlights the need for a national strategy to support and expand these initiatives through integrated care systems, local government, and arts organizations. This report is highly relevant to the field of creative arts and health as it underscores the potential benefits of integrating creativity into healthcare policies and practices.
