## Light and Shade in Art

This lesson explores the significance of light and shadow in visual arts, focusing on their role in creating depth and conveying symbolic meaning. Joseph Wright's painting 'The Philosopher Lecturing on the Orrery' exemplifies the use of chiaroscuro, highlighting the contrast between light and dark to evoke a sense of wonder and enlightenment. The lesson also discusses various techniques such as hatching, cross-hatching, and the dramatic use of lighting in works by Caravaggio and other artists. Modern examples, like Ryoji Ikeda's 'Spectra' and Tim Noble and Sue Webster's 'Wild Mood Swings', illustrate how light and shadow continue to influence contemporary art.
