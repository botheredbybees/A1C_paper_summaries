## MUSTEM: A Dual-Modality System for Vibrotactile and Visual Translation of Music as an Assistive Technology

MUSTEM is a novel assistive technology designed to enable deaf or hard-of-hearing individuals to experience music through both tactile and visual means. The system employs a low-cost, portable hardware prototype that converts audio signals into vibrotactile feedback and an educational visual interface, aiming to provide a comprehensive sensory substitution for auditory information. By mapping distinct frequency bands of the music spectrum onto four channels of vibration and corresponding visual elements, MUSTEM offers users a rich, coherent experience of musical structure and emotion. The paper details the hardware and software architecture, including real-time audio analysis techniques and nature-inspired visual patterns that enhance perceptual clarity and aesthetic appeal.
