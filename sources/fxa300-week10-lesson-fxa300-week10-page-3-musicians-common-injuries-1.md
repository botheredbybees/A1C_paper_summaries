## Common Physical Injuries Sustained by Musicians

This lesson focuses on the common medical conditions musicians may face, including musculoskeletal pain, orofacial problems, and repetitive strain injuries, particularly focal dystonia. It highlights that musicians, like athletes, can suffer from career-ending injuries due to physical stress and repetitive motions. The lesson reviews studies on musculoskeletal pain in professional orchestral musicians in Australia and orofacial disorders in musicians, emphasizing the need for recovery and prevention methods. Techniques such as biofeedback to track muscle movements during performances are discussed as ways to improve muscle usage and prevent injuries.
