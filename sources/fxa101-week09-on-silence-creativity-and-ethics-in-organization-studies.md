## On Silence, Creativity and Ethics in Organization Studies

Vinca Bigo's article 'On Silence, Creativity and Ethics in Organization Studies' explores the concept of silence within organizational contexts, arguing that silence is not merely exclusionary or oppressive but can be a source of creativity and ethical engagement. The author challenges the conventional view that silence is detrimental to organizational communication and dynamics, proposing instead that certain silences can facilitate creative potential and ethical conduct. By drawing on interdisciplinary insights from art, philosophy, and literature, Bigo examines how silence, understood in its multifaceted nature, can enable transformative moments and foster ethical relations within organizations. The article contributes to the understanding of silence as a dynamic and constructive element in organizational studies, highlighting its significance for fostering creativity and ethical behavior in management and leadership.
