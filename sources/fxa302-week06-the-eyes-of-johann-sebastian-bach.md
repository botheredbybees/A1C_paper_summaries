## The Eyes of Johann Sebastian Bach

The article discusses the eye health and vision issues faced by Johann Sebastian Bach, one of the most renowned composers in history. The primary focus is on his deteriorating eyesight during his later years and the two operations performed by an English ophthalmic surgeon named John Taylor in 1750. Although myopia was likely present throughout his life, it is suggested that cataracts were the main cause of his declining vision as he aged. The article delves into the potential risks associated with these intraocular procedures and mentions Bach's complete blindness after the operations which led to his death shortly thereafter. It emphasizes the scarcity of reliable medical records about Bach's health, making this analysis a significant contribution to understanding one aspect of his life that had both personal and professional implications.
