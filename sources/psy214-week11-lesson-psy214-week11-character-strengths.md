## Character Strengths in Positive Psychology

This lesson introduces the concept of character strengths within the field of positive psychology, highlighting the work of Martin Seligman and colleagues. It emphasizes the importance of understanding and developing character strengths as a means to promote well-being. The lesson includes a video and supplementary materials that outline the VIA (Values in Action) classification of character strengths, which categorizes strengths into six virtues. Students are prompted to engage with the material by dragging and dropping character strengths into their corresponding virtue categories.
