## Verbal Humor in Stand-up Comedy 'Rock & Roast' with the GTVH Theory

This paper examines verbal humor in Chinese stand-up comedy through the lens of the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH). The study analyzes four excerpts from 'Rock & Roast,' a popular TV show, using GTVH's six knowledge resources: Script Oppositions (SO), Logical Mechanisms (LM), Situations (SI), Targets (TA), Narrative Strategies (NS), and Language (LA). Excerpts showcase diverse applications of these elements in humor generation. The analysis highlights the role of LM and NS while noting LA can sometimes be absent or irrelevant to humor creation, suggesting GTVH's utility in understanding contemporary Chinese stand-up comedy.
