## Walking With/In Place: The Walking Turn In Socially Engaged Public Art From Aotearoa New Zealand

The paper discusses a 'Walking Turn' in contemporary socially engaged public art, focusing on Aotearoa New Zealand. It highlights how walking-based artworks engage with decolonial and counterneoliberal objectives, emphasizing relationality between humans and the more-than-human world. The theoretical framework of 'walking with/in place' normalizes interdependence and acknowledges Indigenous concepts like hīkoi (protest march) and wayfinding. The author analyzes works by Jeremy Leatinu’u, Christina Houghton, HOOPLA, and Chris Berthelsen, focusing on countermapping, countermonument, and hyperlocality.
