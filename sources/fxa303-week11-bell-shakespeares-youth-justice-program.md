## Bell Shakespeare’s Youth Justice Program

Bell Shakespeare's Youth Justice Program, initiated in 2010 with support from the NSW Department of Youth Justice and various trusts and foundations, aims to use Shakespearean texts as a tool for self-reflection, confidence building, and skill development among incarcerated youth. The program engages students through workshops led by experienced teaching artists, culminating in performances before an audience that includes peers, teachers, staff, and family members. This initiative has expanded significantly since its inception, with generous community support playing a crucial role in its sustained success. Bell Shakespeare emphasizes the importance of storytelling passed down through generations and acknowledges the traditional custodians of the land on which it operates.
