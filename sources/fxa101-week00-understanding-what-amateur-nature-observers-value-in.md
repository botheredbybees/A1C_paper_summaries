## Understanding What Amateur Nature Observers Value in Identification Field Guides: A Content Analysis

This paper presents a content analysis of product reviews in Birding magazine, focusing on user preferences for field guides and related digital tools used by amateur nature observers. The study examines the evolution of these texts from traditional printed books to modern apps and audio identification tools. Key findings include the enduring value placed on high-quality illustrations and scientific accuracy despite technological advancements. Additionally, the paper discusses how such tools support conservation efforts and community engagement in citizen science projects.
