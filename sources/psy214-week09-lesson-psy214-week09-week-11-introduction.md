## Compassion and Self-Compassion in Mindfulness

This final module of the mindfulness series focuses on the concepts of compassion and self-compassion. The module encourages students to reflect on their emotional responses to others' suffering and their own, highlighting the differences between these responses. It covers the context and concepts of compassion, methods to measure compassion and self-compassion, practical exercises to foster these qualities, and the mechanisms behind how compassion and self-compassion function. The lesson also includes critical thinking about mindfulness, guiding students through required and recommended readings to enhance their understanding of the theme. The module emphasizes the importance of self-care and the practice of mindfulness in developing compassion and self-compassion.
