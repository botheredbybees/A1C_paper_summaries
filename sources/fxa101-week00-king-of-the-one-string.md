## King of the One-String

This essay explores the life and art of Glen Faulkner, a Mississippi musician who exclusively plays the diddley-bow, an African-American one-stringed instrument with roots in sub-Saharan Africa. The article discusses how Faulkner learned to play the diddley-bow from his uncle and was inspired by other local musicians. It details Faulkner's unique electric version of the instrument and his musical influences, ranging from country to blues music. The essay also highlights the cultural significance of the diddley-bow as a symbol of African influence in American music and its transition into the modern era.
