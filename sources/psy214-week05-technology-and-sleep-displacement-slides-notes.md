## Technology and Sleep Displacement: Slides Notes

The document discusses how technology use can displace other activities, particularly sleep, through the concept of the 'attention economy.' This economic model leverages design features such as positive reinforcement (e.g., variable ratio reinforcement), social validation, and negative reinforcement to engage users continuously. Design elements like notifications, badges, infinite scroll, and personalized algorithms are employed to keep users engaged without natural stopping cues. The displacement of sleep is highlighted through the example of Netflix competing with sleep for user attention. Strategies to manage this include maintaining a screen-free bedroom environment, buffering screen time from bedtime, turning off autoplay features, disabling notifications, and making screens less visually engaging.
