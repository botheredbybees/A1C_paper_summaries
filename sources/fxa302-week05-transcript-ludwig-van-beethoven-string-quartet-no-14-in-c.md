## Ludwig van Beethoven String Quartet no. 14 in C sharp minor op. 131 - Takács Quartet (2003)

This is a recording of Ludwig van Beethoven's String Quartet No. 14 in C sharp minor, Opus 131 performed by the Takács Quartet in 2003. The work spans over 38 minutes and showcases Beethoven's later compositional style characterized by its structural complexity and emotional depth. The piece consists of seven connected movements that explore a range of moods from introspective contemplation to intense drama.
