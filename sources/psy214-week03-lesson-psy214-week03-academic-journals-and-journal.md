## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - What are scholarly sources?

This lesson module focuses on identifying scholarly sources for psychology assignments. It emphasizes the use of peer-reviewed academic journals and scholarly book chapters as primary resources over less reliable sources like Wikipedia or Psychology Today. The importance of using proper academic literature is highlighted, with a caution against relying solely on introductory textbooks or online content such as MyLO materials. Two videos are recommended: one explaining academic journal articles in detail and another detailing the key sections of a research article including abstract, introduction, method, results, discussion, and references.
