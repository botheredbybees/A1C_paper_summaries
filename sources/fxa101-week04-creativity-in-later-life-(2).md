## Creativity in Later Life

The book 'Creativity in Later Life' challenges conventional perceptions of creativity and aging by arguing for a broader understanding that encompasses various social, cultural, and economic contexts beyond the traditional notion of 'late style'. The editors David Amigoni and Gordon McMullan introduce interdisciplinary dialogue between gerontology and the arts to explore diverse expressions of late-life creativity. Contributions include personal reflections, theoretical analyses, and practical case studies from community art projects to narratives of care for those with dementia. Key insights highlight how creativity in later life is socially embedded and contextually driven, offering value not just artistically but also societally through civic engagement and therapeutic benefits for individuals living with conditions like dementia. The volume appeals to academics, practitioners, students, and anyone interested in the multifaceted nature of late-life creativity.
