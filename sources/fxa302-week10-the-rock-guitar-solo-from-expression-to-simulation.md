## The Rock Guitar Solo: From Expression to Simulation

Ben Goertzel's article discusses the evolution of the rock guitar solo from a tool for augmenting chord patterns to a powerful vehicle for individual expression and creative innovation. The transformation of the guitar solo parallels the development of rock music from a rebellious, experimental art form to a commercialized pop music genre. Goertzel highlights the pivotal role of Jimi Hendrix in establishing the guitar solo as a mythic element of rock culture, emphasizing Hendrix's innovative techniques and stage presence. The article also critiques the current trend of unoriginal and perfunctory guitar solos in mainstream rock, suggesting a shift towards stylized forms. Goertzel's analysis is relevant to the creative arts and health discourse, as it explores the psychological and cultural significance of musical expression and its evolution over time.
