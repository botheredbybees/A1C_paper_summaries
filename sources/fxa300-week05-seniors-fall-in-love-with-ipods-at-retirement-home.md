## Seniors fall in love with iPods at Retirement Home

A retirement community in Cumberland County initiated an experiment to explore the impact of personalized music playlists on residents' health and mood. The project, facilitated by iPod donations through a research grant, involved creating custom playlists tailored to individual preferences and memories for each resident. Libby Gauss, aged 90, expressed joy and increased vitality from listening to polka music, while Al Glover's swing and big band playlist brought back his energetic youth. Jerry Nadler reminisced about old-time radio shows, and Francis Fogarty found solace in opera, significantly improving her mood and reducing the need for medication. The initiative demonstrated substantial improvements in residents' well-being, highlighting the profound influence of music on mental health and quality of life among seniors.
