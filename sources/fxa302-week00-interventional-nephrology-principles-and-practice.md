## Interventional Nephrology: Principles and Practice

This comprehensive textbook on interventional nephrology covers the principles and practices essential for the diagnosis and management of renal disorders using interventional techniques. Edited by leading experts, it includes chapters on preoperative evaluation, patient communication, anticoagulation strategies, and various procedural approaches for vascular access issues. The book emphasizes effective preoperative planning, risk assessment, and complication management. Each chapter is authored by specialists in the field, providing in-depth guidance on a wide range of topics, from physical examination to post-intervention care.
