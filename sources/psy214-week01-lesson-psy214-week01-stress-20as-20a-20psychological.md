## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: The Transactional Model of Stress and Coping

This lesson introduces the transactional model of stress and coping, emphasizing the process by which individuals perceive and respond to events based on their appraisals. Primary and secondary appraisals are key terms discussed, with primary appraisal involving judgment about whether an event is threatening or harmful, while secondary appraisal involves assessing personal resources for dealing with it. The lesson also covers various coping strategies such as problem-focused and emotion-focused coping, meaning-based coping, and proactive coping. Examples illustrate different approaches to handling stressors, highlighting the importance of direct engagement versus emotional regulation.
