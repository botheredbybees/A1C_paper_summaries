## Systematic review of arts and culture-based interventions for people living with dementia and their caregivers

This systematic review explores the impact of arts and culture-based interventions on people living with dementia and their caregivers. The review aims to summarize studies investigating these interventions' effects on well-being and cognition for individuals with dementia, as well as caregiver strain. A comprehensive search of five electronic databases identified 4827 articles, out of which 34 met the inclusion criteria. The review found that arts-based activities can improve well-being outcomes but generally do not enhance cognitive functioning. Museum-based interventions were particularly prominent among the studies reviewed. Despite these benefits, heterogeneity in intervention types and outcome measures hindered generalization of findings. Further research is recommended to employ standardized measures and include individuals with more severe dementia stages.
