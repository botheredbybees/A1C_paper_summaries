## Singing and Wellbeing

This week's lesson in FXA300 Music, Mind and Body introduces the concept of singing as a therapeutic activity for enhancing physical and mental health. Dr Brendan Lamb discusses recent research on the role of singing in promoting better health, including its impact on brain activity, neurochemical release, and quality of life. The lesson also explores specific applications of singing-based therapies for individuals with respiratory and neurological conditions, highlighting the potential benefits for emotional wellbeing and immune system function. Group singing is emphasized for its capacity to foster community and mental health.
