## FXA303 Week 11: Creative Arts Projects and Therapeutic Programs for Gender-Diverse Youth

This document from the FXA303 Creative Arts, Play and Mental Wellbeing course outlines the focus of Week 11's learning materials. It highlights the importance of creative arts programs in supporting mental wellbeing for gender-diverse youth who are at higher risk for depression and anxiety. The text emphasizes how such programs can create safe spaces and social networks that foster resilience against discrimination, negative self-image, and physical or verbal assault. Additionally, it discusses the benefits of trauma-informed arts-based projects for marginalized youth experiencing homelessness or involvement with the justice system, noting improvements in self-esteem, sense of agency, and reduced rates of re-offending among those involved. The document underscores the potential of creative arts to provide a voice to the marginalized and educate broader communities about their experiences.
