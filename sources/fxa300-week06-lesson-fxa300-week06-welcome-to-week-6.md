## Design of Research Studies

In this week's module of FXA300 Music, Mind and Body, the focus is on understanding research design methods within the scientific method framework. The content introduces how to develop a research question, formulate hypotheses, and operationalize variables for testing. Various types of study designs are discussed, including correlational studies, longitudinal studies, experimental studies, quasi-experimental studies, and case studies, highlighting their specific purposes, advantages, and limitations. The lesson aims to prepare students for Assessment Tasks 2 and 4 by enabling them to create testable hypotheses and evaluate different research design methods.
