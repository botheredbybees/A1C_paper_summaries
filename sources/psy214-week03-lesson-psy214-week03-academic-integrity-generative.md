## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness: Academic Integrity, Generative AI, and Confirmation of Learning Checks

The lesson covers academic integrity at the University of Tasmania, emphasizing core values like honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility, and courage. It highlights Turnitin's role in monitoring for plagiarism and outlines guidelines on using Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI). The use of GenAI is discussed with a focus on appropriate and inappropriate usage within the context of academic assignments. Students are advised to document their use of GenAI transparently when submitting work. Additionally, the lesson explains the process for confirmation of learning checks and offers guidance on how students can share OneDrive links in advance to facilitate this process.
