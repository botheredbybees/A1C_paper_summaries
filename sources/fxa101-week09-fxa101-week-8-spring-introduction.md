## FXA101 Week 8 Spring Introduction

This document provides an introduction to Week 8 of the FXA101 course, which marks the beginning of Module 3. The module focuses on further developing creative skills and practices through Creative Project 3 and the subsequent summative Written Reflection (Assessment Task 4). Students are encouraged to continue using their journals to document their brainstorming, planning, and experimentation processes. The week's content highlights the influence of the Default Mode Network (DMN) on creative outputs, emphasizing the benefits of quietness and solitude for creativity. Practical exercises include exploring semantic network activation through improvisation and word games to enhance DMN capacity. The instructor wishes the students well as they progress through the final part of the unit.
