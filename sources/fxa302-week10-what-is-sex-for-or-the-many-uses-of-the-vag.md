## What Is Sex For? or, The Many Uses of the Vag

Alice Dreger's chapter discusses the cultural and linguistic confusion surrounding terms for female genitalia, focusing on the difference between the vulva and the vagina. Dreger emphasizes the importance of distinguishing between these terms for clarity and accuracy in medical and social contexts. She highlights the impact of this confusion on understanding intersex conditions and the implications for sexual health and pleasure. Dreger also critiques the use of vague or euphemistic terms like 'vajayjay' and argues for precise anatomical language to promote better understanding and respect for bodily diversity in the context of health and well-being.
