## Crafting Wellbeing: Exploring the Link Between Textile Craft and Wellbeing

This paper explores the link between textile crafts and wellbeing through a narrative literature review methodology, synthesizing information from various research studies on how activities such as knitting, crochet, sewing, quilting, and needlework contribute to mental, emotional, physical, and social health. The authors examine three thematic areas: creative expression and emotional wellbeing, social and societal wellbeing, and intellectual wellbeing. They find that textile crafts enhance overall wellbeing by fostering a sense of purpose, promoting emotional resilience, increasing community engagement, encouraging cognitive stimulation, and supporting cultural exploration.
