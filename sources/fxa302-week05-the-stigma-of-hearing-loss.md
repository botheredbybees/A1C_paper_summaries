## The Stigma of Hearing Loss

The study explores the stigma experienced by older adults with hearing loss and their communication partners, highlighting its impact on decision-making processes related to accepting hearing loss, seeking assessment, choosing aids, and wearing them. The research identifies three interrelated experiences of stigma: alterations in self-perception, ageism, and vanity, influenced by societal forces such as media and health professionals. These factors contribute significantly to the reluctance of older adults to address their hearing issues, impacting social engagement and quality of life. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for developing interventions that destigmatize hearing loss, promote its assessment and treatment, and support active aging.
