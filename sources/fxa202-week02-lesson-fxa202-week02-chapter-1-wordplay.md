## FXA202 The Photo Essay: Storytelling with Image and Text - Week 4

This week's lesson in FXA202 focuses on the integration of words and images to enhance storytelling. Students are encouraged to share their stories and engage with classmates through discussion boards. The content outlines the importance of captions in photo essays, emphasizing that written text complements photographs effectively. Key points include learning about different ways texts can interact with images, understanding the role of captions, crafting expressive writing for images, and utilizing quotes as captions. A task to share an image and caption is assigned on the Discussion Board, alongside a quiz related to Chapters 1-3.
