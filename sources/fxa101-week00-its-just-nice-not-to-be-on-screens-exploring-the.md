## 'It's just nice not to be on screens': exploring the relationship between pottery making, eudemonic wellbeing, and Instagram

This article explores the relationship between pottery making, eudemonic well-being, and social media use among British potters during the COVID-19 pandemic. The study reveals that while participants found pottery a respite from digital screens, they also relied heavily on Instagram for learning resources and community connections. Research methods included semi-structured interviews with 25 participants across different skill levels via Microsoft Teams. Participants experienced eudemonic well-being through self-fulfilment, empowerment, identity formation, and social connectedness within the pottery-making process. The article discusses how the digital era intersects with traditional craft practices, offering both benefits and challenges for wellbeing.
