## Creative Arts-Based Therapies for Stroke Survivors: A Qualitative Systematic Review

This qualitative systematic review synthesizes the findings from studies exploring the experiences of stroke survivors participating in creative arts-based therapies. The review, conducted by Lo, Lee, and Ho, identifies five main themes: functional restoration, psychological support, social engagement, spiritual experience, and limitations or barriers. The study highlights the potential of creative arts-based therapies to address the psychosocial needs of stroke survivors. It suggests that different art modalities can serve distinct therapeutic purposes and that further research is needed to explore the differential benefits of single or multiple art modalities in creative arts-based therapies. The review underscores the importance of considering the unique characteristics of each therapeutic approach to maximize their effectiveness in rehabilitation.
