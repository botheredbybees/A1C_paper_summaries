## The Diving Bell and the Butterfly as an Emotional Event

Tarja Laine's essay, 'The Diving Bell and the Butterfly as an Emotional Event,' analyzes the film 'The Diving Bell and the Butterfly' (2007) by Julian Schnabel. The film is based on the memoir by Jean-Dominique Bauby, who suffered a stroke leading to locked-in syndrome. Laine explores the film's use of haptic imagery and synesthesia to evoke sensory imagination and memory. Through this analysis, she discusses the concept of entanglement, where the body is interconnected with the world, and how the film transcends the subject-object distinction, engaging viewers emotionally through a 'halfway meeting' of the inside and outside perspectives.
