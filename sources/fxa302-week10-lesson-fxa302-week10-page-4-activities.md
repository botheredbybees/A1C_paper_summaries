## Week 10 Activities - Perspectives of the Arts on Health and Wellbeing

This week's activities for FXA302 include reading Lo, Lee, & Ho's (2019) qualitative systematic review on creative arts-based therapies for stroke survivors. Students are also expected to participate in web lectures and drop-in sessions, which include important discussions about final assessment tasks. Additionally, there is a creative response task based on neurological disorders, where students are encouraged to produce a creative piece and a 150-word statement explaining their work. References and resources on various neurological conditions and creative arts interventions are provided.
