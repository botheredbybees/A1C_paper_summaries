## Week 4 Introduction

The document provides an introduction to Week 4 of the course, which concludes Module 1 focused on creativity across the lifespan and brain changes. It highlights research into activities that promote brain resilience in aging individuals and the effectiveness of various brain training apps. The text emphasizes that creativity does not diminish with age but evolves based on life experiences. Creative engagement can enhance cognitive health and overall well-being in older adults, as supported by scientific findings. Students are reminded to submit their journal entries for Assessment Task 1 and revise materials before taking a quiz covering weeks 1 through 4. The next module will involve more practical creative projects.
