## Clown Doctors Australia National Impact Summary

The document outlines the significant societal and healthcare impacts of Clown Doctors Australia (CDA) in Australia for the year ending June 2025. CDA aims to enhance well-being through humor, imagination, and creative play to transform care environments into more humane spaces. The report details how over 300,000 people benefited from their programs, highlighting improved quality of care, enhanced connections between patients and healthcare providers, increased hope among those facing medical challenges, and reduced stress for families and caregivers. Key findings show that Clown Doctors help reduce the need for chemical interventions, increase patient receptivity to care, and foster a more compassionate atmosphere in hospitals and aged-care facilities. The organization also introduced its first Social Impact Measurement (SIM) Framework to systematically evaluate the effectiveness of their programs across four domains: connection, hope, empowerment, and quality of care. Research indicates that for every dollar invested in CDA's Clown Doctors program, a social return value of up to $9.90 is generated when delivered in specialist clinics. This work underscores the critical role that creative arts such as clowning can play in healthcare, promoting patient well-being and transforming clinical settings into more supportive and joyful environments.
