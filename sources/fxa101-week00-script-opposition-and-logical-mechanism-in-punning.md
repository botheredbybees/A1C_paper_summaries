## Script Opposition and Logical Mechanism in Punning

Christian F. Hempelmann discusses the application of the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH) to punning, focusing on imperfect puns and their mechanisms. The paper elaborates on script opposition (SO) and logical mechanism (LM), two critical components in GTVH for understanding humor. Hempelmann argues that LM functions as a tool for SO in puns, illustrating this through the analysis of sound symbolism and faulty reasoning in cratylism. He examines how imperfect puns fail to achieve humor due to insufficient semantic opposition despite phonological overlap.
