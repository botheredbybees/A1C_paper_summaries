## PSY114 PSY214 Stress, Self-Care and Mindfulness - FAQs

The lesson provides guidance on how to properly cite academic sources in assignments. It emphasizes the importance of using primary scholarly sources over tertiary or non-scholarly ones like MyLO content, Wikipedia, TED talks, and popular websites. Students are encouraged to locate original research articles rather than citing embedded videos or secondary references within primary texts. The lesson also advises against direct quoting, promotes paraphrasing, and explains that APA citation errors will not significantly impact grades but must be avoided for academic integrity.
