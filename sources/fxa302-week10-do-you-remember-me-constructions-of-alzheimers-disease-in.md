## “Do You Remember Me?” Constructions of Alzheimer’s Disease in Literature and Film

E. Ann Kaplan's chapter explores constructions of Alzheimer's disease through two case studies: Judith Levine's memoir 'Do You Remember Me?' and Sarah Polley's film 'Away from Her.' Kaplan challenges the medical lens that stigmatizes people with dementia, arguing that they may develop new subjectivities despite changes in their adapted self. The chapter critiques the societal perception of dementia as a deviation from the rational self, suggesting that art provides a deeper understanding of emotions and values in dementia. Kaplan highlights the importance of recognizing the core self, which remains despite the loss of the adapted self, and discusses the cultural and social implications of dementia.
