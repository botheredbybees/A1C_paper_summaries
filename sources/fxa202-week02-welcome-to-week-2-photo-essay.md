## Welcome to Week 2 - Photo Essay

This document serves as an introduction for students beginning the second week of a photo essay course, emphasizing practical photography skills and creative exploration. The text outlines recommendations for using various types of cameras and encourages both beginners and experienced photographers to refine their techniques. Key topics include understanding color theory and composition principles, which are integral to storytelling through images. Students are invited to post their work on weekly discussion boards where they can receive feedback from tutors and peers. Additional resources like videos and apps are suggested for further learning, with an emphasis on experimenting rather than rushing into advanced technical skills.
