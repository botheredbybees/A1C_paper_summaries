## Creative Responses to Physical Disability through Visual Arts

This lesson explores how engagement with visual arts can aid in emotional resilience and recovery, particularly for individuals recovering from traumatic injuries. It discusses the psychological and physiological benefits of arts-based interventions in complementing medical treatments and physical rehabilitation. The lesson uses the case study of Frida Kahlo to illustrate how visual arts can serve as a therapeutic medium for individuals dealing with physical and emotional pain. Kahlo's work is analyzed in terms of how it reflects her experiences of disability, cultural identity, and personal pain. The lesson also covers the role of creative expression in improving mood and quality of life for individuals with spinal cord injuries, highlighting the importance of providing access to formal creative arts therapy or tailored arts-based activities during rehabilitation.
