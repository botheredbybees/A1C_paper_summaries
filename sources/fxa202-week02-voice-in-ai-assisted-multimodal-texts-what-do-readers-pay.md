## Voice in AI-assisted multimodal texts: What do readers pay attention to?

This study explores the concept of voice in AI-assisted multimodal texts, focusing on photo essays created with generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) tools. The research addresses a significant gap in existing literature by examining how readers perceive voice within this new context. Through a mixed-method approach involving ranking exercises and follow-up interviews with writing instructors, the study reveals that while there is general agreement about certain aspects of voice construction, such as unique ideas, coherence, and appropriate use of quotations, opinions diverge on the use of primary and secondary texts, academic discourse features, and AI-generated images. The findings suggest a nuanced understanding of voice in multimodal contexts and highlight the potential for diverse interpretations among readers. This work contributes to scholarly discussions about writing pedagogy by emphasizing the importance of leveraging technology while considering its impact on student expression.
