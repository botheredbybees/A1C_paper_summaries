## Assessing the Long-Term Impact of the Molecular Gastronomy Movement on Haute Cuisine

This paper examines the evolution and impact of the molecular gastronomy movement in haute cuisine over the last quarter century, focusing on its conceptual boundaries and terminological debates. The authors argue that while scientists label it as 'molecular gastronomy', top chefs increasingly distance themselves from this term due to perceived elitism or misrepresentation of their work. They propose 'experimental cooking' as a more accurate descriptor for contemporary culinary practices, emphasizing innovation, creativity, and engagement with diner's intellect and emotions.
