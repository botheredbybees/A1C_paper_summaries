## Understanding Sleep: From Dreams to REM

This transcript explores the complexities of human sleep, focusing on Mike Birbiglia's unusual experience and delving into the four stages of sleep. It covers non-rapid eye movement (NREM) stages one through three and rapid eye movement (REM). The content discusses various sleep disorders like insomnia, narcolepsy, and REM sleep behavior disorder. Additionally, it examines theories about dreaming including wish-fulfillment, information processing, physiological function, cognitive development, and neural activity models.
