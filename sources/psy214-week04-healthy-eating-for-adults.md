## Healthy Eating for Adults

The document 'Healthy Eating for Adults' provides guidelines and tips to promote healthy dietary habits among Australians, emphasizing the importance of consuming foods from the five major food groups—vegetables, fruits, grains, lean proteins, and dairy—and limiting discretionary choices that are high in saturated fats, added sugars, and salt. The Australian Dietary Guidelines recommend specific daily servings based on age, gender, activity level, and health status to ensure adequate nutrient intake while reducing risks of chronic diseases like heart disease, type 2 diabetes, obesity, and some cancers. Tips include planning meals with whole grains, incorporating a variety of vegetables, cooking methods that preserve nutritional value, choosing lean meats and plant-based protein sources, selecting reduced-fat dairy products, using unsaturated fats sparingly, limiting alcohol consumption, and making informed choices when dining out.
