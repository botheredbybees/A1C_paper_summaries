## Clown Doctors Australia Social Impact Report

The Clown Doctors Australia Social Impact Report for the year ending June 30, 2025, highlights the organization's initiatives to enhance wellbeing through healthcare clowning programs. The report presents a comprehensive framework developed with For-Purpose Evaluations to assess both immediate and long-term impacts of their Clown Doctors and Laughter Care programs in hospitals and aged-care facilities across Australia. Key findings indicate that these programs significantly improve quality of care, foster connections among residents, patients, families, and healthcare workers, promote hope and empowerment, and reduce the need for chemical interventions or pain medication. Healthcare workers consistently report a positive impact on patient receptiveness to care, staff morale, and overall environment. Families and carers also benefit from reduced stress and emotional support during difficult times. The report emphasizes Clown Doctors Australia's commitment to expanding their reach and embedding their social impact framework into everyday practice to ensure continued transformation of healthcare environments.
