## Music interventions for mechanically ventilated patients

This systematic review, originally published in 2010 and updated in 2014, investigates the impact of music interventions on anxiety and physiological functioning in mechanically ventilated patients. The review focuses on evaluating the efficacy of music therapy or music medicine interventions compared to standard care alone. The primary objective is to conduct a meta-analysis comparing the effects of music interventions combined with standard care to standard care alone. Key findings indicate that music interventions can potentially reduce anxiety and improve physiological outcomes such as heart rate and respiratory rate. The study underscores the importance of considering non-pharmacological methods, like music therapy, for managing anxiety and stress in patients on mechanical ventilation. This relevance to creative arts and health is significant, as it highlights the therapeutic potential of music in improving patient well-being and reducing the duration of mechanical ventilation, which can lead to lower hospital costs and improved quality of life.
