## Energy balance and our food environment

This lesson focuses on the concept of energy balance in relation to body weight and health. It explains that positive energy balance leads to weight gain while negative energy balance results in weight loss. The discussion includes proximal influences like physical activity and basal metabolic rate, alongside distal influences such as the modern food environment which can promote unhealthy eating habits due to marketing strategies and convenience foods. Additionally, the lesson addresses the misconception that healthy diets are more expensive than unhealthy ones, highlighting cost-effective options for nutritious meals.
