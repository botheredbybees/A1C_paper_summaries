## Dance and Creativity within Dance Movement Therapy

The book 'Dance and Creativity within Dance Movement Therapy' edited by Hilda Wengrower and Sharon Chaiklin focuses on the core aspects and fundamental concepts of dance movement therapy (DMT). It emphasizes the centrality of dance and the creative process within DMT, exploring their psychological and aesthetic implications for both therapists and patients. Contributions from various disciplines, including philosophy, psychology, anthropology, and dance, offer perspectives on DMT's applicability across different cultural contexts. The editors highlight the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration and the role of cultural differences in shaping DMT practices. This book provides a valuable resource for professionals in the field, enhancing their understanding and application of DMT techniques.
