## Deliberate Metaphor and Humor

This paper examines the role of deliberate metaphors in humorous narratives, analyzing their relationship with elements of the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH). The study posits that deliberate metaphors are more likely to be a source of humor rather than merely a conduit for conveying humor. It utilizes the Deliberate Metaphor Theory and GTVH frameworks to explore how metaphorical language functions in creating humorous effects, highlighting the importance of intentional use and the role of incongruity between source and target domains.
