## Dance and the Quality of Life

The book 'Dance and the Quality of Life' edited by Karen Bond explores the multifaceted ways in which dance contributes to the quality of life, health, and well-being. It bridges the gap between the traditional view of dance as an end in itself and its practical benefits in enhancing personal and community life. The foreword by Sally Gardner highlights the evolution of dance education from self-expression to contributions to quality of life and highlights Bond's rigorous yet passionate approach to dance research. The book discusses dance's role in building community, fostering learning, and providing a mode of nonverbal communication, and it also examines the potential of dance to dissolve boundaries between work and play, leading to a more meaningful and engaged life. This volume is a significant contribution to the field, underscoring the importance of dance in personal and communal well-being.
