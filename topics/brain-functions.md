## Expectation in Music and Brain Function

The role of expectation in music is a theoretical framework that delves into how listeners anticipate musical sequences based on the syntax and structure of compositions. This concept, rooted in cognitive processes, interacts significantly with brain function as it affects emotional responses and neurochemical activity. Expectation theory posits that anticipation plays a crucial role in engaging listeners emotionally through the creation of tension and resolution. Source 2 complements this by highlighting how broader creative experiences, such as those involving music, impact various regions of the human brain related to perception and emotion regulation via neurochemicals and hormones. Together, these sources suggest a deep interplay between musical expectations and neurological responses, underscoring the profound influence of auditory stimuli on mental health and well-being.

## Key Findings

- Expectation in music significantly influences emotional engagement through anticipatory cognitive processes.
- Musical syntax and structure guide listeners' predictions about upcoming sounds, impacting brain activity.
- Creative experiences like listening to or performing music can alter neurochemical levels in the brain.
