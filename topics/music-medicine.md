## Music and Medicine

The integration of music into healthcare practices, known as music medicine, is gaining traction as an interdisciplinary approach to enhancing patient care and well-being. Sources highlight the therapeutic relationship between musicians and patients as a cornerstone of effective music-based interventions. Music medicine encompasses a broad range of practices, including music therapy and performing arts medicine, which cater to various health needs and wellness plans for musicians. While there is agreement on the benefits of music in improving health outcomes and the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration, the specific mechanisms through which music impacts health remain underexplored. Additionally, the role of music in the broader context of healthcare, beyond individual patient care, is an area of ongoing investigation.

## Key Findings

- Music medicine is an interdisciplinary approach to patient care that leverages the therapeutic relationship between musicians and patients.
- Music-based interventions, such as music therapy and performing arts medicine, can improve health outcomes for various conditions.
- Developing wellness plans for musicians as part of music medicine addresses specific health needs within the performing arts community.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific mechanisms through which music impacts health and well-being?
