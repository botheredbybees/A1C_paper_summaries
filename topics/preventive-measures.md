## Preventive Measures in Health and Development

Preventive measures play a pivotal role in both cognitive and physical health throughout life. Environmental and cognitive enrichment in early childhood, as highlighted in Source 1, contribute to the development of a 'cognitive reserve' which can serve as a protective factor against neurodegenerative diseases in later life. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on the preventive role of holistic health approaches, particularly through therapeutic applications of the performing arts. Both sources underscore the importance of proactive interventions for lifelong health, with Source 1 emphasizing early childhood enrichment and Source 2 advocating for ongoing therapeutic practices. The integration of these approaches suggests a broad spectrum of preventive strategies from early life through adulthood.

## Key Findings

- Early childhood enrichment, including cognitive and environmental factors, builds a cognitive reserve that protects against neurodegenerative diseases later in life.
- Holistic health approaches, such as those utilizing performing arts, are effective therapeutic and preventive measures for maintaining health and wellness throughout the lifespan.
- Preventive measures in health and development require a comprehensive approach, integrating early childhood enrichment with ongoing therapeutic practices.

## Open Questions

- How can the benefits of early cognitive and environmental enrichment be extended and sustained throughout adulthood to maximize preventive health outcomes?
