## Creative Therapies in Chronic Pain Management

The exploration of creative therapies as interventions for managing chronic pain reveals a multifaceted approach that includes art therapy, music therapy, expressive writing, and laughter therapy. These therapeutic modalities are grounded in the understanding that chronic pain often places individuals in survival mode, necessitating strategies to address both physical and emotional dimensions. Across sources, there is an emphasis on multidisciplinary care involving narrative development, shared reading groups, creative writing workshops, and specific therapeutic applications such as music therapy. The literature highlights the potential of these interventions to facilitate emotional healing, enhance quality of life, and provide personal meaning in coping with chronic pain. However, while promising, the efficacy and mechanisms underlying creative therapies remain under-researched compared to more conventional medical treatments for chronic pain.

## Key Findings

- Creative therapies can offer significant benefits for individuals managing chronic pain by addressing both emotional and physical aspects of their condition.
- Multidisciplinary care that includes narrative development, shared reading groups, and creative writing workshops can enhance the therapeutic impact on patients with chronic pain.
- Music therapy is particularly effective in chronic pain management due to its ability to provide personal meaning and emotional relief.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific mechanisms through which creative therapies exert their positive effects on chronic pain?
