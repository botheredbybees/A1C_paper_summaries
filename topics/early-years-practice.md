## Person-Centered Approach in Early Years Practice

The person-centered approach is a critical intervention in early years practice that emphasizes the importance of unconditional positive regard, congruence (authenticity), and empathy. Across both sources, this approach underscores the need for inclusive practices and teamwork to ensure that children's needs are met within an environment where they feel valued and supported. Source 1 highlights how clients can take more responsibility in their own care, promoting independence through a person-centered framework, while Source 2 explores the application of these principles in team settings to facilitate leadership and effective planning.

## Key Findings

- Unconditional positive regard fosters an environment where children feel safe and valued.
- Congruence is essential for practitioners to build genuine relationships with children and families.
- Person-centered approach supports inclusive practices that benefit all participants.

## Open Questions

- How can early years practitioners effectively balance individualized care with broader team-based approaches?
