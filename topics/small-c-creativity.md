## Small-C Creativity

The concept of 'small-c' creativity is central to discussions about everyday creative activities that are not necessarily high-impact or groundbreaking but contribute to personal well-being and engagement, especially in vulnerable populations such as those with dementia. In the context of dementia care, small-c creativity can provide a meaningful form of expression and connection despite cognitive decline. Both sources highlight divergent thinking as a key component of this type of creative activity, underscoring its importance in facilitating flexible thought processes that are often diminished by conditions like dementia. While barriers to participation exist, integrating opportunities for small-c creativity into everyday routines can help individuals continue to engage creatively within their capabilities.

## Key Findings

- Small-c creativity emphasizes regular, low-stakes creative activities rather than major innovations or artistic achievements.
- Divergent thinking is crucial for engaging in and benefiting from small-c creativity.
- Incorporating opportunities for small-c creativity can support the well-being of individuals with dementia.

## Open Questions

- What are effective strategies to overcome barriers to participation in creative activities for vulnerable populations?
