## Creative Approaches to Health and Leadership

The integration of creative approaches into health initiatives and leadership practices presents a holistic framework for enhancing well-being and fostering resilience. Both sources underscore the importance of creativity in promoting mental health through various interventions such as arts-based therapies and innovative learning methodologies. Source 1 highlights how creative strategies can contribute to equitable access to healthcare services, ensuring that individuals from diverse backgrounds have opportunities to benefit from these interventions. In contrast, Source 2 focuses more on leadership dynamics, arguing that leaders who adopt creative approaches are better equipped to handle organizational challenges, develop adaptive skills among team members, and lead through transformative change rather than merely focusing on administrative tasks. While there is a clear overlap in the emphasis on creativity as a means of enhancing mental health outcomes across both sources, they differ slightly in their specific applications: healthcare versus leadership contexts.

## Key Findings

- Creative approaches can enhance holistic well-being by addressing mental health needs.
- Equitable access to creative interventions improves health equity.
- Leadership that incorporates creativity is more resilient and adaptable.

## Open Questions

- How do creative health initiatives translate into effective leadership practices, and vice versa?
