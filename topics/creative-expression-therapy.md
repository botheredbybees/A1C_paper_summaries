## Creative Expression Therapy

Creative expression therapy encompasses a range of practices aimed at harnessing the therapeutic potential of various art forms to foster resilience and mindfulness. Olivia Bowman's 'A Mindfulness Stay' highlights how engaging in art can serve as a therapeutic tool, promoting curiosity and observation which are crucial for mental well-being. Meanwhile, the initiative 'You Are the Best Dancer' underscores the importance of cultivating a strong sense-of-self through artistic expression, aiding individuals in building resilience against life's challenges. Similarly, workshops focused on creative expression for children emphasize the innate creativity present in every individual and its role as a therapeutic medium to enhance psychological well-being. Across these sources, there is agreement that creative expression offers significant benefits in developing mindfulness, enhancing personal identity, and bolstering resilience.

## Key Findings

- Creative expression therapy enhances mental health through mindfulness and self-awareness
- Artistic activities foster resilience by strengthening the sense-of-self
- Workshops tailored to children highlight creativity's innate presence and therapeutic value

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of creative expression on building psychological resilience?
