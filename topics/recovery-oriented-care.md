## Music Therapy as a Recovery-Oriented Care Intervention

Music therapy emerges as a robust intervention in mental health care, particularly in the context of recovery-oriented care, emphasizing self-determination and illness management. Both sources highlight its effectiveness in promoting mental health among school children and in managing mental health conditions through recovery-oriented approaches. Source 1 underscores the role of music therapy in fostering mental health interventions in educational settings, whereas Source 2 elaborates on the application of music therapy in the broader context of mental health care, focusing on illness management and recovery. Despite the shared emphasis on recovery orientation, the sources differ in the specific contexts in which music therapy is applied, suggesting a broad utility of the intervention across various mental health scenarios.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy promotes mental health by engaging participants in therapeutic and creative activities.
- Recovery-oriented care principles are integral to the application of music therapy in both school and clinical settings.
- Self-determination theory supports the use of music therapy as a means to enhance self-management and recovery in mental health contexts.
