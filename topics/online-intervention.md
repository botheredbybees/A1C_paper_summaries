## Online Interventions for Older Adults and Caregivers

Recent studies highlight the potential of online interventions to support older adults and caregivers in managing various health concerns. Source 1 discusses a multimodal intervention called REMINDer, aimed at promoting healthy aging and dementia prevention among older individuals through an online platform. The study underscores the feasibility and positive impact of such interventions on mental well-being in this demographic. Meanwhile, Source 2 focuses on an in-home online music-based program designed to reduce stress and enhance coping skills for family caregivers dealing with persons who have dementia. Both studies emphasize the importance of accessible and effective digital tools tailored to meet specific needs, suggesting a growing trend towards leveraging technology in healthcare delivery for targeted populations.

## Key Findings

- Multimodal interventions can effectively promote healthy aging and cognitive health.
- Online music-based programs are beneficial for reducing caregiver stress and improving coping mechanisms.
- Digital platforms offer feasible solutions for delivering mental health support to older adults and caregivers.

## Open Questions

- How can the effectiveness of online interventions be measured in long-term studies beyond initial feasibility phases?
