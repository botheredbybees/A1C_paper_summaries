## Expressive Writing Therapy

Expressive writing therapy has been widely studied and employed as an intervention for enhancing both mental and physical health outcomes. Sources highlight its therapeutic value in fostering self-awareness, promoting psychological well-being, supporting posttraumatic growth, and improving quality of life. Expressive writing is also linked to positive changes in immune system functioning, suggesting physiological benefits beyond the mental health realm. The practice of expressive writing extends into various creative forms, contributing to narrative development and shared reading groups as therapeutic tools. However, there are differing perspectives on how this intervention can evolve within the context of developmental creative writing, indicating a broader potential for its application.

## Key Findings

- Expressive writing therapy enhances mental well-being and supports posttraumatic growth.
- The practice is associated with positive changes in physical health outcomes, such as immune system functioning.
- Expressive writing can be integrated into various therapeutic models including shared reading groups and developmental creative writing.

## Open Questions

- How does the evolving context of expressive writing impact its efficacy in diverse populations?
