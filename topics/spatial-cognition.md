## Spatial Cognition

Essentials of Cognitive Neuroscience explores the neural underpinnings of spatial cognition, focusing on the temporoparietal cortex and its role in the dorsal stream, which is crucial for visual processing and spatial perception. This work highlights the importance of the posterior parietal cortex in spatial attention and the integration of visual and spatial information. In contrast, the Spatial Cognition and Attention: Key Themes document delves into the hippocampus and its contribution to spatial cognition, particularly in establishing allocentric reference frames, which are essential for understanding spatial relationships in the environment. Both sources emphasize the posterior parietal cortex's significance in spatial attention and the representation of space, although they differ in their primary focus on the dorsal stream or the hippocampus.

## Key Findings

- The temporoparietal cortex and dorsal stream are critical for visual processing and spatial perception.
- The posterior parietal cortex plays a significant role in spatial attention and the integration of visual and spatial information.
- The hippocampus is essential for establishing allocentric reference frames in spatial cognition.

## Open Questions

- How do the dorsal stream, posterior parietal cortex, and hippocampus interact to form a comprehensive understanding of spatial cognition?
