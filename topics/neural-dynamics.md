## Neural Dynamics in Music Therapy

The provided sources explore neural dynamics in the context of music therapy interventions, focusing on emotional and cognitive processing. Source 1 investigates the neural dynamics of emotion processing during Guided Imagery and Music (GIM) using dual-EEG technology, highlighting the identification of emotional markers through this technique. In contrast, Source 2 delves into the temporal entrainment of cognitive functions, showing how musical mnemonics can induce brain plasticity and oscillatory synchrony in neural networks involved in memory. Both studies emphasize the importance of neural dynamics in understanding the mechanisms of music therapy, yet they differ in their specific focus on emotional versus cognitive neural processes.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy interventions, such as GIM, can be studied using advanced neuroimaging technologies like dual-EEG.
- Both emotional and cognitive processing can be significantly influenced by music therapy, with neural dynamics playing a crucial role in these processes.
- Music therapy has the potential to induce neural plasticity and improve cognitive functions such as memory through oscillatory synchrony.

## Open Questions

- How do emotional and cognitive neural dynamics interact during music therapy, and what are the implications for therapeutic efficacy?
