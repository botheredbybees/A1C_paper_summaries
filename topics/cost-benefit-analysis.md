## Cost-Benefit Analysis of Music Therapy

Music therapy is increasingly recognized as a valuable intervention for dementia patients, offering benefits beyond those achieved through traditional caregiving methods. The literature highlights its efficacy in reducing agitation and enhancing socialization among participants. However, the cost-benefit analysis remains complex due to variations in program implementation, ranging from structured music therapy sessions to less formal music-assisted caregiving approaches. Despite these differences, both sources concur on the positive psychological impacts of music on dementia patients, such as alleviating depressive symptoms. The challenge lies in quantifying these benefits and comparing them against financial costs, including training for caregivers and therapists.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy is effective in reducing agitation and depression among dementia patients.
- Enhanced socialization is a noted benefit of music therapy programs.
- The implementation of music-assisted caregiving varies widely.

## Open Questions

- What are the cost implications of implementing structured versus less formal approaches to music therapy?
