## Music Therapy in Substance Abuse Treatment

Music therapy appears to be a valuable adjunct to traditional substance abuse treatment methods, enhancing patient engagement and overall therapeutic outcomes. In the context of group cognitive behavior therapy (CBT), music therapy can help patients connect with the treatment process on a deeper emotional level. This engagement is critical in substance abuse treatment, where maintaining motivation and commitment can be challenging. Music therapy also complements other therapeutic modalities such as motivational enhancement therapy and contingency management by fostering a supportive environment that encourages self-expression and healing. However, the integration of music and art therapies into substance abuse treatment programs remains somewhat underexplored, suggesting potential for further research and application.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy increases patient engagement in substance abuse treatment.
- Music therapy complements traditional therapeutic methods like cognitive behavior therapy and motivational enhancement therapy.
- Music therapy can help create a supportive environment for patients undergoing substance abuse treatment.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of incorporating music therapy into substance abuse treatment programs?
