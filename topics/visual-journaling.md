## Visual Journaling

Visual journaling is presented as a method for both creative expression and academic research. In the context of creative projects, it serves as a tool for personal reflection and creative exploration, allowing individuals to document their thoughts and experiences through visual means. This practice aligns with the broader educational goal of encouraging critical thinking and innovation. Source 2, however, extends the concept of visual journaling to a more structured approach in academic research, emphasizing its use as a method for collecting and analyzing visual data. This method is integral in the process of pictorial analysis, which involves the detailed examination and interpretation of visual materials, contributing significantly to the field of visual research. Both sources highlight the importance of thorough documentation and academic integrity in the use of visual journals, ensuring that the visual content and narrative are presented ethically and accurately.

## Key Findings

- Visual journaling is a versatile method for creative expression and academic research.
- Documentation and integrity are crucial in maintaining the validity of visual journals.
- Visual journals can serve as a tool for personal reflection and academic analysis.
