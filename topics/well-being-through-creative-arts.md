## Well-being Through Creative Arts

The integration of arts-based interventions for enhancing the well-being of older adults and people living with dementia highlights a range of methodologies including singing, visual art programs, and general creative activities. Source 1 discusses how communal singing can provide emotional release and a sense of belonging among participants, while Source 2 delves into theoretical foundations of visual art programmes that offer provocative aesthetic experiences and dynamic responsive practices to enhance quality of life for those with dementia. Source 3 further supports the notion that creativity through arts not only aids self-expression but also significantly contributes to overall well-being in individuals affected by cognitive decline or age-related challenges. Finally, Source 4 emphasizes on the myriad benefits of creative activities among older adults, underscoring their role in fostering cognitive resilience and improving quality-of-life. While there is a consensus across sources regarding the positive impacts of arts-based interventions, questions remain about the long-term efficacy and scalability of these programs.

## Key Findings

- Communal singing and visual art programs enhance emotional well-being
- Creative activities contribute to cognitive resilience in older adults
- Arts provide self-expression opportunities for people living with dementia

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term benefits and sustainability of arts-based interventions?
