## Experiential Learning

Experiential learning is a method that immerses participants in real-world scenarios, enabling them to develop a deeper understanding of complex issues such as disabilities and therapeutic practices. In the context of disability awareness, experiential exercises help challenge stereotypes and social barriers by simulating the experiences of individuals with disabilities, thus fostering empathy and transformative learning. Similarly, in music therapy education, experiential learning is pivotal in training therapists to respond effectively to diverse therapeutic opportunities while adhering to professional regulations. Both sources highlight the potential of experiential learning to bridge the gap between theory and practice, enhancing professionals' competencies and participants' awareness.

## Key Findings

- Experiential learning facilitates the understanding and overcoming of social barriers and stereotypes in disability awareness.
- Training in music therapy benefits from experiential methods to prepare professionals for diverse therapeutic situations.
- Experiential learning enhances both personal and professional development by integrating theory with practice.

## Open Questions

- How can the effectiveness of experiential learning in fostering empathy and professional competency be measured and standardized across different disciplines?
