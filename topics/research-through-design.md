## Research Through Design

Research Through Design (RtD) is a methodological approach that emphasizes the iterative process of designing and researching simultaneously. This approach allows practitioners to challenge confirmation bias by engaging in continuous cycles of design, evaluation, and redesign within their communities. Key to this methodology is the designerly way of knowing, which involves understanding problems through the act of creating designs rather than solely relying on standard research methods or traditional academic theories. While RtD offers a robust framework for sustainability-focused research and community engagement, it also introduces complexities in terms of how findings are communicated and integrated into broader theoretical frameworks.

## Key Findings

- Research Through Design (RtD) is an iterative method that combines design with research to mitigate confirmation bias.
- The designerly way of knowing is integral to RtD as a means to understand problems through the creation process itself.
- Community engagement and sustainability are central aspects of RtD practices.
