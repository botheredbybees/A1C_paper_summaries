## Cognitive Performance Enhancement

The synthesis integrates findings on enhancing cognitive performance through various interventions. Source 1 highlights the positive impact of brain training apps on cognitive performance and processing speed in older adults, emphasizing its practical application via mobile technology. In contrast, Source 2 delves into the influence of music on work efficiency, presenting a nuanced perspective that it can help or hinder depending on individual state-of-arousal. While both sources explore methods to improve cognitive functions, their approaches diverge significantly—one focusing on structured brain training exercises, the other examining the ambient effect of auditory stimuli.

## Key Findings

- Brain training apps enhance cognitive performance and processing speed in older adults.
- Music's impact on work efficiency is context-dependent, varying based on individual arousal levels.

## Open Questions

- How do interventions like brain training apps interact with or complement the effects of environmental factors such as music in enhancing cognitive performance?
