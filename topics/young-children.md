## Support Goals and Arts Programs for Young Children

The synthesis of these sources reveals a multifaceted approach to supporting young children's development through arts programs and tailored support goals. Source 1 highlights the importance of arts-inclusive programs in enhancing mental health and wellbeing among young children, with outcomes indicating improvements in social skills, emotional regulation, and self-expression. However, this source focuses primarily on assessing these benefits through various outcome measures without delving into specific community-driven goals or the perspectives of families and educators. In contrast, Source 2 emphasizes the critical role of community input in establishing support goals that are relevant to young autistic children's quality of life and overall development. This approach underscores the necessity for interventions like arts programs to be informed by the unique needs and priorities articulated by those closest to the child, ensuring that such initiatives are not only beneficial but also deeply resonant with the communities they aim to serve.

## Key Findings

- Arts-inclusive programs contribute positively to young children's mental health and wellbeing.
- Community perspectives are essential for determining appropriate support goals for young autistic children.
- Tailored support goals enhance quality of life for young autistic children.

## Open Questions

- How can arts programs be effectively integrated with community-driven support goals to maximize benefits?
