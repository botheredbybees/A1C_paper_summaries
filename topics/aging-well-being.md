## Aging and Well-being

The investigation into the well-being of older adults through various interventions highlights both multimodal approaches and the role of creative activities. Source 1 presents a randomized controlled study evaluating REMINDer, an online intervention aimed at promoting healthy aging and preventing dementia in seniors. The multimodal nature of this intervention incorporates elements to cater to multiple aspects of mental health, demonstrating its feasibility and positive impact on older adults' well-being. In contrast, Source 2 outlines activities under the FXA101 Creativity for Life program, which emphasizes self-reported benefits from engaging in creative pursuits as part of a broader strategy to foster healthy aging among seniors. While both sources underscore different strategies (multimodal vs. creativity-focused) for enhancing quality of life and cognitive health in older adults, they collectively suggest that interventions tailored to the needs and interests of elderly individuals can significantly contribute to their well-being.

## Key Findings

- Multimodal online interventions show feasibility and positive impacts on mental health in older adults.
- Engagement in creative activities is reported by older adults as beneficial for healthy aging.
- Interventions aimed at promoting healthy aging need to be adaptable and tailored to individual preferences.

## Open Questions

- How do the long-term effects of multimodal interventions compare with those of single-focus approaches like creativity-based programs?
