## Peer-Review in Academic Research

Peer-review is a crucial methodological approach in academic research, particularly in the evaluation of scholarly sources and methodologies like Langerian mindfulness. Sources highlight the importance of peer-reviewed academic journals as primary resources, emphasizing their role in validating claims and ensuring the credibility of research findings. The Langer Mindfulness Scale and other similar methodologies underscore the need for rigorous evaluation through randomization and peer-review processes. However, sources do not elaborate on the peer-review process itself but rather highlight its outcomes and the benefits of using peer-reviewed materials.

## Key Findings

- Peer-reviewed journals serve as primary sources for validating academic claims.
- The Langer Mindfulness Scale and other methodologies benefit from rigorous evaluation through peer-review.
- Randomization is an integral part of ensuring the credibility of research findings.
