## Social Impact Assessment

Sources emphasize the importance of social impact assessment in evaluating interventions like Clown Doctors Australia. The use of a Social Impact Measurement (SIM) Framework and Social Return on Investment (SROI) methodology helps quantify non-financial benefits such as hope, empowerment, and connection among patients. Both documents highlight quality of care improvements through these assessments, underscoring the framework's utility in measuring intangible impacts. However, there is an underlying tension regarding how to effectively balance subjective qualitative measures with objective financial metrics.

## Key Findings

- The Social Impact Measurement (SIM) Framework and SROI methodology are critical for assessing non-financial benefits of interventions like Clown Doctors Australia.
- Improvements in the quality of care, as well as patient empowerment and connection, are key outcomes measured by these frameworks.

## Open Questions

- How can social impact assessments effectively integrate subjective qualitative measures with objective financial metrics?
