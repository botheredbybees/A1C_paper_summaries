## Reflection in Education and Learning

Reflection is a critical intervention in education and professional development, enhancing self-awareness and personal growth. Q methodology, as applied in pre-service teacher education, facilitates structured reflection to support learning and professional development. Another model for promoting reflection in learning emphasizes experiential learning and the development of self-awareness as key components. While these sources highlight the importance of reflection in fostering critical analysis and research skills, they also underscore the need for tailored approaches that effectively integrate reflection into educational practices. Each source underscores reflection's role in personal and professional development but suggests varying frameworks and methods for its implementation.

## Key Findings

- Reflection is a critical intervention for learning and professional development.
- Q methodology and experiential learning models both promote structured reflection.
- Critical analysis and research skills are enhanced through reflective practices.

## Open Questions

- What are the most effective methods for integrating reflection into educational practices?
