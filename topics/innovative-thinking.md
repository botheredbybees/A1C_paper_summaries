## Innovative Thinking Under Scarcity

The concept of innovative thinking under scarcity is explored in both sources, emphasizing the paradoxical relationship between resource scarcity and creativity. Source 1 introduces 'functional-fixedness,' which refers to the tendency to perceive objects and their functions in a limited way, hindering creative problem-solving. However, it also suggests that adopting a 'constraint-mindset' can enhance 'creative-consumption,' or the ability to repurpose limited resources creatively. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on 'scarcity salience,' where the awareness of limited resources can shift cognitive focus, potentially promoting innovative thinking by highlighting the necessity of efficient use of available resources. Despite the differing emphases, both sources agree that scarcity can paradoxically foster creativity, though the mechanisms and outcomes vary.

## Key Findings

- Resource scarcity can paradoxically enhance creative problem-solving and innovation.
- Adopting a 'constraint-mindset' can improve the ability to creatively consume and repurpose limited resources.
- The salience of scarcity can shift cognitive focus towards more efficient use of available resources.

## Open Questions

- What are the optimal conditions for scarcity to positively influence innovative thinking without overwhelming cognitive resources?
