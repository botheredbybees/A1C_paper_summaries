## Structural Equation Modeling

Source one discusses the application of fit indexes in structural equation modeling (SEM), highlighting standardized-root-mean-squared-residual (SRMSR) and comparative-fit-index (CFI) as crucial for assessing model fit. It challenges conventional criteria for SEM and introduces alternatives, suggesting a nuanced approach to interpreting these indexes. Source two introduces bricolage and frugal innovation within the context of emerging markets, using structural equation modeling as part of its methodology to understand how organizations can innovate with limited resources during crises. The integration of SEM in both sources showcases its utility across various research domains, from validating theoretical models to understanding practical organizational behaviors.

## Key Findings

- Standardized-root-mean-squared-residual (SRMSR) and comparative-fit-index (CFI) are critical fit indexes for assessing structural equation modeling validity.
- Structural equation modeling can be applied beyond traditional social science research, such as in studying frugal innovation and organizational resilience in emerging markets.
