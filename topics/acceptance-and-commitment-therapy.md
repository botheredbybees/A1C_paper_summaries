## Acceptance and Commitment Therapy

The systematic review on therapeutic interventions for music performance anxiety (Source 1) and the systematic review on mindfulness and acceptance-based trainings for fostering self-care in mental health professionals (Source 2) both highlight the efficacy of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). ACT is characterized by its focus on mindfulness and psychological flexibility, which are key components in addressing stress and anxiety-related issues in various professional contexts. While Source 1 specifically addresses ACT's application in reducing performance anxiety among musicians, Source 2 explores its role in enhancing self-care and reducing stress among mental health professionals. Both sources acknowledge the importance of ACT in cultivating acceptance and commitment towards values-based actions, thereby fostering resilience against anxiety and stress. However, there is a gap in the literature regarding the long-term efficacy of ACT and the factors that contribute to sustained behavioral change.

## Key Findings

- Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) is effective in reducing anxiety and stress.
- Mindfulness and psychological flexibility are core components of ACT.
- ACT can be applied in diverse contexts, including music performance anxiety and professional stress management.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term outcomes of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy interventions?
