## Online Therapy

The shift towards online therapy interventions, as illustrated in the studies on ParkinDANCE Online and online therapeutic clowning, highlights the versatility and potential of digital health platforms in delivering therapeutic services. ParkinDANCE Online focuses on the use of online dance classes for individuals with Parkinson's disease, emphasizing the importance of participant engagement in maintaining the therapeutic benefits of dance. In contrast, the study on online therapeutic clowning explores the effectiveness of virtual healthcare settings for delivering clown-play interventions, a less conventional form of therapy. Both studies underscore the adaptability of traditional therapeutic methods to digital formats, enhancing accessibility and reach. However, they also highlight the challenges in maintaining therapeutic efficacy and participant engagement in the absence of in-person interaction.

## Key Findings

- Online therapy interventions can effectively deliver established therapeutic methods, such as dance and clowning, to participants with various conditions.
- Participant engagement is crucial for the success of online therapy sessions, particularly for maintaining therapeutic benefits.
- Digital health platforms have the potential to enhance accessibility and reach of therapeutic services.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of online therapy interventions on participant outcomes compared to traditional in-person methods?
