## Frugal Innovation

The synthesis of the provided sources highlights frugal innovation as a multifaceted approach to development and problem-solving, particularly in resource-constrained settings. Source 4 critically reviews the concept, identifying core characteristics that include affordability, accessibility, and sustainability. Sources 1, 2, and 3 explore various mechanisms for achieving these goals through different lenses: bricolage (creative use of available resources), constraint-based thinking, and iterative development processes respectively. All sources emphasize adaptability and efficiency in innovation under limitations such as cost, material availability, or market demand. However, while the theoretical underpinnings are robust, the practical implementation of frugal innovations faces challenges including quality assurance and sustaining environmental sustainability (Source 5).

## Key Findings

- Frugal innovation focuses on creating affordable solutions in resource-constrained environments.
- Constraint-based thinking is a structured approach to developing efficient, innovative products or services.
- Bricolage supports frugal innovation by fostering creativity through the flexible use of available resources.

## Open Questions

- How can quality assurance be effectively integrated into the process of frugal innovation without compromising affordability?
