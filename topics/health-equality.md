## Creative Health and Social Justice

The concept of creative health emerges as a holistic approach to healthcare that seeks to incorporate arts-based interventions into therapeutic practices. This theory emphasizes equitable access to healthcare services, aiming to bridge gaps in health equality for diverse populations. Source 1 introduces the idea of creative health, advocating for an inclusive and comprehensive view of wellbeing that includes artistic expression. Meanwhile, Source 2 focuses on the benefits of engaging children and young people with arts within natural settings, highlighting how such activities can promote sustainability and social justice. Both sources underscore the importance of equitable access to healthcare and arts-based opportunities, suggesting they are integral components of a socially just health system.

## Key Findings

- Creative health is a holistic approach to wellbeing that integrates artistic expression into healthcare practices.
- Equitable access to creative health interventions can contribute to reducing health inequalities among diverse populations.
- Incorporating arts-based activities in natural settings promotes both individual and community well-being, aligning with principles of social justice.

## Open Questions

- How can the integration of arts into healthcare be scaled up to ensure equitable access across different socioeconomic groups?
