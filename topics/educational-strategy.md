## Educational Strategies for Enhancing Creative and Cognitive Abilities

The provided sources explore educational strategies that aim to enhance cognitive and creative abilities among students. Source 1 focuses on the use of images as writing prompts to stimulate creativity and aid in memory association, suggesting that visual stimuli can effectively enhance narrative storytelling and creative writing skills. In contrast, Source 2 delves into the educational use of autobiographical narratives of mental illness to facilitate a deeper understanding of subjective experiences related to mental health. While these strategies differ in their approach and subject matter, both emphasize the power of narrative and visual stimuli in educational settings to improve cognitive processes and emotional understanding. However, there is a notable gap in the integration of these methods to address mental health through creative expression.

## Key Findings

- Visual stimuli can enhance memory association and creativity in writing.
- Autobiographical narratives of mental illness can provide valuable insights into subjective experiences.
- Narrative-based educational strategies can be effective in enhancing cognitive and emotional understanding.

## Open Questions

- How can narrative and visual educational strategies be integrated to address mental health through creative expression?
