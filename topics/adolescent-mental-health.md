## Adolescent Mental Health

The mental health of adolescents is a multifaceted issue, with depression and anxiety being prevalent concerns during the identity-exploration phase. Protective environments and intervention strategies are critical in mitigating these issues. A systematic review exploring the impact of arts and creative activities on adolescent mental health suggests potential benefits but highlights the need for rigorous causal inference methods to establish clear relationships between engagement in these activities and improved well-being.

## Key Findings

- Depression and anxiety are significant concerns for adolescents, impacting their identity exploration and overall development.
- Protective environments play a crucial role in supporting adolescent mental health.
- Engagement in arts and creative activities may positively influence adolescent mental health.

## Open Questions

- What specific causal mechanisms underlie the relationship between engagement in arts and creative activities and improved mental health among adolescents?
