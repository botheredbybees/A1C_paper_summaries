## Emotional Effects of Creative Arts

This synthesis explores the emotional impacts of engaging in creative arts, specifically haiku poetry and music, as examined in two distinct studies. The first study focuses on the emotional outcomes associated with creating haiku poetry, revealing a strong association with self-transcendent emotions such as gratitude and awe. The second study delves into the psychological and neural mechanisms behind the emotional responses to music. Both studies highlight the profound emotional benefits derived from artistic engagement, suggesting that these activities can significantly enhance emotional well-being by fostering a sense of serenity and wonder. However, there is a gap in understanding the comparative emotional impacts of different forms of creative arts and their underlying mechanisms.

## Key Findings

- Creative arts, such as haiku poetry and music, are associated with enhanced emotional well-being.
- Haiku poetry creation is linked to self-transcendent emotions like gratitude and awe.
- Music triggers complex emotional responses that are grounded in neural mechanisms.

## Open Questions

- What are the comparative emotional impacts and underlying mechanisms of different forms of creative arts?
