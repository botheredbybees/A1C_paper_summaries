## Lighting in Photography and Creative Experimentation

The integration of lighting in photography and creative experimentation is a central theme across the provided sources. In 'FXA101 Week 10 Welcome', the focus is on the role of lighting in enhancing creative experimentation and project planning, emphasizing its importance in setting the mood and focusing attention on key elements of visual content. Similarly, in 'Smartphone Photography Training for Students of MA Aisyiyah Sungguminasa Gowa', lighting is discussed in the context of composition and camera angles, highlighting its role in creating engaging visual content. Both sources underscore the versatility and significance of lighting as a methodological tool in photography, enabling photographers to effectively manipulate visual environments and enhance storytelling.

## Key Findings

- Lighting plays a crucial role in setting the mood and drawing attention in photographic compositions.
- Creative experimentation with lighting enhances the depth and engagement of visual content.
- Lighting is a fundamental methodological tool in both traditional and smartphone photography.
