## Multidisciplinary Approach in Music and Health

The utilization of a multidisciplinary approach in the domain of music and health highlights the integration of various disciplines to enhance therapeutic and well-being outcomes. Both sources emphasize the therapeutic potential of music, suggesting its role in not only mental and emotional well-being but also in physical health through motor control and innovative exercise programs. Source 1 introduces the concept of music as a vehicle for enhancing well-being, underpinned by a multidisciplinary approach that includes perspectives from psychology, medicine, and music therapy. Source 2 expands on this by incorporating motor control theories and the development of empathetic and present-focused practices within performing arts medicine. This dual focus on emotional and physical health through music underscores the comprehensive nature of the multidisciplinary approach, which can be tailored to address a wide range of health issues.

## Key Findings

- Music can serve as a therapeutic tool for enhancing both physical and mental well-being.
- A multidisciplinary approach, incorporating perspectives from various health and arts disciplines, is essential for optimizing the benefits of music in health interventions.
- Motor control theories and empathy-focused practices can be integrated into music-based health interventions to address specific physical health issues.

## Open Questions

- How can the multidisciplinary approach be further refined to address the unique needs of different populations within health and well-being interventions?
