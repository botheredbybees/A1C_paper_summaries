## Video Games as Cognitive and Therapeutic Tools

The integration of video games into therapeutic and cognitive enhancement practices showcases their versatile application beyond mere entertainment. A meta-analysis highlights the positive impact action video games have on perceptual, attentional, and cognitive skills, suggesting a role for these games in enhancing brain functions. Meanwhile, the MINWii Project demonstrates how interactive video games can serve as an effective medium for music therapy, particularly beneficial in managing behavioral symptoms of Alzheimer's disease through renarcissization. While action-oriented games emphasize cognitive enhancement, interactive music-based interventions focus on emotional and social engagement, indicating a broad spectrum of potential benefits depending on game type and therapeutic context.

## Key Findings

- Action video games can enhance perceptual, attentional, and cognitive skills.
- Interactive music therapy using video games is effective for managing behavioral symptoms in Alzheimer's disease patients.
- The use of video games in therapy highlights their potential beyond traditional entertainment.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of regular video game engagement on cognitive health?
