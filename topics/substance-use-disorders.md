## Music Therapy in Substance Use Disorders Treatment

Music therapy and music-based interventions emerge as promising practices in the treatment of substance use disorders (SUDs), supported by recent systematic reviews. These interventions are particularly effective in managing the psychological stress associated with SUDs. However, while music therapy shows potential, the integration of such interventions within broader therapeutic frameworks, such as trauma-informed care, presents challenges in practical application. The COPE model, which aims to integrate various therapies for comorbid post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and SUDs, highlights the need for further research into how music therapy can be effectively integrated into community treatment programs. The implementation of these interventions requires careful consideration of patient needs and the availability of trained therapists.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy and music-based interventions show promise in treating SUDs.
- Integration of these interventions into trauma-informed care models requires further exploration.
- The COPE model highlights the importance of integrated therapy approaches for comorbid conditions.

## Open Questions

- How can music therapy be effectively integrated into community substance abuse treatment programs?
