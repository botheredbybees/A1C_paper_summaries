## Healthcare Workers' Well-being Interventions

The well-being of healthcare workers (HCWs) is a multifaceted issue encompassing both psychological and occupational challenges. Sources highlight the need for interventions addressing HCW distress during pandemics, such as expressive writing to alleviate emotional burdens (Source 1). Simultaneously, organizational strategies are crucial in mitigating workplace aggression from patients and their advocates by addressing communication issues and mental health conditions (Source 2). While expressive writing provides individual-level support, organizational changes offer systemic solutions. Both sources underscore the importance of tailored interventions that address both psychological resilience and occupational safety to enhance HCW well-being.

## Key Findings

- Expressive writing can reduce psychological distress in healthcare workers during crises like pandemics.
- Addressing communication issues is essential for preventing workplace aggression directed at healthcare workers.
- Combining individual-level and organizational interventions offers a comprehensive approach to improving healthcare worker well-being.

## Open Questions

- How can expressive writing be integrated with organizational policies to enhance its effectiveness in reducing HCW distress?
