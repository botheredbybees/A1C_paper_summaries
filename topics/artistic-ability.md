## Artistic Ability in the Face of Cognitive Decline

The sources highlight the resilience and adaptability of artistic ability in the face of cognitive decline due to conditions like Alzheimer's disease. Source 1 discusses an individual with Alzheimer's who experiences changes in their artistic work, possibly due to visuospatial skills impairment, yet continues to create art. This underscores the continued engagement in creative activities despite cognitive impairment. Source 2 introduces the use of eye-gaze technology by Francis Tsai, an artist who adapted his method of creating art after a spinal cord injury, leading to significant physical limitations. This technology facilitated his artistic expression, showing how innovative tools can help maintain and enhance artistic ability even in the presence of severe physical constraints. Both sources emphasize the importance of adaptive strategies and technology in supporting artistic expression in the context of disability.

## Key Findings

- Artistic ability can persist despite cognitive decline.
- Adaptive technology can support continued artistic expression.
- Individuals with cognitive or physical impairments continue to engage in creative activities.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific neural mechanisms that support artistic activity in the context of cognitive decline?
