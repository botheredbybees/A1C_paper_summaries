## Music and Creative Thought

The reviewed studies highlight the multifaceted impact of music on cognitive states and creative thought. Source 1 explores how music can induce altered states of consciousness, including absorption and flow, which are conducive to creative thought. It suggests that music's ability to engage the mind in a focused yet open manner is crucial for entering a state of creative flow. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on the neurological basis of creative thought in the context of music, using brain-imaging techniques to uncover the roles of executive functions in spontaneous artistic creativity. Together, these sources suggest that music influences both the mental states that facilitate creativity and the neural mechanisms that underpin creative processes.

## Key Findings

- Music can induce altered states of consciousness that enhance creative thought.
- Executive functions play a critical role in musical creativity as revealed by brain imaging studies.
- Both absorption and flow are key states for facilitating creativity, supported by music.

## Open Questions

- How do the neurological processes identified in brain-imaging studies relate to the altered states of consciousness induced by music?
