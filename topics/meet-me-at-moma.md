## Meet Me At MoMA

The Meet Me at MoMA program is a reminiscence-based intervention for individuals with dementia, derived from the ARTZ (Artists and Artistic Works) program. This initiative emphasizes the concept of 'Potential Beyond Problems,' which asserts that people living with dementia still possess significant capabilities and can engage deeply in cultural and artistic activities. Both sources highlight how the program leverages art and memory to enhance cognitive function and social interaction among participants. There is a consistent theme across the sources on the positive impact of these programs on the quality of life for individuals with dementia, reinforcing the notion that artistic engagement can be beneficial in dementia care. However, there is a tension regarding the scalability and accessibility of such programs given the resources required to maintain high-quality artistic experiences.

## Key Findings

- Meet Me at MoMA is based on the ARTZ program, emphasizing reminiscence and artistic engagement.
- The concept of 'Potential Beyond Problems' underscores the abilities and potential of individuals with dementia.
- Artistic interventions like Meet Me at MoMA enhance cognitive function and social interaction in people with dementia.

## Open Questions

- How can such specialized programs be scaled up to reach a wider audience without compromising the quality of the experience?
