## Music Listening

The integration of findings from two studies on music listening highlights its multifaceted benefits for both physiological and psychological well-being. The first study explores the impact of self-selected favorite music, whether stimulative or sedative, revealing that familiar and preferred tunes have distinct effects on arousal levels and relaxation states in listeners. It shows a correlation between personal musical preferences and changes in heart rate variability (HRV) and cortisol responses, indicating that self-chosen music can act as an effective tool for managing stress-related physiological parameters. The second study delves into the role of music listening in enhancing adolescents' sense of agency and emotional well-being, emphasizing its potential to improve self-efficacy through various daily activities involving music engagement. Both studies underscore the significant influence of familiar music on personal health outcomes, suggesting tailored interventions could maximize these benefits.

## Key Findings

- Familiar and preferred music can modulate physiological arousal and relaxation responses in listeners.
- Music listening positively impacts adolescents' sense of agency and self-efficacy.
- Personalized music selection is crucial for optimizing the therapeutic effects on both physical and psychological health.

## Open Questions

- How do cultural backgrounds influence individual preferences for music that induces arousal or relaxation, and how might this affect intervention design?
