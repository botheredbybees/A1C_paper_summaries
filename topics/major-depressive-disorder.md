## Lifestyle Interventions for Major Depressive Disorder

The integration of lifestyle-based interventions as part of a holistic biopsychosocial-cultural framework to treat major depressive disorder (MDD) is gaining traction. Clinical guidelines underscore the importance of incorporating dietary changes, physical activity, and other lifestyle modifications in MDD management strategies. The SMILES trial provides empirical evidence supporting the efficacy of dietary improvement for adults with MDD, using the Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale as a metric to quantify clinical outcomes. These findings emphasize the necessity of considering individual cultural contexts and personal lifestyles when designing treatment protocols for patients suffering from MDD.

## Key Findings

- Lifestyle-based interventions can be effectively integrated into the management of major depressive disorder (MDD).
- The biopsychosocial-cultural framework offers a comprehensive approach to treating MDD, considering biological, psychological, social, and cultural factors.
- Dietary improvements show promise as an intervention strategy for adults with MDD.
