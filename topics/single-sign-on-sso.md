## Single Sign-On (Sso)

Both sources discuss Single Sign-On (SSO) in the context of Shibboleth, a framework used for implementing SSO. Shibboleth is highlighted as a key technology for managing authentication and authorization across multiple service providers. The sources are consistent in their emphasis on Shibboleth as a core component of SSO systems, illustrating its importance in facilitating seamless access to multiple resources without the need for repeated login credentials.

## Key Findings

- Shibboleth is a pivotal technology in implementing Single Sign-On (SSO) systems.
- SSO systems, facilitated by Shibboleth, streamline access to multiple resources.
- Shibboleth is essential for managing authentication and authorization in SSO environments.
