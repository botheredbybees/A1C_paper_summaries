## First-person Narratives in Disability Arts

The use of first-person narratives in disability arts emerges as a critical practice in both sources. Both emphasize the importance of first-person narratives as a tool for disability justice and inclusive culture. The narratives are seen as essential for creating a voice for individuals with disabilities and fostering inclusive practices. However, the sources highlight that for these narratives to be effective, they must be authentic and originate from the experiences of people with disabilities themselves, adhering to the principle that nothing about us should be decided without us. This principle underscores the need for genuine representation and participation in the creation and dissemination of narratives.

## Key Findings

- First-person narratives are crucial for disability justice and inclusive culture.
- Authentic first-person narratives originating from individuals with disabilities are essential for genuine representation.
- The principle 'nothing about us without us' guides the use of first-person narratives in disability arts.

## Open Questions

- What methods can be employed to ensure that first-person narratives truly reflect the experiences of individuals with disabilities?
