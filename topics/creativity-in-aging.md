## Creativity in Aging

The exploration of creativity as an intervention for aging highlights its multifaceted benefits for older adults. Both sources emphasize the importance of engaging in creative activities to enhance cognitive resilience and overall quality of life. Source 1 focuses on drama therapy, a form of expressive arts that encourages storytelling and facilitation among participants, fostering emotional expression and social interaction. This aligns with source 2's broader perspective on various forms of creativity enhancing older adults' well-being through mental engagement and personal fulfillment. However, while both sources stress the benefits of creative interventions, they offer different pathways to achieving these outcomes, highlighting the diversity in approaches and their impacts.

## Key Findings

- Creative activities enhance cognitive resilience in aging populations
- Expressive arts therapies like drama therapy promote emotional expression and social interaction among older adults
- Various forms of creativity contribute significantly to improving quality-of-life for older individuals

## Open Questions

- How do different types of creative interventions compare in their effectiveness for enhancing cognitive resilience and overall well-being?
