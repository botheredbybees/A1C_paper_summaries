## Digital Technologies in Artistic Practice

The integration of digital technologies in artistic practice, particularly for disabled artists, showcases a transformative impact through collaborative efforts and innovative tools. The EyeWriter project exemplifies the empowerment of individuals with disabilities through eye-tracking technology, a significant advancement in the realm of interactive art. This project not only highlights the potential of open-source collaboration but also underscores the importance of technology in enabling artistic expression. Source 2 expands on these themes by discussing the broader impact of digital technologies as assistive tools for disabled visual artists. While both sources emphasize the collaborative nature and empowerment aspects of such technologies, they also hint at the need for broader accessibility and technological advancement to meet the diverse needs of disabled artists. Together, these sources outline the critical role of digital technologies in not only supporting but also enhancing artistic capabilities for those with disabilities.

## Key Findings

- Digital technologies, such as eye-tracking, significantly empower disabled artists to engage in and create art.
- Open-source collaborations are pivotal in advancing and disseminating assistive technologies.
- Digital technologies can have a transformative impact on the artistic practices of disabled individuals.

## Open Questions

- How can digital technologies be further developed to cater to the varied needs of disabled artists beyond existing tools and methods?
