## Frugal Innovation

The concept of frugal innovation is characterized by the development and implementation of solutions that are cost-effective, sustainable, and accessible, particularly in resource-constrained environments. Source 1 provides a critical review highlighting the theoretical underpinnings and core characteristics of frugal innovation, emphasizing its role in addressing economic challenges through innovative yet simple means. Source 2 further elaborates on the practical application of these principles, focusing on sustainability and quality assurance as integral components of effective frugal innovations. Both sources underscore the importance of frugal innovation in providing affordable solutions but also highlight potential tensions regarding standardization and quality control.

## Key Findings

- Frugal innovation focuses on developing cost-effective solutions that address resource constraints and economic challenges
- Sustainability is a key aspect of successful frugal innovations, ensuring long-term impact and viability
- Quality assurance remains crucial despite the emphasis on simplicity and affordability

## Open Questions

- How can quality standards be effectively maintained while adhering to principles of cost-efficiency in frugal innovation?
