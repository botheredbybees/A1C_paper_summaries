## Therapeutic Techniques Across Developmental Stages

The therapeutic techniques of music therapy and play therapy are explored in the provided sources, each with unique applications and theoretical underpinnings. Music therapy, as an evidence-based allied health service, is adaptable across the lifespan, emphasizing client-centered care to address individual needs at various developmental stages. Play therapy, on the other hand, focuses on symbolic-play and Piaget's cognitive stages to enhance children's emotional expression and cognitive processing. Axline play therapy, a non-directive approach, is highlighted for its ability to foster self-discovery and emotional healing in children. Despite differences in target age groups and theoretical bases, both therapies emphasize the importance of therapeutic relationships and the flexibility of techniques to meet diverse client needs.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy is adaptable across the lifespan, catering to various developmental stages.
- Play therapy focuses on symbolic-play and cognitive stages to aid emotional and cognitive development in children.
- Both music and play therapies underscore the significance of client-centered care and therapeutic relationships.

## Open Questions

- How can the principles of music therapy be effectively integrated into play therapy for a more holistic approach to developmental and emotional issues in children?
