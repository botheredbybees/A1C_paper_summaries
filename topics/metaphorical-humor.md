## Metaphorical Humor

Both sources explore the role and construction of metaphorical humor within different comedic contexts, revealing its significance in stand-up comedy and satirical news shows. Source 1 delves into how metaphors serve as a primary mechanism for humor creation through conceptual blending theory and cognitive linguistics, emphasizing that metaphoric humor leverages pre-existing mental models to create novel or unexpected juxtapositions. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on identifying humorous metaphors within satirical content, applying the General Theory of Verbal Humor (GTVH) framework. This source highlights HMSN's unique ability to critique societal norms and institutions through metaphorical language that is both insightful and humorous. Despite varying contexts and theoretical frameworks, both sources underscore the importance of metaphor as a cornerstone in generating humor.

## Key Findings

- Metaphorical humor plays a crucial role in stand-up comedy by blending concepts in innovative ways to create unexpected juxtapositions.
- Humorous metaphors within satirical news shows serve dual functions: entertainment and societal critique, utilizing GTVH principles.

## Open Questions
