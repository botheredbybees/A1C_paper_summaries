## Developmental Trauma Disorder

The topic of Developmental Trauma Disorder (DTD) is approached through the lens of trauma-informed care and sensory-based interventions within creative arts therapies. Sources highlight the importance of understanding DTD as a condition that affects children who experience chronic or repetitive traumatic experiences during critical stages of development, leading to pervasive impairments in emotional regulation, attachment, and cognitive functioning. Trauma-informed care emphasizes the necessity for therapeutic environments and practices to be sensitive to the needs of individuals with a history of trauma, fostering safety, trustworthiness, collaboration, and empowerment. Sensory-based interventions are seen as crucial components of DTD treatment plans due to their ability to address symptoms rooted in traumatic experiences that impact sensory processing and integration. This approach underscores the role of creative arts therapies, such as music therapy, dance movement therapy, and art therapy, which provide non-verbal means for clients to express themselves, process trauma, and develop coping mechanisms.

## Key Findings

- Trauma-informed care is essential in treating developmental trauma disorder.
- Sensory-based interventions are critical components of DTD treatment plans.
- Creative arts therapies offer effective modalities for addressing the symptoms of DTD.
