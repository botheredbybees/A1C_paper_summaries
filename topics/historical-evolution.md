## Historical Evolution of Music Therapy

The historical development of music therapy highlights the interplay between theoretical understanding and practical application. Source 1 focuses on the early recognition of music's therapeutic potential and the professionalization of music therapy as a distinct field. This source underscores the shift from intuitive use to systematic approaches, influenced by evolving medical paradigms and interdisciplinary collaborations. In contrast, Source 2 delves into the physical principles underpinning music, including the evolution of tuning systems over centuries. While this source does not directly address therapeutic applications, it provides foundational knowledge on how physics informs our understanding of musical structures, potentially influencing theoretical frameworks for music therapy.

## Key Findings

- The recognition of music's healing properties has been acknowledged since ancient times and continues to evolve with modern medical research.
- The professionalization of music therapy as a distinct field is marked by formal training programs and institutional recognition.
- Interdisciplinary collaboration, including the integration of physics principles, enriches both theoretical frameworks and practical applications in music therapy.
