## Eye Tracking Technology

Eye tracking technology, as explored in both sources, presents a multifaceted tool with applications in both artificial intelligence and healthcare. Source 1 highlights the EyeWriter, a system that employs machine learning and computer vision to interpret eye movements and enable communication through a virtual keyboard. This demonstrates the potential for eye tracking to facilitate communication in individuals with disabilities that limit their physical mobility. Source 2 focuses on the use of eye tracking communication devices in patients with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). It emphasizes the significant impact these devices have on improving the quality of life and managing disability for individuals with ALS, reinforcing the practical and beneficial role of eye tracking technology in enhancing user interaction and communication. Both sources underscore the potential of eye tracking as an enabling technology in diverse contexts, from personal communication to medical assistance, highlighting the cross-disciplinary utility of eye tracking methods.

## Key Findings

- Eye tracking technology can facilitate communication for individuals with limited physical mobility.
- Machine learning and computer vision enhance the functionality and accessibility of eye tracking devices.
- Eye tracking communication devices significantly improve the quality of life for ALS patients.
