## Mourning Process

The process of mourning is multifaceted, incorporating both emotional journeys and therapeutic interventions. Source 1 emphasizes the emotional journey of grieving at a seaside pier, where the ocean symbolizes the depth and breadth of grief, and through this symbolic interaction, individuals find strength and embark on a path of self-discovery. This emotional exploration is complemented by Source 2, which delves into the therapeutic benefits of music therapy in managing grief and mourning. Music therapy offers a structured way for individuals to express their emotions and integrate their identities in the face of loss. Together, these sources illustrate the importance of both natural and therapeutic environments in the healing process of mourning.

## Key Findings

- Grief can be symbolically processed through natural environments like the seaside.
- Music therapy aids in the emotional expression and integration of identity during mourning.
- Both natural and therapeutic settings provide avenues for self-discovery and strength-building in the face of loss.

## Open Questions

- How can the symbolic power of natural settings be integrated into structured therapeutic interventions like music therapy to enhance the mourning process?
