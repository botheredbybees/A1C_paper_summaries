## Psychosocial Aspects of Burn Injuries

The topic of psychosocial aspects of burn injuries highlights the importance of comprehensive care beyond physical treatment. Art therapy emerges as a vital component in aiding patients' psychosocial adjustment and overall recovery, offering a unique platform for self-expression and emotional processing. Cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) is another intervention that addresses psychological distress, including post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), which commonly affects burn injury patients. The integration of these therapeutic approaches underscores the necessity of a multidisciplinary approach in treating burn injuries, emphasizing the holistic nature of care required for these patients.

## Key Findings

- Art therapy significantly contributes to the psychosocial adjustment of burn injury patients.
- Cognitive-behavioral therapy is an effective intervention for managing PTSD in burn injury patients.
- A multidisciplinary approach incorporating various therapeutic methods is crucial for comprehensive care of burn injury patients.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of combining art therapy and cognitive-behavioral therapy in the management of burn injuries?
