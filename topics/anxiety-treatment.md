## Art Therapy for Anxiety

Both sources discuss the effectiveness of art-based interventions in treating anxiety. Source 1 presents a systematic review focusing specifically on adult populations, employing narrative synthesis and assessing risk-of-bias to evaluate evidence supporting art therapy as an intervention for reducing anxiety symptoms. In contrast, Source 2 broadens the scope by exploring Arts on Prescription (AoP) programs, which integrate social prescribing strategies into healthcare systems to enhance mental well-being. While both sources highlight positive outcomes in reducing anxiety levels through creative arts engagement, there are differences in methodologies and participant demographics considered within each study.

## Key Findings

- Art therapy is effective in mitigating anxiety symptoms for adult populations.
- Arts on Prescription programs demonstrate improvements in anxiety along with depression and overall well-being.
- Narrative synthesis alongside risk-of-bias assessment provides a robust evaluation of art therapy's efficacy.

## Open Questions

- How do social prescribing strategies within Arts on Prescription differ from traditional individual art therapy sessions, and what are the implications for treatment outcomes?
