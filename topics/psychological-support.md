## Psychological Support Interventions

The reviewed literature highlights two distinct yet complementary approaches to psychological support: creative arts-based therapies for stroke survivors and psychological first aid for disaster-affected populations. Creative arts-based therapies aim to provide psychological support, social engagement, and spiritual experiences, fostering functional restoration in stroke survivors. In contrast, psychological first aid focuses on providing timely, compassionate, and practical assistance to individuals experiencing emotional and physical distress following disasters. Both interventions emphasize the importance of psychological support but cater to different populations and contexts, illustrating the versatility and adaptability of psychological support strategies.

## Key Findings

- Creative arts-based therapies and psychological first aid both aim to provide psychological support, albeit in different contexts and to different populations.
- Both approaches highlight the importance of social engagement and emotional support in enhancing recovery and well-being.
- Psychological support interventions can vary widely in their application, targeting specific needs arising from health conditions or environmental disasters.

## Open Questions

- How can creative arts-based therapies and psychological first aid be adapted to address the unique psychological needs of diverse populations affected by both health conditions and disasters?
