## Arts Accessibility

The synthesis integrates the findings from both sources to highlight the role of arts accessibility in engaging various populations. Source 1 emphasizes the importance of participatory arts for creative aging, particularly focusing on the underrepresented demographic of men. It highlights the benefits of engaging in creative activities, such as increased social interaction and improved mental health. Source 2 discusses the integration of assistive technology and inclusive design principles to enhance the accessibility of arts for individuals with disabilities. The sources together suggest a need for a broader approach to arts accessibility that combines participatory arts programs with technological advancements to ensure that all individuals can engage in and benefit from the arts. Tensions exist between the conceptual framework and practical implementation, as participatory arts may not fully accommodate those with physical limitations unless paired with assistive technologies. Furthermore, there is a need to encourage more men to engage in creative aging activities, and to ensure these activities are designed with accessibility in mind.

## Key Findings

- Participatory arts programs can significantly benefit older adults by fostering social connections and enhancing mental health.
- Incorporating assistive technology and inclusive design principles is crucial for making arts accessible to individuals with disabilities.
- Combining participatory arts with assistive technology can provide a more inclusive approach to arts engagement.

## Open Questions

- How can participatory arts programs be adapted to better include men in creative aging activities?
