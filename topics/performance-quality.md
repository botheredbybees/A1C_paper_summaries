## Musical Performance Quality

Sources 1 and 2 both explore dimensions of performance quality in musicians, albeit from different perspectives. Source 1 quantitatively analyzes musical performance anxiety among singing students, finding correlations between anxiety levels and performance quality. Conversely, Source 2 reviews literature on musculoskeletal disorders and postural quality in musicians, emphasizing the importance of posture for physical health and performance quality. Both sources highlight the necessity of addressing these factors to improve overall performance quality and musician well-being. However, while Source 1 focuses on psychological aspects, Source 2 delves into physical health, indicating a need for a more holistic approach that integrates both mental and physical dimensions of performance.

## Key Findings

- Performance anxiety impacts the quality of musical performances.
- Postural quality is crucial for musculoskeletal health and performance quality.
- Integrating medical and music education could enhance the understanding of performance quality and musician well-being.

## Open Questions

- How can interventions address both psychological and physical aspects of performance quality simultaneously?
