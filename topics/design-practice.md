## Research Through and Through Design

The concept of 'research through design' (RtD) is explored in both sources as a methodological approach within the field of design. Source 1 emphasizes RtD as an integral part of the design process, focusing on sustainability-focused research and practice-as-research methodologies. It suggests that designers engage deeply with their projects from conception to completion, integrating research at every stage of the design cycle. In contrast, Source 2 delves into the dialogic model of design inspired by Schön's work, expanding on the concept of 'materials in design' and introducing the broader idea of 'mediums.' This source highlights the importance of understanding not just physical materials but also conceptual mediums that can influence the direction and outcomes of design projects. Both sources converge on the notion that research is an ongoing dialogue within design practice, emphasizing iterative processes and the continuous exchange between theory and application.

## Key Findings

- Research through design involves integrating research into all stages of the design process.
- The dialogic model of design emphasizes a dynamic interaction between materials and conceptual mediums.
- Understanding both tangible and intangible elements is crucial for effective design practice.

## Open Questions

- How can designers effectively balance theoretical insights with practical application in their work?
