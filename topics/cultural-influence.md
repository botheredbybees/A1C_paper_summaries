## Cultural Influence on Human Behavior

The concept of cultural influence is central to both sources. Source 1 examines Albrecht Dürer through a historical and cultural lens, illustrating how an individual's work reflects broader societal shifts and values. This aligns with the theoretical exploration in Source 2, which delves into life themes emerging from sociocultural environments as determinants of human behavior over time. Both sources suggest that cultural context significantly shapes personal expression and behavioral patterns, reflecting a deep interplay between historical conditions and individual experiences.

## Key Findings

- Cultural contexts profoundly influence individual behaviors and expressions
- Historical analysis can reveal the impact of cultural forces on artists and thinkers
- Life themes are shaped by the sociocultural environments in which individuals operate

## Open Questions

- How do changing cultural influences affect life themes over different historical periods?
