## Autism Education Approaches

The educational approaches for students with Autism Spectrum Disorders (ASD) are varied and informed by both narrative ethics and research-driven methods. Source 1 emphasizes the importance of naturalized narrative ethics, suggesting that narratives can enhance moral imagination and ethical sensitivity among individuals with ASD. This approach focuses on storytelling and narrative construction as a means of ethical development. Conversely, Source 2 highlights the necessity of evidence-based educational interventions for ASD. It underscores the need for precise identification and diagnosis, alongside tailored educational approaches that are grounded in empirical research. Both sources recognize the complexity of educating students with ASD, yet they differ in their methodological approaches: one advocating for narrative ethics, the other for research-driven interventions. Despite these divergent methods, both sources agree on the importance of individualized education and ethical considerations.

## Key Findings

- Narrative ethics can enhance moral imagination and ethical sensitivity among individuals with ASD.
- Research-driven educational interventions are crucial for the effective teaching of students with ASD.
- Both narrative and research-driven approaches highlight the importance of individualized education.

## Open Questions

- What is the optimal balance between narrative ethics and research-driven educational interventions in ASD education?
