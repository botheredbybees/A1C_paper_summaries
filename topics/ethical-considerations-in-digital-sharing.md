## Ethical Considerations in Digital Sharing

The sources discussed highlight the increasing importance and complexity of ethical considerations in digital sharing. As technology facilitates creative expression and collaboration across various platforms, individuals are navigating a landscape fraught with privacy concerns, copyright issues, and the potential for misinformation. While some advocate for the unrestricted sharing that technology enables, others raise critical questions about consent, ownership rights, and the moral implications of unchecked dissemination of personal content or ideas. The integration of IT in creativity thus demands robust ethical frameworks to safeguard individual liberties and intellectual property while fostering a collaborative digital culture.

## Key Findings

- Technological advancements enable greater creative expression through digital sharing platforms.
- Ethical considerations such as privacy, consent, and copyright infringement are crucial in the context of digital sharing.
- There is a need for balanced approaches to leverage technology's benefits while addressing its ethical challenges.

## Open Questions

- How can societal norms around ownership and sharing be reconciled with technological capabilities that facilitate rapid dissemination?
