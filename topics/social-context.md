## The Social Context of Health and Music

The social context in which health and music are experienced profoundly shapes their impact and interpretation. Source 1 delves into the social and applied psychology of music, emphasizing the importance of understanding music consumption and behavior within a broader social context. This source highlights interdisciplinary approaches and naturalistic methodologies as crucial for capturing the nuances of how individuals engage with and perceive music. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on the humanities and narrative medicine, advocating for a biopsychosocial approach to patient-professional relationships. It underscores the significance of social determinants of health in influencing health outcomes and the narrative of patients' experiences. Both sources converge in their emphasis on the social environment, but diverge in their specific applications: one in music psychology, the other in health humanities. This synthesis reveals a broader theoretical framework that integrates social determinants into the study of both health and music, promoting a holistic understanding of human behavior and experience.

## Key Findings

- The social context significantly influences both musical behavior and health outcomes.
- Interdisciplinary approaches and naturalistic methodologies enhance the understanding of social determinants in music and health.
- Narrative and biopsychosocial models offer comprehensive frameworks for understanding patient experiences and musical engagement.

## Open Questions

- How can the integration of music into health humanities frameworks improve patient care and well-being?
