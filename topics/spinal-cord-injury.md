## Therapeutic Recreation and Quality of Life for Spinal Cord Injury Patients

The integration of therapeutic recreation and artistic activities has been shown to enhance psychological well-being and functional independence in individuals with spinal cord injuries. Both sources highlight the critical role of non-traditional interventions in the rehabilitation process. Source 1 emphasizes the impact of artistic activities on psychological well-being, using the Psychological General Well-being Index as a measure of success. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on therapeutic recreation and its correlation with functional independence and quality of life. Despite the different emphases, both sources underscore the importance of holistic approaches to rehabilitation that go beyond traditional medical treatments to improve overall life satisfaction and recovery outcomes for spinal cord injury patients. However, there is a gap in understanding the long-term effects of these interventions and the specific mechanisms through which they enhance quality of life.

## Key Findings

- Artistic activities contribute positively to the psychological well-being of spinal cord injury patients.
- Therapeutic recreation interventions enhance functional independence and quality of life for spinal cord injury patients.
- Holistic approaches, including non-traditional interventions like art and recreation, are essential for comprehensive rehabilitation.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of therapeutic recreation and artistic activities on the quality of life for individuals with spinal cord injuries?
