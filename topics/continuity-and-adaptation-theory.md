## Continuity and Adaptation Theory

The continuity and adaptation theory explores how aging individuals maintain a sense of self-continuity while adapting to life changes. This theory is crucial in understanding the reciprocal influence between aging and creative activities, such as art involvement. Sources indicate that engaging in artistic endeavors can facilitate personal growth and cognitive flexibility among older adults, aligning with the concept of 'creative expansion with age'. However, there are differing views on how much this adaptation impacts overall well-being; some studies suggest it contributes to a sense of coherence and fulfillment, while others highlight challenges in maintaining continuous engagement due to physical constraints or societal expectations. The theory also intersects with gerotranscendence, suggesting that artistic activities can help older adults achieve higher levels of existential awareness and emotional depth.

## Key Findings

- Engaging in creative activities like art can enhance personal growth and cognitive flexibility among aging individuals.
- The continuity and adaptation theory helps explain how people maintain a sense of self-continuity while adapting to age-related changes.
- There is variability in the extent to which artistic engagement impacts overall well-being, depending on individual circumstances and societal support.

## Open Questions

- How do physical limitations and societal expectations influence older adults' ability to engage in creative activities that promote continuity and adaptation?
