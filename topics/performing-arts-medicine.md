## Performing Arts Medicine

Performing arts medicine integrates interdisciplinary approaches to address the unique health needs of performing artists. Sources highlight the therapeutic relationship and patient care through music, emphasizing music therapy and music medicine as critical components. The importance of a holistic health approach, preventive measures, and innovative exercise programs, such as those focusing on motor control theory, is underscored. Empathy and presence in the therapeutic relationship are also key elements in enhancing health and wellbeing. Each source emphasizes the multidisciplinary nature of performing arts medicine, which includes perspectives from psychology, physiology, and other health sciences, aiming to provide a comprehensive care framework for musicians and other performers.

## Key Findings

- Interdisciplinary approaches, including music therapy and medicine, are central to performing arts medicine.
- Motor control theory and innovative exercise programs are utilized to address specific health needs of performers.
- Holistic health and preventive measures are essential components of performing arts medicine.
