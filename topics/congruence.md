## Congruence in Person-Centered Approaches

The concept of congruence is a cornerstone principle within the person-centered approach as illustrated by both sources. Source 1 underscores the importance of congruence alongside unconditional positive regard and empathy, suggesting that practitioners must be genuine and authentic in their interactions to foster trust and psychological safety with clients or children. Similarly, source 2 highlights how congruent practices contribute to inclusive educational environments and effective teamwork within person-centered planning initiatives. Both sources agree on the necessity for practitioners to align their words and actions to ensure authenticity and consistency when employing a person-centered approach.

## Key Findings

- Congruence is essential in establishing trust and psychological safety within the person-centered approach.
- The effectiveness of inclusive practices and teamwork is bolstered by congruent behavior among practitioners.

## Open Questions

- How can the concept of congruence be operationalized or measured to assess its impact on the efficacy of person-centered approaches?
