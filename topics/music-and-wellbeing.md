## Music Therapy and Wellbeing

Music therapy is a versatile intervention that enhances wellbeing across diverse populations, including refugees and stroke patients. Both sources highlight its therapeutic benefits, with Source 1 focusing on how music therapy aids stroke recovery through neurologic applications. Meanwhile, Source 2 examines how music helps refugees express emotions, maintain a sense of self, and foster social connections in protracted displacement contexts. Despite these varied applications, both sources underscore the fundamental role of music in enhancing emotional and social health. However, there is a tension regarding the specific therapeutic mechanisms and whether the benefits of music therapy are consistent across different demographic and clinical groups.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy enhances emotional and social wellbeing in diverse populations.
- Neurologic music therapy aids in stroke recovery.
- Music facilitates emotional expression and social cohesion among refugees.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific therapeutic mechanisms of music therapy that make it effective for different populations?
