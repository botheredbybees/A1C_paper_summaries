## Health Professionals' Therapeutic Roles

The exploration of health professionals' therapeutic roles through narratives and communication skills reveals diverse strategies employed to support patient well-being. Source 1 delves into the educational aspect of mental health, emphasizing the significance of autobiographical narratives and subjective experiences in teaching about mental illness. By integrating personal stories, educators can humanize conditions and foster empathy among learners. Conversely, Source 2 focuses on medical clowns, highlighting their unique communication skills and therapeutic goals. Medical clowns contribute to the therapeutic environment through entertainment and emotional support, demonstrating the power of non-traditional approaches in healthcare settings. Both sources underscore the importance of narrative and interpersonal skills in healthcare, suggesting that these elements can play a crucial role in enhancing patient care and education. However, there is a tension between the structured educational approach in mental health and the more spontaneous, playful methods employed by medical clowns.

## Key Findings

- Autobiographical narratives and subjective experiences are valuable tools in mental health education.
- Medical clowns use unique communication skills to achieve therapeutic goals in healthcare settings.
- Narrative and interpersonal skills play a significant role in enhancing patient care and education.

## Open Questions

- How can structured educational methods in mental health be integrated with spontaneous, playful approaches like those used by medical clowns?
