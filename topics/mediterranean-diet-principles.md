## Mediterranean Diet Principles

The Mediterranean diet is a nutritional approach rooted in the traditional eating patterns of countries bordering the Mediterranean Sea. Both sources highlight its core principles and their potential health benefits, focusing on whole foods like fruits, vegetables, legumes, nuts, olive oil, fish, and moderate wine consumption while minimizing processed foods, red meat, and added sugars. Source 1 underscores the Planetary Health approach, which advocates for dietary patterns that not only support individual health but also sustain environmental resources. This aligns with the Mediterranean diet's emphasis on locally sourced plant-based foods. In contrast, Source 2 delves into mental health benefits of a mindful awareness practice when following the Mediterranean diet, suggesting it can enhance nutritional adherence and improve psychological well-being through conscious food selection.

## Key Findings

- The Mediterranean diet emphasizes whole foods and minimizes processed items for better health outcomes.
- Adherence to Mediterranean diet principles supports both individual health and environmental sustainability.
- Mindful awareness practices may improve mental health when combined with the Mediterranean diet.
