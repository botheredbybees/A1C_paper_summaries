## Overprescribing of Mindfulness

Sources indicate a concern over the overprescribing of mindfulness practices, which has led to the commercialization and potential unethical application of these practices. The concept of 'McMindfulness' is introduced, suggesting that mindfulness practices are being oversimplified and overused in a way that undermines their effectiveness and ethical integrity. Both sources emphasize the need for a critical evaluation of mindfulness interventions, highlighting issues related to their overprescription in various contexts. The commercialization of mindfulness has led to concerns about its ethical application, underlining the importance of considering the broader implications of these practices beyond their intended benefits.

## Key Findings

- Mindfulness practices are being overprescribed in various contexts.
- The commercialization of mindfulness has led to ethical concerns.
- Critical evaluation is necessary to ensure the ethical and effective use of mindfulness interventions.

## Open Questions

- How can the overprescription of mindfulness be addressed while maintaining its potential benefits?
