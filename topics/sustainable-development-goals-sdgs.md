## Sustainable Development Goals

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are an overarching framework designed to ensure global sustainability and equity by the year 2030. Source 1 highlights SDG 12, which focuses on responsible consumption and production, through the lens of Material Driven Design (MDD), using waste products such as rosin and sawdust in jewelry design to promote environmental sustainability. In contrast, Source 2 emphasizes another critical aspect of the SDGs: good health and well-being under SDG 3, which underscores health as a fundamental human right and advocates for disease prevention strategies. Both sources underscore the interconnectedness of various goals within the framework of sustainable development. However, while they provide insights into different SDGs, the discussion is limited to two out of the eighteen total goals, hinting at a broader scope and application required for comprehensive sustainability.

## Key Findings

- The Sustainable Development Goals aim to achieve global equity and environmental responsibility by 2030
- SDG 12 focuses on responsible consumption and production through innovative design practices such as Material Driven Design (MDD)
- Good health and well-being, SDG 3, is essential for overall human rights and societal development

## Open Questions

- How can the remaining sixteen SDGs be integrated into broader educational frameworks alongside SDGs 12 and 3?
