## Reminiscence Therapy

The synthesis integrates findings from sources on reminiscence therapy and its applications in dementia care. Source 1 highlights a pilot program combining reminiscence, art making, and cultural heritage to engage individuals with dementia through multi-sensorial experiences. This approach emphasizes the therapeutic value of visual arts interventions in enhancing cognitive function and emotional well-being among patients. Source 2 presents a scoping review on the role of reminiscence within arts-based interventions for dementia care. It underscores that such interventions significantly benefit from incorporating personal memories, leading to improved quality of life and social interaction. Both sources converge on the therapeutic efficacy of reminiscence therapy when integrated into visual art practices or broader cultural activities.

## Key Findings

- Reminiscence therapy enhances cognitive function in individuals with dementia
- Combining reminiscence with arts-based interventions improves emotional well-being
- Incorporating personal memories and multi-sensorial engagement in care programs is beneficial

## Open Questions

- How can the therapeutic benefits of visual arts be maximized for different stages of dementia?
