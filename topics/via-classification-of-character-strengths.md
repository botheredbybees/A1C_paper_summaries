## VIA Classification of Character Strengths

The VIA Classification of Character Strengths, introduced by Dr. Martin Seligman, is a framework designed to identify and apply individual strengths to enhance personal well-being and psychological functioning. Across the three sources, the VIA is consistently highlighted for its ability to categorize strengths into six core virtues: wisdom, courage, humanity, justice, temperance, and transcendence. Each virtue encompasses multiple character strengths, such as creativity under wisdom and kindness under humanity. The VIA Inventory of Strengths (VIA-IS) serves as a standardized assessment tool to measure these strengths, with the research demonstrating high levels of reliability and validity. The VIA-IS has been translated into numerous languages, and studies have shown that these translations maintain measurement invariance, indicating that the construct is consistently measured across different cultures. Despite its robustness, the VIA faces challenges in ensuring that its strengths are accurately translated and interpreted in various cultural contexts, which remains an active area of research. The application of signature strengths, or the most prominent strengths of an individual, has been linked to increased life satisfaction and reduced depressive symptoms, highlighting the practical utility of the VIA in clinical and educational settings.

## Key Findings

- The VIA Classification of Character Strengths categorizes human strengths into six core virtues.
- The VIA Inventory of Strengths (VIA-IS) is a reliable and valid tool for assessing individual strengths.
- Measurement invariance has been demonstrated in multiple translations of the VIA-IS, suggesting cross-cultural consistency.
