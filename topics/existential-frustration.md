## Existential Frustration and Meaning-Centered Therapies

Existential frustration, a fundamental concept in logotherapy, refers to the psychological distress arising from the human experience's inherent conflict between the desire for meaning and the limited capacity to achieve it. Logotherapy, as an intervention, focuses on helping individuals find personal meaning in their lives despite the limitations and frustrations they face. It posits the 'will to meaning' as a primary human motivation and addresses existential frustration through various therapeutic techniques. Meaning-Oriented Music Therapy (MOMT) extends logotherapy's principles by integrating music into the process of discovering and reinforcing personal meaning. Both logotherapy and MOMT address the core issue of existential frustration by offering pathways to overcome this existential challenge and promote psychological well-being.

## Key Findings

- Logotherapy addresses existential frustration through the concept of the 'will to meaning'.
- Meaning-Oriented Music Therapy (MOMT) builds on logotherapy by incorporating music into the therapeutic process.
- Both interventions aim to enhance psychological well-being by helping individuals find personal meaning.
