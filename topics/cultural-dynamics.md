## Cultural Dynamics of Post-War Europe

The cultural dynamics of post-war Europe are explored through the lenses of historical trauma and socio-genetic perspectives, highlighting the societal impact of significant events. Source 1 discusses the socio-genetic perspective, which examines how culture and society influence individual development, while focusing on historical trauma to understand the lasting impact of war on subsequent generations. In contrast, Source 2 delves into the artistic movement of Post-Expressionism in Germany during the hyperinflation era, reflecting the societal upheaval and economic crisis through the New Objectivity movement. Both sources highlight the transformative effects of historical events on cultural and artistic expressions, indicating a deep interconnection between societal conditions and cultural outputs.

## Key Findings

- Historical trauma significantly influences cultural and societal development in the post-war era.
- The socio-genetic perspective provides a framework for understanding the impact of societal changes on individual and collective identity.
- Artistic movements such as Post-Expressionism and New Objectivity serve as reflections of broader societal and economic conditions.

## Open Questions

- How can the socio-genetic perspective be further applied to understand the influence of specific historical events on cultural production and societal identity formation?
