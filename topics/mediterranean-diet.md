## Mediterranean Diet

The integration of multiple studies on the impact of a Mediterranean-style diet and fish oil supplementation highlights its potential benefits for mental health and overall well-being. These sources converge in their focus on depression, dietary behavior change, and health-related quality of life improvements linked to adherence to a Mediterranean diet supplemented with fish oil. Notably, all three documents underscore the modulation of inflammatory responses and regulation of gut microbiota as key mechanisms by which such diets contribute to mental health benefits. The randomized controlled trials further demonstrate that this dietary intervention can lead to significant reductions in depression severity and marked improvements in quality-of-life scores among individuals suffering from depressive symptoms.

## Key Findings

- Adherence to a Mediterranean-style diet supplemented with fish oil leads to improvements in mental health and overall well-being.
- Modulation of inflammatory responses and regulation of gut microbiota are mechanisms through which the Mediterranean diet influences mental health outcomes.
- Randomized controlled trials provide evidence for reductions in depression severity and enhanced quality-of-life scores among individuals on this dietary regimen.

## Open Questions
