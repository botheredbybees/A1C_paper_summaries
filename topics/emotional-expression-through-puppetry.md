## Emotional Expression Through Puppetry

The integration of puppetry as a therapeutic intervention for emotional expression highlights its utility in facilitating non-verbal communication and mediated expression. Both sources emphasize the potential for puppetry to serve as an effective icebreaker, allowing participants to explore their emotions in a controlled and sustainable manner without the immediate pressure of direct verbal communication. The use of puppets appears to create a safe space where individuals can externalize feelings through the manipulation of objects, thereby enhancing therapeutic outcomes. However, both sources also acknowledge challenges related to sustainability in arts practices, suggesting that while puppetry is beneficial, its long-term implementation may face logistical and financial barriers.

## Key Findings

- Puppetry serves as a medium for non-verbal communication and mediated expression of emotions.
- The use of puppets can act as an icebreaker in therapeutic settings.
- Sustainability remains a challenge for maintaining puppetry-based interventions.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term impacts of using puppetry on emotional well-being, and how can its sustainability be improved?
