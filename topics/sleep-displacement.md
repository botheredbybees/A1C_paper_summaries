## Technology and Sleep Displacement

The integration of technology into daily routines has led to significant disruptions in sleep patterns due to factors such as the 'attention economy' and 'variable ratio reinforcement', which are mechanisms designed by tech companies to keep users engaged. Both sources highlight that positive reinforcement through intermittent rewards is a key driver for prolonged screen time, contributing significantly to sleep displacement. Mindfulness strategies and understanding the impact of screen time on sleep quality offer potential interventions to mitigate these effects.

## Key Findings

- Technology use before bedtime disrupts sleep patterns due to variable ratio reinforcement and positive reinforcement techniques.
- Screen-time has significant negative impacts on sleep quality, which is exacerbated by the 'attention economy'.
- Mindfulness strategies can be employed as an intervention to reduce screen time prior to sleeping.

## Open Questions

- How effective are mindfulness strategies in reducing screen time and improving sleep patterns in long-term usage?
