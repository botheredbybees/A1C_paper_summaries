## Cognitive Flexibility in Creative Thinking

Both sources highlight the role of cognitive flexibility in enhancing creative thinking skills. Source 1 focuses on adolescence as a critical period for developing creativity through training divergent thinking, which is closely linked to cognitive flexibility. This source suggests that adolescents are particularly responsive to interventions aimed at fostering cognitive flexibility and thus can enhance their creative abilities during this developmental stage. In contrast, Source 2 explores the use of cognitive-based creativity training programs designed specifically to improve both convergent and divergent thinking skills among participants. This approach underscores the importance of cognitive flexibility in navigating between these two modes of thought to generate novel ideas and solutions effectively.

## Key Findings

- Cognitive flexibility plays a crucial role in enhancing creative thinking.
- Adolescence is an optimal period for training cognitive flexibility due to developmental plasticity.
- Both divergent and convergent thinking skills benefit from interventions that target cognitive flexibility.

## Open Questions
