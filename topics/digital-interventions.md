## Digital Interventions in Health and Therapy

Digital interventions in health and therapy are increasingly gaining traction, especially for populations with specific needs such as Parkinson's disease and autism spectrum disorders. Source 1 discusses the ParkinDANCE Online program, a digital health intervention that provides dance classes for individuals with Parkinson's disease, emphasizing the importance of participant engagement and its positive impact on physical and mental health. Meanwhile, Source 2 focuses on expressive arts therapy and tele-dance movement psychotherapy for individuals on the autism spectrum, highlighting the role of empathy mirroring and digital platforms in enhancing therapeutic outcomes. Both sources underscore the potential of digital tools to expand access to therapeutic interventions and improve patient outcomes, though they vary in their focus on specific conditions and therapeutic modalities.

## Key Findings

- Digital interventions are effective in engaging participants with Parkinson's disease and individuals on the autism spectrum.
- Tele-dance movement psychotherapy and expressive arts therapy are promising digital health interventions for autism spectrum disorders.
- Participant engagement is crucial for the success of digital health programs like ParkinDANCE Online.

## Open Questions

- How can digital health interventions be tailored to address the diverse needs of individuals with Parkinson's disease and autism spectrum disorders?
