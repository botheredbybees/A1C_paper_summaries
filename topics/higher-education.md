## Higher Education Pedagogy

In the realm of higher education pedagogy, the integration of Q methodology and intrinsic motivation emerges as a pivotal approach to enhancing self-reflection, professional development, and lifelong learning among educators and students. Source 1 underscores the application of Q methodology to foster reflective practices and professional growth in pre-service teachers, illustrating how this method facilitates the articulation and analysis of individual perspectives and values. In contrast, Source 2 emphasizes the role of intrinsic motivation in effective teaching, highlighting the significance of a supportive environment that nurtures intrinsic motivation and contributes to sustained engagement in professional development. Both sources highlight the necessity for methods that enhance personal and professional growth, yet they present distinct pathways: Q methodology for systematic reflection and intrinsic motivation for fostering long-term engagement.

## Key Findings

- Q methodology can facilitate reflective practices and professional development in pre-service teachers.
- Intrinsic motivation is crucial for effective teaching and lifelong learning.
- Environmental support plays a vital role in nurturing intrinsic motivation among educators.

## Open Questions
