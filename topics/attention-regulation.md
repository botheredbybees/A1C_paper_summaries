## Attention Regulation Through Mindfulness

Mindfulness is an intervention aimed at enhancing attention regulation, emotion regulation, and decentering through the practice of meta-awareness and awareness. Both sources emphasize the role of the default-mode network and attentional networks in regulating attention and emotions. The concept of decentering, or stepping back from habitual modes of processing, is common to both sources as a mechanism through which mindfulness practices exert their effects. While the default-mode network is highlighted in Source 1, Source 2 underscores the idea of reduced attentional bias as a result of mindfulness practice. Both sources agree on the importance of executive control in facilitating the benefits of mindfulness practices, yet they vary slightly in their emphasis on specific neural networks and cognitive processes involved in the intervention's efficacy.

## Key Findings

- Decentering is a critical mechanism through which mindfulness practices improve attention and emotion regulation.
- Mindfulness interventions reduce attentional biases, aiding in the regulation of emotional responses.
- Executive control is crucial for the successful implementation of mindfulness practices.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific neural mechanisms through which mindfulness practices exert their effects on attention regulation?
