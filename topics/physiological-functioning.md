## Physiological Functioning in Stress and Anxiety

The reviewed sources highlight the importance of interventions aimed at modifying physiological responses in stress and anxiety contexts. In Source 1, music interventions for mechanically ventilated patients are explored, emphasizing their potential to reduce anxiety and improve physiological functioning through non-invasive methods. Conversely, Source 2 delves into affective and physiological consequences of social anxiety disorder, introducing the arousal reappraisal intervention as a tool to alter cognitive appraisal and mitigate stress-related physiological responses. Both sources underscore the versatility of interventions in influencing physiological parameters, yet diverge in their focus: one on physical health and the other on psychological health. The consistency across these studies is the recognition that physiological functioning is malleable and can be positively influenced by tailored interventions.

## Key Findings

- Music interventions can significantly reduce anxiety and improve physiological functioning in mechanically ventilated patients.
- Arousal reappraisal interventions can alter cognitive appraisal and mitigate physiological stress responses in social anxiety disorder.
- Interventions have the potential to positively influence physiological functioning in diverse contexts.

## Open Questions

- How do the physiological benefits of music interventions compare with those of arousal reappraisal interventions in non-ventilated and non-social anxiety contexts?
