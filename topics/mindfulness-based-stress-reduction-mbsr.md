## Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR)

Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) is an intervention that has gained significant traction in the realm of stress management and mental health treatment. Both sources highlight the core tenets of MBSR, focusing on the cultivation of present moment awareness and non-judgmental acceptance of experiences. The intervention is rooted in dispositional mindfulness, which refers to an individual's inherent tendency to be mindful in daily life. MBSR integrates cognitive therapy principles, emphasizing the restructuring of negative thought patterns that contribute to stress and anxiety. Both sources underscore the importance of mindfulness in therapeutic contexts, suggesting that its practice can lead to significant reductions in stress and improvements in psychological well-being.

## Key Findings

- Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) focuses on cultivating present moment awareness and non-judgmental acceptance.
- MBSR integrates cognitive therapy principles to address negative thought patterns contributing to stress.
- Dispositional mindfulness is an essential component of MBSR, influencing its effectiveness.
