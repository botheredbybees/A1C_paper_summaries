## Gender Bias in Technology and Medicine

The provided sources explore the insidious presence of gender bias within two distinct fields: technology and medicine. Source 1, 'Feminist HCI and Narratives of Design Semantics in DIY Music Hardware,' examines gender bias within the realm of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), focusing on DIY music hardware design. It employs feminist HCI principles to critique existing biases and advocate for more inclusive design practices. The narrative emphasizes the importance of understanding the semantics of design and encourages the use of fabulations to create alternative narratives that challenge the status quo. Meanwhile, Source 2, 'I Am Gula, Hear Me Roar: On Gender and Medicine,' delves into the historical and contemporary underrepresentation of women in medical literature and the exclusion of women's health issues. This source highlights the need for better representation and the inclusion of diverse voices in medical discourse to address systemic gender biases.

## Key Findings

- Gender bias is prevalent in both technology design and medical literature.
- Inclusion of diverse voices and perspectives can help mitigate gender bias in both fields.
- Historical exclusion of women's health issues and underrepresentation in medical literature persists, requiring active intervention.

## Open Questions

- How can feminist HCI principles be effectively applied to address gender bias in medical technology design?
