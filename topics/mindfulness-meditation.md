## Mindfulness Meditation

Mindfulness meditation, as an intervention, emphasizes the practice of present-moment awareness and repeated attention redirection to cultivate a non-judgmental observation of one's experiences. Sources highlight the importance of sensory observation and body awareness in establishing mindfulness, such as through body scan meditations. The intervention's effectiveness is discussed within the framework of Research Domain Criteria (RDoC) and its ability to address transdiagnostic factors, suggesting a potential cultural fit and importance of training fidelity for accurate practice and outcomes. Despite these advancements, the definition and application of mindfulness meditation can vary, often leading to confusion about what it entails and how it should be practiced.

## Key Findings

- Mindfulness meditation focuses on present-moment awareness and repeated attention redirection.
- Body awareness and sensory observation are key components in mindfulness practices.
- Mindfulness meditation can address transdiagnostic factors across various mental health conditions.
