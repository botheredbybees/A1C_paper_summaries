## Professional Standards in Music Therapy

The NOAH Code of Ethics 2023 outlines ethical principles that guide the practice of music therapy, emphasizing the importance of respect for persons and the quality of care provided. This code ensures that music therapists adhere to high standards of professionalism and ethics in their work. Source 2, on the other hand, focuses on the training requirements for music therapists, which include a curriculum designed to develop music therapy competencies and the importance of a strong teacher-student relationship during training. Both sources highlight the significance of ongoing professional development to maintain and enhance the quality of care in music therapy. Together, these sources underscore the necessity of professional standards in shaping the ethical and educational frameworks for music therapists, ensuring they are well-prepared to serve clients with the highest integrity and competence.

## Key Findings

- Ethical principles and respect for persons are foundational in music therapy practice.
- Professional development and ongoing training are essential for maintaining quality care.
- Teacher-student relationships are crucial in the development of music therapy competencies.

## Open Questions

- How can professional standards be effectively integrated into the ongoing training and practice of music therapists?
