## Neurological Benefits of Creative Arts Therapies

The integration of creative arts therapies, including dancing and music, into healthcare practices demonstrates significant neurological benefits. Both dancing and music therapy have been shown to enhance executive function and memory improvement, addressing cognitive declines associated with aging and neurodegenerative diseases. Music therapy, in particular, offers a unique advantage in managing pain and enhancing neurological health. However, while both therapies share the potential for cognitive and neurological enhancement, the specific mechanisms and broader applicability across different neurological conditions require further exploration.

## Key Findings

- Both dancing and music therapy improve executive function and memory.
- Music therapy can effectively manage pain in healthcare settings.
- Creative arts therapies have significant neurological benefits.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific neurological mechanisms underlying the benefits of creative arts therapies?
