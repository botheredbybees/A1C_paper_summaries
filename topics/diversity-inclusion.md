## Diversity and Inclusion in Creative Groups

The examination of diversity and inclusion within creative groups, as seen through two distinct contexts, reveals a nuanced interplay between organizational structure and creative output. The Peer Handbook for Assessment Panels emphasizes the importance of conflict-of-interest management and adherence to a code of conduct, underscoring the need for clear guidelines that promote transparency and integrity in decision-making processes involving diverse stakeholders. Conversely, the shOUT - Melbourne Pride Chorus focuses on fostering an inclusive environment through its commitment to diversity and creative repertoire, highlighting the practical benefits such as enriching artistic expression and community engagement. Both sources point towards the necessity of structured frameworks—whether formal codes or inclusive practices—that support a diverse group's creative process while ensuring ethical standards are met.

## Key Findings

- Diverse groups benefit from clear guidelines for managing conflicts of interest to maintain integrity in decision-making processes.
- Inclusive environments enhance creativity and artistic expression by incorporating varied perspectives.
- Organizational structures play a crucial role in enabling or constraining diversity and inclusion initiatives.

## Open Questions

- How can organizations effectively balance adherence to formal codes of conduct with the promotion of creative freedom and inclusivity?
