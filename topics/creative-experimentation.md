## Creative Experimentation in Architectural Design

Creative experimentation in architectural design education and practice is a pedagogical and research methodology that emphasizes the exploration of materials and interdisciplinary connections. Source 1 discusses the incorporation of creative experimentation in the architectural design studio, inspired by Gilles Deleuze's philosophy, to foster interdisciplinary connections and the use of craft mediums in design education. This approach aims to broaden students' understanding of material properties and their potential applications in architectural design. Source 2 introduces the concept of creative experimentation as a part of the broader pedagogical framework for project planning in an architectural design context, focusing on the integration of lighting as an experimental element. Both sources highlight the value of creative experimentation in pushing the boundaries of traditional architectural practices and encouraging innovative design solutions.

## Key Findings

- Creative experimentation fosters interdisciplinary connections in architectural design.
- Incorporating craft mediums and understanding material properties is crucial for innovative design.
- Lighting serves as a critical element in creative architectural experimentation.

## Open Questions

- How can creative experimentation be effectively integrated into professional architectural practice beyond educational settings?
