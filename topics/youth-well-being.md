## Youth Well-being

The two sources provide a comprehensive overview of the challenges faced by youth in terms of their well-being. Both reports highlight similar issues including high cost-of-living and concerns over climate change, which significantly impact mental health and educational opportunities. The first source emphasizes mental health disorders and educational barriers as key issues affecting marginalized youth, while the second underscores discrimination experiences alongside a focus on broader climate change concerns and overall mental health issues. Despite differences in terminology (such as 'mental-health-disorders' versus 'mental-health'), both sources agree on the critical nature of these challenges for youth well-being.

## Key Findings

- High cost-of-living is a major concern impacting youth well-being.
- Climate change concerns significantly affect youth mental health and overall well-being.
- Discrimination experiences are prevalent among youth, further exacerbating their challenges in accessing education and healthcare.

## Open Questions

- What specific interventions can address both the economic barriers and environmental stressors affecting youth mental health?
