## The Arts in Dementia Care

The utilization of arts in dementia care highlights its multifaceted benefits, from improving emotional engagement to facilitating reminiscence-based activities. Both sources emphasize the profound impact of dementia on individuals and society, introducing common types such as Alzheimer's and vascular dementia. These conditions often diminish cognitive functions, leading to a loss of personal identity and social connections. In response, arts programs are introduced to enhance quality of life by stimulating cognitive functions and emotional well-being. The implementation of such programs not only aids in reminiscence but also fosters a sense of community and personal fulfillment among those affected by dementia.

## Key Findings

- Arts programs improve emotional well-being and cognitive engagement in dementia patients.
- Reminiscence-based activities are integral to dementia care, aiding in the recollection of past experiences and personal identity.
- Dementia care interventions like arts programs contribute to social integration and community support.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of arts-based interventions on cognitive decline and quality of life in dementia patients?
