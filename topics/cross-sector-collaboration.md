## Cross-Sector Strategies for Health and Wellbeing

The integration of cross-sector strategies for health and community well-being leverages collaborative approaches from various sectors to enhance public health outcomes. Both sources highlight the importance of incorporating arts-based therapies and artistic engagement within holistic care models to promote health improvement through art. These interventions aim to address social determinants of health by fostering partnerships between healthcare providers, artists, government agencies, and other stakeholders to create comprehensive support systems for individuals and communities.

## Key Findings

- Cross-sector collaboration can enhance health outcomes through arts-based therapies.
- Holistic care models benefit from integrating artistic engagement in community well-being initiatives.
- Partnerships between diverse sectors are essential for addressing social determinants of health.
