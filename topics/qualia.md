## Qualia

Qualia, often described as the subjective, first-person experiences associated with sensory perceptions, emotional states, and thoughts, have been a central topic in philosophy of mind and cognitive science. Source 1 introduces the concept of qualia in the context of learned associations, emphasizing how individual experiences of qualia can be influenced by personal and environmental factors that create unique associations. Source 2 expands on this by linking qualia to the Default Mode Network (DMN), suggesting that qualia experiences may be more prominent during DMN activity, which is associated with introspection and self-referential thinking. Additionally, the source notes the significant role of cultural and social influences in shaping qualia, indicating that subjective experiences can vary widely across different cultural contexts. Both sources highlight the complexity of qualia, suggesting that while qualia are inherently personal and subjective, they are also influenced by broader cognitive and social frameworks.

## Key Findings

- Qualia are subjective experiences that are influenced by personal and cultural factors.
- The Default Mode Network is linked to the introspective and self-referential aspects of qualia experiences.
- Cultural and social contexts play a significant role in shaping the nature of qualia.
