## Mindfulness and Dance Therapy in Psychological Treatment

This synthesis integrates the practices of mindfulness-based psychotherapies and dance therapy as psychological interventions. Both approaches emphasize the importance of embodied experience in the therapeutic process. Dance therapy, as highlighted by Donna Newman Bluestein, uses movement to integrate personality, build empathy, and prevent violence. This contrasts with mindfulness-based interventions like MBCT, ACT, and DBT, which focus on psychological flexibility, acceptance, and the cultivation of present moment awareness through verbal and cognitive exercises. Despite differing methods, both interventions share an underlying principle: the utilization of the body and movement as a means to foster mental health and emotional regulation. However, the integration of these modalities in clinical practice remains underexplored, raising questions about the potential benefits of hybrid interventions.

## Key Findings

- Both dance therapy and mindfulness-based therapies emphasize the importance of the body in psychological treatment.
- Dance therapy aims to integrate personality, build empathy, and prevent violence through movement.
- Mindfulness-based interventions, such as MBCT, ACT, and DBT, focus on psychological flexibility and acceptance.

## Open Questions

- What are the potential benefits and challenges of integrating dance therapy with mindfulness-based interventions in clinical practice?
