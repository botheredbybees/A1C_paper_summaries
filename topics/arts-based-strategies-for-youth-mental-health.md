## Arts-Based Strategies for Youth Mental Health

The exploration of arts-based strategies as interventions to support youth mental health highlights both the universal appeal and cultural specificity of such practices. Shamanic universals, discussed in the context of indigenous healing practices, share a resonance with contemporary arts-and-culture-based interventions aimed at addressing mental health disorders among youth globally. These strategies not only aim to elevate youth voices but also incorporate local cultural practices that are integral to community well-being. However, while these innovative approaches underscore the importance of mental health as a human right and align with sustainable development goals focusing on good health and well-being, they do so within a broader landscape where dietary risks significantly impact non-communicable disease mortality and morbidity across nations. The synthesis reveals an interconnected web wherein arts-based strategies play a crucial role in supporting youth mental health but must be integrated alongside efforts to address nutritional deficiencies that influence overall health outcomes.

## Key Findings

- Arts-and-culture-based interventions are effective in elevating the voices of youth and addressing their mental health needs.
- Incorporation of local cultural practices enhances the efficacy of arts-based strategies for supporting youth mental health.
- The use of shamanic universals reflects a global perspective on healing that parallels contemporary approaches to youth mental health support.

## Open Questions

- How can arts-based interventions be effectively combined with dietary and nutritional strategies to comprehensively address youth mental health?
