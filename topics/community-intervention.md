## Community-Based Creative Interventions

This synthesis examines the impact of community-based creative interventions such as music and movement programs and drama activities for elderly participants. Both sources highlight how these interventions can enhance well-being by fostering a sense of belonging, promoting interdisciplinary programming, and engaging communities in therapeutic activities. The phenomenological approach used in studying music and movement programs underscores the subjective experiences of participants and facilitators, while Applied Theatre emphasizes the role of drama in enhancing life quality for seniors. These creative interventions serve as platforms to integrate diverse disciplines and stakeholder groups within a community setting, contributing positively to mental and physical health.

## Key Findings

- Creative interventions like music and movement programs and drama activities significantly enhance well-being among elderly participants by fostering a sense of belonging and promoting interdisciplinary programming.
- Phenomenological approaches in studying these interventions provide deep insights into the subjective experiences of both participants and facilitators, highlighting the emotional and social benefits.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of community-based creative interventions on participant well-being compared to more traditional health programs?
