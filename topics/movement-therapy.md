## Movement Therapy

The synthesis focuses on the various applications and benefits of movement therapy in managing dementia. Sources demonstrate that dance movement therapy (DMT) is increasingly recognized for its non-pharmacological interventions, with studies showing positive impacts on cognitive functions such as visuospatial abilities and planning skills. Randomized controlled trials highlight improvements over standard care, particularly through enhanced social engagement and cognitive stimulation. Beyond DMT, Biodanza introduces resilience-building elements, emphasizing vivencia (lived experience) to foster emotional well-being. While the focus is predominantly on dementia management, there are indications of broader benefits including violence prevention, empathy building, and improved self-care ability. However, while sources underscore the positive effects of movement therapy on cognitive health and social interaction, there remains a gap in understanding its long-term effectiveness compared to pharmacological treatments for individuals with mild to moderate dementia.

## Key Findings

- Movement therapy improves visuospatial abilities and planning skills in people with dementia.
- Dance movement therapy enhances non-verbal communication and social engagement over standard care.
- Biodanza contributes to emotional resilience through lived experience (vivencia).

## Open Questions

- What is the long-term effectiveness of movement therapies compared to pharmacological interventions for managing mild to moderate dementia?
