## Patient Narratives in Healthcare

The integration of patient narratives into healthcare practice highlights the importance of storytelling and holistic listening, as emphasized in narrative medicine. Source 1 underscores the transformative power of patient narratives in shifting healthcare professionals from merely readers to healers, fostering a deeper understanding and empathy towards patient experiences. This aligns with the health humanities approach discussed in Source 2, which advocates for a biopsychosocial model that considers the broader social determinants of health. Both sources advocate for an approach that goes beyond the clinical symptoms to include the patient's personal story, enhancing the therapeutic relationship and the quality of care.

## Key Findings

- Patient narratives are crucial for healthcare professionals to understand and empathize with patients beyond their medical conditions.
- The use of narrative medicine enhances the therapeutic relationship between patients and healthcare providers.
- A biopsychosocial approach, including social determinants of health, is integral to comprehensive patient care.

## Open Questions

- How can healthcare systems be modified to fully integrate the practice of narrative medicine into routine care?
