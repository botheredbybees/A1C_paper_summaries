## Psychoneuroendocrinology in Music and Health

Psychoneuroendocrinology explores the intricate interplay between the mind, nervous system, and endocrine system, particularly in the context of music and health. Source 1 provides an overview of how musical experiences can influence the hormonal systems, contributing to the understanding of health outcomes. Meanwhile, Source 2 focuses on the practical application of music listening as a method to reduce stress in everyday life, supported by physiological markers. Both sources highlight the potential of music to affect the body's stress response through hormonal regulation, emphasizing the therapeutic value of musical interventions in promoting physiological well-being.

## Key Findings

- Music can influence the hormonal systems, potentially affecting health outcomes.
- Music listening is associated with stress reduction, as indicated by physiological markers.
- Musical experiences offer a practical means to regulate the body's stress response.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific mechanisms by which music influences hormonal systems and stress regulation?
