## Psychological Adjustment

The sources explore different dimensions of psychological adjustment, particularly focusing on the benefits of interventions for individuals experiencing extreme stress and isolation. Source 1 highlights the benefits of expressive writing as an intervention for healthcare workers (HCWs) during the COVID-19 pandemic, demonstrating how such an activity can mitigate psychological distress. The intervention encourages HCWs to articulate their experiences and emotions, which can lead to a better understanding and acceptance of their circumstances. In contrast, Source 2 discusses the psychological adjustment of individuals living with locked-in syndrome, emphasizing the importance of quality of life and social support in maintaining a sense of worth and engagement with life despite severe physical limitations. While both sources acknowledge the role of interventions and social support in enhancing psychological adjustment, they differ in context, focusing on acute stress in HCWs versus long-term adjustment in severely physically impaired individuals.

## Key Findings

- Expressive writing can serve as a therapeutic intervention to alleviate psychological distress in healthcare workers during pandemics.
- Quality of life and social support are critical factors in the psychological adjustment of individuals with severe physical disabilities, such as locked-in syndrome.
- Interventions that help individuals articulate and process their emotions can contribute to improved mental health and adjustment.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of expressive writing interventions on psychological adjustment in HCWs, beyond the immediate context of the pandemic?
