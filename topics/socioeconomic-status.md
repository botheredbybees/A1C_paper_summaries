## Socioeconomic Status

The concept of socioeconomic status (SES) is a critical determinant in the health outcomes of populations. Source 1 discusses the importance of creative approaches to healthcare that aim for holistic well-being and equitable access, which are particularly relevant when addressing disparities stemming from SES differences. Creative health strategies can help mitigate the barriers faced by lower-SES individuals who often lack adequate resources or opportunities for preventive care and wellness initiatives.

In contrast, Source 2 focuses on missed early intervention opportunities specifically for children with autism spectrum disorder (ASD), highlighting how SES-related factors contribute to delayed access to essential services. These delays can result from a lack of awareness about available support under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act Part C, which underscores the need for increased outreach and culturally appropriate communication strategies targeting lower-SES families.

Both sources implicitly touch upon the broader issue of how socioeconomic barriers impact health equity, suggesting that targeted interventions are needed to ensure timely diagnosis and intervention for disadvantaged groups. The emphasis on early detection and comprehensive support systems aligns with efforts aimed at reducing SES-driven disparities in healthcare access and outcomes.

## Key Findings

- Creative approaches can enhance equitable access to health services for socioeconomically disadvantaged populations.
- Early intervention is crucial for improving outcomes, especially among children with autism spectrum disorder.
- SES-related barriers often lead to delayed diagnosis and treatment due to lack of awareness or resources.

## Open Questions
