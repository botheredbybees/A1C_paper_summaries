## Auditory Memory for Music

The sources explore the complex relationship between sensory-motor integration and auditory memory in music. The first source highlights how auditory-motor learning can enhance auditory memory for music, suggesting that mental imagery plays a significant role in this enhancement. It underscores the importance of physical engagement with sound to improve cognitive recall. In contrast, the second source provides an updated model elucidating the neural basis of music perception, which includes auditory memory as one of its critical components. This source also discusses how musical training can influence neural structures involved in auditory memory. Both sources converge on the idea that engaging in musical activities through motor learning enhances the cognitive processes underlying auditory memory.

## Key Findings

- Auditory-motor learning positively influences auditory memory for music.
- Musical training affects the neural basis of auditory perception, including auditory memory.
- Mental imagery is an important component in enhancing auditory memory.

## Open Questions

- How do different forms of musical training (e.g., sight-reading vs. ear-training) impact specific aspects of auditory memory?
