## Inner-Hearing in Music

The concept of 'inner-hearing' is crucial for understanding the mental processes involved in musical composition and perception. Sources indicate that inner-hearing involves a combination of auditory imagery, imagination, and sensorineural deafness. Beethoven's later compositions are often cited as examples of how one can rely on inner-hearing when external hearing becomes impaired due to conditions like sensorineural deafness. This phenomenon underscores the importance of alternative performance methods for musicians who experience such challenges.

## Key Findings

- Inner-hearing plays a significant role in composing music, especially under conditions where auditory perception is compromised.
- Sensorineural deafness does not preclude musical creativity or the ability to compose complex works through mental imagery and inner-hearing.
- Alternative performance methods are necessary for musicians who rely on their inner-hearing due to hearing loss.

## Open Questions

- What are the neural mechanisms underlying auditory imagery in individuals with sensorineural deafness?
