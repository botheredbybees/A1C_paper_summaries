## Creative Project Planning

The synthesis of the concept of creative projects reveals a dual approach to fostering creativity. Source 1 emphasizes the importance of unconventional usage of materials as a means to stimulate creativity, suggesting that breaking free from traditional constraints can lead to innovative ideas. In contrast, Source 2 delves deeper into the psychological mechanisms behind creativity, highlighting the role of the default mode network and associative thinking in creative sensing. Reflective practice is also crucial, as it allows individuals to contemplate and refine their creative processes. Both sources underscore the value of reflective practice in understanding and enhancing one's creative abilities, whether through material manipulation or mental exploration.

## Key Findings

- Unconventional use of materials can enhance creativity in artistic projects.
- The default mode network and associative thinking play critical roles in creative processes.
- Reflective practice is essential for deepening understanding and improving creative skills.

## Open Questions

- How can the integration of unconventional material usage and psychological insights into creativity be optimized for maximum creative output?
