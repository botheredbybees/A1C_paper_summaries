## Empowerment Through Art

The concept of empowerment through art is explored in both the Active Energy project for older adults and a dance initiative aimed at youth in detention. Both projects utilize creative expression to foster social connections, personal growth, and community engagement among marginalized groups. In the context of older individuals facing social isolation, the Active Energy project highlights how artistic activities can combat loneliness by encouraging active participation in communal creativity. Similarly, the dance program for incarcerated youth addresses deeper issues like trauma healing through self-expression, allowing participants to convey their experiences and foster a sense of identity beyond their current circumstances. Despite differences in demographic focus and societal barriers, both interventions underscore art's capacity to empower individuals by providing platforms for meaningful expression and engagement.

## Key Findings

- Art-based interventions can effectively combat social isolation among older adults.
- Creative arts initiatives offer young people in detention a means of self-expression and trauma healing.
- Empowerment through art transcends age and societal status, promoting community participation.

## Open Questions

- How do the long-term impacts of these interventions compare between different demographic groups?
