## Action Research

Action research emphasizes the cyclical process of engaging in practice, reflecting on it, and then adjusting the practice accordingly. The approach is particularly useful for understanding and improving social issues within communities, as it involves direct collaboration with stakeholders. In South Korea, for example, action research has been applied to facilitate arts participation among older adults, enhancing their creative ageing and community wellbeing. This application showcases how action research can be a vehicle for social change, enabling communities to address their unique challenges and develop tailored solutions. Visual methods and creative approaches are often integrated into the reflective practice component of action research, enhancing the depth and breadth of understanding gained from the research process.

## Key Findings

- Action research facilitates tailored solutions for community-specific challenges.
- Visual methods and creative approaches enrich the reflective practice component of action research.
- Action research can enhance creative ageing and community wellbeing in diverse settings.

## Open Questions
