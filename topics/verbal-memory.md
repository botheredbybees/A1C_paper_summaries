## Verbal Memory Enhancement

Both sources emphasize the importance of interventions aimed at enhancing verbal memory. Source 1 highlights the use of commercial computerized cognitive games in older adults, focusing on improvements to processing speed and working memory. Although these aspects are crucial for cognitive functioning, they do not specifically target verbal memory alone. In contrast, Source 2 focuses explicitly on music training as an intervention that improves verbal memory but does not have a significant effect on visual memory. This highlights the specificity of interventions in targeting particular cognitive functions. A cross-sectional and longitudinal approach was used to assess the neuroanatomical developmental effects of music training on children's verbal memory, suggesting long-term benefits from such targeted activities.

## Key Findings

- Commercial computerized games can enhance processing speed and working memory in older adults.
- Music training specifically improves verbal memory without affecting visual memory.
- Interventions targeting specific cognitive functions show distinct developmental effects.

## Open Questions

- How do different interventions compare in their effectiveness on verbal memory enhancement across various age groups?
