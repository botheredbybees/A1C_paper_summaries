## Phenomenological Approaches in Art and Occupation

The sources collectively highlight the application of phenomenology to understand subjective experiences within art-making processes and occupational activities. Source 1 delves into self-awareness through intuitive collage, emphasizing its role as a reflective tool for personal insight. In contrast, Source 2 examines how knitting contributes not only to health and well-being but also serves an integral part in the formation of an occupational identity. Both sources underscore phenomenology's capacity to uncover deeper meanings from seemingly simple activities by focusing on individual experiences rather than objective analysis alone. Meanwhile, Source 3 integrates technology with traditional practices in photography, demonstrating how phenomenological research can bridge different artistic mediums and explore their interconnections within contemporary contexts.

## Key Findings

- Phenomenology enables the exploration of subjective meanings derived from art-making processes and occupational activities
- Intuitive collage is a reflective tool for enhancing self-awareness
- Knitting contributes to health, well-being, and the development of an occupational identity

## Open Questions

- How can phenomenological research further integrate with technological advancements in understanding contemporary artistic practices?
