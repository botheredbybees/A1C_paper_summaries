## Therapeutic Storytelling

Therapeutic storytelling, as explored in both sources, emphasizes the use of narrative as a therapeutic tool. Source 1 focuses on the human connections and fragmented memories reclaiming, particularly relevant for individuals suffering from memory loss due to illness. This approach highlights the importance of transient human connections in providing comfort and aid to individuals navigating the challenges of memory loss. Source 2 further develops the concept by discussing narrative therapy and the humanistic care approach, which centers on understanding illness through narrative and engaging with it as a story to be lived. Both sources underscore the power of storytelling in providing a framework for patients and practitioners to navigate the complexities of illness and memory loss, fostering a sense of agency and identity amidst uncertainty.

## Key Findings

- Therapeutic storytelling offers a humanistic approach to dealing with memory loss and illness.
- Narrative therapy can provide a framework for understanding and living with illness.
- Transient human connections are crucial in the therapeutic process of memory reclaiming.
