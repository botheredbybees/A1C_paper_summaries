## Human Aspects of Technology for Aging Populations

The integration of human aspects of technology for aging populations is a cross-disciplinary effort, drawing insights from systematic musicology and human-computer interaction. Source 1 highlights the importance of bodily motion responses in understanding how music influences mental and physical health, emphasizing the need for a systemic approach to music research. This systemic approach, akin to the comprehensive studies in music psychology, can be paralleled with the systematic evaluation of technological adaptations in aging populations, as seen in Source 2. Both sources underscore the importance of usability and accessibility in enhancing the quality of life through technology. However, while music's impact on bodily motion and mental health is well-documented, its specific role in improving technological adaptation among the elderly remains underexplored. The dialogue between these fields could benefit from further research that integrates music therapy methodologies with user-centered design principles to enhance technology adoption among aging populations.

## Key Findings

- The integration of music's influence on bodily motion and mental health can inform the design of technology for aging populations.
- Usability and accessibility are crucial for enhancing technological adaptation among the elderly.
- There is a need for a systematic approach to studying the impact of music on health and technology use among the aging population.

## Open Questions

- How can music therapy methodologies be integrated with user-centered design to enhance technology adoption among aging populations?
