## Lateral Prefrontal Cortex

The lateral prefrontal cortex (LPFC) plays a crucial role in spontaneous creativity and self-monitoring as highlighted in the provided sources. The LPFC is differentiated from the medial prefrontal cortex (MPFC), which is more involved in introspective and self-referential thinking. Source 1 emphasizes the LPFC's involvement in spontaneous creativity, while Source 2 focuses on its function in self-monitoring, indicating a broader range of cognitive processes regulated by the LPFC. Both sources agree on the importance of the LPFC in executive functions, highlighting its role in real-time decision-making, cognitive flexibility, and inhibitory control. However, there is a gap in understanding how the LPFC specifically contributes to these diverse functions and how it interacts with other brain regions to facilitate these processes.

## Key Findings

- The lateral prefrontal cortex is crucial for spontaneous creativity.
- The lateral prefrontal cortex engages in self-monitoring functions.
- The lateral prefrontal cortex is distinguished from the medial prefrontal cortex in its functional roles.

## Open Questions

- How does the lateral prefrontal cortex interact with other brain regions to facilitate both creativity and self-monitoring?
