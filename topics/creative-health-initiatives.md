## Creative Health Initiatives

Creative health initiatives emphasize the integration of arts and creative practices within healthcare to promote holistic well-being. Source 1 highlights economic benefits and cost-effectiveness ratios associated with such interventions, suggesting they not only enhance patient outcomes but also provide significant financial advantages for healthcare systems. In contrast, Source 2 shifts focus towards user-centric design and inclusive decision-making, underscoring the importance of involving individuals who have lived experiences in designing these creative health initiatives to ensure that their needs are met effectively. While both sources recognize the potential of creative health interventions in improving patient care and economic efficiency, there is a gap between recognizing benefits and ensuring effective implementation through user-centered approaches.

## Key Findings

- Creative health initiatives offer substantial economic benefits alongside therapeutic advantages.
- Incorporating lived experiences into the design process enhances the relevance and effectiveness of creative health interventions.

## Open Questions

- How can healthcare systems effectively balance economic incentives with user-centered approaches in implementing creative health initiatives?
