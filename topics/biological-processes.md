## Biological Processes Associated with Chronic Stress

Both sources explore the complex interplay of biological processes triggered by chronic stress. They highlight inflammation and oxidative stress as critical mechanisms that can lead to immunosuppression, affecting overall health. The first source delves deeper into these specific physiological responses, while the second broadens the scope to include a range of stress-related biomarkers. Both sources agree on the detrimental impacts of prolonged exposure to stress on bodily systems but differ slightly in their focus: one on specific biological pathways and the other on broader indicators of chronic stress.

## Key Findings

- Inflammation and oxidative stress are key biological processes activated by chronic stress.
- Prolonged stress can lead to immunosuppression, compromising overall health.
- Chronic stress is associated with a wide range of biomarkers that indicate physiological strain.

## Open Questions

- What specific mechanisms link chronic stress to the development of systemic inflammation and oxidative stress?
