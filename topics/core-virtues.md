## Core Virtues in Psychology

The concept of core virtues in psychology, as explored in the VIA Classification of Character Strengths, encompasses a broad framework for understanding and fostering positive human traits. Source 1, '340 Ways to Use VIA Character Strengths,' highlights the practical application and personal growth potential of these virtues, emphasizing their role in enhancing resilience and overall well-being. Source 2, 'VIA Character Strengths - Research and Practice: The First 10 Years,' provides a comprehensive overview of the theoretical underpinnings and empirical research supporting the VIA Classification, reinforcing its significance in the field of positive psychology. Both sources underscore the VIA Inventory of Strengths as a tool for identifying and utilizing individual character strengths to promote psychological health and personal development.

## Key Findings

- The VIA Classification of Character Strengths serves as a foundational framework for understanding and applying core virtues in psychological practice.
- Core virtues, as defined by the VIA Classification, are instrumental in enhancing personal growth and resilience.
- The VIA Inventory of Strengths is a valuable tool for assessing and utilizing individual character strengths.
