## Psychological Assessment: Measurement Invariance and Connectedness to Nature

This synthesis focuses on the methods and scales used in psychological assessment, specifically the Connectedness to Nature Scale (CNS) and the measurement invariance of the VIA Inventory of Strengths (VIA-IS). The CNS evaluates the extent to which individuals feel connected to the natural world and its implications for environmental responsibility and subjective well-being. In contrast, the VIA-IS is a tool to assess character strengths as outlined in the VIA Classification of Character Strengths. The VIA-IS has been translated into various languages, and research has been conducted to ensure that these translations maintain measurement invariance across different cultural contexts. This ensures that the assessment of character strengths is consistent and valid across diverse populations. Both scales highlight the importance of reliable and valid measurement in psychological assessment, with the CNS focusing on the human-nature relationship and the VIA-IS on character strengths.

## Key Findings

- The Connectedness to Nature Scale (CNS) is used to measure the extent to which individuals feel connected to the natural world.
- The VIA Inventory of Strengths (VIA-IS) assesses character strengths and has been translated into various languages.
- Ensuring measurement invariance is crucial for the consistency and validity of psychological assessments across different cultural contexts.
