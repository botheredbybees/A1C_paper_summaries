## Case Studies in Neurological and Developmental Conditions

The provided sources explore individual cases of neurological and developmental conditions through therapeutic art practices. Source 1 examines an Alzheimer's patient whose artistic abilities are preserved despite cognitive decline, suggesting the potential for art therapy as a means to maintain creative expression in individuals with dementia. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on a case study involving an adult with autism using photography to explore identity formation within the context of family systems theory and OCD. Both sources highlight the unique challenges faced by individuals with these conditions but also illustrate the positive impact of tailored therapeutic approaches that leverage individual strengths such as artistic expression.

## Key Findings

- Artistic abilities can remain intact in patients with Alzheimer's disease, offering a path for continued creative engagement despite cognitive decline.
- Therapeutic photography and art practices provide valuable tools for identity exploration among individuals with autism spectrum disorders (ASD) and OCD.

## Open Questions

- How do therapeutic interventions leveraging artistic expression impact long-term outcomes for patients with Alzheimer's disease compared to other forms of therapy?
