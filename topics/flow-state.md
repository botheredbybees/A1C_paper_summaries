## Flow State in Creative Arts and Relaxation

Sources indicate the application of Csikszentmihalyi's 'flow' theory within creative arts therapy for mental health and trauma. The neurochemical releases during flow contribute to therapeutic benefits, aligning with interventions aimed at achieving a relaxed state through informal approaches such as the DRAMMA model. Both sources highlight the importance of flow in enhancing relaxation and promoting psychological well-being.

## Key Findings

- Csikszentmihalyi's concept of 'flow' is critical for therapeutic applications in creative arts therapy.
- Flow state facilitates neurochemical releases that are beneficial for mental health recovery, especially from trauma.
- The DRAMMA model provides a framework for achieving relaxation through informal methods that may induce flow.

## Open Questions

- How can the integration of formal and informal approaches enhance the application of flow theory in therapeutic settings?
