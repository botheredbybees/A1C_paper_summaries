## Reflexivity

Reflexivity emerges as a central concept in both educational and theoretical contexts. In the practical session of FXA101 (Source 1), reflexivity is explored alongside concepts like directed-attention-fatigue and cognitive-reserve, suggesting its application to understanding individual cognitive processes and limitations. Meanwhile, in the web lecture from Week 1, 2026 (Source 2) within a health humanities framework, reflexivity is seen as integral to fostering empathy in healthcare professionals and students. Both sources highlight the importance of self-awareness and introspection for professional development but emphasize different applications: cognitive management versus emotional understanding.

## Key Findings

- Reflexivity aids in managing individual cognitive processes and limitations
- Health humanities utilize reflexivity to enhance empathetic engagement with patients

## Open Questions

- How can the application of reflexivity be integrated between cognitive and emotional domains for comprehensive professional development?
