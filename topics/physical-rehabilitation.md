## Music Therapy in Physical Rehabilitation

Music therapy is recognized as a beneficial intervention in various fields of healthcare, with physical rehabilitation being one of its key applications. Registered Music Therapists (RMTs) play a crucial role in using music to aid in the treatment and rehabilitation of individuals with diverse conditions. A systematic literature review highlights the use of music therapy to enhance motivation and engagement in physical rehabilitation interventions. This review underscores the importance of music therapy in not only improving physical outcomes but also in boosting the psychological well-being of patients. While the benefits are widely acknowledged, there is a need for more rigorous studies to further establish the efficacy and mechanisms of music therapy in physical rehabilitation.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy is beneficial for a range of conditions and enhances rehabilitation outcomes.
- Registered Music Therapists (RMTs) use music to motivate and engage patients in physical rehabilitation.
- Music therapy can improve both physical and psychological aspects of rehabilitation.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific mechanisms by which music therapy enhances motivation and engagement in physical rehabilitation?
