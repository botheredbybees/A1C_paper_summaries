## Complete Locked-In Syndrome

The review on the performance of brain-computer interface (BCI) systems for LIS and CLIS patients underscores the critical role of BCI in enhancing the quality of life for individuals with severe neurological conditions. These systems enable communication and control for patients who are otherwise completely unable to interact with their environment, highlighting a significant breakthrough in neurotechnology. The Roald Dahl case study, while specific, emphasizes the emotional and psychological impact of CLIS, illustrating the broader humanistic implications of these technological advancements. Both sources agree on the importance of BCI technology in addressing the needs of CLIS patients, but the review focuses more on the technical and practical aspects, whereas the case study highlights the personal and social dimensions of living with CLIS.

## Key Findings

- Brain-computer interface systems are crucial for improving the quality of life for patients with complete locked-in syndrome (CLIS).
- CLIS patients benefit from BCI technology in terms of communication and control over their environment.
- The emotional and psychological impact of CLIS is significant and requires comprehensive support beyond technological solutions.

## Open Questions
