## Frugal Innovation and Creative Constraints in Healthcare

The sources explore the concepts of creative constraints and frugal innovation as mechanisms for fostering innovative solutions within healthcare. Source 1 highlights how paired constraints can lead to novel creative expressions, suggesting that certain restrictions or limitations encourage inventiveness. This principle is paralleled in Source 2's discussion on frugal innovation, which emphasizes creating effective yet cost-efficient products or services under resource-limited conditions. Both sources agree that the imposition of constraints can spur creativity and innovation by pushing individuals to think outside traditional paradigms, leading to new solutions. However, there exists a tension between defining specific constraints that foster innovative thinking versus overly restrictive boundaries that hinder creative freedom.

## Key Findings

- Paired constraints in creative processes can lead to novel solutions
- Frugal innovation operates under resource-limited conditions and aims for cost-efficiency
- Constraints are necessary but must be balanced to encourage creativity

## Open Questions

- What is the optimal level of constraint to maximize innovative outcomes?
