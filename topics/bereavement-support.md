## Bereavement Support Interventions

The integration of personalized and integrated care models, coupled with creative arts therapies such as music therapy, has emerged as a multifaceted approach to supporting individuals during bereavement. Personalized care emphasizes the unique needs of each individual, whereas integrated care aims to combine multiple therapeutic modalities to address the diverse aspects of grief. Music therapy, as a specific form of creative arts therapy, has shown efficacy in aiding emotional expression and identity integration, both critical components in the bereavement process. Sources indicate that these interventions not only help in managing the acute phase of grief but also contribute to long-term emotional well-being. However, there is a need for more research to understand the long-term benefits of such interventions and how they can be effectively implemented within healthcare systems.

## Key Findings

- Personalized care and integrated care models are essential in bereavement support.
- Creative arts therapies, such as music therapy, effectively aid in emotional expression and identity integration.
- Music therapy is a critical component in the broader context of creative arts therapies for bereavement support.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term benefits of personalized and integrated care models in bereavement support, and how can they be sustained within healthcare systems?
