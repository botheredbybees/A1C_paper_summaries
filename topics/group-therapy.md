## Group Therapy

Group therapy, including interventions like transdiagnostic group music therapy and music-assisted group cognitive behavior therapy, is employed to treat various mental health issues, particularly in settings with high comorbidity. Transdiagnostic approaches in group music therapy aim to address multiple mental health conditions simultaneously, transcending the limitations of traditional diagnosis-specific treatments. Music therapy, combined with cognitive behavior therapy, shows promise in engaging patients in substance abuse treatment, enhancing patient involvement and adherence to treatment protocols. However, while these interventions demonstrate potential benefits, there is a need for further research to establish their efficacy and scalability in diverse patient populations.

## Key Findings

- Transdiagnostic group music therapy addresses multiple mental health conditions effectively.
- Combining music therapy with cognitive behavior therapy enhances patient engagement in substance abuse treatment.
- Music therapy within group settings can be a valuable adjunct to traditional cognitive behavior therapy practices.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term outcomes and sustainability of group music therapy interventions for mental health and substance abuse treatment?
