## Performance Anxiety

Performance anxiety, a prevalent issue among musicians, encompasses both psychological and physiological aspects. Sources highlight the importance of accurate measurement tools, with Cronbach’s alpha indicating strong internal reliability for scales assessing this condition (Source 1). The condition is often categorized into subtypes, and gender differences may influence its occurrence and impact (Source 7). Management strategies include systematic desensitization and relaxation training, alongside cognitive-behavioral interventions (Source 5). Addressing performance anxiety also involves occupational health, as it can correlate with musculoskeletal disorders and requires injury prevention measures (Sources 6 and 3). Additionally, awareness and knowledge about performance anxiety can aid in its management (Source 4). Techniques such as breathing exercises and positive thinking are recommended to mitigate symptoms (Sources 8 and 11). However, there is a need for more comprehensive and integrated approaches to address both the psychological and physical impacts of performance anxiety.

## Key Findings

- Performance anxiety scales have strong internal reliability.
- Systematic desensitization and relaxation training are effective management strategies.
- Performance anxiety is linked to musculoskeletal disorders and requires injury prevention.

## Open Questions

- What are the most effective integrated approaches for managing both the psychological and physical impacts of performance anxiety?
