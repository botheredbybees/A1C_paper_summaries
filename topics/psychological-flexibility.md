## Psychological Flexibility

The concept of psychological flexibility is central in both sources as a pivotal element for enhancing emotional resilience and managing stress-related issues. Source 1 presents dance therapy as an intervention particularly effective for individuals suffering from treatment-resistant depression, emphasizing its role in increasing psychological flexibility. Similarly, Source 2 highlights the importance of mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) and acceptance and commitment therapy (ACT), which are also designed to foster psychological flexibility among mental health professionals, thereby improving their self-care and reducing professional burnout. Both sources underscore that interventions aimed at enhancing psychological flexibility can significantly impact an individual's capacity for emotional resilience and adaptive functioning in stressful situations.

## Key Findings

- Psychological flexibility is a critical component of effective stress management and emotional well-being.
- Interventions such as dance therapy, MBSR, and ACT contribute to the development of psychological flexibility.
- Enhancing psychological flexibility can lead to better self-care practices among mental health professionals.

## Open Questions
