## Scarcity Salience Model

The Scarcity Salience model is a critical framework within the study of creativity and decision-making among older adult artists. It emphasizes how perceived scarcity in resources or time can significantly influence an individual's attention and prioritization processes, affecting their creative output and problem-solving abilities. Both sources underscore the importance of understanding how constraints—whether they are intrinsic (such as cognitive limitations) or extrinsic (like limited financial resources)—can either hinder or stimulate creativity, depending on the context and individual’s adaptive strategies.

## Key Findings

- Scarcity salience influences decision-making by directing attention to critical resource limitations.
- Constraints can be both a challenge and an opportunity for enhancing creative output among older adults.
- Adaptive cognitive strategies play a crucial role in mitigating the negative impacts of scarcity on creativity.

## Open Questions

- How do different types of constraints (cognitive, financial, temporal) uniquely or synergistically impact creative processes?
