## Biopsychosocial Approach

The biopsychosocial approach, as outlined in the sources, integrates biological, psychological, and social factors to provide a comprehensive understanding of health and illness. Source 1 emphasizes the importance of considering neuropathology alongside malignant social psychology and memory processes in dementia care, highlighting the interplay between biological and social factors. Source 2 expands on this by introducing the concept of narrative medicine within the health humanities framework, underscoring the significance of the patient-professional relationship and the social context in shaping health outcomes. Both sources underscore the complexity of the biopsychosocial model, which requires a holistic approach to healthcare that addresses not only the biological aspects of illness but also the psychological and social dimensions. The integration of narrative and humanities perspectives enriches the biopsychosocial approach, providing a deeper understanding of patient experiences and care practices.

## Key Findings

- The biopsychosocial approach integrates biological, psychological, and social factors to understand health and illness.
- Narrative and humanities perspectives enhance the biopsychosocial model by emphasizing patient experiences and social context.
- Dementia care benefits from a holistic approach that considers both biological and social factors.
