## Professional Musicians and Brain Plasticity

This synthesis explores the effects of long-term musical practice on professional musicians, focusing on both brain plasticity and mental health. Source 1 highlights the impact of long-term practice on brain structure through deformation-based morphometry, showing significant brain changes in middle-aged keyboard players. In contrast, Source 2 introduces a preventive program aimed at addressing musical performance anxiety, a common mental health issue among musicians. While these studies address different aspects of musicians' well-being, they collectively underscore the profound influence of prolonged engagement in musical activities on both the physical structure of the brain and psychological health. The interplay between cognitive benefits and psychological challenges provides a comprehensive view of the unique experiences of professional musicians.

## Key Findings

- Long-term musical practice leads to significant brain plasticity.
- Musical performance anxiety is a prevalent mental health issue among professional musicians.
- Interventions aimed at addressing mental health issues can complement the cognitive benefits of musical practice.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term mental health benefits of interventions designed to alleviate performance anxiety in musicians?
