## Sustainability Practices

The concept of frugal innovation is explored in depth as a critical method for achieving sustainability practices. Frugal innovation emphasizes the creation and implementation of simple yet effective solutions with limited resources, aiming to address social needs efficiently. This approach aligns closely with the practical utilitarianism emphasized by designer Shigeru Ban, who advocates for designs that serve both emotional expression and functional utility without compromising on environmental considerations. The integration of these principles highlights a convergence in methodologies aimed at sustainability but also reveals nuances in their application across diverse contexts.

## Key Findings

- Frugal innovation focuses on creating efficient solutions with limited resources.
- Practical utilitarianism supports designs that balance functionality and emotional expression.
- Both approaches emphasize sustainable practices by prioritizing resource efficiency.

## Open Questions

- How can the theoretical underpinnings of frugal innovation be applied universally in diverse cultural contexts?
