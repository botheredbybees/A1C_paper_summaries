## Technology-Assisted Interventions in Dementia Care

The integration of technology in dementia care presents a promising approach to enhancing the quality of life for individuals with dementia. A scoping review underscores the feasibility and potential of custom-made devices to aid creative arts activities, indicating that such interventions can be tailored effectively to meet specific needs and preferences within dementia care settings. Furthermore, research on MARIO, a social robot designed specifically for interacting with people living with dementia, highlights its positive impact on resilience. This study demonstrates how facilitated sessions involving the use of MARIO not only enhance emotional well-being but also foster greater cognitive engagement. Despite these promising findings, both sources identify significant challenges in terms of technological integration and user acceptance.

## Key Findings

- Technology can be effectively integrated into dementia care to support creative arts activities
- Social robots like MARIO positively impact resilience among people with dementia
- Facilitated sessions involving technology enhance emotional and cognitive engagement

## Open Questions

- How can the broader adoption of these technologies be facilitated in diverse care settings?
