## Plant-Based Diet

The sources emphasize the importance of plant-based diets for health and sustainability. Source 1 highlights principles from the Mediterranean diet such as prioritizing whole foods and fresh produce, alongside a Planetary Health approach that considers environmental impacts. This aligns with Source 2's focus on dietary guidelines advocating for reduced consumption of ultra-processed foods in favor of more nutritious options like fruits and vegetables. Both sources underscore the benefits of adopting plant-based eating patterns but also caution against reliance on highly processed plant products, suggesting a preference for minimally processed foods to maximize health benefits.

## Key Findings

- Plant-based diets are encouraged as part of healthy eating guidelines due to their potential health and environmental benefits.
- The Mediterranean diet principles, focusing on whole grains, fruits, vegetables, nuts, and olive oil, align with a plant-forward approach.

## Open Questions

- How do dietary guidelines balance the promotion of plant-based diets with the need to avoid highly processed foods that are marketed as plant-based?
