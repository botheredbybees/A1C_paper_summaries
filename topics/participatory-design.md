## Participatory Design

Participatory design is a method emphasizing iterative research and active community engagement. Source 1 discusses the importance of Research through Design (RtD), which aims to mitigate confirmation bias by iteratively refining designs based on user feedback. This method fosters a collaborative approach where designers work closely with participants, allowing for continuous improvement in project outcomes. Conversely, Source 2 provides an empirical example illustrating how community-driven initiatives like reverse graffiti can significantly enhance local sense of place and overall vitality. Both sources highlight the critical role of inclusive design practices in fostering meaningful engagement and positive transformation within communities.

## Key Findings

- Participatory design methods mitigate confirmation bias through iterative feedback loops.
- Community engagement is crucial for enhancing community vitality and sense of place.
- Reverse graffiti serves as a tangible example of participatory design in action.

## Open Questions

- How can reverse graffiti and similar initiatives be scaled up to address larger urban challenges?
