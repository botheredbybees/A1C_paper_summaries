## Resource Scarcity and Creative Solutions

The theme of resource scarcity emerges as a catalyst for creativity and innovation in diverse contexts. Source 1 explores how frugal innovation and bricolage, or making do with limited resources through improvisation, can be capabilities in crisis situations such as emerging markets facing economic challenges. It highlights structural equation modeling to analyze these phenomena. In contrast, Source 2 introduces constraint-based thinking as a structured approach for developing frugal innovations, emphasizing iterative processes that embrace limitations rather than viewing them as obstacles. Lastly, Source 3 examines the psychological impact of resource scarcity on users' ability to creatively utilize products, suggesting that constraints can foster a mindset shift towards innovative consumption and use, moving beyond functional-fixedness where items are only used in their intended manner.

## Key Findings

- Resource scarcity fosters frugal innovation through improvisation.
- Constraint-based thinking enhances the structured development of frugal innovations.
- Scarcity can induce psychological shifts leading to more creative product utilization.

## Open Questions

- How does constraint-mindset influence individual versus collective innovation processes?
