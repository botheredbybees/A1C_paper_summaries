## Nature-Connectedness

The concept of nature-connectedness is deeply intertwined with psychological theories such as Attention Restoration Theory (ART), Stress Reduction Theory, and the Biophilia Hypothesis. These theories collectively suggest that engagement with natural environments can lead to improved mental health outcomes through mechanisms like reduced stress levels, enhanced cognitive functioning due to restored attentional capacity, and an innate human tendency towards connecting with nature. Both sources highlight how exposure to natural settings can foster a sense of well-being and connection, underscoring the interplay between theoretical constructs and empirical evidence in environmental psychology.

## Key Findings

- Attention Restoration Theory (ART) posits that interaction with nature restores mental capacity after cognitive fatigue.
- The Biophilia Hypothesis argues for an innate human need to affiliate with other living systems, influencing emotional health positively through exposure to natural environments.
- Nature-connectedness is a significant factor in enhancing psychological well-being and reducing stress.

## Open Questions
