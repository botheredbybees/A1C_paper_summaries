## Creative Interventions in Dementia Care

The integration of arts-based interventions in dementia care has been shown to positively influence mental well-being and quality of life. Sources indicate that engaging individuals with dementia through visual arts programs can enhance their communication skills, well-being, and overall engagement. However, methodological critiques suggest the need for rigorous methodologies to control for confounding factors when evaluating these impacts. Key findings from various studies highlight the potential of such interventions in fostering mental health improvement among those affected by dementia. Nevertheless, there remains a gap in understanding how to rigorously assess the effectiveness of arts-based approaches while ensuring cultural inclusivity and appropriateness.

## Key Findings

- Arts-based interventions have positive impacts on quality of life and communication skills for individuals with dementia.
- Visual art programs can enhance well-being among people living with dementia.
- Creative practices contribute to mental health improvement in dementia care.

## Open Questions

- What are the most effective methodological approaches to assess the impact of arts-based interventions on dementia patients?
