## Youth Social Issues

The sources highlight the multifaceted challenges faced by youth today. Source 1 explores how applied theatre can serve as a platform to address issues such as cyberbullying through audience engagement and interactive performances, suggesting that arts-based interventions may offer unique opportunities for dialogue and support. In contrast, Source 2 presents quantitative data from a survey of young people regarding their perceptions of social challenges including cost-of-living pressures, environmental concerns, mental health struggles, and educational hurdles. This report underscores the diverse range of issues affecting marginalized youth and suggests that these challenges are interconnected, requiring comprehensive solutions.

## Key Findings

- Applied theatre offers an innovative way to engage with complex social issues such as cyberbullying among youth.
- Youth face multiple interrelated social challenges including mental health disorders, educational barriers, and economic pressures.
- Cost-of-living concerns are significant for young people, impacting their ability to access essential services and opportunities.

## Open Questions

- How can applied theatre be effectively integrated into existing support systems for youth struggling with mental health issues?
