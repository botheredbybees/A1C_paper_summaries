## Musculoskeletal Disorders in Musicians

The sources collectively emphasize the prevalence of musculoskeletal disorders among musicians, highlighting the need for a musician-centered approach that addresses both the physical and psychological aspects of their performance. Source 1 introduces a specific approach tailored to individual instruments, focusing on injury management. Sources 2 and 4 elaborate on the range of musculoskeletal issues, including nerve entrapment and focal dystonia, along with noise-induced hearing impairment. Source 3 underscores the importance of collaboration between medical professionals and music educators to address postural quality and musculoskeletal health comprehensively. Sources 5 and 6 introduce initiatives aimed at improving occupational health and safety, emphasizing the role of work health safety programs and the impact of performance anxiety on musicians' well-being.

## Key Findings

- Musicians face a variety of musculoskeletal disorders related to their profession, including nerve entrapment and focal dystonia.
- A collaborative approach between medical professionals and music educators is essential for addressing musculoskeletal health.
- Occupational health and safety initiatives can significantly improve the well-being of professional musicians.

## Open Questions

- What are the most effective methods for integrating performance anxiety management into musician-centered musculoskeletal disorder treatments?
