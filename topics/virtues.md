## Character Strengths and Virtues

The concept of character strengths and virtues is central to positive psychology and focuses on understanding and promoting positive human qualities. Character strengths are positive traits that facilitate individual and collective flourishing, while virtues represent the moral and ethical values that transcend cultures and are universally recognized. Both sources highlight the Values in Action (VIA) classification of character strengths and virtues, which is a systematic framework for identifying and developing positive traits. This framework is crucial in the field as it provides a comprehensive structure for the study of human strengths and virtues, fostering individual development and societal well-being.

## Key Findings

- Character strengths are positive traits that facilitate individual and collective flourishing.
- Virtues represent moral and ethical values that are universally recognized.
- The Values in Action (VIA) classification system is a comprehensive framework for understanding and developing character strengths and virtues.

## Open Questions

- How can the Values in Action (VIA) classification system be adapted or expanded to better address cultural and individual differences in the perception and application of virtues and character strengths?
