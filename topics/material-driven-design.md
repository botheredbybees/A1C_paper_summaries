## Material-Driven Design

Material-Driven Design (MDD) is a design approach that emphasizes the role of material properties and processes in shaping product aesthetics, functionality, and sustainability. Across different disciplines such as jewelry design, textile interfaces, and general product design, MDD seeks to enhance user experiences by fostering an emotional connection with materials while also addressing environmental concerns like waste reduction (SDG 12). This method integrates the exploration of material properties with human-centered computing techniques to develop innovative solutions that resonate both aesthetically and emotionally. However, there is a need for further investigation into how MDD can be effectively scaled across diverse industries.

## Key Findings

- Material-Driven Design (MDD) emphasizes the intrinsic qualities and sustainability of materials in product design
- Integration of human-centered computing enhances user experience through emotional connections with materials
- MDD addresses environmental challenges by promoting sustainable practices such as waste reduction

## Open Questions

- How can Material Driven Design be effectively scaled across various industrial sectors to promote widespread sustainability?
