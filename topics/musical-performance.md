## Musical Performance

The study of musical performance integrates the understanding of how auditory-motor learning influences memory for music and explores strategies to accommodate hearing loss in musicians. Source 1 highlights that sensory motor integration plays a critical role in developing auditory memory, suggesting that mental imagery and practice enhance musical recall capabilities. Conversely, Source 2 addresses challenges faced by musicians with hearing impairments, proposing the use of alternative sensory pathways like vision as compensatory mechanisms. Both sources emphasize the importance of inclusive music education practices to accommodate diverse learning needs. The integration of these insights suggests a multifaceted approach to enhancing performance through both cognitive and physiological adaptations.

## Key Findings

- Sensory motor integration is crucial for developing auditory memory in musicians.
- Musicians can use mental imagery as an aid in rehearsing and performing music.
- Inclusive music education practices are essential for accommodating hearing-impaired performers.

## Open Questions

- How can sensory substitution strategies be effectively integrated into the training of hearing-impaired musicians to enhance their performance?
