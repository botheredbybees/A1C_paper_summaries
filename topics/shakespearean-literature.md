## Shakespearean Literature in Youth Justice Programs

The integration of Shakespearean literature into youth justice programs is an intervention aimed at engaging young offenders through educational and therapeutic means. Both sources highlight the use of workshops and performances featuring Shakespeare's texts to foster positive community engagement among participants. These interventions are designed by teaching artists who facilitate a deep understanding and appreciation of the plays, which can promote personal growth and social skills development in youth involved with the justice system. The programs focus on leveraging the universal themes found within Shakespearean literature as tools for self-reflection and societal critique.

## Key Findings

- Shakespearean texts are used as educational and therapeutic interventions in youth justice programs to engage young offenders.
- Workshops and performances facilitated by teaching artists help participants develop an understanding of Shakespeare's plays.
- Community building is a core component of these programs, aiming to foster social skills and personal growth.
