## Chiaroscuro in Art

Chiaroscuro, an Italian term denoting the interplay of light and dark, plays a pivotal role in the enhancement of visual art through the use of contrasting values to create a sense of depth and volume. In the context of FXA101 Creativity and Ageing, activities focused on chiaroscuro techniques highlight the practical application of this method to engage older adults in the exploration of light-shadow relationships and the creation of visually rich artworks. The source materials emphasize the importance of chiaroscuro not only in the technical aspects of creating form and depth but also in the symbolic representation of themes and emotions. This method is seen as a tool for creative expression, enhancing the understanding of light and shadow as integral elements in artistic storytelling and composition.

## Key Findings

- Chiaroscuro techniques enhance visual depth and volume in art by contrasting light and dark.
- Lighting techniques in chiaroscuro are crucial for engaging older adults in creative activities.
- Chiaroscuro is used symbolically to convey emotions and themes beyond mere visual representation.

## Open Questions

- How can the symbolic use of light and shadow in chiaroscuro be effectively taught to enhance emotional expression in older adults?
