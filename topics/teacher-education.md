## Reflective Practice in Teacher Education

Reflective practice in teacher education is a methodological approach that focuses on enhancing teachers' critical awareness and professional development through self-reflection. Across the sources, there is a consensus on the importance of reflective practice in fostering critical awareness and transformative learning. Source 3 and 4 emphasize the role of reflective practice in teacher professional standards and its impact on the theory-practice nexus. Source 5 highlights the potential of online platforms to support reflective practice, promoting social and cognitive development. Source 6 explores the concept of transformative reflective practice, moving beyond conventional reflection to encourage critical thinking and personal growth. Despite these advancements, Source 1 raises concerns about the impact of standardized testing on creative thinking and its implications for teacher education, suggesting a tension between mandated standards and creative, reflective teaching.

## Key Findings

- Reflective practice enhances critical awareness and professional development in teachers.
- Online forums and other digital platforms can support reflective practice and promote social and cognitive development.
- Transformative reflective practice moves beyond conventional reflection to foster critical thinking and personal growth.

## Open Questions

- How do standardized testing requirements influence teachers' engagement in creative thinking and reflective practice?
