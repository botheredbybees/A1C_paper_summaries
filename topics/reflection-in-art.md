## Reflection in Art

The topic of reflection in art is explored in the context of creativity and ageing. Sources emphasize the symbolic use of mirrors and interactive artworks as reflective tools in art. These elements serve as mediums for introspection and self-expression, fostering a deeper understanding of one's identity and experiences. Reflection in art is a method that invites participants to engage with their inner selves, often through the symbolic representation of mirrors, which can evoke a sense of personal introspection and symbolic meaning. The integration of interactive elements in art encourages a participatory and experiential engagement, thereby enhancing the reflective process. However, the sources do not delve deeply into the specific methods or techniques for utilizing reflection in art for therapeutic or educational purposes, leaving room for further investigation into the practical applications and benefits of this method.

## Key Findings

- The symbolic use of mirrors in art facilitates introspection and self-expression.
- Interactive artworks enhance the reflective process by encouraging active participation.
- Reflection in art serves as a method for exploring personal identity and experiences.
