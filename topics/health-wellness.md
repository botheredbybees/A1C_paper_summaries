## Music and Social Prescribing in Health

The integration of music therapy into healthcare illustrates a therapeutic relationship that enhances patient care through an interdisciplinary approach. This method aligns with broader social prescribing initiatives aimed at addressing socio-economic factors affecting health, such as isolation and lack of access to non-clinical resources like community activities. Both approaches emphasize the importance of integrated care strategies that move beyond traditional medical interventions to include social and cultural elements which significantly impact wellness. Music therapy can be seen as a specialized form of social prescribing focused on leveraging music's unique benefits for mental health and rehabilitation.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy contributes positively to patient care through an interdisciplinary approach involving therapeutic relationships
- Social prescribing initiatives address socio-economic factors that influence health outcomes beyond clinical treatments
- Integrated care strategies incorporating music therapy can complement social prescribing efforts in holistic healthcare approaches

## Open Questions

- How can the efficacy and scalability of music therapy as a component of broader social prescribing schemes be further evaluated?
