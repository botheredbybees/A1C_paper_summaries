## Existentialism

Existentialism is a philosophical and psychological theory focusing on individual freedom, choice, and responsibility. The concept of the 'will to meaning' is central, emphasizing the human drive to find significance in life, which counters the 'existential vacuum' – a sense of meaninglessness arising from the realization of our freedom. Viktor Frankl's logotherapy is a therapeutic approach that aims to help individuals discover and live in accordance with their personal meaning, emphasizing experiential, creative, and attitudinal values. Both sources highlight the importance of dereflection, which involves transcending one's self-reflection to find meaning, underscoring the dynamic nature of existentialist thought in shaping human behavior and well-being.

## Key Findings

- The 'will to meaning' is a fundamental drive in human psychology.
- Logotherapy is an existential approach to therapy focusing on discovering personal meaning.
- The existential vacuum represents a crisis of meaning arising from the awareness of freedom.
