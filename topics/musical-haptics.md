## Musical Haptics

The exploration of musical haptics integrates technology and methods designed to convey auditory information through tactile sensation. Source 1 highlights the use of mechanoreceptors in sensory-substitution systems that translate audio signals into touch, emphasizing signal processing as a critical component. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on the practical application of these principles by examining the emotional impact of a commercial wearable system designed to deliver tactile bass experiences. Both sources converge on the concept of audio-tactile substitution, illustrating its potential in enhancing musical experiences through physical sensations that complement auditory inputs. However, while Source 1 delves into technological and methodological aspects, Source 2 demonstrates how such systems can influence emotional responses, indicating a broader application beyond mere sensory augmentation.

## Key Findings

- Mechanoreceptors play a crucial role in translating audio signals into tactile sensations through specialized technologies.
- Signal processing techniques are essential for effective conversion of auditory information to tactile feedback.
- Commercial applications of haptic technology show significant potential in altering emotional responses when experiencing music.
