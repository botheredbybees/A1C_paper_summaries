## Sitting Meditation

Sitting meditation is a core practice in mindfulness interventions, emphasizing breath awareness and a non-judgmental attitude towards thoughts and sensations. It is often a component of mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) programs designed to improve dispositional mindfulness and reduce stress. The practice involves maintaining an upright posture and redirecting attention back to the breath whenever the mind wanders, fostering a present-centered awareness. Sources indicate that sitting meditation is a foundational technique for developing and sustaining mindfulness, though the integration of cognitive therapy elements can enhance its efficacy in managing stress and emotional regulation.

## Key Findings

- Sitting meditation is a core component of MBSR and other mindfulness-based interventions.
- Maintaining a non-judgmental attitude and breath awareness are key elements of sitting meditation.
- Sitting meditation helps in managing stress and enhancing dispositional mindfulness.
