## Skill Development Through Creative Arts

The synthesis of skill development through creative arts, particularly photography and videography (Source 1) and Shakespearean workshops (Source 2), reveals a common thread in enhancing personal growth and mental health. Both sources emphasize the importance of engagement with artistic practices as vehicles for fostering individual capabilities and psychological well-being. Source 1 highlights how mastering skills in photography and videography can lead to increased self-efficacy, alignment with intrinsic motivations, and improved mental health, aligning with Flow Theory and Self-Determination Theory. In contrast, Source 2 discusses the role of teaching artists facilitating workshops based on Shakespearean texts within a youth justice context, which aims at skill development through dramatic performance and critical thinking. Despite differences in medium—visual arts versus literary drama—both interventions underscore the value of active participation and experiential learning in personal development.

## Key Findings

- Skill development through creative arts enhances self-efficacy and mental health.
- Engagement with artistic practices can align with intrinsic motivations, supporting psychological well-being.
- Both photography/videography skills and Shakespearean workshops are effective tools for fostering cognitive and emotional growth.

## Open Questions

- How do the long-term impacts of skill development through creative arts compare between visual media and literary drama?
