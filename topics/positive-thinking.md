## Positive Thinking

Positive thinking is a central theme in both performance psychology and positive psychology, each with unique applications. In performance psychology, positive thinking is utilized to alleviate performance anxiety and stage fright through techniques like breathing exercises. This approach emphasizes the immediate behavioral and cognitive strategies one can employ to mitigate stress and enhance performance. In contrast, positive psychology focuses on the broader dispositional aspect of optimism and benefit-finding, which involves a more enduring outlook on life that can enhance overall wellbeing and resilience. Both fields underscore the power of a positive mindset, yet they differ in the immediacy and scope of its application.

## Key Findings

- Positive thinking is a critical component in managing performance anxiety and stage fright.
- Breathing techniques can serve as a practical tool for immediate stress reduction in performance situations.
- Positive psychology emphasizes dispositional optimism and benefit-finding as essential for long-term wellbeing.

## Open Questions

- How do the immediate benefits of positive thinking in performance situations translate into enduring dispositional optimism?
