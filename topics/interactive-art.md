## Interactive Art

Interactive art integrates various forms of artistic expression and technology to engage users actively. The Louvre Abu Dhabi's Tactile Studio exemplifies this approach by creating inclusive designs that cater to diverse sensory exploration needs, emphasizing the importance of accessibility and inclusivity in museums. This initiative highlights how interactive art can serve as an effective tool for enhancing the museum experience for people with disabilities, promoting sensory engagement beyond traditional visual and auditory experiences. In contrast, the ArtsIT conference emphasizes the role of interdisciplinary collaboration and experimental practice in interactive art, focusing on how technology can be used to create engaging and accessible experiences. Both sources underscore the potential of interactive art to enhance accessibility and inclusivity, though they differ in their primary emphasis—practical application versus theoretical exploration and innovation.

## Key Findings

- Interactive art enhances accessibility and inclusivity in cultural institutions.
- Interdisciplinary collaboration is crucial for the development of interactive art.
- Experimental practices in interactive art can lead to innovative solutions.

## Open Questions

- How can interactive art further bridge the gap between theoretical exploration and practical application in enhancing accessibility?
