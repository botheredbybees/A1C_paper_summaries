## Eye-Gaze Technology

Eye-gaze technology is a transformative intervention that enables individuals with motor disabilities to communicate and interact with the digital world. Both Francis Tsai and Becky share their journeys with eye-gaze technology, highlighting its impact on their ability to express themselves and engage with society. Tsai's experience showcases how the technology facilitates social connectivity and artistic expression, whereas Becky's journey emphasizes the role of eye-gaze technology in opening pathways to digital art and academia. Despite their different backgrounds, both individuals underscore the technology's potential to bridge the gap between disability and societal engagement, transforming the lives of users through enhanced communication and interaction.

## Key Findings

- Eye-gaze technology significantly enhances social connectivity for individuals with motor disabilities.
- The technology opens new avenues for artistic expression and academic engagement.
- Eye-gaze technology is a transformative intervention that can bridge the gap between disability and societal engagement.

## Open Questions
