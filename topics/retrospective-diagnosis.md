## Retrospective Diagnosis

The concept of retrospective diagnosis involves interpreting historical medical conditions through the lens of contemporary understanding. This method is applied in both literary and artistic analyses to understand health issues of figures from past eras, such as those depicted in Renaissance necrologies or artworks like Caravaggio's self-portraits. Both sources emphasize the utility of this methodology for elucidating the ailments of historical subjects based on textual and visual evidence, albeit with inherent limitations due to lack of direct medical records.

## Key Findings

- Retrospective diagnosis aids in understanding past health conditions through indirect evidence
- Both convent necrologies and artistic depictions can serve as valuable sources for retrospective diagnosis

## Open Questions

- What are the limitations and potential misinterpretations of applying modern medical knowledge to historical cases?
