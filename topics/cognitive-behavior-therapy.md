## Cognitive Behavior Therapy

Cognitive Behavior Therapy (CBT) is a widely recognized therapeutic intervention, particularly for treating substance abuse and managing post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) in individuals with burn injuries. Source 1 discusses the potential of music therapy to enhance engagement in group CBT sessions for substance abuse, suggesting that music can serve as an effective adjunct to traditional CBT methods, making the therapy more appealing and engaging for patients. Conversely, Source 2 highlights the application of CBT in a multidisciplinary approach to treating burn injuries, emphasizing the psychological impact of such severe physical trauma and the importance of addressing PTSD and other psychological conditions. While both sources underscore the versatility and efficacy of CBT in diverse therapeutic settings, they diverge in their focus on specific patient populations and the additional methods (such as music therapy) that can complement CBT.

## Key Findings

- CBT is effective for substance abuse treatment.
- CBT is used in multidisciplinary approaches to treating burn injuries.
- Music therapy can enhance patient engagement in CBT for substance abuse treatment.
