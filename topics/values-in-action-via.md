## Values In Action Via

The Values in Action (VIA) classification of character strengths is a theory developed to understand and promote the application of positive virtues in everyday life. This theory has been explored and critiqued from various philosophical and psychological perspectives. Sources highlight the unity of virtues within VIA as a key feature, suggesting that all virtues are interconnected and influence each other. The VIA framework has been seen as a positive psychological tool for individual development, emphasizing the importance of character strengths in personal growth and happiness. However, the application of VIA in political communities and the broader social context raises questions about the practical implications of virtue ethics in public life. The integration of Aristotelian and Thomistic philosophy with VIA provides a richer, more comprehensive understanding of virtues, but also introduces challenges related to the consistency and applicability of these diverse philosophical traditions in modern society.

## Key Findings

- The Values in Action (VIA) framework emphasizes the unity and interconnectedness of virtues and character strengths.
- VIA is utilized as a tool in positive psychology for personal development and enhancing well-being.
- Aristotelian and Thomistic philosophies offer deeper insights into the nature of virtues, complementing VIA.

## Open Questions

- How can the integration of Aristotelian and Thomistic philosophies with VIA be reconciled in practical applications?
