## Methodological Limitations in Psychological Research

Both laughter interventions for well-being in older adults and mindfulness research face significant methodological limitations. These limitations include the challenge of defining clear parameters for interventions and the use of self-report measures, which can introduce biases. In the context of laughter interventions, the classification and standardization of different types of interventions pose a major challenge. This difficulty is mirrored in mindfulness research, where the lack of a universally accepted definition complicates research design and the interpretation of results. Additionally, reliance on self-report measures in both areas can lead to biases, such as social desirability and recall issues. While both fields are aware of these limitations, they continue to face challenges in overcoming them to enhance the reliability and validity of their research.

## Key Findings

- Definition and standardization challenges in interventions
- Use of self-report measures introduces biases
- Challenges in research design due to methodological limitations

## Open Questions

- How can the field overcome the lack of standard definitions and classification systems for interventions?
