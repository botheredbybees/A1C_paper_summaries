## Person-centered Language

The sources emphasize the importance of person-centered language in reducing stigma and promoting respect and inclusive practices. Both documents highlight that effective use of such language can enhance an individual's sense of personhood, uphold their human rights, and foster a more respectful communication environment. The guidelines suggest that by adopting inclusive terminology, professionals and individuals alike can contribute to minimizing societal stigmas associated with certain conditions or identities.

## Key Findings

- Person-centered language aims to reduce stigma and promote respect in communication
- Inclusive language supports the human rights of individuals and enhances their sense of personhood
