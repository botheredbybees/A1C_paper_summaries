## Customized Playlists

The use of customized playlists emerges as a promising intervention for enhancing the quality of life in individuals with dementia. The pilot study highlights that personalized music can yield therapeutic benefits, such as improved mood and social interaction. This approach leverages the emotional resonance and personal significance of music to address specific needs identified during initial assessments.

## Key Findings

- Customized playlists provide significant emotional and social benefits for persons with dementia.
- Initial assessments are crucial in tailoring personalized musical interventions effectively.
- Music therapy through customized playlists can improve life quality by addressing individual therapeutic needs.

## Open Questions

- How scalable is the creation of personalized music playlists across larger populations or different cultural contexts?
