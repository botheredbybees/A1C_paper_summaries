## Hildegard of Bingen's Influence on Medieval Medicine and Spirituality

The study of Hildegard of Bingen reveals a multifaceted figure who significantly influenced medieval medicine, spirituality, and religious music. Her holistic approach to health integrates the humoral theory prevalent at the time with elements from her spiritual visions, reflecting medical pluralism in practice. The welcome document introduces Hildegaard as a visual thinker, utilizing classical anatomy for insights into her medical philosophy. Sources also highlight Hildegard's contributions to medieval medicine through innovative approaches that emphasized the interconnection between body and soul. Her work on antiphonal singing further underscores her impact beyond medicine, into the realm of religious music and spiritual practices.

## Key Findings

- Hildegard of Bingen integrated classical anatomy with visual thinking strategies to enhance medical knowledge during medieval times.
- Her holistic approach combined traditional humoral theory with elements from her spiritual visions, reflecting a form of medical pluralism.
- Hildegard's influence extended beyond medicine into religious music and spirituality.
