## Ethical Issues in Mindfulness and Participatory Photography

The integration of ethical considerations within therapeutic interventions such as participatory photography and mindfulness reveals both therapeutic benefits and significant limitations. Participatory photography offers empowerment and self-reflection for adults with mental health issues, although it carries ethical concerns such as consent and privacy issues. Similarly, mindfulness practices, when commercialized or overprescribed, can lead to McMindfulness, a term highlighting the potential for ethical breaches and misuse. Both interventions face challenges in ensuring ethical integrity while delivering therapeutic benefits, suggesting a need for careful application and oversight. Tensions arise from the potential commodification of these practices, risking their efficacy and integrity.

## Key Findings

- Participatory photography can empower individuals with mental health issues through self-reflection.
- Mindfulness practices, when commercialized, may lose their therapeutic value and ethical integrity.
- Ethical concerns such as privacy and consent are critical for both participatory photography and mindfulness interventions.

## Open Questions

- How can therapeutic interventions like mindfulness and participatory photography maintain ethical integrity while being accessible and widely available?
