## Puppetry in Therapy

The use of puppetry as a therapeutic intervention is explored through its capacity to facilitate emotional expression and promote healing. Sources highlight the utility of non-verbal communication and mediated expression, enabling clients to express complex emotions without direct verbal articulation. This indirect mode supports sustainable practices in therapy by reducing immediate pressure on both therapist and client while encouraging exploration of deep-seated issues via storytelling and imaginative play.

## Key Findings

- Puppetry facilitates emotional expression through non-verbal communication.
- Therapeutic use of puppetry encourages healing through the narrative process.
- The indirect nature of puppetry provides a sustainable practice for therapy sessions.

## Open Questions

- How does the effectiveness of puppetry as a therapeutic tool vary across different cultural contexts?
