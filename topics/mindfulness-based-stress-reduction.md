## Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction

Both sources emphasize the efficacy of Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) in enhancing self-care and reducing stress among mental health professionals. Source 1 highlights MBSR's role alongside Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), both aimed at improving psychological flexibility, which is key for effective self-care. Similarly, Source 2 underscores the importance of teaching mindfulness techniques to behavioral health care staff for similar outcomes. Both studies suggest that MBSR can significantly decrease stress levels among healthcare professionals as measured by tools like the Perceived Stress Scale.

## Key Findings

- Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction is effective in reducing perceived stress and improving self-care among mental health professionals.
- Combining Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction with Acceptance and Commitment Therapy can enhance psychological flexibility, leading to better self-care practices.
- Teaching mindfulness techniques alongside MBSR interventions improves the overall well-being of behavioral health care staff.
