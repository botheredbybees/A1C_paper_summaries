## Economic Challenges Among Youth

The two sources highlight the significant economic challenges faced by youth in contemporary society. Both reports emphasize the rising cost of living as a major concern affecting daily life and long-term planning among young people. Additionally, they underscore the intersectionality of economic issues with environmental concerns, highlighting how climate change impacts exacerbate financial pressures through increased costs for necessities such as food and energy. Mental health disorders are also prominently discussed in both reports, indicating that financial stressors contribute significantly to psychological distress. Educational barriers further compound these challenges by limiting future earning potential, thereby perpetuating a cycle of economic hardship. Discrimination experiences mentioned in the second report suggest that marginalized youth face additional hurdles, including limited access to resources and opportunities.

## Key Findings

- The rising cost of living is a pervasive issue affecting youth
- Climate change impacts contribute to financial strain for young people
- Mental health disorders are exacerbated by economic stressors among youth

## Open Questions

- How can policymakers effectively address the compounded effects of climate change and economic challenges on mental health?
