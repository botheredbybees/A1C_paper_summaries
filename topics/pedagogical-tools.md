## Pedagogical Tools for Well-Being and Learning

The two sources highlight the utility of creative methods in pedagogy aimed at enhancing well-being and learning environments. Source 1 describes an online community art project designed to alleviate daily stress among women, focusing on collective care through inclusive artistic practices that empower individuals. This initiative underscores the importance of providing safe spaces for expression and emotional support, which are essential components for mental health promotion in educational settings. In contrast, Source 2 offers a literature review that emphasizes the use of puppetry as a pedagogical tool to foster positive classroom climates, enhance communication skills among students through cooperative learning, and facilitate attitude changes conducive to academic success. Both sources converge on the theme of employing non-traditional methods to create supportive educational contexts that can improve learner outcomes and well-being.

## Key Findings

- Creative pedagogical tools such as art projects and puppetry contribute positively to classroom environments by fostering emotional support and skill development among students.
- The use of inclusive and participatory teaching strategies helps in creating a sense of community, empowerment, and positive climate within educational settings.

## Open Questions

- What are the specific mechanisms through which creative pedagogical tools like art projects or puppetry influence mental health outcomes and learning capabilities among students?
