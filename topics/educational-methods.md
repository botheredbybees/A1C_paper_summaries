## Educational Methods in Creative Practice

The sources explore the use of visual and symbolic techniques in educational methods for creative practice. Source 1 discusses the practical application of understanding brain regions such as the visual, temporal, and motor cortices to enhance creative skills. This foundational knowledge aims to improve the educational methods in creative practice. Source 2 delves into the use of diptychs and contextual symbolism in photo-based learning, enhancing narrative skills through images. Both sources highlight the importance of visual and symbolic learning in creative education, but differ in their approach: one focusing on neurobiological foundations and the other on narrative and symbolic representation.

## Key Findings

- Educational methods in creative practice benefit from an understanding of the neurobiological basis of creativity.
- The use of visual and symbolic techniques in educational settings can enhance narrative and creative skills.

## Open Questions

- How can the neurobiological understanding of creativity be effectively integrated with visual and symbolic educational methods?
