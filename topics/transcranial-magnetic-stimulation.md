## Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation

The integration of transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) as a therapeutic intervention and its relationship with mental activities' brain changes is highlighted in the first source, emphasizing its potential for treating conditions like depression through neuroplasticity. The second source focuses on mindfulness-based stress reduction techniques aimed at improving self-care among healthcare professionals but indirectly supports TMS by indicating that altering mental states can have beneficial physical outcomes, aligning with TMS's goal of inducing brain changes via external stimuli to improve health.

## Key Findings

- Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) is a neurointervention aimed at enhancing neuroplasticity and treating neuropsychiatric disorders by modifying brain activity patterns.
- Mindfulness-based stress reduction techniques offer an alternative approach to altering mental states, with both methods potentially contributing to similar outcomes of improving patient or caregiver well-being through different mechanisms.

## Open Questions

- How do the long-term effects of mindfulness-based interventions compare to those of TMS in terms of inducing neuroplasticity and treating neuropsychiatric conditions?
