## Effects of Singing on Immune System and Stress

Both sources highlight the impact of singing on physiological and psychological processes, with a focus on the immune system and stress. Source 1 discusses the effects of singing on oral function, stress, and immunity, suggesting that singing can improve these aspects. Source 2 delves into stress-related biological processes, emphasizing chronic stress and its implications for health. Despite differing primary focuses, both sources converge on the detrimental effects of stress on the immune system, indicating a need for interventions such as singing to enhance immune function and reduce stress. There is a shared understanding that chronic stress is detrimental to biological processes and immune health, but singing offers a beneficial intervention.

## Key Findings

- Singing has positive effects on oral function, stress reduction, and immune system improvement.
- Chronic stress negatively impacts biological processes and immune health.
- Interventions such as singing can mitigate the adverse effects of stress on the immune system.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of singing on the immune system and stress levels?
