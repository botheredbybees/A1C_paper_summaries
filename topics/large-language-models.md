## Large Language Models in Creative Collaboration

The integration of large language models (LLMs) into creative processes is transforming human-AI collaboration. Both 'Jokeasy' and 'AutoStory' explore the use of LLMs for thematic joke writing and storytelling image generation respectively, highlighting their capacity to support human creativity through enhanced character identity consistency and thematic coherence. These studies demonstrate that while LLMs can generate content independently, their full potential is realized when they collaborate with humans, enabling a synergy where AI's computational power complements human creativity and narrative intuition. However, both sources also raise questions about the extent of human-AI interaction needed to maximize these benefits.

## Key Findings

- LLMs significantly enhance thematic coherence in creative tasks such as joke writing and storytelling image generation
- Human-AI collaboration leverages AI's computational power while incorporating human creativity for better outcomes
- Maintaining character identity consistency is crucial for effective use of LLMs in creative endeavors

## Open Questions

- What are the optimal levels of human involvement required to maximize the benefits of large language models in creative tasks?
