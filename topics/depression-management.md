## Depression Management Strategies

The management and treatment of depression involve a diverse range of interventions. Source 1 highlights Arts on Prescription (AoP) as an effective social prescribing approach that addresses anxiety, depression, and well-being through engagement in the arts. This intervention encourages individuals to participate in creative activities tailored to their needs, which can provide both therapeutic benefits and opportunities for social interaction. In contrast, Source 2 presents a randomized controlled trial examining the effects of dietary interventions such as a Mediterranean-style diet and fish oil supplementation on adults with depression. The study focuses on improving mental health alongside cardiometabolic health through dietary changes, demonstrating that nutritional strategies are also effective in managing depressive symptoms. Both sources underscore the importance of holistic approaches to treating depression, incorporating not just pharmacological or psychotherapeutic methods but also lifestyle and environmental factors.

## Key Findings

- Arts on Prescription (AoP) is an effective social prescribing intervention for anxiety, depression, and overall well-being.
- Mediterranean-style diet and fish oil supplementation can improve mental health in adults with depression.
- Interventions addressing depression should consider a holistic approach that includes lifestyle changes.

## Open Questions

- How do the effects of Arts on Prescription (AoP) compare to other non-pharmacological interventions for depression, such as cognitive-behavioral therapy or exercise programs?
