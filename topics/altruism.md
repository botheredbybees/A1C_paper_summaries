## Altruism: Motivations and Concepts

Altruism, a fundamental concept in psychology and sociology, is explored through different lenses in the provided sources. Source 1 delves into the motivations behind older men's engagement in DIY culture, highlighting altruism as a significant driving force. This source suggests that men are motivated to create and produce goods not just for personal use but also to support others in their communities, emphasizing the role of altruism in sustaining long-term engagement in such activities. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on compassion as a related but distinct concept to altruism. It discusses the nuances of compassion, which includes empathy and the desire to alleviate suffering, as a core component of altruistic behaviors. Both sources converge on the idea that altruism is not a singular concept but encompasses a range of motivations and emotional responses, which are contextually and individually specific.

## Key Findings

- Altruism is driven by a combination of personal satisfaction and a desire to help others.
- DIY culture among older men serves as a platform for expressing altruistic behaviors.
- Compassion, which includes empathy and the desire to alleviate suffering, is a fundamental aspect of altruistic actions.
