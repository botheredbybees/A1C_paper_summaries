## The Dramma Model

The DRAMMA model is a theoretical framework central to understanding how leisure activities contribute to well-being. It emphasizes detachment-recovery (DR), autonomy in leisure (A), mastery-challenge skill balance (M), meaningfulness (MA) of the activity, and social affiliation (A). This theory highlights that engaging in activities that offer these components can lead to enhanced psychological recovery from stress. Source 2 mentions a flow state as part of this model, indicating that an optimal experience where one's skills match the challenge level also fits within DRAMMA principles, further enriching its applicability in various leisure contexts.

## Key Findings

- The DRAMMA model integrates five critical components (detachment-recovery, autonomy, mastery-challenge balance, meaningfulness, and social affiliation) that together foster well-being through leisure activities.
- Engaging in activities with a balance of challenge and skill leads to increased psychological recovery from stress according to the model.
- The inclusion of the flow state within DRAMMA underscores its comprehensive approach to understanding optimal experiences in leisure.

## Open Questions

- How does the integration of social affiliation and meaningfulness in leisure activities influence individual well-being compared to detachment-recovery and autonomy?
