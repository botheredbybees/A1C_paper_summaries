## Music Participation and Health Benefits

The integration of music participation as an intervention in healthcare contexts highlights its broad spectrum of benefits for mental, physical, and cultural well-being. Both sources underscore the positive impacts of engaging with music actively through creative arts interventions or systematic reviews focusing on active participation. However, there is a notable emphasis from Source 1 on the application specifically to dementia patients, whereas Source 2 presents a wider scope that includes diverse populations such as those experiencing social determinants affecting health outcomes. The literature collectively indicates that structured activities involving musical engagement can significantly enhance well-being across various dimensions.

## Key Findings

- Music participation is associated with improved mental well-being.
- Creative arts interventions through music offer therapeutic benefits for individuals with dementia.
- Active participation in music and dance systematically promotes overall well-being, including physical health improvements.

## Open Questions

- How can the scope of music therapy be expanded to include broader populations beyond those with diagnosed conditions such as dementia?
