## Creative Constraints in Aging

The concept of 'creative constraints' emerges as a pivotal theme in the exploration of creativity among older adults. Week 5's activities emphasize how scarcity and salience influence artistic decision-making processes, aligning with Patricia Stokes' model which underscores the role of individual resources and priorities in creative endeavors. By introducing tasks that require participants to work within set boundaries, the course reveals how such constraints can spur innovative thinking and focus creativity towards specific goals or themes. The interplay between pareidolia—the perception of patterns where none exist—and creative constraints suggests a mechanism through which older adults leverage their unique cognitive strengths to enhance artistic expression.

## Key Findings

- Creative constraints in aging contexts can stimulate innovation by focusing attention on available resources and priorities.
- Scarcity salience plays a critical role in guiding expert artists' decision-making processes within creative limitations.
- Pareidolia, the perception of pattern recognition, interacts positively with creative constraints to foster unique artistic expressions among older adults.

## Open Questions

- How do different types and levels of creative constraints uniquely impact diverse populations of aging individuals in terms of their psychological and cognitive health?
