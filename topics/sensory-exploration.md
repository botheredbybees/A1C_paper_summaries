## Sensory Exploration

Sensory exploration is an intervention that enhances engagement and inclusivity through tactile and sensory experiences. Both sources emphasize the importance of sensory exploration for conscious creativity and accessibility. Source 1 highlights activities that focus on texture and color, which are crucial elements in sensory exploration. Source 2 discusses the Louvre Abu Dhabi's Tactile Studio, an initiative that promotes inclusivity in museums by offering tactile exhibits, thereby extending the concept of sensory exploration to a broader, public domain. Both sources underscore the role of sensory exploration in creating more inclusive and engaging environments, whether in educational settings or cultural institutions.

## Key Findings

- Sensory exploration enhances conscious creativity through tactile and visual engagement.
- Activities focusing on texture and color are central to sensory exploration practices.
- Inclusivity and accessibility are key benefits of sensory exploration in cultural and educational settings.

## Open Questions

- How can sensory exploration be further integrated into various public spaces and institutions to promote wider inclusivity?
