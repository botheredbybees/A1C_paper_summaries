## Academic Integrity

The concept of academic integrity is a fundamental principle discussed across multiple sources. It encompasses the ethical use of information and proper citation practices to avoid plagiarism (Source 3). Academic integrity includes understanding different types of sources such as primary, secondary, and tertiary, which are crucial for students in their assignments (Source 4). The use of technology tools like Turnitin is suggested as a means to check and ensure academic integrity (Source 2). However, while these tools can be useful, they do not entirely replace the need for a student's personal understanding and commitment to ethical practices. There are also discussions about integrating expressive writing, visual journaling, and generative artificial intelligence into courses, which may indirectly support students in maintaining their integrity by fostering self-reflection and mindfulness (Source 1).

## Key Findings

- Academic integrity is a core principle requiring ethical use of information and proper citation practices.
- Understanding different types of sources (primary, secondary, tertiary) aids in upholding academic integrity.
- Technology tools such as Turnitin can assist in checking for plagiarism but are not substitutes for personal commitment to ethics.
