## Dementia Experience

The experiences of individuals with dementia, particularly Alzheimer's disease, have been explored from various perspectives. Source 1 emphasizes the importance of a holistic approach to dementia care that respects the personhood of individuals with dementia, reflecting on how societal attitudes and practices can undermine the identity and dignity of these individuals. It highlights the need for a more compassionate and person-centered approach to dementia care that acknowledges the fullness of each person's life and experiences beyond their diagnosis. Source 2 offers a unique perspective through the lens of self-representation, showcasing the later paintings of William Utermohlen, an artist who documented his journey with Alzheimer's. His artwork provides a subjective view of the disease's progression, highlighting the emotional and cognitive challenges faced by individuals living with dementia. Both sources underscore the complexity of dementia experience and the importance of understanding this experience from the perspective of the person with dementia.

## Key Findings

- Person-centered care is essential in dementia care to respect the individuality and personhood of those affected.
- Artistic expression can provide profound insights into the subjective experience of living with dementia.
- Societal perceptions and practices can significantly impact the quality of life for individuals with dementia.

## Open Questions

- How can societal attitudes towards dementia be shifted to better support the personhood of those affected?
