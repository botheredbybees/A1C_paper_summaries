## Viktor Frankl's Logotherapy

Viktor Frankl's logotherapy offers a unique approach to psychological healing and personal growth centered around the concept of finding meaning in life, especially in the face of suffering. Both sources emphasize the importance of meaning-centered psychotherapy as a therapeutic technique that encourages individuals to seek and discover their unique life purpose and meaning. Logotherapy is highlighted for its ability to foster spiritual well-being and resilience in the face of adversity. The approach combats existential distress and hopelessness by guiding individuals toward the realization of their personal meaning-in-life. The application of Frankl's ideas in contemporary settings underscores the enduring relevance of his work in enhancing mental health and promoting human resilience.

## Key Findings

- Logotherapy addresses existential distress and hopelessness through the pursuit of personal meaning.
- Meaning-centered psychotherapy encourages individuals to find unique life purposes and meanings, enhancing spiritual well-being and resilience.
- Frankl's concept of meaning-in-life remains highly relevant and applicable in contemporary therapeutic settings.

## Open Questions

- How can logotherapy be effectively integrated into modern psychological practices to address the diverse needs of individuals in various cultural contexts?
