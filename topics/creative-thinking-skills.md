## Creative Thinking Skills

The development and enhancement of creative thinking skills are central themes in both sources. Source 1 emphasizes the importance of learning out-of-comfort-zone to foster creativity, suggesting that pushing boundaries leads to innovative problem-solving strategies. This approach aligns with the concept of constraint-induced creativity, where limitations can stimulate new ideas by forcing individuals to think outside traditional frameworks. In contrast, Source 2 focuses on cognitive-based training methods aimed at improving divergent and convergent thinking abilities as well as promoting cognitive flexibility among participants. These methods highlight structured techniques for enhancing creative thinking skills through targeted exercises designed to challenge existing thought patterns and encourage flexible problem-solving approaches.

## Key Findings

- Out-of-comfort-zone learning can enhance creativity by encouraging innovative problem-solving strategies.
- Constraint-induced environments promote new idea generation by challenging individuals to think beyond conventional frameworks.
- Cognitive-based training methods improve creative thinking skills through exercises that focus on divergent and convergent thinking.

## Open Questions

- How do out-of-comfort-zone learning experiences compare in effectiveness with structured cognitive-based training approaches for enhancing creative thinking?
