## Creative Arts Therapy and Its Implications

Creative arts therapy, as reviewed in both sources, is increasingly recognized for its broad therapeutic benefits across diverse settings. Source 1 highlights the role of creative arts therapies in promoting socioemotional competencies and affective awareness among postsecondary students, which in turn can support their academic success by enhancing resilience and coping strategies. Conversely, Source 2 focuses on chronic nonmalignant pain management, underscoring the effectiveness of arts therapy as part of a multidisciplinary care approach, emphasizing its theoretical underpinnings for addressing both physical and psychological aspects of chronic conditions. While both sources acknowledge the importance of group processes in facilitating therapeutic outcomes, they diverge slightly in their focus areas—academic versus medical contexts.

## Key Findings

- Creative arts therapy supports socioemotional competencies and affective awareness among students.
- Arts therapy can be a vital component of multidisciplinary care for chronic nonmalignant pain management.
- Group processes are important in enhancing therapeutic outcomes across various contexts.

## Open Questions

- How can the theoretical frameworks supporting arts therapy in medical settings be adapted and applied to educational environments?
