## Gender and Medicine in Literature and Education

The integration of gender studies in literature and education is explored through two distinct yet interconnected angles: the use of comics as an innovative educational tool for sex and gender education (Source 1) and the historical and contemporary issues of gender representation and bias in medical literature (Source 2). Both sources highlight the importance of inclusivity and diversity in medical education and representation. While Source 1 advocates for the incorporation of comics as a novel approach to engage and educate students on complex topics of sex and gender, Source 2 focuses on the historical underrepresentation of women and gender minorities in medical literature and the resultant gender biases in healthcare practices and patient outcomes. The sources collectively underscore the necessity for a more inclusive and gender-sensitive approach in medical education and practice, suggesting that the integration of diverse perspectives and methodologies can enhance the comprehensiveness and relevance of medical education and patient care.

## Key Findings

- The use of comics in sex and gender education can enhance understanding and engagement.
- Historical exclusion of women and gender minorities in medical literature has led to gender biases in healthcare practices.
- Incorporating diverse perspectives in medical education is crucial for improving patient care and outcomes.

## Open Questions

- How can the integration of diverse perspectives and innovative teaching tools be effectively implemented in medical education systems?
