## A Framework For Arts In Health Programmes

The Aesop framework provides a standardized approach to developing and researching arts in health programs. It emphasizes the importance of integrating various artistic disciplines into healthcare settings, aiming to enhance patient outcomes through creative engagement. Both sources highlight co-production as a core principle within this framework, underscoring the collaborative nature between artists, healthcare providers, and patients in creating effective interventions. Despite the emphasis on collaboration, there is a need for more detailed guidelines on how to implement these principles effectively across different cultural contexts.

## Key Findings

- The Aesop framework offers a standardized approach for integrating arts into health programs.
- Co-production between artists, healthcare providers, and patients is central to the success of arts in health initiatives.

## Open Questions

- How can the principles of co-production be effectively implemented across diverse cultural contexts?
