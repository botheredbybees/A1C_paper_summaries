## Relaxation Response

The relaxation response is a physiological process initiated through various interventions aimed at reducing stress and activating the parasympathetic nervous system. Both sources emphasize its importance in promoting mental and physical health, particularly focusing on its role in enhancing sleep hygiene and overall self-care practices. The vagus nerve plays a crucial role in this process by facilitating communication between the brain and internal organs to regulate heart rate, blood pressure, and digestion, thereby contributing to a state of relaxation.

## Key Findings

- The relaxation response is activated through various interventions aimed at reducing stress.
- Sleep hygiene practices are enhanced by inducing the relaxation response.
- Activation of the parasympathetic nervous system is central to achieving the relaxation response.

## Open Questions

- How do individual differences impact the effectiveness and mechanisms of eliciting the relaxation response?
