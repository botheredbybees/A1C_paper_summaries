## Personal Well-Being

The concept of personal well-being is explored through different theoretical approaches, emphasizing the importance of meaning and character strengths. Source 1 introduces the PERMA model and Steger's three-element model of meaning, highlighting the significance of positive emotions, engagement, relationships, accomplishments, and meaning in life as key components for well-being. Source 2 focuses on the VIA Classification of Character Strengths, underscoring the role of individual strengths and virtues in enhancing personal well-being. Both sources advocate for the application of these theoretical frameworks in practical contexts, suggesting that individuals can improve their well-being by identifying and leveraging their signature strengths and cultivating a sense of meaning in life. However, there is a tension between the specific elements of well-being outlined in the PERMA model and the broader scope of character strengths, indicating the need for further integration of these approaches.

## Key Findings

- The PERMA model and Steger's three-element model of meaning highlight the importance of positive emotions, engagement, relationships, accomplishments, and meaning in life for personal well-being.
- The VIA Classification of Character Strengths identifies individual strengths and virtues as crucial elements for enhancing personal well-being.
- Both approaches emphasize the application of theoretical frameworks in practical contexts to improve well-being.

## Open Questions

- How can the PERMA model and the VIA Classification of Character Strengths be integrated to provide a comprehensive understanding of personal well-being?
