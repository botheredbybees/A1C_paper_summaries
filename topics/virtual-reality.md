## Virtual Reality in Therapeutic Settings

Virtual reality (VR) has emerged as a promising tool in therapeutic settings, particularly in enhancing immersive experiences and promoting inclusivity in music technologies, as discussed in Source 1. VR's ability to create controlled, immersive environments offers unique opportunities for therapeutic interventions, such as music therapy. In Source 2, VR is explored in the context of burn injury rehabilitation, highlighting its potential for treating post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and cognitive-behavior therapy (CBT). Despite the diverse applications, both sources emphasize the need for a multidisciplinary approach, integrating psychological, social, and medical expertise to maximize the benefits of VR interventions. However, while VR shows promise, there are tensions regarding the inclusivity and accessibility of these technologies for all patient populations.

## Key Findings

- Virtual reality offers immersive experiences that can be leveraged in therapeutic interventions.
- VR is being explored for treating PTSD and other psychological conditions through cognitive-behavior therapy.
- A multidisciplinary approach is crucial for effective VR-based interventions.

## Open Questions

- How can virtual reality technologies be made more accessible and inclusive for diverse patient populations?
