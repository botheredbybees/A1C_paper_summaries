## Youth Wellbeing

The topic of youth wellbeing encompasses various dimensions including mental health and emotional regulation. Source 1 emphasizes the role of artistic expression in enhancing emotional regulation among young people, suggesting that creative activities contribute positively to their psychological resilience against stress and adversity. In contrast, Source 2 focuses more on structural issues affecting youth such as housing instability and discrimination, which can profoundly impact their mental health despite protective factors like engagement in arts programs. Together, these sources highlight the complex interplay between individual coping mechanisms and systemic challenges that influence youth wellbeing.

## Key Findings

- Youth face significant mental health challenges exacerbated by both personal and societal stressors.
- Creative expression can serve as a therapeutic tool for emotional regulation among young people.
- Structural issues such as housing instability and discrimination pose substantial barriers to youth wellbeing.

## Open Questions

- How do systemic factors like homelessness and inequality interact with individual interventions aimed at improving mental health outcomes in youth?
