## Dietary Interventions

The role of dietary interventions in promoting health and cognitive aging is a recurring theme across sources. Both studies emphasize the importance of specific diets like the Mediterranean diet, noting their beneficial impacts on mental health and cognition. Exercise benefits are also highlighted as part of a comprehensive lifestyle approach to healthy aging alongside dietary practices. Mindfulness and mindful awareness emerge as key components facilitating adherence to these nutritional strategies, thereby enhancing overall well-being.

## Key Findings

- Dietary interventions, particularly those following Mediterranean diet principles, improve mental health.
- Exercise benefits complement dietary changes in promoting cognitive health during aging.
- Mindfulness enhances the effectiveness of dietary practices by increasing awareness and adherence.

## Open Questions

- How do different cultural diets compare to the Mediterranean diet in terms of their impact on cognitive aging?
