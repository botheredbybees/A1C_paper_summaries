## Patient Safety

Patient safety is a critical area of focus in healthcare, emphasizing the importance of preoperative evaluation, vascular access management, and neuromuscular monitoring. The integration of these interventions is essential for minimizing postoperative complications such as residual paralysis and distressing awareness. Additionally, the incorporation of medical humanities into medical education underscores the need for a more empathetic and democratic approach to patient care, enhancing the narrative understanding of patient experiences and improving overall patient safety practices.

## Key Findings

- Preoperative evaluation and vascular access management are crucial for patient safety in interventional nephrology.
- Neuromuscular monitoring is vital for preventing postoperative complications such as residual paralysis and awareness.
- Medical humanities play a role in improving patient safety by fostering empathy and a narrative approach to patient care.

## Open Questions

- How can medical humanities be effectively integrated into the training of healthcare professionals to enhance patient safety?
