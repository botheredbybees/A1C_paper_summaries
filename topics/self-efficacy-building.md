## Therapeutic Photography

Therapeutic photography is an intervention that aims to enhance self-esteem and build resilience through the act of taking photographs. Both sources emphasize the therapeutic potential of this practice, highlighting its ability to foster a sense of control and empowerment among participants. The democratic nature of photography is also noted as an advantage, making it accessible for individuals from diverse backgrounds. However, while both sources align on the benefits of therapeutic photography, there is no explicit discussion on how these interventions might vary in effectiveness across different populations or contexts.

## Key Findings

- Therapeutic photography enhances self-esteem and resilience
- Photography's democratic nature makes it accessible to diverse groups

## Open Questions

- How does the efficacy of therapeutic photography vary among different demographic groups?
