## Neurosurgical Treatments for Brain Disorders

The sources highlight distinct neurosurgical interventions aimed at addressing specific neurological conditions. Source 1 discusses the impact of a brain tumor on musical function and utilizes intraoperative mapping to understand the role of the superior temporal gyrus in music processing. In contrast, Source 2 explores amygdala ablation as a potential therapy for medically unresponsive PTSD cases, underscoring the variability in neurosurgical approaches based on the condition's pathophysiology. Both sources emphasize the critical need for precise localization and targeted intervention to preserve functional abilities while treating debilitating conditions.

## Key Findings

- Neurosurgery can significantly impact neurological functions such as musical ability and emotional regulation.
- Intraoperative mapping is a valuable tool in neurosurgical procedures to ensure preservation of specific brain functions.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of amygdala ablation on patient quality of life beyond immediate symptom relief?
