## Holistic Care Models

The integration of creative activities and arts into healthcare systems represents a novel approach to enhancing overall patient well-being and engagement. Both sources highlight the concept of 'holistic care models' as central, emphasizing their potential to address multiple dimensions of health beyond merely physical ailments. Source 1 discusses how engaging in creative activities can foster stronger community partnerships for health, thereby supporting broader social determinants of health. Meanwhile, source 2 underscores the role of arts-based therapies and health improvement through art within holistic care frameworks. Despite differing emphases on specific mechanisms—creative engagement versus arts therapies—the sources converge on the importance of a comprehensive approach to healthcare that incorporates non-traditional interventions.

## Key Findings

- Holistic care models integrate creative activities and arts-based therapies into standard medical practice.
- Community partnerships play a crucial role in supporting holistic health initiatives through engagement in creative pursuits.
- Arts therapies offer a viable pathway for improving overall health and well-being.

## Open Questions

- How do different types of creative interventions (e.g., visual arts, music) contribute uniquely to holistic care models?
