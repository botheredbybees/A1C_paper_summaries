## Creative Arts and Music Therapies in Stroke Rehabilitation

Creative arts and music therapies are increasingly recognized as valuable adjuncts to traditional stroke rehabilitation. Creative art therapy has been shown to enhance rehabilitation outcomes and improve quality of life, with studies indicating positive effects on psychological and social dimensions. Neurologic music therapy (NMT), particularly through rhythmic auditory stimulation, has demonstrated feasibility and potential benefits for upper limb function and spontaneous language in stroke patients. However, variability in therapeutic approaches and outcome measures presents challenges in standardizing treatment protocols and evaluating efficacy. The integration of these therapies into multidisciplinary rehabilitation settings appears promising but requires further research to establish best practices and patient-specific interventions.

## Key Findings

- Creative arts therapies positively influence psychological and social well-being in stroke patients.
- Neurologic music therapy shows potential for upper limb and speech rehabilitation.
- Standardized outcome measures and protocols are necessary for consistent evaluation of therapy efficacy.

## Open Questions

- What are the optimal therapeutic approaches and outcome measures for integrating creative arts and music therapies into stroke rehabilitation?
