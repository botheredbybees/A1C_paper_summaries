## Audio-Tactile Rendering

The synthesis of audio-tactile rendering techniques reveals a growing intersection between tactile and auditory technologies to enhance emotional responses and music experience. Source 1 explores the impact of a commercial wearable system that converts audio signals into tactile sensations, finding improvements in the emotional valence when users experience bass frequencies through touch rather than solely hearing them. This suggests a significant role for tactile feedback in amplifying or modulating emotional experiences tied to musical content. Meanwhile, Source 2 delves into the technical details of creating an advanced wearable haptic device that uses multi-feature extraction based on spectral flux and YIN algorithms to provide more nuanced and responsive tactile outputs. These findings underscore the potential for sophisticated audio-tactile systems to offer personalized and enhanced music experiences.

## Key Findings

- Audio-tactile systems enhance emotional valence in listeners, as indicated by increased engagement with bass frequencies when experienced through touch.
- Multi-feature extraction using spectral flux and YIN algorithms allows for the creation of more sophisticated and responsive wearable haptic devices that can render a wider range of tactile feedback.
- The integration of advanced signal processing techniques into wearable technology may lead to personalized audio-tactile experiences, offering new dimensions in music consumption.
