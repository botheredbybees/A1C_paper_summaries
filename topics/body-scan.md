## Body Scan Meditation

Body scan meditation, a core component of Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) and cognitive therapy, aims to enhance dispositional mindfulness through focusing on bodily sensations. This practice, which involves a systematic exploration of the body's physical sensations from head to toe, promotes body awareness and sensory observation. Both sources highlight the technique's role in increasing an individual's present-moment awareness, which is critical for stress reduction and emotional regulation. While the body scan meditation script provides practical guidance on conducting the exercise, the lesson emphasizes the importance of dispositional mindfulness as a key outcome of such practices.

## Key Findings

- Body scan meditation is an integral part of MBSR and cognitive therapy interventions.
- The practice enhances body awareness and sensory observation, contributing to mindfulness.
- Dispositional mindfulness, fostered by body scan meditation, is crucial for emotional regulation and stress reduction.
