## Recruitment Strategies

The sources highlight various dimensions of recruitment and engagement strategies within different contexts. Source 1 discusses volunteer motivations in a citizen science project, identifying career advancement as a significant driver, alongside personal enhancement motives. It employs the Volunteer Functions Inventory (VFI) to assess these motivations comprehensively. In contrast, Source 2 explores how musical education enhances employability by imparting transferable skills that are attractive to recruiters. While both sources diverge in their focus—music education versus citizen science participation—they share a common theme of understanding factors that influence recruitment and retention. The need for clear communication about the benefits of volunteering or employment is highlighted across both studies, emphasizing the importance of aligning participant/recruit expectations with actual project/employment outcomes.

## Key Findings

- Career advancement serves as a primary motivation for volunteer participation in citizen science projects.
- Musical training provides individuals with transferable skills that enhance employability and appeal to recruiters.
- Clear communication about the benefits of volunteering or employment is crucial for successful recruitment.

## Open Questions

- How do different motivational factors influence long-term retention in volunteer-based initiatives versus traditional employment settings?
