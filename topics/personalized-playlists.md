## Personalized Playlists in Dementia Care

The synthesis of personalized playlists in dementia care highlights the therapeutic value of music-based interventions. Both sources emphasize the importance of reminiscence therapy and rhythmic entrainment as key components of these interventions. While Source 1 delves into how personalized playlists can reduce agitation by targeting specific biomarkers, Source 2 focuses on the biological underpinnings of such interventions, noting that rhythmic entrainment stimulates dopamine release. These sources align in their recognition of music's ability to enhance memory and mood among dementia patients but diverge slightly in their emphasis—Source 1 on behavioral outcomes (reduction of agitation) and Source 2 on neurochemical responses.

## Key Findings

- Personalized playlists are effective in reducing agitation in dementia care through targeting specific biomarkers.
- Rhythmic entrainment is a core component that enhances memory recall and improves mood among individuals with dementia.
- Music-based interventions, such as rhythmic entrainment, can stimulate dopamine release, providing neurochemical evidence of their therapeutic effects.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term benefits of personalized playlists in managing symptoms of dementia beyond short-term behavioral changes?
