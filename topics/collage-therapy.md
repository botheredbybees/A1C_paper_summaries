## Collage Therapy

Collage therapy emerges as a versatile intervention in the therapeutic landscape, particularly effective for fostering emotional expression and introspection. Sources indicate its application in both general psychological contexts and specialized medical settings, such as enhancing empathy among junior doctors involved in palliative care. In this capacity, collage therapy serves as a powerful tool within reflective practice frameworks, helping individuals articulate complex emotions and experiences that are otherwise difficult to express verbally. Additionally, the intervention plays a critical role in visualizing internal working models (IWMs) through transactional analysis (TA), thereby aiding therapists in understanding deeper psychological constructs like ego states and attachment dynamics. These applications underscore collage therapy's potential as an integrative approach across various therapeutic modalities.

## Key Findings

- Collage therapy is beneficial for enhancing reflective practice among medical professionals, particularly junior doctors.
- It serves as a tool to visualize internal working models (IWMs) through transactional analysis (TA).
- The intervention helps in understanding and articulating complex emotional experiences.
