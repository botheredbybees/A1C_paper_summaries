## Urban Planning and Community Well-being

The integration of urban planning efforts with community well-being initiatives is highlighted through two distinct yet complementary studies. Source 1, 'The Afloat Project,' focuses on the application of creative recovery methods in urban settings to enhance visual literacy among participants and improve overall community health. This project underscores the importance of engaging communities directly in designing their living spaces to foster a sense of ownership and pride. Conversely, Source 2 investigates the impact of nature experiences on mental health by reducing rumination and subgenual prefrontal cortex activity, suggesting that integrating green spaces into urban planning could provide significant psychological benefits. Despite differing methodologies, both sources converge on the importance of holistic approaches to urban development that prioritize human interaction with natural elements for enhanced well-being.

## Key Findings

- Creative recovery methods enhance community engagement and visual literacy in urban settings.
- Green spaces within cities contribute positively to mental health by reducing rumination.
- Urban planning initiatives benefit from integrating both creative and nature-based interventions.

## Open Questions

- How can urban planners effectively incorporate creative and nature-based elements into existing cityscapes without compromising other critical aspects of urban infrastructure?
