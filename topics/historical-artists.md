## Francisco José de Goya y Lucientes

Francisco José de Goya y Lucientes, a pivotal figure in the transition between Rococo and Romanticism, is celebrated for his deep exploration of the human psyche and social critique. His work often reflects personal and societal trauma, as well as an unflinching examination of the human condition. Goya's later years saw him grappling with significant health issues, including Vogt-Koyanagi-Harada disease and Cogan's syndrome, which impacted his artistic expression and creative process. Despite these challenges, Goya's ability to continue producing profound works underscores his resilience and the intrinsic connection between suffering and creativity. His legacy as an artist who fearlessly confronted societal issues through his art remains a significant influence on the understanding of the artist as a social critic and a witness to trauma.

## Key Findings

- Goya's art is a reflection of societal and personal trauma.
- His work is characterized by a social and psychological critique.
- Health issues in his later life influenced his creative process.

## Open Questions
