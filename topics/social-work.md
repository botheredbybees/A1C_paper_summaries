## Critical Reflection in Social Work Practice

Critical reflection is a pivotal method in social work practice, fostering transformative learning and professional development. The ROMA model, emphasizing reflection, objective analysis, movement towards action, and action itself, offers a structured approach to addressing aversive racism and promoting restorative supervision. This method not only aids practitioners in understanding and confronting personal biases but also equips them with tools to advocate for human rights and ethical practice, such as upholding personal autonomy and addressing ethical dilemmas in diverse contexts, like challenging discriminatory policies.

## Key Findings

- Critical reflection is central to transformative learning and professional development in social work.
- The ROMA model provides a structured approach to anti-racist practice, emphasizing reflection, objective analysis, and action.
- Critical reflection aids in addressing ethical dilemmas and promoting human rights within social work.

## Open Questions

- How can the ROMA model be adapted to address systemic discrimination and uphold fundamental human rights in various social work contexts?
