## Qualitative Methods in Social Work Research

The provided sources explore innovative qualitative methods within the context of social work research. Source one discusses collage as a creative data generation technique, emphasizing its potential for associative creation and ethical considerations specific to arts-based research. In contrast, source two examines photography as a method that encourages self-reflection and acts as a bridge between individual experiences and broader societal issues, highlighting the therapeutic value it holds beyond mere documentation. Both sources underscore the importance of adapting traditional qualitative methods to incorporate more imaginative approaches while maintaining rigorous ethical standards.

## Key Findings

- Collage serves as an effective method for creative data generation in social work research
- Photography is a valuable tool for fostering self-reflection and therapeutic engagement among participants
- Ethical considerations are paramount when using arts-based methods such as collage or photography

## Open Questions

- How can researchers balance the ethical implications of associative creation with the need for rigorous data collection?
