## Body Image in Youth

The reviewed studies on recreational dance interventions highlight significant positive impacts on various aspects of health and well-being among children and young people. A key finding is the improvement in body image, which reflects a broader trend towards enhancing mental health outcomes through physical activity. Additionally, these activities contribute to cardiovascular fitness and bone health. However, there are variations in how anxiety reduction is reported across studies, indicating a need for more standardized measures to capture such psychological benefits effectively.

## Key Findings

- Improvement in body image among young participants
- Positive impact on cardiovascular fitness
- Enhancement of bone health

## Open Questions

- Standardization of measurement methods for assessing anxiety reduction
