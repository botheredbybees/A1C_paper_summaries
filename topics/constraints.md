## Constraints and Creativity

The concept of constraints in creative processes is explored through two sources, both highlighting the multifaceted role of constraints in enhancing creativity. Source 1 discusses how constraints can influence the creative processes of research and development teams, focusing on how contextual factors can shape the way teams approach problem-solving and innovation. It suggests that while constraints can sometimes hinder creativity by limiting options, they can also serve as a catalyst by focusing the mind and providing direction. Source 2 delves into the idea of scarcity salience, illustrating how the awareness of limited resources can heighten the salience of constraints, potentially leading to more innovative and efficient solutions. Both sources underscore that the impact of constraints on creativity is context-dependent, with the nature of the constraint, the creativity of the individuals or teams involved, and the specific circumstances playing crucial roles.

## Key Findings

- Constraints can both hinder and enhance creativity depending on context.
- Scarcity of resources can heighten the salience of constraints, potentially leading to more creative solutions.
- Contextual factors play a significant role in how constraints are perceived and utilized in creative processes.
