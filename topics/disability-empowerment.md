## Disability Empowerment Through Art

The theme of disability empowerment through art is evident in both sources, showcasing the transformative impact of artistic expression on the lives of individuals with disabilities. Source 1 highlights Josie Webster's journey in the art world, emphasizing her Down syndrome inclusion as a strength and her use of art as a form of self-expression, challenging societal norms. This contrasts with Source 2, which focuses on technological empowerment through the EyeWriter project, a collaborative effort that uses eye-tracking technology to enable artists with disabilities to create interactive art. Both sources illustrate how art can serve as a powerful tool for overcoming barriers and enhancing self-worth.

## Key Findings

- Artistic expression is a significant means of empowerment for individuals with disabilities.
- Technology can play a critical role in enabling individuals with physical disabilities to engage in art.

## Open Questions

- How can the integration of technology and art further empower individuals with disabilities who face multiple barriers?
