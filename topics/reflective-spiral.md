## Reflective Spiral

The concept of the Reflective Spiral is a theoretical framework that supports a cyclical and dialogical process of reflection, which is instrumental in facilitating personal and professional growth. Source 1, 'Engaging the Reflective Spiral,' introduces the Model for Structured Reflection (MSR), emphasizing the dialogical movement as a critical component of this spiral, where individuals engage in a continuous conversation with themselves and others. This process involves revisiting and reframing insights through various dialogues, enhancing understanding and action-taking. Source 2, 'Framing Insights,' elaborates on the importance of multiple perspectives and fundamental ways of knowing within the Reflective Spiral. It underscores the necessity of broadening one's understanding to include diverse viewpoints, thereby enriching the reflective process. Both sources agree on the cyclical and dialogical nature of reflection but differ slightly in their emphasis: Source 1 focuses on the MSR and the dialogical aspect, while Source 2 highlights the importance of framing and diverse perspectives. Together, they suggest a comprehensive approach to reflection that integrates structured models with open dialogues and multiple viewpoints.

## Key Findings

- The Reflective Spiral is a theoretical framework emphasizing cyclical and dialogical reflection.
- Multiple perspectives and ways of knowing are integral to the Reflective Spiral.
- The Model for Structured Reflection (MSR) provides a structured approach to engaging in the Reflective Spiral.

## Open Questions

- How do diverse perspectives and structured reflection models complement each other in practice?
