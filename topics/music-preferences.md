## Music Preferences Across the Lifespan

The concept of music preferences across different life stages and generations is explored through various theoretical frameworks. Source 1 highlights how tastes in music evolve over a lifetime, correlating with social status attainment and shifts in musical genres. Source 2 delves into intergenerational transmission of cultural values, specifically examining the continuity between parental and adolescent music choices, suggesting that music preference can be influenced by familial and societal norms. Meanwhile, Source 3 proposes a five-factor model to structure musical preferences, emphasizing emotional/affective responses as key determinants of individual tastes. Lastly, Source 4 investigates how young listeners' cultural identification and bilingualism impact their music style preferences, indicating the role of identity and language in shaping musical choices. These sources collectively offer insights into both personal development stages and broader sociocultural contexts that influence an individual's musical preferences.

## Key Findings

- Music preference evolves over different life stages and is influenced by social status attainment
- Intergenerational continuity plays a role in the transmission of music tastes from parents to children
- A five-factor model can be used to categorize emotional responses that underpin musical preferences

## Open Questions

- To what extent do sociocultural factors outweigh personal development stages in shaping an individual's music preference?
