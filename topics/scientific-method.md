## Scientific Method

The sources highlight the application of the scientific method in experimental designs within music psychology. Both emphasize the importance of a falsifiable hypothesis and the operationalization of variables to ensure clarity and objectivity in research. Source one underscores the need for hypotheses that can be tested against empirical evidence, ensuring their validity and reliability through precise definitions of measurable variables. Source two broadens this focus by introducing the overall framework of the scientific method as applied to designing robust research studies. Together, these sources stress a systematic approach to inquiry involving clear hypothesis formulation, variable measurement, and rigorous testing.

## Key Findings

- A falsifiable hypothesis is essential for conducting meaningful empirical research in music psychology.
- Operationalization of variables enhances the objectivity and reproducibility of experiments within the scientific method framework.

## Open Questions

- What are the best practices for translating theoretical hypotheses into operationalized variables that yield reliable data?
