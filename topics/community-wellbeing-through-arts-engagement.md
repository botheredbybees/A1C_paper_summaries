## Community Well-being Through Arts Engagement

The integration of arts into community well-being initiatives is widely recognized for its ability to enrich healthcare and foster holistic care models. Sources emphasize the role of creative activities in enhancing community health by promoting social bonding, therapeutic benefits, and partnerships between various sectors. Healthcare-enrichment through arts is a central theme that underscores how artistic interventions can positively impact individual and communal wellness. Furthermore, visual arts are highlighted as a means to facilitate social connections and support mental health, contributing to broader efforts aimed at improving public well-being.

## Key Findings

- Creative activities contribute significantly to community well-being by fostering holistic care models.
- Artistic interventions can serve as effective tools in healthcare enrichment, offering therapeutic benefits beyond traditional medical treatments.
- Visual arts and social bonding through art play a crucial role in supporting mental health and strengthening community ties.
