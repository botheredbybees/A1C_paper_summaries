## Creativity in Therapy

The integration of creativity into therapeutic interventions emerges as a multifaceted approach to enhance mental well-being and manage the effects of vicarious trauma among practitioners. Sources converge on the use of creative arts and play therapy to foster emotional development, providing individuals with non-verbal avenues for expression and healing. These practices are shown to benefit both clients and therapists by offering alternative methods for processing emotions and addressing psychological challenges beyond traditional talk therapies.

## Key Findings

- Creativity in therapy offers a complementary approach to traditional verbal-based treatments.
- Creative arts and play therapy aid in managing vicarious trauma among mental health professionals.
- Emotional development through creative means can be beneficial for individuals across various age groups.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of incorporating creativity into therapeutic practices?
