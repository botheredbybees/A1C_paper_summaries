## Cultural Diversity in Education

The integration of cultural diversity into educational practices has been explored through various interventions aimed at enhancing students' understanding and acceptance of diverse cultures. One such intervention is the Sharing Stories youth theatre program, which uses interactive theatre as a method to promote sexual health knowledge among multicultural youth (Source 1). This approach not only educates participants on important health topics but also fosters an environment where cultural differences are celebrated and understood. However, there seems to be a gap in evaluating such programs for their long-term impact on cultural acceptance and educational outcomes beyond the immediate context of the intervention.

## Key Findings

- Interactive theatre can effectively promote sexual health knowledge among multicultural youth.
- Cultural diversity education is crucial for fostering an inclusive learning environment.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term impacts of interactive theatre programs on cultural acceptance and educational outcomes?
