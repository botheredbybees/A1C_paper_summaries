## Neural Circuits in Motor Control and Puberty

The study of neural circuits in motor control and during puberty reveals complex interplay between neural systems and hormones. Source 1 discusses the corticospinal system, cortico-cerebellar system, and cortico-basal ganglia system as key components in skeletomotor control. This source highlights the importance of understanding these neural circuits to gain insight into motor control and potential treatments for motor disorders. Source 2 focuses on pubertal hormones and their organizational effects on the adolescent brain and behavior. It suggests that hormonal changes during puberty have significant impacts on neural development and behavior. Both sources underscore the intricate relationship between neural systems and external factors such as hormones in shaping brain function and behavior. However, while Source 1 is centered on motor control, Source 2 focuses on puberty, indicating a need for research that bridges these two areas to understand the broader impact of hormonal changes on motor control.

## Key Findings

- The corticospinal, cortico-cerebellar, and cortico-basal ganglia systems are crucial for skeletomotor control.
- Pubertal hormones have significant organizational effects on the adolescent brain and behavior.
- Hormonal changes during puberty may influence neural circuits involved in motor control.
