## Digital Health Interventions for Aging Populations

Digital health interventions, such as personalized music playlists and online dance therapy, are emerging as valuable tools to enhance the quality of life and mental health of aging populations. In retirement homes, personalized music playlists have shown significant benefits in improving mental health and enhancing quality of life among seniors. Similarly, dance therapy has been identified as a beneficial physical activity for individuals with neurological disorders like Parkinson's disease. The effectiveness of these interventions is further amplified by online delivery platforms, such as ParkinDANCE Online, which facilitate broader access and engagement for participants. However, the integration of digital health solutions requires careful consideration of user engagement and the specific needs of the target populations. The case studies highlight the potential of digital health to revolutionize care for aging populations and individuals with neurological disorders, but also underscore the need for continued research and tailored approaches to maximize benefits and address unique challenges.

## Key Findings

- Personalized music playlists improve mental health and quality of life in seniors.
- Dance therapy offers an accessible and beneficial physical activity for people with Parkinson's disease.
- Online platforms facilitate broader access and engagement in digital health interventions.

## Open Questions

- How can digital health interventions be tailored to better address the specific needs and challenges of aging populations and individuals with neurological disorders?
