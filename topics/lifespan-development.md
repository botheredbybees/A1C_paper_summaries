## Lifespan Development

Lifespan development is a comprehensive theoretical framework that examines the psychological changes that occur throughout a person's life. It integrates various theories and perspectives to understand the progression of development, emphasizing a holistic approach to studying human growth and change. The theory encompasses both positive growth and challenges faced at different life stages, including the impact of environmental and biological factors. Source 1 focuses on sleep and health, which is a critical aspect of developmental psychology as it influences physiological processes like the HPA axis and sympathetic nervous system. Both sources highlight the importance of biological and environmental factors in shaping development, indicating a mutual reinforcement of physiological health and psychological development throughout the lifespan.

## Key Findings

- Lifespan development is a holistic approach that examines changes throughout a person's life.
- Biological and environmental factors significantly influence developmental processes.
- Sleep quality impacts physiological and psychological health, aligning with lifespan development theories.

## Open Questions

- How do specific biological processes, such as those involving the HPA axis, interact with developmental stages to impact overall health and well-being?
