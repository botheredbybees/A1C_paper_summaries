## Democratization Of Technology

The democratization of technology is exemplified through the Active Energy project, which seeks to engage older people in creative thinking and problem-solving. This initiative emphasizes intergenerational partnerships as a means to foster social innovation. The project illustrates how technological tools can be made accessible and relevant to diverse groups beyond their typical user base, thereby enhancing inclusivity and participation. However, it also highlights the challenge of bridging the digital divide for older populations, suggesting that while technology offers immense potential for empowerment, there are significant hurdles in terms of usability and accessibility.

## Key Findings

- Technology can be democratized to reach diverse age groups.
- Intergenerational partnerships facilitate social innovation through shared learning.
- Creative thinking initiatives require accessible technological tools.

## Open Questions

- How can the digital divide be effectively addressed for older populations in technology-driven interventions?
