## Performance-Related Musculoskeletal Disorders

Performance-related musculoskeletal disorders (PRMDs) represent a significant challenge for musicians, impacting their physical health and career longevity. Sources highlight the importance of a musician-centered approach, emphasizing the need for treatments that are specific to the individual and their instrument. This personalized treatment approach aims to address the unique biomechanical demands of different instruments. Additionally, the role of music performance anxiety as a contributing factor to PRMDs is noted, underscoring the necessity for psychological support alongside physical intervention. Work health and safety initiatives are also crucial, particularly in professional orchestral settings, to mitigate the risks associated with PRMDs. Despite these efforts, there is a need for more comprehensive strategies that integrate medical, psychological, and ergonomic solutions.

## Key Findings

- A musician-centered approach to treatment is essential for addressing performance-related musculoskeletal disorders.
- Music performance anxiety can exacerbate PRMDs, necessitating psychological support in addition to physical treatment.
- Work health and safety initiatives are critical in professional orchestral environments to reduce PRMD risk.

## Open Questions

- How can psychological and physical interventions be more effectively integrated in the treatment of PRMDs?
