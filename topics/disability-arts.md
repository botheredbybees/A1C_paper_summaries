## Disability Arts and Inclusion

Disability arts are increasingly recognized as an essential intervention for fostering societal understanding, building confidence among disabled individuals, and promoting disability justice. Sources emphasize the importance of disability-led initiatives in theater and other artistic fields, with a focus on inclusive practices, first-person narratives, and assistive technologies. These efforts not only enhance the creative output but also challenge societal norms and advocate for the rights of disabled people. However, the integration of assistive technologies remains a critical area for development, as demonstrated by the need for inclusive design and universal accessibility. The field is also characterized by pioneering artists like Lisa Bufano, who use their work to explore themes of body identity and suspension performance, further enriching the discourse on disability arts and inclusion.

## Key Findings

- Disability-led initiatives are crucial for fostering societal understanding and promoting disability justice.
- First-person narratives and inclusive practices are essential components of disability arts.
- Assistive technologies play a vital role in enhancing the accessibility and inclusivity of disability arts.

## Open Questions

- How can assistive technologies be better integrated into the creative process to further promote inclusivity and accessibility in disability arts?
