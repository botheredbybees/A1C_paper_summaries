## Professional Practice in Arts and Healthcare

The integration of arts and healthcare requires a professional practice grounded in ethical standards and inclusive communication. The NOAH Code of Ethics underscores the importance of respect for persons, integrity in practice, and justice and equity as foundational principles for artists and arts administrators working in healthcare settings. This framework ensures that practices are patient-centered, transparent, and equitable, thereby fostering trust and effectiveness. Meanwhile, Source 2 emphasizes the necessity of an affirmative approach towards LGBTIQ+ clients, which involves understanding and respecting gender identity and employing language that is inclusive and respectful. Both sources highlight the critical role of professional practice in enhancing therapeutic relationships and ensuring cultural competency within healthcare environments.

## Key Findings

- Professional ethics in arts-based interventions include respect for persons, integrity, and equity
- Inclusive communication practices are essential when working with diverse client groups such as LGBTIQ+ individuals
