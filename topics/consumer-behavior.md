## Consumer Behavior and Environmental Influences

The synthesis of these sources illuminates the significant influence of environmental factors on consumer behavior. Source 1 focuses on how resource scarcity affects consumers' product use creativity, introducing concepts such as functional-fixedness and constraint-mindset to explain how individuals adapt their consumption habits under constraints. Conversely, Source 2 delves into the impact of music tempo in restaurants on dining duration and spending behaviors, highlighting how external stimuli can alter consumer actions. Both studies underscore that environmental conditions shape consumer behavior through various mechanisms: one by imposing cognitive and resource limitations leading to innovative consumption strategies, while the other shows how sensory inputs like music can subtly manipulate time perception and expenditure patterns. However, there is a notable gap in understanding how these two types of environmental factors interact or influence each other.

## Key Findings

- Resource scarcity encourages consumers to employ creative solutions for product use.
- External stimuli such as music tempo can significantly alter consumer spending habits and dining behaviors.

## Open Questions

- How do cognitive constraints imposed by resource scarcity interact with external sensory inputs like music in shaping consumer behavior?
