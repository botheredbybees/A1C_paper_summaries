## Creative Art Therapy

Creative art therapy emerges as a promising therapeutic intervention for stroke rehabilitation, with a focus on enhancing rehabilitation outcomes and improving quality of life. Both sources highlight the use of creative art therapy to address functional, psychological, and social aspects of recovery. The randomized controlled trial underscores the specific benefits of creative art therapy on physical rehabilitation, while the systematic review aggregates findings from multiple studies to indicate that creative art therapy also positively impacts psychological health by reducing symptoms of anxiety and depression. The integration of both physical and psychological dimensions suggests a holistic approach to stroke rehabilitation that could be more effective than traditional methods alone.

## Key Findings

- Creative art therapy improves rehabilitation outcomes for stroke patients.
- Creative art therapy enhances quality of life in stroke rehabilitation.
- Creative art therapy helps reduce anxiety and depression in stroke patients.
