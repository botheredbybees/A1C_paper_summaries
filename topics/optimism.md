## Optimism

Optimism is a central theme in positive psychology, a field that emphasizes the study of human strengths and virtues to promote well-being rather than focusing solely on the correction of mental illness. Both sources highlight the importance of dispositional optimism, which refers to a tendency to expect positive outcomes in life. This trait is associated with better coping strategies, such as benefit-finding coping, which involves finding positive aspects in stressful situations. However, there is a notable gap in the sources regarding the mechanisms through which dispositional optimism influences well-being and mental health outcomes. Both sources also mention that optimism is a key component of positive psychology but do not delve into how it interacts with mental health issues, suggesting a need for further exploration into its role in clinical populations.

## Key Findings

- Optimism is a central concept in positive psychology.
- Dispositional optimism is linked to better coping mechanisms.
- Benefit-finding coping is an important strategy associated with optimism.

## Open Questions

- What are the underlying mechanisms through which dispositional optimism influences mental health outcomes?
