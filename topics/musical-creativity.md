## Musical Creativity

The exploration of musical creativity encompasses a range of methodologies and perspectives. The lecture on research methods in music highlights the importance of both quantitative and qualitative approaches to studying how music influences cognitive processes, physical engagement, and emotional well-being. It underscores the necessity for mixed-methods studies that can capture the multifaceted nature of human interaction with music. Meanwhile, discussions centered around music and disability introduce a social-cultural perspective that views disability not merely as an impairment but as part of human diversity. This lens emphasizes the role of musical creativity in empowering individuals with disabilities by providing them platforms to express themselves uniquely through music. Both sources underscore the value of interdisciplinary approaches—combining psychological insights with sociocultural understandings—to comprehensively analyze musical creativity.

## Key Findings

- Quantitative and qualitative research methods offer complementary perspectives on understanding musical creativity.
- Disability can be understood from a social-cultural perspective that values diversity, including creative expression through music.
- Interdisciplinary approaches are essential for fully grasping the complexities of musical creativity.

## Open Questions

- How do mixed-methods studies effectively integrate quantitative and qualitative data to enhance understanding of musical creativity?
