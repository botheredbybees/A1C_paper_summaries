## Self-Representation in Digital and Artistic Contexts

Self-representation is a multifaceted concept that involves the expression of one's identity and experiences through various media. The source on digital storytelling highlights the ethical and political dimensions of self-representation through the remediation of personal photographs, emphasizing the transformative power of digital technologies in reshaping how individuals perceive and present themselves. Conversely, the study on Alzheimer's disease through art underscores the subjective experience of individuals suffering from the disease, showing how self-representation can serve as a therapeutic intervention for patients to maintain a sense of self and communicate their experiences to others. Both sources suggest that self-representation can be a powerful tool for personal expression and social critique, but also highlight the challenges and ethical considerations involved in its practice.

## Key Findings

- Self-representation in digital contexts involves ethical and political considerations.
- Artistic self-representation can serve as a therapeutic tool for individuals with Alzheimer's disease.
- Both digital and artistic forms of self-representation can empower individuals to express their subjective experiences.

## Open Questions

- How do different cultural contexts influence the ethics and politics of self-representation in digital and artistic practices?
