## Mindfulness in Education

Mindfulness, a practice emphasizing present-moment awareness, is explored in two sources with a focus on its educational applications. The first source delves into the concept of mindfulness, its benefits such as reducing autopilot behavior and fostering compassion and self-compassion, while introducing the Langerian concept of mindfulness. This approach contrasts with traditional mindfulness practices by emphasizing active engagement with the environment rather than mere presence. The second source, however, covers a broader range of psychological concepts and practical applications, which may include mindfulness but does not specifically focus on it. This suggests a gap in the literature regarding the detailed implementation of mindfulness in educational contexts. Both sources highlight the potential of mindfulness in enhancing psychological well-being, although there is a need for more specific guidance on how to integrate mindfulness into educational settings effectively.

## Key Findings

- Mindfulness practices can foster compassion and self-compassion, which are beneficial for psychological well-being.
- The Langerian concept of mindfulness, which emphasizes active engagement with the environment, provides a unique perspective on traditional mindfulness practices.

## Open Questions

- How can mindfulness be effectively integrated into educational curricula to enhance student well-being and engagement?
