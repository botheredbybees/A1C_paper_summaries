## Medication Reduction

Both sources highlight the effectiveness of specific programs in reducing unnecessary medication among residents. The 'Implementation Survey Phase III - Student Volunteer Program' underscores the importance of resident engagement and volunteer program effectiveness as key factors contributing to a reduction in the use of psychotropic medications. Similarly, the Wisconsin Music & Memory Implementation Survey Results indicate that music therapy not only improves well-being but also leads to a reduction in medication usage. However, while both studies emphasize positive outcomes related to medication reduction, there is less detail provided on the mechanisms by which these interventions lead to such reductions or how they might be scaled up effectively.

## Key Findings

- Both resident engagement and music therapy are effective strategies for reducing unnecessary psychotropic medications in care settings.
- Programs aimed at medication reduction tend to report high levels of stakeholder satisfaction.

## Open Questions

- What specific mechanisms enable interventions like resident engagement programs or music therapy to reduce the use of psychotropic medications?
