## Creativity and Constraints

The concept of creativity under constraints is explored through a cross-disciplinary lens, revealing both the inhibiting and facilitating roles of constraints on creative processes. Source 1 emphasizes the inverted-U-shaped relationship between constraints and creativity, suggesting that moderate levels of constraint can enhance creativity while excessive or insufficient constraints may hinder it. Mediating mechanisms such as cognitive flexibility and goal-directed behavior play crucial roles in this relationship. Source 2 complements this understanding by introducing the concept of scarcity salience, which highlights how the perception of scarcity can sharpen focus and resourcefulness, thereby enhancing creativity. Both sources underscore the dynamic nature of constraints and their potential to serve as catalysts for innovation when managed effectively. However, the sources also highlight the complexity and variability in how different types of constraints influence creativity, indicating that a nuanced approach is necessary to fully understand their impact.

## Key Findings

- Constraints can both inhibit and enhance creativity, depending on their level and nature.
- Cognitive flexibility and goal-directed behavior are critical in mediating the relationship between constraints and creativity.
- Perception of scarcity can positively influence creativity by enhancing focus and resourcefulness.

## Open Questions

- How do different types and levels of constraints impact creativity differently across various domains and contexts?
