## Puppet Education Framework

The Puppet Education (Pup-Ed) framework has emerged as an innovative intervention in pediatric care settings. Focused on the therapeutic use of puppets to engage and communicate with children, it aims at reducing anxiety among young patients through a creative and non-threatening interaction method. Sources highlight that nurses have found these sessions beneficial for addressing medical fears and facilitating discussions about treatment processes. However, there is a tension between the structured nature of hospital environments and the fluid, less predictable use of puppets as an educational tool. This intervention shows promise in enhancing patient engagement and psychological support but also highlights the need for more standardized training methods for healthcare providers to effectively implement Pup-Ed.

## Key Findings

- Puppet education (Pup-Ed) framework utilizes therapeutic puppet interactions to reduce child anxiety in pediatric settings
- Nurses report positive outcomes from integrating puppets into patient care, facilitating easier discussions about medical procedures
- Implementation challenges exist due to the structured nature of hospital environments conflicting with the fluid use of educational puppets

## Open Questions

- What are the most effective training methods for healthcare professionals to integrate puppet education into their practice?
