## Creativity Enhancement

The sources discuss various strategies for enhancing creativity under different constraints and approaches. Source 1 highlights the impact of financial constraints on creativity, emphasizing the use of top-down processing strategies to develop cost-effective solutions and the limitations posed by limited budgets. In contrast, Source 2 delves into the psychological aspects of creativity, focusing on the Default Mode Network and the practice of journaling as a means to stimulate creative thinking. Both sources underscore the importance of adapting creative processes to environmental and personal constraints, yet they differ in their emphasis on practical (Source 1) versus theoretical (Source 2) methodologies for creativity enhancement. There is a clear agreement on the significance of context-specific strategies for maximizing creative output, but there is a notable gap in how financial limitations specifically influence and interact with psychological factors that support creative processes.

## Key Findings

- Adopting top-down processing strategies can help mitigate financial constraints on creativity.
- Journaling and the Default Mode Network play significant roles in fostering creativity.
- Both practical and theoretical approaches are necessary to enhance creativity, though their interaction remains underexplored.

## Open Questions

- How do financial constraints and psychological factors interact to affect the Default Mode Network and overall creative potential?
