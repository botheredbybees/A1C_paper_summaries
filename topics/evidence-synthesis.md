## Music Therapy for Serious Mental Illness

The integration of music therapy in the treatment and management of serious mental illness (SMI) is a burgeoning field with significant potential benefits, as highlighted in the global scoping review 'The Use of Music in the Treatment and Management of Serious Mental Illness'. This review underscores the importance of rigorously designed Randomized Controlled Trials (RCTs) to establish the efficacy of music interventions. However, the review also identifies significant challenges in synthesizing evidence, including methodological inconsistencies and a paucity of high-quality studies. The ROB-ME tool, designed to assess the risk of bias due to missing evidence in systematic reviews, offers a method to mitigate these challenges. By identifying selective non-publication and other biases, the ROB-ME tool can enhance the reliability of evidence synthesis in evaluating music therapy interventions for SMI. This integration of methodological rigor with therapeutic innovation highlights the critical need for robust, systematic approaches in advancing the field of music therapy.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy shows promise in treating serious mental illness but requires further high-quality research.
- Randomized controlled trials are essential for establishing the efficacy of music interventions.
- The ROB-ME tool can help assess and mitigate biases in the synthesis of evidence for music therapy.
