## Creative Ageing

The concept of Creative Ageing emphasizes the role of arts participation in enhancing the quality of life for older adults. Both sources highlight the importance of tailored programs and facilitation techniques that cater to diverse populations, including South Korean communities (Source 1) and male participants who are often marginalized within creative ageing initiatives (Source 2). While Source 1 underscores the community well-being benefits derived from active engagement in arts activities, Source 2 points out significant gender disparities within existing programmes. These sources collectively advocate for a more inclusive approach to Creative Ageing that addresses gaps and fosters equal opportunities for all.

## Key Findings

- Creative ageing initiatives can significantly enhance community well-being through arts participation
- There is a need for targeted approaches to engage marginalized groups such as men in creative ageing programs

## Open Questions

- How can existing creative ageing programs be adapted to better serve male participants and close gender disparities?
