## Adults with Mental Health Conditions

This synthesis focuses on the mental health and wellbeing of adults with mental health conditions. Source 1 examines the role of community choir singing in improving these outcomes. It highlights that participation in group singing can lead to enhanced mental health, contributing to a sense of community and emotional support among participants. In contrast, Source 2 discusses how exposure to nature can positively impact individuals suffering from depression through restorative environments that enhance mood and cognitive functions. While both sources suggest interventions beneficial for mental health, they differ in their approaches: music therapy versus environmental immersion.

## Key Findings

- Adults with mental health conditions benefit significantly from group singing, experiencing improved mental health outcomes.
- Nature exposure offers therapeutic benefits by reducing depression symptoms through restorative environments.

## Open Questions

- What are the comparative efficacy and access implications of community choir singing versus nature-based interventions for adults with mental health conditions?
