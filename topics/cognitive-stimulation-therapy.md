## Cognitive Stimulation Therapy

Cognitive stimulation therapy (CST) is a non-pharmacological intervention designed to enhance cognitive function and quality of life for individuals with dementia. Sources highlight that CST involves activities aimed at stimulating mental processes such as memory, language, problem-solving skills, and orientation. Personalized care plans are crucial in tailoring the therapeutic approach to individual needs. The therapy is often associated with person-centered care, focusing on improving communication skills and emotional well-being among patients. While sources agree on the efficacy of CST for managing behavioral and psychological symptoms of dementia (BPSD), there remains a need for further exploration into the specific mechanisms through which CST operates and its long-term effects on cognitive function.

## Key Findings

- Cognitive stimulation therapy is an effective non-pharmacological intervention in dementia care.
- Personalized care plans are essential for tailoring CST to individual patient needs.
- Person-centered care is integral to the success of CST interventions.

## Open Questions

- What specific mechanisms underlie the efficacy of cognitive stimulation therapy?
