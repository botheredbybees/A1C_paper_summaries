## Visual Arts in Health and Wellbeing

The integration of visual arts into health settings emphasizes the transformative power of creativity on patient care. Both sources highlight how close observation skills and artistic engagement can lead to significant improvements in the wellbeing of patients, particularly through activities that involve creative transformation and reflective practice. The use of art as a therapeutic tool also addresses broader themes like disability iconography, illustrating how visual arts interventions not only improve individual health outcomes but also contribute to societal understandings of healthcare and wellbeing.

## Key Findings

- Visual arts can enhance patient care through improved observational skills
- Artistic engagement aids in the creative transformation of personal narratives among patients with disabilities
- Interventions using visual arts address both individual and societal health needs

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term impacts of visual arts interventions on patient health outcomes?
