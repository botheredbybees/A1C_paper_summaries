## Expressive Writing Paradigm

The expressive writing paradigm is a practice centered around the therapeutic benefits derived from putting personal thoughts and feelings into words. Originating as an intervention aimed at improving mental health, it has evolved to encompass developmental creative writing models which aim to foster personal growth through various forms of expression beyond just catharsis. Sources highlight its efficacy in managing stress-related illnesses and enhancing physical health outcomes. However, there is a need for further research exploring the nuances between different modes of expressive writing and their specific impacts on diverse populations.

## Key Findings

- Expressive writing has been shown to improve mental health by facilitating emotional processing.
- Developmental creative writing models expand on traditional expressive writing practices.
- There are significant physical health benefits associated with engaging in regular expressive writing activities.

## Open Questions

- How do different forms of creative expression within the paradigm uniquely impact psychological and physiological well-being?
