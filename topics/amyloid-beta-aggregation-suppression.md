## Amyloid-Beta Aggregation Suppression

The exploration of natural products as therapeutic agents against Alzheimer's disease reveals their significant potential in suppressing amyloid-beta aggregation and mitigating oxidative stress, which are key pathogenic mechanisms in the disease. Across various studies, both in vitro and in vivo models underscore the neuroprotective effects of these compounds. However, while there is agreement on the beneficial effects of natural products, questions remain about their efficacy and safety in long-term clinical use.

## Key Findings

- Natural products have shown significant potential in suppressing amyloid-beta aggregation
- These compounds mitigate oxidative stress associated with Alzheimer's disease
- In vitro and in vivo studies indicate neuroprotective benefits from these interventions

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term efficacy and safety profiles of natural product-based therapies for Alzheimer's disease?
