## Creativity in Schools

The integration of creativity and arts into school curricula is increasingly recognized for its potential to enhance mental health, foster critical thinking skills, and promote cultural understanding. Sources highlight the importance of creative activities as a means to improve overall wellbeing among students, going beyond traditional educational goals such as academic performance. Both sources emphasize that universal access to arts programs can help mitigate mental health issues prevalent in youth populations today. Additionally, they underscore the role of creativity not just for personal development but also in building cultural competence and critical thinking abilities which are crucial for navigating a globalized world.

## Key Findings

- Creative activities positively impact students' mental health
- Arts programs contribute to developing critical thinking skills
- Universal access to arts is essential for comprehensive education
