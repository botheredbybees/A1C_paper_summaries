## Epigenetics

The study of human development and the factors that influence changes over a lifetime have long been a focus in psychology. Epigenetics emerges as a critical aspect within this field, offering insights into how environmental influences can modify gene expression without altering the underlying DNA sequence, impacting developmental trajectories. This theory suggests that both genetic predispositions and life experiences contribute to human development. Sources highlight the interplay between nature (genetic inheritance) and nurture (environmental factors), emphasizing epigenetics as a mediator in this relationship. While cognitive-development-theory primarily focuses on intellectual growth processes, epigenetics broadens our understanding by integrating biological mechanisms with psychological theories of change.

## Key Findings

- Epigenetics provides a framework for understanding how environmental influences can alter gene expression without changing DNA sequences.
- The theory highlights the interplay between genetic inheritance and life experiences in human development.
- There is an integration of cognitive-development-theory and epigenetic mechanisms to provide a comprehensive view of developmental changes.

## Open Questions

- How do specific environmental factors trigger epigenetic modifications, and what are their long-term effects on human development?
