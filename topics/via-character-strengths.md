## VIA Character Strengths

The VIA Character Strengths theory offers a framework for identifying and cultivating positive traits in individuals. Both sources emphasize the importance of core virtues and personal growth through a strengths-based approach. They highlight that VIA character strengths are central to positive psychology, focusing on resilience and the identification of personal virtues to enhance well-being. While there is overlap in the conceptual framework and application, the specific ways to use these strengths are elaborated in Source 1, offering practical strategies for personal and professional development.

## Key Findings

- VIA character strengths provide a comprehensive framework for understanding and enhancing positive traits.
- A strengths-based approach is central to positive psychology and personal growth.
- The application of VIA character strengths can enhance resilience and overall well-being.
