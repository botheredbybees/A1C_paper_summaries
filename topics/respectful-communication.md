## Respectful Communication

Both sources emphasize the importance of respectful and person-centered language in improving interactions with individuals affected by dementia. The first source provides guidelines for using dementia-friendly language and imagery, focusing on reducing stigma and enhancing dignity through communication that acknowledges the person's identity beyond their condition. Similarly, the second source outlines specific language guidelines aimed at promoting a more person-centered approach to care. Both sources highlight how respectful communication can improve quality of life and affirm the human rights of individuals with dementia by fostering empathy and understanding among caregivers and the broader community.

## Key Findings

- Respectful communication reduces stigma and improves dignity for people with dementia
- Person-centered language guidelines enhance quality of care
- Language plays a critical role in upholding the human rights of those with dementia
