## Socioeconomic Disparities

The interplay between socioeconomic disparities and outcomes such as transformative learning and mental health is a recurring theme in the provided sources. Source 1 emphasizes how interdisciplinary approaches can foster social justice through education, industry, and community arts perspectives, focusing on transformative learning outcomes that seek to address economic inequalities. In contrast, Source 2 explores how the arts interface with the broader determinants of health, specifically highlighting mental health outcomes among populations affected by socioeconomic disparities. Both sources underscore the significant impact of socioeconomic status on educational and healthcare experiences, suggesting that interdisciplinary efforts are necessary to combat these inequities. The sources highlight the necessity for comprehensive strategies involving multiple sectors and disciplines to mitigate the negative impacts associated with economic inequalities.

## Key Findings

- Interdisciplinary approaches can enhance social justice initiatives aimed at addressing socioeconomic disparities.
- Socioeconomic status significantly influences both educational and health outcomes, particularly in mental health.
- The arts play a critical role in enhancing learning experiences and supporting mental health across various communities.

## Open Questions

- What are the most effective interdisciplinary methods for reducing socioeconomic disparities in education and healthcare?
