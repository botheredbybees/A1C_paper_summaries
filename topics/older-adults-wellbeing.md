## Wellbeing Interventions for Older Adults

The synthesis of sources on wellbeing interventions for older adults highlights the importance of creative and engaging activities to enhance both physical and emotional health. Source one, focusing on ballet as a motivator for adult participation in creative health initiatives, underscores how structured yet enjoyable activities can improve overall wellbeing outcomes among older populations. Conversely, source two provides an extensive systematic review of laughter and humor interventions, indicating their significant impact on enhancing well-being in the same demographic. However, while both sources acknowledge positive impacts, they also reveal methodological challenges and limitations that must be addressed to strengthen the validity and applicability of these intervention strategies.

## Key Findings

- Ballet participation can serve as a motivational tool for adults seeking creative health activities.
- Laughter and humor interventions are effective in improving well-being among older adults.
- Interventions such as ballet and laughter sessions face methodological limitations that affect their efficacy evaluation.

## Open Questions

- What are the most effective methods to overcome methodological challenges in evaluating creative health interventions for older populations?
