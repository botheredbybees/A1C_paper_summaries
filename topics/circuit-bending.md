## Circuit Bending

The concept of circuit bending involves repurposing and reconfiguring electronic devices to create unique and unconventional musical instruments. This intervention emphasizes collaboration between humans and nonhuman entities, as seen in the use of discarded electronics that are creatively repurposed through human ingenuity. The practice highlights technological fluency, where individuals extend their technical knowledge beyond conventional applications into artistic expression and experimentation with sound art. Circuit bending is not just a physical act but also embodies rhetorical elements where malfunction becomes an aesthetic choice rather than a failure to be repaired. This blurs the lines between intentional design and accidental discovery, challenging traditional notions of technology use and creativity.

## Key Findings

- Circuit bending involves repurposing electronic devices into musical instruments through creative reconfiguration
- The practice emphasizes collaboration between humans and nonhuman entities in technological fluency contexts
- Circuit bending challenges conventional views on technological failure by treating malfunction as a form of aesthetic expression

## Open Questions

- How does circuit bending influence broader perceptions of technology and creativity outside specialized circles?
