## Musicians' Health and Performance

The health and performance concerns of musicians span a range of issues, including temporomandibular disorders (TMDs), performance anxiety, and musculoskeletal disorders related to posture. A review of TMDs among musicians highlights the prevalence of these conditions, often exacerbated by playing habits. Performance anxiety, a common psychological challenge, can manifest in different subtypes, affecting various aspects of performance and well-being. Musculoskeletal health is a critical area, with research emphasizing the importance of postural quality and advocating for collaborative efforts between medical professionals and music educators to address these issues comprehensively. While these sources provide insights into specific health concerns, they also suggest a need for interdisciplinary approaches to support musicians' overall health and performance.

## Key Findings

- Temporomandibular disorders are prevalent among musicians and can be exacerbated by playing habits.
- Performance anxiety is a significant psychological issue among musicians, manifesting in multiple subtypes.
- Improving postural quality can significantly impact the musculoskeletal health of musicians.
