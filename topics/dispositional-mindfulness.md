## Dispositional Mindfulness

Dispositional mindfulness, as explored in both sources, is a theoretical construct that describes a stable trait or characteristic of an individual's tendency to be present and attentive to their experiences without judgment. This trait is posited as a significant predictor of emotional resilience and balanced positive emotions. Both sources highlight the relationship between dispositional mindfulness and mental health outcomes, emphasizing its role in stress reduction and emotional regulation. While mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) and cognitive therapy are mentioned in the first source as interventions that can enhance dispositional mindfulness, the second source focuses on the outcomes and benefits associated with this trait, such as increased emotional resilience and the ability to maintain balanced positive emotions. The theoretical framework of dispositional mindfulness thus offers a lens through which to understand the benefits of mindfulness practices beyond the immediate context of intervention.

## Key Findings

- Dispositional mindfulness is a stable trait that predicts emotional resilience and balanced positive emotions.
- Mindfulness-based stress reduction and cognitive therapy can enhance dispositional mindfulness.
- Dispositional mindfulness is associated with better mental health outcomes.

## Open Questions

- How does dispositional mindfulness develop over time, and what are the factors that contribute to its stability or change?
