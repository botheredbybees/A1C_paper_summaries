## Student Volunteers in Dementia Care Programs

The utilization of student volunteers in dementia care programs demonstrates significant potential for enhancing resident engagement and overall quality of life. The Wisconsin Music & Memory Program, a personalized music therapy initiative involving student volunteers, showcases the positive impact of intergenerational programming on individuals with dementia. This program emphasizes the use of tailored music to evoke personal memories and improve emotional well-being. Similarly, an implementation survey of Phase III highlights how student volunteer programs can effectively contribute to resident engagement and potentially reduce reliance on medications for managing behavioral symptoms in dementia patients. Both sources indicate that student volunteers play a crucial role in not only providing direct care but also in raising awareness about dementia among younger generations through these intergenerational activities. However, there is a need for further research into the long-term impact of such programs on both volunteer and recipient populations.

## Key Findings

- Student volunteer programs significantly enhance resident engagement in dementia care settings.
- Personalized music therapy improves emotional well-being among individuals with dementia.
- Volunteer programs can contribute to reducing medication use for managing behavioral symptoms.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term benefits of student volunteer programs on both volunteers and residents?
