## Sensory Tours

Sensory tours at the Art Gallery of New South Wales aim to cater to a diverse range of visitors including those with visual impairments, intellectual disabilities, and dementia. These tours emphasize inclusive practices to ensure all individuals have access to the art, often through tactile experiences that allow visitors to touch replicas or models of artworks. Two sources highlight the gallery's commitment to accessibility and inclusive design, though they differ slightly in the specific groups targeted, with one focusing more on intellectual disabilities and the other on dementia care. Both sources acknowledge the importance of visual impairment support and the implementation of tactile tours to enhance the experience for these audiences. While the core intervention of sensory tours is consistent, the emphasis on particular groups indicates a nuanced approach to accessibility based on specific needs.

## Key Findings

- Sensory tours offer tactile experiences to enhance accessibility for visitors with disabilities.
- The Art Gallery of New South Wales implements inclusive practices to cater to a diverse audience.
- Tactile tours are a key component in making art accessible to visually impaired individuals.
