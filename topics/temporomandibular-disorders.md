## Temporomandibular Disorders

Temporomandibular disorders (TMD) represent a complex set of conditions affecting the jaw and its surrounding muscles. Sources highlight that TMD is particularly prevalent among musicians, where repetitive motion and high physical demands can exacerbate the condition. The biopsychosocial model is integral in understanding TMD among vocal performers, such as singers, suggesting a multifaceted approach that includes biological, psychological, and social factors. This model underscores the importance of comprehensive assessment and treatment strategies, acknowledging the interplay between pain, function, and social context. Integrating the biopsychosocial model offers a holistic approach to managing TMD, particularly in populations where physical and emotional stress can significantly impact jaw and muscle health.

## Key Findings

- TMD is highly prevalent among musicians due to the physical demands of their profession.
- The biopsychosocial model is crucial for understanding and managing TMD, especially in populations with high physical and emotional stress.
- Interventions for TMD should address multiple dimensions including biological, psychological, and social factors.

## Open Questions

- How can the biopsychosocial model be effectively integrated into clinical practice for TMD management among musicians and vocal performers?
