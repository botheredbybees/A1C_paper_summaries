## Economic Impact of Arts-Based Interventions

The synthesis of the economic impact of arts-based interventions, particularly in the context of Alzheimer's disease and broader societal contexts, reveals a dual benefit framework. Both sources highlight intrinsic values such as quality of life improvement (Source 1) and extrinsic benefits including potential healthcare cost savings through arts engagement (Source 1). The review of arts and culture's value to society underscores that these activities provide significant intrinsic satisfaction and personal growth while also contributing economically through reduced medical expenses and enhanced community involvement. However, there is a tension between quantifying the economic benefits of unquantifiable intrinsic values like emotional fulfillment derived from art participation.

## Key Findings

- Arts-based interventions improve quality-of-life metrics for individuals with Alzheimer's disease.
- There are potential healthcare cost savings associated with engaging in arts activities among those with chronic conditions.
- The value proposition of arts and culture includes both intrinsic (personal fulfillment) and extrinsic (economic) benefits.

## Open Questions

- How can the economic impact of arts engagement be accurately measured without diminishing its intrinsic personal value?
