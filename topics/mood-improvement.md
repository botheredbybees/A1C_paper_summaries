## Mood Improvement Interventions

Both sources discuss interventions aimed at improving mood in a clinical setting. Source 1 examines the effectiveness of art therapy in enhancing mood and reducing anxiety and pain among hospitalized patients, emphasizing its bedside application during acute treatment phases. In contrast, Source 2 explores the impact of clown therapy (laughing therapy) on emotional state and hostility levels in adult hospital patients. Both interventions aim to provide psychological support by engaging patients emotionally through creative or humorous means. While art therapy focuses more broadly on mood enhancement along with pain and anxiety reduction, clown therapy specifically targets reducing patient hostility and improving their emotional states.

## Key Findings

- Mood improvement is facilitated through both art therapy and laughing therapy.
- Art therapy can reduce anxiety and manage pain alongside enhancing mood.
- Clown therapy reduces hostility levels in adult patients.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term effects of using humor-based interventions like clown therapy compared to creative therapies such as art therapy?
