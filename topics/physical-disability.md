## Physical Disability: Adaptation and Rehabilitation

The integration of various forms of therapy and technology to assist individuals with physical disabilities reveals a multifaceted approach to rehabilitation and adaptation. Both sources highlight the importance of creative and technological interventions in improving quality of life. Source 1 discusses the use of arts-based therapy as a means to address phantom limb syndrome, emphasizing the role of creativity in rehabilitation. This approach suggests that therapeutic interventions can extend beyond conventional physical and occupational therapies, incorporating psychological and emotional well-being. Source 2 introduces eye-gaze technology as a means for individuals with severe physical disabilities to communicate and engage in creative activities, such as painting and music. This technology not only aids in social connectivity but also offers a platform for self-expression and artistic pursuit, further reinforcing the role of creativity in rehabilitation. The integration of technology and arts-based therapies underscores the potential for innovative solutions to enhance the lives of those with physical disabilities.

## Key Findings

- Creative therapies and technology can significantly improve the quality of life for individuals with physical disabilities.
- Eye-gaze technology enables social connectivity and creative expression for individuals with severe physical disabilities.
- Arts-based therapy can effectively address psychological and emotional challenges, such as phantom limb syndrome, in rehabilitation.

## Open Questions

- How can the integration of eye-gaze technology and arts-based therapy be optimized to provide comprehensive rehabilitation solutions for individuals with a range of physical disabilities?
