## Quality-of-life Enhancement

The integration of creative story therapy and personalized music interventions presents a comprehensive approach to enhancing the quality of life for elderly individuals, particularly those with dementia. Creative story therapy has shown promise in improving cognitive function and overall well-being by engaging patients in storytelling activities that stimulate memory recall and emotional connections. Similarly, the use of iPods loaded with personalized music playlists not only improves mental health but also enhances daily living experiences through tailored auditory stimuli that trigger positive memories and emotions. Both interventions highlight the importance of individualized care strategies in addressing diverse needs within elderly populations.

## Key Findings

- Creative story therapy can improve cognitive function and enhance quality of life for individuals with dementia.
- Personalized music playlists significantly contribute to mental health improvement and overall well-being among seniors.
- Individualized interventions such as creative storytelling and tailored music experiences positively impact memory recall and emotional engagement.

## Open Questions

- How do the long-term effects of these interventions compare in terms of sustaining cognitive function and quality-of-life improvements?
