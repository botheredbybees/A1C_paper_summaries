## Musical Empathy

Both sources emphasize the concept of musical empathy as a critical aspect of human interaction through music. The first source discusses how music can induce emotional contagion, where listeners experience and sometimes share emotions expressed in the music, creating a form of empathetic response. This emotional contagion is facilitated by earworms—repetitive tunes that stick in one's mind—and are shown to influence mood and behavior, potentially enhancing or triggering empathetic connections with others experiencing similar auditory loops. The second source expands on this idea by introducing absorption as an additional factor that deepens musical empathy. Absorption refers to the immersive engagement a listener experiences when deeply involved with music, which can amplify emotional reactions and personal associations tied to specific tunes or soundscapes. This heightened involvement can lead to deeper empathetic understanding and shared emotional states among individuals who are absorbed in the same musical piece.

## Key Findings

- Earworms contribute to emotional contagion through repetitive auditory loops.
- Absorption in music enhances the depth of empathy experienced between listeners.
- Musical empathy involves both emotional contagion and immersive engagement with music.

## Open Questions
