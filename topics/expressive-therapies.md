## Expressive Therapies

The integration of expressive therapies across various modalities such as mask-making and photography offers a dynamic approach to therapeutic expression. Both sources highlight the use of creative processes in addressing psychological needs, with mask therapy focusing on the physical creation and wearing of masks to explore identity and resilience (Source 1), while PhotoTherapy leverages visual imagery for emotional exploration and healing (Source 2). The common thread between these methods is their ability to facilitate self-reflection and emotional processing through artistic expression. However, there are differences in how each modality engages with the therapeutic process, with mask therapy emphasizing continuity and resilience through physical engagement, whereas PhotoTherapy focuses on capturing and reflecting upon personal experiences through photography.

## Key Findings

- Expressive therapies use creative processes to facilitate emotional healing and self-discovery.
- Mask therapy emphasizes continuity and resilience by engaging in the creation of masks that reflect one's identity.
- PhotoTherapy leverages photography as a means for capturing and reflecting on personal experiences.

## Open Questions
