## Musical Behavior

The study of musical behavior encompasses the influence of various factors such as age, sex, and cultural expectations, alongside the broader social and applied psychological contexts. Source 1 highlights significant differences in musical behavior between males and females in secondary schools, suggesting that gender stereotypes and cultural expectations play a substantial role in shaping how students engage with and perceive music. This source also underscores the importance of considering developmental stages and age-specific patterns when examining musical behaviors. In contrast, Source 2 presents a comprehensive view of the social and applied psychology of music, focusing on the interplay between music and various social contexts. This source employs interdisciplinary approaches and naturalistic methodologies to explore music consumption and musical behavior, highlighting the dynamic and multifaceted nature of how individuals interact with music. Both sources converge on the idea that musical behavior is heavily influenced by social and cultural factors, while diverging on the specific methodologies and scopes of investigation.

## Key Findings

- Musical behavior varies significantly by gender and age within secondary school populations.
- Gender stereotypes and cultural expectations significantly influence musical interests and attitudes.
- Interdisciplinary approaches and naturalistic methodologies provide deeper insights into the social and applied psychology of music.

## Open Questions

- How do age-specific patterns of musical behavior differ across various cultural contexts?
