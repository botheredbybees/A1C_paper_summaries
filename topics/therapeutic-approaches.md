## Music Therapy

The application of personalized music playlists as a therapeutic tool in elderly care, specifically for individuals with dementia, showcases the effectiveness of tailored interventions to enhance quality of life and reawaken emotions (Source 1). The concept aligns with broader principles of creativity in practice which emphasize the use of innovative approaches to improve mental health and well-being. Although Source 2 does not provide specific examples related to music therapy, its endorsement of creative application supports the utility of personalized musical interventions as a part of an integrative care strategy (Source 1).

## Key Findings

- Personalized music playlists can enhance quality of life for elderly individuals with dementia.
- Music-based therapies are effective in reawakening emotions and improving mental health.
- Creative approaches, such as tailored musical interventions, contribute to comprehensive well-being strategies.

## Open Questions

- How can the principles of personalized music therapy be extended to other patient populations beyond those with dementia?
