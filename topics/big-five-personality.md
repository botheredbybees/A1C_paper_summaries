## Big Five Personality and Musical Taste

Research on the relationship between personality traits as measured by the Big Five model and musical taste highlights consistent patterns across studies. The trait of openness to experience consistently emerges as a strong predictor of an individual's preference for various types of music, including more complex genres. Extraversion also plays a role in how individuals use music for emotional regulation or social purposes. However, there are limitations in using genre labels alone to capture the nuanced relationship between personality and musical preferences, suggesting that other factors such as individual differences in listening behavior need consideration. Additionally, gender variations influence certain aspects of this relationship.

## Key Findings

- Openness to experience strongly correlates with a preference for complex and diverse music genres.
- Extraversion influences the use of music for social interactions and emotional regulation.
- Limitations in genre labels suggest that individual differences in listening behavior also impact musical preferences.

## Open Questions

- How do other personality traits beyond openness to experience and extraversion influence musical taste?
