## Creative Therapies in Child Rehabilitation

The integration of creative and unconventional therapies such as puppetry and clowning into child rehabilitation settings highlights the importance of engaging children through playful and imaginative methods. Puppetry serves as a motivational tool, enhancing physical engagement while simultaneously boosting self-esteem. In contrast, clowning leverages humor to reduce stress, employing foolishness as an expression of bravery within a relational model of care that emphasizes connection over medical intervention. Both approaches underscore the efficacy of non-traditional therapeutic techniques in improving children's emotional and physical well-being during rehabilitation.

## Key Findings

- Puppetry enhances physical engagement and self-esteem building among children in rehabilitation settings.
- Clowning utilizes humor to reduce stress and foster a sense of bravery through foolhardy behavior.
- Both puppetry and clowning therapies emphasize the importance of relational models over conventional medical approaches.

## Open Questions

- How can creative therapeutic methods be effectively integrated into standardized rehabilitation protocols without undermining traditional care practices?
