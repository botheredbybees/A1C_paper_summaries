## Adult Education Through Creative Arts

The integration of creative arts in adult education, as exemplified by ballet and community-based learning programs, highlights the multifaceted benefits these interventions offer. Source 1 discusses how ballet participation among adults is motivated not only by physical health considerations but also by a desire for emotional wellbeing and personal growth through artistic expression. Similarly, Source 2 underscores the mental health benefits of creative arts activities within adult communities, suggesting that such programs foster intrinsic educational value and social connectivity. Both sources converge on the notion that engaging in creative arts can significantly enhance the quality of life for adults by addressing both psychological and physical needs. However, there is a gap regarding how these interventions are effectively scaled and sustained within diverse community settings.

## Key Findings

- Adults engage in ballet and other creative arts to improve their mental and emotional health.
- Creative arts programs promote intrinsic educational value and social connection among adults.
- Participation in such activities can contribute positively to both physical and psychological wellbeing.

## Open Questions

- What are the barriers and facilitators for scaling and sustaining adult education through creative arts in different community contexts?
