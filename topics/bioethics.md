## Bioethics in Medical Practice and Enhancement

The sources delve into the complex ethical frameworks that underpin medical practices and genetic enhancements, highlighting the importance of informed consent and the ethical dilemmas associated with paternalism and societal pressures. Source 1 emphasizes the ritual performance and Confucian philosophy in the context of bioethics, focusing on the importance of informed consent and the moral responsibility of healthcare providers to respect patient autonomy. Conversely, Source 2 addresses genetic enhancement and parental autonomy, arguing that societal pressures and moral philosophy significantly influence decisions about genetic modification. Both sources underscore the need for a balance between individual rights and societal norms, as well as the ethical challenges posed by emerging technologies. The integration of Confucian ethics and moral philosophy highlights the cultural and philosophical dimensions of bioethics, which are crucial for understanding and addressing contemporary ethical issues in medicine.

## Key Findings

- Informed consent is a critical component of ethical medical practice and genetic enhancement.
- Societal pressures and moral philosophy significantly influence decisions regarding genetic enhancement.
- Confucian philosophy and moral philosophy provide important frameworks for understanding and addressing ethical dilemmas in bioethics.

## Open Questions

- How can the principles of Confucian philosophy and moral philosophy be effectively integrated into modern bioethical frameworks to address the ethical challenges posed by emerging medical technologies?
