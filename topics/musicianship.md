## Musicianship

The study of musicianship encompasses the various challenges and experiences unique to this professional group. Two key studies highlight different aspects of musicianship: performance anxiety and hearing health. The first study investigates the prevalence of performance anxiety among musicians, revealing significant career impacts and gender-based differences. This anxiety, often exacerbated by high-pressure environments, can severely affect a musician's professional life and mental health. The second study delves into the auditory mechanics and hearing loss issues common among musicians, such as sensorineural and conductive hearing loss. These conditions are often a result of prolonged exposure to loud music and lack of proper hearing protection. Both studies underscore the multifaceted nature of musicianship, encompassing both psychological and physiological dimensions. Despite these studies, there is a clear gap in understanding how these two critical aspects—performance anxiety and hearing loss—interact and compound each other's effects on the overall health and career of musicians.

## Key Findings

- Musicians experience significant performance anxiety that impacts their career and mental health.
- There are gender differences in the occurrence and manifestation of performance anxiety among musicians.
- Musicians are at high risk for various types of hearing loss due to occupational hazards.

## Open Questions

- How do performance anxiety and hearing loss interact and affect the overall well-being and career trajectory of musicians?
