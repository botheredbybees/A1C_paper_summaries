## Creative Therapies in Education

The integration of creative therapies in educational settings highlights their potential to enhance quality of life and foster critical evaluation methods among participants, as seen in the lecture notes for 'The Arts and Dementia Care'. This intervention is further elucidated through empirical investigations into creativity and flow in musical composition, applying Csikszentmihalyi's theory to understand the psychological processes involved in creative endeavors. Both sources underscore the importance of engaging individuals in activities that not only stimulate creativity but also promote a state of 'flow', which is essential for deep engagement and personal satisfaction. However, there is a noted gap in the application of these theories and methods to broader educational contexts beyond specific therapeutic settings or artistic practices.

## Key Findings

- Creative therapies can improve quality of life for individuals in therapeutic settings
- Csikszentmihalyi's theory of flow is integral to understanding the psychological benefits of creative activities
- Creative therapies foster critical evaluation skills in participants

## Open Questions

- How can the principles of creative therapies and flow be effectively integrated into broader educational curricula?
