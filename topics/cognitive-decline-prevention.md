## Cognitive Decline Prevention

The exploration of interventions aimed at preventing or mitigating cognitive decline among aging populations highlights the importance of mental wellbeing and creative activities in maintaining cognitive function. Source 1 introduces the concept of 'creative process journaling' as a method to enhance mental well-being, suggesting that engaging in reflective and imaginative practices can support brain health by promoting neuroplasticity and reducing stress-related hormones linked to cognitive decline. Conversely, Source 2 delves into the role of musical memory in preserving cognitive abilities through emotional engagement with music. The study finds that individuals retain a strong connection to their musical memories even as other forms of cognition may deteriorate over time. This highlights how emotionally laden experiences can act as powerful stimuli for maintaining cognitive faculties in later life, reinforcing the notion that targeted interventions involving creative and affective engagement might be particularly beneficial.

## Key Findings

- Creative process journaling supports mental well-being by enhancing neuroplasticity.
- Musical memories remain robust despite age-related cognitive decline.
- Emotional engagement through music can serve as a protective factor against cognitive impairment.

## Open Questions

- How do different forms of creative and emotional engagement compare in their effectiveness for preventing cognitive decline?
