## Music Therapy

Music therapy has evolved as a significant psychological intervention in mental health settings, emphasizing the therapeutic relationship and clinical objectives tailored to diverse service users. The historical overview underscores the importance of establishing a therapeutic relationship through music, which is central to achieving clinical objectives such as reducing symptoms of mental health disorders and enhancing overall quality of life. Meanwhile, the focus on diverse service users highlights the necessity of cultural humility and acculturation in music therapy practices. This integration of cultural sensitivity is crucial for ensuring that music therapy effectively addresses the unique needs of individuals from various cultural backgrounds. However, while music therapy shows promise, its effectiveness in specific cultural contexts remains an area of exploration.

## Key Findings

- Music therapy emphasizes the therapeutic relationship as a key component of clinical success.
- Cultural humility and acculturation are essential for music therapy to be effective with diverse service users.
- Music therapy aims to improve mental health outcomes through tailored clinical objectives.

## Open Questions

- How can music therapy be adapted to be culturally appropriate for all service users, especially those with distinct cultural backgrounds?
