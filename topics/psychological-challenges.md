## Psychological Challenges in Trauma Recovery

The sources highlight psychological challenges faced by individuals recovering from trauma, particularly in the context of burn injuries and emotional distress. Source 1 emphasizes the importance of a multidisciplinary approach in treating burn injuries, which includes addressing post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) through cognitive-behavior therapy (CBT). This therapeutic approach aims to modify dysfunctional thoughts and behaviors associated with trauma. Source 2 introduces puppet-assisted play therapy as a tool for emotional and psychological challenges, focusing on the use of puppetry techniques to build trust and facilitate emotional expression in children and adults. Both sources underscore the need for tailored interventions that address the unique psychological needs of trauma victims, advocating for the integration of various therapeutic modalities to enhance recovery outcomes.

## Key Findings

- Multidisciplinary approaches are essential in treating psychological challenges associated with trauma recovery.
- Cognitive-behavior therapy is effective in managing post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) in burn injury survivors.
- Play therapy, including puppet-assisted techniques, aids in emotional expression and trust building for trauma victims.
