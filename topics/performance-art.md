## Performance Art

Performance art is a diverse and multi-faceted practice that challenges traditional notions of art and performance. Source 1 discusses the process of creating a full-length stand-up show, emphasizing the importance of 'know-what' and 'tacit knowledge' in the creation of performance art. This highlights the necessity for performers to not only understand the content but also the tacit skills and knowledge required to effectively deliver a performance. In contrast, Source 2 introduces Lisa Bufano's work which focuses on the body as a medium of expression. Bufano's performance art explores themes of disability and body identity, using prosthetics and adapted stilts to challenge societal perceptions. This source underscores the role of the body in performance art, particularly in the context of disability and the creation of new body identities. While both sources highlight the importance of the performer and their body, they differ in their focus, with the first emphasizing the cognitive and intellectual aspects of performance creation, and the second focusing on the physical and embodied aspects of performance.

## Key Findings

- Performance art is a complex and multifaceted practice that incorporates both cognitive and physical elements.
- The creation of performance art requires an understanding of both explicit and tacit knowledge.
- Disability and body identity are central themes in contemporary performance art.

## Open Questions

- How can the cognitive and physical aspects of performance art be better integrated to create more compelling and meaningful works?
