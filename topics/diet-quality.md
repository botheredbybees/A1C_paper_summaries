## Diet Quality

The quality of one's diet is crucial for both physical and mental health. Two prominent approaches to improving diet quality are the 'Eat food' principle, which encourages consuming minimally processed foods as recommended by the NOVA classification system, and dietary interventions based on Mediterranean diet principles that emphasize plant-based eating, whole grains, healthy fats, and seafood while minimizing red meat and sweets. These interventions also highlight the importance of mindful awareness in the context of nutritional science to enhance consumption patterns positively. While both approaches aim at enhancing health outcomes through better nutrition, there is a need for more research on how these principles can be effectively integrated into everyday life, considering cultural and economic barriers.

## Key Findings

- The NOVA classification system advocates against ultra-processed foods for better dietary quality.
- Mediterranean diet principles focus on plant-based eating, whole grains, healthy fats, and seafood to improve health outcomes.
- Mindful awareness is integral in enhancing the effectiveness of dietary interventions.

## Open Questions

- How can cultural and economic factors be addressed to ensure widespread adoption of dietary interventions?
