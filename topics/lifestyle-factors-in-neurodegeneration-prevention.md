## Lifestyle Factors in Neurodegeneration Prevention

The review of lifestyle factors influencing neurodegeneration prevention highlights the importance of autophagy-mediated pathways as a mechanism for intervention. Source 1 emphasizes that diet, physical activity, and sleep can regulate these pathways critical to preventing protein aggregation associated with Alzheimer's disease. In contrast, Source 2 broadens the scope by incorporating dietary interventions, exercise benefits, and mindfulness practices within a comprehensive lifestyle medicine framework aimed at healthy cognitive aging. Both sources underscore non-pharmacological strategies as essential for mitigating neurodegeneration risks through enhancing brain-specific autophagy markers and overall cognitive health.

## Key Findings

- Dietary interventions are pivotal in regulating pathways that prevent protein aggregation linked to Alzheimer's disease.
- Physical activity and mindfulness practices contribute significantly to maintaining healthy cognitive aging, alongside dietary considerations.
- Non-pharmacological strategies focusing on lifestyle changes can enhance brain-specific autophagy markers and reduce neurodegeneration risks.

## Open Questions

- What are the optimal combinations of diet, physical activity, sleep, and mindfulness for maximizing prevention efficacy against neurodegenerative diseases?
