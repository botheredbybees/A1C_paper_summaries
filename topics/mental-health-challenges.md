## Mental Health Challenges Among Musicians and Homeless Youth

The sources highlight the unique mental health challenges faced by musicians and homeless youth, suggesting that creative arts can play a crucial role in mental health management. Musicians, despite being highly creative individuals, face unpredictable working conditions and may lack adequate support services, potentially leading to mental health issues. In contrast, homeless youth often turn to art creation as a self-care practice, which helps them manage their mental health. Both groups benefit from arts-based interventions, though the nature of support differs due to their distinct social and economic circumstances. The findings underscore the importance of tailored mental health support services for both communities, emphasizing the need for creative arts as an effective therapeutic tool.

## Key Findings

- Creative arts can serve as a significant mental health management tool for both musicians and homeless youth.
- Musicians face unique challenges such as unpredictable working conditions and lack of support services.
- Homeless youth use art creation as a self-care practice to manage mental health.

## Open Questions

- How can tailored mental health support services be effectively developed for musicians and homeless youth?
