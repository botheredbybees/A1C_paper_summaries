## Leisure Activities

The DRAMMA model serves as a foundational framework for understanding the impact of leisure activities on well-being. Both sources highlight that detachment from work and recovery through leisure are crucial components. Autonomy in choosing leisure activities is emphasized, alongside the importance of finding a balance between skills and challenges to induce a flow state. Additionally, meaningfulness and social affiliation within leisure pursuits contribute significantly to overall happiness and mental health. Tensions arise over varying definitions of what constitutes detachment versus engagement in active recovery.

## Key Findings

- The DRAMMA model outlines critical components for leisure's positive impact on well-being.
- Autonomy and mastery are key elements that enhance the enjoyment derived from leisure activities.
- Meaningful and social leisure activities contribute to increased mental health.

## Open Questions

- What constitutes detachment versus active engagement in recovery through leisure?
