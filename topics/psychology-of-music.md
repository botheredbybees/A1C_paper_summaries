## Psychology of Music

The psychology of music explores the interplay between musical structures and human cognition. Sources highlight two complementary aspects: dynamic dualism and influences on musical preferences. Dynamic dualism, as discussed by Kurth and Riemann, emphasizes the tension between harmonic stability and instability within musical compositions, suggesting a balance is crucial for engaging listening experiences. Meanwhile, studies indicate that personal taste in music develops through complex interactions of emotional memories and cognitive processes, underscoring the individualized nature of musical appreciation. Despite these insights, there remains an underexplored area regarding how dynamic dualism specifically shapes or is shaped by the psychological factors contributing to musical preferences.

## Key Findings

- Dynamic dualism explains tension between stability and instability in music.
- Musical preferences are influenced by emotional memories and cognitive processes.

## Open Questions

- How does dynamic dualism interact with personal influences on musical preference?
