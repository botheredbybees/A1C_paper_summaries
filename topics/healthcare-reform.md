## Healthcare Reform Through Interdisciplinary Collaboration

The synthesis of healthcare reform initiatives highlighted in the provided sources underscores the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration and the development of evidence-based practices. Source 1 discusses the creation of a national arts and health policy framework, emphasizing the need for stakeholder involvement to develop policies grounded in evidence-based practices. This source highlights the necessity of engaging various stakeholders to ensure that healthcare reform is inclusive and effective. Source 2 focuses on the role of music therapy within interprofessional teams, advocating for a transdisciplinary approach to patient-centered care. This source emphasizes the importance of collaboration among healthcare professionals to provide holistic care to patients. The sources collectively suggest that healthcare reform should prioritize interdisciplinary collaboration and evidence-based practices to enhance patient care and improve healthcare outcomes.
