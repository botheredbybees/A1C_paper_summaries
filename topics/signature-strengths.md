## Signature Strengths

The concept of signature strengths is a key aspect of positive psychology, focusing on individual strengths rather than weaknesses to enhance personal well-being and life satisfaction. Across the sources, the VIA Classification of Character Strengths is consistently highlighted as the framework for identifying and applying signature strengths. The Positive Activity Model guides the application of signature strengths by emphasizing self-concordant goals—goals that align with one's deepest interests, values, and needs. This model underscores the importance of integrating one's signature strengths into daily activities to foster well-being. Each source also highlights the importance of the VIA Survey in identifying an individual's character strengths and their signature strengths, which are the top five strengths that an individual possesses in abundance. The integration of these strengths into daily activities, through self-concordant goals, is proposed as a pathway to personal well-being and fulfillment.

## Key Findings

- The VIA Classification of Character Strengths is a foundational framework for identifying and applying signature strengths.
- Self-concordant goals, which align with an individual's deepest values and interests, are crucial in the application of signature strengths.
- The VIA Survey is a reliable tool for identifying an individual's character strengths, including their signature strengths.

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term impacts of consistently applying signature strengths in daily activities on overall life satisfaction and well-being?
