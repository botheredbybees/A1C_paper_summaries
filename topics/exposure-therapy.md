## Exposure Therapy

Exposure therapy emerges as a critical intervention for various anxiety disorders, including social phobia and musical performance anxiety. Both sources underscore its efficacy, with Source 1 indicating that exposure-only treatment can be as effective as cognitive-behavioral therapy for social phobia, suggesting its standalone utility. Source 2 highlights the use of exposure therapy in conjunction with cognitive restructuring for addressing musical performance anxiety, indicating its versatility across different anxiety contexts. However, both sources hint at the importance of evidence-based practices and the need for tailored approaches to maximize therapeutic benefits. Despite the robust evidence supporting exposure therapy, the integration of cognitive techniques remains a critical component for enhancing treatment outcomes, suggesting a nuanced approach to therapeutic intervention.

## Key Findings

- Exposure therapy is as effective as cognitive-behavioral therapy for treating social phobia.
- Exposure therapy, combined with cognitive restructuring, is effective for treating musical performance anxiety.
- Evidence-based practices are crucial for the effective application of exposure therapy.

## Open Questions

- How can exposure therapy be optimized when used as a standalone intervention versus in combination with cognitive restructuring?
