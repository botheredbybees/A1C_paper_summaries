## Veterans' Creative Arts Therapy

Creative arts therapy is increasingly recognized as a potent intervention for veterans dealing with post-deployment challenges. Drayton's article underscores the transformative potential of creative arts, such as visual art and music, in fostering recovery, resilience, teamwork, and skill development among veterans. Ramirez's review specifically highlights how art therapy can mitigate symptoms associated with Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) for military service members and veterans. Despite a notable agreement on the efficacy of creative arts interventions, there is an observed gap regarding the long-term benefits and scalability of such therapeutic approaches.

## Key Findings

- Creative arts therapies are effective in aiding recovery from PTSD among veterans
- Arts-based interventions foster resilience and teamwork skills among military personnel
- The integration of creative arts into standard care plans can enhance overall mental health outcomes for veterans

## Open Questions

- What are the long-term efficacy and sustainability of creative arts therapies in veteran populations?
