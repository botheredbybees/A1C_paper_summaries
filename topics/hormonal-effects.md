## Hormonal Effects on Development and Behavior

Both sources delve into the complex interplay between hormonal systems and behavioral outcomes, but they focus on different stages of development. Source 1 explores the impact of hormonal systems, particularly through the lens of psychoneuroendocrinology, on the effects of music and health. This source highlights the multifaceted nature of how hormonal changes can influence various aspects of an individual's well-being, including psychological and physiological responses to music. In contrast, Source 2 centers on the organizational effects of pubertal hormones on the adolescent brain and behavior, emphasizing the critical role of these hormonal changes in shaping neural architecture and behavior patterns during adolescence. Despite the differences in developmental stages, both sources underscore the profound influence of hormonal changes on behavioral and psychological health, suggesting a broader framework for understanding the interplay between hormonal systems and human development across different contexts and stages of life.

## Key Findings

- Hormonal changes significantly influence psychological and behavioral responses.
- Pubertal hormones play a critical role in organizing the adolescent brain and behavior.
- Music can have significant health impacts through psychoneuroendocrinological mechanisms.

## Open Questions

- How do hormonal changes during different developmental stages interact to influence long-term health and behavior outcomes?
