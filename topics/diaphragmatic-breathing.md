## Diaphragmatic Breathing

The concept of diaphragmatic breathing is explored in the provided sources as a technique used to enhance vocal performance and improve physical well-being. Diaphragmatic breathing involves using the diaphragm muscle, located below the lungs, to draw breath deeper into the chest, thereby enhancing lung capacity and potentially improving overall respiratory efficiency. This method is often recommended for individuals looking to improve their singing abilities or manage certain medical conditions related to respiration. Across sources, there's an agreement on its efficacy in increasing lung capacity and supporting vocal health.

## Key Findings

- Diaphragmatic breathing increases lung capacity.
- It is beneficial for improving vocal performance.
- The technique is also used to address respiratory health issues.

## Open Questions

- Further research is needed on the long-term effects of diaphragmatic breathing on chronic respiratory conditions.
