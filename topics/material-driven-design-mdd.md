## Material-Driven Design

Material-Driven Design (MDD) is a design philosophy that emphasizes the role of material properties and experiences in shaping design outcomes. This approach encourages designers to consider the intrinsic qualities, sustainability implications, and emotional connections of materials throughout the creative process. Source 1 highlights how MDD can be used to advance Sustainable Development Goal 12 by exploring waste materials like rosin and sawdust in jewelry making, thereby promoting circular economy principles within design practice. Meanwhile, Source 2 elaborates on MDD as a method that designs for material experiences, focusing on the emotional engagement designers have with different materials during creation. Both sources underscore the importance of understanding material properties deeply to foster innovative solutions that are both sustainable and emotionally resonant.

## Key Findings

- Material-Driven Design (MDD) emphasizes the intrinsic qualities and sustainability implications of materials in design.
- Designers can leverage MDD to explore waste materials, thereby contributing to sustainable development goals.
- Emotional connection with materials is a critical aspect of MDD, influencing creative outcomes.

## Open Questions

- How do designers balance the emotional and practical aspects of material selection within the framework of MDD?
