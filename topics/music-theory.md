## Music Theory

The sources provide insights into various aspects of music theory, focusing on foundational elements such as consonance and dissonance in Source 1, which is crucial for understanding the pitch processing described in Source 2. Both sources highlight the importance of frequency relations in defining musical intervals that are perceived as consonant or dissonant. Additionally, they suggest a strong link between these perceptual qualities and neural processes involved in music cognition, such as long-term memory templates and specific brain regions responsible for rhythm entrainment.

## Key Findings

- Consonance and dissonance perceptions influence pitch processing
- Frequency relations define musical intervals' consonance or dissonance
- Music theory elements are linked to neural processes in the brain

## Open Questions

- What is the role of cultural context on perception of consonance and dissonance?
