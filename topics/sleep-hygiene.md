## Sleep Hygiene

The concept of sleep hygiene is widely discussed across the provided sources as a set of practices aimed at improving or maintaining quality sleep. Key interventions include avoiding stimulants and screen time, using relaxation techniques, reserving the bed for sleeping activities alone, and establishing consistent sleep habits to support circadian rhythms. These strategies are designed to help individuals fall asleep more easily and stay asleep throughout the night without disruptions. The sources highlight how modern behaviors such as increased screen use can interfere with melatonin production, which in turn affects the body's natural sleep-wake cycles. By addressing these issues through targeted hygiene practices, individuals may improve their overall sleep quality and duration.

## Key Findings

- Sleep hygiene includes avoiding stimulants like caffeine and alcohol near bedtime
- Consistent bedtime routines and reserving the bed for sleeping contribute to better rest
- Reducing screen time before bed helps regulate melatonin production
