## Existential Questions in Healthcare

The integration of philosophical inquiry and existential questions in healthcare is emphasized by both sources. Source 1 underscores the importance of a philosophical toolkit in addressing the existential questions patients and practitioners face in healthcare settings. It highlights the value of human life and the need for a deeper understanding of these questions to provide more holistic care. Source 2, through reflections by an anesthesiologist, delves into the spiritual and transformative aspects of healthcare, suggesting that the blurring of boundaries between medicine and the humanities can enrich the practice. Both sources indicate a growing recognition of the role of existential questions in shaping patient care and the personal and professional growth of healthcare providers.

## Key Findings

- Existential questions are integral to understanding the value of human life in healthcare contexts.
- The integration of philosophy and humanities can offer deeper insights into patient care.
- Healthcare professionals benefit from reflecting on existential questions to enhance their practice.

## Open Questions

- How can healthcare institutions better incorporate philosophical inquiry and existential reflection into their practice and education?
