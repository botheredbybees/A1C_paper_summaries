## Health Services for Dementia Care in Nursing Homes

This synthesis focuses on health services for dementia care in nursing homes, highlighting a fragmented healthcare system in Italy and potential interventions for improving care quality. Source 1 discusses the challenges of integrating digital systems and ensuring staff availability in Italian nursing homes, which is crucial for delivering effective care to dementia patients. Despite these challenges, there is an absence of discussion on innovative therapeutic interventions. In contrast, Source 2 introduces community music therapy as an intervention that promotes social inclusion and uses an interdisciplinary approach, potentially offering a positive environment for dementia patients. However, the applicability of community music therapy within the context of Italian nursing homes, considering their structural and systemic challenges, is an open question.

## Key Findings

- Italian nursing homes face significant challenges in integrating digital systems and ensuring adequate staff availability.
- Community music therapy can enhance social inclusion and quality of life for dementia patients through an interdisciplinary approach.

## Open Questions

- How can community music therapy be effectively implemented in nursing homes within a fragmented healthcare system?
