## Personality Correlates of Musical and Artistic Preferences

The sources collectively explore the relationship between personality traits and aesthetic preferences for music and art. Two primary theoretical frameworks are utilized: The Five-Factor Model (FFM) and Openness to Experience within the Big Five trait model. Source 1 identifies a five-factor model specifically tailored to musical preferences, focusing on emotional/affective responses that align with broader personality constructs. Similarly, Source 2 discusses how music preference correlates with personality traits but also highlights limitations in using genre labels to capture these correlations accurately. Source 3 extends this discussion by examining the role of Openness to Experience and other Big Five Personality Traits in shaping aesthetic preferences across various domains including art, architecture, and music. Across all sources, there is a consistent theme that personality significantly influences individual preferences for artistic media; however, the complexity and nuance required to accurately map these preferences remain underexplored.

## Key Findings

- Personality traits influence individual musical preferences as well as broader aesthetic preferences.
- The Five-Factor Model of Personality (FFM) and Openness to Experience are significant in understanding personality-based correlations with artistic appreciation.
- There is a need for more nuanced approaches beyond simple genre categorizations when examining the link between music preference and personality.

## Open Questions

- What additional dimensions beyond the Big Five traits could better explain individual differences in musical and aesthetic preferences?
