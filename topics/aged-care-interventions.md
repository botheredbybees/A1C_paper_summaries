## Puppetry in Aged Care Interventions

The integration of puppetry into aged care interventions emerges as a versatile and sustainable method for fostering emotional expression among elderly individuals. Both sources emphasize the use of non-verbal communication through mediated expressions facilitated by puppets, which can help older adults articulate emotions they might find difficult to convey verbally. Puppetry also functions as an effective icebreaker in therapeutic settings, providing a safe space where residents feel comfortable sharing their feelings and engaging with peers. Sustainability is another critical aspect highlighted by the sources; using puppetry allows for continued engagement over time without diminishing returns or participant fatigue.

## Key Findings

- Puppetry serves as an accessible tool for elderly individuals to express emotions non-verbally
- It acts as a social lubricant in aged care settings, facilitating interpersonal connections and communication
- Sustainability is enhanced through the continued use of puppetry due to its engaging nature and adaptability
