## Person-Centred Approach

The synthesis integrates the person-centred approach's emphasis on unconditional positive regard, empathy, and congruence across early years practice and team-based settings. Both sources highlight that a practitioner’s authenticity (congruence) and genuine understanding of the client's experiences through empathetic engagement are foundational elements in promoting clients' autonomy and responsibility-taking. Additionally, Source 2 underscores how these principles can be extended to foster inclusive practices within teams, emphasizing the importance of collaboration among professionals who adhere to person-centred values.

## Key Findings

- Unconditional positive regard and empathy are crucial for fostering client self-efficacy.
- Practitioner authenticity (congruence) is essential in both individual and team settings.
- The person-centred approach supports inclusive practices by encouraging collaboration and shared decision-making among professionals.

## Open Questions
