## Creative Arts Education

Creative arts education is an intervention that emphasizes the use of artistic activities to promote personal and social development. Sources discuss reflection as a critical component of learning, enabling individuals to gain deeper insights into their experiences and foster personal growth. Experiential learning, facilitated through creative arts, encourages self-awareness and the ability to connect personal experiences with broader educational goals. Borton's 'Reach, Touch, and Teach' approach provides a structured method for addressing student concerns through a three-step reflective process: What, So What, Now What. This model encourages learners to critically examine their experiences, understand their implications, and plan future actions, thus integrating reflection into the creative arts education framework.

## Key Findings

- Creative arts education enhances personal and social development through reflective practices.
- Experiential learning in creative arts promotes self-awareness and personal growth.
- Borton's three-step reflective process integrates well with creative arts education to address student concerns.

## Open Questions

- How can creative arts education be further developed to better integrate reflective practices and address the diverse needs of all learners?
