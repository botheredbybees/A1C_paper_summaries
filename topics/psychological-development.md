## Psychological Development Through Creative Expression

The integration of creative and therapeutic interventions such as phototherapy and play-based activities significantly influences psychological development. Phototherapy and therapeutic photography, which leverage visual mediums to aid in emotional expression and healing (Source 1), have gained relevance in the digital age by providing accessible tools for individuals to process their emotions visually. Play, as explored through serial mediation models (Source 2), enhances positive developmental outcomes such as resilience and emotional intelligence during emerging adulthood. Despite differing modalities, both interventions support personal growth and mental health improvement through self-expression and emotional regulation techniques.

## Key Findings

- Phototherapy and therapeutic photography offer significant benefits for emotional healing in the digital age.
- Play-based activities can enhance psychological development by fostering resilience and emotional intelligence.
- Both phototherapy and play interventions facilitate emotional expression and contribute to mental health improvement.

## Open Questions

- How do phototherapy techniques compare with traditional talk therapy methods in promoting long-term psychological well-being?
