## Medical Model Of Disability

The concept of the medical model of disability is critiqued in both sources for its narrow focus on individual pathology and limitations. Source 1 discusses how music can be understood through the lens of cognitive styles, highlighting that the medical model may overlook the diverse ways people with disabilities experience and engage with art forms such as music. In contrast, Source 2 examines unconscious biases within society that perpetuate the dominance of the medical model over the social model of disability. It emphasizes the importance of recognizing these biases to foster a more inclusive understanding of disability rights under laws like the Equality Act.

## Key Findings

- The medical model focuses on individual pathology rather than societal barriers
- Unconscious biases in society support the medical model at the expense of a social model approach
- The medical model overlooks diverse experiences and engagement with art forms among people with disabilities

## Open Questions

- How can unconscious bias be addressed to promote the adoption of the social model over the medical model?
