## Photography Course

The provided sources from a photography course highlight the importance of foundational concepts such as color theory and composition techniques in crafting effective visual narratives. Source 1 introduces students to these essential elements during Week 2, setting the stage for understanding how colors and arrangements within a frame can significantly impact the message conveyed by a photograph. Moving forward, by Week 10, as detailed in Source 2, critical reflection becomes a pivotal aspect of learning, encouraging students not only to capture images but also to analyze their work critically through written reflection and interaction with fellow learners. This approach underscores the course's emphasis on both technical skills and conceptual understanding, blending practical exercises like photo essays with theoretical insights.

## Key Findings

- The photography course emphasizes foundational concepts such as color theory and composition techniques in Week 2 to build a strong visual narrative foundation for students.
- By Week 10, critical reflection is integrated into the curriculum to encourage deeper understanding of one's own work and its broader context within photography.
